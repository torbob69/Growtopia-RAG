# Pilgrim's Hat

There are all kinds of things to be thankful for during the holidays, but maybe one of the most important is just how dang awesome those pilgrims looked! Top your ensemble with this traditional hat and join the fun!

The **Pilgrim's Hat** is a hat item that was added as part of the Thanksgiving Week 2018 update on November 22, 2018.
##  Function
When equipped, Pilgrim's Hat serves a fully cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the pilgrim (male) set, which consists of the following items:

    * Pilgrim's Hat (hat)

    * Pilgrim's Coat (shirt)

    * Pilgrim's Shoes (feet)
