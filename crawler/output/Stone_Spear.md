In ancient times, sticks and stones were about as good as it got. The upside is it's a magic stone - this spear will teleport back into your hand once thrown!

The **Stone Spear** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the Growassic Park update on October 7, 2016.
##  Function
You got a spear, so toss it.

Enough of that

When equipped, the **Stone Spear** grants the _Throwing Spear_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further. It will also replace the player's punching animation with a spear.
