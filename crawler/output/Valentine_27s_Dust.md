A sprinkle of romance, a touch of affection, and a dash of intimacy, all ground up into one passionate powder! What could it be for?

Special Event

Found in the _Valentine's Card_ special event.
One has 30 seconds to find 5 dropped Valentine's Dust.
The **Valentine's Dust** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2018.
