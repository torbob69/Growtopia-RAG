The **Evil Space Helmet** is an unsplicable hair item which was added alongside the Shop Pack on January 24, 2014.
##  Function
You command a starship!.

Your fleet has abandoned you.

When equipped, this item grants the _Space Commander_ mod, which summons a spaceship that follows the player around and shoots red laser beams when punching.
##  Trivia
  * The Evil Space Helmet is based on the helmets of the fictional Imperial pilots—specifically, that of the TIE pilots—from the _Star Wars_ franchise.
    * A common misconception found within the playerbase is that the item was inspired by the helmet worn by the franchise's main villain Darth Vader. A post by the ex-moderator Zerkon debunked this claim.[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Evil_Space_Helmet#cite_note-1)
  * The spaceship flying around the player resembles a Star Destroyer, also from the said franchise.
