The **Magnifico Carne Guacamole - Bueno** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2018 on May 4, 2018.
##  Function
Get 100 Gems for beating a Pet Trainer.

Your stomach's rumbling.

When consumed, this item grants the player the _Food: Pet Gems 100_ mod, which will give the player 100 gems for every Pet Trainer defeated for half an hour.
##  Trivia
  * This item is one of the three Magnifico Carne Guacamole variants that depend on the food quality percentage achieved by the player.
    * Magnifico Carne Guacamole - Perfecto is earned at 96%-100%.
    * Magnifico Carne Guacamole - Sabroso is earned at 85%-95%.
    * Magnifico Carne Guacamole - Bueno is earned at 60-84%.
  * The Spanish phrase "Magnifico Carne Guacamole - Bueno" can be translated to "Magnificent Beef Guacamole - Good".
