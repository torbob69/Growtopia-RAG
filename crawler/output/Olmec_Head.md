The **Olmec Head** is an unsplicable solid toggleable animated foreground block which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2013 on May 3, 2013.
##  Function
<https://growtopiawiki.com/w/File:Cumbia.ogg>The Olmec Head's music.
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, an Olmec Head can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, an Olmec Head will change its appearance, showing a 2-frame animation, and play a piece of music.
##  Trivia
  * In the game files, the Olmec Head's music is named cumbia.
  * The Olmec Head's music is based on Kevin MacLeod's No Frills Cumbia (faster).
  * The music plays in the main menu during Cinco De Mayo Week.
