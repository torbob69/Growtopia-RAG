The **Happy Flower Grass** is an unsplicable non-solid animated foreground block which was added as part of Easter Week 2015.
##  Function
As an animated foreground block, a Happy Flower Grass features an automatic 2-frame animation of itself waving around.

##  Trivia
  * Happy Flower Grass Seeds used to be able to be found in the Small Seed Pack; however, they, along with other seeds of unsplicable items, were removed from the pack.
  * As part of the Block Party 2 update, the quantity of Happy Flower Grasses obtained from combining was increased from 1 to 5.
  * Prior to Easter Week 2021, players only had a chance to obtain 1 Happy Flower Grass from EGGRESCUE at a time.
  * As part of the Easter Week 2022, the quantity of Happy Flower Grasses obtained from combining was increased from 5 to 10.
