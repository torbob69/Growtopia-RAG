# Tournament Ticket

This ticket allows you entry into one Grand Tournament. Visit Master Peng in the world TOURNAMENT to enter the tournament. You can battle as many times as you wish in the current tournament, but when it ends, your ticket will self-destruct.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **6 Hits** **5 Hits** – Restores after **5s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
**Tournament Ticket** is an unsplicable consumable item that was added in The Grand Tournament update. It can be bought from Master Peng for 10,000 gems. This ticket allows players to participate in the tournament. Upon participation, the ticket will become a Punched Tournament Ticket.
