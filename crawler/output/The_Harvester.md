# The Harvester

**The Harvester** is a villain added during Harvest Festival 2016. It can be spawned by using a Harvest Horn and has a chance to spawn from using Crime Waves.
Cards she uses

Cosmic Power - Life Harvest
Superpower - Incinerate
Superpower - Frozen Mirror
Superpower - Crush
Superpower - Megawatt Pulse
Health: 20
Method: Bring Frost Breath, Frozen Mirror, Super Strength, Super Speed and Crush. Alternate between using **Frost Breath** and **Frozen Mirror**. When The Harvester uses **Incinerate** , use **Super Speed** right afterwards and use a card to defeat whatever card The Harvester will use next.
Upon defeating, the possible rewards to receive are:
  * Henchman
  * Superhero Mask - Autumn
  * Crimefighter Mask - Autumn
  * Superheroine Top - Autumn
  * Super Tights - Autumn
  * Heroic Tights - Autumn
  * Super Shirt - Autumn
  * Heroic Boots - Autumn
  * Super Cape - Autumn
  * Super Eye Mask - Autumn
  * Muscle Shirt - Autumn
  * Super Logo - Leaf
  * Villain Portrait - The Harvester
  * Cosmic Power - Life Harvest
  * Harvest Witch Mask
  * Cloud Rabbit
  * Rake
  * Centerpiece
