The **Magic Infused Stone Background** is an unsplicable background block which was added as a part of Spelunkorama Strikes Back! update.****
##  Function
As a dark background block, a Magic Infused Stone Background enshrouds the tile it is placed on in darkness.
Light source blocks may provide light to neighboring dark tiles when placed. In a similar manner, a player can illuminate their dark surroundings by equipping certain light source clothes. The Night Vision Goggles disregards darkness completely.
