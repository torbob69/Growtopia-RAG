The **Thanksgiving Bell** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2018.
##  Function
When used, the bell will summon several Thanksgiving Turkeys in the world, which will disappear after 90 seconds if they aren't punched enough times. Bashing one of these turkeys will reward a Dead Thanksgiving Turkey.
##  Trivia
  * As of Thanksgiving Week 2021, the Thanksgiving Bell now comes in packs of 5. However, its price was also increased, now costing 10,000 Gems for each purchase instead of 5,000.
  * As of Thanksgiving Week 2022, it now costs 7,500 Gems for each purchase.
