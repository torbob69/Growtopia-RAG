The **Riding Rabbid** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of UbiWeek 2022 Day 6 on January 29, 2022.
##  Function
Charge!!!

Flushed Away!

When equipped, the **Riding Rabbid** grants the _Punch Range: Riding Rabbid!_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further.
##  Trivia
  * The Riding Rabbid is based on a fictional character Rabbid in the platform game Rayman Raving Rabbids, developed and published by Ubisoft.
  * During UbiWeek 2022, the Riding Rabbid could be obtained from completing Star Voyages. As of UbiWeek 2023, the Riding Rabbid is only obtainable from UbiCrates.
