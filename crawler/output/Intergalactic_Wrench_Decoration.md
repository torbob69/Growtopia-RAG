The **Intergalactic Wrench Decoration** is a wrench decoration which was added as part of the May Season on May 1, 2025.
##  Function
When equipped through the 'Wrench Customization' tab when wrenching oneself, the Intergalactic Wrench Decoration adds dark blue spikes surrounding the player's wrench connected to their name.
