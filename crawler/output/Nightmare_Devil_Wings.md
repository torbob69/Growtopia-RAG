The **Nightmare Devil Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2014 on October 30, 2014.
##  Function
You can jump in mid-air! It's like flying, but less.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Nightmare Devil Wings** grants the _Double Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
