This item was given to **Tony26** for their contributions as a voluntary moderator.
**Tony's Tiger Mask** is an unobtainable hat item which was added as part of the Rise of the Desert Dragons update on August 3, 2013.
##  Function
When equipped, Tony's Tiger Mask serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Tony's Tiger set, which consists of the following items:

    * Tony's Tiger Mask (Hat)

    * Tony's Tiger Suit (Shirt)
  * Both items are based on Tony the Tiger, the cartoon tiger mascot for Kellogg's Frosted Flakes breakfast cereal.
  * The descriptions of the items are a reference to Tony the Tiger's catchphrase, "_They're grrrrrreat!_ ", which is also the slogan of the cereal.
  * Before Tony received his suit and mask, his exclusive reward as a moderator was a special purple skin, which is now available as an exclusive skin color for supporters.
