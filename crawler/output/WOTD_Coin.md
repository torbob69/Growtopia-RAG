The **WOTD Coin** is an unobtainable and unbreakable foreground block that was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2022 Day 3. It appears at a random free spot in the current World Of The Day.
##  Function
When punched by players, it gives them a Growtoken. However, there is a limit of one Growtoken per player per day.
##  Trivia
  * **WOTD Coin's** sprite resembles a spinning Growtoken.
