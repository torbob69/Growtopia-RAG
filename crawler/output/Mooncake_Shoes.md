# Mooncake Shoes

New Balance Mooncake shoes! Good on all terrain, hard wearing and also yummy! Seriously, don't eat them! Who knows what you have stepped in!

The **Mooncake Shoes** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2020 on September 24, 2020.
##  Function
When equipped, the Mooncake Shoes serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * As suggested by the item's description, the  Mooncake Shoes' design is based on the  Balance Mooncake.
