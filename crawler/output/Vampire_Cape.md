The **Vampire Cape** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2013 on October 28, 2013.
##  Function
You can jump in mid-air! It's like flying, but less.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Vampire Cape** grants the _Double Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
Equipping this item, alongside a Vampire Fangs, Slick Black Hair and Vamp Vest, will earn one the _Costume Contest_ achievement.

##  Trivia
  * There is a small chance for an Inside-Out Vampire Cape to be given instead when one consumes 25 Cape Tatters.
  * Prior to Halloween Week 2015, sacrificing a stack of items with rarity 101 or above to the Growganoth can directly reward one with a Vampire Cape.
  * Upon consumption of 25 cape tatters and getting a Vampire Cape, a message will appear saying "_You crafted a Vampire Cape!_ "
  * This item is considered a part of the Vampire set, which consists of the following items:

    * Slick Black Hair (Hair)

    * Vampire Fangs (Face)

    * Vamp Vest (Shirt)

    * Vampire Cape (Back)
