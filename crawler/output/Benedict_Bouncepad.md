The **Benedict Bouncepad** is an unsplicable solid trampoline foreground block item which was added as part of Easter Week 2024 on April 7, 2024.
##  Function
Jumping on a trampoline block such as a Benedict Bouncepad will cause the player to bounce, and by holding the jump button, the player can jump even higher. The _High Jump_ mod also boosts one's jump height on a trampoline block.
