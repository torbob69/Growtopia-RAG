The **I love Dirt Shirt** is an untradable shirt item that was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2021 Day 3 on August 25, 2021.
##  Function
When equipped, the I love Dirt Shirt serves a fully cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The I love Dirt Shirt was added as per the suggestion of the forum user GODAPOLLYON,[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/I_love_Dirt_Shirt#cite_note-suggestion-1) who stated in their suggestion thread, '_Anyways, its a simple White Shirt with an image of dirt in the middle and text that says "I 💚 Dirt"._ '
