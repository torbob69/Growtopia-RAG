The **Battle Mutant Fish** is an unsplicable pet hand item which was added as part of the Gone Fishin'! update on January 24, 2018.
##  Function
When equipped, the Battle Mutant Fish will summon a Mutant Fish which will appear and float in the air, following the player around. The Battle Mutant Fish serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
