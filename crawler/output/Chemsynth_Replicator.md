**Chemsynth Replicator** is a consumable item which is used in synthesizing chemicals. One of these come in the Chemsynth Pack.
##  Function
When used on a Chemsynth Tank, it will duplicate the chemical to the right-next tank, pushing all other chemicals to the right and removing the right-most chemical.
