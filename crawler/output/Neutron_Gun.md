##  Function
You fire Neutron Beams (requires Neutron Pack to work)

You're afraid of a ghost.

When equipped, this item grants the player the _Hunting Ghosts_ mod. When a player punches while wearing this item, they will shoot a beam of neutrons (requires equipping a Neutron Pack) that pulls Ghosts touching the beam. When two or more beams cross, all of the players who shot the beam will die.
