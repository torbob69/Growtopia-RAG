The **Legal Briefs** is an unsplicable pants item which was added as part of the Daily Challenges update on November 18, 2015.
##  Function
When equipped, the **Legal Briefs** allows the player to remove the _Malpractice_ mod. This process will consume the item if the mod is present. Otherwise, it functions as a regular clothing item.
