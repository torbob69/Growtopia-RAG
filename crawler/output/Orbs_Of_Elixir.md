The **Orbs Of Elixir** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of The Carnival update on October 13, 2014.
##  Function
You can jump in mid-air! It's like flying, but less.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Orbs Of Elixir** grants the _Double Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
