The **Topiary Hedge** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Spring Clash 2019 on March 1, 2019.
##  Function
When a player equipping a Garden Shears punches a Topiary Hedge, its appearance will transform into one of the four available ones: box, bird, sphere, or spiral. Each punch will guarantee a different outcome.
