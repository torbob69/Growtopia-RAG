# Sombrero Assault

Go big or go home! Why wear just one hat when you can wear three at a time! No matter how many hats you throw you still have 3 on your head... It's basically magic!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be transmuted.

**1h**
Pinata Bash

Participate in successfully smashing an  Ultra Pinata with a  Lucky Pinata Hat equipped during the **Pinata Bash!** event.
May give **1** item each time.
The **Sombrero Assault** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2021 on May 5, 2021.
##  Function
3 Hats, free hats, yay... hats!!

That's enough hats!

When equipped, the **Sombrero Assault** grants the _Punch Damage: Sombrero Assault_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. The player will throw hats when punching instead of the fist.
