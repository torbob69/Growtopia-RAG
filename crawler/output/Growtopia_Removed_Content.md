##  Features
The added & removed **Features** to Growtopia
### Discontinued Platforms
Growtopia used to be available for the following consoles: **Xbox One** , **PlayStation 4** , **Nintendo Switch**. A WebOS build of the game was planned during its early stages, but it was never published, because WebOS was deemed 'a dying platform'.[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Growtopia/Removed_Content#cite_note-1)[[2]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Growtopia/Removed_Content#cite_note-2)

### Removed Daily Challenges
The following Daily Challenges have been removed due to large public outcry and abuse. Their special prizes were given new recipes.
  * **Bunny Battle:** Collect naturally spawned Magic Eggs for points. Removed due to players exploiting the challenge by hoarding them prior to the event and cycling popular worlds filled with Wooden Platform to maximise chance to spawn. +1pts Collect a naturally spawned Magic Egg.

  * **Zombie Stomp:** Kill players who have been infected with the g-Virus during the Pandemic for points. Removed due to players simply exploiting the Broadcasting system to bring infected players into their world. +1pts Per zombie killed.

  * **Cosmic Crown:** Collect naturally spawned Comet Dust and Antimatter Dust for points. Only appears during the Night Of The Comet event. Removed due to players exploiting the challenge by hoarding them prior to the event and cycling popular worlds filled with Wooden Platform to maximise chance to spawn. +1pts Collect a naturally spawned Comet Dust. +20pts Collect a naturally spawned Antimatter Dust.

### Super-Block Event     _Main article:Super-Block Event_
The **Super-Block Event** was a removed Grow Event added as part of the Guilds update on April 1, 2018. It began on April 13, 2018, a day after Easter Week had concluded,[[3]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Growtopia/Removed_Content#cite_note-3) and ended on April 16, 2018, thereby lasting for 4 days in total. This was the first and only occurrence of the event.
The Super-Block Event was the first ever guild event added to the game, and is considered a precursor to the current version of Seasonal Clashes.
* * *
### Ubisoft Club Rewards     _Main article:Ubisoft Club Rewards_
The **Ubisoft Club Rewards** system was one of the few features available only to the discontinued console edition of _Growtopia_. By earning achievements, players could obtain exclusive tokens, which would be able to be traded for unique items.
Four of the exclusive items obtained through this system has been added to the PC/mobile version of _Growtopia_ : Glowing Shirt, Plunger, The Brother Hood, and the Unicorn. One, however, is still yet to be added: the Energy Drink.

###  Nyan Hat / Taco Dog/Turtle Hat     _Main article:Turtle Hat_
Nyan Hat

1 could be obtained from purchasing the item itself for 25,000 gems in the Growtopia Store.
"NYAN NYAN NYAN NYAN NYAN NYAN NYAN NYAN NYAN"

The **Nyan Hat** was a hat item which was added alongside Phonecats Hat on March 7, 2013. Both items were based on the Nyan Cat internet meme.
In May 2013, Christopher Torres, the copyright owner of Nyan Cat, sued Warner Bros. for an unauthorized usage of the character.[[5]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Growtopia/Removed_Content#cite_note-5) In light of this, the developers decided to alter the Nyan Hat's design in order to avoid any copyright issue themselves.
Taco Dog

1 could be obtained from purchasing the item itself for 25,000 gems in the Growtopia Store.
"Taco Dog would never sue anybody, he just likes delicious tacos."

Per fan suggestion, the **Taco Dog** was introduced on May 11, 2013 as the Nyan Hat's replacement. While the Taco Dog seemed like a good idea at that time, it was soon discovered that the Taco Dog was another creation of Torres.[[6]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Growtopia/Removed_Content#cite_note-6) A poll was soon posted, asking the playerbase for ideas for the second redesign.[[7]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Growtopia/Removed_Content#cite_note-7)
The possible choices for the redesign at that time were Yerfdog, Piggy Bank, Turtle, Kitty, and Puppy. The poll closed on May 13, with Turtle winning 45.12% of the votes. The Turtle Hat was then rolled out on May 16. A teaser was first posted on the RTsoft forums hours before its release.[[8]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Growtopia/Removed_Content#cite_note-8)

An animation made by Christopher Torres, featuring his copyrighted Fiesta Dog design, which later inspired the ill-fated Taco Dog item[[9]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Growtopia/Removed_Content#cite_note-9)[[10]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Growtopia/Removed_Content#cite_note-10)

##  Old Sponsoring An Item Rules
These are the terms if you want to sponsor the items.
  * You get to suggest an item and be its sponsor. (Could be clothes or an object like a chair or a brick).
  * If the name is deemed inappropriate or conflicts with something we already have planned we'll let you know. We have complete veto power over anything you suggest.
  * It won't have special powers (invisibility) or new functionality that doesn't exist in other items, we get to decide that part.
  * It costs 3,000 World Locks (yes, really! and we're not kidding) which you must have all set aside in a single world that you have world locked.
  * It may take up to 6 weeks for the item to be available in the game, once we agree to create it.
  * It will probably have a recipe, and it will be available to all players in some way (though we decide how they get it!).
  * You do not get a free item - you'll have to earn the item just like everyone else.
  * This is for one item, so something like "Doctor outfit and tools" wouldn't work as it would be like 6 items.
  * It will be announced in the in-game news "New item: _________ (Sponsored by <Your User Name>)" and we'll make sure it stays up there for at least a few days. (We can thank up to three names, but the deal is done with only one person).
  * If an idea is too hard or conflicts with something else we have planned, or for any other reason, we reserve the right to cancel and refund the World Locks.
  * Since everybody sponsors wings all the time, we are no longer accepting sponsorship for any wings or capes.
  * This offer is a temporary experiment and we might stop doing it, or change it at anytime. Of course we'd send back any World Locks if we can't make your item.
  * Using a player's world that is not yours on the application will result in a ban, since you are trying to steal 3000 World Locks from another player. The moderators can find out who you are.
