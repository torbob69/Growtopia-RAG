The **Potato Couch** is a non-solid seat foreground block which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2016.
##  Function
When a player stands in front of a seat such as the Potato Couch for a few seconds, the player will automatically be seen "sitting" on it.

##  Trivia
  * Unlike most rarityless blocks, a Potato Couch may drop a seed or the block itself when broken.
  * The name and description of the block is a play on the term couch potato, which refers to someone who typically spends little or no time exercising and a great deal of time watching television.
