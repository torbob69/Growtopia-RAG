Dungeons

It can be found naturally generated in Dungeon worlds.

Dungeons Reward

1 may be awarded to the player for completing a Dungeon at **Silver Tier or higher**.
The **Altered Throne** is an unsplicable non-solid seat foreground block which was added as part of the Dungeons update on December 9, 2024.
##  Function
When a player stands in front of a seat such as the Altered Throne for a few seconds, the player will automatically be seen "sitting" on it.
