The **Music Box** is a splicable non-solid toggleable animated foreground block which was added as part of _Growtopia_ 's launch.
##  Function
<https://growtopiawiki.com/w/File:Music_box.ogg>The Music Box's music.
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Music Box can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Music Box will change its appearance, showing a 2-framed animation, and play a piece of music.

##  Trivia
  * The original theme which the Music Box's tune is based on is Johannes Brahms' "Wiegenlied", which can now commonly be found as a stock lullaby in various media.
  * In the game files, the Music Box's music is named music_box.
