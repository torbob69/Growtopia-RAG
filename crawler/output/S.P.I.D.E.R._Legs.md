The **S.P.I.D.E.R. Legs** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Spring Clash 2019.
##  Function
Freed from the wire web!

Back into storage!

When equipped, the **S.P.I.D.E.R. Legs** grants the _Speedy: S.P.I.D.E.R.!_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and cling onto Climbing Walls by pressing the jump button.
The item also allows the player to cling onto Climbing Walls by holding their jump. Other items which serve the same purpose include:

  * Claws of Barko

  * Climbing Boots

  * Climbing Picks

  * Ezio's Boots

  * Mountain Boots

  * Tarantula Legs

  * Will of the Wild (earth version)
