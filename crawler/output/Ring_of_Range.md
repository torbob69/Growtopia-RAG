# Ring of Range

Reach out, touch space! As well as touching space, this Ring will increase your punch and build range in worlds which you own. In non owned worlds your build and punch range is only slightly increased.

This item cannot be dropped or traded.
This item never drops any seeds.

The **Ring of Range** is an unsplicable and untradable hand item added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2021 Day 5 on August 27, 2021.
##  Function
Stretch Growstrong!

Snapped!

When equipped, the **Ring of Range** grants the _Extended Punch and Build Range..._ mod, which allows the player to punch three tiles further (11x11) and build three tiles further (11x11). Note that in worlds they don’t own, the range is limited to one additional tile (7x7).
##  Trivia
  * The equip and unequip text of this item is a reference to Stretch Armstrong, the presumably unable to be snapped stretch toy.
