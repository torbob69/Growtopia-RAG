The **Haunted Mausoleum** is an unsplicable solid foreground block which was added as part of Halloween Week 2014 on October 30, 2014.
##  Function
When placed, the Haunted Mausoleum serves a purely aesthetic purpose. Every once in a while, a ghost particle will be emitted from the block.
##  Trivia
  * The ghost particle effect used by the Haunted Mausoleum is shared with the Ghost Costume and Harvester Of Sorrows.
