The **Monkey Warrior's Staff** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Lunar New Year 2022 on February 1, 2022.
##  Function
Rising against the elite!

Falling to the ground!

When equipped, the **Monkey Warrior's Staff** grants the _Punch Damage + Punch Power: Monkey Warrior's Staff_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and knock back other players further.
##  Trivia
  * The Monkey Warrior's Staff is based on Ruyi Jingu Bang, a magical staff wielded by the immortal monkey Sun Wukong in the 16th-century classic Chinese novel _Journey to the West_.
  * This item is considered a part of the Monkey Warrior set, which consists of the following items:

    * Monkey Warrior's Circlet (hat)

    * Monkey Face Mask (face)

    * Monkey Warrior's Shirt (shirt)

    * Monkey Warrior's Pants (pants)

    * Monkey Warrior's Staff (hand)
