# Wildlife Supporter Pack
During Wildlife Weekend

|
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Wildlife Supporter Pack** for **14.99 USD** in the Growtopia Store.
"Be a part of the solution and help responsibly plant billions of trees every year. 2$ from every purchase will go to supporting the work of Ecologi. Grow these little seeds and be rewards for your contribution. Also includes an endangered Arlequin Toad for you to look after and play your part in preserving the wildlife in Growtopia."
Every purchase will give **1** item.

The **Wildlife Supporter Pack** is an item pack which was added as part of Wildlife Weekend on March 10, 2023.
##  Items
The pack contains the following items:
  * Gems ×540,000
  * Eco Seed ×50
  * Golden Eco Seed ×5
  * Arlequin Toad Pet ×1
