# Tapjoy

This is the page for **Tapjoy**.
You may be looking for **MyChips** instead.
**Tapjoy** is a platform that uses ads to generate virtual rewards for users, such as gems in _Growtopia_. Tapjoy can be found in game in the "Earn Free Gems" tab in the shop which is **only visible for mobile users**.
##  Conditions
**Tapjoy Offerwall** is only available to players aged 13 and above and must be accessed from a mobile device. It will not appear in the Growtopia Store for younger accounts or on non-mobile platforms. To let Tapjoy work properly, Players also need to enable all options under “Edit Data Sharing Preferences” in the Growtopia settings.
To get rewards from **Tapjoy** , players need to complete several tasks that have been assigned. The rewards will be given to players when they successfully complete the tasks on time. After completing a task within the required time, players must log in to game to receive the Gems specified by the offers.
##  Rewards
  * Players can earn Gems for free by completing Tapjoy offers, different applications provide different amounts of Gems depending on the task.
  * During special events, Tapjoy rewards may be boosted up to 2x or 5× the normal amount.
  * Players can also obtain the Supporter or Super Supporter for free by earning Gems through **Tapjoy** offers.
