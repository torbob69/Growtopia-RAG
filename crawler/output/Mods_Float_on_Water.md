# Mods/Float on Water

< Mods
The _**Float on Water**_ mod allows the player to float on water. This mod can be subdivided in to the following categories:
  * _**Forced Float:**_ Allows the player to float on water permanently. When this mod is granted to a player whilst being inside water, the player will slowly ascend to the water's surface.
  * _**Surfboard:**_ Allows the player to float on water for 3 seconds upon contact.
