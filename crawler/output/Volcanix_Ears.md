# Volcanix Ears

Very pointy! And the pointier the ears, the wiser the Volcanix, as they say. The founding race of the Stellarix Union, the Volcanix are a deeply pragmatic and calculating people with long lifespans. These factors have led them to become distant, even detached from galactic affairs, which is why it's up to the Startopian Empire to keep the peace!

The **Volcanix Ears** is an unsplicable face item which was added as part of the Startopia update.
##  Function
When equipped, the Volcanix Ears serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The Volcanix Ears are based on the ears of the eponymous Vulcan, a fictional extraterrestrial humanoid race from the _Star Trek_ franchise. They are generally similar to humans in appearance, distinguished mainly by their pointed ears.
