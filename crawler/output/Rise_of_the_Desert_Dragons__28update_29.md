# Rise of the Desert Dragons (update)

Growtopia Gazette
> August 3rd: Today we have a range of amazingly delicious new features and items to make Growtopia a more interesting place!
> NEW: HERE BE DRAGONS - Command your very own tiny ferocious winged creature of lore with the Dragon Hand (Added to store)
> NEW: Desert Blast - An ancient device rumored to be capable of creating unique and mysterious new worlds... (Only attainable by a new random event)
> NEW: WEATHER MACHINES - Growtech has done it again! Amaze your friends by installing a Night, Arid, or Sunny Weather Machine from the store.
> NEW: COOKING - with the right ingredients, you can make dough, bread, pizza and more in the new E-Z Cook Oven. (A Laboratory will work too!)
> NEW: Scoreboard - Punching this is the same as doing /top, we'll install one in the START world.
> TWEAK: The Death Ray now has visual effects
> MODSTYLE: @Tony26 may look a bit different today
> - @Seth and @Hamumu

The **Rise of the Desert Dragons** update was introduced on August 3, 2013.
