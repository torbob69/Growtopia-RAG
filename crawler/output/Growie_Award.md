# Growie Award

Congratulations! Your film has been selected as the best picture of the week! Or you just bought this trophy from someone else like a common peasant.
Properties
This item can only be used in World-Locked worlds.
This item can't be destroyed - smashing it will return it to your backpack if you have room!
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.

Video Of The Week

Rewarded to a player when their YouTube video gets chosen as the Video Of The Week.
The **Growie Award** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added alongside the Cinema Pack on May 16, 2014.
##  Function
When placed, the Growie Award serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
Step 16 of the  Quest For Honor requires the player to deliver 1 of this item. 1 Neptune's Crown may also be used as an alternative.
##  Trivia
  * The Growie Award is based on the Grammy Award. The name is a portmanteau of "_grow_ " and "_grammy_ ".
  * A snippet of the Growtopia Gazette which announced the implementation of the Growie Award said, "_To celebrate the creativity of Growtopians we're announcing a new prestigious weekly award called a Growie that we'll give to the creator of an epic Growtopia-related Youtube video. It also gets featured in the news for the entire week!_ "
  * GrowNoobShow, a channel run by players Hakanss and RuneSport, won the first-ever Growie Award with their music video.
  * When a new Growie Award is handed out, a global message will say, "_Growtopia Video of the Week: <video name> by <video creator>! Type /news to go watch it!_"
  * As part of the Quality of Life Changes update on May 20, 2022, the Growie Award is no longer placeable in public worlds and worlds the player is an admin in.
