# Shirt Rack

Show off your best shirts with your very own Shirt Rack!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item has special properties you can adjust with the Wrench.
Data: Paint PreviewType — Display Shelf – Foreground — Aqua Blue Charcoal Green Purple Red Varnish Yellow
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **10 Hits** **8 Hits** – Restores after **4s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Paint Preview Aqua Blue Charcoal Green Purple Red Varnish Yellow
Consumption

Consume a(n)  Black Friday Black Box Xtreme **(x1)**.
May yield **1** item each.

The **Shirt Rack** is an unsplicable foreground block which was added as part of Black Friday 2024 on November 28, 2024.
##  Function
When wrenched, the player can select up to 4 shirt items to display in the Shirt Rack.
