**Jin Chan Leash** is a hand item that was added as part of Lunar New Year 2020.
##  Function
Hop on

Hop off

When equipped, this item grants the _Jin Chan Leash_ mod, which serves a purely cosmetic purpose. Moreover, the item summons a golden bullfrog which follows the player around. When punching, the frog will spew out fireballs out of its mouth.
##  Trivia
  * The Jin Chan Leash is based on the Jin Chan (金蟾), commonly translated as "Money Toad" or "Money Frog". According to traditional feng shui beliefs, this mythical creature helps attract and protect wealth, and guards against bad luck.
