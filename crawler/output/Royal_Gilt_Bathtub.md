The **Royal Gilt Bathtub** is an unsplicable non-solid SFX foreground block which was added as part of the Summer Clash 2018 on June 1, 2018.
##  Function
Being an SFX foreground block, a Royal Gilt Bathtub makes a shower noise and plays a water animation when punched.
