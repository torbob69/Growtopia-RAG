The **Pants of Summer** is an unsplicable pants item that was added as part of Equinox Weekend on March 25, 2022.
##  Function
When equipped, the Pants of Summer serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Equinox Summer set, which consists of the following items:

    * Crown of Summer (hat)

    * Shirt of Summer (shirt)

    * Pants of Summer (pants)

    * Boots of Summer (shoes)
