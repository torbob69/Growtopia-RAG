The **Golden Sparkling Mallet** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2025 on February 13, 2025.
##  Function
Shining, Shimmering, Splendid!

Can't touch this!

When equipped, the **Golden Sparkling Mallet** grants the _Punch Damage + Punch Range: Golden Sparkling Mallet_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and punch one tile further.
