# Black Shoes

From the most formal of occasions to the swankiest of clubs (on the darkest of nights, of course), everyone needs a pair of black shoes! These are extra-special, however, because they're YOURS.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Feet – Clothes
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: Full Collision
Hardness: **0 Hits** **0 Hits** – Restores after **0s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Black Espadrille **(×5)** ,
Air Robinsons **(×5)** and
Heroic Boots - Black **(×2)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Black Shoes** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2019 Day 1 on August 19, 2019.
##  Function
Smooth!

Put on the shoe rack!

When equipped, the **Black Shoes** grants the _Speedy: Shoes_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.
