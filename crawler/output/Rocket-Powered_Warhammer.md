Place in the top three of the **Star Strife** Guild Daily Challenge, only if the player had contributed points to the challenge.
The **Rocket-Powered Warhammer** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the Challengers Approach! update.
##  Function
Charged to 11!

What? Are you crazy?

When equipped, the **Rocket-Powered Warhammer** grants the _Punch Damage: Rocket Power_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. It also has a special punching effect that causes particles of smashed blocks to fly.
##  Trivia
  * The Rocket-Powered Warhammer could be a reference to Reinhardt's Rocket Hammer from Overwatch.
