# Fish Trainin' Pack
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Fish Trainin' Pack** for **10,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"Get ready to train your favorite fish! This pack contains 2x Fish Flakes, 2x Fish Medicine, AND a Rare Training Port - use it to put a perfect fish into your fish tank for training!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Fish Trainin' Pack** was added alongside the Gone Fishin'! update on 14 January 2018.
##  Items
The pack includes:
  * Fish Flakes x2
  * Fish Medicine x2
  * Training Port x1
