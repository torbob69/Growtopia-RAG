The **Pet Battle Gym Healer** is a non-solid NPC foreground block which was added as part of the Pet Battle Update on March 19, 2025.
##  Function
A **Pet Battle Gym Healer** is seen in six Gym worlds: Terraforge, Zephyrwing, Abyssal Tide, Emberflame, Stormcrag, and Pyrodelve. Players can punch them to show a window allowing them to heal both their battle pets for a Pet Bandage which regenerates every hour or players can purchase five for $2.99. Alternatively, players can heal their battle pets by by wrenching oneself and clicking on 'Battle Pets' under the 'Leashed Pets' tab.
