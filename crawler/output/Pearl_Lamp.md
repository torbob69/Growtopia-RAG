The **Pearl Lamp** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable animated foreground block which was added as part of Beach Party 2022 on June 24, 2022.
##  Function
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Pearl Lamp can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Pearl Lamp will change its appearance, showing a 2-framed animation of itself providing light to its surrounding. As a light source, a Pearl Lamp emits light in dark areas.
