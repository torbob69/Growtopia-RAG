##  Trivia
  * The first four variants of the Card Block, which are based on playing cards, are spliced with the Tangram Block Seed and Das Red Balloon Seed, while the other Card Blocks, which are only seen at the Carnival, are unobtainable by regular means.
  * While they used to drop from trees in-game, due to a change made to certain seeds on May 11, 2013, seeds of the splicable Card Blocks couldn't be obtained anymore, leading to them being considered as glitch seeds.
    * Seeds of the Carnival-exclusive Card Blocks are never obtainable in the first place.
