The **Roasted Pineapple Block** is an unsplicable solid lava pain foreground block which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2015 on May 30, 2015.
##  Function
Being a lava pain block, a Roasted Pineapple Block knocks the player back and puts the player on fire until the player stops all motion when touched. A player will die after making contact with a lava pain block five times in quick succession (recovering from damage takes roughly 5 seconds), but a player with the _Fireproof_ mod may take up to ten consecutive hits.
