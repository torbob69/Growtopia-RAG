Awarded from the KYDERBY event, which is typically hosted on the first Saturday in May by the moderators.
The **Mint Julep** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2014 on May 2, 2014.
##  Function
Ya'll are feelin' minty, sugah.

Healthy color restored.

When consumed, a Mint Julep grants the _Minty_ mod for 15 minutes, giving the player a green skin color.
Step 16 of the  Quest Of Steel requires the player to deliver 1 of this item. 1 Neptune's Chariot may also be used as an alternative.
### Silkworm Food     _Main article:Guide:Silkworm Care_
This consumable can be fed to a Silkworm to give it a white color, thus allowing it to produce 1–10  Silk Thread - White per harvest. However, due to the Mint Julep's rarity, it isn't recommended to feed the item to a Silkworm.
