The **Incense Urn** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable animated foreground block which was added as part of the Chinese New Year 2019 on February 1, 2019.
##  Function
Being a toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block, the Incense Urn can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, an Incense Urn will change its appearance, showing a 2-framed animation.
