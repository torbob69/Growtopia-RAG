# Steam Spikes

Steam forces razor-sharp spikes from the ground in this dastardly trap! It can only be powered from below. Power it again to lower the spikes.

The **Steam Spikes** is toggleable deadly block that was added on Player Appreciation Week 2016 Day 1.
##  Function
When in contact with steam, _Steam Spikes_ will turn from a non-solid foreground block into a deadly solid foreground block.
