##  Trivia
  * The Whatchamacallit is one of the six machine parts required to create the Legendary Wizard, alongside the Doohickey, Doodad, Thingamabob, Thingamajig, and Kerjigger.
  * In real life, the term whatchamacallit is used to name something whose name is unknown.
  * The seed of a Whatchamacallit can be obtained by harvesting its tree.
