# Celestial Halo

Ripped from the heavens, stolen by demons, and now bequeathed to you. This miraculous crown will make you as light as the aether itself - a fitting gift for one who would stand against the darkness.

Breaking a Demon Crystal

Smash a Demon Crystal using a Celestial Lance after a player was killed by the Demon Crystal. This can only be done while the crystal is active for a short while.

The **Celestial Halo** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of the Strange Crystals update on November 13, 2017.
##  Function
When equipped, the **Celestial Halo** grants the _Slow Fall_ mod, which allows the player to glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button. It will also provide a special respawn effect.
