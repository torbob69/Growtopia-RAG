The **Pineapple Warrior Charm** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2022 on June 10, 2022.
##  Function
Say hello to my pineapple friend!

Lost a friend!

When equipped, the **Pineapple Warrior Charm** grants the _Punch Range & Build Range : Pineapple Warrior Charm_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further and build one tile further.
##  Trivia
  * The warrior summoned by the Pineapple Warrior Charm resembles a stand, a manifestation of one's soul or life energy from _JoJo's Bizarre Adventure_.
