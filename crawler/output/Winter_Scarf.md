The **Winter Scarf** is a splicable chest item which was added as part of the Growtokens! update on March 30, 2014.
##  Function
Equipping the Buckskin Hood, Buckskin Jacket, Buckskin Pants, and Winter Scarf allows the player to create Mega-Pellet Bait from Frozen Chunks.
