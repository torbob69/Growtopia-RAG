The **Protostar Starship Blast** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Star Trek Prodigy on June 27, 2022.
##  Function
When consumed, it will create a protostar starship blasted world where players can do interactive Startopia missions using the same Star Tools. Every mission completed awards the player a Starfleet Cadet's Supply Box they can consume for a random Star Trek prize. After reaching 550,000 star miles (about 20 missions), they reach The Anomaly, a final mission the player has to complete. After the player completes The Anomaly, they receive the Starfleet Officer's Supply Box which drops a random and better rewards when consumed.
