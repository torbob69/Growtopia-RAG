# Boots of Winter

Dash through the snow in these boots without a lace!

The **Boots of Winter** is an unsplicable feet item that was added as part of Equinox Weekend on March 25, 2022.
##  Function
Its freezing shoes!

You've got cold feet!

When equipped, the **Boots of Winter** grants the _Speedy: Boots of Winter_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Equinox Winter set, which consists of the following items:

    * Crown of Winter (hat)

    * Shirt of Winter (shirt)

    * Pants of Winter (pants)

    * Boots of Winter (shoes)
