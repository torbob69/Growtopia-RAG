# Basic Splicing Kit
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Basic Splicing Kit** for **200 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"The basic seeds every farmer needs!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Basic Splicing Kit** is a pack which gives 10 Rock Seeds and ten of any seed with rarity 2.
##  Seeds obtainable
  * Rock Seed ×10
  * Cave Column Seed
  * Door Seed
  * Glass Pane Seed
  * Grass Seed
  * Lava Rock Seed
  * Martian Tree Seed
  * Sign Seed
  * Wood Block Seed

##  Trivia
  * As of Anniversary Week 2017, Mission Block seeds are no longer obtainable from the pack.
