The **Chocolate Bunny** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Easter Week 2018.
##  Function
Your feet spring to life.

Your feet shrink back to normal.

When consumed, it grants the _High Jump_ mod to the player for 30 minutes. However, it will not stack with other similar mods.
##  Trivia
  * Attempting to cook a Chocolate Easter Bilby without equipping Cooking Recipe - Chocolate Easter Bilby will end up creating a Chocolate Bunny instead.
