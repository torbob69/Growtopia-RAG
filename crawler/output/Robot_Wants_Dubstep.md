##  Function
<https://growtopiawiki.com/w/File:Loopport_dubstep.ogg>The Robot Wants Dubstep's music.
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Robot Wants Dubstep can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Robot Wants Dubstep will change its appearance, showing a 2-framed animation, and play a piece of dubstep music.
Step 4 of the  Quest Of Steel requires the player to deliver 600 of this item.

##  Trivia
  * The Robot Wants Dubstep's name and design is based on ex-developer Mike Hommel's earlier game series: Robot Wants Kitty, Robot Wants Puppy, Robot Wants Fishy and Robot Wants Ice-cream.
  * In the game files, the Robot Wants Dubstep's music is named loopport_dubstep.
