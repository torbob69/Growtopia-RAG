The **Lil Growpeep's Baaaa Blaster** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Easter Week 2021 on April 1, 2021.
##  Function
Scope up: Lil Growpeep found their sheep!

Scope down: Lil Growpeep lost their sheep!

When equipped, the **Lil Growpeep's Baaaa Blaster** grants the _Punch Range: Snipeshot_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further.
Step 18 of the  Quest Of The Owl requires the player to deliver 1 of this item.
##  Trivia
  * Lil Growpeep's Baaaa Blaster is based on _Little Bo-Peep_, a popular English nursery rhyme first recorded around 1805.
    * The item's equip and unequip message alludes to "Little Bo-Peep has lost her sheep," which is the first verse of the rhyme.
