The **Heatbow** is a hand item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2016.
##  Function
You fire flaming arrows!

You are disarmed.

When equipped, the **Heatbow** grants the _Flaming Arrows_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further. It will also replace the player's punching animation with flaming arrows.
