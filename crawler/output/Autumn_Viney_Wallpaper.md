The **Autumn Viney Wallpaper** is an unsplicable non-solid background block which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2015 on September 25, 2015.
##  Function
When placed, the Autumn Viney Wallpaper serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * Autumn Viney Wallpaper is one the three blocks added for the blast during Harvest Festival 2015, alongside Autumn Leaf Block and Autumn Viney Block.
