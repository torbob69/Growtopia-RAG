# Store Packs

**Store Packs** are the many kinds of packs in the Growtopia Store that can be purchased. These packs vary from gem packs, to item packs, and to event packs.
##  Gem Packs
The **Gem Packs** in the game can be bought for real money. They grant large amounts of Gems alongside various bonus items depending on the selected pack.
Some seasonal events also include exclusive bonuses when purchasing Gem Packs.
###  **Chest o' Gems**
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Chest o' Gems** for **1.99 USD** in the Growtopia Store.
"Get a chest containing gems and 56 World Locks. Please make sure you have room in your inventory to receive these items."
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Chest o' Gems** pack earns you:
  * Gems ×250,000
  * World Lock ×84

### **Piggy Bank**

The **Piggy Bank** allows players to accumulate up to **1,500,000 Gems** by completing:
  * Life Goals — 65,000 Gems
  * Daily Bonuses — 65,000 Gems
  * Crazy Jim's Daily Quests — 90,000 Gems

Players may purchase the accumulated Gems at any time for **$2.99**.
