# Rotting Fish Guts Club Sandwich

Gah! Expired mayonnaise is the least of your worries. Fetid fish guts flop out between your fingers as you hold this horrific sandwich.

The **Rotting Fish Guts Club Sandwich** is a consumable which was added as part of the Gross-Out update.
##  Function
When consumed, it raises your mutation level, which when maxed out will give a Mutant Kitchen prize.
