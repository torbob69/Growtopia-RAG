The **Aquatic Volcanic Pauldrons** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2023 on May 5, 2023.
##  Function
Ready to erupt!

Pumice!

When equipped, the **Aquatic Volcanic Pauldrons** grants the _Aquatic Volcanic Pauldrons: Fireproof + Punch Damage_ mod, which allows the player to take 50% less damage from lava pain blocks and break blocks faster.
