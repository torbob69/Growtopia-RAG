# Change of Address

If you own two worlds, you can use this handy form to swap their names permanently. See a new name you want that nobody owns? Lock it up, and do a name swap! Requires a World Lock in both worlds. WARNING: Both worlds will be UNTRADEABLE for 5 min after swapping the names.

The **Change of Address** is an unsplicable consumable. One can be purchased from the Store for 20,000 Gems.
##  Function
A Change of Address is used for swapping the names of two worlds. The player who wishes to use this must have both worlds world-locked by themselves.
##  Trivia
  * On January 4, 2023, the Change of Address' functionality was temporarily disabled due to unknown reasons. The item's functionality was later re-enabled on February 8, 2023.
    * This was later fixed on May 16, 2023 with a few changes to the item's functionality:
      * Once the Change of Address Item has been consumed, the system will lock both swapped worlds until the swap gets completed.
      * The worlds will not be accessible while the world swap is in progress.
      * There will be a 5 minute cool down after the worlds has gotten their address changed, where both worlds will be inaccessible.
      * Players can now only use the Change of Address item once every 5 minutes.
