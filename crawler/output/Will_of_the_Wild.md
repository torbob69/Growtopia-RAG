The **Will of the Wild** is an unspliceable hair item which was added as March 2018's Item Of The Month on March 1, 2018.
##  Function
Fire, Water and Earth, sorry no Air!

Back to your boring hair!

When equipped, the **Will of the Wild** grants the _Will of the Wild_ mod, which allows the player to take 50% less damage from lava pain blocks, cling onto Climbing Walls by pressing the jump button, or move faster in water.
When worn, players can change the elements by equipping and unequipping the item, similar to the Diamond Horns.
The following abilities will be given when its respective element is equipped:
  *  Fire — Players will receive the Fireproof mod, which reduces damage taken from lava pain blocks by 50%.
  *  Water — Players will receive the Speedy in Water mod, allowing them to move faster in water.
  *  Earth — Players will receive the Wall Climbing mod, allowing them to cling onto Climbing Walls by holding their jump.

##  Trivia

  * The Golem's Gift matches with the elements of the Will of the Wild.
