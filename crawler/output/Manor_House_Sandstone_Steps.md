Steps to greatness. Carefully crafted to be impossible to climb for those lacking the requisite degree of nobility!

The **Manor House Sandstone Steps** is an unsplicable platform foreground block which was added as part of Spring Clash 2019 on March 1, 2019.
##  Function
A Manor House Sandstone Steps behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath.
