# Shirt of Spring

A perfect shirt to wear while seeing trees of green and roses bloom!

This item can be transmuted.

The **Shirt of Spring** is an unsplicable shirt item that was added as part of Equinox Weekend on March 25, 2022.
##  Function
When equipped, the Shirt of Spring serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Equinox Spring set, which consists of the following items:

    * Crown of Spring (hat)

    * Shirt of Spring (shirt)

    * Pants of Spring (pants)

    * Boots of Spring (shoes)
