The **Kiwi Umbrella** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2026 on June 11, 2026.
##  Function
Kiwi sugar high!

Out of juice!

When equipped, the **Kiwi Umbrella** grants the _High Jump + Slowfall: Kiwi Umbrella_ mod, which allows the player to jump one tile higher and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
##  Trivia
  * The equip message of the **Kiwi Umbrella** is a reference to the song Watermelon Sugar by English singer-songwriter Harry Styles.
