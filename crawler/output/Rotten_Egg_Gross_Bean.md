##  Function
AAARGH! Why does it taste of green?

I survived!

When consumed, a Rotten Egg Gross Bean grants the _Rotten Egg!_ mod for 10 seconds, giving the player a yellow-green skin color. The player will also seemingly hold their breath for the first few seconds.
