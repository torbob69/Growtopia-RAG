The **Space Junk Background** is an unsplicable background block which was added as part of the Startopia 2.0 update on June 1, 2020. Multiple layers of Space Junk Background can be found in Descended Planet worlds after completing the Desolated Waste sector.
##  Function
When placed, the Space Junk Background serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
