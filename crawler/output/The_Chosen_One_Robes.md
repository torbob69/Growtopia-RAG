**The Chosen One Robes** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2023 Day 2 on August 29, 2023.
##  Function
When equipped, The Chosen One Robes serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Chosen One set, which consists of the following items:

    * The Chosen One Hair (Hair)

    * The Chosen One Robes (Shirt)

    * The Chosen One Pants (Pants)
  * **The** **Chosen One Set** is a reference to Anakin Skywalker, one of the main characters in the Star Wars franchise.
