The **Tutorial Update, Piggy Bank, and Rewarded Video Feature** update was added on June 6, 2024.
##  Tutorial Update
Growtopia's Tutorial was updated to add advanced quests, which is a step-by-step quest system shown after completing the tutorial quests that guide the player on the feature. As of the update, advanced quests were added for the Wrench, Worlds Exploration and Creation, as well as Surgery. Additionally, a small notification is added in Crazy Jim's Quest Emporium that alerts the player for new and available quests.

##  Piggy Bank
The **Piggy Bank** is a feature where players accumulate gems in the Piggy Bank by completing Crazy Jim's Daily Quests, Life Goals, and Daily Bonuses. To be specific, completing a Daily Quest fills the Piggy Bank with 20,000 gems, Life Goals fills the Piggy Bank with 15,000 gems, and Daily Bonuses fills the Piggy Bank with 10,000 gems. Players can purchase the accumulated gems for $2.99 at any point, however, the maximum amount of gems the Piggy Bank can hold can be up to 1,500,000 gems.

##  Rewarded Video Feature
The **Rewarded Video Feature** is a feature on mobile devices where players can watch advertisement videos from the Growtopia Store and gain a 1-Day Free Subscription Token for the first six consecutive days, and an exclusive random cosmetic item on the seventh day.
