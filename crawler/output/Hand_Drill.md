# Hand Drill

Bore through the surface of frozen blocks with this hand drill and get fishing! Be careful not to put too much strain on it, though - it may break!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: Full Collision
Hardness: **3 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **1s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Fishin' Pack** for **10,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"Relax and sit by the shore... this pack includes a Fishing Rod, Wiggly Worms for bait, Hand Drill, Nuclear Detonator, and a Rare Tackle Box which provides you with more free bait every two days, Fish Tanks, and a Rare Fish Tank Port to put the fish you catch into your fish tank!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Radioactive Chemical **(×10)** ,
Chemical R **(×10)** and
Chemical B **(×2)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Hand Drill** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Gone Fishin'! update on January 14, 2018.
##  Function
When consumed on a Water Bucket on top of an Ice Crust Block, it will create an ice enviornment that adds unique fishes and items when from fishing on it.
