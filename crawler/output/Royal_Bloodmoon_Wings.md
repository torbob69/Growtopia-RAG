The **Royal Bloodmoon Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as November 2023's Item Of The Season on November 1, 2023.
##  Function
Bleeding edge!

Lost your edge!

When equipped, the **Royal Bloodmoon Wings** grants the _Double Jump + Light Source: Royal Bloodmoon Wings_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and emit light in dark areas.
