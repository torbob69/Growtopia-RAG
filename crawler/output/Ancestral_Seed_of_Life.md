Fantastic powers of verdant might have been combined with the building blocks of creation to forge this seed. Equip it and the bloom of life will bend to your will...

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...

The **Ancestral Seed of Life** is an unsplicable artifact which was added as part of the Hidden Riches update on September 1, 2017.
## Function
Effortless Growth...

Aw, no more extra growth.

When equipped, the Ancestral Seed of Life grants the _Reduced Growtime..._ mod, which reduces the grow time of seeds planted by the player by 2%.

  * Once Ancestral Seed of Life is upgraded, it will become an untradeable item.
