Legendary Quest Reward

Approach the  Legendary Wizard and complete the  Quest Of The Owl.
The **Legendary Owl** is an unsplicable face item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2021 Day 5 on August 27, 2021.
##  Function
Dominate the night!

Your watch is over!

When equipped, the **Legendary Owl** grants the _Double Jump + Speedy: Legendary Owl_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and move faster on land. Additionally, it also summons a Legendary Owl which drop feathers if you move and throws golden feathers instead of the fist when punching.
##  Quest Steps
  1. Deliver 2,000 Clouds Wallpapers.
  2. Kill 100 players in PvP via a Game Generator.
  3. Break 10,000 Blocks.
  4. Deliver 50 Owl Masks.
  5. Plant seeds that add up to 100,000 rarities.
  6. Earn 50 Growtokens.
  7. Deliver 50 Alaskan King Crab Crowns.
  8. Earn 20,000 XP.
  9. Deliver 10 Sun Shooter Bows.
  10. Deliver 100,000 Gems.
  11. Break 200,000 rarity worth of blocks.
  12. Defeat 100 Villains.
  13. Train 10 fishes.
  14. Deliver 1 Golden Silk Scarf or Neptune's Weather Machine - Atlantis.
  15. Collect 200,000 rarity worth of fruit from trees.
  16. Deliver 2 Snow Leopard Tails.
  17. Deliver 3 Ultraviolet Auras.
  18. Deliver 1 Lil Growpeep's Baaaa Blaster.
  19. Deliver 1 Draconic Soul Aura.
  20. Deliver the Legendary Orb.
