For every 1 World Lock given, the player will receive 30 **Golden Tickets**.   |
The **Golden Ticket** is an unsplicable component which was added as part of The Carnival update on October 13, 2014.
##  Function
Golden Tickets are used to play games at the Carnival. Each game consumes one ticket, whether the player ends up winning or not. 10 Golden Tickets are required to start a Ring Quest.
##  Trivia
  * The ability to trade World Locks for Golden Tickets was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2015.
