# Growtopia ADventures

Growtopia Gazette
> June 22nd: Grow ADventures!
> Unleash your creativity! Manage your own ads with Growtopia ADventures, now available on Windows PC! Step up your game to passively earn gems when more Growtopians see your awesome billboards!
> The biggest change you're going to see, are the billboards in the Sunny Weather Machine; nestling in the background like a scene from a Hollywood movie. Real-world Ads will earn you gems for your worlds. Don't worry if you don't see real-world Ads straight away, they will appear as they become available!
> Growtopia Ads which will not generate any gems are displayed for underage players;and to those who have ad disabled in general.
> Find out more about Growtopia ADventures in the Dev Diary here!
**Growtopia ADventures** is an update that was added on June 22, 2021 exclusively for Windows PC users. It introduces real-life ads which are naturally generated in worlds with a sunny background weather, and Ad Blocks, which also provide the same purpose if placed correctly.
Players who view these ads in another player's world will generate up to 30,000 Gems every day for the latter.
##  Items
Item: RecipeAd Control Block: 1 can be obtained from purchasing the item itself for 10 gems in the Growtopia Store.
1 can be obtained from purchasing the Advertisers Pack for 10,000 gems in the Growtopia Store.
Ad Block 3x2: 12 can be obtained from purchasing the Advertisers Pack for 10,000 gems in the Growtopia Store.
Ad Block 2x3: 24 can be obtained from purchasing the Advertisers Pack for 10,000 gems in the Growtopia Store.
Ad Block 6x1: 14 can be obtained from purchasing the Advertisers Pack for 10,000 gems in the Growtopia Store.
##  Trivia
  * The idea was inspired by real-world billboard, which was invented in the 1830s by Jared Bell in America.
  * As of July 16, 2021, the requirement of 8x1 ad blocks was changed to 7x1 to show an AD. This caused the name change, and the reduction of receiving 14 blocks instead of 16 being needed to create 2 ad placements.
