The **CyberCycle** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as September 2024's Item Of The Season on September 1st, 2024.
##  Function
Roll out!

Rolling in the deep!

When equipped, the **CyberCycle** grants the _Double Jump + Speedy: CyberCycle_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air, move faster on land, and float on water for three seconds.
##  Trivia
  * The CyberCycle is the first Item of the Season to occupy a different slot than its Royal version: Royal CyberWings, which is a back item.
