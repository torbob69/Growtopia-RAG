Lock onto your prey and fire! This incredibly deadly shoulder cannon is designed to leave a Greezak warrior's hands free so he can fight and fire at the same time! The plasma bolts it shoots are low-energy, so they don't ruin animal pelts.

**Greezak Horde - Shoulder Cannon** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of the Startopia update.
##  Function
Gree-ak-gree-zak!

kaz-eerg-ka-eerg

When equipped, the **Greezak Horde - Shoulder Cannon** grants the _GREEZAK!_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. Additionally, it gives a cannon punching effect.
##  Trivia
  * The mod given from equipping a Greezak Horde - Shoulder Cannon used to be able to stack with the mods from other items, such as the Pickaxe, allowing the player to break blocks in even fewer hits. This, however, was later changed in version 2.98.
  * Unlike many other Startopia items, this item can't be purchased with Buckazoids from the Sales-Man.
