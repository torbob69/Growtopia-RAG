##  Function
A Water Bucket is used to place water in a world. It slows down any player who walks through it unless they are wearing Swim Fins, Ring Of Water, Mermaid Tail, Megalodon, or Demon Megalodon. If a player is wearing a Sea Monster Floatie, Unicorn Floatie or Water Wings, they will automatically float to the top of the water. If a player is wearing a type of surfboard, it will cause them to "walk" on the Water's surface.
In order to remove a block of Water, a Water Bucket must be used on the Water to remove it, with a small chance of not getting it back. Removing a block of Water with 200 Water Buckets in the inventory will not return a Water Bucket.
A Water Bucket can be used on a Silkworm to quench its thirst.
The  Myths and Legends : The Search for Nessie mission requires 200 of this item.

##  Trivia
  * Worlds that have been undersea-blasted will be automatically filled with Water.
    * Beach-blasted worlds also have Water automatically generated in the world.
  * Using Water Bucket on a Silkworm quenches its thirst.
  * Using a Water Bucket in an area which the player does not own will just splash a few drops of water particles without actually using it up.
  * A Water Bucket Seed can only be obtained as a rare drop from harvesting a Water Bucket Tree or purchasing a Small Seed Pack.
  * Players standing in Water will look like they are holding their breath underwater.
  * You can use Water Buckets to put out Fires.
  * The placed water bucket texture is a recolored version of the default dirt block.
