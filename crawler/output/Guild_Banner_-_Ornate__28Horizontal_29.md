# Guild Banner - Ornate (Horizontal)

Show off your guild colors with this honorable - and ornate - banner. Its colors will adapt to the seeds from your guild mascot!

This item never drops any seeds.

May yield **1** item each.

The **Guild Banner - Ornate (Horizontal)** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added alongside the Guilds update on April 1, 2018.
##  Function
When placed, the Guild Banner - Ornate (Horizontal) displays the player's guild mascot. The colour of the banner will depend on the seeds of the item used for the mascot.
