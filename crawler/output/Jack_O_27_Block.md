The **Jack O' Block** is an unsplicable solid foreground block which was added as part of Halloween Week 2013 on October 28, 2013.
##  Function
When placed, the Jack O' Block serves a fully aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The  Jack O' Block is one of the three pumpkin-based blocks added in Halloween Week 2013, alongside the  Pumpkin Block and  Lit Jack O' Block.
