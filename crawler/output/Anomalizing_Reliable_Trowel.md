The **Anomalizing Reliable Trowel** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Summer Clash Series 1.
##  Function
Anomalizing Reliable Trowels are used to score points during Block Builders. While equipping this item, each block placed down yields 20 points. Reliable anomalizers, such as this item, will not break upon usage.
