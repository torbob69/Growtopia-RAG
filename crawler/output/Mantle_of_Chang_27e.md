The **Mantle of Chang'e** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2023 on September 21, 2023.
##  Function
When equipped, Mantle of Chang'e serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
When equipped along the Kimono of Chang'e, clouds and sparkles will emit from the player during movement.
##  Trivia
  * The **Mantle of Chang'e** is a reference to the Chinese mythology goddess of the moon of the same name's mantle.
  * This item is considered a part of the Chang'e set, which consists of the following items:

    * Mantle of Chang'e (chest)

    * Kimono of Chang'e (shirt)
