**Cheval de Frise (Siege Barrier)** is an unsplicable solid pain foreground block which was added as part of Winter Clash 2018 on November 2, 2018.
##  Function
Being a type of pain block, a Cheval de Frise (Siege Barrier) knocks the player back and stings the player until the player stops all motion. A player will die after making contact with a pain block five times in quick succession (recovering from damage takes roughly 5 seconds). The _Fireproof_ mod will not allow the player to take more hits, unlike with lava pain blocks.
##  Trivia
  * Cheval de Frise is based on a historical defensive obstacle of the same name, which typically features a log covered with wooden spikes or spears.
