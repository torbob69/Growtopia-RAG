# Shamrock Sign

Write stuff on it to tell your friends! Use your Wrench on it to change the message.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item has special properties you can adjust with the Wrench.

Render Type: Single

Hardness: **5 Hits** **4 Hits** – Restores after **8s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**

May yield **1** item each.
The **Shamrock Sign** is an unsplicable sign which was added as part of the St. Patrick's Week 2015 update on March 13, 2015.
##  Function
Being a sign, a Shamrock Sign allows players with access to it to edit its message for other players to view.
