The **Mobile Suit Wings** are an unsplicable back item which were added as part of Valentine's Week 2024 on February 14, 2024.
##  Function
Ignition!

Mayday!

When equipped, the **Mobile Suit Wings** grants the _Double-Jump: Mobile Suit Wings_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
