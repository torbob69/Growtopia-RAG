Participate in successfully smashing an  Ultra Pinata during the **Pinata Bash!** event.
May give **1** item each time.
The **Fiesta Cape** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2024 on May 3, 2024.
##  Function
Party rockin' in the house tonight!

Sorry for party rockin'!

When equipped, the **Fiesta Cape** grants the _Fiesta Cape: Double Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
