This ring floats you on a cloud. Hold the jump button while falling to glide safely down to earth.

The **Ring Of Winds** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of The Carnival update on October 13, 2014.
##  Function
When equipped, the **Ring Of Winds** grants the _Slow Fall_ mod, which allows the player to glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button. When equipped, the Ring of Winds allows the player to glide slowly to the ground when pressing the jump button.
##  Trivia
  * When drifting by the means of the Ring of Winds, the ring will glow in the wearer's hand.
