The **Focused Eyes** is an untradeable face item which was added on October 24, 2013. One was given to InFocus for his contributions as Growtopia's voluntary moderator.
##  Function
Give people the evil eye.

Your vision is normal.

When equipped, the item gives the player the _Focused Eyes_ mod which replaces the player's punch with lightning from the player's eyes. The Focused Eyes does not affect the player's punch damage, power, or range.
##  Trivia
  * This item was made available to the public on March 30 2014, five months after InFocus' demotion.
