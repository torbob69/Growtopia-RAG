The **Datemaster's Rose** is a hand item which was added as a part of the Valentine's Week 2020.
##  Function
Ready for tonights date!

Forever alone!

When equipped, the **Datemaster's Rose** grants the _Punch Damage: Roses_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. Additionally, when punching blocks, rose petals appears and falls slowly; when punching players, larger rose petals appears and falls to the bottom of the world.
