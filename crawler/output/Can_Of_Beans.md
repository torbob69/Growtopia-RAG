**Can of Beans** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2017 Day 1.
##  Function
When consumed, the player will make a fart sound and automatically do the /troll emote.
Consuming a Tournament snack such as this item may give the player a random block typically earned from the lower tiers of the Prize Ladder.
Snack — Price — Chance of PrizePow Stones — 50  Gems — 1%

Overall, the Wasabi Peas is considered the most efficient snack in terms of gems-to-prize ratio.
