# Buckazoid

The currency of the stars! This buckazoid is your reward for successfully completing a mission for the Startopian Empire. You can exchange buckazoids for rare interstellar prizes by calling Sales-Man on your Telephone at 53785! Double-tap this in your inventory to combine 100 of them into a Mega Buckazoid!
Properties
This item cannot be dropped or traded.

The **Buckazoid** is a component item that was added as part of the Startopia Update on July 1, 2018. It can be obtained from successfully completing a Star Voyage.
##  Function
Buckazoids can be used to purchase Startopia related items from the Sales-Man when using a Telephone by dialing 53785.
