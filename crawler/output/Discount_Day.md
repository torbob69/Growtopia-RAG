# Discount Day

Discount Day was a day-long event that happened on Player Appreciation Week 2022 Day 6. During the event most items in the Growtopia Store would be 20% off the regular price and items in the Growtoken Store would be 5% off. The prices of the World Lock and the World Lock Pack were not changed for the day.
During the event there was a chance for double gems to drop from breaking blocks.
