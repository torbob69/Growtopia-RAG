Special Event

Found in the _Hidden In Plain Sight!_ special event.
One has 90 seconds to locate a randomly spawned block and punch it for a prize.
The **Pine Needles** is an unsplicable foreground block which was added as a part of Player Appreciation Week 2025 on September 18, 2025.
##  Function
The Pine Needles behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath.
