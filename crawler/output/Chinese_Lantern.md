# Chinese Lantern

A soothing dim light for evening festivities. You can punch this to turn it on or off.

The **Chinese Lantern** is an unsplicable toggleable animated foreground block which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2013 on September 18, 2013.
##  Function
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Chinese Lantern can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Chinese Lantern will change its appearance, showing the lamp being lit up. As a light source, a Chinese Lantern emits light in dark areas.
##  Trivia
  * Prior to Harvest Festival 2014, players had to combine 1 Songpyeon, 1 Peace Mooncake and 1 Prosperity Mooncake in a Chemical Combiner to obtain a Chinese Lantern.
