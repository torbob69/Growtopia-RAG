# Harvest Festival Clothes

**Harvest Festival Clothes** refers to various clothing items which all have the same recipe; mixing Mooncakes in any Chemical Combiner.
The possible clothes that can drop from the recipe are:
  * Gat
  * Moon Mask
  * Festival Hair Bun
  * Yellow Hanbok Top
  * Purple Hanbok Top
  * Green Hanbok Top
  * Blue Hanbok Top
  * Orange Hanbok Top
  * Yellow Hanbok Pants
  * Purple Hanbok Pants
  * Green Hanbok Pants
  * Blue Hanbok Pants
  * Orange Hanbok Pants
  * Pink Hanbok Skirt
  * Blue Hanbok Skirt
  * Orange Hanbok Skirt
  * Green Hanbok Skirt
  * Yellow Hanbok Skirt
