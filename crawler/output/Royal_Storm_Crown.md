The **Royal Storm Crown** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as October 2025's Item Of The Season on October 1, 2025.
##  Function
Blow 'em Away!

Blown Away!

When equipped, the **Royal Storm Crown** grants the _High Jump + Slow Fall: Royal Storm Crown_ mod, which allows the player to jump one tile higher and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
