The **Soundwave Helm** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as August 2023's Item Of The Season on August 1, 2023.
##  Function
You can't stop the beat!

You stopped the beat!

When equipped, the **Soundwave Helm** grants the _Light Source + Double Jump + Speedy: Soundwave Helm_ mod, which allows the player to emit light in dark areas, jump a second time in mid-air, and move faster on land. Additionally, when the player dances, the music from the Weather Machine - Digital Rain will play.
##  Trivia
  * The player actions affect the amplitude of the soundwaves to varying degrees, which are from smallest to largest: idling or walking, jumping or falling, dancing, punching, and double jumping.
