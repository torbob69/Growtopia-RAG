##  Trivia
  * If the Legen Tree is grown by itself, no "fruits" will be shown on the tree (same with Dary Tree). If harvested, it will drop back the Legen Seed. The Legen block was obtainable for a short period of time and could be obtained by using a Monster Truck on a Clothing Compactor.
  * As part of the Quality of Life Changes update on May 20, 2022, the Legen Seed is no longer placeable in public worlds and worlds the player is an admin in.
