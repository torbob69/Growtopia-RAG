The **Vintage Jukebox** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable animated block that was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2021 on November 18, 2021.
##  Function
<https://growtopiawiki.com/w/File:Bar_music.ogg>The Vintage Jukebox's music.
Being a toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block, a Vintage Jukebox can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Vintage Jukebox will change its appearance, showing a 2-framed animation, and play a piece of music.
