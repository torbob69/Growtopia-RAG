# Cosmic Power - Comet Strike

This is a Cosmic Superpower card for use in fighting crime. You can also place it as a block! Cosmic Cards aren't used, they just have an effect as long as they are in your hand. Card effect: 25% chance of doing 1 extra damage whenever your card counters the opponent's.
