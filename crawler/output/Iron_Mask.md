##  Function
You can't speak through an Iron Mask.

Ahh, sweet oxygen!

When equipped, it grants the _Iron MMMFF!_ mod, which will practically mute the player, muffling the player's speech to _mfmfffm_ s in a similar manner to Duct Tape.
##  Trivia
