# Bountiful Seed Pack

Contains 5 randomly chosen bountiful seeds, including 1 rare bountiful seed! Use it on yourself to open it - who knows what you'll get?!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **5 Hits** **4 Hits** – Restores after **8s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Bountiful Seed Pack** for **1,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"Contains 5 randomly chosen bountiful seeds, including 1 rare bountiful seed! Use it on yourself to open it - who knows what you'll get?!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
**Bountiful Seed Pack** is a consumable that was introduced during the Roots Rebalanced! update.
##  Function
When consumed, it will randomly give the player 5 bountiful seeds including a rare bountiful seed.
