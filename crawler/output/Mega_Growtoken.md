# Mega Growtoken

Now you're talking! This is worth 100 growtokens! Double-tap this in your inventory to break it back down into 100 growtokens. Spend them in the store on special items! Try dialing 12345 or 53785 on your Telephone for details!
Properties
This item cannot be dropped or traded.
This item never drops any seeds.
This item has no use... by itself.

**1h**
Double Tap/Click in Inventory

Made by double-tapping/clicking  Growtoken **(×100)** in one's inventory.
Always creates **1** item each.
The **Mega Growtoken** is an untradeable component released as a part of Player Appreciation Week 2018 Day 3 on August 8, 2018.
##  Function
Similar to a Diamond Lock, the Mega Growtoken makes spending and storing large amount of Growtokens easier.
##  Trivia
  * As of Player Appreciation Week 2023 Day 5, players were enabled to purchase items from the Growtoken Store directly using the Mega Growtokens. (Previously, players had to convert Mega Growtokens into regular Growtokens in order to purchase an item.)
