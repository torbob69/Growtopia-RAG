The **Wicked Wizard Robes** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2022 on October 22, 2022.
##  Function
When equipped, the Wicked Wizard Robes serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Wicked Wizard set, which consists of the following items:

    * Wicked Wizard Hair (hair)

    * Wicked Wizard Scar (face)

    * Wicked Wizard Robes (shirt)

    * Wicked Wizard Pants (pants)

    * Wicked Wizard Shoes (feet)

    * Wizard's Wand (hand)
  * The Wicked Wizard Hair's description is a reference to the fantasy novel Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban written by British author J. K. Rowling. To view the contents of Marauder's Map, one would tap it with one's wand and recite, _"I solemnly swear that I am up to no good."_
