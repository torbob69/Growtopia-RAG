# Globe

Add a little class to your office with a globe! You can also wrench it to check the current top worlds.
Properties
This item has special properties you can adjust with the Wrench.
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.

Render Type: Single

Carnival Prize

Won as prize in **any game** (except for Hall of Mirrors and Waste a Ticket) in the Carnival.
**Globe** is a non-solid block foreground block which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2016 Day 5 on August 9, 2016. It can be obtained in the Carnival as a Mystery Prize.
##  Function
Similarly to the  Scoreboard or typing the /top command, wrenching or punching a Globe will open the World Rankings menu.
