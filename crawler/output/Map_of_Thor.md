##  Function
This map can only be used during **Thursday** (Growtopia Time), using this map in the other day will make the second hint turned into:
In the pale moonlight the shapes on the page begin to swirl and glow, but can't seem to form anything solid. Perhaps the time is not right?

###  First Hint
This map's letters are hard to see in the light of day, and even those you CAN make out seem to change from moment to moment.

#### Solution
Go to any world with Weather Machine - Night.
###  Second Hint
XCVGGZIBZ TJPMNZGA OJ OCZ KVOC JA OCZ CPIO, V GPKDIZ HVMF, V GJAOT AMJIO. JA TJPM AZVON, OCDN HVT WZ OCZ HJNO XNVMMDIB, NZO VN DO DN DI OCZ ADIVG AVMOCDIB

#### Solving
Use Caesar Shift +5 to decode this into:
CHALLENGE YOURSELF TO THE PATH OF THE HUNT, A LUPINE MARK, A LOFTY FRONT. OF YOUR FEATS, THIS MAY BE THE MOST SCARRING, SET AS IT IS IN THE FINAL FARTHING.

#### Solution
Complete WOLFWORLD_4.
###  Prize
Successfully completing the quest has a chance to reward the player with 1  Thor's Chariot.
