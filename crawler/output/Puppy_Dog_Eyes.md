The **Puppy Dog Eyes** is an unsplicable face item that was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2020 Day 7.
##  Function
When equipped, the Puppy Dog Eyes serve a purely cosmetic purpose, giving the player a pair of enlarged eyes looking upwards.
