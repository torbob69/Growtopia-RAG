The **Atomic Shadow Scythe** is a hand item which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2014 on February 10, 2014.
##  Function
You shoot dark lightning!

You are boring again.

When equipped, the **Atomic Shadow Scythe** grants the _Black Lightning_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. It will also fire black lightning particles when punching.
##  Trivia
  * The Atomic Shadow Scythe was the exclusive item of the ex-moderator AtomicShadow. It was released to the public as part of Player Appreciation Week 2018.
