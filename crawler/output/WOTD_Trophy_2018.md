The **WOTD Trophy 2018** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added alongside the Guilds update on April 1, 2018.
##  Function
When placed, the WOTD Trophy 2018 serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
Step 16 of the  Quest For Fire requires the player to deliver 1 of this item. (or the subsequent trophies)
