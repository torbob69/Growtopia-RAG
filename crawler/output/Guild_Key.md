# Guild Key

The key to your kingdom! If you trade this to another player while in your guild world, they will become the new guild leader, and you will be demoted to Co-Leader, so BE CAREFUL!

The **Guild Key** is a component that was added as part of the Guilds update.
##  Function
The Guild Key is used to transfer guilds safely. One is obtained when the player uses their Wrench on their Guild Lock and clicks the _Get Guild Key_ button. When clicking the button, the following message will be displayed:
  * _The key to this lock can be used to transfer leadership of the guild to another guild Member._
  * _Use it like a World Lock Key, but you can only give it to another Member of your guild._
  * _**Warning!** By giving the Guild Key to another Member, you are effectively promoting them to Guild Leader and demoting yourself at the same time!_
  * _Are you sure you want to take the Guild Key from the Guild Lock?_
