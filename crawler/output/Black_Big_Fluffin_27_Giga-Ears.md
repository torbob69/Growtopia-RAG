The **Black Big Fluffin' Giga-Ears** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Black Friday 2023 on November 24, 2023.
##  Function
Hop to the top!

Toppled down!

When equipped, the **Black Big Fluffin' Giga-Ears** grants the _High Jump & Slow Fall: Black Big Fluffin' Giga-Ears!_ mod, which allows the player to jump one tile higher and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
##  Trivia
  * The  Black Big Fluffin' Giga-Ears is the black reskin of the  Big Fluffin' Giga-Ears
