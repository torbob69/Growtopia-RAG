The **Mage's Orb** is a hand Item introduced during Winter Clash 2018. It is a possible reward from completing the CLASHPARKOUR during Winter Clash.
##  Function
One for the mages!

Stupid puny hands!

When equipped, the **Mage's Orb** grants the _Punch Range & Damage: Fire and Wind_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further and break blocks faster. It also replaces the player's fist by switching between throwing fire and wind like sprites when punching.
