The **Ring Of Force** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of The Carnival update on October 13, 2014.
##  Function
Your punches are imbued with force!

If you liked this mod, you should've put a ring on it.

When equipped, the **Ring Of Force** grants the _Ring of Force_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and knock back other players further. It also changes the player's arm's color to transparent red. Additionally, when the player breaks a block, it will explode into pieces.
