The **Cracked Egg Hat** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Easter Week 2022 on April 15, 2022.
##  Function
When equipped, the Cracked Egg Hat serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
When equipped together with the Cracked Egg Onesie, the player will leave a trail of yolk behind when walking.
