The **Burning Pearl Spinner** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Beach Party 2024 on August 24, 2024.
##  Function
Flame on!

Flame off!

When equipped, the **Burning Pearl Spinner** grants the _Fireproof + Punch Range: Burning Pearl Spinner_ mod, which allows the player to take 50% less damage from lava pain blocks and punch one tile further. It will also shoot consecutive blue flame spikes and when punching a block or player, the block or player will show continuous flow of blue flames for a few seconds.
