The **Sandy Wrench Decoration** is a wrench decoration which was added as part of the July Season on July 1, 2026.
##  Function
When equipped through the 'Wrench Customization' tab when wrenching oneself, the Sandy Wrench Decoration adds sandcastles below the player's wrench connected to their name.
