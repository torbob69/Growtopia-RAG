# Wood Chips

Somethin' got chopped, and here's the result! Nice work!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **3 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **4s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Chopping

Punch 10 Weeping Willows or Sequoia Trees dropped on a Chopping Wood Block with a Headsman's Axe or Lumber Axe equipped. This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Wood Chips** is an unsplicable consumable that was added as part of Harvest Festival 2019 on September 12, 2019.
