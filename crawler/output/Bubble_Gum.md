The **Bubble Gum** is a splicable consumable which was added on Player Appreciation Week 2014 Day 6 on August 13, 2014.
##  Function
Whoooooooaaaaaaaa...

Gravity - it's the law.

When consumed on any player, the player will have the _Floating!_ mod for 3 seconds, which makes their character float in the air. A bubble will start inflating on the player's face, as the mod disappears, the bubble will splat on the character's face.

### Silkworm Food     _Main article:Guide:Silkworm Care_
This consumable can be fed to a Silkworm to give it a purple color, thus allowing it to produce 1–10  Silk Thread - Purple per harvest.
