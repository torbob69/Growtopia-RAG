The **Tamed Looming Triffid** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the Stuff! Update on October 1, 2018.
##  Function
Good boy, Triffid!

Back to your nest!

When equipped, the **Tamed Looming Triffid** grants the _Punch Range: Tentacle Attack!_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further and pull other players towards them upon punching them. It will also summon a Looming Triffid pet which will follow the player around.
##  Trivia
  * Tamed Looming Triffid also provides Punch Pull, although it is not stated in its label.
