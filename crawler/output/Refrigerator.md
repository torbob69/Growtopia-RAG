The **Refrigerator** is a splicable chest foreground block.
##  Function
Being a chest, a Refrigerator can be punched to be opened or closed. When opened, the chest will act as a non-solid block, and items can be dropped inside in order for them to be stored; when closed, nobody can see or access the items, and the chest will become a solid block.
