The **Destroyed Balloon-O-Matic** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of the Balloon Warz update.
##  Function
After the Balloon-O-Matic explodes, the Destroyed Balloon-O-Matic serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * There is no legitimate way of obtaining the Destroyed Balloon-O-Matic. However, it is possible to obtain it in the player's backpack by consuming a Flashback Flan to the item.
