This super-fabulous device will crush any clothing and extract its pure essence. Just tap clothing items on here and they will be crushed! You might get back seeds, gems, or who knows what?

"Got too many clothes? Why give them to the poor when you can crush them instead?! This whizbang crackerjack gizmo can squash almost any clothing and extract whatever juice might lurk inside."
**Clothing Compactor** is an unsplicable clothes compactor introduced along with Locke The Salesman.
##  Function
Players can turn any splicable clothes (except Night Of The Comet-related clothes) into either gems, clothing seeds, the parent seeds, Garbage or the following rare drops by tapping a Clothing Compactor while selecting the particular clothes.
  * Patchwork Quilt
  * Blacksmith's Apron
  * Tiny Red Skirt
  * Plate Greaves
  * Mystery Dress (when compacting Fashionable Dresses only)
  * Much-Too-Small Shirts
  * Stripy Jumpsuit
  * Anubis Boots
  * Kiss the Cook Apron
  * Silk Face Scarf (only available during Grow4Good Week after 25,000,000 global points have been accumulated)

##  Trivia
  * Items above rarity 200 cannot be compacted, however, items spliced with a high rarity seed are more likely to return the seed used when splicing.
  * As of March 24, 2016, only players with access to a Clothing Compactor can use it.
  * As of March 2017, items spliced with a Starseed or a Galactic Starseed cannot be compacted due to abuse of players compacting these items for a high chance to give back the seed.
