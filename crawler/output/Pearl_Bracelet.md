The **Pearl Bracelet** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Beach Party 2022 on June 24, 2022.
##  Function
I've got the pearl power!

Broken Pearl.

When equipped, the **Pearl Bracelet** grants the _Punch Range: Pearl Bracelet_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further.
