**The 'Other' Royal Throne** is an unsplicable non-solid SFX seat foreground block which was added as part of Winter Clash 2018 on November 2, 2018.
##  Function
When a player stands in front of a seat such as the The 'Other' Royal Throne for a few seconds, the player will automatically be seen "sitting" on it.
