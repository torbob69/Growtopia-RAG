# Growmoji Songpyeon

Celebrate the harvest in your chat as well as your worlds! Just consume this to add it to your available growmojis!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **5 Hits** **4 Hits** – Restores after **8s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Harvesting

Harvest **any tree** using either a  Harvester or a  Harvester Of Sorrows.__
The Growmoji Songpyeon is a consumable that was released as part of Harvest Festival 2018. Once consumed, it will allow the player to use the (song) growmoji.
