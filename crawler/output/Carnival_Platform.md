Carnival Prize

Won as a common prize in **Concentration (Right)** in the Carnival.
The **Carnival Platform** is an unsplicable platform foreground block which was added as part of The Carnival update on October 13, 2014.
##  Function
A Carnival Platform behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath.
