The **g-Virus** is an unsplicable consumable which was added alongside the Zombie Defense Pack on May 17, 2013.
##  Function
You've been infected by the g-Virus. Punch others to infect them, too! Braiiiins...

You've been cured.

When consumed on a player, the g-Virus grants the player the _Infected!_ mod, which transforms the player into a zombie for 5 minutes. This direct infection ignores the immunity granted by an Antidote or the Zombie Jammer. When another player gets punched by the zombie, the former also becomes a zombie themselves.
When an infected player chats, their message appears in dark green, with "Zombie speak" added on occasion, such as _"BRAINS!"_ or _"Brraaiinnss..."_. The zombie speak originally extended vowels and alternated capitalization randomly, but that was cut so chatting as a zombie would be less annoying to players.
##  Trivia
  * The item was added as a prize for the winner of an event hosted by the developers in the GROWTECHPHARMA world on May 17, 2013. A player named Viking later won the g-Virus on that day. Before May 18, the item was unusable and had the name "_Mysterious Liquid_ " (instead of g-Virus). The event's storyline suggests that the zombie virus outbreak began with the stolen g-Virus.
    * Prior to May 17, the Mysterious Liquid was named "_Stop snooping!_ ".
  * Consuming a Homeopathic Medicine on a player also has a chance to turn them into a Zombie.
  * When a Weather Machine - Apocalypse is placed in a world, a random player would turn into a Zombie every hour, who could be killed with a gun, although Zombie Brain would not be awarded unless the g-Virus pandemic is in progress.
  * Breaking an Infected Block infects the player with the **G-Virus**.
  * When infecting a human, there is a chance to obtain a Vile Vial - Moldy Guts.
  * Only zombies can go through an Infected Door, while other players will be refused entry.
  * Zombies cannot enter a Barricaded Door. When attempted, the player will receive a message saying "_Zombies can't get through this door!_ "
  * Zombies always wear Worthless Rags.
  * Only zombies can view the message on a Zombie Sign.
  * As of Player Appreciation Week 2023, g-Virus now lasts 5 minutes, previously 1 minute and 30 seconds.
