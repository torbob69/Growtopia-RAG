The **Nightmare Magnifying Glass** is an unspliceable hand item which was was added as part of Halloween Week 2014 on October 30, 2014.
##  Function
You have a pet that spits fire.

Your pet has left you.

When equipped, a virus pet appears and follows the player around. The Nightmare Magnifying Glass grants the _Fiery Pet_ mod, which replaces the player's punch with fireballs coming out of the pet while turning the pet's eyes red.
