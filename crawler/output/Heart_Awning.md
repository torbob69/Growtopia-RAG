The **Heart Awning** is an unsplicable platform trampoline foreground block which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2017 on February 10, 2017.
##  Function
A Heart Awning behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath. Moreover, it also counts as a trampoline block. Jumping on one will cause the player to bounce, and by holding the jump button, the player can jump even higher. The _High Jump_ mod also boosts one's jump height on a trampoline block.
