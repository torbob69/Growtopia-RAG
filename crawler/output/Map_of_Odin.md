**Map of Odin** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the Treasure Hunt update.
##  Function
This map can only be used during **Wednesday** (Growtopia Time), using this map in the other day will make the second hint turned into:
In the pale moonlight the shapes on the page begin to swirl and glow, but can't seem to form anything solid. Perhaps the time is not right?

###  First Hint
This map's letters are hard to see in the light of day, and even those you CAN make out seem to change from moment to moment.

#### Solution
Go to any world with Weather Machine - Night.
###  Second Hint
PDA CKZO PDAIOAHRAO XKS PDAEN DAWZO, PK PDA PNQA SAWRANO KB WHH SKNHZO' PDNAWZO. PWGA BNKI PDAI UKQN FQOP NASWNZO, PDAOA BAWNOKIA BKAO, KQN EIIKNPWH HKNZO

#### Solving
Use Caesar Shift +22 to decode this into:
THE GODS THEMSELVES BOW THEIR HEADS, TO THE TRUE WEAVERS OF ALL WORLDS' THREADS. TAKE FROM THEM YOUR REWARDS, THESE FEARSOME FOES, OUR IMMORTAL LORDS.

#### Solution
Defeat Almighty Seth or Devil Ham in crimefighting.
###  Prize
Successfully completing the quest has a chance to reward the player with 1  Huginn and Muninn - Odin's Ravens.
