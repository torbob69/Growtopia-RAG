Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Red Bicycle** for **30,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"It's the environmentally friendly way to get around! Ride this bicycle at high speed hither and zither throughout Growtopia. Not available any other way!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Red Bicycle** is an unsplicable feet item which was added on 14 August 2015, the first day of Player Appreciation Week 2015.
##  Function
You can move faster!

You are slow again.

When equipped, the **Red Bicycle** grants the _Speedy_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.
The  Myths and Legends : The Search for Nessie mission requires 10 of this item.
