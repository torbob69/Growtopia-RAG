# Mailbox (block category)

A **mailbox** is a type of foreground block that can contain messages left by visiting players in the forms of Letters. Anyone who has access to the mailbox can view these messages as well as the players' names. Any player, with or without access, can also punch the mailbox to see how many letters have been left.
A mailbox may contain up to 20 Letters.
