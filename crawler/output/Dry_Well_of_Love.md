The **Dry Well of Love** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block that was added as part of Valentine's Week 2021 on February 13, 2021. One can be created in the place of a Well of Love after filling it with 200 Golden Booty Chests.
##  Function
When placed, the Dry Well of Love serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
