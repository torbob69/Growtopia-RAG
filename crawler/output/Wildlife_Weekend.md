# Wildlife Weekend

Growtopia Gazette
> March 10th: Wildlife Weekend
> We are proud to present you Wildlife Weekend, a 3-day event running from the 10th of March till the 12th of March in cooperation with Green Game Jam 2023! Growtopians, let's start #Playing4thePlanet and together, learn, share, and act on protecting our environment! Your mission is to reach and exceed personal milestones by planting Eco seeds and growing Eco trees! You will be in for exciting surprises all while making a difference for the world!
> The Wildlife Supporter Pack is available throughout the event where $2 from each pack will be given to Ecologi! Take this opportunity to help create impactful climate solutions for our wildlife, our people, and our planet!
> Please make sure to read Morse's Dev Diary to find out more.
**Wildlife Weekend** is an update that was added on March 10, 2023. For three days, Eco Seeds are purchasable in the Growtopia Store for 10,000 Gems, and have a chance to be obtained from certain in-game activities.
Players can plant Eco Seeds to earn points. When these seeds are fully grown into tree's, a player can collect their bountiful harvest of Wildlife related blocks, items and cosmetics. Upon planting 20 Eco Seeds, the player will be able to claim a Golden Eco Seed, which will yield premium rewards when fully grown.
