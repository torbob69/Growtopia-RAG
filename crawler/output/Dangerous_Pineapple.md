The **Dangerous Pineapple** is a splicable consumable which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2019 on June 7, 2019.
##  Function
Tragic Magic floods your system!

The tragedy has come to an end!

When fed to another player with a Pineapple Body while the feeder is one themself, the other player will be transformed into a Dangerous Pineapple Body. Doing so will result in the feeder being granted the _Pineapple Tragic_ mod. Otherwise, this item functions as a normal Pineapple.

### Dangerous Pineapple Body
OUCH! Tragedy befalls those who try to feed the Dangerous Pineapple.

Attempting to feed any kind of pineapple to a player who has a Dangerous Pineapple Body will cause the feeder to explode themself.
