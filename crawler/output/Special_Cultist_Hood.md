The **Special Cultist Hood** is an unsplicable hair item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2014 on October 30, 2014.
##  Function
You are slow and creepy.

You are back to normal!

The Special Cultist Hood is only used by a staff disguised as a Mysterious Cultist during his special event that usually occurs several days before Halloween Week.
##  Trivia
  * This item shares the same sprite as the Cultist Hood.
  * This item is considered a part of the Mysterious Cultist set, which consists of the following items:

    * Special Cultist Hood (hair)

    * Cultist Robe (shirt)
