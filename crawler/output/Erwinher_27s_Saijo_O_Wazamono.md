##  Function

Let go of all your hopes and dreams!

When equipped, the **erwinher's Saijo O Wazamono** grants the _Punch Range : erwinher's Saijo O Wazamono!_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further.
##  Trivia
  * This item is sponsored by erwinher for completing all the Epic Quests.
  * This item's appearance is based on Enma, a katana from One Piece anime.
  * Since erwinher's Saijo O Wazamono starts with a lower case letter, it is placed at the end of the player's inventory instead of being placed in alphabetical order like other items.
