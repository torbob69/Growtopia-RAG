The **Teeny Angel Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2015.
##  Function
You can jump in mid-air! It's like flying, but less.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Teeny Angel Wings** grants the _Double Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
