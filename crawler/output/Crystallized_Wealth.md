# Crystallized Wealth

A pure piece of prosperity! The solidified richness surrounding it could certainly be used for something spectacular... or set on a shelf to look pretty!

Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Sorcerer Stone **(×200)** ,
Inferno Gate **(×4)** and
Fire Essence **(×5)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Crystallized Wealth** is an unsplicable solid foreground block which was added as part of the Hidden Riches update.
##  Function
When placed, the Crystallized Wealth serves a fully aesthetic purpose.

##  Trivia
  * There are currently five blocks in the crystallized series, all of which are primarily used to create and upgrade ancestral artifacts.
    * Crystallized Brilliance →  Ancestral Totem of Wisdom
    * Crystallized Nature →  Ancestral Seed of Life
    * Crystallized Reality →  Ancestral Tesseract of Dimensions
    * Crystallized Time →  Ancestral Orb of Time
    * Crystallized Wealth →  Ancestral Lens of Riches
  * The  Crystallized Wealth's sprite features an  Ice Cube with a  Gem inside.
