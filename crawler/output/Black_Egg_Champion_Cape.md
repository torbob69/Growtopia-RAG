The **Black Egg Champion Cape** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Black Friday 2020 on November 27, 2020.
##  Function
Flutter through the sky with a smile... only darker!

Please don't run away!

When equipped, the **Black Egg Champion Cape** grants the _Double Jump: Black Egg Champion Cape_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
