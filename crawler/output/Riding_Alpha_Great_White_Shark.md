The **Riding Alpha Great White Shark** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of the Gone Fishin'! update. One can be obtained from completing the level 10 training of an Alpha Great White Shark.
##  Function
When equipped, the **Riding Alpha Great White Shark** grants the _Speedy in Water_ mod, which allows the player to move faster in water.
