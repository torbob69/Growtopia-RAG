The **Forge** is a splicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of the Daily Challenges update on November 21st, 2015.
##  Function
The Forge is used to forge items on an Anvil placed directly next to itself. Nuclear Fuel must first be added to the Forge in order to activate it. Each fuel usage will increase its temperature by 100 degrees. When it has reached at least 1,000 degrees, items can then be forged on the Anvil.
Forging an item will drain the Forge's temperature down to zero. The Forge may also lose heat every second.
