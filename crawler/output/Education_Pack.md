Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Education Pack** for **5,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"If you want to build a school in Growtopia, here's what you need! You'll get 10 Chalkboards, 3 School Desks, 20 Red Bricks, a Bulletin Board, 10 Pencils, a Growtopia Lunchbox (Rare), a Grey Hair Bun, an Apple and a random School Uniform clothing."
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Education Pack** is an item pack added in April 13th, 2013
##  Items
The pack contains the following items:
  * Grey Hair Bun x1
  * School Desk x3
  * Chalkboard x10
  * Red Bricks x20
  * Bulletin Board x1
  * Apple x1
  * Pencil x10
  * Growtopia Lunchbox (rare) x1
  * 1 random School Uniform clothing.

##  Another Items added with pack
  * Crimson Eagle Wings
