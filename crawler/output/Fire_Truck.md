Purchase the **Fire Truck** for **50,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"Race to the scene of the fire in this speedy vehicle! Not available in any other way!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Fire Truck** is an unsplicable feet item which was added alongside the Firefighter Pack on October 8, 2015.
##  Function
You can move faster!

You are slow again.

When equipped, the **Fire Truck** grants the _Speedy_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land. It plays a short siren sound once while the lights at the front of the truck blink blue and red alternately, emitting smokes at the back.
##  Trivia
  * Prior to the Monster Truck's addition, the Fire Truck was the only item in Growtopia to have the word "_truck_ " in its name.
  * The Fire Truck is one of the two clothes that will play a siren sound when the player equips the item, the second item is Ambulance.
