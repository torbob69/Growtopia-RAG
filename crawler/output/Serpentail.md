The **Serpentail** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Wonder Week 2024 Day 7 on October 20, 2024.
##  Function
Trainin' to be a snake!

I have had it with snakes!

When equipped, the **Serpentail** grants the _Speedy + Speedy in Water: Serpentail_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and move faster in water.
##  Trivia
  * The  Serpentail is a reference to a mythological creature known as Shahmaran. Rooted in Middle Eastern mythology, this creature is described as a wise and benevolent queen of snakes, often portrayed with the upper body of a woman and the lower body of a serpent. Shahmaran is associated with healing, wisdom, and the secrets of the natural world.
