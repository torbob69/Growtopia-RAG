# Blarney Bonanza
During St. Patrick's Week

|
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Blarney Bonanza** for **250,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"Welcome to the Blarney Bonanza! As the community completes Blarney Worlds and kiss the Blarney Stone, items will unlock for your to pick up in the store.  You can purchase the Blarney Bonanza to receive one of nine EXCLUSIVE items and a Blarney Coin. If you don't get the item you're after, you can collect 10 coins to convert them into a specific item in a Laboratory!  This will only remain unlocked for a short amount of time, so make sure you come back often! Blarney Bonanza can be unlocked multiple times throughout the week."
Every purchase will give **1** item.
**Blarney Bonanza** is an addition which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2022 on March 11, 2022.
The following items are featured in Blarney Bonanza:
Item: YearEye of Balor: St. Patrick's Week 2022 & St. Patrick's Week 2023
Cauldron of the Dagda
Harp of Aibell
Mallet of Sucelles
The Oillipheist: St. Patrick's Week 2023
Shamrock Staff
Banshee's Hair
Werewolf Hood of Ossory
Phantom Queen's Girdle: St. Patrick's Week 2025
Feathers of Fate: St. Patrick's Week 2026
As of St. Patrick's Week 2025, every purchase of the **Blarney Bonanza** guarantees a  Blarney Coin.
