The **Reception Desk** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as a part of Player Appreciation Week 2024 Day 1 on August 26, 2024.
##  Function
A Reception Desk allows the owner of the hospital world to view information about their hospital. The desk shows the level and ratings of the hospital while allowing the owner to:
  * View their Hospital Stats.
  * Level up their hospital.
  * Add other players as doctors in their hospital.

Reception Desks can be upgraded to higher levels, relevant information about this upgrading process can be found in the table below:

### Formula
The following formula is used to calculate the cured patients requirement:
`**P = 30*L^2 - 30*L - 40**`
Given that:
  * _L_ is the level of the Reception Desk.
  * _P_ is the amount of cured patients required to reach level L.

##  Trivia
  * Punching a Reception Desk pops up this message: "WARNING! Breaking the Reception Desk will reset all Hospital Stats!"
  * Attempting to break the World Lock in a world with Reception Desk pops up this message: "Can't smash the World Lock while a Reception Desk exists in the world!"
