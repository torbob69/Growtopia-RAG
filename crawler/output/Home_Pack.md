# Home Pack

Growtopia Gazette
> Feb 26th: Today we introduce The Home Pack! Congrats to JonasB for guessing it first on our Facebook page. -@Seth and @Hamumu
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Home Pack** for **5,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"Welcome home to Growtopia! Decorate with a Television, 2 Window Curtains, 4 Couch, a Rare Wall Clock that actually tells time, and a Microwave to cook in. Then dress up in a Meaty Apron and Ducky Pajamas to sit down and eat Eggs Benedict, which increases the amount of XP you earn!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Home Pack** is an item pack which was added as on February 26, 2014.
##  Items
The pack contains the following items:
  * Wall Clock x1 (Rare)
  * Television x1
  * Window Curtains x2
  * Couch x4
  * Microwave x1
  * Meaty Apron x1
  * Ducky Pajama Top x1
  * Ducky Pajama Pants x1
  * Eggs Benedict x1

##  Trivia
  * The Black-Rimmed Glasses and the Donation Box were also implemented into the game alongside this pack.
