# Harvester Of Sorrows

This demonic harvesting machine will mangle trees as it runs them over. If you wear a Fuel Pack, it has a 10% chance of harvesting double fruit from each tree below rarity 100! This burns up one Fuel Pack.

The **Harvester Of Sorrows** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2014 on October 30, 2014.
##  Function
Tractors are not speedy.

You can walk faster than that thing!

When equipped, the **Harvester Of Sorrows** grants the _Putt Putt Putt_ mod, which allows the player to move slower, but allows the player to harvest trees by walking through them. Additionally, it will also give a 10% chance to double the fruits earned from a harvested tree while a Fuel Pack is equipped. The Fuel Pack will only be consumed when the bonus is activated. This effect does not apply to trees of rarity 100 or above. When the Fuel Pack gets consumed, there will be an additional ghost particle effect that cannot be found in other types of harvesters.
The only other difference between the Harvester Of Sorrows and its regular counterpart is that the latter does not work in conjunction with Ecto Packs. Do note that Ecto Pack does not double fruit drops, but only give certain Halloween-themed bonus drops, as seen below.
