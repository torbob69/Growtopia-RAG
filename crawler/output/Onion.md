The **Onion** is a splicable consumable which was added as part of the Cooking update on April 1, 2017.
##  Function
You really really smell.

The air clears.

When consumed, this item grants the player the _Stinky_ mod for 5 minutes, which causes stink lines to come out from the player and make them have the ":P" face. Moreover, Onions are cooking ingredients.
