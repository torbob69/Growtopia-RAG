"You look like your soul is dark enough... this is just for you! Careful you don't really abduct souls though. Ah, who am I to tell you what to do? Just play safe ok!"
**Samille's Soul Abductor** is an unsplicable artifact which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2021 Day 6 on August 28, 2021.
##  Function
Jump into souls!

Jump out of souls!

When equipped, the **Samille's Soul Abductor** grants the _Double Jump : Samille's Soul Abductor_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
