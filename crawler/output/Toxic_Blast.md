# Toxic Blast

Shoots a toxic blast that deals a good amount of damage from a distance, also slowly poisoning the enemy for a short period.

The Toxic Blast is a dungeon ability that was added in dungeons update on October 23rd, 2025.
##  Function
When receiving Toxic Blast ability in dungeons, the player's attack effect shoots green small fireball that damages enemies in a distance. Additionally, enemies being hit by Toxic Blast changed their color to green, slowly decreasing their health.
