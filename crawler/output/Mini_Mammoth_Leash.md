The **Mini Mammoth Leash** is a splicable pet hand item which was added as part of the Prehistoric Pack update on December 13, 2013.
##  Function
When equipped, a mammoth pet appears and follows the player around. The Mini Mammoth Leash serves a purely cosmetic purpose.

##  Trivia
  * The Mini Mammoth Leash is the first pet to be obtained from splicing seeds, the second one being the Ice Calf Leash. Both are currently the only pets which can be made by splicing seeds.
  * This item boasts the highest rarity number in the game (excluding items without rarity, which technically have a rarity of 999).
  * Since its rarity is over 200, the Mini Mammoth Leash cannot be compacted in a Clothing Compactor.
