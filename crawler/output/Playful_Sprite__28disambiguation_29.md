# Playful Sprite (disambiguation)

This is a **disambiguation** page.
There are more than one subjects in the **Growtopia Wiki** using this name.
**Playful Sprite** s were added with Player Appreciation Week 2017. They exist in 4 different variants: wood, fire, water and wind.
Playful Wood Sprite
Playful Fire Sprite
Playful Water Sprite
Playful Wind Sprite

##  Trivia
  * A random Playful Sprite will appear during the Playful Sprite special event.
  * Players have 15 seconds to find the sprite dropped in the world.
  * All Playful Sprites have a Pet Battle ability.
