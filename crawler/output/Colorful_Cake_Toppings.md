The **Colorful Cake Toppings** is an unsplicable solid foreground block that was added as part of the Voucher Dayz on November 14, 2024.
##  Function
The item has four different textures and when placed, a random one will appear based on the block's location. The four textures are:
  * A green apple textured block.
  * A blue blueberry textured block.
  * A yellow banana textured block.
  * A red strawberry textured block.
