Contains an assortment of tools and supplies use in the Startopia system! Place it in your world and break it to open - who knows what you'll get?!

This item does not have any recipe because it is no longer, or was never obtainable.
The **Voucher Dayz Startopia Crate** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block that was supposed to be added as a part of Voucher Dayz on May 21, 2021.
##  Function
When broken, the Voucher Dayz Startopia Supply Crate will drop:
  * 25 Star Fuel:
  * Star Fuel
  * 5 of each Startopia tool:
  * AI Brain
  * Cyborg Diplomat
  * Galactibolt
  * Gigablaster
  * Growton Torpedo
  * HyperShields
  * Quadriscanner
  * Space Meds
  * Star Supplies
  * Stellar Documents
  * Tactical Drone
  * Teleporter Charge

##  Trivia
  * This item is unobtainable and was never released.
  * The Voucher Dayz Startopia Supply Crate was supposed to cost 10 Vouchers for 6 Voucher Dayz Startopia Supply.
