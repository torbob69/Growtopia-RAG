The **Beach Party Training Ticket** is an unsplicable component which was added as part of Beach Party 2023 on June 16, 2023.
##  Function
During a Beach Party weekend, a player who wishes to join the BEACHPARTYGAME mini-game may punch  The Bouncer. Should the player have at least one Beach Party Training Ticket in their inventory, they will instantaneously join the queue and be teleported into a waiting room. A single Beach Party Ticket will be removed from the player once the next round starts, which will happen 30 seconds after at least ten players have joined the queue.
During the game, participants are given a time limit of two minutes to break  Clam Chests hidden inside the  sand, while dodging any appearing  spikes and bees. Breaking a Clam Chest will award  1-10 pearls to the player.
