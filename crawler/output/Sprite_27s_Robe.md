# Sprite's Robe

Don this beautiful robe to summon the power of a playful sprite! Every jump will transform you into a Sprite for a time and take you bounding above the world!

The **Sprite's Robe** is a shirt item which was added in the Treasure Hunt Update as part of Winter Clash 2018. One has a chance to be obtained as a prize from the FINALEWORLD.
##  Function
You've been enchanted!

The enchantment has worn off!

When equipped, the **Sprite's Robe** grants the _Jump: Enchantment Mod_ mod, which allows the player to jump one tile higher, jump a second time in mid-air, or decrease the player's jump height.
Decided by the color of the sprite, player will be given either:
→ High Jump mod (Yellow)
→ Double Jump mod (Blue)
→ Low Jump mod (Green)
