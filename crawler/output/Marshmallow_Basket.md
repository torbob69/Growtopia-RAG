# Marshmallow Basket

These marshmallow treats are really pretty gross, but having one follow you around is fun!

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Easter Egg - Rainbow **(×1)** ,
Easter Egg - Jagged **(×1)** and
Easter Egg - Polka Dots **(×1)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Marshmallow Basket** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Easter Week 2015.
##  Function
Your pet is highly aggressive!

Punch for yourself.

When equipped, the **Marshmallow Basket** grants the _Marshmallow Strike_ mod, which allows the player to knock back other players further. Additionally, it will emit a squeak sound effect every time the player punches.
