The **Floatie Crown** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of SummerFest 2020 on July 3, 2020.
##  Function
When a player punches while wearing a Floatie Crown, a sound effect similar to that of Rubber Ducky will be played. The Floatie Crown serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
