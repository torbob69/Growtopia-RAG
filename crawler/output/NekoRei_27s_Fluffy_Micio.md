This item was given to **NekoRei** (alongside NekoRei's Fluffy Tail) for their contributions as part of the Ubisoft Abu Dhabi team.
**NekoRei's Fluffy Micio** is a hair item which was added as part of the Block Party 2 update on April 1, 2019.
##  Function
When equipped, a pet based on @NekoRei's real-life cat appears and follows its owner around. It is currently unknown whether the item grants a mod or serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the NekoRei's Fluffy set, which consists of the following items:

    * NekoRei's Fluffy Micio (hair)

    * NekoRei's Fluffy Tail (back)
