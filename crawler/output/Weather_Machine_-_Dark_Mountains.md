The **Weather Machine - Dark Mountains** is an unsplicable non-solid weather machine foreground block which was added as part of Halloween Week 2022 on October 22, 2022.
##  Function
As a weather machine, this item can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, it will change its appearance, transforming the world's background weather into a mountainous scenery during a red moon.
