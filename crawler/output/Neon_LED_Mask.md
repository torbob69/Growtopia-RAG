The **Neon LED Mask** is an unsplicable face item which was added as part of Black Friday 2024 on November 28, 2024.
##  Function
Don't stop me now!

Not having a good time!

When equipped, the **Neon LED Mask** grants the _Build Range + Light Source: Neon LED Mask_ mod, which allows the player to build one tile further and emit light in dark areas.
