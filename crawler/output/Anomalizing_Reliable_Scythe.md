The **Anomalizing Reliable Scythe** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Summer Clash Series 1.
##  Function
Anomalizing Reliable Scythes are used to score points during Harvest Heroes. While equipping this item, each tree harvested yields 20 points. Reliable anomalizers, such as this item, will not break upon usage.
