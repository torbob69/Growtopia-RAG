The **Hooves of Cernunnos** is a feet item that was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2021 on March 11, 2021.
##  Function
Rootin' and a tootin!

Root, I'm outta here!!

When equipped, the **Hooves of Cernunnos** grants the _Speedy & Build Range: Hooves of Cernunnos!_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and build one tile further. When walking on blocks, it will show a root crawling animation on the block. Additionally, it allows you to wrench things one tile farther as well.
The  Cinco De Mayo Exclusive Mission - So you really are an artist! mission requires 1 of this item.
##  Trivia
  * The Hooves of Cernunnos is based on a god of the same name from the Celtic mythology, who is commonly depicted as a horned being with hooves.
  * As of St. Patrick's Week 2022, the Hooves of Cernunnos was removed from the Store and instead became a prize from completing BLARNEY.
