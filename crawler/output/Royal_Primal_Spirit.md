The **Royal Primal Spirit** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as July 2024's Item Of The Season on July 1st, 2024.
##  Function
Long live the King!

Remember who you are!

When equipped, the **Royal Primal Spirit** grants the _Build Range + High Jump: Royal Primal Spirit_ mod, which allows the player to build one tile further and jump one tile higher.
