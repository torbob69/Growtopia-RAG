Switch your patent-pending MAGPLANT 5000 to plant mode, and this handy remote will place any blocks or seeds it contains wherever you target! Wow! It's not a miracle - it's SCIENCE! (TM)

##  Function
Upon wrenching the MAGPLANT 5000, players can retrieve this remote which allows them to place blocks or plant seeds with a limit of 5000 rather than 200.
