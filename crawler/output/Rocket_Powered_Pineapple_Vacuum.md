The **Rocket Powered Pineapple Vacuum** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2021 on June 3, 2021.
##  Function
Rocket powered cleaning!

Too mean, too clean!

When equipped, the **Rocket Powered Pineapple Vacuum** grants the _Speedy: Rocket Powered Pineapple Vacuum_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land. It also replaces the player's punch with puffs of smoke coming from the vacuum's mouth.
