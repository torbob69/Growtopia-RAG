The **Mystic Snow Fox** is an unsplicable back item that was added as December 2020’s Item Of The Month on December 3, 2020.
##  Function
Mystic tails propel me!

A fox no more!

When equipped, the **Mystic Snow Fox** grants the _Double-Jump: Mystic Snow Fox!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and switch between its wearable form and its morph form by wrenching oneself and clicking 'Transform'. Additionally, it will transform the player's character into a mystic fox using the Mystic Transform Crystal by consuming it. The Mystic Snow Fox releases small particle-like stars while walking or standing.
##  Trivia
  * The Mystic Snow Fox's design is based on a mythical fox appearing in ancient Korean folklores.
  * The Mystic Snow Fox was the last Item Of The Month that was available for 200,000 Gems.
  * As of the Player Appreciation Week update on August 15, 2022, players can now switch between the wearable item and its morph by wrenching oneself and clicking "Transform".
  * As of Player Apprecation Week 2023, Mystic Snow Fox will no longer un-transform upon entering a world.
