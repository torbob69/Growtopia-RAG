# Battle Boots

Stomp your foes... but not your wallet! These boots are made for victories, so put 'em on and get to winning!

Daily Challenge

Place in the top three of the **Card Clash** Guild Daily Challenge, only if the player had contributed points to the challenge.
The **Battle Boots** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of the Challengers Approach! update on October 9, 2019.
##  Function
You've got a trick or two in your boots.

No more games for you.

When equipped, the **Battle Boots** grants the _Redraw Cards: Battle Boots_ mod, which allows the player to increase the odds of not using up a card while battling a villain.
