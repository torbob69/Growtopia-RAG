The **Surfboard - Electric Green** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of SummerFest 2017 on June 30, 2017.
##  Function
When equipped, the **Surfboard - Electric Green** allows the player to float on water for three seconds.
The  Myths and Legends : The Search for Nessie mission requires 1 of this item.
