# Climbing Picks

These handy picks allow you to climb Climbing Walls. Just jump and hold the arrow towards the wall!

The **Climbing Picks** is an unsplicable hand item that was added as part of the Spelunkorama! update on May 2, 2016.
##  Function
You slice and dice! And climb!

Back to punching.

When equipped, the **Climbing Picks** grants the _Pickin' N' Climbin_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. The item also allows the player to cling onto Climbing Walls by holding their jump. Other items which serve the same purpose include:

  * Claws of Barko

  * Climbing Boots

  * Ezio's Boots

  * Mountain Boots

  * S.P.I.D.E.R. Legs

  * Tarantula Legs

  * Will of the Wild (earth version)
