# Super Pineapple Guardian Charm

Pining for protection? Make the power of the pineapple guardian personal with this amazing necklace! When activated, grants immunity to thrown consumables for 5 seconds (starts as soon as you get hit), followed by a 5-minute cooldown.

The **Super Pineapple Guardian Charm** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2018 on June 1, 2018.
##  Function
The Pineapple presence protects you!

The Pineapple presence leaves you!

When equipped, this item grants the _The Pineapple protects you!_ mod, which makes the player immune to thrown consumables for 5 seconds with a 5-minute cooldown.
##  Trivia
  * As of Player Appreciation Week 2020 Day 4, the Super Pineapple Guardian Charm's cooldown was changed from 30 minutes to 5 minutes.
