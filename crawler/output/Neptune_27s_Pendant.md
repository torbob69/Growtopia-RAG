The **Neptune's Pendant** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of SummerFest 2025 on July 10, 2025.
##  Function
To me, my fishies!

Fish out of water!

When equipped, the **Neptune's Pendant** grants the _Build Range + Punch Range: Neptune's Pendant_ mod, which allows the player to build one tile further and punch one tile further.
Step 17 of the  Quest For Fire requires the player to deliver 1 of this item.
