The **Creepy Baby Head Mask** is an unsplicable hair item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2018 on October 26, 2018.
##  Function
When equipped, the Creepy Baby Head Mask serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The  Creepy Baby Block, which was released later in 2019, was suggested as a spoof on the  Creepy Baby Head Mask. [[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Creepy_Baby_Head_Mask#cite_note-1)
  * This item is considered a part of the creepy baby set, which consists of the following items:

    * Creepy Baby Head Mask (face)

    * Creepy Baby Suit (shirt)
