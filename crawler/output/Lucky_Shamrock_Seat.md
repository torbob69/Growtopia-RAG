The **Lucky Shamrock Seat** is an unsplicable non-solid seat foreground block which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2023 on March 17, 2023.
##  Function
When a player stands in front of a seat such as the Lucky Shamrock Seat for a few seconds, the player will automatically be seen "sitting" on it.
