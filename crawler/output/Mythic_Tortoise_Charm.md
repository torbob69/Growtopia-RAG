The **Mythic Tortoise Charm** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of Lunar New Year 2025 on January 23, 2025.
##  Function
You've got the power!

You've lost the power!

When equipped, the **Mythic Tortoise Charm** grants the _Punch Damage + Punch Power: Mythic Tortoise Charm_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and knock back other players further.
