The **Moon Mask** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2013 on September 18, 2013.
##  Function
When equipped, the Moon Mask serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The Moon Mask's design is based on Mac Tonight, a fictional character who appeared in television commercials for McDonald's during the 1980's.
  * It isn't guaranteed that the player will get a Moon Mask by following the item's combining recipe, as all the items below share the same recipe:
    * Yellow Hanbok Top
