The **Dark King's Crown** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2023 on October 19, 2023.
##  Function
Send shivers down your spine!

Ghost... busted!

When equipped, the **Dark King's Crown** grants the _Punch Range: Dark King's Crown_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further and move faster on land.
##  Trivia
  * Dark King's Crown also provides speedy, although it is not stated in its label.
