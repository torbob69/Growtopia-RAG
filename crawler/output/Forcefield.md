The **Forcefield** is an unsplicable foreground entrance block which was added alongside the Astro Pack on October 21, 2013.
##  Function
Being an entrance, a Forcefield allows players with access to walk through it, or, when its property is changed, allowing any player to walk through it. When placed in a public area, it is impossible to be closed for anyone.
