The **Royal Grow Pass Token** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Grow Pass update on October 5, 2021.

In addition to unlocking rewards in the royal tier, Royal Grow Pass users get all the perks of the Monthly Subscriber Token:
  * Earning 5,500 gems daily
  * Having a reduced grow time on all seeds planted
  * Getting a small chance to earn bonus XP
  * Unlocking exclusive skin colors

Moreover, these players are given access to all Daily Bonuses, regardless of their player or awesomeness level.
