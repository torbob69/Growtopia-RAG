# Saxamaphone

You can't punch when holding this, you're too busy ROCKING! Punch anywhere to play music. If you punch to your left or right, you can bend the note higher or lower. Punch in your own column to play the same notes that Sheet Music: Sax Note plays.

The **Saxamaphone** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2017 on May 5, 2017.
##  Function
You're too busy rocking to punch anybody!

The music has stopped.

When equipped, this item grants the _Musical_ mod, which restricts the player from actually punching, and plays the note of Sheet Music: Sax Note when the player attempts to do so. The pitch of the note depends on the tile punched.
