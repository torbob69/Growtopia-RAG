##  Function
One small step for a man, one giant leap for mankind!

Houston, we have a problem!

When a player stands on the block, they will get the _High-Jump, Slow-Fall: Moon Landing_ mod, will allows them to jump a little higher and fall slower. This mod will be removed once the player is no longer standing on the block.
