The **Clam Cruiser** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of the Grow Pass update as October 2021's Item Of The Season on October 5, 2021.
##  Function
Ahoy! A cruisin' we shall go!

Land lubbers!

When equipped, the **Clam Cruiser** grants the _Punch Damage: Clam Cruiser_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster.
