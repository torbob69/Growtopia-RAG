The **Federation Starship Hull - Ramp 1** is an unobtainable platform bedrock foreground block which was added as part of Star Trek Prodigy on June 27, 2022.
##  Function
The Federation Starship Hull - Ramp 1 is used as the ramp for the Protostar Starship. This item also behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath.
