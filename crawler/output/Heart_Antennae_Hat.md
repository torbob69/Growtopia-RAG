# Heart Antennae Hat

My loving senses are tingling... there must be someone nearby that needs some loving. Better go give them a hug! Now as a hat! Can be transformed into a headband by combining 1 Heart Antennae Hat, 1 Transmog Crystal, and 10 Candy Hearts in a Chemical Combiner.

The **Heart Antennae Hat** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2022 on February 11, 2022.
##  Function
When equipped, the Heart Antennae Hat serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
