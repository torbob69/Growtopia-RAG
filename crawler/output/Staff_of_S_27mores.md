# Staff of S'mores

Use the unconventional staff to "fire" delicious warm s'mores!
Properties

**1h**
Scythe Harvesting

Harvest **trees of rarity no more than 100** with a  Hand Scythe during the Harvest Festival.
The **Staff of S'mores** is an unsplicable hand item added as part of Harvest Festival 2021 on September 16, 2021.
##  Function
Time to get toasty!

Too yummy, all gone!

When equipped, the **Staff of S'mores** grants the _Punch Range: Staff of S'mores_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further. Upon contact with a block, the block will emit flaming smores sprite for a few seconds and a flame explosion whenever the block gets punched.
