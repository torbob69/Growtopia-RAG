# Ring of Nature

The call of the wild... in the palm of your hand! Wear this ring and enjoy a special bond with all things that grow! Reduces the grow time of seeds by 2% when planting or splicing!

The **Ring of Nature** is a hand item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2018.
##  Function
Effortless Growth...

Aw, no more extra growth.

When equipped, this item grants the _Reduce Growtime..._ mod. When a player plants a seed with the item equipped, the tree's growth will be automatically sped up by 2%. The player will then say, "Accelerated tree growth!"
##  Trivia
  * As of Player Appreciation Week 2021 Day 5 on August 27, 2021, Ring of Nature's effect has been buffed to speed trees up from 1% to 2%.
