The **Burning hands** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2017 Day 1 on August 17, 2017.
##  Function
Your hands are quite warm.

Everything feels so much cooler!

When equipped, the **Burning Hands** grants the _Burning Hands_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. Similar to its counterpart, Burning Eyes, it shoots two fire beams when punching.
##  Trivia
  * The Burning Hands have a slightly bigger fire animation than the Dragon Hand and Burning Eyes. It also shoots two fire beams instead of one.
