I know, they're supposed to have feathers and stuff nowadays... Well, this is an old-school raptor!

This recipe will yield **5** items each.
The **Red Riding Raptor** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Growassic Park on October 7, 2016.
##  Function
You can move faster!

You are slow again.

When equipped, the **Red Riding Raptor** grants the _Speedy_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.
##  Trivia
  * The description of a Red Riding Raptor references how raptors are found to have feathers in modern scientific studies.
  * As part of the Player Appreciation Week 2022 Day 4, the quantity of all raptors obtained from a DNA Combination was increased from 1 to 5.
