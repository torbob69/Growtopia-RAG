The **Prehistoric Flowering Fern** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Growassic Park on October 7, 2016.
##  Function
When placed, the Prehistoric Flowering Fern serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The description of the Prehistoric Flowering Fern is a reference to "Welcome... to Jurassic Park," a quote uttered by the fictional character John Hammond in the 1993 science fiction adventure drama film Jurassic Park.
  * As part of Player Appreciation Week 2020 Day 4, the quantity of Prehistoric Flowering Fern's obtained from a DNA Combination was increased from 1 to 5.
  * As part of Player Appreciation Week 2022 Day 4, the quantity of Prehistoric Flowering Fern's obtained from a DNA Combination was increased from 5 to 15.
