##  Trivia
  * The description of The Darkness contains a reference to the line "I'm attacking the darkness!" from the _Dungeons and Dragons_ skit by the Dead Alewives, which is said after a player acting as the wizard casts a Magic Missile spell while residing in an empty hallway.
  * In the early beta version of _Growtopia_ , both the Checker Wallpaper Tree and The Darkness Tree were accidentally given the same recipe, preventing players from splicing the latter. This mistake was fixed shortly thereafter.[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/The_Darkness#cite_note-1)
