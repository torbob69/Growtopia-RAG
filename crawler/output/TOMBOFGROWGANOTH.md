# TOMBOFGROWGANOTH

TOMBOFGROWGANOTH is a world open during Halloween Week only. It was first introduced during Halloween Week 2019. Players can join the game by consuming a Dark Ticket.
After using a Dark Ticket, the player has 20 minutes to explore the world and find a total of 6 Sacrificial Wells and sacrifice items to it. The wells only accept items between rarity 10-200, and 1 rarity worth of items grants 1 corrupted soul. Each well will accept only up to 8,000 rarity.
The various TOMBOFGROWGANOTH worlds have the same layouts, but the Sacrificial Wells and the Main Door are in different places.
When the player is done sacrificing, the player has to go to Growganoth's tomb to collect their prize. There are a few corruption tiers, depending on the total corrupted souls collected.
##  Corruption Tiers
### Lowly Corruption
  * You must collect at least 16,000 corrupted souls to get this tier.
Prizes: Uses(if any)Bone Checkpoint: Works like a checkpoint.
Bone Platform: Works like a platform.
Bone Fence: A non-solid, foreground decoration.
Spooky Bunting: A non-solid, foreground decoration.
### Great Corruption
  * You must collect at least 32,000 corrupted souls to get this tier.
Ultimate Prizes: Uses(if any)Spooky Ghost Block: A non-solid, foreground decoration.
Organ Piano: A toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block, plays a piece of music when punched.
Mary's Mirror: A toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block.
Kuchisake Mask: Serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
### Ultimate Corruption
  * You must collect at least 48,000 corrupted souls or higher to get this tier.
Ultimate Prizes: Uses(if any)Cloak of Equilibrium: Grants the Double Jump mod.
Cursed Katana: Grants the Punch Damage: Cursed! mod.
Familiar Hat: Serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
Gargoyle Mask: Serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
Horns of Growganoth: Grants the Speedy mod and gives bonus 250XP for each Dark Ticket used.
Pharaoh's Pendant: Grants the player the Long Range Mod.
Scythe of the Underworld: Grants the Punch Damage: Underworld! mod.
Supernaturally Speedy Black High Heels: Grants the Speedy mod.
Apocalypse Scythe: Grants the _Punch Damage: AXE!_ Mod
Waay Chivo Cannon: Grants the _Punch Range: Waay Chivo Cannon!_ mod
Monster Koach: Grants the _Speedy: Monster Koach_ mod
Dashing Devil Mask: Serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
Scarabslushie Suit: Serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
Scarabslushie Hair: Serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
Scarabslushie Face Paint: Serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
Bloated Bullfrog Balloon: Grants the _Punch Range + Slow Fall : Bloated Bullfrog Balloon_ mod
##  Trivia
  * When the Tomb Of Growganoth update was first released, there was a major bug which caused the total souls to not increase, resulting in a maintenance 15 minutes after the Halloween Week 2019 was released. Subsequently, the event was extended for a day.
