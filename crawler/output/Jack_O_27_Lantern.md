The **Jack O' Lantern** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable animated foreground block that was added as part of Halloween Week 2013 on October 28, 2013.
##  Function
<https://growtopiawiki.com/w/File:Loonysong.ogg>The Jack O' Lantern's music
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Jack O' Lantern can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Jack O' Lantern will change its appearance, showing a 2-frame animation, and play a piece of music.
##  Trivia
  * In the game files, the Jack O' Lantern's music is named loonysong.
