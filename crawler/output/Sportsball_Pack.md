# Sportsball Pack
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Sportsball Pack** for **20,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"We like sports and we don't care who knows! This pack includes 2 Basketball Hoops, 2 Sporty Goals, 5 Stadiums, 5 Crowded Stadiums, 10 Field Grass, a Football Helmet, a Growies Cap, a Ref's Jersey, a World Cup Jersey, and randomly one of the seven Rare sports items that you can use to launch sportsballs at people or a Rare Growmoji!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Sportsball Pack** was released as part of an update with a similar name on July 17, 2015.

##  Trivia
  * The pack was updated on July 4, 2018 to celebrate the World Cup.
    * Previously, the pack cost 15,000 gems each. Its description would say, "We like sports and we don't care who knows! This pack includes 2 Basketball Hoops, 2 Sporty Goals, 5 Stadiums, 5 Crowded Stadiums, a Football Helmet, a Growies Cap, and randomly one of the six Rare sports items that you can use to launch sportsballs at people!"
    * As stated in the description, its contents included 2 Basketball Hoop, 2 Sporty Goal, 5 Stadium, 5 Crowded Stadium, 1 Football Helmet, 1 Growies Cap, and one of the following: 1 Baseball Glove, Basketball, FC Cleats, FC Jersey, Man U Cleats, Man U Jersey, Pigskin, or Tennis Racquet.
  * On June 2, 2023, World Cup Winner's Jersey 2018 was replaced by World Cup Winner's Jersey 2022 in the pack.
  * On July 22, 2026, World Cup Winner's Jersey 2022 was replaced by World Cup Winner's Jersey 2026 in the pack.
