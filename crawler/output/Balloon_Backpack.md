The **Balloon Backpack** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of Easter Week 2024 on April 7, 2024.
##  Function
Bob to the top!

Pop goes the weasel!

When equipped, the **Balloon Backpack** grants the _High Jump + Slow Fall: Balloon Backpack!_ mod, which allows the player to jump one tile higher and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
