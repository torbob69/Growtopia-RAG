The **Action Hero Helicopter** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as May 2023's Item Of The Season on May 1, 2023.
##  Function
Get to the Choppa!

I'll Be Back!

When equipped, the **Action Hero Helicopter** grants the _High Jump + Double Jump + Slowfall: Action Hero Helicopter_ mod, which allows the player to jump one tile higher, jump a second time in mid-air, and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
