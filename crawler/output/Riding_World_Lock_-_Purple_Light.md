The **Riding World Lock - Purple Light** is an unsplicable feet item which was introduced as September's Item Of The Month for 2019. The item, along with the White, Green, and Yellow Light were possible drops from purchasing September 2019's Item Of The Month for 200,000 Gems.
##  Function
Giddy-up!

Locked away!

When equipped, the **Riding World Lock - Purple Light** grants the _Speedy: World Lock_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land. A trail of purple light is also produced as the player moves around.
