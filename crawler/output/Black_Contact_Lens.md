# Black Contact Lens

Wrap your pupils in ebon darkness with these bleakly black lenses. When your friends stare into your eyes, the void will stare back! You may need multiple lenses - and other colors - to achieve a desired effect.

The **Black Contact Lens** is a consumable which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2018 Day 6 alongside the Contact Lens Pack on August 11, 2018.
##  Function
To use, one must stand in front of a Bathroom Mirror, then consume the contact lens. This will give a light shade of black to the player's pupils. The more eye drops one use (maximum of 8 drops), the darker the color gets.
