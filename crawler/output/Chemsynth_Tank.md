The **Chemsynth Tank** was added with the Night Of The Chemicals. 10 tanks must be placed in a line to the right of a Chemsynth Processor in order for it to be used. 10 of these items come in the Chemsynth Pack in the gem store.
##  Function
Placing 10 tanks in a line to the right of a Chemsynth Processor will allow it to be used to Chemsynth.
