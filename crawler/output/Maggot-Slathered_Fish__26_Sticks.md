# Maggot-Slathered Fish & Sticks

Why. Because undercooked fish and moldy breading isn't awful enough, you've managed to top it with a slithering slurry of fresh maggots.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **4 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **5s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Rotting Fish Guts Club Sandwich **(×1)** ,
Disgusting Mess **(×3)** and
Water Bucket **(×10)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Maggot-Slathered Fish & Sticks** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Gross-Out update on May 1, 2019. One can be dropped from combining a Rotting Fish Guts Club Sandwich, 3 Disgusting Mess, and 10 Water Buckets in any Chemical Combiner.
