# Watermelon Slice

Juicy and sweet, but definitely NOT seedless! Take a bite and get ready to blast the world with weaponized watermelon!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Hand – Clothes
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: Full Collision
Hardness: **0 Hits** **0 Hits** – Restores after **0s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Super Fireworks Reward

May be obtained from firing off  Super Fireworks with **200** Fireworks in your inventory.
The **Watermelon Slice** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of SummerFest 2018 on July 6, 2018.
##  Function
Spit seeds at your enemies.

Your mouth is free again.

When equipped, the Watermelon Slice grants the _Mouthfull of Seeds_ mod, which replace the player's punching animation with seeds coming out of the player's mouth.
##  Trivia
  * As of Super Pineapple Party 2023, the Watermelon was renamed to **Watermelon Slice**.
