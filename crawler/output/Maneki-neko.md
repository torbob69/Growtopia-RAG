The **Maneki-neko** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Lunar New Year 2023 on January 21, 2023.
##  Function
When placed, the Maneki-neko serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The Maneki-neko's appearance is a reference to a common Japanese figurine of the same name, which is often believed to bring good luck to the owner.
