The **Rapier** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2015 on May 1, 2015.
##  Function
You slice and dice!

Back to punching.

When equipped, the **Rapier** grants the _Rapier_ mod, which allows the player to kill zombies during a Pandemic. It will also replace the player's punching animation with a slashing effect and will carve the letter "_G_ " on the bodies of other players.
