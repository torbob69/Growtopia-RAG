The **Lace Parasol** is an unsplicable hand item which were added as part of Valentine's Week 2024 on February 14, 2024.
##  Function
What a wonderful feeling!

I'm sinking in the rain!

When equipped, the **Lace Parasol** grants the _Double Jump & Slowfall : Lace Parasol_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air, glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button, and jump one tile higher.
##  Trivia
  * Lace Parasol also provides high jump, although it is not stated in its label.
