|  Cooking Recipe - Alien Bloodied Brains Strudel  | To cook this recipe you will need the following:

Ingredients:
- Space Poop
- Zombie Brain
- Slime
- Putrid Gas

Seasoning:
- Acid to taste

Process:
Add Space Poop to an oven on Low heat. While the Space Poop is still cold, add Zombie Brain. Before the Space Poop begins to cook, add Slime. Add the seasonings and wait until perfectly cooked all the way through. Then add Putrid Gas and remove from the oven straight away.

Timings may vary so you will need to experiment.   |
