**Huginn and Muninn - Odin's Ravens** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of the Treasure Hunt update.
##  Function
Rise with wings of Thought and Memory!

You are slowly going insane!

When equipped, the **Huginn and Muninn - Odin's Ravens** grants the _Double Jump: Odin's Ravens_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air. A pair of raven pets will also appear and follow the player around while the player is moving. When the player is stationary, they can be seen perching on the player's shoulders.
##  Trivia
  * This item is a reference to Huginn and Muninn, the pet ravens of Odin, a Germanic god stemming from Norse mythology.
