The **Scarf of Prometheus** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2023 Day 7 on September 3, 2023.
##  Function
This scarf is on fire!

We didn't start the fire!

When equipped, the **Scarf of Prometheus** grants the _High Jump + Fireproof : Scarf of Prometheus_ mod, which allows the player to jump one tile higher and take 50% less damage from lava pain blocks.
##  Trivia
  * Scarf of Prometheus' equip message is a reference to Alcia Key's Girl on Fire.
  * Scarf of Prometheus' unequip message is a reference to Billy Joel's We Didn't Start the Fire.
  * The **Scarf of Prometheus** is a reference to "God of Fire" in Greek Mythology, and Prometheus the Sun in Japanese manga and anime series of One Piece.
