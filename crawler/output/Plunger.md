The **Plunger** is a hand item that was added as part of the Voucher Dayz update on May 28, 2021. It was first made purchasable along with the Glowing Shirt during May 28-30, 2021.
##  Function
When equipped, the Plunger serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The Plunger is one out of five items that were previously only available to the discontinued console edition of Growtopia, along with the Energy Drink, Glowing Shirt, The Brother Hood, and Unicorn.
    * All of them were obtained from Ubisoft Club Rewards and are now being released to the mobile/PC version of Growtopia through the Console Classix Pack.
  * The Plunger's description was changed when it was brought over to the main version of Growtopia. It originally said, "_From the weird and wacky world of the Rabbids! Fixes toilets and makes a stylish fashion statement!_ "
    * The reason the overt _Rabbids_ reference was dropped from the sentence is currently unknown.
