The **E-Z Cook Oven** is a splicable Chemical Combiner foreground block which was added as part of the Rise of the Desert Dragons update on August 3, 2013.
##  Function
An E-Z Cook Oven can be opened or closed by being punched. As a Chemical Combiner, it can be used to combine three different sets of items. A stack of new items will be created afterwards.
