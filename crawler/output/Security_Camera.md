The **Security Camera** is a non-solid security camera foreground block which can record data about player activities in the world. It was released alongside the Shop Pack on January 24, 2014.
##  Function
Upon wrenching, those with access may read and turn on or off certain event triggers. These triggers are as follows:
  * Show item taking (shows what a player picks up, the quantity, the player's name, and the time and date they picked it up)
  * Show item dropping (shows what a player drops, the quantity, the player's name, and the time and date they dropped it)
  * Show people entering (shows the entering player's name, as well as the time and date they entered)
  * Show people exiting (shows the exiting player's name, as well as the time and date they exited)
  * Don't show owner (does not log data involving the owner of the world)
  * Don't show admins (does not log data involving any person with access to the world)
  * Don't show people without access (does not log data involving any player without access to the world).
