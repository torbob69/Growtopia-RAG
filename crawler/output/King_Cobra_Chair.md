# King Cobra Chair

A cobra chair fit for a king and a cobra and you!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.
Data: Paint PreviewType — Foreground Block – Foreground — Aqua Blue Charcoal Green Purple Red Varnish Yellow
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **4 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **1s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Paint Preview Aqua Blue Charcoal Green Purple Red Varnish Yellow
Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Emerald Block **(×2)** ,
Lazy Cobra **(×10)** and
Potato Couch **(×2)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **King Cobra Chair** is an unsplicable non-solid seat foreground block which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2020 on April 13, 2020.
##  Function
When a player stands in front of a seat such as the King Cobra Chair for a few seconds, the player will automatically be seen "sitting" on it.
