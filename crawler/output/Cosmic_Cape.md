**Cosmic Cape** is a unsplicable back item which was added as part of the Daily Challenges update on November 18, 2015.
##  Function
You can jump in mid-air! It's like flying, but less.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Cosmic Cape** grants the _Double Jump!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and have an extremely small chance of dropping Comet Dust when breaking block.
##  Trivia
  * It was the special prize which the top 3 players in the **Cosmic Crown** Daily Challenge used to obtain. However, it is now obtainable in the Growtopia Store on the Night Of The Comet for 200,000 Gems since the challenge was removed.
