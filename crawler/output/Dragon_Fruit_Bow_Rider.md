The **Dragon Fruit Bow Rider** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2025 on June 5, 2025.
##  Function
Shots fired!

Out of ammo!

When equipped, the **Dragon Fruit Bow Rider** grants the _Speedy + Punch Range: Dragon Fruit Bow Rider_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and punch one tile further.
