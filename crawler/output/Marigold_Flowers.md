The **Marigold Flowers** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2022 on May 5, 2022.
##  Function
When placed, the Marigold Flowers serves a fully aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The Marigold Flowers is based on the eponymous flowers of the _Tagetes_ genus, which are often used to decorate altars during Day of the Dead.
