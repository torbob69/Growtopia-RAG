# Uranium Chair

Glowing with the radioactive power of the apocalypse! Take a seat, and even if you don't have any mutants to rule, you'll get a "healthy" green glow for your efforts!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can't be destroyed - smashing it will return it to your backpack if you have room!
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.

Daily Challenge

Place in the top three of the **Radiation Race** Guild Daily Challenge, only if the player had contributed points to the challenge.
The **Uranium Chair** is an unsplicable non-solid seat foreground block which was added as part of the Challengers Approach! update on October 9, 2019.
##  Function
When a player stands in front of a seat such as the Uranium Chair for a few seconds, the player will automatically be seen "sitting" on it. Additionally, the player may receive the _Irradiated_ mod for as long as the player stays on the tile.
