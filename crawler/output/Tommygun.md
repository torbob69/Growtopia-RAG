##  Function
You shoot bullets instead of punching!

You are disarmed

When equipped, the **Tommygun** grants the _Armed and Dangerous_ mod, which allows the player to kill zombies during a Pandemic. It will also replace the player's punching animation with bullets being fired from the gun. Additionally, it will give the /mad expression to the player.

##  Trivia
  * The Tommygun is based on the real-life Thompson submachine gun (also known as a "Tommy Gun"), which became notorious as a signature weapon of various organized crime syndicates in the United States during the 1920s.
  * This item is considered a part of the gangland set, which consists of the following items:

    * Fedora (hat)

    * Dame's Fedora (hat)

    * Pinstripe Suit (shirt)

    * Pinstripe Pants (pants)

    * Cigar (face)

    * Tommygun (hand)
