The **Harmonipan** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2022 on May 5, 2022.
##  Function
<https://growtopiawiki.com/w/File:Harmonipan2022.ogg>The Harmonipan's music.
Being a toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block, a Harmonipan can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Harmonipan will change its appearance, showing a 2-framed animation, and play a piece of music.
##  Trivia
  * The Harmonipan is based on a traditional Mexican instrument of the same name, which is played in a similar manner to the barrel organ.
  * In the game files, the Harmonipan's music is named harmonipan2022.
  * The piece of music which the Harmonipan plays is a remake of the song La Raspa and Mexican Hat Dance.
