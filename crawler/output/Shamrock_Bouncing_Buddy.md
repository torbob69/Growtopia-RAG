Look at its little bouncy face! Bounce to your heart's content! They won't mind, we promise!

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Cloverleaf **(×1)** ,

Moss-Covered Stone **(×5)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
**Shamrock Bouncing Buddy** is a trampoline block that was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2021 on March 11, 2021.
##  Function
Jumping on a trampoline block such as a Shamrock Bouncing Buddy will cause the player to bounce, and by holding the jump button, the player can jump even higher. The _High Jump_ mod also boosts one's jump height on a trampoline block.
