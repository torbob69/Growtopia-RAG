The **Anomalizing Pristine Scythe** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Summer Clash Series 1.
##  Function
Anomalizing Pristine Scythes are used to score points during Harvest Heroes. While equipping this item, each tree harvested yields 80 points. Pristine anomalizers, such as this item, may sometimes break upon usage during their respective Grow Events.
