The Bullfrog is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of UbiWeek 2024 Day 5 on April 2, 2024.
##  Function
Serve the light!

In the dark!

When equipped, the **Bullfrog** grants the _Punch Damage + Punch Range: Bullfrog_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and punch one tile further.
