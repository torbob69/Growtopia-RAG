The Rollback Plaque III is a solid rollback plaque foreground block which was added in August 2015, after a major rollback had occurred.
##  History
On August 9, 2015, ex-developer Hamumu made a thread on the forums, confirming that another rollback would take place, and Rollback Plaque III would be splicable, but would not drop any seeds.
The item was added shortly after.
##  Trivia
  * Rollback Plaque III features an icon of toasted bread etched on the block, symbolising a RAID array burning up as this was the cause of the rollback, which ended up resulting in loss of data.
