The **Turkey Sucker** is a unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2020.
##  Function
I'm gonna get you, sucka!

Need to exhale a litte!

When equipped, this item grants the _AVIAN SUCTION : Turkey Sucker_ mod, which allows the player to suck in Thanksgiving Turkeys by punching them a few times.
Most of the time, a Turkey Sucker doesn't do anything when it successfully captures a Thanksgiving Turkey. However, there is an approximately 10% chance that it will explode after catching one, which will give:
  * Organic Turkey ×1 (slightly rare)
  * Roasted Turkey ×4–11 (common)

Moreover, there is also a small chance that a  Turkey Feather will be left behind when a Turkey Sucker catches a Thanksgiving Turkey, regardless if the sucker explodes or not.
