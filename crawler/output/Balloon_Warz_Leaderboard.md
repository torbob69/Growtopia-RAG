Wrench this board to see how the Balloon Warz are going! Is your team ahead?

**Balloon Warz Leaderboard** is a non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Balloon Warz 2017.
##  Function
While the Balloon Warz event is active, a Balloon Warz Leaderboard displays the amount of points each team has, the period of time until the end of the war, the top 5 players for the hourly challenge, and the number of hits the player has themself.
At the end of the war, players can wrench the board and claim their trophy. All players have one week to collect their trophies.
