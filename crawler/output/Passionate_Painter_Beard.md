The **Passionate Painter Beard** is an unsplicable face item which was added as a part of Harvest Festival 2025 on September 11, 2025.
##  Function
When equipped, the Passionate Painter Beard serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia

    * Passionate Painter Beard (face)
