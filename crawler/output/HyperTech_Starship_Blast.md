# HyperTech Starship Blast

Command your very own Starship and explore the cosmos! This blast contains a HyperTech ship, the finest craft in the galaxy! Comes with a full tank of gas, an Imperial Helm - Mk. I, Imperial Reactor - Mk. I and an Imperial Viewscreen - Mk. I, so you'll be all set for your adventure among the stars! Also comes with an assortment of space-age blocks!

The **HyperTech Starship Blast** is a consumable that was introduced along with the Startopia Update.
##  Function
When used, it creates a new HyperTech Starship, which is required in order for the player to perform Star Voyages.
