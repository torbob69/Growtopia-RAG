##  Function
To infinity and beyond!

To obscurity, so long!

When equipped, the **Wings of Infinity** grants the _Double Jump + Light Source : Wings of Infinity_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and emit light in dark areas.
These wings feature several special customization functions. To set the color for the wings the player needs to enter an RGB (Red, Blue, Green) value. Moreover, the player can choose between two different wing designs for each individual wing: feathered or mechanical. The player can also choose to hide individual wings, as well as change the trail type between feathered or mechanical.
