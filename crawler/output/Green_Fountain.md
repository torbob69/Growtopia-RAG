The **Green Fountain** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2021 on March 11, 2021.
##  Function
Being a toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block, a Green Fountain can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a green circle particle will burst out, and the Green Fountain will change its appearance, showing a 6-framed animation of water flowing out from the fountain.
