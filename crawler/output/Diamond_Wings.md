The **Diamond Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of The Carnival update on October 13, 2014.
##  Function
You can jump in mid-air It's like flying, but less

Gravity - it's the law

When equipped, the **Diamond Wings** grants the _Double Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
##  Trivia
  * Unlike the Blazing Electro Wings, which can also be obtained from a Carnival game, the Diamond Wings do not give the player a unique respawn animation.
