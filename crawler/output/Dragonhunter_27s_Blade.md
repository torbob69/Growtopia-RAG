The **Dragonhunter's Blade** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as February 2025's Item Of The Season on February 1, 2025.
##  Function
Rock the dragon!

Hidden dragon!

When equipped, the **Dragonhunter's Blade** grants the _Punch Damage: Dragonhunter's Blade_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster.
