The **Great Legendary Orb** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of the Legendary update on August 22, 2014.
##  Function
A Great Legendary Orb is found at the end of the parkour of LEGENDARYMOUNTAIN. When a player walks past the Great Legendary Orb, the player will receive a Legendary Orb if they do not have one in their backpack. One Legendary Orb is used for the last quest in every Legendary Quest.
##  Trivia
Great Legendary Orb has a noticeable similarity to Blarney Stone. They are both internal items unobtainable to normal players and grants an item when touched.
