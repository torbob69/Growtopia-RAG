Unlocked during Grow4Good after at least **120,000,000** global points have been accumulated.
The **Fish Tank Head** is an unsplicable face item which was added as part of Grow4Good Week 2022 on April 22, 2022.
##  Function
Just keep swimming!

Fish out of water!

When equipped, the **Fish Tank Head** grants the _Punch Range: Fish Tank Head_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further.
##  Trivia
  * During Grow4Good Week 2025, the point requirement to unlock the Fish Tank Head was lowered from 140,000,000 to 120,000,000.
