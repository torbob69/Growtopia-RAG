The Black Crystal Dragon is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the Crystals update on January 20, 2015.
##  Function
You command a mighty (dark) dragon!

Your dragon has abandoned you.

When equipped, the **Black Crystal Dragon** grants the _Dragonborn_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and knock back other players further. Additionally, it spawns a black dragon that follows the player around, which also replaces the player's punching animation with black shards.
