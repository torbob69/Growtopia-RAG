The **Demon Night Stallion** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2019 on October 28, 2019.
##  Function
You're a tiny demon prancer!

No more evil prancing for you!

When equipped, the **Demon Night Stallion** grants the _Speedy_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.
