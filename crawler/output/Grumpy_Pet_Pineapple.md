The **Grumpy Pet Pineapple** is an unsplicable pet hand item which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2019 on June 7, 2019.
##  Function
Would you like some juice?

This doesn't help my grumpiness!

When equipped, the Grumpy Pet Pineapple grants the _Grumpy Pineapple!_ mod which summons a grumpy-looking pineapple pet that follows the player around.
The  Super Pineapple Party Exclusive Mission - Pucker up for more Pineapple fun! mission requires 10 of this item.
