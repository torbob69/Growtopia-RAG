# Sharkzooka

Fire off blasts of water with your very own Sharkzooka!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be transmuted.
Data

Type: Hand – Clothes
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: Full Collision
Hardness: **0 Hits** **0 Hits** – Restores after **0s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Super Fireworks Reward

May be obtained from firing off  Super Fireworks with **200** Fireworks in your inventory.
The **Sharkzooka** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of SummerFest 2026 on July 9, 2026.
##  Function
Chompy shark doo-doo, doo-doo, doo-doo!

We're going to need a bigger boat!

When equipped, the **Sharkzooka** grants the _Punch Range + Punch Damage: Sharkzooka_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further and break blocks faster.
