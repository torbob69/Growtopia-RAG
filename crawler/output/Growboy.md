##  Function
Get your game on!

Batteries outta juice!

When equipped, the **Growboy** grants the _Punch Range: Growboy_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further.
##  Trivia
  * The Growboy was one of the three items obtainable for purchase with Growtokens added on Player Appreciation Week 2020 Day 3, alongside the Tactical Stealth Espionage Device and Really Dangerous Pet Llama.
