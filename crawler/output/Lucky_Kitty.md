# Lucky Kitty

This kitty sits in your world, constantly waving, and reminding you of how lucky you are to have a kitty in your world.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.

The **Lucky Kitty** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block that was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2017.
##  Trivia
  * The Lucky Kitty's appearance is based on Maneki-neko, a common Japanese figurine which is often believed to bring good luck to the owner.
