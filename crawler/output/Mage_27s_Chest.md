# Mage's Chest

A chest sealed by a mage of great renown.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.
Data: Paint PreviewType — Chest – Foreground — Aqua Blue Charcoal Green Purple Red Varnish Yellow
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision if turned on
Hardness: **6 Hits** **5 Hits** – Restores after **4s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Paint Preview Aqua Blue Charcoal Green Purple Red Varnish Yellow
Dungeons

It can be found naturally generated in Dungeon worlds.

Dungeons Reward

1 may be awarded to the player for completing a Dungeon at **Silver Tier or higher**.
The **Mage's Chest** is an unsplicable chest foreground block which was added as part of the Dungeons update on December 9, 2024.
##  Function
The Mage's Chest can be broken by players inside of Dungeons. When broken it will reward the player who broke it with 50 Souls for every player that is in the dungeon.
