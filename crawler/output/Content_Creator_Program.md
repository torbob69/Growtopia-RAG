# Content Creator Program

The **Growtopia Content Creator Program** was introduced on February 17, 2021.[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Content_Creator_Program#cite_note-1) The program aims to show appreciation and support for the existing Growtopia content creators.[[2]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Content_Creator_Program#cite_note-2)
##  Requirements
To become part of the Growtopia Content Creator Program, one needs to meet the following requirements:[[3]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Content_Creator_Program#cite_note-3)
  * The content creator must be 18 years of age or older.
  * The content creator must be a community member with a positive attitude.
  * The content creator's account must be with a good standing.
  * The content creator's account must have Advanced Account Protection enabled.
  * The content creator needs either an active YouTube, TikTok, Twitch, or Instagram account with at least 50% Growtopia related content and must meet one of the following minimum requirements for their channel. These minimum requirements need to be retained within 90 days upon acceptance to the program.
  * The Social Media account must be compliant with the platform's Terms of Service.
  * The content must not violate or promote the violation of Ubisoft Terms of Service.

###  YouTube
  * 2,000 subscribers and/or 2000 views per video on average
  * 2,000 views in the past 30 days
  * 1 video about Growtopia per month on average

###  TikTok
  * 500 followers
  * 1000 views on average per post
  * 2 posts about Growtopia in the past 30 days

###  Twitch
  * 300 followers
  * 30 concurrent viewers on average
  * 2 hours of Growtopia streamed in the past 30 days

###  Instagram
  * 2,000 followers
  * 150 likes on average per post
  * 3 posts in total in the past 30 days

##  Applying
Any application has to be submitted through the Official Growtopia Discord Server. The Growtopia Team will reach out to the content creator via mail and ask for their Discord account should they end up passing the survey.
##  Benefits
Being an official content creator will grant multiple benefits to the player.[[4]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Content_Creator_Program#cite_note-4)
  * **Exclusive Communication:** Content creators will receive access to the official Growtopia Discord server and a dedicated Discord content creator channel in order to communicate with the Growtopia Team.
  * **Virtual Currency:** Content creators will receive in-game currency (gems) every month. Additionally, the Growtopia Team may reach out to creators and ask them to create a specific piece of content for Ubisoft in exchange for an additional amount of Gems.
  * **Affiliate Code:** Content creators will have the capability to be part of our Xsolla program, giving them the ability to gain revenue share from purchases from their viewers using their code.
  * **In-Game Badge:** Content Creators will receive an in-game badge to display next to their name showing off that they're an Official Content Creator.
  * **Early Access to Updates:** Content creators will get access to all new items and features (at the Growtopia team's discretion) that are related to the newest update for recording.
  * **Community Hub Rotation:** Content creators will be included in the Community Hub rotation in the game on a monthly basis.
  * **YouTube Playlist:** Content creators on YouTube will be added to a dedicated playlist on the official Growtopia YouTube channel.
  * **Social Media Features:** Content may be featured on the official Growtopia Social Media channels with a maximum of 3 features per week. Who will be featured is up to the Growtopia Team's discretion.

##  History
  * On July 3, 2023, the requirements to be an official content creator has been adjusted to the following:
    *  **YouTube**
      * 2,000 subscribers and/or 2000 views per video on average (previously 3,000 subscribers and/or 3000 views per video on average)
      * 2,000 views in the past 30 days (previously 5,000 views in the past 30 days)
    *  **Twitch**
      * 300 followers (previously 500 followers)
      * 30 concurrent viewers on average (previously 50 concurrent viewers on average)
      * 2 hours of Growtopia streamed in the past 30 days (previously 4 hours of Growtopia streamed in the past 30 days)
    *  **Instagram**
      * 2,000 followers (previously 3,000 followers)
      * 150 likes on average per post (previously 300 likes on average per post)
      * 3 posts in total in the past 30 days (previously 4 posts in total in the past 30 days)
  * On August 8, 2023, TikTok has been added as an official platform for Content Creators.
  * On November 1, 2023, titles have been added exclusively for official Content Creators, which can be toggled on or off by wrenching oneself and clicking title.
    * A blue checkmark is given to Instagram, YouTube, and Twitch official Content Creators
    * A black checkmark is given to TikTok official Content Creators.
