The **Ultra Trophy 3001** was a test item. Down to the graphics, it is an exact copy of the Ultra Trophy 3000. It is set as an ordinary item rather than a perma-item.
##  Trivia
  * Seth has stated that this item will never be released, and was never intended to even be seen. He has stated that the trophy is an administrator-only test item.

Seth also said this:
