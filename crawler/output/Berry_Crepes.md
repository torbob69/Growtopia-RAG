**Berry Crepes** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Cooking update.
##  Function
Gain 30% more XP from Surgery!

Your stomach's rumbling.

When consumed, this item grants the _Food: Surgery XP_ food buff mod, which allows the player to gain 30% more experience points upon successfully completing a surgery for 30 minutes.
##  Trivia
  * Berry Crepes is one of the three food consumables which require Blueberry in their recipes, besides Blueberry Muffin and Blueberry Pie.
  * The experience boost granted by Berry Crepes' food buff stacks with Emergency Dragon Claw's mod.
