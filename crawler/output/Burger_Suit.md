The **Burger Suit** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2020.
##  Function
When equipped, the Burger Suit serves a purely cosmetic purpose. The Burger Suit leaves behind trails of lettuce and tomato chunks as the player moves around, as well as splashes of brown liquid when the player jumps.
##  Trivia
  * The Burger Suit is modeled after the modern hamburger, a food which consists of a round patty of ground beef placed inside a sliced bun or bread roll and garnished with various condiments.
    * The hamburger—and fast food in general—has become synonymous with the United States of America since the 20th century,[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Burger_Suit#cite_note-1) thus inspiring the Burger Suit's addition during Thanksgiving Week 2020, which is based around American culture as a whole.
