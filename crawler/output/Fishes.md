# Fishes

This is a list of all items which can be placed into a Fish Tank.
##  Regular Fish
These fish are the most commonly caught fish—typically caught using a Wiggly Worm, Shiny Flashy Thing, Salmon Eggs, Fishing Fly, Shrimp Lure, or Whizmo Gizmo.

##  Crabs
Crabs can only be caught using Lobster Traps. Using five of any bait and waiting anywhere between 1-3 days will yield a crab. Depending on the bait and trap's location, a Lobster Trap may yield crabs of different sizes.

##  Radioactive/Ice Fish
These fish can only be caught in special environments. Ice environments are created by using a Hand Drill on an Ice Crust Block with a Water Bucket on top, while radioactive environments are created by using a Nuclear Detonator on a Uranium Block with a Water Bucket on top.
Only the Uranium Glowing Lure can be used to catch fish in radioactive environments, while only Mega-Pellet Bait can be used to catch fish in ice environments.

##  COTD Fish
A COTD fish may be caught during its respective event using a Catch-of-the-Day Bait. All fish caught using a Catch-of-the-Day Bait have a max perfect size of 150lb, but the bait's extra effect may allow the player to catch even bigger fish, causing them to be labeled "Massive". The maximum massive fish size is 186lb.

##  Trivia
  * A bug involving the Shrimp Lure made players able to catch any kind of fish with a greatly higher size than perfect during the first hours after the fishing update was released. This bug had since been fixed.
