The **USMom's Talaria** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2018.
##  Function
Run like the wind!

Your feet are back on the ground!

When equipped, the **USMom's Talaria** grants the _Fleet of Foot_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.

##  Trivia
  * In Roman mythology, the Talaria of Mercury are winged sandals owned by the messenger god Mercury.
  * USMom, the player who sponsored this item, was the fifth person to complete all the Epic Quests.
  * The item used to be called Talaria, but was later changed into USMom's Talaria as part of the Summer Season Events update.
  * USMom's Talaria is the only sponsored item to have recolored versions, which are  Golden Talaria and  Pegasus Shoes.
