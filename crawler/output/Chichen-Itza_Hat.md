The **Chichen-Itza Hat** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2020 on May 1, 2020.
##  Function
When equipped, the Chichen-Itza Hat serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
The  Cinco De Mayo Exclusive Mission - So you really are an artist! mission requires 50 of this item.
