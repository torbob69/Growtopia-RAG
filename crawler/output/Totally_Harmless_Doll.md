# Totally Harmless Doll

A completely ordinary, absolutely not-possessed, super-safe doll, perfect for children and other helpless creatures. Please don't be afraid. It's your friend. You should keep it close. Especially when you're asleep.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.

This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Totally Harmless Doll** is a non-solid SFX foreground block which was added as part of Halloween Week 2017 on October 30, 2017.
##  Function
Being a type of SFX foreground block, a Totally Harmless Doll plays a short animation and a doll sound effect when punched.
