The **Cursed Spirit Pendant - Green** is an unsplicable chest item which was added on October 3, 2019.
##  Function
Spirits released!

Spirits locked away...

When equipped, the **Cursed Spirit Pendant - Green** grants the _Double-Jump: Cursed_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air. When the player dies, an explosion of green fire will occur and the spirit leaving the player's body will have a more spooky design.
