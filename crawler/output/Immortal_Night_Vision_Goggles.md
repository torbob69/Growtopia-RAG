The **Immortal Night Vision Goggles** is an unsplicable face item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2026 on August 28, 2026.
##  Function
When equipped, the **Immortal Night Vision Goggles** grants the _Night Vision : Immortal Night Vision Goggles_ mod, which allows the player to eliminate the darkness effect created by dark background blocks. Additionally, unlike the  Night Vision Goggles, the Immortal Night Vision Goggles does not require a  D Battery to work.
