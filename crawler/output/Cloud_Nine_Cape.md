The **Cloud Nine Cape** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2020.
##  Function
Rise to Cloud Nine!

Your feet are back on solid ground!

When equipped, the **Cloud Nine Cape** grants the _Double Jump: Cloudyleap!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
