# Halloween Candy

A pack of treats given for performing tricks during Halloween. A hundred of these can be converted into a basket that you can use to trade for gifts from the Shady Salesman in Growganoth.
Properties
This item cannot be dropped or traded.
This item never drops any seeds.
This item has no use... by itself.
Data

Type: Component
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **3 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **4s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Trick or Treat Tasks

1-100 is obtained from doing Trick or Treat Tasks found in the Growtopia Store.
Double Tap/Click in Inventory

Made by double-tapping/clicking  Halloween Basket **(×1)** in one's inventory.
Always creates **100** item each.
The **Halloween Candy** is an unsplicable component which was added as part of Halloween Week 2022 on October 22. 2022.
