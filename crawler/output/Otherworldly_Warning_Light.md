The **Otherworldly Warning Light** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable animated foreground block which was added as part of the B.O.O. Update on May 1, 2018.
##  Function
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, the Otherworldly Warning Light can be toggled on or off by punching. When toggled on, the item will change its appearance whenever a Ghost is nearby, showing the Otherworldly Warning Light being lit up. As a light source, the Otherworldly Warning Light emits light in dark areas.
##  Trivia
  * As part of the Block Party 2 update, the quantity of Otherworldly Warning Lights obtained from a Spirit Storage Unit explosion was increased from 1 to 5.
