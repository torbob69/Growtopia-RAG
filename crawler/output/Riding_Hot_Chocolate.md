The **Riding Hot Chocolate** is an unsplicable feet item that was added as part of the Pre-WinterFest 2025 update on December 1, 2025. The item was formally introduced on December 25, 2025.
##  Function
Sweet, sweet victory!

Cup's empty!

When equipped, the **Riding Hot Chocolate** grants the _Speedy + High Jump : Riding Hot Chocolate_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and jump one tile higher.
