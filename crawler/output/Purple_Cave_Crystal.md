The **Purple Cave Crystal** is an unsplicable solid foreground pain block that was added during the Spelunkorama! update on May 2, 2016.
##  Function
Being a lava pain block, a Purple Cave Crystal knocks the player back and puts the player on fire until the player stops all motion when touched. A player will die after making contact with a lava pain block five times in quick succession (recovering from damage takes roughly 5 seconds), but a player with the _Fireproof_ mod may take up to ten consecutive hits.
