The **Pearl Tiki Crown** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Beach Party 2022 on June 24, 2022.
##  Function
A crowning glory!

Ex communicated

When equipped, the **Pearl Tiki Crown** grants the _Build Range: Pearl Tiki Crown_ mod, which allows the player to build one tile further.
