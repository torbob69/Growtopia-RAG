The **Horned Crown** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as June 2024's Item Of The Season on June 1st, 2024.
##  Function
Fueled by hatred!

Released of your pain!

When equipped, the **Horned Crown** grants the _Build Range + Punch Range: Horned Crown_ mod, which allows the player to build one tile further and punch one tile further.
