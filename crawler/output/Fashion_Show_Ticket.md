# Fashion Show Ticket

Your ticket to participate in Growtopia's Fashion Show!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **5 Hits** **4 Hits** – Restores after **8s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Fashion Show Ticket** for **7,500 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"Your ticket to participate Growtopia's Fashion Show!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Fashion Show Ticket** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as a part of Player Appreciation Week 2025 on September 25, 2025.
##  Function
The Fashion Show ticket is used to join a Fashion Show in a FASHIONSHOWGAME world by punching the Fashion Show Bouncer while wearing at least four different items. Joining the show will consume the ticket after the show starts. More information here.
Winning a Fashion Show will grant the player one of the following prizes:
  * 14DEViL's Friendly Doggy Leash
  * Bubble Gum Hair
  * Duckling Hood
  * Growmoji Lol Block
  * Growmoji Shades Block
  * Limousine Block
  * Purple Idol Hair
  * REALLY Really Big Jeans
  * Riding Soapbar
  * Selfie Stick
  * Sleeping Mask
