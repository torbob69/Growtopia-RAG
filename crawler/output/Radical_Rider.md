The **Radical Rider** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of the Voucher Dayz update on May 21, 2021.
##  Function
Ready to glide and be radical!

Less radical by half!

When equipped, the **Radical Rider** grants the _Speedy: Radical Rider_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land. Additionally, when the player dances (using the `/dance` command), it forces nearby players to dance as well.
