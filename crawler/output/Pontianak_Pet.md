##  Function
Thriller Time!

Resting Time!!

When equipped, the **Pontianak Pet** grants the _Build Range: Pontianak Pet_ mod, which allows the player to build one tile further. It also summons the pet Pontianak that follows the player around.
##  Trivia
  * The Pontianak Pet is a reference to Kuntilanak, also known as _Pontianak_ , a mythological creature in Indonesia, Malaysia and Singapore. The Pontianak is often depicted as a long-haired woman dressed in white, and it represents local variations of a vampire.
  * The Pontianak Pet's description is a reference to the lyrics of Girls Just Want to Have Fun, a song recorded and performed by American musician Robert Hazard, who released it as a single in 1979. The song is best known for the version of American singer Cyndi Lauper, who covered the song in 1983.
