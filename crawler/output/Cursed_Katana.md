# Cursed Katana

A once-honorable blade, this katana was renowned as a slayer of evil, but the void stares back, as they say, and centuries of carnage have twisted it to the ways of darkness! Beware this blade, for those who wield it... are forever cursed!

The **Cursed Katana** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as a part of Halloween Week 2019 on October 28, 2019.
##  Function
For honor... pah, for darkness!

Cast away, until it calls again...

When equipped, the **Cursed Katana** grants the _Punch Damage: Cursed!_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. It also replaces the player's punching animation with red slashes.
