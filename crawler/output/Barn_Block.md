##  Trivia
  * The Description of the Barn Block is a reference to an episode of The Simpsons called "Bart Of Darkness" where Homer tries to build a swimming pool but accidentally builds a barn instead. Then an Amish man appears and says "Tis a fine barn, but sure 'tis no pool, English."
