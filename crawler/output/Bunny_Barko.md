# Bunny Barko

##  Bunny Barko (Easter Week Villains)
**Bunny Barko** is a villain added during Easter Week 2015. It is an event-limited villain and can be summoned by consuming Crime Wave (chance) during Easter Week or by consuming a Cursed Jet Engine.
Cards he uses

Superpower - Egg Shield
Superpower - Frost Breath
Superpower - Frozen Mirror
Superpower - Crush
Superpower - Super Speed
Health: 15
Method: Bring Frost Breath, Megawatt Pulse, any Fire Superpower Card, any Lightning Superpower Card and any Muscle Superpower Card (Regeneration is recommended).
First use Frost Breath (he is never able to block this card), then play Megawatt Pulse (He cannot plays any card). If he plays Egg Shield, use Megawatt Pulse (might be countered). After he plays Super Speed, use either Ice Card or Muscle Card (he is unable to counter these cards). Follow this method until he is defeated.
Upon defeating, the possible rewards to receive are:
  * Henchman
  * Dark Bunny Mask
  * Villain Portrait - Bunny Barko
  * Superpower - Egg Shield
  * Super Logo - Bunny
  * Super Cape - Pink
  * Superhero Mask - Pink
  * Crimefighter Mask - Pink
  * Super Eye Mask - Pink
  * Super Shirt - Pink
  * Muscle Shirt - Pink
  * Superheroine Top - Pink
  * Super Tights - Pink
  * Heroic Boots - Pink
  * Heroic Tights - Pink
  * Carrot Earrings
  * Claws of Barko
  * Fancy Birdbath
  * Mess-Egg from the Past
  * Street Bunny Mask

The player will also receive the "A Dead Rabbit" achievement after defeating him.
##  Trivia
The name "Bunny Barko" is a reference to a movie called Donnie Darko.
