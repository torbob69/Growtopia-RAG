# Unicorn Madness (update)

Growtopia Gazette
> May 30: MAGICAL ADVENTURE TIME!
> Who doesn't love a beautiful and graceful creature of legend? The rumors are true, Unicorns have come to Growtopia!
> NEW: Prancing spiral horned mythical animals weren't the only thing added... what else?
**Unicorn Madness** is an update that mainly introduced a variety of unicorn-related items. The update was released on May 30, 2014.
