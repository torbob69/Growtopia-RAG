The **Emergency Dragon Claw** is an unsplicable pet hand item which was added as part of the Dragon Invasion update on February 4, 2019.
##  Function
Emergency dragonic wisdom!

Your medical wisdom is no more.

When equipped, the **Emergency Dragon Claw** grants the _XP Buff: Surgery_ mod, which allows the player to gain an extra 300 XP when successfully completing a surgery. It will also replace the player's punching animation with green flames and floating green crosses.
