The **Lovey Dovey Mantle** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as a part of Valentine's Week 2025 on February 13, 2025.
##  Function
Love is in the air!

Oh, oh, oh, oh!

When equipped, the **Lovey Dovey Mantle** grants the _Build Range: Lovey Dovey Mantle_ mod, which allows the player to build one tile further.
