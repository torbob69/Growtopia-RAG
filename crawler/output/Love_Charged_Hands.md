**Love Charged Hands** is a hand item that was added as part of Valentine's Week 2021 on February 13, 2021.
##  Function
Powered up with love!

Just normal love now!

When equipped, the **Love Charged Hands** grants the _Punch Damage: Love Charged_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. Additionally, when punching, the player will shoot out sparkly hearts.
