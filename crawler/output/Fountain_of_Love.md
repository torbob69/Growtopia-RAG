The **Fountain of Love** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable foreground block which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2023 on February 10, 2023.
##  Function
Being a toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block, a Fountain of Love can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Fountain of Love will change its appearance, showing an animation of water flowing out of the fountain.
