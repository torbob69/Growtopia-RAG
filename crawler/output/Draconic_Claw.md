The **Draconic Claw** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Lunar New Year 2020 on January 23, 2020. It can also be obtained from winning the raffle.
##  Function
Unleash the fire!

Another village spared!

When equipped, the **Draconic Claw** grants the _Punch Damage: Draconic Breath_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. It also replaces the player's punching animation with red flames.
