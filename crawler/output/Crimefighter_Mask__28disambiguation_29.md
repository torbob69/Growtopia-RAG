# Crimefighter Mask (disambiguation)

This is a **disambiguation** page.
There are more than one subjects in the **Growtopia Wiki** using this name.
**Crimefighter Mask** can be obtained randomly by splicing or defeating a villain.
There are 6 **Crimefighter Mask** which consists of 6 different colours.
  * Crimefighter Mask - Red
  * Crimefighter Mask - Blue
  * Crimefighter Mask - Green
  * Crimefighter Mask - Purple
  * Crimefighter Mask - Black
  * Crimefighter Mask - Gold

There are also 4 **Crimefighter Masks** that are based on St. Patrick's Week, Easter Week, Harvest Festival and Super Pineapple Party.
  * Crimefighter Mask - Emerald
  * Crimefighter Mask - Pink
  * Crimefighter Mask - Pineapple
  * Crimefighter Mask - Autumn
