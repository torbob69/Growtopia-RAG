##  Function
Cosmic energy!

Your hair, what happened to your hair?

When equipped, the **Legendary Comet Hair** grants the _Punch Range + Slow-Fall: Comet_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button. It will also constantly leave glowing round blue particles.
##  Trivia
  * The Legendary Comet Hair was able to be dropped and traded when it was first released. This glitch was patched soon after, leaving very few instances of dropped/displayed Legendary Comet Hairs in game.
