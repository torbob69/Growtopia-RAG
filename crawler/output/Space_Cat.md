##  Function
Set to stun!

Set to none!

When equipped, the **Space Cat** grants the _Double Jump & Punch Range : Space Cat_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air, punch one tile further, and switch between its wearable form and its morph form by wrenching oneself and clicking 'Transform'.
