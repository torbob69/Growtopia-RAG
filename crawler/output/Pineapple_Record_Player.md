The **Pineapple Record Player** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable animated foreground block which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2021 on June 3, 2021.
##  Function
<https://growtopiawiki.com/w/File:Pineapple_record_player.ogg>The Pineapple Record Player's music.
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Pineapple Record Player can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Pineapple Record Player will change its appearance, showing a 2-frame animation, and play a piece of music.
##  Trivia
  * In the game files, the Pineapple Record Player's music is named pineapple_record_player.
