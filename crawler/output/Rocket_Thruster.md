The **Rocket Thruster** is a splicable solid lava pain foreground block which was added alongside the Astro Pack on October 21, 2013.
##  Function
Being a lava pain block, a Rocket Thruster knocks the player back and puts the player on fire until the player stops all motion when touched. A player will die after making contact with a lava pain block five times in quick succession (recovering from damage takes roughly 5 seconds), but a player with the _Fireproof_ mod may take up to ten consecutive hits.
Step 18 of the  Quest For Fire requires the player to deliver 1,000 of this item.
The  Super Pineapple Party Exclusive Mission - Pucker up for more Pineapple fun! mission requires 200 of this item.
