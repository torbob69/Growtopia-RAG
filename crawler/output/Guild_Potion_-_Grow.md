# Guild Potion - Grow

A mighty guild potion! Only a Guild Leader can consume it, which will give its buff to ALL guild members. Guild Potion Buff: 5% Grow time reduction! - Buff lasts for a limited time.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **2 Hits** **2 Hits** – Restores after **2s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Guild Potion: Black Mixer **(×5)** ,
Guild Potion: White Mixer **(×15)** and
Unearthly Chemical **(×1)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Guild Potion - Grow** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Guilds update on April 1, 2018.
##  Function
The Guild Leader released the magic!

The effects of the Guild Potion have ended!

When consumed by a guild leader, all guild members are granted a 5% growth time reduction when planting seeds. This effect lasts for 12 hours.
