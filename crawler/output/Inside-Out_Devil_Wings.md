# Inside-Out Devil Wings

You don't jump higher than with normal devil wings, but man, you DO look pretty cool, like an inside-out devil. Wait... doesn't that make you an angel?!

The **Inside-Out Devil Wings** is an unsplicable back item that was added on the third day of Player Appreciation Week 2017.
##  Function
You can jump in mid-air! It's like flying, but less.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Inside-Out Devil Wings** grants the _Double Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
