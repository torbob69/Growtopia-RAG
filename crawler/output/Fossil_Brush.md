# Fossil Brush

You can use this to gently brush a fossil before you pick it up, improving its quality. A Fossil Brush will be destroyed upon use (fossils are very rough until you brush them!). If you pick up a fossil, your finger oils will make a Fossil Brush useless, so be sure you brush before collecting!

##  Function
A Fossil Brush is used to brush a Fossil by clicking on it. However, it can only be used when the Fossil dropped directly from a Fossil Rock and hasn't been previously picked up. It will then turn the Fossil into a Polished Fossil.
