Every purchase will give **1** item.
**Vending Hub - Checkout Counter** is a foreground block that was introduced on January 2, 2020 as part of the Store Wars update.
##  Function
The hub allows visitors to the world to have a comprehensive list of all items being sold in the designated row/column of DigiVend Machines in that world, off of which the visitor can make purchases without having to visit the DigiVend Machines themselves. The list is able to be filtered solely by name and does not cover all items in the world, just those in the area within the visible dashed lines.
