The **GHX Speaker** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable animated foreground block which was released alongside the Cinema Pack on May 16, 2014.
##  Function
<https://growtopiawiki.com/w/File:About_theme.ogg>GHX Speaker music.
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a GHX Speaker can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a GHX Speaker will change its appearance, showing a 2-frame animation, and play a piece of music.
##  Trivia
  * The item's name and description are a reference to THX sound systems, and the famous (or, infamous) Deep Note sound which is known for being an incredibly loud and rich sound (almost 'deafening' in a theater).
  * When the GHX Speaker was first implemented, there was a glitch where a seed may drop when a GHX Speaker was destroyed. Being rarity 999, a GHX Speaker tree would take over 31 years to fully grow. This glitch was patched soonly after, making the GHX Speaker seed unobtainable and an immensely rare glitched seed. By visiting the world GHXSPEAKERSEED, you can see a GHX Speaker tree; the tree is also fully grown since rarity of GHX Speaker was also removed, reducing the tree's grow time to 1 hour.
  * It plays the same music as the world menu screen.
