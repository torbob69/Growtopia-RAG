The **Adventure Item - Rope** is a splicable non-solid adventure item foreground block which was added alongside the Adventure Pack on June 14, 2014.
##  Function
As a type of adventure item, the Adventure Item - Rope may work in conjunction with the Gateway To Adventure and the Adventure Rope Piton. A player who has picked up an Adventure Item - Rope will be able to latch onto an Adventure Rope Piton, in a similar manner to how one may use the Climbing Boots to latch onto a Climbing Wall.
