# Cooking (update)/Important Items & Ingredients

< Cooking (update)
Items: RecipeHome Oven: Splicing Pocket Lighter Seed and Air Duct Seed.
Food Grinder: Combining 10 Synthetic Chemical, 10 Mysterious Chemical and 10 Chemical G in any Chemical Combiner.
Salt Block: Splicing Texas Limestone Seed and Stalactite Seed.
Fish Chunk: Cut any fish on a Cutting Board. A Fish Chunk is given for every 10lbs of fish cut.
Beef: Cut a Cow on a Cutting Board.
Chicken Meat: Cut a Chicken on a Cutting Board.
Onion: Splicing Foliage Seed and Venus Guytrap Seed.
Lemon: Splicing Pastel Yellow Block and Yellow Wallpaper Seed.
Avocado: Possible drop from an Ultra Pinata.
Cherry: Splicing Dark Red Block Seed and Blueberry Seed.
Chocolate Sprinkles Block: Combining 25 Sugar Cane, 10 Candy Cane Block and 10 Chocolate Swirl Block in a Microwave on a Chocolate Block.
###  Salsa
Mix a Habanero Pepper and a Tomato in a cooking oven. The item itself is not obtainable, but other items can be added to the Salsa to cook an item.
###  **Other Ingredients**
  * Blueberry
  * Honey
  * Swiss Cheese Block
  * Habanero Pepper
  * Rice
  * Apple
