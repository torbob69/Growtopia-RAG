##  Function
Butter be fast!

Spread out!

When equipped, the **Home Made Butter Lamb** grants the _Speedy : Home Made Butter Lamb_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.
##  Trivia
  * The Home Made Butter Lamb is one of the three items added in Easter Week 2021 which was based on the real-life Easter tradition with the same name, alongside the Detective Butter Lamb and Failed Butter Lamb.
    * In real life, a butter lamb is made by sculpting butter into a lamb, either by hand or in a lamb-shaped mold. This Easter food traditionally accompanies the Easter meal for many Russian, Slovenian and Polish Catholics.
