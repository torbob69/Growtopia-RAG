The **Lion Dancer Mask - Red** is an unsplicable hair item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2023 on September 21, 2023.
##  Function
When equipped, the Lion Dancer Mask - Red serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
When equipped with Lion Dancer Cape - Red, Lion Dancer Shirt - Red, Lion Dancer Pants - Red, and Lion Dancer Feet - Red, the player will gain an extra pair of legs resembling a Lion Dancer costume.
