The **Rumah Adat Asmat** is an unsplicable solid foreground block which was added as part of the Garuda's Glory update on August 26, 2022.
##  Function
When placed, the Rumah Adat Asmat serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The Rumah Adat Asmat is a reference to a Jew, a traditional house of the Asmat tribe in South Papua.
  * Upon release, the item was named Rumah Adat Asmet. This typographical error was fixed soon after.
