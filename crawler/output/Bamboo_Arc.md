# Bamboo Arc

A wonderful work of bamboo architecture!

Render Type: Horizontal

Consumption

Consume a(n)  Garuda Chest **(x1)**.
May yield **5** item each.

The **Bamboo Arc** is an unsplicable foreground platform block which was added as part of the Garuda's Glory update on August 26, 2023.
##  Function
A Bamboo Arc behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath.
##  Trivia
  * The Bamboo Arc is references to the bamboo structures at Green School Bali designed by the company IBUKU.
