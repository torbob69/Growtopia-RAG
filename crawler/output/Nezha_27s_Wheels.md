The **Nezha's Wheels** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Lunar New Year 2026 on February 19, 2026.
##  Function
Fired up!

Lost the fire!

When equipped, the **Nezha's Wheels** grants the _Speedy + Light Source: Nezha's Wheels_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and emit light in dark areas.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Nezha set, which consists of the following items:

    * Nezha's Pauldrons (chest)

    * Nezha's Vest (shirt)

    * Nezha's Wheels (feet)
