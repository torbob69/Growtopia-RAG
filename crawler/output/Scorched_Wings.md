The **Scorched Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2023 on October 19, 2023.
##  Function
Burn Buddy Burn!

Spooky Inferno!

When equipped, the **Scorched Wings** grants the _Double Jump + Fireproof: Scorched Wings_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and take 50% less damage from lava pain blocks.
