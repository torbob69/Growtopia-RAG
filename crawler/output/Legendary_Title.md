# Legendary Title

The **Legendary Title** is a legendary item that gives the player a larger name with a legendary orb replacing the flag icon. It also adds "of Legend" to the end of the player's name. It is obtained by completing the Quest of Honor and can be temporarily removed for 24 hours by consuming a Golden Apple or by unchecking 'of Legend' from the Title window which can be found by wrenching oneself.
Legendary Quest Reward

Approach the  Legendary Wizard and complete the  Quest For Honor.
##  Quest Steps
  1. Bring 2,000 Sand.
  2. Kill 100 players in PvP via a Game Generator.
  3. Break 5,000 Blocks.
  4. Bring 600 Display Boxes.
  5. Plant seeds that add up to 50,000 rarities.
  6. Earn 50 Growtokens
  7. Bring 3 Golden Diapers.
  8. Earn 10,000 XP.
  9. Bring 1,000 Tombstones.
  10. Deliver 100,000 gems.
  11. Break 100,000 rarity worth of blocks.
  12. Complete 100 successful surgeries.
  13. Collect from 1,000 providers.
  14. Bring 3 Golden Heart Crystals.
  15. Collect 100,000 rarity worth of fruit from trees.
  16. Bring a Growie Award or a Neptune's Crown.
  17. Bring 3 Super Fireworks.
  18. Bring 10 pairs of Rainbow Wings.
  19. Bring 3 Birth Certificates.
  20. Deliver the Legendary Orb.

##  Trivia
  * ADMex1 was the first player to complete the Quest For Honor and receive the Legendary Title.
  * On July 28, 2022 Step 16 was changed to allow players to use a Neptune's Crown in place of the Growie Award.
