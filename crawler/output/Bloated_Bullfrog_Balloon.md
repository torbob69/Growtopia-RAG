##  Function
Hey now, you're an all star!

Shape of an L on your forehead!

When equipped, the **Bloated Bullfrog Balloon** grants the _Punch Range + Slow Fall : Bloated Bullfrog Balloon_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
