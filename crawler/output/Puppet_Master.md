The **Puppet Master** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Wonder Week 2025 - Day 6 on October 11, 2025.
##  Function
Pulling your strings!

No strings on me!

When equipped, the **Puppet Master** grants the _Double Jump + Slow Fall: Puppet Master_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Puppet set, which consists of the following items:

    * Puppet Master (Back)

    * Puppet Face Paint (Face)
