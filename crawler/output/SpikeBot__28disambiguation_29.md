The **SpikeBot - Level 1** is an unsplicable NPC that was added as part of the Cyblocks and Growmojis update on March 1, 2018.
## Function
When wrenched, the player can apply CyBot Commands, causing it to move/kill. It can be leveled up to level 4, where each level up allows the bot to take an extra 5 commands. The bot is deadly to any player (even the owner) who comes in contact with it.
