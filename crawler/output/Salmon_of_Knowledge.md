##  Trivia
  * The Salmon of Knowledge is based on a mythical creature of the same name in Irish mythology. In the tale of _The Boyhood Deeds of Fionn_, the eponymous salmon was said to have eaten nine hazelnuts that fell into the Well of Wisdom, thus gaining all the world's knowledge.
