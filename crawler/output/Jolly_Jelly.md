The **Jolly Jelly** is an unsplicable solid trampoline foreground block which was added as part of Halloween Week 2022 on October 22, 2022.
##  Function
Jumping on a trampoline block such as a Jolly Jelly will cause the player to bounce, and by holding the jump button, the player can jump even higher. The _High Jump_ mod also boosts one's jump height on a trampoline block.
