The **Articulated Reading Lamp** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable foreground block which was added in the Summer Clash update on June 1, 2018.
##  Function
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, an Articulated Reading Lamp can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, an Articulated Reading Lamp will change its appearance, showing a 2-framed animation. As a light source, an Articulated Reading Lamp emits light in dark areas.
