**The Bartender** is a non-solid NPC foreground block which was added as part of the Beach Party 2022 on June 24, 2022. The Bartender can be found in the world BEACHPARTYGAME.
##  Function
Punching The Bartender during Beach Party opens a dialog box, allowing a player to purchase pearl chests from The Bartender.
