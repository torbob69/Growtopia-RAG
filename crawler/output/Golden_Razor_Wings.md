The **Golden Razor Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added on 2 March 2017. One can be obtained from purchasing it in the Store for 100,000 gems as March 2017's Item Of The Month.
##  Function
You can jump in mid-air! It's like flying, but less.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Golden Razor Wings** grants the _Double Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
##  Trivia
  * Golden Razor Wings are incredibly similar to the Da Vinci Wings and Legendary Wings in terms of animation style and texture size.
  * This item is the first Item Of The Month released in Growtopia.
  * Furthermore, it is also the first Item Of The Month in the back clothing slot.
