The **Mariachi Suit** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2017 on May 5, 2017.
##  Function
When equipped, the Mariachi Suit serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia

    * Mariachi Hat (hat)

    * Mariachi Suit (shirt)

    * Mariachi Pants (pants)

  * The  Mariachi Bobble Head can be seen wearing the full mariachi set.
