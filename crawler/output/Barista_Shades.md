The **Barista Shades** is an unsplicable face item which was added as part of Beach Party 2022 on August 6, 2022.
##  Function
When equipped, the Barista Shades serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Barista set, which consists of the following items:

    * Barista Hair (Hair)

    * Barista Shades (Face)

    * Barista Shirt (Shirt)

    * Barista Pants (Pants)

    * Barista Shoes (Feet)
