The **Royal Astral Crest** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as January 2024's Item Of The Season on January 2, 2024.
##  Function
Power of the stars!

Final Eclipse!

When equipped, the **Royal Astral Crest** grants the _Double Jump + Punch Range: Royal Astral Crest_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and punch one tile further.
