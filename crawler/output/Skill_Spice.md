The **Skill Spice** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Role Up! update on September 17, 2018.
##  Function
Reduce your skill fails by half in both Surgery and Startopia missions.

Your stomach's too spicey.

When consumed, this item grants the _Spicey Skills_ food buff mod to the player, which halves the player's chances of getting a skill fail in surgery and/or Startopia missions for 30 minutes.
