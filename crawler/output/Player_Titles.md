# Player Titles

**Player Titles** encompass a range of personalized customization that players can add to their names, most of which can be toggled on/off in the Titles button upon wrenching oneself.
##  Legendary Title
The **Legendary Title** was introduced as part of the Legendary update on August 22, 2014. To obtain, the player needs to complete the Quest of Honor. When equipped, it enlarges the player's name with a  Legendary Orb replacing the flag icon and adds "of Legend" to the end of the player's name. The title can be temporarily removed for 24 hours by consuming a Golden Apple or by unchecking 'of Legend' from the Title window which can be found by wrenching oneself.

A player with the Legendary Title equipped

##  Level 125 Title
The **Level 125 Title** was introduced as part of the Player Appreciation week Day 3 on August 7, 2018. To obtain, the player needs to reach level 125. When equipped, it changes the player name's color to transparent blue and pulses slowly.

A player with the Level 125 Title equipped

##  Dr. Title
The **Dr. Title** was introduced as part of the Blocktor's Orders! update on June 13, 2019. To obtain, the player needs to complete 3,000 surgeries while wearing the Angel of Mercy's Wings (progress is displayed in the description of the wings, for every surgery done, it counts as an Angelic Healing). When equipped, it changes the name's color to red and adds "Dr." before the player's name.

A player with the Dr. Title equipped

##  Mentor Title
The **Mentor Title** was introduced as part of Mentorship & Referral update on May 1, 2020. To obtain, the player needs to have 1,000 apprentices reach level 20. When equipped, it replaces the player's flag with a  Dumb Mentor Title. As of date, no player has achieved the title yet.
##  Grow4Good Title
The **Grow4Good Title** was introduced as part of Grow4Good Week 2022 on April 22, 2022. To obtain, the player must be one of the top 20 contributors during the Grow4Good Week event. When equipped, it changes the player's name into a mix of pastel colors for every letter with a  Grow4Good Title replacing the flag icon and the player name pulsing slowly.

A player with the Grow4Good Title equipped

##  Content Creator Badge and TikTok Creator Badge
The **Content Creator Badge** and **TikTok Creator Badge** was introduced on November 1, 2023. To obtain, the player must be part of the Content Creator Program. When equipped, it replaces the player's flag to a blue checkmark if the player is an official Instagram, YouTube, or Twitch content creator or a black checkmark with red and blue borders if the player is an official TikTok content creator.

Players wearing the Tiktok Creator and Content Creator Badges

##  Thanksgiving Title
The **Thanksgiving Title** was introduced as part of Thanksgiving Week 2023 on November 17, 2023. To obtain, the player must be the top player on the leaderboard in the fight against Sgt. Stuffins at SGTSTUFFINSREVENGE. When equipped, it adds an  Organic Turkey Feather on both sides of the player's name.

A player with the Thanksgiving Title equipped

##  10-Year Anniversary Title (Old Timer Title)
The **10th Year Anniversary Title / 10-Year Anniversary Title / Old Timer Title** was introduced as part of Anniversary Week on January 25, 2024. To obtain, the player must have owned their account for at least 10 years or 3650 days, be level 50 or above, and have played for over 1,000 hours total. When equipped, it adds a  Party Popper on both sides of the player's name.

A player with the 10-Year Anniversary Title equipped.

##  Party Animal Title
The **Party Animal Title** was introduced as part of Anniversary Week on January 25, 2024. To obtain, the player must be one of the top 20 players on the Anniversary Global Leaderboard during Anniversary Week. When equipped, it changes the player's name into a repeating pattern of purple, yellow, and green for every letter.

A player with the Party Animal Title equipped.

##  Gold/Silver/Bronze Grow Royal of the Month Title
The **Gold Grow Royal of the Month Title** was introduced as part of the Grow Pass second update on March 6, 2024. To obtain, the player must ranked first/second/third on the Grow Pass leaderboard for accumulating the highest/second highest/third highest number of Grow Pass points during the month. When equipped, it adds a // on both ends of the player's name.

A player with the Gold Grow Royal of the Month equipped.

A player with the Silver Grow Royal of the Month equipped.

A player with the Bronze Grow Royal of the Month equipped.

##  Award-Winning Title
The **Award-Winning Title** was introduced as part of Anniversary Week 2025 on January 16, 2025. To obtain, the player must solve the Award-Winning Title riddle. The player can toggle this title on and off upon wrenching oneself and tapping on Title. When equipped, the Award-Winning Title adds a laurel wreath on both sides of the player's name.

A player with the Award-Winning Title equipped.

##  Pet Battle League Organizer and Pet Battle League Moderator
The **Pet Battle League Organizer** and **Pet Battle League Moderator** was introduced on May 15, 2025. To obtain, the player must be chosen as the League Captain or League Moderator. When equipped, it shows the  Pet Battle League Organizer (for League Captains) and  Pet Battle League Moderator (for League Moderators) on the left of the player's name and moves other flags to the left.

A player wearing the Pet Battle League Organizer title

A player wearing the Pet Battle League Moderator title

##  Royal Winter Title
The **Royal Winter Title** was introduced as part of WinterFest 2025 on December 18, 2025. To obtain, the player must spend 18,000 seals on Branch 5 of Winterfest Glory Reward. The player can toggle this title on and off upon wrenching oneself and tapping on Title. When equipped, the Royal Winter Title adds a rainbow-shooting star on both sides of the player's name.

A player wearing the Royal Winter Title.
