The **Lucky Pinata Hat** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2014 on May 2, 2014.
##  Function
When equipped, the **Lucky Pinata Hat** allows the player to get a random bonus prize from smashing an Ultra Pinata. The Lucky Pinata Hat will transform into an Unlucky Pinata Hat.
