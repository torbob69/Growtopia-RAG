# Legendary Dragon Knight's Wings

Bestowed upon only the bravest, most prestigious, most skilled Dragon Knights, these wings are so rare that in all the centuries since the first Dragon Knights arose, barely a handful have been awarded. Will you be the next to bear their illustrious might? To take to the skies and soar like the very dragons you hunt? If you possess these wings, there's little question you've proven your worth - and dedication.

The **Legendary Dragon Knight's Wings** is an unsplicable and untradable back item that was added as part of the Dragon Invasion update on February 4, 2019.
##  Quest Steps
  1. Deliver 2,000 Obsidians.
  2. Kill 100 players in Games or Deathmatch events.
  3. Destroy 10,000 blocks.
  4. Deliver 1,000 Knight Helmets.
  5. Plant 100,000 rarity worth of trees.
  6. Earn 50 Growtokens.
  7. Deliver 10 Blanket Capes.
  8. Earn 20,000 XP.
  9. Deliver 800 Blazing Electro Wings.
  10. Deliver 140,000 Gems.
  11. Destroy 200,000 rarity worth of blocks.
  12. Perform 100 successful surgeries.
  13. Train 10 fish.
  14. Deliver 10 Autumn Wings.
  15. Harvest 200,000 rarity of fruits from trees.
  16. Deliver 1 Golden Dragon Statue or 1 Neptune's Armor
  17. Deliver 5 Chaos Dragon.
  18. Deliver 1 Draconic Wings.
  19. Deliver 10 Dragon Knight's Chestplate.
  20. Deliver 1 Legendary Orb.

##  Function
Legendary dragon warrior!

Better not get burninated!

When equipped, the **Legendary Dragon Knight's Wings** grants the _Double Jump: Legendary Dragon Knight's Wings_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
##  Trivia
  * When the Legendary Dragon Knight's Wings was first introduced, 100 Blanket Capes were required to complete the 7th step; however, the requirement was later changed to 10 after the developers realized how difficult it was for players to gather 100 of them.
  * ELIGRO was the first player to complete the Quest Of The Sky and receive the Legendary Dragon Knight's Wings.
