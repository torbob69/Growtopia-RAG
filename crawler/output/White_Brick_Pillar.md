# White Brick Pillar

You'd think adding a pillar to a boring white wall made of boring white bricks would make it more exciting, but... turns out it's a boring pillar, too. Dang.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.

Boss Goo Reward

Bonus reward from exploding a  Spirit Storage Unit with a  Boss Goo.

The **White Brick Pillar** is an unsplicable background block which was added as part of the B.O.O. Update on May 1, 2018.
##  Function
When placed, the White Brick Pillar serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * As part of the Block Party 2 update, the quantity of White Brick Pillars obtained from a Spirit Storage Unit explosion was increased from 1 to 10.
