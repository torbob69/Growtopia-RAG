# E-Z Rod

Equip this and then use bait on water next to you to go fishing! All you have to do is wait for a bite, and then punch the fish before it escapes! If you miss, your bait is wasted, so punch fast. This is a beginner's fishing rod - it keeps fish on the line twice as long as other rods, so it's easy as pie.
