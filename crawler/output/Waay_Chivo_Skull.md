The **Waay Chivo Skull** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2021 on October 21, 2021.
##  Function
Way to goat man!

De-goated!

When equipped, the **Waay Chivo Skull** grants the _Punch Range: Waay Chivo Skull_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further.
