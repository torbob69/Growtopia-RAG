The **Riding Comet** is a splicable feet item which was added as part of the original Night Of The Comet update on November 27, 2014.
##  Function
You can move faster!

You are slow again.

When equipped, the **Riding Comet** grants the _Speedy_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land. Equipping a Riding Comet also leaves a trail of green comet dusts behind the player.
