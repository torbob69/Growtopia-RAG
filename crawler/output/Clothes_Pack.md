# Clothes Pack
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Clothes Pack** for **50 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"Why not look the part? You'll get three randomly chosen wearable items. Some may even have special powers..."
Every purchase will give **3 randomly chosen low-rarity clothing** items.
The **Clothes Pack** is an item pack. It consists of 3 randomly chosen low-rarity clothing items (Rarity 10 and below) .
