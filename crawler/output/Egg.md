The **Egg** is an unsplicable consumable which was added alongside the Farm Pack on July 6, 2013.
##  Function
You have egg on your face.

You washed your face!

When consumed on a player, the Egg grants the _Egged_ mod, which turns the player's skin yellow for 3 minutes. An Egg can also be cooked on a Cooking Oven, and takes around 59s to cook perfectly.

### Silkworm Food     _Main article:Guide:Silkworm Care_
This consumable can be fed to a Silkworm to give it a white color, thus allowing it to produce 1–10  Silk Thread - White per harvest.
