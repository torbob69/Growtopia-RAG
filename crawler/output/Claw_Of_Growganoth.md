# Claw Of Growganoth

A creeping being from the darkest depths of the void! Summoned by the fractured claw of its master, is this monstrous thing truly a creation of Growganoth... or something darker?
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be transmuted.

Daily Challenge

Place in the top three of the **Growganoth's Game** Guild Daily Challenge, only if the player had contributed points to the challenge.
The **Claw Of Growganoth** is an unsplicable hand which was added as part of Halloween Week 2019.
##  Function
The CLAAAAAW's coming for ya!

Oh, it won't be too happy about this!

When equipped, this item grants the _Growganoth's Claw!_ mod, which summons a mini-version of the Eye Of Growganoth pet that replaces the player's punch, shooting out small versions of itself from its mouth.
