The **Sale Television** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Black Friday 2023 on November 24, 2023.
##  Function
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Sale Television can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Sale Television will change its appearance, showing a 2-framed animation.
