##  Function
You can fly!! Well, you can jump higher than normal.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Soda Jetpack** grants the _High Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump one tile higher and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
##  Trivia
  * Soda Jetpack provides slow fall effect as well, although it is not stated in its label. Similar to the  Super Squirt Gun Jetpack and  Super Squirt Gun Jetpack Pants.
