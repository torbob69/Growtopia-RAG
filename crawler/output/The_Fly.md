##  Function
Buzz, buzzzz, buzz!

Buzz off!

When equipped, the **The Fly** grants the _Double-Jump: Buzzzz!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
##  Trivia
  * The Fly is a reference to the scientist André Delambre from the American science fiction horror film named The Fly. In the film, André Delambre is experimenting with a matter-transporter. After some successful tests on inanimate objects and animals, he decides to build a man-sized pair of chambers. However, when André tries to transport himself, a fly gets caught in the chamber with him, resulting in the mixing of their atoms.
