No summer holiday is complete without a dip in the water with an inflatable Dolphin! This one is extra inflated so it's also quite bouncy. Don't worry, it won't mind if you jump up and down on it!

The **Inflatable Dolphin** is an unsplicable solid trampoline foreground block which was added as part of SummerFest 2020 on July 3, 2020.
##  Function
Jumping on a trampoline block such as a Inflatable Dolphin will cause the player to bounce, and by holding the jump button, the player can jump even higher. The _High Jump_ mod also boosts one's jump height on a trampoline block.
