The **Anomalizing Pristine Cultivator** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Summer Clash Series 1.
##  Function
Anomalizing Pristine Cultivators are used to score points during Speedy Splicers. While equipping this item, each tree spliced yields 80 points. Pristine anomalizers, such as this item, may sometimes break upon usage during their respective Grow Events.
