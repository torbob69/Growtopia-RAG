The **Dumb Friend** is a button which will show a list of the player's friends, ignored players, and the player's Guild. A player is allowed to add up to 400 friends.
##  Friends Menu
When a player clicks an online friend's name, they will see two buttons: an unfriend button and a "Warp to location" button. The message and warp button are only shown when the friend makes their location public.
##  Offline/Ignored Menu
This shows the player's offline friends and ignored players. If the player clicks on a friend, they will see an unfriend option. If the player clicks on an ignored player, they will see an unignore option.
##  Friend Options
This is where players can choose the following options:
  * To make their location public or private to friends.
  * To turn friend notifications on or off (i.e. see when players log on or off).

##  Edit Friends
Here, players can select multiple friends at once to choose more personalized options, such as:
  * Unfriend, mute/unmute, or block/unblock trade with a friend(s)

##  Show Apprentices
This is where it shows the mentor Milestone Rewards, My Recruits and Help regarding the Mentorship & Referral update.
##  Show Guild Members
This shows the player's Guild Statement, Guild Home, Guild Member Options, online Guild Members, and if the player is the Guild Leader, the ability to edit the Guild Statement.
##  Community Hub
This shows Featured Worlds, past and current World Of The Days, Top Rated and Top Worlds of the day, overall Top Worlds, Official YouTube Channel, and Content Creators.
##  Trade History
Here, players can find the 10 most recent trades that they have completed with other players.
##  Trivia
  * If a player unfriend somebody, a message will appear saying: "_Ok, you are no longer friends with**[Player's Name]**."_

  * The Dumb Friend is a reference to Hamumu's website slogan _Dumb games for smart kids_.
