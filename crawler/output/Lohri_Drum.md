The **Lohri Drum** is an unsplicable solid trampoline foreground block that was added as part of Harvest Festival 2024 on September 12, 2024.
##  Function
Jumping on a trampoline block such as a Lohri Drum will cause the player to bounce, and by holding the jump button, the player can jump even higher. The _High Jump_ mod also boosts one's jump height on a trampoline block.
