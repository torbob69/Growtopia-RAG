The **Raijin's Taiko** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Lunar New Year 2024 on February 1, 2024.
##  Function
Thunder Thunder Thunder-Attack!

Out of charge!

When equipped, the **Raijin's Taiko** grants the _Double Jump + Punch Range: Raijin's Taiko_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and punch one tile further.
