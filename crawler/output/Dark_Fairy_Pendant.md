The **Dark Fairy Pendant** is an unsplicable pet chest item that was added as part of Halloween Week 2019.
##  Function
Get in with the wrong crowd!

Back on the straight and narrow!

When equipped, it grants the _Dark Fairy Pendant_ mod which summons a dark fairy that follows the player around while emitting dark purple sparkles from the player's body.
