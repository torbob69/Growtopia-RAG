This item does not have any recipe because it is no longer, or was never obtainable.
The **Clam Chest** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Beach Party 2022 on June 24, 2022.
##  Function
When broken, a Clam Chest may give one of the following rewards to the player who broke the Clam Chest:
  * Pearls Currency (×1-100)

##  Trivia
  * As of June 25, 2022, the Clam Chest no longer drops Pearls Currency and Pearl Chest when destroyed and will be automatically picked up by the player who destroys the Clam Chest.
  * As of August 06, 2022, the Clam Chest is now visible to everyone.
