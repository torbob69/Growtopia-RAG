The **Anomalizing Pristine Hammer** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Summer Clash Series 1.
##  Function
Anomalizing Pristine Hammers are used to score points during Block Bashers. While equipping this item, each block punched yields 50 points. Pristine anomalizers, such as this item, may sometimes break upon usage during their respective Grow Events.
