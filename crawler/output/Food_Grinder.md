# Food Grinder

The high speed blades and crushers in this device can grind up some foods into more ground-up foods! Just tap your food on the Food Grinder to begin.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can't be destroyed - smashing it will return it to your backpack if you have room!
This item has special properties you can adjust with the Wrench.

The **Food Grinder** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of the Cooking update on April 1, 2017.

##  Trivia
  * Attempting to use a Food Grinder while there is a player standing at the same tile as the grinder (even if it's the player themself) will result in the error message, "Somebody is in the way of the Food Grinder!"
  * Attempting to use an ineligible item on a Food Grinder will result in the message, "[That's not grindable]"
