The **Golden Halo** is a splicable consumable which has been added since Growtopia's launch.
##  Function
You have been good.

You're falling out of favor.

When consumed on a player, this item grants the _Angelic Halo_ mod, which adds a golden halo over the player's head for 30 minutes.
It can be fed to a Silkworm to give it a yellow color. Additionally, this will give the silkworm a golden halo above its head.

##  Trivia
  * The Holy Jeans grants a similar halo when equipped. Unlike the Golden Halo, it lasts for an indefinite time, until the jeans are removed.
  * The Golden Halo is one of the clothing items required to earn the _Stupid Cupid_ achievement, alongside the Ruby Necklace, Angel Wings, Heartbow, and Diaper.
