In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Bunny Boba Block **(×20)** ,

Easter Egg - Golden **(×1)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Big Fluffin' Giga-Ears** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Easter Week 2023 on April 7, 2023.
##  Function
Hop to the top!

Toppled down!

When equipped, the **Big Fluffin' Giga-Ears** grants the _High Jump & Slow Fall: Big Fluffin' Giga-Ears!_ mod, which allows the player to jump one tile higher and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
