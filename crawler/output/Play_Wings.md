# Play Wings

Super special wings that are only for Play!
Properties
This item cannot be dropped or traded.
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Back – Clothes
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: Full Collision
Hardness: **0 Hits** **0 Hits** – Restores after **0s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Exclusive Item

This item was given to **Play** for their contributions as a voluntary moderator.
The **Play Wings** is an unsplicable back item.
##  Function
You can jump in mid-air! It's like flying, but less.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Play Wings** grants the _Double Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
##  Trivia
  * The Play Wings was the first wings without an animation to be added in Growtopia.
