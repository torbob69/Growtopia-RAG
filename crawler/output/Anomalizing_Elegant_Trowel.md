The **Anomalizing Elegant Trowel** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the Role Up! update.
##  Function
Anomalizing Elegant Trowels are used to score points during Block Builders. While equipping this item, each block placed down yields 220 points. Elegant anomalizers, such as this item, may rarely break upon usage during their respective Grow Events.
