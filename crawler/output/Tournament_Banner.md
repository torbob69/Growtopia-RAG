The **Tournament Banner** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of The Grand Tournament update. One can be obtained from Prize Ladder Level 11 and Level 14.
##  Function
### Tournament Rankings
When punched or wrenched while a Tournament is active, a list of rankings in the current (if any) or previous tournament is displayed. During other times, it will display the following message instead:
"_The Grand Tournament starts on the 7th of every month._
Be sure to visit TOURNAMENT to buy a ticket now, because they stop selling tickets when the Tournament begins!_"_
