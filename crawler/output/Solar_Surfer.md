The **Solar Surfer** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Wonder Week 2024 Day 1 on October 4, 2024.
##  Function
Stick to it, no matter the squalls!

Chart your own course!

When equipped, the **Solar Surfer** grants the _Speedy + High Jump : Solar Surfer_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and jump one tile higher.
##  Trivia
  * Solar Surfer and its description are a reference to a surf with the same name, which is owned by a fictional character called Jim Hawkins from a Disney-made film Treasure Planet.
