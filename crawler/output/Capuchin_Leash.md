# Capuchin Leash

An adorable pet Capuchin armed to the back teeth with enough bananas to feed an entire planet of apes! Remember what to say if they try to steal your coffee... Capuchin-no!

Won as prize in **any game** (except for Hall of Mirrors and Waste a Ticket) in the Carnival.
The **Capuchin Leash** is an unsplicable pet hand item that was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2020 Day 6 on August 22, 2020.
##  Function
Ban'armed to the back teeth!

Stop monkey'ing around now!

When equipped, the **Capuchin Leash** grants the _Punch Damage : Capuchin Leash!_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. A capuchin pet will also appear and rest on the player's shoulder. Additionally, it throws tiny banana projectiles while punching.
