The **365-Day Premium Subscription Token** is a consumable that was released as part of 2018's Player Appreciation Week.
##  Function
When consumed, the player will receive one year worth of benefits. They will receive 70 season tokens (if there is one running), 1 World Lock and 9600 gems every day. They will also obtain access to two exclusive skin colors, have all XP doubled and tree growth time reduced and will be given the Monthly Subscriber's Item every month.
##  Trivia
  * On 11th January 2019, the following was updated to Premium Subscriptions:
    * Players with a Premium Subscription now have a chance to get a new exclusive item every month. Every player with an active Premium Subscription (earned by consuming either a 30-Day or 365-Day token) who logs in between the 1st and the 5th of __ month will be entered into a draw. One player will be chosen to design and name a single wearable item or block for the game with the following rules:
      * No mods
      * No animations
      * No special effects
      * No player name reference (that's reserved for the sponsored items!)
      * If the chosen player does not respond within 48 hours of selection, a new candidate will be chosen from the same pool.
    * On the 1st to the 5th of the following month, all players with an active Premium Subscription (earned by consuming either a 30-Day or 365-Day token) will be automatically given the custom item. See more _here_
  * As of 1 April 2019, the 1-Year Subscription Token replaced the 365-Day Premium Subscription Token in the Growtopia Store. Eventhough they share the same sprite " ", the 1-Year Subscription Token is untradeable.
  * As of 24 February 2021, 1-Year Subscription Token, 365-Day Premium Subscription Token and 30-Day Premium Subscription Token now rewards the player 4000 Gems daily, previously 2500.
  * As of an unknown date, the 1-Year Subscription Token and 365-Day Premium Subscription Token now rewards the player 7000 Gems daily, previously 4000.
  * As part of Player Appreciation Week 2022 Day 5, the 1-Year Subscription Token and 365-Day Premium Subscription Token now rewards the player 8000 Gems + 1 World Lock daily.
  * As part of Player Appreciation Week 2023 Day 4, the 1-Year Subscription Token and 365-Day Premium Subscription Token now rewards the player 9,600 Gems, previously 8,000 Gems.
