The **Harvest Broomstick** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as a part of Harvest Festival 2025 on September 11, 2025.
##  Function
Flying High!

Grounded!

When equipped, the **Harvest Broomstick** grants the _Speedy + High Jump : Harvest Broomstick_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and jump one tile higher.
