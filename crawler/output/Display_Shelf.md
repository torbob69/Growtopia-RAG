The **Display Shelf** is a splicable display shelf platform foreground block which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2016.
##  Function
A Display Shelf behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath. In addition, it is the prime example of a display shelf, allowing the player to display up to 4 items.
