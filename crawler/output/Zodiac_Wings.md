##  Function
Let's jump to the stars!

All stargazed out!

When equipped, the **Zodiac Wings** grants the _Double Jump : Zodiac Wings!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air. When a player jumps while equipping the Zodiac Wings, a Zodiac sign particle will appear in the screen, accordingly to the astrological order.
