The **Royal Mean Machine** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as March 2023's Item Of The Season on March 2, 2023.
##  Function
They see me rollin'!

They hatin'!

When equipped, the **Royal Mean Machine** grants the _Speedy + High Jump + HIGH ROLLIN': Royal Mean Machine!_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and jump one tile higher. Additionally, when driving over another player, it will visually stretch the player's character, making them short and stubby.
##  Trivia
