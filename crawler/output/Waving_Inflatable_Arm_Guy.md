The **Waving Inflatable Arm Guy** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block which was added as part of Grow4Good Week on April 15, 2021.
##  Function
Being a toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block, a Waving Inflatable Arm Guy can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Waving Inflatable Arm Guy will change its appearance, showing an animation of itself moving.
