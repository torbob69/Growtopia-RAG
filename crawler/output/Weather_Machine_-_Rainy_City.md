The **Weather Machine - Rainy City** is an unsplicable non-solid weather machine foreground block which was added alongside the City Pack on August 18, 2013.
##  Function
As a weather machine, this item can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, it will change its appearance, showing a 2-frame animation, and transform the world's background weather into a raining nighttime city scenery.
