The **Musical Gong** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable animated foreground block which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2015 on September 25, 2015.
##  Function
<https://growtopiawiki.com/w/File:Theme3.ogg>The Musical Gong's music.
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Musical Gong can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Musical Gong will change its appearance, showing a 2-frame animation, and play a piece of music.
##  Trivia
  * In the game files, the Musical Gong's music is named theme3.
  * The same music can be heared at the world selection menu during Harvest Festival.
