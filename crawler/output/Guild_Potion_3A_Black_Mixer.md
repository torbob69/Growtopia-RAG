# Guild Potion: Black Mixer

A small part of a mighty guild potion! Collect enough mixers to brew a truly terrific concoction!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **2 Hits** **2 Hits** – Restores after **2s** of inactivity

Consumption

Consume a(n)  Guild Chest **(x1)**.
May yield **1** item each.

The **Guild Potion: Black Mixer** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Guilds update on April 1, 2018.

This item serves no purpose by itself.
