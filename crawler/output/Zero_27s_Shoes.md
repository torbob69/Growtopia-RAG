The **Zero's Shoes** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Star Trek Prodigy on June 27, 2022.
##  Function
When equipped, the Zero's Shoes serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia

    * Zero's Containment Hood (Hair)

    * Zero's Medusan Mask (Face)

    * Zero's Arms (Hand)

    * Zero's Legs (Pants)
