# Golden Air Robinsons

These sporty shoes are coated with a layer of pure sparkling gold. Sponsored by Kubeti and Rooted.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Feet – Clothes
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: Full Collision

Gems Drop: **N/A**

Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Air Robinsons **(×1)** ,
Candy Heart **(×200)** and
Golden Heart Crystal **(×1)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Golden Air Robinsons** was added as part of the Valentine's Week 2014 update on February 10, 2014.
##  Function
You can move faster!

You are slow again.

When equipped, the **Golden Air Robinsons** grants the _Speedy_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.
