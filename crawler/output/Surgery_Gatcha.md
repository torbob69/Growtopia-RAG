# Surgery Gatcha

Contains randomly chosen rewards! Use it on yourself to open it - who knows what you'll get?!
Properties
This item cannot be dropped or traded.
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **5 Hits** **4 Hits** – Restores after **8s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Advert Reward

| Earned after watching an advertisement via the GrowShow TV.
May reward the Locked and Loaded Gatcha instead!   |
The **Surgery Gatcha** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2020 on May 1, 2020.
##  Function
When consumed, the player will earn one of the following rewards:
  * Medical Scarf ×1
  * Techno Visor ×1
  * Random Surgical Tool ×1
  * Gems ×100
  * Gems ×250
  * Gems ×500
