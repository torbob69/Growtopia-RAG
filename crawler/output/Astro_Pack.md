# Astro Pack
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Astro Pack** for **5,000** in the Growtopia Store.
"Boldly go where no Growtopian has gone before with an entire Astronaut outfit, a Rocket Thruster a Solar Panel, 6 Space Connectors, a Porthole, a Compu Panel and a Forcefield. As a special bonus, you can have this Rare Zorbnik DNA we found on a distant planet. It doesn't do anything by itself, but by trading with your friends, you can collect 10 of them, and then... well, who knows?"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Astro Pack** was released as part of an update with a similar name on October 18, 2013.
