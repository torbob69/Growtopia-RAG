# Sunflower Hair Pin

When sunflowers are out of season, stick this plastic replacement in your hair.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.

1 may be received as a common reward from offering mooncakes using an Offering Table.
The **Sunflower Hair Pin** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2016 on September 15, 2016.
##  Function
When equipped, the Sunflower Hair Pin serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
