# Crown of Winter

This wonderful crown will turn you into Winter Royalty!

The **Crown of Winter** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as a part of the Equinox Weekend update on March 25, 2022.
##  Function
Tis the season!

Tis ain't the season!

When equipped, the **Crown of Winter** grants the _Build Range : Crown of Winter_ mod, which allows the player to build one tile further.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Equinox Winter set, which consists of the following items:

    * Crown of Winter (hat)

    * Shirt of Winter (shirt)

    * Pants of Winter (pants)

    * Boots of Winter (shoes)
