Just before knocking him unconscious, you snapped a lovely photo of Paddy O'Malice for posterity. Put it up on the wall, show off your achievement!

The **Villain Portrait - Paddy O'Malice** is an unsplicable background block which was added as part of the St. Patrick's Week 2015 update on March 13, 2015.

When placed, the Villain Portrait - Paddy O'Malice serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
