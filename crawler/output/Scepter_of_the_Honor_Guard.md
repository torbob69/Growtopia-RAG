The **Scepter of the Honor Guard** is a hand item that was added as part of Summer Clash.
##  Function
Come forth my honor guard!

I return thee to thy realm!

When equipped, the **Scepter of the Honor Guard** grants the _For Honor_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. It also summons two knights on either side of the player, making an attack motion while the player punches.
##  Trivia
  * When first introduced to the game, equipping another leash item with the Scepter of the Honor Guard equipped would summon two of the leashed item instead. This visual bug has since been fixed. Currently, the Crown of Duplication was given the function of summoning two of the leashed item.
