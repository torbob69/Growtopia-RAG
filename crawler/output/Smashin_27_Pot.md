The **Smashin' Pot** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Easter Week 2024 on April 7, 2024.
##  Function
Buddy Smash!

Be gone demon!

When equipped, the **Smashin' Pot** grants the _Punch Range + Punch Damage : Smashin' Pot_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further and break blocks faster.
