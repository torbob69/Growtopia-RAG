The **Jet Ski** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Brr-lloon Warz 2018.
##  Function
Motorbike of the Sea!

No more ski.

When equipped, the **Jet Ski** grants the _Speedy in Water: Jet Ski_ mod, which allows the player to move faster in water and float on water permanently with slow ascent.
##  Trivia
  * Prior to Brr-lloon Warz 2020, the Jet Ski could be claimed after reaching 500 personal points.
  * Jet Ski has a recolored version:  Golden Jet Ski.
  * Initially, the Jet Ski's float-on-water effect was categorized as a _Surfboard_ , but at an unknown update, it was changed to _Forced Float_.
