The **Riding Demon Dog** is a feet item that was introduced during Halloween Week 2015.
##  Function
You can move faster!

You are slow again.

When equipped, the **Riding Demon Dog** grants the _Speedy_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land. Additionally, blue flames will appear underneath the player while running.
