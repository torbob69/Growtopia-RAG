##  Trivia
  * The  Dame's Fedora reuses the  Fedora's sprite, albeit with hair underneath.
    * The Dame's Fedora was released during a time when hat items and hair items belonged to the same slot. At that time, this limitation prevented the developers from creating a separate hair item which would be able to be equipped alongside the regular Fedora.
  * This item is considered a part of the gangland set, which consists of the following items:

    * Fedora (hat)

    * Dame's Fedora (hat)

    * Pinstripe Suit (shirt)

    * Pinstripe Pants (pants)

    * Cigar (face)

    * Tommygun (hand)
