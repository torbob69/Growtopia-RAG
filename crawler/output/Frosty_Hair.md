Frosty Hair is a hair item that was originally Coldplay's Moderator item. It was later released to the public on August 7th, 2018 as part of Player Appreciation Week.
##  Function
You are cold as ice.

You're warming up!

When equipped, the **Frosty Hair** grants the _Icy Flesh_ mod, which allows the player to change their skin color to white. Additionally, it creates snowflakes falling from the player's hair.
