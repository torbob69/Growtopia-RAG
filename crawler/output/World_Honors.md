# World Honors

Honors are a reward for the value of multiple people visiting a world. The owner of a world could earn Growtokens from people rating 5 in his/her world with the /rate command until the fourth Anniversary Week.
Honors are divided into three categories: Today, Yesterday, and Overall. World Honors are removed if no one visits the world for a long time. You can see your world in /top if your world is very popular.
