Summons a Healing Aura every 10 seconds that heals a small amount of health to yourself and nearby players.

The **Healing Aura** is a Dungeon ability which was added as part of the Dungeons update on December 9, 2024.
##  Function
When the player receives this ability, it summons a Healing Aura every 10 seconds that heals a small amount of health to the player and nearby players.
