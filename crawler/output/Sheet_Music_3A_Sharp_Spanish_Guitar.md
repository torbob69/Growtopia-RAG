The **Sheet Music: Sharp Spanish Guitar** is a splicable sheet music background block which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2018 on May 4, 2018.
##  Function
Sheet Music: Sharp Spanish Guitar is a sheet music that plays a sharp spanish guitar note, depending on its position vertically. Its note is half-step higher than that of Sheet Music: Spanish Guitar Note's.
