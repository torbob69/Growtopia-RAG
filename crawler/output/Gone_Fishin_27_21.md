# Gone Fishin'!

Growtopia Gazette
> January 14th: The Fish Are Biting!
> It's fish o'clock!
> We've searched the deepest waters of Growtopia to bring you an all-new and enhanced fishing update!
> Enjoy the freezing fun of Ice Fishing, the glowing glee of Radioactive Fishing, and over 40 new items! New baits, leashes, treats, and - of course - types of fish await! There's more beneath the surface, too: dive into our Fish Training system and nurture your fish into awesome rewards!
> We fish you a reely good time!
> - The Growtopia Team
The Gone Fishin'! update was introduced on the 14th of January, 2018, revolving around the theme of fishing. It introduced the Fish Trainin' Pack as well as new baits, new fish and other fishing-related items.
##  Fish Trainin' Pack
The Fish Trainin' Pack was introduced in the store for 10,000 Gems.

The pack includes:
  * Fish Medicine x2
  * Fish Flakes x2
  * Training Port x1

##  New Fishing Environments
Introduced in the update were two new environments which could be fished in (previously being just water). They are **ice environments** and **radioactive environments**. These new environments must be created in order to be fished in.
**Radioactive Environment** : Made by using a Nuclear Detonator on a Uranium Block with a Water Bucket over it. **Ice Environments** : Made by using a Hand Drill on an Ice Crust Block with a Water Bucket over it.
##  Fish Training
In order to train a fish, players will need a Training Port and a perfect fish. The Training Port must be placed on a Fish Tank, just like a Fish Tank Port. Upon wrenching the Training Port, the player will have the option to "add a fish". Players can ONLY add perfect fish as all other fish will be rejected.
Fish which can be trained (and their rewards):
  * Goldfish
  * Picasso Triggerfish
  * Whale
  * Dogfish
  * Mahi Mahi
  * Bass
  * Sunfish
  * Catfish
  * Weird Manta Ray
  * Alpha Great White Shark
  * Seahorse
  * Gar
  * Mutant Fish
  * Sand Shark
  * Stonefish
  * Cuttlefish
  * Absolutely Real Elephant Fish
  * Double-Headed Angel-Devil Fish
  * Maidmare Fish
  * Rainbowfish
  * Totally Legit Bunnyfish
  * Axolotl
  * Pineapple Fish
  * Dragon Turtle
  * Turkey Fish

The fish will automatically be on level 1 and will require to be fed in order to gain XP. Fish can only be fed specific fish, Fish Chunks or Fish Flakes. Fish Chunks will give 1XP each, Fish Flakes will give 50XP each and depending on the type of fish, different amounts of XP may be given.
This is a table of the amount of XP required to level up:
Level: XP Required1: 50
2: 150
3: 175
4: 250
5: 350
6: 450
7: 600
8: 750
9: 1000
10: 1500
If the fish are not properly fed, they have the possibility of starving and dying. Fish can be revived using a Fish Reviver. When the player has successfully completed all the training, they will receive their respective leashes/rides.
