The **Fairy Light Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of the Voucher Dayz update on December 1, 2021. The item was formally introduced on December 3, 2021, the date when the event arrived.
##  Function
To festivity and beyond!

Back from beyond!

When equipped, the **Fairy Light Wings** grants the _Fairy Light Wings : Double Jump!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and emit light in dark areas.
