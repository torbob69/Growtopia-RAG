The **Captain Bark** is an unsplicable back item which was added as May 2025's Item Of The Season on May 1, 2025.
##  Function
Let's Fly!

Come back here boy!

When equipped, the **Captain Bark** grants the _Double Jump + Punch Range : Captain Bark_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and punch one tile further.

##  Trivia
  * Captain Bark and Royal Captain Bark were originally transmutable upon release. However, due to bugs that occurred when the items were transmuted, their transmutability was later removed a day after its release.
  * Captain Bark's Pet Battle ability was added as part of second Dungeons Update on 30th May, 2025, instead of its original release.
