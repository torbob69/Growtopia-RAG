# Halloween Special Gift Box

Open this box to receive a special spooky gift!
Properties
This item is PUBLIC: Even if it's locked, anyone can smash it.
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **5 Hits** **4 Hits** – Restores after **3s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
NPC Trading

Buy 1 of this item from ** Shady Salesman** for  Halloween Candy **(x100)**.
"Welcome Stranger! Complete your Trick or Treat Tasks to earn some sweet candy. You can get your tasks by scaring my good old pal Crazy Jim while wearing Vampiress Dress item today and doing his tasks. In case you forgot, dial 12345 on a phone to call him. In return for candy I can trade you these spooky boxes!"
The **Halloween Special Gift Box** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Hallowen Week 2022 on October 22, 2022.
