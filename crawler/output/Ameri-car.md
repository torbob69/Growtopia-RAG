The **Ameri-car** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2021 on November 18, 2021.
##  Function
Cars and stripes!

Outta gass!!

When equipped, the **Ameri-car** grants the _Speedy & Double Jump: Ameri-car_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and jump a second time in mid-air.
##  Trivia
  * The Ameri-car's name is a portmanteau of "America," referring to the item's design, which is based on the flag of the United States of America, and "car," befitting its vehicle type.
