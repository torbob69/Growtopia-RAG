Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Ridin' Sgt. Stuffins' **(×1)** ,
Organic Turkey Feather **(×2)** and
Transmog Crystal **(×1)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Sgt. Stuffins' Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2022 on November 19, 2022.
##  Function
Fly just like a bird!

Into thin air!

When equipped, the **Sgt. Stuffins' Wings** grants the _Double-Jump: Sgt. Stuffins' Wings_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
