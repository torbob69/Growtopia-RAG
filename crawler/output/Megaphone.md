The **Megaphone** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Locke The Traveling Salesman (update).
##  Function
When consumed, the Megaphone allows the player to send a super broadcast without spending Gems.
##  Trivia
  * One Megaphone is also given from the purchase of the It's Rainin' Gems bundle.
  * As of the Systems Update, players can buy a Megaphone for 10 Growtoken from the Store.
  * As of Valentine's Week 2018, Locke's Megaphone price was increased from 20 World Lock to 25 World Lock.
  * As of WinterFest 2020, Megaphone's price was increased from 25 World Locks to 30 World Locks in Locke.
  * As of Player Appreciation Week 2021, the Megaphone was removed from Locke The Salesman and was replaced with Locke's Megaphone, which requires Advanced Account Protection, and costed 40 World Locks instead of 30.
    * The same information paragraph was used for Locke's Megaphone in the sale-screen of Locke.
  * As of September 7, 2023, the Broadcast system undergone changes:
    * The Megaphone can only be purchased from In-App Purchases and has been removed from the Growtoken Store
    * The Locke's Megaphone is no longer sold by Locke The Salesman and Lock-Bot.
    * The prices of Broadcast increased with the following prices:
      * Non-Supporters: 12,000 Gems
      * Supporters: 9,000 Gems
      * Super-Supporters: 6,000 Gems
