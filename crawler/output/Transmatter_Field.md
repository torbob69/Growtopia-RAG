The **Transmatter Field** is a splicable non-solid display box foreground block.
##  Function
The Transmatter Field is one of the two types of display boxes in _Growtopia_ (alongside the original Display Box). Items dropped inside one can only be picked up by players with access to the block. It may also be used to hold gems.
