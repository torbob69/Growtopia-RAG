# Bed (block category)

A **bed** is a type of non-solid foreground block which allows the player to visually sleep in it. A player can do so by executing the /sleep command in chat while standing on the same tile as the bed.
##  List of Beds
Items: Special FunctionBed: Allows one to obtain a Bed Head or a Sleepy Santa Hat. When a Bed is broken, there is a small chance that a Pet Sock will drop.
Hammock: None.
Hospital Bed: Allows players without the _Doctor Repulsion_ mod to be operated on by another player.
Royal Canopy Bed: None.
Siesta Sombrero Bed: None.
Spooky Bed: None.
Starship Sickbay Bed: Allows players without the _Doctor Repulsion_ mod to be operated on by another player. Performing a surgery on a Starship Sickbay Bed may grant the latter exclusive rewards.
Vampire Bed: None.
