The **Monochromatic Cave Background** is an unsplicable non-solid background block that was added as part of the Black Friday 2018 on November 22, 2018.
##  Trivia
  * Placing two **Monochromatic Cave Backgrounds** next to each other will cause them to join, creating a continuous pattern.
  * The sprite of Monochromatic Cave Background is a recolor of the Cave Background.
