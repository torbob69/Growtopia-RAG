The **30-Day Premium Subscription Token** is a consumable that was released as part of 2018's Player Appreciation Week.
##  Function
When consumed, the player will receive a premium subscription for 30 days. (Max 390 Days). They will receive 70 season tokens (if there is one running) and 4000 gems every day. They will also obtain access to two exclusive skin colors, have all XP doubled and tree growth time reduced and will be given the Monthly Subscriber's Item.
##  Trivia
  * During the 11th of January, 2019 there was an update on premium subscriptions, every player with an active Premium Subscription (earned by consuming either a **30-Day Premium Subscription Token** or 365-Day Premium Subscription Token) who logs in between the 1st and the 5th of the month will be entered into a draw. One player will be chosen to design and name a single wearable item or block for the game with specific limitations and rules needed to be followed.. For more information, please refer to this thread.
  * As of April 1st, 2019 any new premium token (365-Day or 30-Day) purchased from the store will be untradeable. This change was implemented to prevent fraudulent refunds. Tokens that were bought before this change are still tradeable and provides the same perks. Although, the untradeable counterpart of 365-Day Premium Subscription Token was renamed as 1-Year Subscription Token, there were no changes made to the name of untradeable counterpart of 30-Day Premium Subscription Token causing 2 different items with the same name exist.
  * As of 24 February 2021, 365-Day Premium Subscription Token, 1-Year Subscription Token and 30-Day Premium Subscription Token now rewards the player 4000 Gems daily, previously 2500.
  * As part of Grow Pass update, the Royal Grow Pass Token replaced the 30-Day Premium Subscription Token in the Growtopia Store, causing 30-Day Premium Subscription Token to become fully unobtainable.
