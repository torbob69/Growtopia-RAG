# Eir's Healing Rune

Infused with the medical might of Eir, a healing goddess, this rune will grant swift recovery and aid to any who hold it! Also grants minty fresh breath, which is an especially nice benefit when you consider the standard Viking diet.

**Eir's Healing Rune** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of the Treasure Hunt update.
##  Function
Swift recovery and minty fresh!

No more medical might!

When equipped, the **Eir's Healing Rune** grants the _Healing: Eir's Healing Rune_ mod, which allows the player to heal faster than usual after receiving damage from hitting a pain block.
##  Trivia
  * While the healing effect is activated, the player will not be able to double-jump for a split second, only when they have the latter active in the first place.
