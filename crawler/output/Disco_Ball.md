The **Disco Ball** is a splicable non-solid toggleable animated foreground block which has been added since Growtopia's launch.
##  Function
<https://growtopiawiki.com/w/File:Loop_disco.ogg>The Disco Ball's music.
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Disco Ball can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Disco Ball will change its appearance, showing a 2-frame animation, and play a piece of music.
