##  Function
Paint with all the colours!

Out of ink!

When equipped, the **sSpark's Paintin' Backpack** grants the _Punch Range + Punch Damage: sSpark's Paintin' Backpack_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further and break blocks faster.
In addition, sSpark's Paintin' Backpack can also be used in a similar manner to the Paintbrush, allowing players to paint blocks in areas they are accessed to.
##  Trivia
  * This item is sponsored by sSpark for completing all the Epic Quests.
  * Since sSpark's Paintin' Backpack starts with a lower case letter, it is placed at the end of the player's inventory instead of being placed in alphabetical order like other items.
