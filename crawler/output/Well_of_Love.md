**Well of Love** is an unsplicable provider that was added as part of Valentine's Week 2021 on February 13, 2021.
##  Function
During Valentine's Week the player can give 5 Golden Booty Chests to receive a random Valentine's Week item, and to fill up the well. Once the well has been given 200 Golden Booty Chests, punching it will provide either a Pure Love Essence with 96.75% chance or one of the following epic rewards with 3.25% chance:
  * Golden Heart Crystal
  * Stained Glass Crown
  * Cotton Candy Cape
  * Harp of Love
  * Lovey Dovey Mantle

After collecting the drop, the Well of Love turns into the Dry Well of Love.
##  Trivia
  * Prior to Valentine's Week 2022, filling up the Well of Love only gave a Pure Love Essence.
  * The Well of Love could be used outside of Valentine's Week prior to August 1, 2023.
  * The Well of Love also counts towards earning points to claim Super Golden Booty Chest during the event.
