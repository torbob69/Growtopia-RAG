The **Diamond Dragon** is a hand item which was added as part of the Locke The Traveling Salesman update on March 22, 2015.
##  Function
You command a mighty (valuable) dragon!

Your dragon has abandoned you.

When equipped, the **Diamond Dragon** grants the _Dragonborn With A Silver Spoon_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and punch one tile further. It also summons a diamond dragon that follows the player around.
