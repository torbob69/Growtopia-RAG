# Druid's Mask

A dark, dark mask for a dark, dark Druid! For when you are in one of those, "leave me alone" moods!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be transmuted.

The **Druid's Mask** is an unsplicable face item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2020 on October 22, 2020.
##  Function
When equipped, the Druid's Mask serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Druid set, which consists of the following items:

    * Druid's Crown (Hat)

    * Druid's Mask (Face)

    * Druid's Robes (Shirt)
  * The Druid's Crown is one of the three items added as part of Halloween Week 2020 that are obtainable as a PVP Card Battle reward, alongside the Druid's Mask and Druid's Robes.
