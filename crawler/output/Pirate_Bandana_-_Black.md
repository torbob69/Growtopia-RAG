The **Pirate Bandana - Black** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2023 Day 3 on August 30, 2023.
##  Function
When equipped, the Pirate Bandana - Black serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item's appearance is a reference to the bandana worn by Edward Newgate, a character in the manga and anime series One piece.
