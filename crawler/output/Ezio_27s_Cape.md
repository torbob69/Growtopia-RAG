The **Ezio's Cape** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Ezio update on October 14, 2018.
##  Function
Have faith!

Faith no more!

When equipped, the **Ezio's Cape** grants the _Double Jump: Assassin's Cape_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
