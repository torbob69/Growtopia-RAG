# Super Cape (disambiguation)

This is a **disambiguation** page.
There are more than one subjects in the **Growtopia Wiki** using this name.

There are 6 **Super Capes** which consists of 6 different colours.
  * Super Cape - Blue
  * Super Cape - Purple
  * Super Cape - Green
  * Super Cape - Red
  * Super Cape - Gold
  * Super Cape - Black

There are also 4 **Super Capes** that are based on St. Patrick's Week, Easter Week, Harvest Festival and Super Pineapple Party.
  * Super Cape - Emerald
  * Super Cape - Pink
  * Super Cape - Autumn
  * Super Cape - Pineapple
