The **Slash** is a Dungeon ability which was added as part of the Dungeons update on December 9, 2024.
##  Function
When the player receives this ability, players can perform a melee slash that deals damage to nearby enemies.
##  Trivia
  * After the Dungeons updated at October 23rd, The **Slash** changes to Blade Slash.
  * The **Slash** shares same sprite as Blade Slash 2,Slash 3, and Critical Slash
