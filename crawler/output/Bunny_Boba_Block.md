In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Easter Egg - Growtopia **(×1)** ,

The **Bunny Boba Block** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Easter Week 2022 on April 15, 2022.

When placed, the Bunny Boba Block serves a fully aesthetic purpose.
