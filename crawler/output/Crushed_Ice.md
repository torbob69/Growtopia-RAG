The **Crushed Ice** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of SummerFest 2018 on July 6, 2018.
##  Function
Your body has turned to ice. You can't move!

You've thawed out.

When consumed on a player, it applies the _Frozen!_ mod for 2 seconds. The player won't be able to move at all during this phase, even if they are not standing on a solid block.
