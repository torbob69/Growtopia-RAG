**Bouncy Balloon** is an unsplicable solid trampoline block which was added as part of the Balloon War 2017 update on August 2, 2017.
##  Function
Jumping on a trampoline block such as a Bouncy Balloon will cause the player to bounce, and by holding the jump button, the player can jump even higher. The _High Jump_ mod also boosts one's jump height on a trampoline block.
