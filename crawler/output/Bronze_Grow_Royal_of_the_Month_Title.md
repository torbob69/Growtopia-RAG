# Bronze Grow Royal of the Month Title

Show off your pride with this epic title. Awarded for placing 3rd in the Grow Pass Leaderboard. Only lasts for the month and then will be handed over to the next victor.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item has no use... by itself.

Grow Pass Leaderboard

Awarded to the player who ranked third on the Grow Pass leaderboard for accumulating the third highest number of Grow Pass points during the month.
The **Bronze Grow Royal of the Month Title** is an unsplicable component which was added as part of the Grow Pass second update on March 6, 2024.
##  Function
The sprite for this item is used for the Bronze Grow Royal of the Month Title. Players with a Bronze Grow Royal of the Month Title can toggle this on and off by wrenching oneself and clicking title. Once toggled, it adds a  on both ends of the player's name.
