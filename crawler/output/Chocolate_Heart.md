# Chocolate Heart

Chocolate heals all wounds. Emotional wounds that is. It is pretty useless for broken bones. And really it doesn't do much for emotional problems either, it just tastes good.

**Chocolate Heart** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2017 on February 10, 2017.
##  Fuction
A Chocolate Heart can be fed to a Silkworm to give it a grey color.
