The **Towel Rack Checkpoint** is a non-solid checkpoint foreground block that was added as part of Balloon Warz 2017.
##  Function
Being a checkpoint block, the Towel Rack Checkpoint acts as where the player respawns after dying. Additionally, it may also remove the _Dripping Wet_mod from any affected player.
After going through a Race Start Flag and then through a Race End Flag, all checkpoints will be reset and the player would respawn at the Main Door on death. The same goes with the Challenge Timer and /kick, /kickall command.
