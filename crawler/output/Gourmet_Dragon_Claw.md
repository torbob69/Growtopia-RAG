The **Gourmet Dragon Claw** is an unsplicable pet hand item which was added as part of the Dragon Invasion update.
##  Function
Gourmet draconic wisdom!

Your gastronomic wisdom is no more.

When equipped, the **Gourmet Dragon Claw** grants the _XP Buff: Cooking_ mod, which allows the player to gain an extra 200 XP when successfully cooking a food item. It will also summon a gluttonous dragon pet in a chef attire and will follow the player around.
