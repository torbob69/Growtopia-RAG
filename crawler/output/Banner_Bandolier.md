The **Banner Bandolier** is an unsplicable and untradable chest item which was added as part of Spring Clash 2022 on March 18, 2021.
##  Function
Show off mode enabled!

Where's my flag?

When equipped, the Banner Bandolier grants the _Raise the Flag : Banner Bandolier_ mod which serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
The banner features several special customization functions. The player can wrench oneself to and choose between the five different flag patterns (Harlequin, Slant, Stripe, Panel, and Cross) as well as choose an item to display (excluding seeds, Background Blocks, Super Logo - RTSoft, Super Logo - Yerfdog, Zorbnik Leash, Freeze Wand, Fire Wand, Wrench and Fist). The flag's color will vary from the color of the seeds of the item displayed.
