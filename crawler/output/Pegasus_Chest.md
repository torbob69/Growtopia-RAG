The **Pegasus Chest** is an unsplicable consumable that was added as part of the Pegasus Pursuit update on June 28, 2021.
##  Function
When consumed, the player will earn one of the following rewards:
  * Pegasus Crown ×1
  * Pegasus Lance ×1
  * Pegasus Pants ×1
  * Pegasus Pauldrons ×1
  * Pegasus Shirt ×1
  * Pegasus Shoes ×1
  * Pegasus Block ×3
  * Pegasus Block BG ×3
