The **Bouncer Shades & Beard** is an unsplicable face item which was added as part of Beach Party 2022 on August 6, 2022.
##  Function
When equipped, the Bouncer Shades & Beard serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Bouncer set, which consists of the following items:

    * Bouncer Shades & Beard (Face)

    * Bouncer Shirt (Shirt)

    * Bouncer Pants (Pants)

    * Bouncer Shoes (Feet)
