The **Death Stone** is an unsplicable consumable that was released as a part of the Summer Clash 2018.
##  Function
It is used to make the Gauntlet of Elements - Tier IV when consumed with the Gauntlet of Elements - Tier III equipped.
##  Trivia
When used, when the player wearing the gauntlet respawns, it would have a cool effect where the player is taken down by a blue hand into a portal.
