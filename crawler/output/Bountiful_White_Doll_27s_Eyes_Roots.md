The **Bountiful White Doll's Eyes Roots** is an unsplicable solid foreground block which was added as part of the Roots (update) on July 11, 2019.
##  Function
Bountiful Roots go through 3 stages before they become Mature Roots, where they then are able to be harvested. The player must plant and harvest the same type of Bountiful tree each time to progress the level of the Roots.
