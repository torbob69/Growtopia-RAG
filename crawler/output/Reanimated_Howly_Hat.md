The **Reanimated Howly Hat** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2020 on October 22, 2020.
##  Function
Full Moon Rising!

Morning is broken!

When equipped, the **Reanimated Howly Hat** grants the _Punch Damage : Reanimated Howly Hat_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. When punching, it will also replace the player's fist with a slashing claw effect.
