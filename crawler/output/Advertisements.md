# Advertisements

**Advertisements** were added on June 22, 2021 as part of the Growtopia ADventures update. They can be naturally generated in worlds with a sunny weather or summoned in any world using Ad Blocks. Advertisements may promote in-game products or third-party brands.
Placing an Ad Control Block in a world allows the player to collect up to 30,000 Gems every day from any visitor and toggle naturally generated advertisements on or off.
##  Features
These features are currently only available in worlds with a sunny weather.
