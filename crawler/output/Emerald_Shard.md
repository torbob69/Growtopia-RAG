# Emerald Shard

Watch out, this is a really sharp fragment of Emerald. It seems strangely magnetically attracted to other shards like it. Maybe if you got 200 of them together, they'd fuse into something! Yeah, that's how reality works!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **8 Hits** **6 Hits** – Restores after **8s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Randomly Spawning

Randomly spawns at certain intervals in BLARNEY worlds (the more players are inside, the more will spawn).
This item only appears during St. Patrick's Week.
Complete BLARNEY

May yield **1** item each.
The **Emerald Shard** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the St. Patrick's Week 2015 update on March 13, 2015.
