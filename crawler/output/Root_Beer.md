The **Root Beer** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of SummerFest 2016 on June 30, 2016.
##  Function
When consumed, the player will slowly raise their arms, open their mouth wide, and vigorously shake their face. A belching noise can also be heard for a short time.
##  Trivia
  * The Root Beer is based on a type of soft drink with the same name. Originally made from an extract of the roots and bark of the sassafras tree, this carbonated beverage is often drunk as a summer refreshment.
