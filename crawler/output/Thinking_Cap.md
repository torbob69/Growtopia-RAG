The **Thinking Cap** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of the Pet Battles update.
##  Function
I'm thinking very hard!

Duh!?!

When equipped, the **Thinking Cap** grants the _Brainpower_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further. It will also summon a floating brain pet which follows the player around. Additionally, this pet can be trained as a battle pet.
