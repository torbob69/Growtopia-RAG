The **Anomalizing Elegant Scanner** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the Role Up! update.
##  Function
Anomalizing Elegant Scanners are used to score points during Super Startopians. While equipping this item, each successful star voyage yields 24,200 points. Elegant anomalizers, such as this item, may rarely break upon usage during their respective Grow Events.
