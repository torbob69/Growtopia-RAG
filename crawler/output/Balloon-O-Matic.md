##  Function     _Main article:Balloon-O-Matic Rewards_
The Balloon-O-Matic is primarily used to convert any item from rarity 1-200 into War Balloons. For every 200 rarity of items donated, a random balloon will be given. The remaining rarity will be stored as credits inside the Balloon-O-Matic. For every 8,000 rarity of items donated, a player will be given a Balloon Warz-related item. The Balloon-O-Matic has the possibility of exploding at any time and will say "_Overheating!_ " before exploding.
Only players who are in the same team as the placed Balloon-O-Matic will be able to use it. The Balloon-O-Matic's team color follows the team of the player who placed it. Attempting to use another team's Balloon-O-Matic will say "_Go use your own team's Balloon-O-Matic!_ "
##  Trivia
