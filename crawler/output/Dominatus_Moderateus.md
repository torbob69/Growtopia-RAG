The **Dominatus Moderateus** is an unsplicable hat item which was added on Player Appreciation Week 2014 Day 4 on August 11, 2014.
##  Function
You are very pale and fuzzy.

You are human again!

When equipped, the **Dominatus Moderateus** grants the _Bunnyesque_ mod, which allows the player to change their skin color to white-skinned.
