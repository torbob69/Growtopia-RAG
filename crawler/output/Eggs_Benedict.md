The **Eggs Benedict** is an unsplicable consumable which was added alongside the addition of Home Pack on February 26, 2014.
##  Function
25% chance of double XP for all actions.

Your stomach's rumbling.

When consumed, it grants the _Food: Extra XP_ food buff mod for 30 minutes, which provides a 25% chance for the player to receive double XP for every action that grants XP.

### Silkworm Food     _Main article:Guide:Silkworm Care_
This consumable can be fed to a Silkworm to give it a white color, thus allowing it to produce 1–10  Silk Thread - White per harvest.
