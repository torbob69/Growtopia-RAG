The **Barbecue Grill** is an unsplicable chemical combiner foreground block which was added as part of SummerFest 2014 on June 27, 2014.
##  Function
A Barbecue Grill can be toggled on or off by being punched. As a Chemical Combiner, it can be used to combine three different sets of items. A stack of new items will be created afterwards.
##  Trivia
  * Making a Cheeseburger in a Barbecue Grill gives the Summer Grillin’ Achievement.
