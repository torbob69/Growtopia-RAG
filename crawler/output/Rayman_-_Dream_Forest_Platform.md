The **Rayman - Dream Forest Platform** is an unsplicable platform foreground block which was added as part of UbiWeek 2023 Day 1 on February 24, 2023.
##  Function
An Rayman - Dream Forest Platform behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath.
