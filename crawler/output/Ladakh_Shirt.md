# Ladakh Shirt

Part of a cultural celebration of life, this shirt isn't just a good-looking potential piece of your outfit! It's also used in a major dance festival in the Indian region of - you guessed it - Ladakh!

The **Ladakh Shirt** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2019 on September 12, 2019.
##  Function
When equipped, the Ladakh Shirt serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
