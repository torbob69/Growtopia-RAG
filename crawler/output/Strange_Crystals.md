# Strange Crystals

Growtopia Gazette
> November 13th: Strange Crystals
> Powers from beyond the veil have supercharged our crystals! Thanks to a rare alignment of the heavens, fascinating new recipes have been made possible - can you discover them all?
> Crystals aren't the only things the constellations have enhanced, either! Locke, our lock-loving salesman, has been inspired to add a few new surprises to his collection, as well! Check them out now and dazzle your friends!
> - The Growtopia Team
The **Strange Crystals** update was introduced on November 13, 2017. New items were introduced revolving around the theme of Crystals and items involving crystal recipes.
