The **Muscle Suit** is a splicable shirt item which was added as part of the Legendary update on August 22, 2014.
##  Function
When equipped, the Muscle Suit serves a purely cosmetic purpose. This item has the color of a translucent gray in its inventory icon, but changes color to match the player's skin tone.
