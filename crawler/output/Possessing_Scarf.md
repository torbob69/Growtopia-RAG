The **Possessing Scarf** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2021 on October 21, 2021.
##  Function
Let your spirits soar!

You feel like yourself again!

When equipped, the **Possessing Scarf** grants the _Double Jump + Slow Fall: Possessing Scarf_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
