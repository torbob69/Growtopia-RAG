The **Drednok's Chestplate** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as a part of Star Trek Prodigy on June 27, 2022.
##  Function
Tentacles of Doom!

Good once more!

When equipped, the **Drednok's Chestplate** grants the _Punch Range + Punch Damage: Drednok's Chestplate_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further and break blocks faster.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Drednok set, which consists of the following items:

    * Drednok's Helmet (Face)

    * Drednok's Chestplate (Chest)

    * Drednok's Robe (Shirt)
