The **Protostar Captain's Seat** is an unsplicable non-solid seat foreground block which was added as part of Star Trek Prodigy on June 27, 2022.
##  Function
When a player stands in front of a seat such as the Protostar Captain's Seat for a few seconds, the player will automatically be seen "sitting" on it.
