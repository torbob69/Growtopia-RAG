• Royal Grow Pass users who gain **900 Grow Pass Points** earn **1** of this item for the month of March 2025.
The **Lucky Wrench Style** is a wrench style which was added as part of the March Season on March 1, 2025.
##  Function
When equipped through the 'Wrench Customization' tab when wrenching oneself, the Lucky Wrench Style replaces the player's wrench connected to their name.
