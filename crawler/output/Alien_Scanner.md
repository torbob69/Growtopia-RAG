The **Alien Scanner** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of the Aliens update on November 14, 2021.
##  Function
Scanning for Landing Pods!

Scanner off!

When equipped, the Alien Scanner grants the _Pod Scanner : Alien Scanner_ mod, which grants players a chance to drop Alien-related items or a rare Alien Landing Pod when harvesting trees.
The following are the prizes from harvesting trees:
  * Alien Landing Pod
  * City Bench
  * Pizza Sign
  * Space Conveyor Block
  * Space Stowaway Shirt
  * Storm Drain Cover
  * Street Clock
  * Street Sidewalk
  * Subway Stairs
  * Subway Wall
  * Takeaway Joint
  * Takeaway Joint Roof
