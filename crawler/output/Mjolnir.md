The **Mjolnir** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the Treasure Hunt update on January 2, 2019.
##  Function
The hammer of kings.

You are no longer worthy.

When equipped, the **Mjolnir** grants the _Punch Damage : Mjolnir_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. Additionally, it constantly produces crackling sound effects and a thunderclap sound and visual effect when punching.
##  Trivia
  * The Mjolnir is based on Mjölnir, the hammer of the thunder god Thor in Norse mythology.
