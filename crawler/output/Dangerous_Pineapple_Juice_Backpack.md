The **Dangerous Pineapple Juice Backpack** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2019 on June 7, 2019.
##  Function
A Dangerous Pineapple Juice Backpack may be used to power up a Flamethrower. A single Dangerous Pineapple Juice Backpack will be consumed with each use of the Flamethrower.
A Dangerous Pineapple Juice Backpack can also be used with a Harvester or other similar items; there will be a slight chance that a harvested tree (with a rarity of less than 100) will drop a Dangerous Pineapple Block, Rotten Pineapple Block, or Sourloaf Pineapple Block. The Dangerous Pineapple Juice Backpack will be consumed every time this occurs. Unlike the Fuel Pack, the Dangerous Pineapple Juice Backpack does not double fruit drops when harvesting trees.
Firing a T-Shirt Cannon while equipping a Dangerous Pineapple Juice Backpack will give the targeted player a Dangerous Pineapple Fan Shirt. Firing an Easter Egg Cannon while equipping a Dangerous Pineapple Juice Backpack will give an Easter Egg - Growtopia.
