The **Googly-Woogly Glooberhellifreakilisticexpialigrossness Fleshy Eye** is an unsplicable toggleable deadly foreground block which was added as part of the Gross-Out update on May 1, 2019.
##  Function
The item functions like the Evil Eye, killing a player upon contact with it when toggled on (by a player with access). When it is toggled off, players can walk through the block.
