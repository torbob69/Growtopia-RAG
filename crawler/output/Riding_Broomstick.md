May yield **1** item each.
The **Riding Broomstick** is an unspliceable feet item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2016 Day 2 on August 6, 2016.
##  Function
You can move faster!

You are slow again.

When equipped, the **Riding Broomstick** grants the _Speedy_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land. It also causes the player to slowly drift up and down, simulating levitation.
