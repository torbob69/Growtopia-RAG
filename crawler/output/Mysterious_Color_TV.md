The **Mysterious Color TV** is an unsplicable toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2021 on May 5, 2021.
##  Function
Being a toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block, a Mysterious Color TV can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Mysterious Color TV will change its appearance, showing an 8-framed animation of itself shining and flashing.
##  Trivia
  * As referenced by the item's description, the Mysterious Color TV is based on a specific color-wheel type of television, invented by the Mexican electrical engineer Guillermo González Camarena.
