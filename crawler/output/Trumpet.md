# Trumpet

Toot Toot Tootin' Mexican Trumpet! Some sweet chiily pepper trumpet vibes for all to hear!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be transmuted.
Data

Type: Hand – Clothes
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: Full Collision
Hardness: **0 Hits** **0 Hits** – Restores after **0s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Pinata Bash

Participate in successfully smashing an  Ultra Pinata during the **Pinata Bash!** event.
May give **1** item each time.
The **Trumpet** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2020 on May 1, 2020.
##  Function
<https://growtopiawiki.com/w/File:Mex_trumpet.ogg>Trumpet's music
When the player enters the command /dance or while they are standing still, a fiesta music plays while the player performs a special animation of themself playing the Trumpet.
##  Trivia
  * In the game files, the Trumpet's music is named mex_trumpet.
