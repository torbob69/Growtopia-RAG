# Ultraviolet Aura

The Growtopian eye has evolved to see more of the light spectrum than some creatures, which allows them to see this beautiful Ultraviolet Aura. Warning: Ultraviolet Radiation is what causes sunburn. Prolonged exposure is not advised and don't forget to wear your hat and sunscreen.

The **Ultraviolet Aura** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of SummerFest 2021 on July 8, 2021.
##  Function
Rising with the light of the aura.

The light has faded.

When equipped, the **Ultraviolet Aura** grants the _Double Jump: Ultraviolet Aura_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
Step 17 of the  Quest Of The Owl requires the player to deliver 3 of this item.
