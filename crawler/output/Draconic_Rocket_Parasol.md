The **Draconic Rocket Parasol** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Chinese New Year 2019.
##  Function
Rocket propelled parasol!

Where did it go?

When equipped, the **Draconic Rocket Parasol** grants the _Double-Jump & Slow-Fall: Rocket_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
