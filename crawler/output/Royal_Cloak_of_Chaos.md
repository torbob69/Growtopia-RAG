The **Royal Cloak of Chaos** is an unsplicable back item which was added as July 2026's Item Of The Season on July 1, 2026.
##  Function
Spawned from the dead!

Back to the Underworld!

When equipped, the **Royal Cloak of Chaos** grants the _Double Jump + Speedy : Royal Cloak of Chaos_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and move faster on land. When worn, players can switch between opened and closed cloak by equipping and unequipping the item.
