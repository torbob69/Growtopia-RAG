# Super Logo - D

This is the logo of the supervillain Dr. Destructo. Or at least it's his first initial. His name's Dennis. Are you sure you want to wear it?
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.
Data

Type: Chest – Clothes
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: Full Collision
Hardness: **0 Hits** **0 Hits** – Restores after **0s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Crimefighting Reward

Defeat  Dr. Destructo in crimefighting.
May give **1** item each time.
The **Super Logo - D** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of the Supervillain update on Febuary 20, 2015.
##  Function
When equipped, a "D" appears on the wearer's chest and matches the color of the shirt they are wearing.
