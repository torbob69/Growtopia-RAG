The **Pineapple Juice** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2015 on May 30, 2015.
##  Function
A Pineapple Juice may be used to power up a Flamethrower. A single Pineapple Juice will be consumed with each use of the Flamethrower.
A Pineapple Juice can also be used with a Harvester or other similar items; there will be a slight chance that a harvested tree (with a rarity of less than 100) will drop a Pineapple Block, Frozen Pineapple Block, Roasted Pineapple Block, or Sugarloaf Pineapple Block. The Pineapple Juice will be consumed every time this occurs. Unlike the Fuel Pack, the Pineapple Juice does not double fruit drops when harvesting trees.
Firing a T-Shirt Cannon while equipping a Pineapple Juice will give the targeted player a Pineapple Fan Shirt, while firing an Easter Egg Cannon while equipping one will give an Easter Egg - Pineapple.
