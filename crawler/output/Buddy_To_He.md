The **Buddy To He** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2022 on September 9, 2022.
##  Function
When placed, the Buddy To He serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The **Buddy To He** is a reference to a Vietnamese traditional toy for children of the same name made from glutinous rice powder in the form of characters.
