# Valentine's Goodies
During Valentine's Week

|
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Valentine's Goodies** for **7,500 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"Contains 5 Golden Booty Chests and a bonus Valentine's Card to share the love. Smash the Golden Booty Chests to find a random assortment of Valentine's surprises."
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Valentine's Goodies** was released as part of Valentine's Week 2013. It is only available during the entirety of Valentine's Week.
##  Items
The pack contains the following items:
  * Golden Booty Chest ×5
  * Valentine's Card ×1

##  Trivia
  * The Valentine's Goodies has undergone multiple changes since it was first introduced during Valentine's Week 2013.
    * In 2013, a single Valentine's Goodies cost 200 gems, with each purchase only giving one item.
    * The cost of Valentine's Goodies was increased during Valentine's Week 2014, now requiring 1,000 gems each to purchase. However, this increase in price also came with more items per pack, with each purchase now giving 5 items. Its store description would say, "A random assortment of Valentine's surprises. It's the same amount you'd find by smashing 5 Heartstones."[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Valentine's_Goodies#cite_note-1)
    * Ever since Valentine's Week 2018, each purchase of the Valentine's Goodies comes with a Valentine's Card.
    * As of February 17, 2019, each purchase of the Valentine's Goodies gives five Golden Booty Chests, replacing the five random Valentine's items. This was done to prevent players from abusing the system and filling up their inventories in order to prevent getting certain items (usually of low value or common prizes).
    * Valentine's Week 2021 saw a second increase to the price; a single Valentine's Goodies would then cost 5,000 gems to purchase.
  * Recently, as of Valentine's Week 2022, a single Valentine's Goodies costs 7,500 gems each.
