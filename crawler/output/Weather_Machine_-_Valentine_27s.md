**Weather Machine - Valentine's** is an unsplicable non-solid weather machine foreground block that was added as part of Valentine's Week 2018.
##  Function
As a weather machine, this item can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, it will change its appearance, showing a 2-frame animation, and transform the world's background weather into a mountainous daytime scenery with a pink background, complete with giant hearts falling down.
