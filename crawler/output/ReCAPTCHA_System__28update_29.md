# ReCAPTCHA System (update)

Growtopia Gazette
> May 17th: New reCAPTCHA System!
> We have updated our existing reCAPTCHA System from a mathematical equation to a fun interesting puzzle format!
> Please make sure to read our announcement to find out more details!

The **reCAPTCHA System** update is a minor update that was introduced on May 17, 2022. It features a new CAPTCHA system that verifies players by allowing them to slide to position the Puzzle Piece in the missing space. Currently, this feature is only available on Windows; other platforms still use the math CAPTCHA.
##  Items
Item: RecipeDummy Puzzle Icon: Unobtainable; cannot be obtained by regular means.
##  Trivia
  * reCAPTCHA is a product by Google, but Growtopia does not use it and has been incorrectly labelled.
  * On an unknown date, the CAPTCHA image was deep-fried.
  * Failing the CAPTCHA multiple times will grant the player the Silenced mod for 10 minutes.
