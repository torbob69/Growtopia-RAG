The **Mexican Spinning Top** is an unsplicable solid toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2020 on May 1, 2020.
##  Function
Being a toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block, the Mexican Spinning Top can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Mexican Spinning Top will change its appearance, showing a 2-framed animation.
