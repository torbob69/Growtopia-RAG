# Ancient Plant Seed

This seed from the ancient past is completely intact - there may still be some DNA inside! Plant DNA, but still that's exciting, right?

The **Ancient Plant Seed** is an unsplicable component that was added as part of the Growassic Park update.
##  Function
An Ancient Plant Seed can be used in DNA Extractor for a chance to extract a random DNA strand. This item serves no purpose by itself.
The Ancient Plant Seed is not an actual seed, despite its name. It cannot be spliced with anything.
