The **Sheet Music: Drums** is a splicable and farmable background block which was added alongside the Composer's Pack on March 1, 2013.
##  Function
Sheet Music: Drums is a sheet music that plays a drum sound, depending on its position vertically.
The available sounds are as follows:
  * C - Crash cymbal
  * D - High tom
  * E - Low tom
  * F - Snare
  * G - Hi-hat
  * A - Clap
  * B - Kick
