The **2022 - Zodiac Year of the Tiger** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Lunar New Year 2022 on February 1, 2022.
##  Function
Zodiac Powers!

Another Year!

When equipped, the **2022 - Zodiac Year of the Tiger** grants the _Speedy & Cookie Hunter : Zodiac Year of the Tiger_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and provides a significant chance of doubling fruit drops from Fortune Cookie and Lucky Fortune Cookie Trees.
##  Trivia
  * The Zodiac Year of the Tiger costs 500,000 gems, instead of the Zodiac Animals of the previous year, which cost 200,000 gems each.
