The **Aurora and Igniculus** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of UbiWeek 2023 Day 6 on February 28, 2023.
##  Function
Light it up!

Out of power!

When equipped, the **Aurora and Igniculus** grants the _Light Source : Aurora and Igniculus_ mod, which allows the player to emit light in dark areas and switch between its wearable form and its morph form by wrenching oneself and clicking 'Transform'. Unlike other Light Source mod items, the light does not emit from the player, instead follows the pet Igniculus; similar to the Pet Alebrije.
