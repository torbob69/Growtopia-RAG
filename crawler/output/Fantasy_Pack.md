# Fantasy Pack
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Fantasy Pack** for **5,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"Hear ye, hear ye! It's a pack of magical wonders! You'll get a mystical Wizard Hat Seed, a Wizard's Robe, a Golden Sword, an Elvish Longbow, 10 Barrels, 3 Tavern Signs, 3 Treasure Chests, and 3 Dragon Gates!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Fantasy Pack** is an item pack which was released since Growtopia launched.
##  Items
The pack contains the following items:
  * Wizard's Robe x1
  * Elvish Longbow x1
  * Golden Sword x1
  * Tavern Sign x3
  * Barrel x10
  * Dragon Gate x3
  * Treasure Chest x3
  * Wizard Hat Seed x1
