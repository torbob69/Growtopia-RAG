# World Of The Day

The **World Of The Day** , abbreviated as **WOTD** , is a daily award that was introduced in 2013. Previously, it was chosen by @Seth, but since Ubisoft’s acquisition of Growtopia, it is now chosen by the Ubisoft Abu Dhabi Community Team.
Contestants can nominate their worlds by posting on the Growtopia Forums, Discord or Instagram with a certain hashtag (correct hashtags are posted on forums every WOTD cycle).
##  Rules
  * The world owner should be the one submitting the world.
  * Next to the white door a board should be placed, giving credit to other players who helped with building/funding the world and worlds (or online sources) which were used as inspiration.
  * The world should contain at least a Zombie Jammer and Punch Jammer, unless the world does not require having one of them.
  * Vend worlds, Storages and Pixel Art only worlds will not be considered.
  * Contestants who have been banned for more than 3 days in the last 6 months will not be considered. However, if the punishment was cancelled before it expired, the player will still be eligible to win WOTD.
  * Worlds are allowed to be submitted multiple times. However, there should be at least a 3-day gap in between submissions.

##  Rewards
Reward: AmountKing Of The World (Classic): 1
WOTD Trophy 2026: 1
Growtoken: 10
Once a world is awarded with World of the Day, a message is sent to the chat:
  * _** THE SKY PARTS AND A BEAM OF LIGHT SHINES DOWN! ** This world has been dubbed World Of The Day!_

The world receives a 'WOTD Winner' label, which can be seen when players enter the world, and it appears in the list of World Of The Day Winners found by typing _/top_ in the chat. It is also permanently posted on Growtopia's Instagram, and featured on the Growtopia Gazette and Growtopia’s Website for a day. Additionally, Growtokens can be found randomly in an active World of the Day!

##  Trivia
  * The first World Of The Day was NASA11 by BBongi.
  * As of 1 April 2018, winning WOTD would no longer reward the player with an Ultra Trophy 3000, but a WOTD Trophy 2018 instead.
  * As of Player Appreciation Week 2022 Day 1 on August 15, 2022, players can now go directly to the WOTD from the world select screen.
  * As of Player Appreciation Week 2022 Day 3 on August 17, 2022, a WOTD Coin can be found randomly in an active World of the Day. However, this was disabled in 2023 due to an abundance of bot usage.
