**Riding Turtle** is an unsplicable feet item which was added on Player Appreciation Week 2017 Day 6. One can be purchased from a Pet Trainer for 75 Pet Trainer Medals.
##  Function
When equipped, the **Riding Turtle** serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * Unlike most feet items whose images are that of animals being ridden, the Riding Turtle does not give any mod when equipped.
