The **Death's Scarf** is a chest item which was added as part of Haloween Week 2014 on October 30, 2014.
##  Function
When equipped, a personification of Death appears as a pet and follows the player around. The Death's Scarf serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
### Pet Battle Ability

##  Trivia
  * The Death's Scarf's icon used to feature the scarf instead of the actual pet.
  * When The Grand Tournament was released, the Death's Scarf Pet Battle ability marked the target for death in 15 seconds, but it was increased to 20 seconds in an unknown update.
