The **Royal Alpha Wolf** is an unsplicable pants item which was added as March 2022's Item Of The Season on March 1, 2022.
##  Function
Roll in like a wrecking ball!

Wolf needs a break!

When equipped, the **Royal Alpha Wolf** grants the _Punch Damage : Royal Alpha Wolf_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. Additionally, summons a pet wolf that follows the player around.
