The **Pineapple Checkpoint Totem** is an unsplicable non-solid checkpoint foreground block that was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2019 on June 7, 2019.
##  Function
Being a checkpoint block, the Pineapple Checkpoint Totem acts as where the player respawns after dying.
After going through a Race Start Flag and then through a Race End Flag, all checkpoints will be reset and the player would respawn at the Main Door on death. The same goes with the Challenge Timer and /kick, /kickall command.
