The **Giant Eye Head** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of the Gross-Out update on May 1, 2019.
##  Function
Whattcha looking at?

Back to tiny eyes!

When equipped, the **Giant Eye Head** grants the _Punch Damage: Giant Eye_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster.
##  Trivia
  * As of the Mutant Kitchen update on July 27, 2019, the Slithering Serpent Hair replaces the Giant Eye Head as a possible drop from consuming a Rotting Fish Guts Club Sandwich.
  * As of December 25, 2021, players have a chance to obtain the Giant Eye Head from the Winterfest Calendar - 2021 as a final day drop.
