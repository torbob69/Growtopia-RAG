The **Candlewick's Hair** is an unsplicable hair item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2024 on October 24, 2024.
##  Function
Burn Buddy Burn!

Fire's put out!

When equipped, the **Candlewick's Hair** grants the _Light Source + Fireproof: Candlewick's Hair_ mod, which allows the player to emit light in dark areas and take 50% less damage from lava pain blocks.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Candlewick set, which consists of the following items:

    * Candlewick's Hair (hair)

    * Candlewick's Mask (face)

    * Candlewick's Wax (chest)

    * Candlewick's Dress (shirt)
  * The Candlewick's Hair cannot be dyed. If any hair dye is used on it, the color of the hair remains the same, wasting the dye.
