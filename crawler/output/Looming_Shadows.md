The **Looming Shadows** is an unsplicable non-solid looming deadly foreground block which was added as part of Halloween Week 2017 on October 30, 2017.
##  Function
Being a looming deadly block, a Looming Shadows acts as a deadly block for a while when a player passes by. However, if the player happens to be wearing a Demented Cowl, they will not be killed by the Looming Shadows.
