The **Carnival Creep Platform** is an unsplicable and unobtainable platform foreground block which was added in the Stuff! Update.
##  Function
Several of this item can be seen in the Spiky Survival Carnival game. Every once in a while, some of these platforms will turn into Disabled Carnival Creep Platforms, while some of the disabled ones will turn back to normal.
