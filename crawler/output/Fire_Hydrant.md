The **Fire Hydrant** is a splicable solid trampoline foreground block which was added alongside the City Pack on August 18, 2013.
##  Function
Jumping on a trampoline block such as a Fire Hydrant will cause the player to bounce, and by holding the jump button, the player can jump even higher. The _High Jump_ mod also boosts one's jump height on a trampoline block.
