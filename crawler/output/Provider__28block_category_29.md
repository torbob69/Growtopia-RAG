# Provider (block category)

A **provider** is a non-solid foreground block which can be harvested periodically (time depends on the provider) for an item.

##  Trivia
  * Collecting 100 items from providers will earn the achievement **Milkin' It (Classic)**.
  * Collecting 500 items from providers will earn the achievement **Moo Juice (Classic)**.
  * Collecting 10,000 items from providers will earn the achievement **Collector Extraordinaire (Classic)**.
  * Collecting 1,000,000 items from providers will earn the achievement **Providers Will Provide**.
