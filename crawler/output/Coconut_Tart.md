# Coconut Tart

Sweet and silky, this tart is made from tropically-ripened coconuts and some very precise, very ancient baking secrets! These secrets are so ancient, in fact, that they're able to connect with the ghosts of other coconut aficionados! Food Buff: Grants anyone who eats this tart 50 XP from capturing ghosts! Note: Apparently, all ghosts REALLY enjoy coconut, so this bonus applies to any of them! Who knew?

##  Function
Earn an extra 50 xp from catching ghosts!

Your stomach's rumbling.

When consumed, this item gives a food buff mod which grants 50 XP from capturing any ghost for 30 minutes, including ones released from jars.
