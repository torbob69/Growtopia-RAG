##  Trivia
  * A total of 149 unique seeds can be obtained from the **Small Seed Pack**.
  * As part of the Systems update, the price of the Small Seed Pack was changed from 50 gems to 100.
  * As part of Anniversary Week 2017, all seeds which cannot be made through splicing were removed from the pack.
  * On January 24, 2018, the Small Seed Pack was introduced as a consumable. Players were also allowed to buy 10 Small Seed Pack at once from the Store.
