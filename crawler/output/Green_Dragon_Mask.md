The **Green Dragon Mask** is a unsplicable hair item exclusive to former game moderator @GreenSwe, rewarded on October 14, 2013.
##  Function
Your pet has a fiery temper!

Your pet has fled.

When worn, it spawns a green dragon which breathes green fire that replaces the player's punch. The item had the same benefits as the Dragon Hand and its variants: pushing other players farther and doing more damage to blocks. However, when worn together the effects would be applied twice, making it too strong, and hence those effects got removed.
