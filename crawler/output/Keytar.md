##  Function
You're too busy rocking to punch anybody!

The music has stopped.

When equipped, it grants the _Musical_ mod, which restricts the player from actually punching, and plays the note of Sheet Music: Piano Note when the player attempts to do so. The pitch of the note depends on the tile punched.
