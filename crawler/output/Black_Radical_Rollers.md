The **Black Radical Rollers** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Black Friday 2021 on November 26, 2021.
##  Function
Rollin' Rollin' Rollin'

Back to boring walking!

When equipped, the **Black Radical Rollers** grants the _Speedy: Black Radical Rollers_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land. When the player executes a dance command, the shoes under the player start shining and moving up and down. Upon moving with the shoes, there will be a black retro-wave like trail left behind the player.
