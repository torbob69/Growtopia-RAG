Enter a world of fertile soil, cheerful sunshine, and bountiful new trees! This blast is your ticket to a different kind of farming!

Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Bountiful Blast** for **5,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"Enter a world of fertile soil, cheerful sunshine and lush green hills, and bountiful new trees! This blast is your ticket to a different kind of farming!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Bountiful Blast** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Roots update.
##  Obtaining
A Bountiful Blast can be purchased from the Store for 5,000 gems.
##  Function
Upon consumption, the blast will create a new world featuring a lush and mountainous background weather.
