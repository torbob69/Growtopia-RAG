The **Mystery Block** is a splicable solid foreground block.
##  Function
When placed, the Mystery Block serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
A Mystery Block may drop up to 50 gems when broken. Breaking a Mystery Block will not drop the block itself or any seed.
##  Trivia
  * The Mystery Block's design is based on the ? Block, one of the many blocks found in the _Mario_ franchise. It is commonly seen floating in mid-air, often containing helpful items such as coins.
  * A Mystery Block or its seed has a chance to be dropped when a Winterfest Calendar - 2017 or Winterfest Calendar - 2018 is harvested.
  * When spawned through the _Magical Seeds_ special event, Mystery Block Seeds will only stay in the world for 60 seconds, until the event ends.
    * Prior to the addition of the _Magical Seeds_ special event, Mystery Block Seeds that are part of a special event stay for an indefinite amount of time.
