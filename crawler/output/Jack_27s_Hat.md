The **Jack's Hat** is a hat item that was released as part of the Winter Clash. One can be obtained from the Winter Awards.
##  Function
Giving you extra Role points for Supplier and Crafter roles!

No more a Jack of All Trades!

When equipped together with the Jack's Vest, Jack's Jeans, and Jack's Shoes, it grants the _Jack of All Trades!_ mod, which allows role quests to give 3% bonus points. It can be stacked with other items that grants a similar mod, such as Supplier's Cape or Crafter's Cape.
##  Trivia

    * Jack's Vest (Shirt)

    * Jack's Jeans (Pants)

    * Jack's Shoes (Feet)
