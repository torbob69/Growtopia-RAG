The **Mooncake Paper Lanterns** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2022 on September 9, 2022.
##  Function
To the skies!

To the ground!

When equipped, the **Mooncake Paper Lanterns** grants the _Build Range + High Jump: Mooncake Paper Lanterns_ mod, which allows the player to build one tile further and jump one tile higher.
