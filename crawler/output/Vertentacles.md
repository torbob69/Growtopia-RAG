# Vertentacles

Writhing, horrible mutant tentacles that love to reach for the sky! Maybe they're afraid of falling...?

The **Vertentacles** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of the Mutant Kitchen update on July 26, 2019.
##  Function
When placed, the Vertentacles serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * Vertentacles are the vertical counterparts of Horizontacles. Unlike the latter, Vertentacles do not function as platforms.
  * Vertentacles replaced the Hairy Leg from Gross-Out update for being "too gross".
