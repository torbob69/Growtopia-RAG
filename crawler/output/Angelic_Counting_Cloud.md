Place three in a row and punch to let the angels do their thing. If you can get three in a row of the angelic number you may be rewarded by the angels themselves.

The **Angelic Counting Cloud** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2020.
##  Function
When three Angelic Counting Clouds are placed in a row horizontally, the player can punch it and will roll into three numbers. If a player rolls a 555, they will win Pink Teeny Angel Wings or King of Hearts (1 in 1000 chance). Once rolled, the blocks will turn into Angelic Heart Clouds.
