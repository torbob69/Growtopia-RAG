The **Year of the Rabbit Lucky Token** is a spliceable non-solid Lucky Token foreground block which was added as part of Lunar New Year 2023 on January 21, 2023.
##  Function
Placing a Year of the Rabbit Lucky Token in a World would enter the player in a raffle. Placing more would improve their chance of winning the raffle. The items given out from the raffle are:
  * 100 Draconic Crowns
  * 200 Builder's Locks
  * 500 Vending Machines
  * 700 gems (×5,000)
