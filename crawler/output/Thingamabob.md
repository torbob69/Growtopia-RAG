The **Thingamabob** is an unsplicable machine part component. One has an extremely tiny chance to be obtained from successfully performing a surgery.
##  Function
Step 19 of the  Quest Of Steel requires the player to deliver 2 of this item.
Below is a list of items which can possibly be made by combining the Thingamabob with two other items in any Chemical Combiner.

##  Trivia
  * The Thingamabob is the rarest of all six machine parts used in the creation of the Legendary Wizard.
  * Due to a duplication glitch, the Thingamabob and other affected items was made untradable for some while on January 19, 2019.
