The **Tropical Bar Stool** is an unsplicable non-solid seat foreground block which was added as part of SummerFest 2021 on July 8, 2021.
##  Function
When a player stands in front of a seat such as the Tropical Bar Stool for a few seconds, the player will automatically be seen "sitting" on it.
##  Trivia
  * The Tropical Bar Stool is one of the two blocks added during SummerFest 2021 that are obtained from the Building Blocks Machine, the other being Tropical Bar.
