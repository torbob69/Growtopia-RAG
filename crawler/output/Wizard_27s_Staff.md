The **Wizard's Staff** is a splicable hand item which was added as part of SummerFest 2015 on July 3, 2015. However, the recipe of the **Wizard's Staff** was discovered almost 2 months after.
##  Recipe
Splicing

The method of obtaining a **Wizard's Staff** is different from the usual splicing method. A player must build a replica of the staff; 5 Cliffsides placed vertically with each other and an Orange Block placed on top of the column of Cliffsides (seen in the picture).
The player must then splice a Wooden Background Seed with a Poppy Seed on the Orange Block to grow a **Wizard's Staff Tree** , which takes an hour to grow. This completes one of the Epic Quests.
##  Function
You have a staff that simulates legendary power!

Thats enough of that!

When equipped, the **Wizard's Staff** grants the _Wizardly Force_ mod, which allows the player to knock back other players further. It will also replace the player's punching animation with smoke-like particles.
