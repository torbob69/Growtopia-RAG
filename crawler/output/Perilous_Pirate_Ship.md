**Perilous Pirate Ship** is a feet item that was added as February 2021's Item Of The Month. It comes along a Magical Captain's Compass which can be consumed to wear the pirate ship.
##  Function
Up anchors, set the course for adventure!

Run aground again!

When equipped, the **Perilous Pirate Ship** grants the _Speedy & Punch Damage: Pirate Ship!_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and break blocks faster. Additionally, a flying pet pirate ship that follows the player around is summoned. Upon consuming the Magical Captain's Compass, the player will wear the Perilous Pirate Ship and will shoot cannon balls when punching.
##  Trivia
  * As of the Player Appreciation Week update on August 16, 2022, player can now switch between the wearable item and its morph by wrenching oneself and clicking "Transform".
