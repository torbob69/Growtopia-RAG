The **Crimson Thief Coat** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2023 on February 10, 2023.
##  Function
When equipped, the Crimson Thief Coat serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia

    * Crimson Thief Hair (Hair)

    * Crimson Thief Mask (Face)

    * Crimson Thief Coat (Shirt)

    * Crimson Thief Pants (Pants)
