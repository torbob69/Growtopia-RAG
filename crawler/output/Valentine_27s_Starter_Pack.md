# Valentine's Starter Pack
On 14 February, 2024

|
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Valentine's Starter Pack** for **14.99 USD** in the Growtopia Store.
"An extra sweet deal to go with the event!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Valentine's Starter Pack** is a Store-bought IAP which was added as part of the Valentine's Week 2023 update and is only available on the first day.
The first incarnation of the Valentine's Starter Pack contained the following:
  * Gems ×540,000
  * Golden Booty Chest ×50
  * Super Golden Booty Chest ×3
  * Well of Love ×1
  * Corny Couple Shirt ×1
  * Heartstone Circlet ×1
