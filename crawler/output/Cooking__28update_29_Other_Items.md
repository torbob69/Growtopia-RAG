# Cooking (update)/Other Items

< Cooking (update)
##  Other Items
Item: RecipeDisgusting Mess: Cook a proper recipe with quality lower than 60% (or 50% for the Master Chef's Oven).
Bland Mush: Cook anything that isn't a proper recipe.
Mac n' Cheese Star: Obtained by cooking any recipe with a cooking percentage of 95% or above.
Plethora Of Mac n' Cheese Stars: Obtained by consuming 100 Mac n' Cheese Stars (1 can also be reverted back to 100 Mac n' Cheese Stars by consuming 1 Plethora Of Mac n' Cheese Stars.
Gingerbread Man: Obtained by cooking a Gingerbread Cookie with a percentage of 95% or above. It is only obtainable during WinterFest.
