# Pineapple Air Freshener

This brings a fresh pine(apple) scent to the area... a dangerous scent.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: Full Collision
Hardness: **3 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **8s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
**Pineapple Magic**

Rewarded from the  Pineapple Magic mod.
_May yield**1** item each._
_Unlocked when at least 75,000,000 pineapples have been eaten globally. This item can only be obtained duringSuper Pineapple Party._
Consumption

Consume a(n)  Pineapple Gift Box **(x1)**.
May yield **1** item each.

The **Pineapple Air Freshener** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2015 on May 30, 2015.
##  Function
When consumed in an empty space, it summons Pine Fresh, the Villain exclusive to the event.
