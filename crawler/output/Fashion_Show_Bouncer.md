**Fashion Show Bouncer** is a non-solid NPC foreground block which was added as part of the Player Appreciation Week 2025 on September 25, 2025. The Bouncer can be found in the world FASHIONSHOWGAME.
##  Function
Punching the Fashion Show Bouncer in FASHIONSHOWGAME allows the player to join the fashion show mini-game in the world.
