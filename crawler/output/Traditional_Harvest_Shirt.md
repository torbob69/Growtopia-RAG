The **Traditional Harvest Shirt** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2022 on September 9, 2022.
##  Function
When equipped, the Traditional Harvest Shirt serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Traditional Harvest set, which consists of the following items:

    * Traditional Harvest Hat (Hat)

    * Traditional Harvest Shirt (Shirt)

    * Traditional Harvest Dress (Shirt)
