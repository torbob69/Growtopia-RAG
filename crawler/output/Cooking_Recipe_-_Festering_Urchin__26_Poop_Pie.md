# Cooking Recipe - Festering Urchin & Poop Pie

To cook this recipe you will need the following:Ingredients: - Space Poop - Gruel - Rotting Sea Goo - Sea UrchinSeasoning: - 3x Acid to tasteProcess:Add Space Poop to an oven on Low heat. Just before the Space Poop begins to cook, add Gruel. While the Space Poop is underdone, add Rotting Sea Goo. While the Gruel is stil cold, before it begins to cook, add Sea Urchin. Add the seasonings and wait until perfectly cooked all the way through before removing from the oven.Timings may vary so you will need to experiment.
To cook this recipe you will need the following:

Ingredients:
- Space Poop
- Gruel
- Rotting Sea Goo
- Sea Urchin

Seasoning:
- 3x Acid to taste

Process:
Add Space Poop to an oven on Low heat. Just before the Space Poop begins to cook, add Gruel. While the Space Poop is underdone, add Rotting Sea Goo. While the Gruel is stil cold, before it begins to cook, add Sea Urchin. Add the seasonings and wait until perfectly cooked all the way through before removing from the oven.

Timings may vary so you will need to experiment.

The **Cooking Recipe - Festering Urchin & Poop Pie** is an unsplicable component which was added as part of the Mutant Kitchen Rebalanced! update on November 12, 2019.
##  Function
This cooking recipe's description guides the player on how to cook a Festering Urchin & Poop Pie. It is permanent and will not be consumed when the player cooks the item.
