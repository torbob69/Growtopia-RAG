The **Potpourri Dispenser** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable foreground block which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2026 on February 12, 2026.
##  Function
When placed, being a toggleable animated foreground block, a _Potpourri Dispenser_ can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a _Potpourri Dispenser_ will change its appearance, showing potpourri being thrown by the dispenser. Players can also paint the Potpourri Dispenser to alter the color of the potpourri particles accordingly.
##  Trivia
  * A maximum of ten **Potpourri Dispensers** can be placed in one world at a time.
