# Affiliate Program

The **Growtopia Affiliate Program** was introduced on August 5, 2025. The program is run in partnership with Xsolla and allows eligible players to generate unique affiliate links to Growtopia's official Xsolla shop.
##  Requirements
To be part of Growtopia's Affiliate Program, players must apply through this link. Eligible players should meet certain minimum social media follower requirements to qualify.
###  YouTube
  * 1,000 subscribers

###  TikTok
  * 2,000 followers

###  Twitch
  * 100 followers

###  X
  * 100,000 followers

##  Benefits
Approved and eligible affiliates can generate their unique Xsolla link to share. The benefits are as follows:
### Revenue Share
  * Affiliates will earn 12% of the purchase amount for every item bought through the affiliate's unique Xsolla link.

### Discount Codes
  * Affiliates can generate 5% discount codes players can use at checkout.

##  Disclaimer
All payouts are handled directly by Xsolla. Growtopia is not responsible for payment processing, payout timelines, or any tax-related matters.
##  Changes
  * On September 19, 2025, the revenue share for affiliates increased from 10% to 12%, and the minimum X follower requirement increased from 100 to 100,000.
