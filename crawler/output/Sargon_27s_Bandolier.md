The **Sargon's Bandolier** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of UbiWeek 2024 Day 1 on March 28, 2024.
##  Function
Now an immortal!

Mortal again!

When equipped, the **Sargon's Bandolier** grants the _Speedy + Light Source : Sargon's Bandolier_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and emit light in dark areas.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Sargon set, which consists of the following items:

    * Sargon's Hair (Hair)

    * Sargon's Bandolier (Chest)

    * Sargon's Trousers (Pants)

    * Sargon's Boots (Feet)

    * Qays & Layla (Hand)
