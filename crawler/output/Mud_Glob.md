The **Mud Glob** is a splicable consumable which was added as part of _Growtopia_ 's launch.
##  Function
You've been splattered with mud!

Clean again!

When consumed on a player, it grants that player the _Muddy_ mod, which causes their skin to become dark brown, while also reducing their walking speed and jumping height. This mod lasts only for three seconds.
