**Zraei's Dragoscarf** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as April 2017’s Item Of The Month on April 1, 2017.
##  Function
This dragon's got your back!

Your neck feels empty.

When equipped, the **Zraei's Dragoscarf** grants the _Dragoscarf_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. Additionally, it replaces the player's punching animation with purple flames coming from the Dragoscarf's mouth. The punch damage effect does not stack with other items that grant a similar effect.
