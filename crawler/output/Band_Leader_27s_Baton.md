The **Band Leader's Baton** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2023 on November 17, 2023.
##  Function
Go to town riding a pony!

Stick a feather on your hat and call it macaroni!

When equipped, the **Band Leader's Baton** grants the _Punch Damage + Punch Range: Band Leader's Baton_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and punch one tile further. Additionally, it forces nearby players to begin to march.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Marching Band set, which consists of the following items:

    * Marching Band Hat (hat)

    * Marching Band Jacket (shirt)

    * Marching Band Pants (pants)

    * Band Leader's Baton (hand)
