The **Pipa Jing's Instrument** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of Lunar New Year 2025 on January 23, 2025.
##  Function
Music to my ears!

The sound of silence!

When equipped, the **Pipa Jing's Instrument** grants the _Build Range + Speedy: Pipa Jing's Instrument_ mod, which allows the player to build one tile further and move faster on land.
