The **Skull Launcher** is a splicable hand item which was added as part of the Night of the Sewing Dead update on February 3, 2017.
##  Function
You shoot skulls at people!

That was pretty antisocial.

When equipped, the **Skull Launcher** grants the _Rainin' Skulls_ mod, which allows the player to kill zombies during a Pandemic. It will also replace the player's punching animation with skull projectiles that create black holes upon impact.
