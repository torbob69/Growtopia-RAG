The **Royal Red Panda Spirit** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as December 2022's Item Of The Season on December 1, 2022.
##  Function
Panda Power!

Nobody likes you!

When equipped, the **Royal Red Panda Spirit** grants the _Double-Jump: Royal Red Panda Spirit_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and switch between its wearable form and its morph form by wrenching oneself and clicking 'Transform'.
