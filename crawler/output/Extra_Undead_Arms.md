##  Function
Wham! Bam! Pow! Bonk!

Ah, stupid two arms!

When equipped, the **Extra Undead Arms** grants the _Double-Jump + Punch Damage: Four-Arms Attack_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and break blocks faster.
