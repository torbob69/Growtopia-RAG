The **Holiday Lights Scarf** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of Pre-WinterFest 2024 on December 1, 2024.
##  Function
It's time!!!!!

That was Fun!!

When equipped, the **Holiday Lights Scarf** grants the _Double Jump + Slow Fall: Holiday Lights Scarf_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
##  Trivia
  * This item's equip message is a reference to singer Mariah Carey.
  * Holiday Lights Scarf is a redesigned version of  Silk Scarf and its golden counterpart  Golden Silk Scarf.
