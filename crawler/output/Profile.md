# Profile

Tap this to check out your own personal info!

This item cannot be dropped or traded.

**1h**

This item does not have any recipe because it is no longer, or was never obtainable.
**Profile** is the player's personal profile in Growtopia. It can be opened by when a player Wrenches their username. It will display the following:
  * The player's username, account level and Card Battle level
  * Guild and rank in the guild
  * Goals and Quests (such as Life Goals and CARNIVAL ring quests)
  * Daily Bonuses
  * Achievements
  * Mods which are currently active
  * Fires put out
  * CARNIVAL games won
  * Current world the player is in, the coordinate they are standing on and the number of people currently in the world
  * Worlds the player owns
  * Whether the player is a Supporter/Super Supporter or not, and whether they have the Recycle Tool or not
  * Musical note the player is standing on
  * Growmojis available and those which can be unlocked
  * Hours played and number of days ago since the creation of the account
