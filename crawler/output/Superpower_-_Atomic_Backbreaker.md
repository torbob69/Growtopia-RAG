# Superpower - Atomic Backbreaker

This is a Rare Fire Superpower card for use in fighting crime. You can also place it as a block! Card effect: Smash your opponent for 1 damage, and while they're dazed, automatically follow up with a randomly chosen card from your hand.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.
