# Steam Valve

This links up to other Steam blocks and can conduct steam power. If steam enters this block from the top or bottom, it will go out the bigger side, and the valve will flip for the next time.

The **Steam Valve** is a splicable solid steam foreground block which was added alongside the Steampack as part of Anniversary Week 2016 on January 8, 2016.
##  Function
When linked with other steam blocks, a Steam Valve will conduct steam power from above or below. The steam power will then exit sideways depending on the previous exit direction.
##  Trivia
  * As the same with all the other steam blocks (except Steam Tubes), a Steam Valve or its tree has a chance to drop a Steampunk Sprocket when broken.
