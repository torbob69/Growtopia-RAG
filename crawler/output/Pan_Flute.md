# Pan Flute

A popular folk instrument which can be made from wood, plastic, metal and ivory. This one is made of pixels though!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be transmuted.
Data

Type: Hand – Clothes
Render Type: Single

**1h**
Pinata Bash

Participate in successfully smashing an  Ultra Pinata during the **Pinata Bash!** event.
May give **1** item each time.
The **Pan Flute** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2021 on May 5, 2021.
##  Function
<https://growtopiawiki.com/w/File:Panflutemelody.ogg>Panflute's music
When the player enters the command /dance or while they are standing still, a fiesta music plays while the player performs a special animation of themself playing the Flute.
