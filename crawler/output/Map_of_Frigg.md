The **Map of Frigg** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the Treasure Hunt update on January 2nd, 2019.
##  Function
This map can only be used during **Friday** (Growtopia Time), using this map in the other day will make the second hint turned into:
In the pale moonlight the shapes on the page begin to swirl and glow, but can't seem to form anything solid. Perhaps the time is not right?

###  First Hint
This map's letters are hard to see in the light of day, and even those you CAN make out seem to change from moment to moment.

#### Solution
Go to any world with Weather Machine - Night.
###  Second Hint
NUEY WULY HIQ, XI HIN LOMB CH PUCH, ZIL IHFS QCNB XCFCAYHWY QCFF PCWNILS LYCAH. MHUJ QBUN'M VYMN, MBUNNYL CN QYFF, UHX JYLBUJM CH NBCM WIHZFCWN QCFF LCWBYM MQYFF

#### Solving
Use Caesar Shift +6 to decode this into:
TAKE CARE NOW, DO NOT RUSH IN VAIN, FOR ONLY WITH DILIGENCE WILL VICTORY REIGN. SNAP WHAT'S BEST, SHATTER IT WELL, AND PERHAPS IN THIS CONFLICT WILL RICHES SWELL.

#### Solution
Break any Anomalizing item while a grow event is running during a Seasonal Clash.
###  Prize
Successfully completing the quest has a chance to reward the player with 1  Eir's Healing Rune.
##  Trivia
  * In Germanic/Norse mythology, Frigg is a goddess described as the wife of Odin.
