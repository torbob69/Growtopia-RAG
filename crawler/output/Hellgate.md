# Hellgate

It seems like the destination of this portal is obvious, but actually you can choose any place you like with your Wrench.
Hellgate (Rarity: 40)
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item has special properties you can adjust with the Wrench.

Rewards of Sacrifice

Sacrifice **any item with a rarity of 40 or above** to Growganoth.
The **Hellgate** is an unsplicable non-solid portal foreground block which was added as part of Halloween Week 2015.
##  Function
Being a type of portal, the Hellgate immediately moves the player to another tile when touched. A block with an ID must first be placed on the tile for this to work.
