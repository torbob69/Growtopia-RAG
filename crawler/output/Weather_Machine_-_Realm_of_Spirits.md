The **Weather Machine - Realm of Spirits** is an unsplicable non-solid weather machine foreground block which was added as part of Garuda's Glory 2023 on August 17, 2023.
##  Function
As a weather machine, the Weather Machine - Realm of Spirits can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, it will change its appearance, transforming the world's background weather into an eerie scenery with a floating haunted house at the center.
