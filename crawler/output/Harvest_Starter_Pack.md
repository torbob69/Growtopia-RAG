# Harvest Starter Pack
On September 12, 2024

|
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Harvest Starter Pack** for **14.99 USD** in the Growtopia Store.
"An extra sweet deal to go with the event!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Harvest Starter Pack** is a Store-bought IAP which was added as part of the Harvest Festival 2023 on September 21, 2023, and is only available on the first day of the event each year.
The first incarnation of the Harvest Starter Pack contained the following:
  * Gems ×650,000
  * Unity Mooncake ×30
  * Courage Mooncake ×30
  * Ancestor Mooncake ×30
  * Balance Mooncake ×30
  * Songpyeon ×10
  * Mooncake Bundle ×50
  * Moon Hair Pin ×1
