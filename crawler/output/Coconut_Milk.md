# Coconut Milk

This is the page for **Coconut Milk**.
You may be looking for **Coconut Water** instead.
A delicious component in many fine curries and dishes, this is made from crushing coconut meat and has many useful applications! Makes for great desserts, drinks, sauces, and more! So much better than coconut water, right?
