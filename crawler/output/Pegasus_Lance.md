The **Pegasus Lance** is an unsplicable hand item that was added as part of the Pegasus Pursuit update on June 28, 2021.
##  Function
Pointy flying horse stick activate!

Nag nag nag!

When equipped, the **Pegasus Lance** grants the _Punch Damage: Pegasus Lance!_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster.
### Pegasus Pursuit
During the Pegasus Pursuit event, equipping a Pegasus Lance increases the player's chance by an unknown percentage to obtain a Pegasus Chest whilst fishing, performing surgeries, or completing startopia missions.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Pegasus set, which consists of the following items:

    * Pegasus Crown (hat)

    * Pegasus Pauldrons (neck)

    * Pegasus Shirt (shirt)

    * Pegasus Pants (pants)

    * Pegasus Shoes (shoes)

    * Pegasus Lance (hand)
