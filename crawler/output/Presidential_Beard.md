The **Presidential Beard** is a face item that was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2020.
##  Function
When equipped, the Presidential Beard serves a fully cosmetic purpose.
The  The Seven Seas : The Curse of the Ghost Pirate! mission requires 10 of this item.
##  Trivia
  * This item's appearance is a reference to the beard of the 16th president of the United States, Abraham Lincoln.
