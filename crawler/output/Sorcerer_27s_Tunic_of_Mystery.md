The **Sorcerer's Tunic of Mystery** is an unsplicable shirt that was added as September 2018's Item Of The Month on September 1, 2018.
##  Function
Colour changing sorcery!

Back to a dull boring life!

When equipped, the **Sorcerer's Tunic of Mystery** grants the _Mysterious_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. The tunic also changes color based from the color of the block that the player is standing on.
