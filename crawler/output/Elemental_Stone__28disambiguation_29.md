# Elemental Stone (disambiguation)

This is a **disambiguation** page.
There are more than one subjects in the **Growtopia Wiki** using this name.
**Elemental Stones** are consumables that were released as part of September's monthly update. The stones are used to upgrade the Gauntlet of Elements. To upgrade a gauntlet, the player must have a Soul Stone and one of the following stones:
  * Earth Stone ➝ Gauntlet of Elements - Tier I
  * Wind Stone ➝ Gauntlet of Elements - Tier II
  * Fire Stone ➝ Gauntlet of Elements - Tier III
  * Death Stone ➝ Gauntlet of Elements - Tier IV
  * Water Stone ➝ Gauntlet of Elements - Tier V
  * Life Stone ➝Exquisite Gauntlet of Elements
