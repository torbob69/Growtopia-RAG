The **Mosquito King's Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2019 on September 12, 2019.
##  Function
Bzzzzzzzzzz!

Buzz-off!

When equipped, the **Mosquito King's Wings** grants the _Double-Jump: Mosquito_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air. Additionally, the Mosquito King's Wings will emit mosquito particles around the player.
