# Legendary Katana Update

This is a **stub** page.
Please add more information to this page.
The **Legendary Katana update** is an update which was introduced on April 18, 2015.
##  New Additions
New additions introduced in this update include:
### New Command
The command /omg was added, which makes the player puts on a surprised look.
### New Items
Item: RecipesLegendary Katana
  * 1 can be obtained from completing the Quest For The Blade.

 |
Change of Address
  * 1 can be purchased from the Store for 20,000 Gems.

 |
Muddy Pants
  * 1 can be purchased from the Store for 125 Growtokens.

 |
Black Braids
  * Up to 4 can be dropped from harvesting its tree, which is made by splicing a Dark Purple Block Seed with a Dark Blue Block Seed.

 |
Ginger Hair Bun
  * Up to 4 can be dropped from harvesting its tree, which is made by splicing a Red Wood Wall Seed with a Dark Orange Block Seed.

 |
Sassy Boots
  * 1 can be dropped from combining a Mysterious Chemical, 8 Chemical R, and 4 Chemical Y in any Chemical Combiner.

 |
Carnival Spikeball
  * 1 can be obtained as a Common Prize from the Brutal Bounce mini-game in The Carnival.

 |
Carny Vest
  * 1 can be obtained as a Rare Prize from the Brutal Bounce mini-game in The Carnival.

 |
Flame Scythe
  * 1 can be obtained as an Ultimate Prize from the Brutal Bounce mini-game in The Carnival.

 |
Holographic Sign
  * 1 has a chance to be obtained as a common prize from from successfully performing a surgery.

 |
Ride'O'Gold
  * 1 has a chance to be dropped from breaking a Pot O' Gold.

 |
Teddy Bear
  * 1 has a small chance to be dropped from harvesting an Awkward Friendly Unicorn.

 |
##  Trivia
  * This update replaced the Bouncy House in The Carnival with the Brutal Bounce mini-game.
  * When the Black Braids was first released, its tree took 11539 days, 17 hours, 29 minutes, and 33 seconds to fully grow. This glitch has been fixed since then.
