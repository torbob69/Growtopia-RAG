**Neon Nerves** is an unsplicable chest item introduced during the Strange Crystals update on November 13, 2017.
##  Function
Let the dazzling commence!

I'm all dazzled out!

When equipped, the **Neon Nerves** grants the _Neon Nerves_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land. Additionally, this item can stack with up to two other Speedy clothing items.
##  Trivia
  * Neon Nerves do not Stack with Speed Medallion, though they do stack with other items which grant **Speedy** mod.
