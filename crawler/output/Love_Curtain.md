The **Love Curtain** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2018.
##  Function
If two players who both have the _Valentine_ mod and are each other's Valentine stand behind a Love Curtain, red hearts will start coming out of the top.
Otherwise, if two players who aren't each other's Valentine stand behind a Love Curtain, a cloud of dust will form to simulate fighting.
