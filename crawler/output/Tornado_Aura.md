The **Tornado Aura** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Wonder Week 2023 Day 3 on October 11, 2023.
##  Function
Spinning right round!

Feeling Dizzy!

When equipped, the **Tornado Aura** grants the _Speedy + High Jump : Tornado Aura_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and jump one tile higher. Moreover, the Tornado Aura's color changes based on the seed color of the pants the player is wearing.
