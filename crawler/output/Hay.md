# Hay

Haaaaay! A big bale of harvest goodness, this is the perfect treat for a hungry horse. Just don't feed them too much. It makes them crazy.

**Hay** is an unsplicable solid foreground block that was added as part of Harvest Festival 2017.
##  Function
Similar to the Snow Block, walking on Hay reduces the player's jump height.
