The **Dewi Sri Statue** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2021 on September 16, 2021.
##  Function
When placed, the Dewi Sri Statue serves a fully aesthetic purpose.
The  Harvest Festival Exclusive Mission - I can seed clearly now my gun I've got! mission requires 2 of this item.
##  Trivia
  * The Dewi Sri Statue is a reference to Dewi Sri, a goddess of rice and fertility in Javanese, Sundanese, and Balinese Hindu beliefs.
