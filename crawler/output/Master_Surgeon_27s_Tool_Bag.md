# Master Surgeon's Tool Bag
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Master Surgeon's Tool Bag** for **45,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.

The **Master Surgeon's Tool Bag** is a pack introduced on Player Appreciation Week 2022 Day 5 on August 19, 2022.
