The **Golden Ox Pendant** is an unsplicable chest item that was added as part of Lunar New Year 2021.
##  Function
Be more Ox!

A little less Ox!

When equipped, the **Golden Ox Pendant** grants the _Build Range: Golden Ox Pendant_ mod, which allows the player to build one tile further.
