The **Headsman's Axe** is a splicable hand item that was added alongside the Dungeon Pack on April 19, 2013.
##  Function
When equipped, the Headsman's Axe serves a fully cosmetic purpose.
Step 9 of the  Quest For The Blade requires the player to deliver 800 of this item.
##  Trivia
