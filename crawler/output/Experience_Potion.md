# Experience Potion

Drink this fizzy potion to gain 10,000 XP instantly! Only works on yourself, no pouring it down other peoples' throats.
Properties

This item never drops any seeds.

Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Experience Potion** for **10 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"This Untradeable delicious fizzy drink will make you smarter! 10,000 XP smarter instantly, to be exact."
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Experience Potion** is an unsplicable and untradeable consumable which was added as part of the Growtokens! update on March 30, 2014.
##  Function
When consumed, this item instantly gives 10,000 XP to the player. It can only be used on the player who owns the item.
### Silkworm Food     _Main article:Guide:Silkworm Care_
This consumable can be fed to a Silkworm to give it a aqua color, thus will produce 10  Silk Thread - Aqua on the next harvest. However, due to the Experience Potion's rarity, it isn't recommended to feed the item to a Silkworm.
