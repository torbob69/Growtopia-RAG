The **Mooncake Bundle** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2020 on September 24, 2020.
##  Function
Consuming a Mooncake Bundle will give the player an assortment of different items.
Item: QuantityHarmony Mooncake: ×0–10
Longevity Mooncake: ×0–11
Peace Mooncake: ×0–10
Prosperity Mooncake: ×0–10
Unity Mooncake: ×0–2
Popped Corn Head: ×0–1
##  Trivia
  * A Mooncake Bundle is only dropped as a bonus reward when a player equipping a Mooncake Magnificence receives double Balance Mooncake drops.
