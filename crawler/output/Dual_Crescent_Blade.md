The **Dual Crescent Blade** is an unsplicable hand item that was added as part of Summer Clash 2019.
##  Function
Slice is nice!

Nice slicin' - now take a break!

When equipped, the **Dual Crescent Blade** grants the _Punch Damage: Crescent Blade!_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. It also replaces the player's punching animation with a thrown blade that bounces back to the player.
