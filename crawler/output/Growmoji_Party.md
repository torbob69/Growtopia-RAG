# Growmoji Party

Don't be a party pooper - pop a party vibe into your chat! Just consume this to add it to your available growmojis!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **5 Hits** **4 Hits** – Restores after **8s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
The **Growmoji Party** is a Growmoji that was released as part of Player Appreciation Week 2018. One can be found in a Special Event. It allows the player to use the (party) growmoji.
