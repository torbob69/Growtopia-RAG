The **Pineapple Coronet** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2020 on June 4, 2020.
##  Function
Arise! You're royally jumping

Back down to earth

When equipped, the **Pineapple Coronet** grants the _Double Jump: Pineapple Coronet!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
The  The Seven Seas : The Ray of the Manta! mission requires 10 of this item.

##  Trivia
  * As referenced by the item's description, a coronet is the name given to a small or relatively simple crown intended to be worn by lesser royalty that rank below a sovereign.[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Pineapple_Coronet#cite_note-1)
  * As of Super Pineapple Party 2025, the Pineapple Coronet is no longer obtainable through Pineapple Magic.
