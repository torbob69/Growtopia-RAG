The **Prismatic Aura** is an unobtainable back item which is the Item Of The Month for September 2017, costing 200,000 gems in the Growtopia Store.
##  Function
Shiny rainbow fashion star growtopian!

Aw, back to your dull self.

When equipped, the **Prismatic Aura** grants the _Prismatic Aura_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
