The **Snack Fu** is a non-solid foreground block which was added as part of The Grand Tournament update.
##  Function
Punching or wrenching a Snack Fu while a Tournament is active opens a dialog box, allowing them to purchase Tournament snacks from the Snack Fu.
Consuming a Tournament snack may give the player a random block typically earned from the lower tiers of the Prize Ladder.
Snack — Price — Chance of PrizePow Stones — 50  Gems — 1%
Popcorn — 100  Gems — 2%
Ramune — 500  Gems — 10%
Wasabi Peas — 2,000  Gems — 50%
Can Of Beans — 10,000  Gems — 100%
Overall, the Wasabi Peas is considered the most efficient snack in terms of gems-to-prize ratio.
