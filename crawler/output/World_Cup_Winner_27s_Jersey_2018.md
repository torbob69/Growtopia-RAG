The **World Cup Winner's Jersey 2018** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as part of the Startopia update on July 1, 2018.
##  Function
When equipped, the World Cup Winner's Jersey 2018 serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * Before France was announced as the winner of the 2018 World Cup,[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/World_Cup_Winner's_Jersey_2018#cite_note-1) the developers added this item as a component item under the name of "World Cup Winner Jersey".[[2]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/World_Cup_Winner's_Jersey_2018#cite_note-2)
    * The item's description would state, "To make this jersey, we had to steal a sports almanac from the distant future and used it to find the winners of this year's World Cup! However, to prevent a paradox, we're keeping it in this time-locked box until the results are official!"[[3]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/World_Cup_Winner's_Jersey_2018#cite_note-3)
  * The World Cup Winner's Jersey 2018's item description is a reference to the on November 22, 1989 released American science fiction film Back to the Future Part II. In the movie, Biff Tannen steals the time machine and uses it to give an almanac containing the results of major sporting events from 1950 to 2000 to his younger self.
  * On June 2, 2023, the World Cup Winner's Jersey 2018 was removed from the Sportsball Pack and replaced by the World Cup Winner's Jersey 2022, making it unobtainable.
