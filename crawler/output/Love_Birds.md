The **Love Birds** is an unspliceable non-solid toggleable foreground block which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2018 on February 9, 2018.
##  Function
<https://growtopiawiki.com/w/File:Love_birds.ogg>The Love Birds' music.
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Love Birds can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Love Birds will change its appearance, showing a 2-frame animation, and play a piece of music.
