The **Fuel Pack** is an unsplicable back item which was added on Player Appreciation Week 2014 Day 2 on August 9, 2014.
##  Function
A Fuel Pack may be used to power up a Flamethrower. A single Fuel Pack will be consumed with each use of the Flamethrower.
A Fuel Pack can also be used with a Harvester or other similar items; there will be a 10% chance that a harvested tree (with a rarity of less than 100) will have its fruits doubled. The Fuel Pack will be consumed every time this occurs. Unlike the Fuel Pack, the Ecto Pack, Pineapple Juice, and Dangerous Pineapple Juice Backpack do not double fruit drops when harvesting trees.
Firing a T-Shirt Cannon while equipping a Fuel Pack will give the targeted player a Growtopia Fan Shirt, while firing an Easter Egg Cannon while equipping one will give an Easter Egg - Growtopia.
