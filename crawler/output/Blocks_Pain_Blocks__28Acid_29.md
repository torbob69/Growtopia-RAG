# Blocks/Pain Blocks (Acid)

< Blocks
**Pain Blocks (Acid)** knocks the player back and stings the player until the player stops all motion. A player will die after making contact with a pain block five times in quick succession (recovering from damage takes roughly 5 seconds). The _Fireproof_ mod will not allow the player to take more hits from Acid, unlike with lava pain blocks. Instead, the _Acid Proof_ mod, which is currently exclusively granted by the Krampus Crampons, serves this purpose instead.
