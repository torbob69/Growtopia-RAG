# Supporter

The **Supporter** rank is a special status that can be achieved in the game granting various quality-of-life benefits to the player. There is also a Super Supporter status, an upgrade from Supporter granting extra benefits.
##  Requirements
One can become a Supporter if they meet at least **one** of the following criteria:
  * The player has earned 26,000 Gems through MyChips offers or from watching ads.
  * The player has made at least 1 In-App Purchase.

##  Benefits
After achieving the Supporter rank, the player is granted special benefits and privileges. They include:
### Supporter Achievement
Supporters receive the Supporter achievement once they meet the criteria.
### Guild Creation
Supporters are able to create a Guild for 200,000 Gems. (100,000 Gems if the player is also Super Supporter)
### Billboard Feature
Supporters are able to access the Billboard feature for buying or selling in-game items.
### Access to the Recycle Tool
Supporters are able to use the Recycle Tool which can be used to recycle items for gems, replacing the Trash Tool in their inventory.
### Additional Skin Colors
Supporters gain access to 6 new skin colors; Red, Purple, Dark Green, Light Green, Aqua and White.
### Bypass Broadcast Level Requirement
Supporters are able to Broadcast without the need to reach level 21.
### Cancel Role Quests
Supporters are able to cancel and renew Role quests.
### Daily Challenges
Supporters are able to compete in the Daily Challenges.
### Access to Challenge Timer
Supporters are able to use the Challenge Timer.
##  Trivia
  * The requirements for Supporter were changed as part of Player Appreciation Week 2021.
    * The player must earn a total of 20,000 Gems through MyChips offers, previously 7,000.
