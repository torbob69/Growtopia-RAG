Sip some milkshakes and eat some fries while sitting at the Diner!

The **Diner Couch** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2022 on November 19, 2022.
##  Function
When a player stands in front of a seat such as the Diner Couch for a few seconds, the player will automatically be seen "sitting" on it.
