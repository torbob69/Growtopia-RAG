The **Time-Space Rupture** is a splicable non-solid portal foreground block which was added as part of Growtopia's launch.
##  Function
Being a type of portal, the Time-Space Rupture immediately moves the player to another tile when touched. A block with an ID must first be placed on the tile for this to work.
