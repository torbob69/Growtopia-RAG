The **Glowing Shirt** is a shirt item that was added as part of the Voucher Dayz update on May 28, 2021. It was first made purchasable along with the Plunger during May 28-30, 2021.
##  Function
When equipped, it serves a fully cosmetic purpose and shows an animation of the shirt glowing.
##  Trivia
  * The Glowing Shirt is one out of five items that were previously only available to the discontinued console edition of Growtopia, along with the Energy Drink, Plunger, The Brother Hood, and Unicorn.
    * All of them were obtained from Ubisoft Club Rewards and are now being released to the mobile/PC version of Growtopia through the Console Classix Pack.
