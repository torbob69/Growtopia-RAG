The **One-Way Block** is a splicable one-way foreground block which was added as part of Day 6 of Player Appreciation Week 2016 on August 10, 2016.
##  Function
Being a type of horizontal one side collision block, the **One-Way Block** allows players to pass through the block only in the direction it is facing.
