This is bait for fishing with three high-tech features: it appeals to all sizes and types of fish, it homes in on the largest fish around, and special filtering fins keep it from hooking on anything other than a fish!

The **Whizmo Gizmo** is a consumable which was added alongside the Fishin' Pack on September 11, 2015.
##  Function
Whizmo Gizmo can be used to catch fishes with a maximum size of 200lbs. It has a 66.67% fish catch rate (68.75% with Stonefish included).
A full list of items which can be caught with Whizmo Gizmo include:
  * Battle Trout
  * Electric Eel
  * Great White Shark
  * Jellyfish
  * Perch
  * Bass
  * Catfish
  * Dogfish
  * Dragon Turtle (exclusive to Whizmo Gizmo)
  * Gar
  * Goldfish
  * Mahi Mahi
  * Sand Shark
  * Stonefish (dark environment only)
  * Sunfish
  * Whale
