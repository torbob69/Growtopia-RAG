The **Golden Talaria** is a feet item that was added as part of Valentine's Week 2018.
##  Function
Run like the wind!

Your feet are back on the ground!

When equipped, the **Golden Talaria** grants the _Fleet of Foot_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.
Step 17 of the  Quest For Candour requires the player to deliver 3 of this item.
##  Trivia
  * In Roman mythology, the Talaria of Mercury are winged sandals owned by the messenger god Mercury.
