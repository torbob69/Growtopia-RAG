The **Bathtub** is a splicable non-solid SFX foreground block which has been added since Growtopia's launch.
##  Function
Being an SFX foreground block, a Bathtub makes a shower noise and plays a water animation when punched.
Players have to stand over a Bathtub when applying Shampoo or hair dye (Black Hair Dye, Red Hair Dye, Blue Hair Dye, or Green Hair Dye).
