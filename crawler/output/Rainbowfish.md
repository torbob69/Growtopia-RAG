##  Trivia
  * A Magical Rainbow Fishing Rod and a Catch-of-the-Day Bait can be used to catch a Rainbowfish anytime, mimicking the maximum weight of the catch-of-the-day. Should the Whale or Sand Shark be selected as the catch-of-the-day, one could potentially catch a 200lb. Rainbowfish.
