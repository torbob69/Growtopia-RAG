The **World Owner Blingin' Chain** is an unsplicable neck item which was added as April 2023's Item Of The Season on April 2, 2023.
##  Function
Out of bright!

Out of sight!

When equipped, the **World Owner Blingin' Chain** grants the _Punch Damage + Double Jump: World Owner Blingin' Chain!_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and jump a second time in mid-air.
