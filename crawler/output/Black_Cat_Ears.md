The **Black Cat Ears** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2019 Day 3 on August 21, 2019.
##  Function
When equipped, the Black Cat Ears serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * An update on August 23, 2019 saw a redesign to the Black Cat Ears. JamSandwich, who worked as part of the developer team, pushed for this change after reviewing the playerbase's reaction towards the previous design.[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Black_Cat_Ears#cite_note-1)
    * The old version of the sprites could be found in player_cosmetics1.rttex and player_cosmetics1_icon.rttex, and no longer exist in the current versions of the files.
