# Golden Silk Scarf

The finest silk was once weaved for a great goddess but now it adorns your shoulders. Imbued with the goddess' power, this scarf will lift you through the air and make your feet glide lightly wherever they fall.

**Golden Silk Scarf** is a neck item that was added as part of Valentine's Week 2021 on February 13, 2021.
##  Function
Float through the air with the greatest of ease!

Back down to earth!

When equipped, the **Golden Silk Scarf** grants the _Double Jump + Slow Fall: Silk Scarf_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
Step 14 of the  Quest Of The Owl requires the player to deliver 1 of this item. Neptune's Weather Machine - Atlantis can be given as an alternative.
