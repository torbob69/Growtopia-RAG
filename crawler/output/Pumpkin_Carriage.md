The **Pumpkin Carriage** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2024 on October 24, 2024.
##  Function
I'm something of royalty myself!

You know how much I've sacrificed?!

When equipped, the **Pumpkin Carriage** grants the _High Jump + Speedy: Pumpkin Carriage_ mod, which allows the player to jump one tile higher and move faster on land.
