# Video Of The Week

The **Video Of The Week** , abbreviated as **VOTW** , is a weekly award that was added with the Cinema Pack on May 16, 2014. Previously, it was chosen by @Hamumu. Now, it is chosen by the Ubisoft moderators.
Contestants can nominate their videos by posting it on the Growtopia Forums, or by using the _!submit VOTW_ command in the Official Growtopia Discord Server.
##  Rules
  * The length of the video must be 1 minute to 30 minutes.
  * Videos can only submitted 3x per week!
  * Contestants who have already won a VOTW are less likely to win another one in the same month.
  * Contestants who have been banned for more than 3 days in the last 6 months will not be considered. However, if the punishment was cancelled before it expired, the player will still be eligible to win VOTW.
  * The video has to be family-friendly.
  * The video has to be related to Growtopia.

##  Rewards
Reward: AmountGrowie Award: 1
Growtoken: 20
If a video is awarded with VOTW, it appears in the list of Video Of The Week Winners.
In addition, their video is featured on the Growtopia Gazette.
##  Trivia
  * The first **Video Of The Week** was Pharrell Williams - Happy by GrowNoobShow.
