##  Function
Oh lots of fancy numbers!

Too much weird maths!

When equipped, the **Matrix Aura** grants the _Double Jump : Matrix Aura_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
Step 9 of the  Quest Of The Mech requires the player to deliver 10 of this item.
The  The Seven Seas : The Heart of the Ocean! mission requires 1 of this item.
