Find your fall footing with these turkey toes! Gobble gobble gobble!

The **Turkey Onesie Feet** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2017 Day 4 on November 26, 2017.
##  Function
When equipped, the Turkey Onesie Feet serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Turkey set, which consists of the following items:

    * Turkey Onesie Hood (Hair)

    * Turkey Onesie Beak (Face)

    * Turkey Onesie Body (Shirt)

    * Turkey Onesie Legs (Pants)

    * Turkey Onesie Feet (Feet)

    * Turkey Onesie Tail (Back)

    * Turkey Carving Set (Hand)
  * In 2017, the Turkey Onesie Feet could be obtained as a random drop by smashing blocks on the fourth day of Thanksgiving Week 2017.
