The **Scarf of Chains** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as January 2023's Item Of The Season on January 6, 2023.
##  Function
Chained to power!

Broken Chains!

When equipped, the **Scarf of Chains** grants the _Double Jump + Slowfall: Scarf of Chains_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
