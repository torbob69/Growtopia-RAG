The **Monkey Wrench Decoration** is a wrench decoration which was added as part of the February Season on February 1, 2026.
##  Function
When equipped through the 'Wrench Customization' tab when wrenching oneself, the Monkey Wrench Decoration adds a tail to the player's wrench connected to their name.
