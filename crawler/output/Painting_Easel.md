Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Paintbrush **(×1)** ,
Art Wall **(×20)** and
Transmog Crystal **(×1)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Painting Easel** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of the Artsy April update.
##  Function
A Painting Easel allows players with access to the block to paint any item in their inventory on it. Doing so costs 2 of each Paint Bucket (besides Paint Bucket - Varnish).
Removing a painting from an easel costs 4 Paint Bucket - Varnish.
##  Trivia
A Flashback Flan cannot be used on the **Painting Easel**.
