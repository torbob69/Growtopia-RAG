The **Terracotta Growtopian** is an unsplicable solid foreground block that was added as part of Lunar New Year 2021.
##  Function
When placed, the Terracotta Growtopian serves a fully aesthetic purpose. The item has four different textures and when placed, a random one will appear. The four textures are:
  * A Terracotta Growtopian with a mad expression.
  * A Terracotta Growtopian with a wry expression.
  * A Terracotta Growtopian showing a stuck-out tongue expression.
  * A Terracotta Growtopian showing a sleeping expression.

##  Trivia
  * This item is inspired from the Terracotta Army, depicting the armies of Qin Shi Huang, the first Emperor of China.
