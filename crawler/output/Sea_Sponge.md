The **Sea Sponge** is an unsplicable solid trampoline foreground block which was added as part of Role Up! update on September 17, 2018.
##  Function
Jumping on a trampoline block such as a Sea Sponge will cause the player to bounce, and by holding the jump button, the player can jump even higher. The _High Jump_ mod also boosts one's jump height on a trampoline block.

##  Trivia
  * The description of the Sea Sponge refers to the fact that the word "sponge" can refer to any of various marine invertebrates of the phylum Porifera, or a piece of porous material commonly used for washing.[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Sea_Sponge#cite_note-1)
