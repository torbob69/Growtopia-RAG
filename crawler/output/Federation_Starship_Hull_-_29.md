The **Federation Starship Hull - 29** is an unobtainable and unbreakable platform foreground block which was added as part of Star Trek Prodigy update on June 27, 2022. It is used as the outer part for the Protostar Starship.
##  Function
A Federation Starship Hull - 29 behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath.
