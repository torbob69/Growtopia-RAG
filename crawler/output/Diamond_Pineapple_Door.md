The **Diamond Pineapple Door** is an unsplicable non-solid door foreground block which was added as part of the Pre-WinterFest 2022 update on December 1, 2022.
##  Function
As a door, the Diamond Pineapple Door can be entered (via punching) by a player, moving them instantly to another tile. A block with an ID must first be placed on the tile for this to work.
##  Trivia
  * The  Diamond Pineapple Door is a recolor of the  Super Pineapple Door.
