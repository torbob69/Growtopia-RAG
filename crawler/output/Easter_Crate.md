The **Easter Crate** is an unsplicable and untradable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Easter Week 2019.
##  Function
An Easter Crate cannot be broken by other players, even when placed in a public area.
When broken, an Easter Crate will drop:
  * 20-40×  Magic Eggs
  * 1×  Golden Egg Shard - Middle
  * 1× random Easter Egg
    * Easter Egg - Striped
    * Easter Egg - Jagged
    * Easter Egg - Wavey
    * Easter Egg - Checkered
    * Easter Egg - Polka Dots
    * Easter Egg - Dark
    * Easter Egg - Rainbow (extremely rare)

##  Trivia
  * As of Easter Week 2021, the Easter Crate no longer drops Pastel Suit, Pastel Pants, Wicker Basket, Bunny Mask, Bunny Ears, Bunny Tail, and Hippie Sandals.
