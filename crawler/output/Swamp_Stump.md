The **Swamp Stump** is an unsplicable platform foreground block which was added as part of Wildlife Weekend on March 10, 2023.
##  Function
An Swamp Stump behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath.
##  Trivia
  * The Swamp Stump's description is a reference to Star, a released pop song by the American rock band Smash Mouth on May 4, 1999.
