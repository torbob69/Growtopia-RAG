The **Marching Band Jacket** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2023 on November 17, 2023.
##  Function
When equipped, the Marching Band Jacket serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Marching Band set, which consists of the following items:

    * Marching Band Hat (hat)

    * Marching Band Jacket (shirt)

    * Marching Band Pants (pants)

    * Band Leader's Baton (hand)
