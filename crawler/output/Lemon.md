The **Lemon** is a splicable consumable which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2017 on May 5, 2017.
##  Function
When consumed on a player, the Lemon will make the player cry while saying "AH MY EYES!!!" in red text.
### Silkworm Food     _Main article:Guide:Silkworm Care_
This consumable can be fed to a Silkworm to give it a yellow color, thus allowing it to produce 1–10  Silk Thread - Yellow per harvest.
