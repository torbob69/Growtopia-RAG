The **Golden Egg Carton** is an unsplicable and untradable consumable which was added as part of Easter Week 2021 on April 1, 2021.
##  Function
When consumed, a Golden Egg Carton gives the player one of the following:
  * Golden Egg Shard - Top (rare)
  * Golden Egg Shard - Middle
  * Golden Egg Shard - Bottom

and one of the following:
  * Balloon Backpack (epic)
  * Easter Clouds (epic)
  * Easter Egg - Checkered
  * Easter Egg - Dark
  * Easter Egg - Ghost
  * Easter Egg - Growtopia
  * Easter Egg - Jagged
  * Easter Egg - Pineapple
  * Easter Egg - Polka Dots
  * Easter Egg - Rainbow (epic)
  * Easter Egg - Striped
  * Easter Egg - Wavey
  * Easter Viking Cape (epic)
  * Pogo Stick (epic)
  * Rocket Block (rare)

##  Easter Gold Streak
When the Golden Egg Carton unlocks in the Growtopia Store, the player will be given a free reward for every 20 Golden Egg Carton purchased. Additionally, a claim milestone is available where claiming rewards during the event increases the chance of getting an epic reward. The base chance is 5.17% chance of getting an epic item, which increases by 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, and 6x, for every 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, and 5th claim, respectively.
##  Trivia
  * Consuming a Golden Egg Carton is currently the only known method to receive a Golden Egg Shard - Top.
