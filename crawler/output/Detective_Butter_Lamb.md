**Detective Butter Lamb** is a consumable that was added as part of Easter Week 2021 on April 1, 2021.
##  Function
When consumed, a Detective Butter Lamb reveals a random part of a sewing recipe and displays it on the player's chat box, similar to how Secret Of Growtopia works.
##  Trivia
  * The Detective Butter Lamb is one of the three items added in Easter Week 2021 which was based on the real-life Easter tradition with the same name, alongside the Home Made Butter Lamb and Failed Butter Lamb.
    * In real life, a butter lamb is made by sculpting butter into a lamb, either by hand or in a lamb-shaped mold. This Easter food traditionally accompanies the Easter meal for many Russian, Slovenian and Polish Catholics.
