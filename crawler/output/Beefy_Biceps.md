The **Beefy Biceps** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as a part of the Valentine's Week 2020 update on February 13, 2021.
##  Function
Whoa Momma!

Wimpy arms!

When equipped, the **Beefy Biceps** grants the _Punch Damage : Beefy Biceps!_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster.
