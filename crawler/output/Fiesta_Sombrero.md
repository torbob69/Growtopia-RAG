The **Fiesta Sombrero** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2013 on May 3, 2013.
##  Function
When equipped, streamer and confetti particles come out of the Fiesta Sombrero as the player moves around. The Fiesta Sombrero serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
