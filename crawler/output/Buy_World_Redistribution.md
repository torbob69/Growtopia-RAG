# Buy World Redistribution

Back in 2018, official staff members would redistribute worlds related to newly released items, most of which had been locked by dataminers.
##  March 2018
The worlds redistributed during this period are the ones locked by dataminers before the Cyblocks and Growmojis update went live on March 1, 2018.[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Buy_World_Redistribution#cite_note-1) The winners of the first batch of worlds are chosen randomly from the replies to an official Twitter post, while the winners of the second batch are chosen from the replies to a different Twitter post.

##  July 2018
The worlds redistributed during this period are the ones locked by dataminers before the Startopia update went live on July 1, 2018.[[3]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Buy_World_Redistribution#cite_note-3) Each winner is chosen randomly from the replies to an official forum thread and Twitter post.
New rules added for this session prohibited winners from accepting rentals or selling the worlds to other players. Players who've been found to violate this rule would supposedly have their worlds taken away without any prior warning.
