The **Jankom Pog's Multi-Mitt** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Star Trek Prodigy on June 27, 2022.
##  Function
Jankom power up!

Jankom power Down!

When equipped, the **Jankom Pog's Multi-Mitt** grants the _Build Range & Punch Range: Jankom Pog's Multi-Mitt!_ mod, which allows the player to build one tile further, punch one tile further, and switch between its wearable form and its morph form by wrenching oneself and clicking 'Transform'.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Jankom Pog set, which consists of the following items:

    * Jankom Pog's Hair (Hair)

    * Jankom Pog's Goggles (Face)

    * Jankom Pog's Shirt (Shirt)

    * Jankom Pog's Pants (Pants)

    * Jankom Pog's Shoes (Feet)

    * Jankom Pog's Multi-Mitt (Hand)
