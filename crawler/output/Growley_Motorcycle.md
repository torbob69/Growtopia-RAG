Purchase the **Growley Motorcycle** for **50,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"The coolest motorcycles available are Growley Dennisons. Get a sporty blue one today! It even moves faster than walking, which is pretty good for a motorcycle. Not available any other way!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Growley Motorcycle** is an unsplicable feet item that was added as part of Halloween Week 2014 on October 31, 2014.
##  Function
You can move faster!

You are slow again.

When equipped, the **Growley Motorcycle** grants the _Speedy_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.

##  Trivia
  * The Growley Motorcycle is based on the motorcycles manufactured by the real-life motorcycle company Harley Davidson.
    * The phrase "_Growley Dennison_ " is a reference to the company's name.
