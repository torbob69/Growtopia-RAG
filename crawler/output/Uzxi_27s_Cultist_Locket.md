The **uzxi's Cultist Locket** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as a part of Player Appreciation Week 2025 on September 18, 2025.
##  Function
Locked In!

Locked Out!

When equipped, the **uzxi's Cultist Locket** grants the _Double Jump + Punch Range : uzxi's Cultist Locket_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air, punch one tile further, and switch between its wearable form and its morph form by wrenching oneself and clicking 'Transform'.
##  Trivia
  * This item is sponsored by uzxi for completing all the Epic Quests.
  * uzxi's Cultist Locket is a recolored version of  Datemaster's Heart Locket, but it lacks the visual effects.
  * Since **uzxi's Cultist Locket** starts with a lower case letter, it is placed at the end of the player's inventory instead of being placed in alphabetical order like other items.
