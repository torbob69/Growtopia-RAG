The **Sweet Potato Tots** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2020.
##  Function
Now we are cooking on gas!

Too hot!

When consumed, this item grants the _Cooking Boost: Sweet Potato Tots_ mod for 30 minutes, which increases cooked food's quality by 10%. This effect stacks with other items that provide a similar function, such as the Exquisite Rolling Pin and Dumplings Hat.
