The **Steam Scrambler** is a solid steam foreground block which was added as part of the Gone Crabby! update on March 3, 2016.
##  Function
When steam passes through the block, it will exit through a random side (excluding from where it came in).
##  Trivia
  * The Steam Scrambler and Steam Collector were both added in the same update.
