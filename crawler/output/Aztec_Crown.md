Participate in successfully smashing an  Ultra Pinata with a  Lucky Pinata Hat equipped during the **Pinata Bash!** event.
May give **1** item each time.
The **Aztec Crown** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2023 on May 5, 2023.
##  Function
Leaves like the wind!

Leaves hate the wind!

When equipped, the **Aztec Crown** grants the _Punch Range + Build Range: Aztec Crown_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further, build one tile further, move faster on land, and jump a second time in mid-air.
