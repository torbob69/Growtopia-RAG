The **Ultra Sportsball Bat** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2020.
##  Function
Batter up, home run!

Strike 3, you're out!

When equipped, the **Ultra Sportsball Bat** grants the _Punch Range: Ultra Sportsball Bat_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further. It will also replace the player's punching animation with a baseball ball.
