##  Function
<https://growtopiawiki.com/w/File:Jazz_loop.ogg>The Victrola's music
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Victrola can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Victrola will change its appearance, showing a 2-frame animation, and play a piece of music.
