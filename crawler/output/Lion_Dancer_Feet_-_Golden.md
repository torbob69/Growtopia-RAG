The **Lion Dancer Feet - Golden** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2023 on September 21, 2023.
##  Function
When equipped, the Lion Dancer Feet - Golden serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
When equipped with Lion Dancer Cape - Golden, Lion Dancer Mask - Golden, Lion Dancer Pants - Golden, and Lion Dancer Shirt - Golden, the player will gain an extra pair of legs resembling a Lion Dancer costume.
