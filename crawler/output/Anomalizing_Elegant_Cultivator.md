The **Anomalizing Elegant Cultivator** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the Role Up! update.
##  Function
Anomalizing Elegant Cultivators are used to score points during Speedy Splicers. While equipping this item, each tree spliced yields 220 points. Elegant anomalizers, such as this item, may rarely break upon usage during their respective Grow Events.
