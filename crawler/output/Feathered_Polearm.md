The **Feathered Polearm** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2023 on November 17, 2023.
##  Function
It's stuffin' time!

Stuffed to the brim!

When equipped, the **Feathered Polearm** grants the _Punch Damage + Punch Range: Feathered Polearm_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and punch one tile further.
