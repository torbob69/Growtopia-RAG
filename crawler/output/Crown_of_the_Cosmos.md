The **Crown of the Cosmos** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as January 2021's Item Of The Month on January 2, 2021.
##  Function
Cosmos, I command thee!

Cosmic Crash!

When equipped, the **Crown of the Cosmos** grants the _Double Jump: Crown of the Cosmos_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
##  Trivia
  * Crown of the Cosmos was the first IOTM which had its price increased to 350,000 Gems (previously 200,000 Gems).
