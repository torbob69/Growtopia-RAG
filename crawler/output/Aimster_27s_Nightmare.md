This item was given to **Aimster** for their contributions as a voluntary moderator.
The **Aimster's Nightmare** is an unsplicable feet item exclusive to game Moderator @Aimster, rewarded on September 24, 2013.
##  Function
You can move faster!

You are slow again.

When equipped, the **Aimster's Nightmare** grants the _Speedy_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land. It also emits flames from its hooves, just like the Phoenix Wings.
