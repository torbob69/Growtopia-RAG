The **Enchanted Broomstick** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Voucher Dayz on February 24, 2022. The item was formally introduced on February 25, 2022, the date when the event arrived.
##  Function
Rotten to the core!

What a world, what a world!

When equipped, the **Enchanted Broomstick** grants the _Enchanted Broomstick: Speedy + High Jump_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and jump one tile higher.
