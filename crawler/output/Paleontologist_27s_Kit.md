# Paleontologist's Kit
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Paleontologist's Kit** for **20,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"If you want to dig up fossils, this is the kit for you! Includes everything you need: 5 Fossil Brushes, a Rock Hammer and a Rock Chisel, a Blue Hardhat for safety, and a Rare Fossil Prep Station to get your fossils ready for display."
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The Paleontologist's Kit is a pack introduced on 8 October 2016 as part of the Growassic Park update. It is available from the Growtopia Store for 20,000 Gems.
##  Items
The pack includes:
  * Fossil Brush x5
  * Rock Hammer x1
  * Rock Chisel x1
  * Blue Hardhat x1
  * Fossil Prep Station x1
