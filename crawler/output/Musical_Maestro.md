The **Musical Maestro** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as November 2024's Item Of The Season on November 1st, 2024.
##  Function
Precise DJ!

How the turntables...

When equipped, the **Musical Maestro** grants the _Punch Range: Musical Maestro_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further. In addition, punching or dancing plays the Boombox song on loop.
