The **Tombstone** is a splicable non-solid foreground sign block which was added alongside the Zombie Defense Pack on May 17, 2013.
##  Function
Being a sign, a Tombstone allows players with access to it to edit its message for other players to view.
Step 9 of the  Quest For Honor requires the player to deliver 1,000 of this item.
