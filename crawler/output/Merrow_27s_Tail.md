The Merrow's Tail is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2024 on March 14, 2024.
##  Function
Under the water, Ya we in luck here!

What are they called again, oh... feet!

When equipped, the **Merrow's Tail** grants the _Speedy + Surfing: Merrow's Tail_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and float on water for three seconds.
Additionally, Merrow's Tail also changes color depending on the pants item the player is wearing.
##  Trivia
  * Merrow's Tail is a reference to _Merrows_ , who are depicted as mermaids or mermans in Irish folklore.
