# Mutated Cell

Strange mutated cells collected during the Zombie Apocalypse. If you combine 200 of them, you might be able to create new mutant life...
Mutated Cell (Rarity: 10)
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **4 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **5s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **0 - 2**
**21m 40s**
Infect a Player

| May be earned from punching another player while having the zombie status effect during a g-Virus Pandemic.
→  _A Vile Vial - Moldy Guts may also be earned (available anytime)._  |
Randomly Spawning

Randomly spawns at certain intervals in worlds (the more players are inside, the more will spawn).
This item only appears during Zombie Apocalypse.
The **Mutated Cell** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Night of the Sewing Dead update.
