The **Fancy Flute** is an unsplicable hand item added as part of Valentine's Week 2022 on February 11, 2022.
##  Function
Ya like Jazz?

I'll take that as a no

When equipped, the **Fancy Flute** grants the _Punch Damage: Fancy Flute!_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. It will also play a flute SFX and emit spontaneous bursts of note-like particles when punching.
