The **Pet Battle Update** is an update which was released on March 19, 2025.
##  Pet Battle Changes
There are six Gym worlds: Terraforge, Zephyrwing, Abyssal Tide, Emberflame, Stormcrag, and Pyrodelve. To access these gym worlds, players can wrench themselves and click on 'Battle Pets'. Players need to battle six Pet Battle Gym Trainers and one Pet Battle Gym Leader to earn the badge of honor on each gym. Collecting all six badges is required to purchase a Tournament Ticket from Master Peng at The Grand Tournament.
The health points (HP) of pets no longer regenerate automatically after each battle. Instead, players must use Pet Bandage by wrenching oneself and clicking on 'Battle Pets' under the 'Leashed Pets' tab or visiting Pet Battle Gym Healers — found in each Gym world — to restore both their pets' health. Players can hold up to 5 Pet Bandages which regenerate per hour or players can purchase five for $2.99.
