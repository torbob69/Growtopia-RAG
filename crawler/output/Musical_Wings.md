The **Musical Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Wing Week 2020 Day 6 on October 6, 2020.
##  Function
A symphonic high!

All the low notes!

When equipped, the **Musical Wings** grants the _Double Jump : Musical Wings!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air. Additionally, these wings are able to play up to 16 music notes, and each note is triggered as the player passes over a block. The player can change the notes by wrenching themselves.
