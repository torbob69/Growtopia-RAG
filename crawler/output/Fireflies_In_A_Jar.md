# Fireflies In A Jar

You would not believe how many of these guys light up the world as you sleep.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data: Paint PreviewType — Foreground Block – Foreground — Aqua Blue Charcoal Green Purple Red Varnish Yellow
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **3 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **4s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Paint Preview Aqua Blue Charcoal Green Purple Red Varnish Yellow
Party Reward

It has a chance to drop as one of the five items from the _Anniversary Party!_ special event.
In addition to spawning randomly during Anniversary Week, the event can also be spawned directly from consuming a  Party-In-A-Box.
The **Fireflies In A Jar** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Anniversary Week 2022 on January 6, 2022.
##  Function
As a light source, a placed Fireflies In A Jar emits light in dark areas.
##  Trivia
  * The description of Fireflies In A Jar is a reference to the lyrics of "Fireflies",[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Fireflies_In_A_Jar#cite_note-1) a song by the electronic music group Owl City.
  * When the Fireflies In A Jar was first released in Anniversary Week, a glitch caused the item's animation not to play at all.
