The **Rollback Plaque** is a solid foreground block made to commemorate the first rollback in Growtopia, and was available during the Apology Weekend that followed it on February 23, 2013.
##  Function
When placed, Rollback Plaque serves a fully aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * Rollback Plaques were originally released as "perma-items," which meant it returned directly to the player's inventory when broken. After the item became unsplicable, it was changed to never drop anything when broken.
  * When the Rollback Plaque was released, the item's seed was available as a random item from the Small Seed Pack. This was an oversight which was patched shortly after it was discovered, but any seeds which were given out were not removed, making the Rollback Plaque Seed a highly valued collectable.
  * When asked by a forum user regarding whether a version of the Rollback Plaque with a happy face would be added,[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Rollback_Plaque#cite_note-1) ex-developer Hamumu answered that "This is a somber occasion. We will smile another day."[[2]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Rollback_Plaque#cite_note-2) The recipe of the Rollback Plaque was replaced by the  Happy Joy Plaque directly after the Rollback Plaque was discontinued.
