# Dead Geiger Counter

This Geiger Counter is out of battery power. It will charge up from the static electricity you generate all the time, so just hold it in your hand until it's charged.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Hand – Clothes

**1h**
The **Dead Geiger Counter** is an unsplicable hand item. One can be obtained from finding an item using with a Geiger Counter.
##  Function
Your Geiger Counter is charging...

Geiger Counter is no longer charging!

When equipped, the **Dead Geiger Counter** grants the _Charging Geiger Counter_ mod. It will allow the player to recharge a Geiger Counter for around 30 minutes to full charge (~1% every 18 seconds), as long as the player does not unequip it or stay in the EXIT menu.
##  Trivia
  * Players can use Hot Chocolate on themselves to remove the _Irradiated_ mod and instantly charge the Geiger Counter.
  * During Geiger Day, Dead Geiger Counters charge twice as fast (1% power every 10 second, which means it will fully charge after 16 minutes and 40 seconds).
