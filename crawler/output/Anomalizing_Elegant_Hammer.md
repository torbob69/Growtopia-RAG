The **Anomalizing Elegant Hammer** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the Role Up! update.
##  Function
Anomalizing Elegant Hammers are used to score points during Block Bashers. While equipping this item, each block punched yields 170 points. Elegant anomalizers, such as this item, may rarely break upon usage during their respective Grow Events.
