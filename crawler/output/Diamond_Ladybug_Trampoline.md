The **Diamond Ladybug Trampoline** is an unsplicable solid foreground block which was added as part of Pre-WinterFest 2025 on December 1, 2025.
##  Function
Jumping on a trampoline block such as a Diamond Ladybug Trampoline will cause the player to bounce, and by holding the jump button, the player can jump even higher. The _High Jump_ mod also boosts one's jump height on a trampoline block.
##  Trivia
  * The  Diamond Ladybug Trampoline is a recolor of the  Ladybug Trampoline.
