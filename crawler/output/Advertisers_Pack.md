# Advertisers Pack
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Advertisers Pack** for **10,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"With this pack you can build custom Ad billboards in your world. There are 3 sizes you can choose from, 4x3, 3x2 and 7x1. You need to arrange these in a grid to activate the Ad. Remember to check your Gem earnings each day using the Ad Control Block."
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Advertisers Pack** is a store pack that was added as part of the Growtopia ADventures update on June 22, 2021.
The pack includes:
Items

Ad Control Block x1
Ad Block 2x3 x24
Ad Block 3x2 x12
Ad Block 6x1 x14
