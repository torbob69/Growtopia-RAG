# Armored WinterBot - Greaves

One of five legendary pieces of the great WinterBot, guardian of the Winter Fortress. Gives you an icy stance, not sure if it's the resolve of guarding the fortress for eternity or just that you've lost all feeling in your legs.

**Armored WinterBot - Greaves** is an unpslicable pants item which was added as a part of WinterFest 2019.
##  Function
When equipped, the **Armored WinterBot - Greaves** serves a purely cosmetic purpose. However, equipping the entire Armored WinterBot set will transform the player into the Armored WinterBot.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Armored WinterBot set, which consists of the following items:

    * Armored WinterBot - Back (back)

    * Armored WinterBot - Mail (shirt)

    * Armored WinterBot - Greaves (pants)

    * Armored WinterBot - Feet (feet)

    * Armored WinterBot - Fists (hand)
