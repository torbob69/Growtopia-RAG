# El Peligro

**El Peligro** is a villain added during Cinco De Mayo Week 2015. It is an event-limited villain and can only be found during Cinco De Mayo Week. He can be summoned by consuming a Crime Wave (chance) during Cinco De Mayo Week or by consuming a Luchador Announcer.
Cards he uses

Superpower - Atomic Backbreaker
Superpower - Frozen Mirror
Superpower - Super Strength
Superpower - Super Speed
Superpower - Shocking Fist
Health: 15
Method: Bring Liquify, Frost Breath, Frozen Mirror, Super Speed and Regeneration.
Use Super Speed and try to Liquify _El Peligro'_ s Shocking Fist.
Once you Liquify his Shocking Fist, repeat Frost Breath and Frozen Mirror until he dies.
If he uses Atomic Backbreaker, use Regeneration.
Upon defeating, the possible rewards to receive are:
  * Henchman
  * Seductive Rose
  * Luchador Championship Belt
  * China Poblano Dress
  * Villain Portrait - El Peligro
  * Superpower - Atomic Backbreaker
  * Luchador Mask - El Astro
  * Luchador Jersey - El Astro
  * Luchador Tights - El Astro
  * Luchador Mask - El Peligro
  * Luchador Jersey - El Peligro
  * Luchador Tights - El Peligro
  * Luchador Mask - El Tigre
  * Luchador Jersey - El Tigre
  * Luchador Tights - El Tigre
  * Luchador Boots
  * Avocado Advocate
  * Macho Mustache
  * Magma Hair
  * Prickly Pet
