The **Olde Timey Radio** is a spliceable solid toggleable animated foreground block which has been added since Growtopia's launch.
##  Function
<https://growtopiawiki.com/w/File:Old_timey.ogg>The Olde Timey Radio's music.
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Olde Timey Radio can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Olde Timey Radio will change its appearance, showing a 2-frame animation, and play a piece of music.
