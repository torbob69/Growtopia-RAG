**Hellfire Horns - Orange** is an unsplicable hat item which was the Item Of The Month for April 2019. The item, along with the Hellfire Horns - Blue, Black and Ruby, was one of the the possible drops from purchasing April 2019 Item Of The Month for 200,000 gems in the Gem Store.
##  Function
Raising hell!

Your fire has been doused!

When equipped, the **Hellfire Horns - Orange** grants the _Punch Range & Fireproof: Hellfire_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further and take 50% less damage from lava pain blocks. It will also replace the player's punching animation with orange flames coming out from the horns.
