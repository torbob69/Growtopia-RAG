The **Pet Leprechaun** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2014 on March 14, 2014.
##  Function
Your friend shoots rainbows!

You are lonely.

When equipped, this item summons a leprechaun to follow the player and grants them the _Rainbows!_ mod, which substitutes the player's punches with rainbows fired from the leprechaun. The rainbows deal more damage to blocks than the unequipped fist. The player's character will hold a four-leaf clover when equipping the Pet Leprechaun.
##  Trivia
  * Upon usage of ten Blarney Pebbles, a message will appear saying "_ZING! A leprechaun appears!_ ", and the Pet Leprechaun will be instantly equipped.
  * The inventory image used to be a four-leaf clover, but it was patched on April 10, 2013 to show the Leprechaun instead.
