The **Mechanical Butler** is an unsplicable face item which was added as September 2020’s Item Of The Month on September 1, 2020.
##  Function
Tea for 2 and 2 for tea!

Down to the tea leaves!

When equipped, the **Mechanical Butler** grants the _Mechanical Butler : Punch Damage & Punch Range_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and punch one tile further. A robotic butler pet will also appear and follow the player around. The item replaces the player's punch effect with teacups being thrown by the pet.
When punching a player, the targeted player will receive the _Soaked!_ mod, which shows tea particles dropping from their body.
