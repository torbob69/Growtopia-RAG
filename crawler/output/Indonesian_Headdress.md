# Indonesian Headdress

A luxurious headdress originating from Indonesia!

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Emerald Block **(×1)** ,
Turkey Feather **(×1)** and
Pineapple Coronet **(×1)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Indonesian Headdress** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2021 Day 7 on August 29, 2021.
##  Function
When equipped, the Indonesian Headress serves a fully cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia

    * Indonesian Skirt (Pants)
