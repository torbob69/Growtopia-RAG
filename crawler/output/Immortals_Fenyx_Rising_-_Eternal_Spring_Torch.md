##  Function
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Immortal Fenyx Rising - Eternal Spring Torch can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Immortal Fenyx Rising - Eternal Spring Torch will change its appearance, showing it being lit up. As a light source, a Immortal Fenyx Rising - Eternal Spring Torch emits light in dark areas.
