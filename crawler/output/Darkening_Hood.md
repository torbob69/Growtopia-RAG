The **Darkening Hood** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as June 2019's Monthly Subscriber's Item on June 1, 2019.
##  Function
When equipped, the Darkening Hood serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The Darkening Hood is based on the fictional character Reaper from the 2016 video game _Overwatch_.
  * On June 2, 2019, the Darkening Hood was renamed from Maskie Hood since the names of monthly subscriber's items are not meant to reference their designers' own.
