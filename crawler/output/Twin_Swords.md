# Twin Swords

Why waste time with a shield? Just get two swords!
Properties
This item can kill zombies during a Pandemic!
This item never drops any seeds.

Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Gift of the Unicorn **(×1)** ,
Sword Pommel **(×2)** and
Sword Blade **(×2)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Twin Swords** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the Daily Challenges update on November 18, 2015.
##  Function
You slice and dice!

Back to punching.

When equipped, the **Twin Swords** grants the _Slasher_ mod, which allows the player to kill zombies during a Pandemic. It will also replace the player's punching animation with sword slashes.
