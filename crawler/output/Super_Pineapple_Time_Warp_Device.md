The **Super Pineapple Time Warp Device** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2021 on June 3, 2021.
##  Function
Consuming a Super Pineapple Time Warp Device instantly gives the player their _Pineapple Magic_ prize. This item can only be used on the player's own self.
Attempting to consume the item without having the mod will cause the player to say the message, "_You don't have any Pineapple Magic!]_ "
