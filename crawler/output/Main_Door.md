The **Main Door** , also known as the White Door, is a door that is placed in a random location in a world. This door cannot be wrenched or be broken.
Players can exit a world using the **Main Door** by standing in front of it and punching it or by pressing the punch button. Along with the **Main Door** , a Bedrock is also generated below it.
A Door Mover can be used to change the location of the **Main Door** in a world.
##  Trivia
  * In the past, the **Main Door** in HELL displayed 'go go go...'.
  * The **Main Door** always spawns on the height of 24 blocks, the bedrock under it on the height of 23 blocks.
  * Trying to break the **Main Door** displays a message which reads: _"(stand over and punch to use)"_.
  * The Howler, the Trickster and the Murder Hole - Hot Liquid Tar cannot kill players standing on the **Main Door**.
