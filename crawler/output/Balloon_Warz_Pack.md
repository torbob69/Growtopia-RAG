# Balloon Warz Pack
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Balloon Warz Pack** for **25,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"Everything you need to dominate the Balloon Warz! Pack includes 50 Small War Balloon, 20 Medium War Balloon, 5 Large War Balloon, 1 Towel Rack Checkpoint, and a Rare Balloon-O-Matic."
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Balloon Warz Pack** was released on the 2nd of August, 2017. This pack is only available during Balloon Warz Event. All items were first introduced during Beta to some players on the 24th of July 2017, however was only released to the public on the 2nd of August.
The **Balloon Warz Pack** consists of:
  * Small War Balloon x50
  * Medium War Balloon x20
  * Large War Balloon x5
  * Towel Rack Checkpoint
  * Balloon-O-Matic
