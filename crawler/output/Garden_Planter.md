Stop leaving your poor plants in the dirty ground! Use this planter to keep them nice and secure, instead!

The **Garden Planter** is an unsplicable platform foreground block which was added as part of Spring Clash 2019 on March 1, 2019.
##  Function
A Garden Planter behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath.
