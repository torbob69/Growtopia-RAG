The **Turkey Wings** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2018 on November 22, 2018.
##  Function
Gobble-Gobbble-Gobbble

No feathers for you!

When equipped, the **Turkey Wings** grants the _Double-Jump: Gobble-Gobbble!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
The  Myths and Legends : Mothman Rising mission requires 1 of this item.
