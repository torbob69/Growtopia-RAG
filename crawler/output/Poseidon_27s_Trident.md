# Poseidon's Trident

This is the page for **Poseidon's Trident**.
You may be looking for **Poseidon's Digger's Trident** instead.
This trident has been blessed by Poseidon, God of the seas. The glowy blue signifies all of the servants who served under Poseidon in his battles. What Poseidon left along with this trident is unknown - that's for you to figure out. Sponsored by mythicdawn and angel42.

The **Poseidon's Trident** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of SummerFest 2015 on July 3, 2015.
##  Function
You have a mythical trident!

-

When equipped, the **Poseidon's Trident** grants the _Poseidon's Power_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further. It will also replace the player's punching animation with shooting water.
The  The Seven Seas : The Wings of Atlantis! mission requires 1 of this item.
