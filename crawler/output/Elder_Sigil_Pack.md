# Elder Sigil Pack
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Elder Sigil Pack** for **10,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"This mystical item will teleport you directly into the gaping maw of Growganoth as many times you want for a short period. You must be in the GROWGANOTH world in order to use this item!"

The **Elder Sigil Pack** is an item pack which was added as part of Halloween Week 2023 on October 19, 2023.
