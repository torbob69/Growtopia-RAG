##  Trivia
  * Gruel and Water are the only items which can be used to feed a Silkworm that doesn't change its color.
  * Giving a Silkworm Green Beer or Rotten Egg can also make it sick.
  * At approximately 15 days of age, a Silkworm will reach an elderly stage and begin having wrinkles.
  * One can only feed a Silkworm in an area they have access to.
  * Using a Spike Juice or an Experience Potion on a Silkworm causes it to be supercharged, which ensures that the next harvest yields 10 Silk Threads.
  * A Silkworm Toy can be used on a Silkworm, making it dance for a short while. The toy has a slight chance of breaking when used.
  * Giving an inappropriate name to a Silkworm will cause it to be deleted.
  * The maximum amount of silkworm in a world is 40.
