# Dragon Scales

Shimmering layers of reptilian armor fit for the mightiest of drakes. Surprisingly enough, they also make for a really nice wallpaper!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data: Paint PreviewType — Background Block – Background — Aqua Blue Charcoal Green Purple Red Varnish Yellow
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **6 Hits** **5 Hits** – Restores after **8s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Paint Preview Aqua Blue Charcoal Green Purple Red Varnish Yellow
Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Peace Mooncake **(×4)** ,
Ancestor Mooncake **(×1)** and
Prosperity Mooncake **(×6)**.
This recipe will yield **3** items.
Mooncake Offering

1 may be received as a common reward from offering mooncakes using an Offering Table.
The **Dragon Scales** is an unsplicable background block which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2017 on October 4, 2017.
##  Function
When placed, the Dragon Scales serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
