The **Toxic Waste Barrel** is a splicable solid foreground block which was added alongside the Zombie Defense Pack on May 17, 2013.
##  Function
Breaking Toxic Waste Barrels will explode them and spread Toxic Wastes around the immediate area, which will damage the player the same way as Acid.
