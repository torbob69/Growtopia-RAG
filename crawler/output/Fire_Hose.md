##  Function
Where does the water come from?

Guess you finally ran out of water.

When equipped, the **Fire Hose** grants the _Fire Hose_ mod, which allows the player to extinguish fires, knock back other players further, and punch one tile further. No damage can be dealt to blocks while the mod is active.
The  Super Pineapple Party Exclusive Mission - Pucker up for more Pineapple fun! mission requires 100 of this item.
