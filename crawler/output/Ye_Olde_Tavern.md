##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Ye Olde Tavern set, which consists of the following items:

    * Ye Olde Tavern (background block)

    * Ye Olde Tavern Door (door)

    * Ye Olde Tavern Roof (platform)
  * The phrase "ye olde" is a pseudo–Early Modern English phrase used to suggest a connection between a place and the late medieval period.
