The **Dragon Fruit Dragon** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2023 on June 9, 2023.
##  Function
Tropical Duo!

Tropical Uno!

When equipped, the **Dragon Fruit Dragon** grants the _Double Jump : Dragon Fruit Dragon_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
