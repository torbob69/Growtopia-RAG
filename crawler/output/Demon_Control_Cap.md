The **Demon Control Cap** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of the Battlers Of the Otherworldly update on July 15, 2016.
##  Function
Your pet is highly aggressive!

Punch for yourself.

When equipped, the **Demon Control Cap** grants the _Demon Control_ mod, which allows the player to knock back other players further. It will also replace the player's punching animation with slashes.
