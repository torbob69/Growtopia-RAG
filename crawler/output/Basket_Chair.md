# Basket Chair

Who wouldn't want a cozy, hanging chair! Kick back, relax and enjoy mocking the ground dwellers while you sit a couple of inches from the ground.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.

Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **4 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **1s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**

Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Easter Egg - Wavey **(×1)** ,
Easter Egg - Growtopia **(×1)** and
Easter Egg - Dark **(×1)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Basket Chair** is an unsplicable non-solid seat foreground block which was added as part of Easter Week 2020.
##  Function
When a player stands in front of a seat such as the Basket Chair for a few seconds, the player will automatically be seen "sitting" on it.
