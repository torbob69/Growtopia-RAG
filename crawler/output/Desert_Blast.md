The **Desert Blast** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Rise of the Desert Dragons update on August 3, 2013.
##  Function
Upon consumption, the blast will create a whole new desert world featuring an arid background weather. The world will contain pyramids of different sizes. On top of the largest one there will be a dropped desert treasure, which can be one of the following:
  * Sungate
  * Silver Idol
  * Anubis Pharaoh Mask (rare)
  * Pharaoh Mask (rare)

In a newly generated world, the following blocks can be found:
  * Sand
  * Cave Background
  * Boulder
  * Sandstone Wall
  * Wall Like An Egyptian
  * Sandstone
  * Ancient Stone Gate
  * Obelisk
  * Lava
  * Cactus
