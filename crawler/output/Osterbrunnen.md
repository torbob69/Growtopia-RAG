The **Osterbrunnen** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Easter Week 2022 on April 15, 2022.
##  Function
When placed, the Osterbrunnen serves a fully aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The Osterbrunnen is based on a German tradition of the same name, which revolves around decorating public wells or fountains with Easter eggs for Easter.
