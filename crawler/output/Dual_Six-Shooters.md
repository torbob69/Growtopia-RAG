The **Dual Six-Shooters** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2018 on May 4, 2018.
##  Function
You shoot bullets instead of punching!

You are disarmed.

When equipped, the **Dual Six-Shooters** grants the _Armed and Dangerous_ mod, which allows the player to kill zombies during a Pandemic. It will also replace the player's punching animation with bullets coming out of the gun.
