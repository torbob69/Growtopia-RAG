The **Fruit in Sand** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2026 on June 11, 2026.
##  Function
When placed, Fruit in Sand serves a purely aesthetic purpose. It has 4 different textures that appear randomly based on the block's location.
