# Silk Vest (disambiguation)

This is a **disambiguation** page.
There are more than one subjects in the **Growtopia Wiki** using this name.
**Silk Vests** are shirt items introduced during the Silkworm Update. They are all made through sewing.
They come in red, green, blue, purple, grey and black. There are no white, yellow or aqua Silk Vests.
  * Silk Vest - Red
  * Silk Vest - Green
  * Silk Vest - Blue
  * Silk Vest - Purple
  * Silk Vest - Grey
  * Silk Vest - Black
