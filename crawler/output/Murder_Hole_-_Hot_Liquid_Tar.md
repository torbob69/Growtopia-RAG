The **Murder Hole - Hot Liquid Tar** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added in the Winter Clash update on November 2, 2018.
##  Function
When placed, the Murder Hole - Hot Liquid Tar acts as a deadly block for a while when a player stands 1 tile under the block for too long.
