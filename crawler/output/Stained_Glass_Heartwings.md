# Stained Glass Heartwings

Shining, radiant, wondrous. These wings aren't powered by magic or science or anything so mundane - they're infused with purest love, capable of uplifting all who wear them into the skies! Touch the heavens and smile, for by wearing these beauties, you have proclaimed yourself a true champions of the heart!

The **Stained Glass Heartwings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2019 on February 8, 2019.
##  Function
Glowing with love!

Your love has gone!

When equipped, the **Stained Glass Heartwings** grants the _Double Jump: Heart_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
