The **Neptune's Gauntlet** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of SummerFest 2026 on July 9, 2026.
##  Function
Rise Atlantis!

Rolling in the deep!

When equipped, the **Neptune's Gauntlet** grants the _Punch Range + Punch Damage: Neptune's Gauntlet_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further and break blocks faster.
Step 16 of the  Quest For The Blade requires the player to deliver 1 of this item.
