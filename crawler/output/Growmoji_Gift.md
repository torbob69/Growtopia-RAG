# Growmoji Gift

Celebrate Winterfest and toss some gifts in your chat at the same time! Just consume this to add it to your available growmojis!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **5 Hits** **4 Hits** – Restores after **8s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Breaking

| May drop in sets of **1** from breaking:
•  Growmoji Chest  |
**Growmoji Gift** is an unsplicable growmoji consumable which was added on December 1, 2018.
##  Function
Upon consuming, the player will now be able to use the (gift) growmoji.
##  Trivia
  * As of Player Appreciation Week Day 6 on August 28, 2021, all growmoji consumables which were included as part of the Monthly Bundle IAPs now have a chance to be dropped from breaking a Growmoji Chest, which is purchasable from Locke for 150 World Locks.
