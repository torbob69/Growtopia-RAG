"Become the lord of fire and be pulled around by flaming horses! All the world is at your command as you zip around at the speed of fire."
The **Solar Chariot** is an unspliceable feet item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2017 Day 7 on Augest 23, 2017.
##  Function
You can move faster!

You are slow again.

When equipped, the **Solar Chariot** grants the _Speedy_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.
##  Trivia
  * The Solar Chariot is based on the chariot of the Greek God of Sun, Helios.
