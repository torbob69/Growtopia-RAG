The **Pegasus Pauldrons** is an unsplicable chest item that was added as part of the Pegasus Pursuit update on June 28, 2021.
##  Function
When equipped, the Pegasus Pauldrons serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia

    * Pegasus Pauldrons (neck)

    * Pegasus Shirt (shirt)
