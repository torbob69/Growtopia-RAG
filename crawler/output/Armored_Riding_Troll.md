# Armored Riding Troll

A tamed monstrosity of the deep caverns of the world. Throws dirt at your foes and moves with surprising speed for its size.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be transmuted.
Data

Type: Feet – Clothes
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: Full Collision
Hardness: **0 Hits** **0 Hits** – Restores after **0s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Mining

1 has a chance to be dropped from exploding  Pure Magic Ore using  Mining Explosives, which are naturally generated in mines.
The **Armored Riding Troll** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of the Spelunkorama Strikes Back! update.
##  Function
Tame the wild.

Back on your own two feet.

When equipped, the **Armored Riding Troll** grants the _Speedy: Armored Riding Troll_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land. When punching, the Armored Riding Troll will throw small dirt blocks.
