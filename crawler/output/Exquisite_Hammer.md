##  Trivia
  * Exquisite items can be converted back to 10 pristine items by combining them in a Laboratory or any chemical combiner with 5 Golden Block and 5 Steel Block.
  * There are four types of hammers: the **Anomalizing Exquisite Hammer** , Anomalizing Elegant Hammer, Anomalizing Reliable Hammer and the Anomalizing Pristine Hammer.
  * It was used to earn 1200 points for every punch during the Block Bashers Grow Event by punching blocks with the hammer equipped. This item no longer gives points.
