Players can punch this to begin a challenge: get from the Challenge Start Flag to the Challenge End Flag before time runs out! Wrench the timer to set up your challenge and insert prizes.

Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Challenge Timer** is an foreground interactive block that was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2016. A Challenge Start Flag and Challenge End Flag is required to create a challenge.
##  Function
To take the challenge, players must wrench the Challenge Timer and it will show the time limit, prize and the amount of players in the challenge. Only Supporters can take the challenge. The player will be teleported to the Challenge Start Flag and must reach the Challenge End Flag in order to claim the prize.
If the player runs out of time, they will teleport back to the Main Door.

##  Trivia
  * If the player fails the Challenge (From the Challenge Timer), they will teleport to the Challenge Timer that the player chose to take the challenge on.
  * The player will also teleport to the Challenge Timer upon completing the challenge provided.
  * A seventh rollback occurred on 3 July 2020 when a duplication glitch was found, involving negative integers input into Challenge Timers which resulted in many expensive items being duplicated. The servers were quickly placed into maintenance, however, it was found that the damage to the economy was too big to manually repair. A 24-hour rollback occurred to shortly after the duplication glitch started with many items becoming temporarily locked.
