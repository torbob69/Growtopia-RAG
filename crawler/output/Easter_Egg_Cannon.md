# Easter Egg Cannon

Powered by a Fuel Pack on your back, this gun can launch free easter egg blocks at your fellow Growtopians! Don't waste shots at anything else, though, because they still burn fuel!

The **Easter Egg Cannon** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Easter Week 2019.
##  Function
When a player equipping an Easter Egg Cannon punches another player, the latter will obtain an easter egg whose type is based on the fuel pack the former is also equipping. Each punch will still consume a fuel pack, whether or not the player manages to hit another one.
Fuel Packs: Corresponding Easter EggsFuel Pack: Easter Egg - Growtopia
Ecto Pack: Easter Egg - Ghost
Pineapple Juice: Easter Egg - Pineapple
Dangerous Pineapple Juice Backpack: Easter Egg - Growtopia
##  Trivia
  * The Easter Egg Cannon is the second item that allows players to give others items at the cost of fuel packs, with the first one being the T-Shirt Cannon.
