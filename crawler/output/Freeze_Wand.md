# Freeze Wand

Use this on someone to freeze them solid for 10 seconds.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: Full Collision
Hardness: **20 Hits** **15 Hits** – Restores after **8s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Freeze Wand

Given exclusively to moderators and cannot be obtained by regular players by normal means, except when a moderator gives away or sells theirs.
The **Freeze Wand** is an unobtainable consumable which was added as part of _Growtopia_ 's release.
##  Function
Freeze!

You've thawed out.

When consumed on a player, this item grants the _Frozen_ mod for ten seconds.

##  Trivia
  * The Snowball also grants the _Frozen_ mod in a similar manner to the Freeze Wand; however, the former only lasts for two seconds.
  * Upon their introduction in December 2012, the Wands' sprite was offset by a pixel up and to the right. This was fixed upon the release of the game in 2013.
