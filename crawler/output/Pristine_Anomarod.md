The **Pristine Anomarod** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Summer Clash Series 1.
##  Function
Pristine Anomarods are used to score points during Fishing Fanatics. While equipping this item, each fish caught yields 15,700 points. Pristine anomalizers, such as this item, may sometimes break upon usage during their respective Grow Events.
