The **Nocturnal Crown** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as June 2022's Item Of The Season on June 1, 2022.
##  Function
Striking fear into your enemies!

Hiding in the shadows!

When equipped, the **Nocturnal Crown** grants the _Double Jump and Light Source : Nocturnal Crown_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and emit light in dark areas.
