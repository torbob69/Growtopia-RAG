# Great Wolf Totem

Players that touch this get a wolf prize, and kicked out of the wolfworld.
Properties

This item cannot be dropped or traded.

##  Function
One of this item is present in every WOLFWORLDS. When a player stands on it, the Great Wolf Totem will reward the player with a random prize. The player would also automatically exit the world.
