##  Function
I am speed!

I am slow!

When equipped, the **Ridin' Sgt. Stuffins'** grants the _Speedy + Double Jump : Ridin' Sgt. Stuffins'_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and jump a second time in mid-air.
