The **Pineapple Shoulder Cannon** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2021 on June 3, 2021.
##  Function
Get Juicy!

Really not juicy any more!

When equipped, the **Pineapple Shoulder Cannon** grants the _Punch Damage: Pineapple Shoulder Cannon!_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. Additionally, it gives a cannon punching effect.
##  Trivia
  * The Pineapple Shoulder Cannon drops in place of the Greezak Horde - Shoulder Cannon during Super Pineapple Party.
