The **Ta Couch** is an unsplicable non-solid seat foreground block which was added as part of Lunar New Year 2024 on February 1, 2024.
##  Fucntion
When a player stands in front of a seat such as the Ta Couch for a few seconds, the player will automatically be seen "sitting" on it.
