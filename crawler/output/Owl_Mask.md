The **Owl Mask** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2020 on September 24, 2020.
##  Function
When equipped, the Owl Mask serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
Step 4 of the  Quest Of The Owl requires the player to deliver 50 of this item.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the owl set, which consists of the following items:

    * Owl Mask (hat)

    * Owl Suit (shirt)
