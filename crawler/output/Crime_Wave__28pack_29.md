# Crime Wave (pack)
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Crime Wave** for **5,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"In this pack, you'll get 5 each of 5 random Superpower Cards, and what good would that be without a Rare Crime Wave to use them on? A Crime Wave is a one-use item that calls four villains to your world for you to battle. Beware: Villains only stick around for 24 hours once they appear."
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Crime Wave** is an item pack which was released alongisde the Superhero Pack on December 12, 2014.
The pack includes:
  * Crime Wave
  * 5 random Superpower Cards
