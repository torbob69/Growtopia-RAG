Participate in successfully smashing an  Ultra Pinata during the **Pinata Bash!** event.
May give **1-5** items each time.
The **Pueblock Window** is an unsplicable solid foreground block which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2019 on May 3, 2019.
##  Function
The item has four different textures and when placed, a random one will appear. The four textures are:
  * Silver squared window with a blue Pueblock background.
  * Lime-Green rectangular window with a green Pueblock background.
  * Two Brown curved windows with an orange Pueblock background.
  * Pink rectangular window with orange curtains with a pink Pueblock background.
