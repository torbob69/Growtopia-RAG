The **Mariachi Pants** is an unsplicable pants item which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2017 on May 5, 2017.
##  Function
When equipped, the Mariachi Pants serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the mariachi set, which consists of the following items:

    * Mariachi Hat (hat)

    * Mariachi Suit (shirt)

    * Mariachi Pants (pants)

    * Mariachi Guitar (hand)
  * The  Mariachi Bobble Head can be seen wearing the full mariachi set.
