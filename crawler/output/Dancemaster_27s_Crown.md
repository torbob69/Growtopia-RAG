The **Dancemaster's Crown** is an unsplicable hat item that was added as part of Summer Clash 2018 on June 1st, 2018.
##  Function
<https://growtopiawiki.com/w/File:Disco.ogg>The Dancemaster's Crown music.
When equipped, it forces nearby players to begin to dance when the player with the Dancemaster's Crown equipped uses the /dance command. It also grants you immunity to knockback when the player is dancing.
