The **Royal Celestial Tendrils** is an unsplicable back item which was added as March 2024's Item Of The Season on March 6th, 2024.
##  Function
Angel's Light!

Light's Out!

When equipped, the **Royal Celestial Tendrils** grants the _Double Jump + Light Source: Royal Celestial Tendrils_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and emit light in dark areas.
