# Eye Drop Pack
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Eye Drop Pack** for **30,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"Need a fresh new look? This pack includes a Rare Bathroom Mirror, and 10 random Eye Drop colors (may include Eye Cleaning Solution, to leave your eyes sparkly clean)!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Eye Drop Pack** was released along with WinterFest 2017 on 18 December 2017. One can be purchased from the Store for 30,000 gems.
