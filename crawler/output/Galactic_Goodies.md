# Galactic Goodies
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Galactic Goodies** for **15,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"Get all the Star Tools you need to boldly go where no Growtopian has gone! Use these to help you command a starship and seek victory in the Galactic Nexus! you’ll get 5 of each of the 12 Star Tools you’ll need to complete missions and some bonus Star Fuel to help power a Starship!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Galactic Goodies** pack is a pack which was released as apart of the Startopia Update. It is purchasable in the Growtopia Store for 15,000 Gems.
The pack includes:
  * AI Brain ×5
  * Cyborg Diplomat ×5
  * Galactibolt ×5
  * Gigablaster ×5
  * Growton Torpedo ×5
  * HyperShields ×5
  * Quadriscanner ×5
  * Space Meds ×5
  * Star Fuel ×25
  * Star Supplies ×5
  * Stellar Documents ×5
  * Tactical Drone ×5
  * Teleporter Charge ×5

##  Trivia
  * During the Role Up! update, the Galactic Goodies pack was reduced in price from 25,000 Gems to 15,000 Gems.
