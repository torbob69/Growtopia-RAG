The **Elegant Anomarod** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the Role Up! update.
##  Function
Elegant Anomarods are used to score points during Fishing Fanatics. While equipping this item, each fish caught yields 42,800 points. Elegant anomalizers, such as this item, may rarely break upon usage during their respective Grow Events.
