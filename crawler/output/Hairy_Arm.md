The **Hairy Arm** is an unsplicable platform foreground block which was added as part of the Gross-Out update on May 1, 2019.
##  Function
A Hairy Arm behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath.
##  Trivia
  * As of the Mutant Kitchen update, Hairy Arm recipe was removed for being "too gross". It was previously obtainable from consuming any gross food item from the Gross-Out update. It has been replaced with the Horizontacles.
