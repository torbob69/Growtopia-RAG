Gross-Dosed Explosion

After eating **3** of any Mutant consumable, overload before reaching a specific explosion type by eating more consumables.
Each explosion may give **1** item.
The **Slithering Serpent Hair** is an unsplicable hair item which was added as part of the Mutant Kitchen update on July 26, 2019.
##  Function
Hissy hair, attack!

There, there, my pretties - calm yourselves...

When equipped, the **Slithering Serpent Hair** grants the _Punch Damage: SNAKEY SNAP!_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. The item also replaces the fist with blue goo when punching.
The  The Seven Seas : A Tale of Tentacles! mission requires 10 of this item.
##  Trivia
  * The Slithering Serpent Hair replaced the Giant Eye Head from the Gross-Out update for being "too gross".
