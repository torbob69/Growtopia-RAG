The **Fiesta Mariachi Guitar** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2018 on May 4, 2018.
##  Function
When the player enters the command /dance or while they are standing still, fiesta music plays while the player performs a special animation of themself playing the Fiesta Mariachi Guitar.
