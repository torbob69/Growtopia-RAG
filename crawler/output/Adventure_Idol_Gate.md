The **Adventure Idol Gate** is a splicable toggleable foreground block which was added as part of The Adventure Pack Strikes Back! update on June 1, 2017.
##  Function
Being a toggleable foreground block, an Adventure Idol Gate works like a Dragon Gate, allowing eligible players to open and close the Adventure Idol Gate by punching it. When toggled on, the item acts as a solid block which players cannot pass while killing all players trapped inside it. When toggled off, it opens and acts as a non-solid block which players can walk through. It can be wrenched to be made public or not.
If a player who has picked up an Adventure Item - Golden Idol steps on an Adventure Pedestal, the idol will be placed onto the pedestal, causing every Adventure Idol Gate in the world to be toggled on or off, depending on whether it was previously open or closed.
