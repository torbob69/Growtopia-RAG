The **Vamp Vest** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2013 on October 28, 2013.
##  Function
When equipped, the Vamp Vest serves a fully cosmetic purpose.
Equipping this item, alongside the items Vampire Fangs, Slick Black Hair and Vampire Cape, will reward one the _Costume Contest_ achievement.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Vampire set, which consists of the following items:

    * Slick Black Hair (Hair)

    * Vampire Fangs (Face)

    * Vamp Vest (Shirt)

    * Vampire Cape (Back)
