**ERROR!** is an unobtainable component item. It was given to players with Da Vinci Wings or Thingamabobs during the duplication glitch that occurred in 2019 with the Techno-Organic Engine.
##  Trivia
  * There were 2 items with different IDs bearing this same name. They were the Da Vinci Wings and the Thingamabob.
  * This unobtainable component item was given to the players whom legitimately earned the above-mentioned items.
