The **Wind Surfer** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of SummerFest 2023 on July 13, 2023.
##  Function
Surfs up!

You're in the doldrums!

When equipped, the **Wind Surfer** grants the _Speedy & Surfboard : Wind Surfer!_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and float on water for three seconds.
