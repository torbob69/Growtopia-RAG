The **Anomalizing Reliable Bone Saw** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Summer Clash Series 1.
##  Function
Anomalizing Reliable Bone Saws are used to score points during Surgery Stars. While equipping this item, each successful surgery yields 1,500 points. Reliable anomalizers, such as this item, will not break upon usage.
