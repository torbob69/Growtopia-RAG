The **Elegant Potion** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Role Up! update.
##  Function
Gain extra Points from elegant anomalizers!

Your stomach's rumbling for more potion goodness!

When consumed, it will grant the player the Food: Anomalizers mod for 30 minutes, which will increase all points gained by the player from elegant anomalizers when used during an active Grow Event by 10%.
