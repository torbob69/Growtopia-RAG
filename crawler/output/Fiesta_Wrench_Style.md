The **Fiesta Wrench Style** is a wrench style which was added as part of the March Season on May 1, 2026.
##  Function
When equipped through the 'Wrench Customization' tab when wrenching oneself, the Fiesta Wrench Style replaces the player's wrench connected to their name.
