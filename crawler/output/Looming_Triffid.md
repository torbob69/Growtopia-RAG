The **Looming Triffid** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added in the Startopia Update.
##  Function
Being a looming deadly block, a Looming Triffid acts as a deadly block for a while when a player passes by.
##  Trivia
  * This item's name and appearance is based on a Triffid, a fictitious carnivorous plant species.
