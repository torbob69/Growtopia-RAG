The **Orange Hanbok Pants** is an unsplicable pants item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2016 on September 15, 2016.
##  Function
When equipped, the Orange Hanbok Pants serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * It isn't guaranteed that the player will get a Orange Hanbok Pants by following the item's combining recipe, as all the items below share the same recipe:
  * Blue Hanbok Top
  * Blue Hanbok Pants
  * Orange Hanbok Top
  * Orange Hanbok Pants
  * Green Hanbok Skirt
  * Yellow Hanbok Skirt
