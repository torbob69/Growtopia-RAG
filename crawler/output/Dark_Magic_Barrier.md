The **Dark Magic Barrier** is an unsplicable foreground entrance block which was added as part of Halloween Week 2015 on October 29, 2015.
##  Function
Being an entrance, a Dark Magic Barrier allows players with access to walk through it, or, when its property is changed, allowing any player to walk through it. When placed in a public area, it is impossible to be closed for anyone. Only the texture and the sound effect is different from the regular Forcefield.
