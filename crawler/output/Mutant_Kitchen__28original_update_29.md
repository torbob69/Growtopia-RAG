Growtopia Gazette
> July 26: Mutant Kitchen!
> Hello Growtopians,
> Mutant madness has made its appearance in our kitchens! Cook up some monstrous mutated treats, chow down, and enjoy a different - but still rewarding, of course - side of Growtopia with Mutant Kitchen!
The **Mutant Kitchen** update is a monthly update which was introduced on July 26, 2019. Part of this update includes the re-release of Totally Gross as Mutant Kitchen.
