The **Shawl of Shadows** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2022 on October 22. 2022.
##  Function
Straight from the Shadow Realm!

Back to the Shadow Realm!

When equipped, the **Shawl of Shadows** grants the _Double Jump : Shawl of Shadows_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
