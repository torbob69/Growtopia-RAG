Even as tiny as they are, these cute pink wings allow you to jump in mid-air!

The **Pink Teeny Angel Wings** is a wing which was added as a part of the Valentine's Week 2020.
##  Function
It's like flying, but less.

Gravity, it's the law.

When equipped, the **Pink Teeny Angel Wings** grants the _Double Jump: Pink Teeny Angel Wings_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
