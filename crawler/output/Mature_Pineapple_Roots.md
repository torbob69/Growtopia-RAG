A **Mature Pineapple Roots** is formed when a Pineapple Tree grown on top of a Fertile Soil Block is ready to be harvested.
**Mature Pineapple Roots** is an unsplicable solid roots foreground block which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2020 on June 4, 2020.
##  Function
Punching a Mature Pineapple Roots block allows the player to harvest it, yielding 6 XP each and turning the block back to a Fertile Soil Block. There's also a 10% chance for a single Pineapple Root Cutting to appear on top of the block.
