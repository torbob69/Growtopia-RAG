The **Heartbreaker Hammer** is a hand item that was introduced during Valentine's week 2015.
##  Function
It's hammer time.

You can now touch this.

When equipped, the **Heartbreaker Hammer** grants the _Hammer Time_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. Additionally, it replaces the player's fist and throws the Heartbreaker Hammer when punching.

##  Trivia
  * The 'Hammer Time' mod is most likely a reference to MC Hammer's song "U Can't Touch This".
  * This item is a reference to an item worn by Bouapha, a protagonist in the game Dr. Lunatic developed by Hamumu Software, one of the old developers of Growtopia.
