# Composer's Pack
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Composer's Pack** for **5,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"With these handy blocks, you'll be able to compose your own music, using your World-Locked world as a Sheet of Music. Requires a World Lock (sold separately!)."
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Composer's Pack** is an item pack that was added on March 1, 2013.
##  Items
The pack contains the following items:
  * Sheet Music: Blank ×20
  * Sheet Music: Piano Note ×20
  * Sheet Music: Sharp Piano ×5
  * Sheet Music: Flat Piano ×5
  * Sheet Music: Drums ×20
  * Sheet Music: Bass Note ×20
  * Sheet Music: Sharp Bass ×5
  * Sheet Music: Flat Bass ×5

##  Trivia
  * Sheet Music blocks can only be placed in World-Locked worlds.
