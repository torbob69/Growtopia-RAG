The **Blackfire Crown** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Black Friday 2025 on November 27, 2025.
##  Function
This crown is on fire!

Not lit anymore!

When equipped, the **Blackfire Crown** grants the _Double Jump & Speedy: Blackfire Crown_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and move faster on land.
