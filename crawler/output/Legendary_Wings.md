# Legendary Wings

Turns out you had to do a lot more than ring a bell to get these suckers.

Legendary Quest Reward

Approach the  Legendary Wizard and complete the  Quest Of The Heavens.
The **Legendary Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added in the Legendary update on August 22, 2014.
##  Function
You can jump in mid-air! It’s like flying, but less

Gravity - it’s the law.

When equipped, the **Legendary Wings** grants the _Double Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
##  Quest Steps
  1. Deliver 1000 Clouds.
  2. Kill 100 players in PvP via a through a Game Generator.
  3. Break 5,000 blocks.
  4. Deliver 600 Fairy Wings.
  5. Plant seeds that add up to 50,000 rarities.
  6. Earn 50 Growtokens.
  7. Deliver 3 Bubble Wings.
  8. Earn 10,000 XP.
  9. Deliver 800 Crimson Eagle Wings.
  10. Deliver 100,000 Gems.
  11. Break 100,000 raritys worth of blocks.
  12. Successfully perform 100 surgeries.
  13. Collect from 1,000 Providers.
  14. Deliver 20 Rainbow Wings.
  15. Collect 100,000 raritys worth of fruit from trees.
  16. Deliver 3 Golden Angel Wings.
  17. Deliver 100 Ripper Wings.
  18. Deliver 1 Phoenix Wings or Neptune's Trident.
  19. Deliver 50 Parrot Wings.
  20. Deliver the Legendary Orb.

##  Trivia
  * Due to the dwindling number of Phoenix Wings available in the game, during SummerFest 2020, Step 18 gives the player the option to deliver a Neptune's Trident or Phoenix Wings.
  * growmir was the first player to complete the Quest Of The Heavens and receive the Legendary Wings.
    * This was also the first Legendary Quest ever completed by any player.
  * The design of  Occean's Eternal Wake is based on  Legendary Wings.
