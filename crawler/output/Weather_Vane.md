The **Weather Vane** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block which was added as part of Winter Clash 2018 on November 2, 2018.
##  Function
Being a toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block, a Weather Vane can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Weather Vane will change its appearance, showing a 4-framed animation of itself spinning around.
