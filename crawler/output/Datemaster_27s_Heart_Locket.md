The **Datemaster's Heart Locket** is a chest item which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2020 on February 13, 2020.
##  Function
Rise on the wings of love!

Bling!

When equipped, the **Datemaster's Heart Locket** grants the _Speedy + Double Jump: Datemaster_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and move faster on land. As the player moves around, a trail of hearts are released and as the player jumps, a pair of pink Legendary Wings appear and fades away with a white pair of Legendary Wings.
