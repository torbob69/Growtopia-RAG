The **Heartbow** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2013 on February 10, 2013.
##  Function
Blast blocks from farther away! Weaker damage than punch though.

You are disarmed.

When equipped, the **Heartbow** grants the _Cupid's Firepower_ mod, which allows the player to pull other players towards them upon punching them, punch one tile further, and break blocks more slowly. It will also replace the player's punching animation with hearts.
