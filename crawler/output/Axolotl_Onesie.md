# Axolotl Onesie

The perfect item for when you wake up in the morning and think, "I really want to dress like an Axolotl today".
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be transmuted.

The **Axolotl Onesie** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2020 Day 1 on August 17, 2020.
##  Function
When equipped, the Axolotl Onesie serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
The  Myths and Legends : Finding the Swamp Monster mission requires 2 of this item.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Axolotl set, which consists of the following items:

    * Axolotl Hood (Hair)

    * Axolotl Hoodie (Shirt)

    * Axolotl Onesie (Shirt)

    * Axolotl Scarf (Chest)

    * Axolotl Slippers (Feet)
  * The Axolotl Onesie was one of the five clothing items added on Player Appreciation Week 2020 Day 1 that were based on the Axolotl, alongside Axolotl Hood, Axolotl Hoodie, Axolotl Slippers, and Axolotl Scarf.
    * Among these items, three of them are obtainable through defeating villains: the Axolotl Hood, Axolotl Slippers, and Axolotl Onesie.
