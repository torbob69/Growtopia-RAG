##  Function
<https://growtopiawiki.com/w/File:DiscoFever.wav>The Disco Fever's music when punching.
Feel the city breakin' and everybody shakin'!

No longer a ninja!

When equipped, the **Disco Fever** grants the _Punch Power + Punch Damage: Disco Fever_ mod, which allows the player to knock back other players further and break blocks faster. It also forces nearby players to dance when the player with the Disco Fever equipped uses the /dance command.
##  Trivia
  * The Disco Fever's item description is a reference to the in 1977 released song Stayin' Alive by the British musical group Bee Gees.
