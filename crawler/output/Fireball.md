The **Fireball** is a Dungeon ability which was added as part of the Dungeons update on December 9, 2024.
##  Function
When the player receives this ability, players can perform shoot a fireball that deals a small amount of damage to enemies from a distance.
After the Dungeons updated at October 23rd, the Fireball can now be upgraded as long as the player currently uses fireball.
The **Powerful Fireball (Level 2)** ability allows player to shoot a blue fireball, dealing more damage than fireball.
The **Powerful Fireball (Level 3)** ability allows player to shoot a purple fireball, dealing more damage than powerful fireball.
##  Trivia
After the Dungeons Updated at October 23rd, the cost of fireball is now changed from 200 to 100.
