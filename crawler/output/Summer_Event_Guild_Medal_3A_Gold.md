The **Summer Event Guild Medal: Gold** is an untradable platform which was released as part of the Summer Season Events. One is given to the leader of a Guild that places 1st in a Summer Grow Event.
##  Function
A Summer Event Guild Medal: Gold behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath.
##  Trivia
  * After the first Summer Clash has ended, the earned medal could be dropped and traded. The glitch was fixed soonly after, leaving very few instances of dropped Winter Clash Medals behind. However, moderators removed the dropped Summer Clash medals since it was unfair for players to obtain rewards they did not earn.
