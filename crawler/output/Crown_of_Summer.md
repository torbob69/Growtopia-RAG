# Crown of Summer

This wonderful crown will turn you into Summer Royalty!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be transmuted.

Type: Hat – Clothes
Render Type: Single

Consumption

Consume a(n)  Equinox Box **(x1)**.
May yield **1** item each.

The **Crown of Summer** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as a part of the Equinox Weekend update on March 25, 2022.
##  Function
Leaf Majesty!

Leaft Alone!

When equipped, the **Crown of Summer** grants the _Build Range : Crown of Summer_ mod, which allows the player to build one tile further.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Equinox Summer set, which consists of the following items:

    * Crown of Summer (hat)

    * Shirt of Summer (shirt)

    * Pants of Summer (pants)

    * Boots of Summer (shoes)
