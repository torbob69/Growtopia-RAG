# Golden Sunset Cape

Practically glowing with the eye-popping colors of a perfect sunset, this once-cloudy cape has been transformed into a stunning piece of radiant cloth! Don this beauty, and everyone will treat you like a sky set ablaze with all the majesty of a setting sun - they won't be able to look away!

The **Golden Sunset Cape** is a back item which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2020 update on February 13, 2020.
##  Function
Rise to the golden sunset!

Your feet are back on solid ground!

When equipped, the **Golden Sunset Cape** grants the _Double Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
