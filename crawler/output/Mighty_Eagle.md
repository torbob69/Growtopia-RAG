The **Mighty Eagle** is an unsplicable hand item that was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2024 on November 21, 2024.
##  Function
Mightier than the pen!

Scared off!

When equipped, the **Mighty Eagle** grants the _Punch Range + Punch Damage: Mighty Eagle_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further and break blocks faster.
