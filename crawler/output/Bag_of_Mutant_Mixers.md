# Bag of Mutant Mixers
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Bag of Mutant Mixers** for **10,000** in the Growtopia Store.
"A bag of Gross Mixers to make deliciously gross consumables! You also get some slippery Strawberry Slime Blocks."
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Bag of Mutant Mixers** is a store pack that was released during the Gross-Out update. The pack contains:
  * Burnt Slime x10
  * Putrid Gas x10
  * Rotting Sea Goo x10
  * Space Poop x10
  * Strawberry Slime Block x10
  * Random Cooking Recipe x1

##  Trivia
  * When first introduced during the Gross-Out update, it was called the Bag of Gross Mixers. The name was later changed during the Mutant Kitchen Rebalanced! update.
