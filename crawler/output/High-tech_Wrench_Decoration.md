The **High-tech Wrench Decoration** is a wrench decoration which was added as part of the June Season on June 1, 2026.
##  Function
When equipped through the 'Wrench Customization' tab when wrenching oneself, the High-tech Wrench Decoration adds a wire around the player's wrench connected to their name.
