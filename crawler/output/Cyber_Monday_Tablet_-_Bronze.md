Cyber Monday Contest

**1** is given out to the 3rd placer of the Cyber Monday contest hosted yearly.
The **Cyber Monday Tablet - Bronze** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Cyber Monday 2017 on November 27, 2017.
##  Function
When placed, the Cyber Monday Tablet - Bronze serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
##  Contest History
### Rules
During the 28th of November, 2017, players can enter the world GROWCYBERMONDAY1-9 and answer the riddles. The first three players to finish the world's riddles will receive a Cyber Monday Tablet trophy.
### Prizes
1st Place:  Cyber Monday Tablet - Gold
2nd Place:  Cyber Monday Tablet - Silver
3rd Place:  Cyber Monday Tablet - Bronze
_Winner Thread:_ GROWCYBERMONDAY Riddle World
### Rules
Players can join the contest by replying to the contest thread a story and screenshot or video about their all-time best item purchase, along with a detailed description on what they like to collect in the game.
### Prizes
1st Place:  Cyber Monday Tablet - Gold +  Diamond Flashaxe
2nd Place:  Cyber Monday Tablet - Silver +  Frosty Wings
3rd Place:  Cyber Monday Tablet - Bronze +  Lightning Umbrella
_Entries and Winner thread:_ (FORUM) The Collectors Spotlight
