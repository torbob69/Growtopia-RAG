##  Function
You have a soccer ball!

You're taking your ball and going home.

When equipped, the **World Cup Cleats** grants the _Speedy_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land. Upon running, the animation shows the player kicking the ball which bounces back after a few seconds.
##  Trivia
