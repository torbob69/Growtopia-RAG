The **Black Burning Eyes** is an unsplicable face item which was added as part of Black Friday 2020 on November 27, 2020.
##  Function
Your eyes are quite warm and really dark!

Everything looks so much cooler!

When equipped, the **Black Burning Eyes** grants the _Punch Range : Black Burning Eyes_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further. It also replaces the player's punching effect with black flames coming from the player's eyes.
##  Trivia
  * The  Black Burning Eyes is the black reskin of the  Burning Eyes.
