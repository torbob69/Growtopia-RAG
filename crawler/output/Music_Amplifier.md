Place this on the right side of a musical block and spread the music to the world!

The **Music Amplifier** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable animated foreground block which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2022 Day 4 on August 18, 2022.
##  Function
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Music Amplifier can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on whist being placed on the right side of a musical block, the Music Amplifier will amplify the music to the whole world.
