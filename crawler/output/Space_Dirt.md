The **Space Dirt** is an unsplicable solid foreground block which was added as part of the Startopia 2.0 update on June 1, 2020. Multiple layers of Space Dirt can be found in Ascended Planet worlds after completing the Heart of the Galaxy sector.
##  Function
When placed, the Space Dirt serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
