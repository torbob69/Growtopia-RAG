# Space Badger

A curious critter from outer space! A lean, mean, adventuring machine! A badger with attitude! Wrench yourself and choose Transform to transform!!

The **Space Badger** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the Aliens update on November 14, 2021.
##  Function
Set to stun!

Set to none!

When equipped, the **Space Badger** grants the _Double Jump & Punch Range: Space Badger_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air, punch one tile further, and switch between its wearable form and its morph form by wrenching oneself and clicking 'Transform'.
