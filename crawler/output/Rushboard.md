This item was given to **Diversa (formerly known as Rush)** for their contributions as a voluntary moderator.
The **Rushboard** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of the Growtokens! update on March 30, 2014.
##  Function
You can move faster!

You are slow again.

When equipped, the **Rushboard** grants the _Speedy_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land. It also causes the player to slowly drift up and down, simulating levitation.
##  Trivia
  * Similar to the StarBoard, the Rushboard's design resembles a hoverboard, a fictional skateboard-like vehicle which hovers above the ground.
