The **Trick or Treat Troll** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2021 on October 21, 2021.
##  Function
Tame the wild.

Back on your own two feet.

When equipped, the **Trick or Treat Troll** grants the _Speedy: Trick or Treat Troll_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.
