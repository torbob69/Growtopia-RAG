# Golden Heartthrob Helm

Only the most elite Champions of Heart have worn a helm such as this! Polished to a brilliant shine, it's hard to tell if this helm is shimmering from the elegance of its design or the incredible heart of its bearer, but either way, all who look on it will be awed by its beauty.

The **Golden Heartthrob Helm** is an unsplicable hat which was added as a part of the Valentine's Week 2020 on February 13, 2020.
##  Function
Heartthob Hearts!

Gone is the manly love!

When equipped, the **Golden Heartthrob Helm** grants the _Punch Damage: Heartthrob Helm!_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster.
