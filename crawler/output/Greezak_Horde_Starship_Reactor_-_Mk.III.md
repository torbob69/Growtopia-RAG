# Greezak Horde Starship Reactor - Mk.III

Make sure your ship can survive the rigors of a lengthy hunt with the Greezak Horde reactor! Provides a Ship Rating of 2640. One of three components - Helm, Viewscreen, and Reactor - needed to fly a starship. Wrench this to add Star Fuel to your ship!
