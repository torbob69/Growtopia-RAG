The **Growpedia** is a splicable consumable which was added as part of the Growtopia's Tutorial update on August 16, 2018.
##  Function
When a player double-taps their Growpedia, they will be able to view a list of tutorial quests that they have completed. As of 1 May, 2020, the quests for the Growpedia had been updated into the following quests with their rewards:

##  Trivia
  * In December 2012, _Growtopia_ 's creator Seth stated that he had an idea for a "Growpedia" item, which would have functioned differently compared to the version of Growpedia that currently exists in the game. It was supposed to record all the seed combinations a player has discovered, as well as provide information regarding each of the items they have created.[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Growpedia#cite_note-1)
  * As of November 20, 2023, the Growpedia became tradable and can be obtained by splicing Mission Block and Happy Joy Plaque.
