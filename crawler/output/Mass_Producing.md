# Mass Producing

**Mass producing** is where players create items from scratch using basic/farmable seeds. The main types of basic seeds are Lava, Dirt, Cave Background, Door, Rock, Bricks, Grass, Glass Pane, Window, Garbage and Wood Block seeds. These seeds are usually spliced to make a certain tree, harvested, smashed and spliced again to make other items before being repeated again. **Mass producing** is time-consuming as players have to wait for the trees to grow, as well as the time is taken for the player to break the blocks.
