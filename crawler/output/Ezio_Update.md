> Hello Growtopians,

The **Ezio Update** was an update that was released on 14 October 2018. The update introduced a new IAP which costs $10 USD from the store and gives the player nine Ezio related items. They are:
  * Ezio's Vest
  * Ezio's Cape
  * Ezio's Trousers
  * Ezio's Armguards
  * Ezio's Beard
  * Ezio's Hair
  * Ezio's Boots
  * Ezio's Hood
  * Hay Cart
