The **All You Can Eat Bib** is a shirt item that was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2020.
##  Function
Foooooood Fight!!

Time to clean up!

When equipped, the **All You Can Eat Bib** grants the _Punch Range: All You Can Eat Bib!_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further. It will also replace the player's punching animation to throwing different thanksgiving food sprites.
