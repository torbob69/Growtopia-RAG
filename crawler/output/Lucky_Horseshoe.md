# Lucky Horseshoe

Lucky you! You've been awarded this golden horseshoe by the Leprechaun's Guild in honor of your Blarney achievements. Of course, one horseshoe isn't much good. Why don't you get four of them together and shoe a horse? (Note: horse not actually required, just get 4 horseshoes and tap them anywhere)
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **8 Hits** **6 Hits** – Restores after **8s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Complete BLARNEY 1 to 10 in a single day

May yield **1** item each.
The **Lucky Horseshoe** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2015 on March 13, 2015.
