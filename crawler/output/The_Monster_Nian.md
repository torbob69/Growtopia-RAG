**The Monster Nian** is an unobtainable non-solid NPC foreground block which was added as part of Lunar New Year 2023 on January 21, 2023.
##  Function
**The Monster Nian** is an NPC you encounter after completing the parkour at REALMOFNIAN, which was added during the Lunar New Year 2023 update. Use a Chinese Firework on **The Monster Nian** to receive a prize (with a slight chance of getting two if wearing any Draconic item).
If enough players use Chinese Fireworks a bar fills up and the Nian gets scared away. After the Nian gets scared away, it disappears for 3 hours. During the 3 hours the Lunar Chest appears in the store for 25,000 gems.
