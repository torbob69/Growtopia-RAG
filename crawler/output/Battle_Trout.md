The **Battle Trout** is an unsplicable hand item which was added on the 11th of April, 2014, alongside Mid-Pacific Owl and Undersea Blast.
##  Function
You're holding a dead fish.

You're making better life choices now.

When equipped, the **Battle Trout** grants the _Fishy_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further and break blocks more slowly.
