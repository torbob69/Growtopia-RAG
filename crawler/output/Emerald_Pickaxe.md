The **Emerald Pickaxe** is a hand item released on the fourth day of the Player Appreciation Week 2017.
##  Function
It's all mine!

I can't dig it anymore.

When equipped, the **Emerald Pickaxe** grants the _Diggin' It_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. It also replaces the player's punching animation with a slashing effect.
##  Trivia
  * Before the seventh day of Player Appreciation Week 2017, it was untradeable. Because it wasn't intended to be so, it was later made tradeable.
  * Like the Golden Pickaxe, upon wearing it, it emits gold sparkles from the item itself.
