Participate in successfully smashing an  Ultra Pinata during the **Pinata Bash!** event.
May give **1** item each time.
The **Pinata** is an unsplicable event mystery foreground block which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2013 on May 3, 2013.
##  Function
As an event mystery block, the Pinata can be placed on top of any dropped items. When smashed, it will behave similarly to the Ultra Pinata and sprays its contents over a small area. It swells up in size as the player punches it, rather than developing cracks like most items do while breaking.
