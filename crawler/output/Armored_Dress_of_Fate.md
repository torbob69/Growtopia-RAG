The **Armored Dress of Fate** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as part of the Dungeons update on December 9, 2024.
##  Function
When equipped, the Armored Dress of Fate serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The Armored Dress of Fate is a reference to Artoria Pendragon (Saber), a character from the visual novel series Fate.
