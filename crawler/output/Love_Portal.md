The **Love Portal** is an unsplicable non-solid portal foreground block which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2021 on February 13, 2021.
##  Function
Being a type of portal, the Love Portal immediately moves the player to another tile when touched. A block with an ID must first be placed on the tile for this to work.
