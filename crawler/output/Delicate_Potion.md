The **Delicate Potion** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Role Up! update.
##  Function
Gain extra Points from delicate anomalizers!

Your stomach's rumbling for more potion goodness!

When consumed, it grants the _Food: Anomalizers_ mod, which gives more points while using a reliable anomalizer.
