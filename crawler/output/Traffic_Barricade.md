The **Traffic Barricade** is a splicable non-solid animated foreground block which was added alongside the Zombie Defense Pack in version 1.22.2 on May 17, 2013.
##  Function
As an animated foreground block, a Traffic Barricade features an automatic 2-frame animation of its light flashing.
