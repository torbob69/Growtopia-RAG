# Tainted Cheese

The rat summoned by this cheese is very friendly, but also very ill! Why would you want this plagued vermin for a pet? Well, getting a rare malady isn't always a bad thing, assuming you have a good doctor to help cure it!

Daily Challenge

Place in the top three of the **Malady Mash** Guild Daily Challenge, only if the player had contributed points to the challenge.
The **Tainted Cheese** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the Challengers Approach! update.
##  Function
The stinkier the better.

I guess cheddar will have to do.

When equipped, the **Tainted Cheese** grants the _Malady Increase: Tainted Cheese_ mod, which allows the player to have a higher chance of getting a malady upon performing a successful surgery. It will also summon a sewer rat pet which will follow the player around. Unlike  Dark Doctor's Cloak, this item does not increase the odds of contracting another player with a vicious malady.
