There ain't no mountain far enough that these boots can't take you to!

The **Boots of Summer** is an unsplicable feet item that was added as part of Equinox Weekend on March 25, 2022.
##  Function
Beat the heat!

Bare feet!

When equipped, the **Boots of Summer** grants the _Speedy: Boots of Spring_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Equinox Summer set, which consists of the following items:

    * Crown of Summer (hat)

    * Shirt of Summer (shirt)

    * Pants of Summer (pants)

    * Boots of Summer (shoes)
