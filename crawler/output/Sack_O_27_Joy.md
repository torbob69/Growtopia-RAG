The **Sack O' Joy** is a splicable back item which was added as part of _Growtopia_ 's release.
##  Function
You suddenly feel very generous!

Bah, humbug.

When equipped, the **Sack O' Joy** grants the _Cheerful Giver_ mod, which allows the player to drop gems from their own balance as they move around. When the player runs out of gems, no more will be dropped.
##  Trivia
  * The Sack O' Joy is the first item to be based on Santa Claus's sack of gifts, the second one being WinterFest 2021's Santa's Super Sack.
  * This item is considered a part of the santa set, which consists of the following items:

    * Santa Hat (hat)

    * White Beard (face)

    * Santa Vest (shirt)

    * Santa Pants (pants)

    * Sack O' Joy (back)
  * Dropping gems with a Sack O' Joy used to make the player lose XPs, since that players were able to gain them from picking up gems.
  * Dropping gems into a Display Box or Transmatter Field no longer works due to a patch, which is intended to prevent players from selling dropped gems to other players.
  * A blog post written by ex-developer Hamumu revealed that the Sack O' Joy is the first clothing item in _Growtopia_ to grant "special powers".[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Sack_O'_Joy#cite_note-1)
