##  Function
<https://growtopiawiki.com/w/File:Upbeat.ogg>Dabstep Low Top Sneakers' music
Dabbing to the beat!

The dab is over!

When equipped, the **Dabstep Low Top Sneakers** grants the _Dabbing_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land. When the player dances or dabs they will let out spontaneous bursts of sound waves from the feet when using the command /dab, similar to the Party Blaster's effect.
