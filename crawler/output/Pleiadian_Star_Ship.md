# Pleiadian Star Ship

Without a source of antimatter, all this ship can do is hover along the ground. Sponsored by Pleiadian.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.

Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Pleiadian Star Ship** for **25,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"Float on, my brother. It's all groovy. This star ship can't fly, but you can still zoom around in it, leaving a trail of energy rings and moving at enhanced speed. Sponsored by Pleiadian. Not available any other way!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Pleiadian Star Ship** is an unsplicable feet item which was added alongside the Zombie Defense Pack on May 17, 2013.
##  Function
You can move faster!

You are slow again.

When equipped, the **Pleiadian Star Ship** grants the _Speedy_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land. Additionally, blue rings will be left behind as the player moves or jumps.
##  Trivia
  * Both the Zeta Reticulant Mask and Pleiadian Star Ship are sponsored by the player Pleiadian, who claimed to have seen hundreds of UFO sightings. Pleiadian alleged that those were potential sightings of benevolent extraterrestrials called the Pleiadians, thus inspiring the player's in-game name, as well as the Pleiadian Star Ship itself.[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Pleiadian_Star_Ship#cite_note-1)
  * The Howler's projectiles share the same sprite as the particles left by this item.
