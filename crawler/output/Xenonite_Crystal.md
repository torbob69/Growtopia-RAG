# Xenonite Crystal

This alien crystal has the power to grant or remove superpowers from all the Growtopians in your world! Use your wrench to set what powers it affects. Only one is allowed per world.
Properties
This item can only be used in World-Locked worlds.
This item can't be destroyed - smashing it will return it to your backpack if you have room!
This item has special properties you can adjust with the Wrench.

The **Xenonite Crystal** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added alongside the Superhero Pack on December 12, 2014.
##  Function
A Xenonite Crystal can be used to grant or remove the following mods to or from players in the world:
  * Double Jump
  * High Jump
  * Heat Resist
  * Strong Punch
  * Long Punch
  * Speedy
  * Long Build
  * Speedy in Water
  * Punch Damage

Additionally, the Xenonite Crystal grants the option to block the use of Powerups in the world. The selected effect is present as the _Xenonite_ mod. These are the Powerups that can be blocked with Xenonite:
  * Bubble Gum
  * Coffee
  * Das Red Balloon
  * Janeway's Coffee
  * Snowball

##  Trivia
  * Creating one for the first time will complete an Epic Quest.
  * As of Player Appreciation Week 2022 Day 3, the Xenonite Crystal now includes two additional mods:
    * Speedy in Water
    * Punch Damage
