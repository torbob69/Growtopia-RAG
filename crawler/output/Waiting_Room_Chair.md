The **Waiting Room Chair** is an unsplicable non-solid seat foreground block which was added as part of Block Party 2 on April 1, 2019.
##  Function
When a player stands in front of a seat such as the Waiting Room Chair for a few seconds, the player will automatically be seen "sitting" on it.
