The **Scarlet Macaw Pants** is an unsplicable pants item which was added as part of Wildlife Weekend on March 10, 2023.
##  Function
When equipped, the Scarlet Macaw Pants serves a fully cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia

    * Scarlet Macaw Hood (Hair)

    * Scarlet Macaw Charm (Chest)

    * Scarlet Macaw Wings (Back)
