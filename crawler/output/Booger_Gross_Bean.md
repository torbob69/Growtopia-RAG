The **Booger Gross Bean** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Gross-Out update on May 1, 2019.
##  Function
When consumed, a Booger Gross Bean grants an unknown mod for 10 seconds, giving the player a dark green skin color. The player will also be visually encased in a snot puddle while the mod is still active.
