The **Weather Machine - Mt. Growmore** is an unsplicable non-solid weather machine foreground block which was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2022 on November 19, 2022.
##  Function
As a weather machine, this item can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, it will change its appearance, transforming the world's background weather into a replica parody of Mount Rushmore.
##  Trivia
  * The **Weather Machine - Mount Growmore's** appearance is based on Mount Rushmore located at Pennington County, South Dakota.
  * The background shows familiar faces from the game itself:
    * From left to right: Buddy (Growtopia's mascot), the Legendary Wizard, Locke, and the Ringmaster
