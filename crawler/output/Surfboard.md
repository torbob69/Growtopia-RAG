The **Surfboard** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of SummerFest 2017 on June 30, 2017.
##  Function
When equipped, the **Surfboard** allows the player to float on water for three seconds.
The  Winterfest Exclusive Mission - Shred the Ice! mission requires 20 of this item.
