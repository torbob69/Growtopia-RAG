# Bubble Wings

With a pair of wings, you can double-jump! With these wings, you can also bubble.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be transmuted.
Data

Type: Back – Clothes
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: Full Collision
Hardness: **0 Hits** **0 Hits** – Restores after **0s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Bunny Egg Reward

Break a  Bunny Egg at 90-99% completion. 1 of this item is given out each time.
Bunny Eggs may explode randomly when their sizes are above 100%.
The **Bubble Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Easter Week 2014 on April 19, 2014.
##  Function
You can jump in mid-air! It's like flying, but less.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Bubble Wings** grants the _Double Jump: Bubble Wings_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
Step 7 of the  Quest Of The Heavens requires the player to deliver 3 of this item.
