The **Candle Wall Sconce** is an unsplicable toggleable animated foreground block which was added as part of Spring Clash 2019 on March 1, 2019.
##  Function
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Candle Wall Sconce can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Candle Wall Sconce will change its appearance, showing it being lit up. As a light source, a Candle Wall Sconce emits light in dark areas.
