##  Function
In order to catch Ghost, a **Ghost Jar** must be placed and the Ghost is pulled using a Neutron Gun and Neutron Pack, directly above the **Ghost Jar** which lasts for only five seconds. To see Ghosts, use Spectral Goggles or Synthetic Oculum and to prevent getting slimed, use Rubber Boots. Successfully captured Ghosts will give a Ghost-In-A-Jar while capturing Mind Ghosts will give a Mind-Ghost-In-A-Jar. Capturing an Ancestor Ghost will give an Ancestor Mooncake.
