The **Overstuffed Vintage Leather Armchair** is a non-solid seat foreground block that was added as part of Summer Clash 2018.
##  Function
When a player stands in front of a seat such as the Overstuffed Vintage Leather Armchair for a few seconds, the player will automatically be seen "sitting" on it.
