The **Scrolls** is an unsplicable component which was added as part of the Dungeons update on December 9, 2024.
##  Function
A Scroll is generated every 36 minutes (reduced to 18 minutes with an active Dungeons Pass) and is displayed in the upper-left corner of the player's screen. Players can access the Dungeon window to begin playing Dungeons, which requires five Scrolls. If a player loses all three lives, they can use two additional Scrolls to gain an extra life and continue their progress.
