The **Brown Pet Apatoceratops** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Growassic Park on October 7, 2016.
##  Function
When equipped, the Brown Pet Apatoceratops serves a purely cosmetic purpose and summons a Brown Pet Apatoceratops which follows the player around.
