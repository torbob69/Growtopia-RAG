"Growtopia's most loved gameshow now brings you its very own TV to watch up to 5 ads per day for AMAZING prizes."
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **GrowShow TV** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground interactive block which was added alongside Cinco De Mayo Week 2020 on May 1, 2020.
##  Function
When wrenched, it will prompt the player to watch an ad which will award a player either a Locked and Loaded Gatcha or a Surgery Gatcha. The player may only watch a maximum of 5 videos per day.
##  Trivia
  * The seed was obtainable prior it's first released. However, this was patched shortly after few hours after the release of the item.
  * The GrowShow TV only works on mobile devices, wrenching one while on Windows/Mac will show the message _"There seems to be no reception from this device. Maybe try again from a mobile device."_
  * As of Player Appreciation Week 2023 Day 3, GrowShow TV costs 1 Gem, previously 50 Gems.
  * As of January 16, 2024, players can now watch 5 ads per day, previously 3.
