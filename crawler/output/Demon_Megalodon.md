# Demon Megalodon

Oh, what have you DONE?! This terrifying shark combines prehistoric predator power with the unholy corruption of Growganoth to create an unstoppable chomping machine! Class: DEMON-SIZED! See this shark in action in Hungry Shark World!

The **Demon Megalodon** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2017.
##  Function
Swim faster!

No shark for you.

When equipped, the **Demon Megalodon** grants the _I'm On A Shark!_ mod, which allows the player to move faster in water. Unlike items such as the Swim Fins and Mermaid Tail, the Demon Megalodon does not slow the player down on land.
##  Trivia
  * Demon Megalodon shares exactly the same mod as its regular counterpart, the  Megalodon.
