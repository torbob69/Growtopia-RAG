The **Dual Spears of Diarmuid** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2024 on March 14, 2024.
##  Function
Double Power!

Zero Power!

When equipped, the **Dual Spears of Diarmuid** grants the _Punch Damage + Punch Range: Dual Spears of Diarmuid_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and punch one tile further.
##  Trivia
  * Dual Spears of Diarmuid is a reference to _Diarmuid Ua Duibhne_ who is a hero and demigod in the Fenian Cycle of Irish mythology.
    * However, the item's design is a reference to Diarmuid Ua Duibhne from a video game called Fate/Grand Order.
  * Dual Spears of Diarmuid has a recolored version:  Dual Obsidian Spears.
