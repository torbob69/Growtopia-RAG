The **Great Pink Jawbreakers** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Beach Party 2025 on August 14, 2025.
##  Function
Catch thee jaws!

No time to chomp!

When equipped, the **Great Pink Jawbreakers** grants the _Punch Damage + Punch Range: Great Pink Jawbreakers_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and punch one tile further.
