The **Fireworks** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of SummerFest 2013 on June 21, 2013.
##  Function
When consumed, several fireworks will fly out vertically and explode into colorful explosions. This item's effect is identical to that of Lunar New Year's Chinese Fireworks or Anniversary Week's Skyrocket. Unlike the other two, consuming Fireworks during SummerFest has a small chance to spawn a Summer Surprise in the world.
