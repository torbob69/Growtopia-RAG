# Pet Mech Robot

This cute little mech is powered by pure love essences. Gives a small increase in the chance of getting a Golden Heart Crystal when opening Golden Booty Chests, Super Golden Booty Chests and Heartstones.

The **Pet Mech Robot** is an unsplicable face item which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2024 on February 14, 2024.
##  Function
Take to the skies and increased chance of Golden Heart Crystal!

Fallen from love!

When equipped, the **Pet Mech Robot** grants the _Lucky in Love & Punch Range & Punch Damage : Pet Mech Robot_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further and break blocks faster. Additionally, the Pet Mech Robot grants the player a small increase in the chance of getting a Golden Heart Crystal when opening Golden Booty Chests, Super Golden Booty Chests and Heartstones.
