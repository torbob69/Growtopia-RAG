The **Pinata Whistle** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2013 on May 3, 2013.
##  Function
When consumed, an Ultra Pinata Event will occur spawning an Ultra Pinata. All players in the world can smash it as it moves around the world. The last few players to punch it will receive various Cinco De Mayo items.
