The **Golden Moyaimorph** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2022 Day 4 on August 18, 2022.
##  Function
Golden Head!

Just a myth...

When equipped, the Golden Moyaimorph grants the _Golden Moyaimorph: Sparkes_ mod, which causes the player to emit sparkles.
##  Trivia
  * The  Golden Moyaimorph is a recolor of  Moyaimorph.
  * As of WinterFest 2022, the Golden Moyaimorph can be obtained from Royal Winter Seals (Branch 4) and is no longer a discord-exclusive item.
  * There are a total of 4 Moyaimorphs in Growtopia, including  Moyaimorph,  Golden Moyaimorph,  Diamond Moyaimorph, and  Pearl Moyaimorph.
