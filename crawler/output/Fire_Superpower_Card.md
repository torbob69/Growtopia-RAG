# Fire Superpower Card

This Superpower Card is usually red in colour with an orange background.
Superpower - Heat Vision
Superpower - Incinerate
Superpower - Flame On!
Superpower - Liquify
Superpower - Overheat
Superpower - Death Ray
Superpower - Atomic Backbreaker

##  Trivia
  * The Flame On! card refers to the Human Torch's line "Flame on!" when he ignites himself.
