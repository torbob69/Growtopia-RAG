The **Pants of Autumn** is an unsplicable pants item that was added as part of Equinox Weekend on March 25, 2022.
##  Function
When equipped, the Pants of Autumn serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Equinox Autumn set, which consists of the following items:

    * Crown of Autumn (hat)

    * Shirt of Autumn (shirt)

    * Pants of Autumn (pants)

    * Boots of Autumn (shoes)
