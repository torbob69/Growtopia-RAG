# Growtokens! (update)

Growtopia Gazette
> March 30th: Growtokens are here! You earn them each day based on how many people visit your worlds, so go make some awesome worlds, then spend your Growtoken on fabulous prizes in the shop!
> NEW: Growtokens! You'll need to wait a day or more to earn some, happens on first logon of the day. If you only earn part of a Growtoken, it will be remembered for the future. They can't be traded.
>  NEW: Some assorted surprise items
> - @Seth and @Hamumu
The **Growtokens!** update was introduced on March 30, 2014. It mainly revolved around the addition of Growtokens and the Growtoken Store into Growtopia.
##  New additions
New additions introduced in this update include:
### New items
Item: RecipesGrowtoken: _See main article:Growtoken._
Experience Potion: 1 can be obtained from purchasing the item itself for 10 Growtokens in the Growtopia Store.
Weather Machine - Nothingness: 1 can be obtained from purchasing the item itself for 50 Growtokens in the Growtopia Store.
Doodad: 1 can be obtained from purchasing the item itself for 75 Growtokens in the Growtopia Store.
Rushboard: 1 was given to Rush for his contributions as one of Growtopia's voluntary moderators.
