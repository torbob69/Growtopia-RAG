The **Candy Cane Cruiser** is an unsplicable feet item that was added as part of the Pre-WinterFest 2021 update on December 1, 2021. The item was formally introduced on December 25, 2021.
##  Function
Sugar crash!

Sweet speed!

When equipped, the **Candy Cane Cruiser** grants the _Speedy: Candy Cruiser_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.
