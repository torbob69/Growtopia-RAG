The **Prehistoric Dragon Claw** is an unsplicable pet hand item which was added as part of the Dragon Invasion update.
##  Function
Prehistoric draconic wisdom!

Your ancient wisdom is no more.

When equipped, the **Prehistoric Dragon Claw** grants the _XP Buff: Fossil Processing_ mod, which allows the player to gain an extra 50 XP when harvesting a Fossil Prep Station.
