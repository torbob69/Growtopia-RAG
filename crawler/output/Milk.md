The **Milk** is an unsplicable consumable which was added alongside the Farm Pack on July 6, 2013.
##  Function
Your bones are nigh unbreakable.

You can feel your bones softening.

When consumed, this item grants the _Strong Bones_ mod, which grants no effect.

### Silkworm Food     _Main article:Guide:Silkworm Care_
This consumable can be fed to a Silkworm to give it a white color, thus allowing it to produce 1–10  Silk Thread - White per harvest.
##  Trivia
  * Milk takes around 20 seconds to cook perfectly.
  * Contrary to popular belief, Milk does not boost punching speed or power.
