The **Grow Stusios Globe** is an unsplicable toggleable multi-framed animated block that was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2024 on November 21, 2024.
##  Function
Being a toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block, the Grow Studios Globe can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, an Grow Studios Globe will change its appearance, showing an animation.
