# Loafers

Keep your feet toasty all year round with these loafers, there is nothing butter than wearing bread on your feet!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be transmuted.

**1h**
Bunny Egg Reward

Break a  Bunny Egg at 120-139% completion. 1 of this item is given out each time.
Bunny Eggs may explode randomly when their sizes are above 100%.
The **Loafers** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Easter Week 2020.
##  Function
Bread for speed!

Hungry again!

When equipped, the **Loafers** grants the _Speedy: Loafers_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.

##  Trivia
  * The name "Loafers" is a play on the word "loafers", referring to leather shoes that are shaped like moccasins with a low flat heel, and the word "loaf", referring to a rounded mass of food, usually of bread.
