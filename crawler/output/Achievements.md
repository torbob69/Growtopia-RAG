##  Trivia
  * In a forum post,[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Achievements#cite_note-1) _Growtopia_ 's creator Seth revealed that he had already planned to add achievements for a long time, even keeping track of player progress in certain activities. Theoretically, this would allow longtime players to immediately earn the achievements they deserve when the feature was properly implemented.
  * On October 1, 2018, along with the Stuff! Update, 46 more achievements were added, increasing the total number of achievements from 100 to 146.
  * On February 8, 2019, along with Valentine's Week, the Kevin Bacon achievement was adjusted from 99 players in a single world to 70, due to lowered maximum player count in a world making this achievement previously impossible to obtain.
  * On June 1, 2020, 5 more achievements were added alongside the Startopia 2.0 update, bumping up the total from 162 to 167.
  * On March 13, 2026, the requirements for the Daily Challenge (Classic) and Monthly Challenge (Classic) achievements were changed now only requiring players to place in the top 5 of a Daily Challenge. Previously requiring players to place in the top 3.
  * The three achievements:_Legen..._ ,  _Wait For It..._ and  _DARY!_ are all references to Barney Stinson's catchphrase from the American sitcom  _How I Met Your Mother._
  * _Tea, Earl Grey, Hot_ achievement is a reference to a quote from a fictional character called Jean-Luc Picard from Star Trek franchise.
  * Completing the _Growtopian of the year_ achievement will complete an epic quest.
  * Completing all 100 _Classic_ achievements will complete another epic quest.
