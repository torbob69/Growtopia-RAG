# Mystic Record Player

This mystic record player will transport you to far off lands with an enchanting melody. Just punch it to play the tune. Note : please do not punch record players outside of Growtopia, it will hurt and no music will play!
Properties
This item has special properties you can adjust with the Wrench.
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can't be destroyed - smashing it will return it to your backpack if you have room!
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.

The **Mystic Record Player** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2020 on September 24, 2020.
##  Function
<https://growtopiawiki.com/w/File:Mooncakemelody.ogg>The Mystic Record Player's music
Being a toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block, a Mystic Record Player can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Mystic Record Player will change its appearance, showing a 9-framed animation, and play a piece of music.
##  Trivia
  * In the game files, the Mystic Record Player's music is named mooncakemelody.
