The **Loveseat** is an unsplicable non-solid seat foreground block which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2017 on February 10, 2017.
##  Function
When a player stands in front of a seat such as the Loveseat for a few seconds, the player will automatically be seen "sitting" on it.
##  Trivia
  * The Loveseat's name is a play on the word "loveseat", which refers to both the item's heart-inspired design and a style of chair in real life.
