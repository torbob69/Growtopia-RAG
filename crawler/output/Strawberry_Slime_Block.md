The **Strawberry Slime Block** is an unsplicable solid slippery foreground block which was added as part of the Gross-Out update.
##  Function
Being a slippery block, walking on a Strawberry Slime Block causes the player to slide.
The  Myths and Legends : Finding the Swamp Monster mission requires 200 of this item.
##  Trivia
  * The  Strawberry Slime Block is a recolor of  Slime.
    * Unlike the Pet Slime, which is introduced after its block counterpart, the Strawberry Slime pet is introduced before the Strawberry Slime Block.
