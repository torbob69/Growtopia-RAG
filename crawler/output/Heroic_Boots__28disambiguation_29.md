# Heroic Boots (disambiguation)

This is a **disambiguation** page.
There are more than one subjects in the **Growtopia Wiki** using this name.

Heroic Boots can be obtained randomly by defeating a villain.
There are 6 **Heroic Boots** which consists of 6 different colours.
  * Heroic Boots - Red
  * Heroic Boots - Blue
  * Heroic Boots - Green
  * Heroic Boots - Purple
  * Heroic Boots - Black
  * Heroic Boots - Gold

There are also 4 **Heroic Boots** that are based on St. Patrick's Week, Easter Week, Harvest Festival and Super Pineapple Party.
  * Heroic Boots - Emerald
  * Heroic Boots - Pink
  * Heroic Boots - Pineapple
  * Heroic Boots - Autumn
