The **Hover Platform** is a splicable platform which was added as part of Night Of The Chemicals update on 2nd March 2017.
##  Function
A Hover Platform behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath. In addition, it hovers around the tile it is placed on visually.
