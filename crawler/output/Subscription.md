# Subscription

**Subscription Tokens** are untradable consumables that were added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2018. They are obtained through various ways: the 1-Day Free Subscription Token are obtained through watching advertisements, while the 1-Year Subscription Token can be obtained through in-app purchases costing $49.99 or from reaching a milestone of mentored 200 apprentices to level 20 respectively.
Subscription tokens like the 3-Day Free Subscription Token and 14-Day Free Subscription Token were obtainable through watching ads but were later removed and replaced with the 1-Day Free Subscription Token.
When consumed, a subscription token will give the player access to unique skin colors, a chance of doubling any XP earned, seasonal tokens (as long as there's a seasonal clash running), and a number of Gems daily.
On the 2nd until 6th of each month, a limited yet tradable item is made available to every player with an active premium subscription (has consumed a 30-Day Premium Subscription Token, 1-Year Subscription Token or a 365-Day Premium Subscription Token before). A player can only be given the item by the game once.
The six types of subscription tokens are:
1-Day Free Subscription Token
3-Day Free Subscription Token
14-Day Free Subscription Token
30-Day Premium Subscription Token
1-Year Subscription Token
365-Day Premium Subscription Token
