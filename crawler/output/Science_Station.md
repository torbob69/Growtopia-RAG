The **Science Station** is a splicable non-solid provider foreground block which was added alongside the Mad Science Kit on July 20, 2013.
##  Function
When a Science Station is harvested (by being punched), a random basic chemical will randomly drop. One can be harvested approximately every 12 hours.
A list of harvestable chemicals and their odds can be seen below.
  * Chemical G (1/~3)
  * Chemical R (1/~4.5-5)
  * Chemical Y (1/~7.5)
  * Chemical B (1/~8.5-9)
  * Chemical P (1/~17)
