The **Egg Rescue Ticket** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Easter Week 2018.
##  Function
When consumed during Easter Week, the player will be automatically directed to the world EGGRESCUE. This ticket allows the player to join in the Egg Rescue mini-game.
