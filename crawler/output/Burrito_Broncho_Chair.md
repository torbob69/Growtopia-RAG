Strap on, keep a hold of that salsa and chips as things are about to get messy! A rideable Burrito Broncho chair for when you want to ride.... and eat the rodeo!

The **Burrito Broncho Chair** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable multi-framed animated seat foreground block which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2020 on May 1, 2020.
##  Function
When a player stands in front of a seat such as the Burrito Broncho Chair for a few seconds, the player will automatically be seen "sitting" on it, going back and forth with their hand raising high.
