Piles of fall leaves are good for exactly two things: Jumping into them, and who cares? I'm gonna jump in some leaves!

Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Longevity Mooncake **(×4)** ,
Ancestor Mooncake **(×1)** and
Peace Mooncake **(×4)**.
This recipe will yield **3** items.
Mooncake Offering

1 may be received as a common reward from offering mooncakes using an Offering Table.
The **Leaves** is a non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2017 on October 4, 2017.
##  Function
When placed, the Leaves serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
