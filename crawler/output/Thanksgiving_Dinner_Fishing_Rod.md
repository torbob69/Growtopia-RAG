# Thanksgiving Dinner Fishing Rod

A rod of such great finery!

Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Turkey Feather **(×50)** ,
Organic Turkey Feather **(×5)** and
Goldenrod **(×1)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Thanksgiving Dinner Fishing Rod** is an unspliceable fishing rod hand item which was added on Thanksgiving Week 2020.
##  Function
When equipped, the **Thanksgiving Dinner Fishing Rod** allows the player to increase their odds by 100% of catching a fish.
##  Trivia
  * While the Thanksgiving Dinner Fishing Rod normally guarantees a fish, it does not prevent rarer drops such as Lucky Lizard Hat from being caught.
