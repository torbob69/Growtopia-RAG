The **Fortune Cookie** is a consumable that was added as part of Lunar New Year 2018 on February 16, 2018. Harvesting **Fortune Cookie** trees with any Zodiac Animal equipped gives a chance of twice the yield.
##  Function

Fortune Cookies can only be consumed during Lunar New Years.
