It can be found naturally generated in Monochrome-blasted worlds.

The **Monochromatic Lava** is an unsplicable lava pain foreground block that was added as part of the Black Friday 2018 on November 22, 2018.
##  Function
Being a lava pain block, a Monochromatic Lava knocks the player back and puts the player on fire until the player stops all motion when touched. A player will die after making contact with a lava pain block five times in quick succession (recovering from damage takes roughly 5 seconds), but a player with the _Fireproof_ mod may take up to ten consecutive hits.
##  Trivia
  * Placing two Monochromatic Lava next to each other will cause them to join, creating a continuous pattern.
  * The sprite of Monochromatic Lava is a recolor of Lava.
