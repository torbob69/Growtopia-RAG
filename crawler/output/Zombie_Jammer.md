The **Zombie Jammer** is an unsplicable solid foreground jammer block which was added as part of the Mystery Update on November 11, 2013.
##  Function
When toggled on (via punching), a Zombie Jammer will change its appearance and prevent visitors of the world to be infected with the g-Virus, except with direct injection.
##  Appearance
The Zombie Jammer glows green when activated and has a big Z engraved on it, which stands for "Zombie". When activated, it makes a kind-of-whirring-like sound which can be silenced with the wrench. It also puts the "IMMUNE" tagline in green whenever players enter the world, to indicate that they are immune to the g-Virus as long as they are inside the world, or as long as the jammer is activated.
##  Trivia
  * The Zombie Jammer can be set to public, allowing anyone to turn it on and off. To activate one, punch it once, and to deactivate it, punch the jammer again.
  * The Zombie Jammer has no effect during a pandemic.
