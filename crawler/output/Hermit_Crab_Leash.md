The **Hermit Crab Leash** is an unsplicable hand item introduced as part of Player Appreciation Week 2021 Day 5 on August 27, 2021.
##  Function
When equipped, _Hermit Crab Leash_ summons a Hermit Crab which follows you around.
The  The Seven Seas : The Crust of the Crab! mission requires 1 of this item.
