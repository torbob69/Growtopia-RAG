# Blocks/Treasure Blocks

< Blocks
**Treasure Blocks** are blocks which drops a random number of items when broken, depending on the block.
**Treasure Blocks**

Item: Description
Deep Iron: Mine this to forge stuff out of Deep Iron!
Golden Treasure Hoard: Ooh, smash that pile of gold, jewels and priceless treasure! There's probably something GOOD inside!
Hidden Treasure: Glittering secrets! But be careful! Don't tell anyone about it! If you do, it won't be hidden anymore - it'll just be regular treasure, and who wants that?
Locke's Mystery Box: This block is a total mystery. Smash it and see what's inside!
Mystery Block: What could possibly be inside this block!?!? Gems, that's what.
Pot O' Gold: An entire pot filled with gold!!
Pure Magic Ore: A block of pure magic ore!
