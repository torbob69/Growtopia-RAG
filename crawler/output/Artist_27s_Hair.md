The **Artist's Hair** is an unsplicable hair item which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2022 on May 5, 2022.
##  Function
Beauty is in the hair of the beholder!

Withered to pieces!

When equipped, the **Artist's Hair** grants the _Build Range : Artist's Hair_ mod, which allows the player to build one tile further.
##  Trivia
  * The Artist's Hair is based on that of the well-known Mexican painter Frida Kahlo.
