The **Jormungandr** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of the Dragon Invasion update. One has a chance to be dropped from breaking a ready Hatching Water Egg.
##  Function
You have the world serpent's wisdom!

Your wisdom is no more.

When equipped, the **Jormungandr** grants the _XP Buff: Wolf Worlds_ mod, which allows the player to move faster in water and gain an extra 2,000 XP when successfully completing a wolf world.
##  Trivia
  * The name and appearance of this item is a reference to Jörmungandr, a sea serpent in Norse mythology.
