# Superb Chef's Super Spatula

Get your flip on! This spatula will flip any objects with a single slap! Use it on chairs, beds, and anything else you find facing the wrong way! Best of all, it's made of patented GrowTech spatulanium, guaranteed to stay firm even after a thousand slaps!

Daily Challenge

Place in the top three of the **Chef Showdown** Guild Daily Challenge, only if the player had contributed points to the challenge.
The **Superb Chef's Super Spatula** is an unsplicable hand item that was added as part of the Challengers Approach! update.
##  Function
Tiles look like really big flapjacks.

The world looks bland.

When equipped, the **Superb Chef's Super Spatula** grants the _Flipping: Superb Chef's Super Spatula_ mod, which allows the player to flip any eligible block by punching it. Unlike the Enchanted Spatula, it does not break upon usage.
