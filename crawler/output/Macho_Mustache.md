Punch to put on the most macho mustache you've ever seen!

The **Macho Mustache** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2023 on May 5, 2023.
##  Function
Macho Libre!

Not so Libre!

When equipped, the Macho Mustache grants the _Punch Range + Card Battle Strength: Macho Mustache_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further and reduces 2 health from the villain or another player.
