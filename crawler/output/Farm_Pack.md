The **Farm Pack** is an item pack which was added on July 6, 2013.
##  Items
The pack contains the following items:
  * Cow ×1
  * Chicken ×1
  * Wheat ×10
  * Barn Block ×10
  * Red Wood Wall ×10
  * Barn Door ×1
  * Straw Hat or  Distinguished Straw Hat ×1
  * Overalls ×1
  * Pitchfork ×1
  * Farmgirl Hair ×1
  * Dear John Tractor ×1 (Rare)

##  Trivia
  * The Egg, Milk, Piglet Leash, Chick Leash, Calf Leash and the Butterfly Leash were also implemented into the game alongside this pack.
