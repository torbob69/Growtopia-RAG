**Beehive** is a splicable solid pain foreground block which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2016.
##  Function
Being a type of pain block, a Beehive knocks the player back and stings the player until the player stops all motion. A player will die after making contact with a pain block five times in quick succession (recovering from damage takes roughly 5 seconds). The _Fireproof_ mod will not allow the player to take more hits, unlike with lava pain blocks.
When a player with access punches a Beehive, bees will come out of the Beehive and immediately target the nearest player. If a player comes in contact with the bees, they will be killed.
