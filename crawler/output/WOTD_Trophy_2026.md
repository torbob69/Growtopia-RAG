World Of The Day

Rewarded to a player when their world gets chosen as the World Of The Day between January 1, 2026 and December 31, 2026.
The **WOTD Trophy 2026** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block that is given to players who win World Of The Day during 2026 by Ubisoft Staff.
##  Function
When placed, the WOTD Trophy 2026 serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
Step 16 of the  Quest For Fire requires the player to deliver 1 of this item. (or the subsequent trophies)
