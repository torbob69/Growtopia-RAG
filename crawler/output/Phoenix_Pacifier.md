The **Phoenix Pacifier** is an unsplicable pet hand item which was added as part of SummerFest 2015 on July 3, 2015.
##  Function
You have a pet that spits fire.

Your pet has left you.

When equipped, the **Phoenix Pacifier** grants the _Fiery Pet_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and punch one tile further. It also causes the player to shoot fire out of its mouth, replacing the punch.
When the player respawns, the pet will disappear into a ball of flames before spawning beside the player on the white door or checkpoint.
