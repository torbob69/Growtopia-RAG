##  Function
Ultimate POWER!!!

Noooooooo!

When equipped, the **Radiant Doom Staff** grants the _Punch Damage: Purple Lightning_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. It will also summon a floating staff next to the player and replace the player's fist with purple lightning when punching.
##  Trivia
  * The _Radiant Doom Staff'_ s appearance is a reference to Eson the Searcher's weapon from the Marvel Cinematic Universe's Movie called "Guardians of the Galaxy".
  * As of Player Appreciation Week 2021 Day 1, the Radiant Doom Staff was added a lightning sound effect when punching.
