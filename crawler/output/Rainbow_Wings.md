The **Rainbow Wings** is a splicable back item which was added as part of SummerFest 2014 on June 27, 2014.
##  Function
You can jump in mid-air! It's like flying, but less.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Rainbow Wings** grants the _Double Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
Step 18 of the  Quest For Honor requires the player to deliver 10 of this item.
Step 14 of the  Quest Of The Heavens requires the player to deliver 20 of this item.
##  Trivia
  * The description of the Rainbow Wings contains a reference to the song "Over the Rainbow", which was written for the 1939 film _The Wizard of Oz_.
  * The Rainbow Wings was the first sponsored item to be released since item sponsoring was reintroduced.
  * This item has the second highest rarity in the game with Mini Mammoth Leash having the highest rarity.
  * Even though it is splicable, the wings cannot be compacted, due to its rarity being over 200.
