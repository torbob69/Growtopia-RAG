##  Function
Silent, invisible, deadly.

You are less sneaky now.

When equipped, the **Ninja Mask** grants the _Ninja Stealth_ mod, which allows the player to change their skin color to translucent dark grey.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Ninja set, which consists of the following items:

    * Ninja Mask (hair)

    * Ninja Vest (shirt)

    * Ninja Tights (pants)

    * Ninja Slippers (feet)
