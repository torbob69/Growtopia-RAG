The **Mighty Legend Gauntlets** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the Garuda's Glory update on August 26, 2022.
##  Function
Spirit Punch!

Spirit Calm!

When equipped, the **Mighty Legend Gauntlets** grants the _Punch Damage: Mighty Legend Gauntlets_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. Additionally, when equipped alongside the  Mighty Legend Helm,  Mighty Legend Armor,  Mighty Legend Pants and  Mighty Legend Boots, the player will hover above the ground.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Mighty Legend set, which consists of the following items:

    * Mighty Legend Helm (Hat)

    * Mighty Legend Armor (Shirt)

    * Mighty Legend Pants (Pants)

    * Mighty Legend Boots (Feet)

    * Mighty Legend Gauntlets (Hand)
  * The Mighty Legend set is a reference to the fictional character Gatotkaca from the Sanskrit epics of Ancient India titled Mahabharata.
  * The Gauntlets design may referring to the Gatotkaca design from the MOBA game named Mobile Legends. Which is inspired by the Spirit who reside on Gatotkaca's arms. The Spirit on his left arm named as Brajamusti, while on his right arm named as Brajadenta
