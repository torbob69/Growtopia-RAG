The **Sun Block** is an unsplicable solid foreground block which was added as part of SummerFest 2015 on July 3, 2015.
##  Function
When placed, the Sun Block serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
The  Myths and Legends : The Menace of the Mini Minokawa mission requires 100 of this item.
