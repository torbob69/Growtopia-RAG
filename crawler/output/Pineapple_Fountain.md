The **Pineapple Fountain** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable foreground block which was added as part of the Super Pineapple Party 2022 update on June 10, 2022.
##  Function
Being a toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block, a Pineapple Fountain can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Fountain will change its appearance, showing a 4-framed animation of yellow water flowing out of the fountain.
