# Mariachi Hat

Not only does this fancy hat cover your head from the heat, it also makes you look amazing while you sing and dance.

The **Mariachi Hat** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2017 on May 5, 2017.
##  Function
When equipped, the Mariachi Hat serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the mariachi set, which consists of the following items:

    * Mariachi Hat (hat)

    * Mariachi Suit (shirt)

    * Mariachi Pants (pants)

    * Mariachi Guitar (hand)
  * The  Mariachi Bobble Head can be seen wearing the full mariachi set.
