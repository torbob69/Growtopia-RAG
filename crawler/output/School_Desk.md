The **School Desk** is a splicable platform seat foreground block added with Education Pack in 13 April, 2013.
##  Function
The School Desk has the appearance of an orange chair pulled into a metal desk.
When a player stands in front of a seat such as the School Desk for a few seconds, the player will automatically be seen "sitting" on it. In addition, a School Desk behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath.
