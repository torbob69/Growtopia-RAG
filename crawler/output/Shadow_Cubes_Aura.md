The **Shadow Cubes Aura** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Black Friday 2021 on November 26, 2021.
##  Function
Cubes ahoy!

Byeeeeee cubes!

When equipped, the **Shadow Cubes Aura** grants the _Build Range : Shadow Cubes Aura_ mod, which allows the player to build one tile further.
