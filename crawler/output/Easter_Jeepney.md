The **Easter Jeepney** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Easter Exclusive Mission, the second set of Marvelous Missions, on April 1, 2021.
##  Function
Beep Beep, I'm in my Jeep...ney

Off the Buses!

When equipped, the **Easter Jeepney** grants the _Speedy: Easter Jeepney!_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.
##  Trivia
  * The Easter Jeepney is inspired by the real-life jeepney, a type of public transportation which originated in the Philippines.
  * As of Easter Week 2022, The Easter Jeepney mission no longer requires 10 Minecarts.
