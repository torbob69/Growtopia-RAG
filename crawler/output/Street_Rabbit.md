# Street Rabbit

A curious critter blending into the streets! Ready for action with a hop skip and a jump! Wrench yourself and choose Transform to transform!
Properties

Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Space Rabbit **(×2)** ,
Space Connector **(×200)** and
Greetings Earthlings Sign **(×10)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Street Rabbit** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the Aliens update on January 27, 2023.
##  Function
Hop to the top!

Toppled down!

When equipped, the **Street Rabbit** grants the _Double Jump & Punch Range : Street Rabbit_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air, punch one tile further, and switch between its wearable form and its morph form by wrenching oneself and clicking 'Transform'.
