"Long thought to be extinct, it turns out that these dinosaurs are actually alive and easily tamed. And riding one lets you run around faster than normal! Not available any other way!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Riding Raptor** is an unsplicable feet item that was added as part of the Prehistoric Pack update on December 13, 2013.
##  Function
You can move faster!

You are slow again.

When equipped, the **Riding Raptor** grants the _Speedy_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.
