The **Tunic of Fionn** in an unsplicable shirt item which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2024 on March 14, 2024.
##  Function
When equipped, the Tunic of Fionn serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Fionn set, which consists of the following items:

    * Hair of Fionn (Hair)

    * Tunic of Fionn (Shirt)
  * The full Fionn set is a reference to Fionn mac Cumhaill, who is a heroic figure from Irish mythology.
