# Howler (block category)

When placed, a **howler** will constantly shoot projectiles which, upon contact, instantly kill players. The speed of the projectiles can be changed from 10 to 255 pixels per second. The firing speed of a **howler** can be changed from 2 to 20 seconds for a shot to be fired. Only 10 blasts can exist at the same time.
