##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the knight set, which consists of the following items:

    * Knight Helmet (hat)

    * Plate Mail (shirt)

    * Plate Greaves (pants)
  * The Plate Greaves was suggested by the player Knightmare, who sponsored the Knight Helmet in 2014. Knightmare had suggested the item for three concurrent years (in 2015, 2016, and 2017) before the item finally made it into the game.
