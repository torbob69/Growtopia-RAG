The **Adventure Item - Golden Idol** is a splicable non-solid adventure item foreground block which was added alongside the Adventure Pack on June 14, 2014.
##  Function
As a type of adventure item, the Adventure Item - Golden Idol may work in conjunction with the Gateway To Adventure, the Adventure Pedestal and Adventure Idol Gate. When a player who has picked up an Adventure Item - Golden Idol steps on an Adventure Pedestal, all Adventure Idol Gates in the world will be toggled on or off for that player only depending on its previous state.
