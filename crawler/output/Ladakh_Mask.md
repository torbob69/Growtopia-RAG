# Ladakh Mask

Part of a cultural celebration of life, this mask isn't just a good-looking potential piece of your outfit! It's also used in a major dance festival in the Indian region of - you guessed it - Ladakh!

The **Ladakh Mask** is an unsplicable face item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2019 on September 12, 2019.
##  Function
When equipped, the Ladakh Mask serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia

    * Ladakh Mask (face)

    * Ladakh Shirt (shirt)

    * Ladakh Pants (pants)
