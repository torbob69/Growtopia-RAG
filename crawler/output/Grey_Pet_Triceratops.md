The **Grey Pet Triceratops** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Growassic Park on October 7, 2016.
##  Function
When equipped, the Grey Pet Triceratops serves a purely cosmetic purpose and summons a Grey Pet Triceratops which follows the player around.
