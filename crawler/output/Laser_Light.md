Unlocked during Grow4Good after at least **25,000,000** global points have been accumulated.
The **Laser Light** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Grow4Good Week 2023 on April 20, 2023.
##  Function
When activated, the Laser Light will play a 3-framed animation, as well as display laser lights that extend upwards in the air.
##  Trivia
  * During Grow4Good Week 2025, the point requirement to unlock the Laser Light was lowered from 70,000,000 to 25,000,000.
