The **Cherry** is a splicable consumable which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2017 on June 8, 2017.
##  Function
Cherry red lips!

The effects of the Cherry have worn off.

When consumed, the Cherry grants the _Blushing Red_ mod, which giving the player a red skin color for 30 minutes.
