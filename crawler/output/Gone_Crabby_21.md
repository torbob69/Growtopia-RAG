# Gone Crabby!

Growtopia Gazette
> March 3rd: GONE CRABBY!
> Something smells fishy you say? Why yes it does. Every body of water from the mighty oceans to the tiniest puddles now contain new aquatic friends to catch. And possibly eat.
> -@Seth and Hamumu

The **Gone Crabby!** update was released on March 3rd 2016. It released a number of new items relating to crabs/fishing.
The new items released were:
