The **Golden Ticket Booth** is an unobtainable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of The Carnival update on October 13, 2014.
##  Function
Several Golden Ticket Booths can be found at certain spots in each CARNIVAL world.
When punched, it will say, "_Golden Tickets cost 100 Rarity worth of any items. If you give me extras, I'll keep a record. You can also pay withWorld Locks. I'll give you 30 Tickets per World Lock. Just tap items on me to give them. You currently have a credit of <number> Rarity._"
Using any item with rarity or a World Lock on a Golden Ticket Booth will allow the player to trade 100 rarity worth of items for a Golden Ticket, or 1 World Lock for 30.
##  Trivia
  * The ability to trade World Locks for Golden Tickets was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2015.
  * The Golden Ticket Booth uses the same sprite as Geoff, The Guard.
