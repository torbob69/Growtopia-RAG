The **Devil Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2013 on October 28, 2013.
##  Function
You can jump in mid-air! It's like flying, but less.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Devil Wings** grants the _Double Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
Step 19 of the  Quest For Fire requires the player to deliver 3 of this item.
The  Myths and Legends : Mothman Rising mission requires 1 of this item.
