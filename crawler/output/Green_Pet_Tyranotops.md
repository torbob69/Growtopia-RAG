The **Green Pet Tyranotops** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Growassic Park on October 7, 2016.
##  Function
When equipped, the Green Pet Tyranotops serves a purely cosmetic purpose and summons a Green Pet Tyranotops which follows the player around.
