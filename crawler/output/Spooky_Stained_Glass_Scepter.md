The **Spooky Stained Glass Scepter** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2023 on October 19, 2023.
##  Function
Let the light blind!

Moon's down!

When equipped, the **Spooky Stained Glass Scepter** grants the _Punch Damage : Spooky Stained Glass Scepter_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster.
