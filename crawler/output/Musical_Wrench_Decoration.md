The **Musical Wrench Decoration** is a wrench decoration which was added as part of the November Season on November 1st, 2024.
##  Function
When equipped through the 'Wrench Customization' tab when wrenching oneself, the Musical Wrench Decoration adds golden circles around the player's wrench connected to their name.
