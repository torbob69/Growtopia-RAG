# Staff

The staff of Growtopia is a group of people assigned with different tasks to assist the players, engage with the community, collate feedback, and enforce the rules in the game, Discord server, and the forums. Under Ubisoft's reign, there are currently four types of staff members in Growtopia, namely: **Abu Dhabi Community Team** , **Abu Dhabi Dev Team** , **Moderators** (split into three tiers: Veteran Moderator, Moderator & Trainee Moderator), and **Guardians**.
However, previously, during Seth and Hamumu's reign, there were only two types of staff members: **Developers** and **Moderators**.
##  Original Developers
The original developers are the creators of the game. They were responsible for things implemented in the game. Like moderators, they enforced the rules in the game.

##  Ubisoft Abu Dhabi Community Team
They manage the Community and oversee all Customer Support matters, ensuring all of the Growtopian's feedback is heard, communicated internally & responded to. They raise concerns by the community to improve the game experience. This team also engages with the players, moderates in-game, monitors the game health and creates community content on the Growtopia Social Media channels and on the forums. ##  Moderators
They monitor the player’s behavior in-game, on the forums and in the official discord server, enforce the game rules and punish those who do breach them. They help players with their issues, interact & engage with them, making our community a friendlier place!
Moderators are picked from the community and are granted special commands that normal players are restricted to use. As a reward for their hard work and dedication of moderating the community, they are usually given various items. Such items include:
  * Duct Tape
  * Freeze Wand
  * Fire Wand

They are also broken down into tiers (Veteran Moderator, Moderator and Trainee Moderator). After successful promotion from Guardian they will start out as a Trainee Moderator, where they will be evaluated based on their performance and activity at the end of a six month cycle, they will then either be promoted to Moderator or removed from the team. The powers and commands vary by each tier, but the specifics are not public.

##  Guardians
They monitor the game, the forums and the official discord server to help Growtopians have a better experience, as well as warn players and issue light punishments.
Roughly every six months, Guardians are recruited from Guardian Applications. They will be evaluated based on their performance and activity at the end of a six month cycle, they will then either be promoted to Trainee Moderator (T-MOD) or removed from the team.

For issues related to account recovery or purchasing, contact support at<https://support.ubi.com/en-GB/games/5847>"_
  * On October 7, 2019, Baskerville publicized a Meet the Team forum thread, which features all staff member's introductions and social media accounts.

  * As of November 14, 2019. Guardians are now the entry point before becoming a Moderator.[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Staff#cite_note-1)
  * Fournos was the first Moderator to obtain the Dr. Title.
  * On December 2, 2022, CoconutShrimp became the first Guardian to obtain the Dr. title.
  * On November 24, 2023, Trainee Moderator was introduced to give Guardians a fair and equal chance of moving on to becoming a Moderator for Growtopia.
  * On March 3, 2026, @Misthios was the first Moderator to have obtained both the Legendary Title & Dr. title.
