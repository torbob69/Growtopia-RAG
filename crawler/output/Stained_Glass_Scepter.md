The **Stained Glass Scepter** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2023 on February 10, 2023.
##  Function
Let the light shine!

Sun's down!

When equipped, the **Stained Glass Scepter** grants the _Punch Damage : Stained Glass Scepter_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster.
