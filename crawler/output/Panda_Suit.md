# Panda Suit

Aww, it's adorable! Don this suit (with associated mask) and you'll instantly look like the second-cutest type of panda (after red pandas) and second-most monochromatic bear (after polar bears) in the world! Just goes to show that you can be second-place in looks... and first in our hearts.

The **Panda Suit** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2018 on September 21, 2018.
##  Function
When equipped, the Panda Suit serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Panda set, which consists of the following items:

    * Panda Mask (Hat)

    * Panda Suit (Shirt)
  * The Panda Suit has the same combining recipe as the Panda Mask.
