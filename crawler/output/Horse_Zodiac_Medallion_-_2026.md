The **Horse Zodiac Medallion - 2026** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Lunar New Year 2026 on February 19, 2026.
##  Function
Zodiac Powers!

Another Year!

When equipped, the **Horse Zodiac Medallion - 2026** grants the _Speedy & Cookie Hunter : Horse Zodiac Medallion - 2026_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and provides a significant chance of doubling fruit drops from Fortune Cookie and Lucky Fortune Cookie Trees.
