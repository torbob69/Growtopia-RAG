The **Gemonade** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Role Up! update on September 17, 2018.
##  Function
Get extra gems from a variety of actions!

Your stomach has too many bubbles!

When consumed, this item grants the _Sparkling Gems_ food buff mod, which provides the player with bonus gems for 30 minutes from performing the following tasks:
→ Catching fish — 125 gems
→ Performing surgery — 250 gems
→ Cooking any recipe — 300 gems
→ Completing a Startopia mission — 500 gems
##  Trivia
  * Gemonade is based on lemonade, a sweetened lemon-flavored beverage. Its name is a portmanteau of "gem" and "lemonade", befitting its food buff, which provides the player with bonus gems.
  * An update on June 1, 2022 temporarily disabled Gemonade's ability to provide gems when the player cooks a recipe. This was done as a short-term solution to combat bot abuse.[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Gemonade#cite_note-1)
    * As part of Player Appreciation Week 2024 Day 4, the Gemonade's ability to provide gems when the player cooks a recipe was re-enabled.
