# Tunnel of Love

Venture through the wonderful tunnel of love!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data: Paint PreviewType — Foreground Block – Foreground — Aqua Blue Charcoal Green Purple Red Varnish Yellow
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **8 Hits** **6 Hits** – Restores after **4s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Paint Preview Aqua Blue Charcoal Green Purple Red Varnish Yellow
Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Candy Heart **(×10)** ,
Cotton Candy Clouds **(×1)** and
Valentine's Dust **(×10)**.
This recipe will yield **5** items.
The **Tunnel of Love** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2022 on February 11, 2022.
##  Function
When placed, the Tunnel of Love serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
