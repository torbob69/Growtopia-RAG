The **Sea Urchin** is a solid pain foreground block that was added as part of Block Party 2 on April 1, 2019.
##  Function
Being a type of pain block, a Sea Urchin knocks the player back and stings the player until the player stops all motion. A player will die after making contact with a pain block five times in quick succession (recovering from damage takes roughly 5 seconds). The _Fireproof_ mod will not allow the player to take more hits, unlike with lava pain blocks.
