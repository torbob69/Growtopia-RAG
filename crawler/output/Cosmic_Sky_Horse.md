# Cosmic Sky Horse

This winged beast hails from a distant star system. Those mighty wings let you move fast, but they're not strong enough to double jump (living in space has atrophied its muscles).

The **Cosmic Sky Horse** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of the Night Of The Chemicals update.
##  Function
You can move faster!

You are slow again.

When equipped, the **Cosmic Sky Horse** grants the _Speedy_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.
