The **Pineapple Disco Ball** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable animated foreground block which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2022 on June 10, 2022.
##  Function
<https://growtopiawiki.com/w/File:PineappleDisco2022.ogg>The Pineapple Disco Ball's music.
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Pineapple Disco Ball can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Pineapple Disco Ball will change its appearance, showing a 2-frame animation.
