The **Reality Visor** is an unsplicable face item which was added as part of Wonder Week 2024 Day 2 on October 15, 2024.
##  Function
Around the world, around the world!

Stranded!

When equipped, the **Reality Visor** grants the _Build Range + Punch Damage : Reality Visor_ mod, which allows the player to build one tile further and break blocks faster.
##  Trivia
  * Reality Visor's design is combination of 2 weather machines: Epoch Machine and Weather Machine - Crack in Reality. Epoch Machine's _Floating Islands_ image could be seen in the glass shards which are scattered behind the player similar to the shards in Weather Machine - Crack in Reality.
