The **Perfume** is an unsplicable consumable that was added as part of Valentine's Week 2013 on February 10, 2013.
##  Function
You really really smell.

The air clears.

When consumed on a player, it will grant them the _Stinky_ mod. When walking past other players, it will make the player's show the tongue emote similar when doing (:P).
