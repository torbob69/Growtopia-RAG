The **Mining Mech** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of the Spelunkorama Strikes Back! update on February 6, 2020.
##  Function
Heigh-ho, Heigh-ho, it's off to work I go!

Heigh-ho, Heigh-ho, it's home from I go!

When equipped, the **Mining Mech** grants the _Punch Damage: Mining Mech_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and break Cave Dirt, Dark Cave Background, Magic Infused Stone and Magic Infused Stone Background in a single hit.
Step 17 of the  Quest Of The Mech requires the player to deliver 5 of this item.
##  Trivia
  * The Mining Mech's equip and unequip messages are a reference to Heigh Ho, a song from Walt Disney's 1937 animated film Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs.
