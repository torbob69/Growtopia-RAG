# Bait (disambiguation)

This is a **disambiguation** page.
There are more than one subjects in the **Growtopia Wiki** using this name.
**Baits** are consumables that are required for fishing. It is used with a Fishing Rod.
##  Normal Baits
These baits can be obtained from a Tackle Box.
Bait: Method(s) to ObtainWiggly Worm: From Tackle Box or breaking Dirt.
Shiny Flashy Thing: From Tackle Box or Fishing.
Salmon Eggs: From Tackle Box or Fishing.
Fishing Fly: From Tackle Box or Fishing.
Mega-Pellet Bait: From Tackle Box or  Frozen Chunks.
Uranium Glowing Lure: From Tackle Box or Radioactive Bits.
Shrimp Lure: From Tackle Box or Fishing.
##  Special Baits
Bait: Method(s) to ObtainWhizmo Gizmo: From fishing with Shrimp Lure, Mega-Pellet Bait or Uranium Glowing Lure.
Catch-of-the-Day Bait: Sewing  Silk Bolt - Purple +  Silk Bolt - Yellow (Saddle Stitch) +  Silk Bolt - Green (Locking Stitch)
##  Event Baits
Bait: Method(s) to ObtainOtherworldly Bait: Sacrificing a Whizmo Gizmo or a Candy Cane Bait in The Maw of Growganoth.
Lesser Otherworldly Bait: Sacrificing a Fishing Fly, a Shrimp Lure, a Mega-Pellet Bait or a Uranium Glowing Lure in The Maw of Growganoth.
Minor Otherworldly Bait: Sacrificing a Wiggly Worm, a Shiny Flashy Thing, or Salmon Eggs in The Maw of Growganoth.
Candy Cane Bait: From a 20-29% or a 60-69% decorated Giving Tree.
