# Contact Lens Cleaning Solution

Tired of your current pupil color? Give them a wash with this hygienic cleaning solution! Note: You might need more than one application to finish the job!

Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Contact Lens Pack** for **15,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"Need a colorful new look? This pack includes 10 random Contact Lens colors (and may include Contact Lens Cleaning Solution, to return to your natural eye color)!"
Every purchase has a chance of giving **1** item.
The **Contact Lens Cleaning Solution** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2018 Day 6 alongside the Contact Lens Pack on August 11, 2018.
##  Function
When consumed, it removes one layer of contact lens coloring from the player's pupils.
