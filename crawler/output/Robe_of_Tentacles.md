The **Robe of Tentacles** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2022 Day 3 on August 17, 2022.
##  Function
Speedy Squid!

Out of Ink!

When equipped, the **Robe of Tentacles** grants the _Speedy: Robe of Tentacles_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.
