# Farmable

**Farmables** are items which have the possibility of dropping 8 or more blocks from trees.
There are many blocks that have been found to be **farmable**. These blocks include Dirt, Sugar Cane, Sheet Music: Sharp Bass, Venus Guytrap, and many more. However, over the years, some blocks have been _nerfed_ , which made the blocks less farmable than before.
To calculate the number of blocks earnt from farming a specific block, a farming calculator has been created by the forum user, ID:BEASTLY.

##  Trivia
  * An update in January 2014 reduced the maximum amount of gems obtainable from breaking blocks of rarity 5-30 by 25%, thus resulting in a 12.5% reduction in the total gems given.[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Farmable#cite_note-1) This change was mostly done to make low-rarity farmables such as Sugar Cane less powerful compared to high-rarity ones.
    * In order to compensate with this nerf, the developers also reduced the amount of hits needed to break certain blocks, such as the Purple Block and Coral.[[2]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Farmable#cite_note-2)
