# Treasure Hunt (update)

Growtopia Gazette
> January 2nd: January Updates!
> Hello Growtopians,
> The New Year has arrived, and awesome updates have joined it!
> Search icy ruins for long-lost treasures of the past! Ancient maps and mysterious fragments await - use their clues to find the treasures of the gods scattered throughout Growtopia!
> A new Winter Clash Branch is now available along with a final Legendary Item. Get your anomalizers ready to unlock all the fantastic Winter prizes!
> The New Item of the Month: Hovernator Drone - a new leash that follows you around, buzz-bombs your targets, and comes in different colors!
> Happy New Year, everyone!
> - The Growtopia Team
The **Treasure Hunt** update is a monthly update which was introduced on 2 January 2019. It introduced the concept of allowing players to go treasure hunting using treasure maps, as well as a new blast, the Treasure Blast.
