The **Super-Dragon Mask** is an unsplicable pet hand item which was added as part of the Dragon Invasion update.
##  Function
Super draconic wisdom!

Your super wisdom is no more.

When equipped, the **Super-Dragon Mask** grants the _XP Buff: Crime Fighting_ mod, which allows the player to gain an extra 200 XP when defeating a villain. It will also summon a super dragon pet which will follow the player around. Additionally, it will replace the player's punching punching with red lasers.
