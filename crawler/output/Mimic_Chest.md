The **Mimic Chest** is an unsplicable solid toggleable animated foreground block which was added as part of Halloween Week 2023 on October 19, 2023.
##  Function
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Mimic Chest can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Mimic Chest will change its appearance, and functions as a deadly block.
