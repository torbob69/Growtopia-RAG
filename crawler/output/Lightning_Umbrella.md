The **Lightning Umbrella** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Wonder Week Day 6 on October 16, 2021.
##  Function
Touch the sky!

Hit the ground!

When equipped, the **Lightning Umbrella** grants the _Double Jump + Slowfall_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
##  Trivia
  * The recipe was originally from crimefighting; however, the recipe changed to Mines due to the former activity causing server crashes.
