The **Sheet Music: Spanish Guitar Note** is an unsplicable sheet music background block which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2018 on May 4, 2018.
##  Function
Sheet Music: Spanish Guitar Note is a sheet music that plays a spanish guitar note, depending on its position vertically.
