**Arroz Con Pollo** is an unsplicable food consumable which was added as part of the Cooking update.
##  Function
10% chance of a gem when you break a block.

Your stomach's rumbling.

When consumed, this item grants the _Food: Breaking Gems_ mod which gives the player a 10% chance of making one extra gem drop every time they break a block. The buff lasts for 30 minutes.
##  Trivia
  * Arroz Con Pollo is based on _arroz con pollo_ (Spanish for rice with chicken), a traditional dish of Latin America.
