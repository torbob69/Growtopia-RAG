# Dragon Knight's Sabatons

Forged in a master blacksmith's furnace and stamped with runes of protection, a Dragon Knight's suit of armor is their last and best line of defense against the horrid flames and claws of their scaly foes! These shoes are but one link of many in that chain of defense, a perfect way to keep your feet safe! Keep these in mind the next time you want to try walking on coals at a luau, too - I mean, who would do that in bare feet??

The **Dragon Knight's Sabatons** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of the Dragon Invasion update on February 4, 2019.
##  Function
When equipped, the Dragon Knight's Sabatons serves a fully cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
