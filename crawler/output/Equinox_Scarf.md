The **Equinox Scarf** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of Equinox Weekend on March 25, 2022.
##  Function
Season d'etre!

Enlightened!

When equipped, the **Equinox Scarf** grants the _Double Jump: Equinox Scarf!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
The Equinox Scarf features special customizable functions. The player can set the season by wrenching oneself. The player can choose between Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter.
##  Trivia
  * **Equinox Scarf** is the item that requires the highest number of Grow Vouchers to be purchased from the Growtopia Store, with a price of  30.
