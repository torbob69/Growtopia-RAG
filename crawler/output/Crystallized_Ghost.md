# Crystallized Ghost

This ghost underwent some kind of reaction during a Spirit Storage explosion, and now it's been crystallized inside a solid block. Maybe one day scientists will extract its DNA and make a theme park of ghosts. Probably not, since ghosts don't have DNA.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.

When placed, the Crystallized Ghost serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
