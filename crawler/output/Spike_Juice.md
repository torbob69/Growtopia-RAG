# Spike Juice

By carefully drinking small amounts of Death Spikes, you can build up a tolerance! Well, what you can actually do is be immune to Lava and Death Spikes for 5 seconds.
Properties
This item cannot be dropped or traded.
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Hardness: **4 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **5s** of inactivity

**1h**
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Spike Juice** for **60 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"It's fresh squeezed, with little bits of spikes in it! Drinking this Untradable one-use potion will make you immune to Death Spikes and Lava for 5 seconds."
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Spike Juice** is an unsplicable and untradeable consumable which was added on July 2, 2014.
##  Function
You are briefly immune to spikes and lava

You feel vulnerable again.

When consumed, the Spike Juice grants the _Spikeproof_ mod, which makes the player immunity to hazards such as Death Spikes and Lava for 5 seconds.
### Silkworm Food     _Main article:Guide:Silkworm Care_
This consumable can be fed to a Silkworm to give it a yellow color, thus will produce 10  Silk Thread - Yellow on the next harvest. However, due to the Spike Juice's rarity, it isn't recommended to feed the item to a Silkworm.
##  Trivia
  * Before the Spike Juice was released, ex-developer Hamumu posted an announcement in the forums, warning players to stop using spikes to protect dropped items.
  * This item looks similar to the Antidote, except its liquid looks lighter than the Spike Juice.
