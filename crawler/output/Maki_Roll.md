# Maki Roll

Dude, you know that fish is raw, right?
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **4 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **5s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Rice **(×10)** ,
Fish Chunk **(×3)** and
Seaweed **(×1)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Maki Roll** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Gone Crabby! update on March 3, 2016.
##  Function
When consumed, the player will say "YUM!" and do the :D ⦅⦆ emote.
### Silkworm Food     _Main article:Guide:Silkworm Care_
This consumable can be fed to a Silkworm to give it a black color, thus allowing it to produce 1–10  Silk Thread - Black per harvest.
