The **China Poblano Dress** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2022 on May 5, 2022.
##  Function
When equipped, the China Poblano Dress serves a fully cosmetic purpose.
The  Cinco De Mayo Exclusive Mission - So you really are an artist! mission requires 5 of this item.
##  Trivia
  * The China Poblano Dress is based on the _china poblana_ , a traditional Mexican garb typically consisting of an embroidered white blouse and a red and green skirt with sequins.
