The **Heart Chocolate Block** is an unsplicable non-solid SFX background block which was added as part of Voucher Dayz 2026 on April 16, 2026.
##  Function
As an SFX background block, a **Heart Chocolate Block** will play a "crunching" sound effect and undergo a slight visual change when a player passes through. The block resets back to normal after 10 seconds, or all players have left the world.
