# Super Logo - MightyBOOSH

Emblazon this logo on your super costume to fight crime as MIGHTYBOOSH, with his trademark lightning shield!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item cannot be dropped or traded.
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.

Exclusive Item

This item was given to **MightyBOOSH** for their contributions as a voluntary moderator.
**Super Logo - MightyBOOSH** is an unobtainable super logo chest item which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2015 on March 14, 2015.
##  Function
When equipped, Super Logo - MightyBOOSH serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This super logo bears the letters 'M' and 'B', which is an acronym of the name MightyBOOSH.
  * Besides being the first super logo not to be earned from defeating a supervillain, this super logo is also the only super logo exclusive to a moderator.
