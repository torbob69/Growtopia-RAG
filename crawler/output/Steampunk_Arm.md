##  Function
Wind back the Clock!

Keep on ticking!

When equipped, the **Steampunk Arm** grants the _Punch Damage + Slowfall: Steampunk Arm_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
