The **Royal Master Magic** is an unsplicable artifact item which was added as September 2022's Item Of The Season on September 1, 2022.
##  Function
Got the magic in me!

Houdini!

When equipped, the **Royal Master Magic** grants the _Double Jump & Punch Range: Royal Master Magic_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and punch one tile further.
##  Trivia
