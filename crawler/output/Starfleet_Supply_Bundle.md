# Starfleet Supply Bundle
During Star Trek Prodigy

|
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Starfleet Supply Bundle** for **25,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"Help the crew recover their things with 10 Starfleet Cadet's Supply Box!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.

The **Starfleet Supply Bundle** is an item pack which was added as part of Star Trek Prodigy on June 27, 2022.
##  Items
The pack contains the following items:
  * Starfleet Cadet's Supply Box ×10

##  Trivia
  * On July 5, 2022, the amount of Starfleet Cadet's Supply Boxes obtainable from one pack got increased from 5 to 10.
