# Fireworks Feet

Shoot across the skies as you strap these fireworks on to your feet!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be transmuted.

Type: Feet – Clothes

Consume a(n)  Lucky Fortune Cookie **(x1)**.
May yield **1** item each.

The **Fireworks Feet** is an unsplicable feet item that was added as part of Lunar New Year 2022 on February 1, 2022.
##  Function
Lights Out!

Brighter than the Moon!

When equipped, the **Fireworks Feet** grants the _Speedy: Fireworks Feet_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.
