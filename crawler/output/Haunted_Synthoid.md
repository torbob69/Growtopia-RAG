The **Haunted Synthoid** is a leash that has human-like characteristics. It was added with the Night Of The Chemicals update on March 2, 2017.
##  Function
Your pet shoots lasers!

Your pet left you.

When equipped, a haunted synthoid pet appears and follows the player around. The Haunted Synthoid grants the _Laserbeast_ mod, which replaces the player's punch with lasers coming out of the pet.
