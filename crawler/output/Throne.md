The **Throne** is an unsplicable non-solid seat foreground block which was added as part of Summer Clash 2018 on June 1, 2018.
##  Function
When a player stands in front of a seat such as the Throne for a few seconds, the player will automatically be seen "sitting" on it. Additionally, the player will be seen reading a medieval scroll.
