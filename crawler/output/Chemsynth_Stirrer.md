**Chemsynth Stirrer** is a splicable consumable item which was added in the Night Of The Chemicals update, alongside the addition of Chemsynth Pack. One can also be obtained from purchasing said pack.
##  Function
When used on a Chemsynth Tank, it switches chemicals with each other in surrounding tanks without changing the selected tank.
