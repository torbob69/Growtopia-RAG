# Cotton Candy Cape

Roam around the world with this sweet cape!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be transmuted.
Data

Type: Back – Clothes
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: Full Collision
Hardness: **0 Hits** **0 Hits** – Restores after **0s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Well of Love

One may be received after filling a Well of Love with 200 Golden Booty Chests.
After receiving the Cotton Candy Cape, the Well of Love will turn into a Dry Well of Love.
The **Cotton Candy Cape** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2023 on February 10, 2023.
##  Function
To the skies!

To the ground!

When equipped, the **Cotton Candy Cape** grants the _Double Jump : Cotton Candy Cape_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
