The **Corrupted Blade** is an unsplicable hand item that was added as part of the Voucher Dayz update on September 26, 2024.
##  Function
The virus overtakes you.

The infection subsides.

When equipped, the **Corrupted Blade** grants the _Punch Damage: Corrupted Blade_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster.
##  Trivia

    * Corrupted Armor (shirt)

    * Corrupted Trousers (pants)

    * Corrupted Greaves (feet)
