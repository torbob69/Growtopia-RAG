Approach the  Legendary Wizard and complete the  Quest For Fire.
The **Dragon of Legend** is an unsplicable pet hand item which was added as part of the Legendary update on August 22, 2014.
##  Quest Steps
  1. Deliver 2,000 Lava.
  2. Kill 100 players in PvP via a Game Generator.
  3. Break 5,000 Blocks.
  4. Deliver 600 Dragon Gates.
  5. Plant seeds that add up to 50,000 rarity.
  6. Earn 50 Growtokens.
  7. Deliver 10 Dragon Hands.
  8. Earn 10,000 XP.
  9. Deliver 1,000 Dragon Tails.
  10. Deliver 100,000 Gems.
  11. Break 100,000 rarity worth of blocks.
  12. Complete 100 successful surgeries.
  13. Collect from 1,000 providers.
  14. Deliver 3 Fiesta Dragons.
  15. Collect 100,000 rarity worth of fruit from trees.
  16. Deliver an Ultra Trophy 3000 or any WOTD Trophy.
  17. Deliver 1 Neptune's Pendant.
  18. Deliver 1,000 Rocket Thrusters.
  19. Deliver 3 Devil Wings.
  20. Deliver the Legendary Orb.

When equipped, the **Dragon of Legend** grants the _Legendary!_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster, punch one tile further, and knock back other players further. It also summons a large purple dragon which follows the player around and shoots fire from it's mouth.
##  Trivia
  * Cukuzz was the first player to complete the Quest For Fire and receive the Dragon of Legend.
  * On the 10th of July on SummerFest 2025, Step 17 of Quest For Fire was changed from "Deliver 3 Ice Dragon Hands" to "Deliver 1 Neptune's Pendant".
