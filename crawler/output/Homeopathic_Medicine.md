The **Homeopathic Medicine** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Artsy April update.
##  Function
When consumed, the player will say "_Mmm, sugar!_ " There's also a slight chance that the player will turn into a zombie.
### Silkworm Food     _Main article:Guide:Silkworm Care_
This consumable can be fed to a Silkworm to give it a grey color, thus allowing it to produce 1–10  Silk Thread - Grey per harvest.
##  Trivia
  * The Homeopathic Medicine was added as a spoof of homeopathy, a fraudulent pseudoscientific treatment in which patients are provided with small doses of natural substances that would normally produce symptoms of disease in a healthy person.
    * _Growtopia_ 's creator Seth has publicly criticized homeopathy on at least two separate occasions, comparing it to in-game scams.[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Homeopathic_Medicine#cite_note-1)[[2]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Homeopathic_Medicine#cite_note-2)
