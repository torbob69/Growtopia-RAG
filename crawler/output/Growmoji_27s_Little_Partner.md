The **Growmoji's Little Partner** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as a part of Harvest Festival 2025 on September 11, 2025.
##  Function
Everybody wants a cat!

Meow, meow, meow, meow!

When equipped, the **Growmoji's Little Partner** grants the _Punch Range + Slow Fall : Growmoji's Little Partner_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
