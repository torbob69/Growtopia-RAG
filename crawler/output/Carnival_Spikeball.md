The **Carnival Spikeball** is an unsplicable solid pain foreground block.
##  Function
Being a type of pain block, a Carnival Spikeball knocks the player back and stings the player until the player stops all motion. A player will die after making contact with a pain block five times in quick succession (recovering from damage takes roughly 5 seconds). The _Fireproof_ mod will not allow the player to take more hits, unlike with lava pain blocks.
Carnival Spikeballs are primarily featured in the Brutal Bounce game, where players are given the ability to spawn in Carnival Spikeballs to kill opponents by punching empty spots. These blocks are also featured in the Spiky Survivor game.
