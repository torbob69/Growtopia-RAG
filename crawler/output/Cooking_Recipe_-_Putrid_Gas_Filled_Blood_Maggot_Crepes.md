# Cooking Recipe - Putrid Gas Filled Blood Maggot Crepes

To cook this recipe you will need the following:Ingredients: - Milk - Rotten Egg - Burnt Slime - Putrid GasSeasoning: - A pinch of SaltProcess:Add Milk and Rotten Egg to an oven on Low heat. While the Milk is still cold, add Burnt Slime. Add the seasonings and wait until perfectly cooked all the way through. Then add Putrid Gas and remove from the oven straight away.Timings may vary so you will need to experiment.

This cooking recipe's description guides the player on how to cook a Putrid Gas Filled Blood Maggot Crepes. It is permanent and will not be consumed when the player cooks the item.
