The **Blood Truffle Biscuits with Sweet Radish Pie** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Star Trek Prodigy on June 27, 2022.
##  Function
Punch like a mechanic!!

Out of energy!

When consumed, the Blood Truffle Biscuits with Sweet Radish Pie grants the _Blood Truffle Biscuits with Sweet Radish Pie: Punch Damage_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. This mod lasts for 10 minutes.
