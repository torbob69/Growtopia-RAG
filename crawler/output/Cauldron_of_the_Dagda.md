The **Cauldron of the Dagda** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2022 on March 11, 2022.
##  Function
Spirits released!

Spirits locked away!

When equipped, the **Cauldron of the Dagda** grants the _Double-Jump & Speedy : Cauldron of the Dagda_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and move faster on land. This effect may stack with other items that grant a similar effect.
##  Trivia
  * Cauldron of the Dagda is a reference to a god in Irish mythology with the same name, who is associated with fertility, agriculture, strength, and wisdom.
