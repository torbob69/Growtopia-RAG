# Unlucky Pinata Hat

This hat is still festive and suitable for formal occasions, but all the luck has been sucked out of it.

Participate in successfully smashing an  Ultra Pinata with a  Lucky Pinata Hat equipped during the **Pinata Bash!** event.
May give **1** item each time.
The **Unlucky Pinata Hat** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2015 on May 1, 2015.
##  Function
When equipped, the Unlucky Pinata Hat serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
