# Pet Pidgeon

This is the page for **Pet Pidgeon**.
You may be looking for **Pigeon** instead.
Known for the exceptional navigation capabilities, this pet will guide you down the right path!

Won as prize in **any game** (except for Hall of Mirrors and Waste a Ticket) in the Carnival.
The **Pet Pidgeon** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2022 Day 5 on August 19, 2022.
##  Funtion
When equipped, a pidgeon pet will spawn an follow the player around. The Pet Pidgeon serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
