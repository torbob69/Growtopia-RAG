# Geiger Day

**Geiger Day** is a monthly event which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2017 on May 5, 2017. During this day, Geiger Counters take 15 minutes to charge. Moreover, the _Irradiated_ mod will also last for 15 minutes only. Additionally, the signal rings subtly guide players toward the direction of the radioactive source in the world.
##  Rewards
A Geiger Day may affect certain Geiger Counter prizes differently. The following items are added to the prize pool:
  * Hazmat Helmet
  * Hazmat Suit
  * Hazmat Pants
  * Hazmat Boots
  * Anime Female Hair
  * Anime Male Hair
  * Digital Sign

Meanwhile, the following items replace Black Stuff, Blue Stuff, Green Stuff, Orange Stuff, Purple Stuff, Red Stuff, and White Stuff:
  * Blue Geiger V-Neck
  * Orange Geiger V-Neck
  * Purple Geiger V-Neck
  * Green Geiger V-Neck

##  Supervillain
During a Geiger Day, a unique supervillain may also spawn sometimes in place of the usual rewards after one uses a Geiger Counter. Normally, it would say, _(someone)_ found a _(name of item)_. Instead, it will say, _(someone)_ was attacked by H.R. Geiger. The villain then appears as a Crime In Progress, giving the player an option to fight it in card battles.
Defeating H.R. Geiger may give one of the following exclusive prizes:
  * Xenoid Mask
  * Xenoid Suit
  * Xenoid Leggings
  * Xenoid Shoes
  * Xenoid Claws
  * Villain Portrait - H.R. Geiger
  * Superpower - Alien Egg

Note that H.R.Geiger — much like the other criminals — will only stay for a single day.
