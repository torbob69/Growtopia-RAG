The **Rabbid Buddy** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of UbiWeek 2023 Day 4 on February 26, 2023.
##  Function
Charge!!!

Flushed away!

When equipped, the **Rabbid Buddy** grants the _Double Jump & Punch Range: Rabbid Buddy_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air, punch one tile further, and switch between its wearable form and its morph form by wrenching oneself and clicking 'Transform'.
