# Blocks/Pushing Blocks

< Blocks
**Pushing Blocks** are non-solid foreground blocks which pushes players away according to the direction of the block.
**Pushing Blocks**

Item: Description
Clover Fan: A super powerful clover fan! Probably best not to use in small spaces as that was quite the kerfuffle!
Conveyor Block: Keep moving forward... or back, your choice really!
Megawatt Bass Speaker: Push partygoers around with the power of deep bass sound! The speaker focuses sound waves in the direction you place it, so pick its placement for maximum effect!
Space Conveyor Block: Don't run on a Space Conveyor. Stay safe, slow down!
Starship Turbo Fan: Blast solar winds wherever you want with this vortex-funneling turbo fan! Warning: Do not use at parties. Well, unless you want your parties to be blown away by turbo winds.
Summer Breeze: These gentle breezes smell like freshly mown grass. They also blow with great ferocity!
Winter Wind: Place this anywhere you want the wind to come ripping through and push players around! The wind blows in the direction that you would blow air when placing it, so you can place it either way.
