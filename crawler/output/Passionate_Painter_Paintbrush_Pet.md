The **Passionate Painter Paintbrush Pet** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as a part of Harvest Festival 2025 on September 11, 2025.
##  Function
When equipped, the **Passionate Painter Paintbrush Pet** serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
