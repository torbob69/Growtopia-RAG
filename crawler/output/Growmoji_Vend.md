# Growmoji Vend

Put your chat up for sale with a vending machine! Just consume this to add it to your available growmojis!
Properties
A tree of this type can bear surprising fruit!
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **5 Hits** **4 Hits** – Restores after **8s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Growmojis** for **15 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"Express yourself! This mysterious box contains one of five fun growmojis you can use to spice up your chat! Which will you get?"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The Growmoji Vend is a Growmoji that was released alongside the March Update. When consumed, the (vend) Growmoji becomes available for use. It can be obtained from the Growtoken Store for 15 Growtokens (chance to obtain).
