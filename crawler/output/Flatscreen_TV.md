The **Flatscreen TV** is a splicable non-solid toggleable animated foreground block which was added on Player Appreciation Week 2014 Day 1 on August 8, 2014.
##  Function
<https://growtopiawiki.com/w/File:Battle_theme_loop.ogg>The Flatscreen TV's music.
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Flatscreen TV can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Flatscreen TV will change its appearance, showing a 2-frame animation, and play a piece of music.
