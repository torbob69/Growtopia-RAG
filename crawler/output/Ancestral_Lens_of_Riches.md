## Function
Effortless Gems...

Aw, no more bonus gems.

When equipped, the **Ancestral Lens of Riches** grants the _Extra Wealth..._ mod, which allows the player to get a 5% chance to receive extra gems when breaking blocks.

##  Trivia
  * The item used to be able to make the Toxic Waste Barrel drop up to 500 gems, before it was patched shortly after the update came out.
  * Wearing the Monocle with an upgraded Ancestral Lens of Riches will give the Monocle special effects, depending on the upgrade level of the item.
  * Upgrading the Ancestral Lens of Riches to Level 5 will summon a red gem to rotate around the player.
  * Once Ancestral Lens Of Riches is upgraded, it will become an untradeable item.
  * Wearing the Ancestral Lens of Riches will cause blocks that normally don't drop gems, such as the Bountiful blocks, to drop gems upon breaking them.
