# Gift of the Unicorn

The unicorn has gifted you great wisdom! The wisdom is thus: If you stick this wisdom, 2 Sword Pommels, and 2 Sword Blades, into a Laboratory and SCIENCE them... well, let's just say it'll be super cool.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item has no use... by itself.
