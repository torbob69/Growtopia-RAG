The **Riding Phoenix** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of SummerFest 2016 on June 30, 2016.
##  Function
You can move faster!

You are slow again.

When equipped, the **Riding Phoenix** grants the _Speedy_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and emit light in dark areas.
