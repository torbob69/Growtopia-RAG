_Requires a hand-drilled Ice Crust Block with water on top._
The **Large Frozen Chunk** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Gone Fishin'! update on January 14, 2018.
##  Function
Consuming 10 Large Frozen Chunks while wearing a Winter Scarf, Buckskin Hood, Buckskin Jacket and Buckskin Pants will give a Mega-Pellet Bait.
