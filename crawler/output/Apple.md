The **Apple** is a splicable consumable which has been added since Growtopia's launch.
##  Function
Doctors won't come near you!

You no longer repel doctors.

When consumed, it gives the player the _Doctor Repulsion_ mod, which prevents players from performing surgery on the player for 24 hours.

##  Trivia
  * On the 25th of July, an update was implemented allowing players to disable their Legendary Title for 24 hours by consuming an Apple. The use of Apples on other players was also disallowed, to prevent players purposely removing the Legendary Title off other players.
  * If a player tries to use an Apple on another player, they will say, "_No sharing apples!_ "
  * The Apple's effect is based on the popular belief that "an apple a day keeps the doctor away". In reality, eating an apple a day does not keep the doctors away but it gives health benefits.
  * Consuming Apple while having **_Dr.title_** will cause the title to disappear for the next 24 hours.
