The **Cliff, The Cerebrus** is an unsplicable solid trampoline foreground block which was added as part of Halloween Week 2022 on October 22, 2022.
##  Function
Jumping on a trampoline block such as a Cliff, The Cerberus will cause the player to bounce, and by holding the jump button, the player can jump even higher. The _High Jump_ mod also boosts one's jump height on a trampoline block.
##  Trivia
  * Cliff, The Cerberus' appearance is a reference to an American children's book series named Clifford the Big Red Dog.
