##  Function
Folded to a sharp point!

Unfolded!

When equipped, the **Paper Wasp Pet** grants the _Punch Range : Paper Wasp Pet_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further. It will also summon a Paper Wasp Pet that follows the player around. Additionally, it will replace the player's punching animation with throwing sharp paper-like sprites. A random origami appears once the block has been broken.
