This machine will send your entire world hurtling through space at warp speed. This sensitive technology will not function while damaged.

Purchase the **Weather Machine - Warp Speed** for **10,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"This Weather Machine will launch your world through space at relativistic speeds, which will cause you to age more slowly, as well as see stars flying by rapidly in the background. It's a perma-item, is never lost when destroyed."
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Weather Machine - Warp Speed** is an unsplicable non-solid weather machine foreground block which was added on Player Appreciation Week 2014 Day 1 on August 8, 2014.
##  Function
As a weather machine, this item can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, it will change its appearance, showing a 2-frame animation, and transform the world's background weather into a warp speed scenery.
