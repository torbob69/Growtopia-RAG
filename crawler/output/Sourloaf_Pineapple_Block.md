The **Sourloaf Pineapple Block** is an unsplicable solid foreground block which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2019 on June 7, 2019.
##  Function
When placed, the Sourloaf Pineapple Block serves a fully aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The Sourloaf Pineapple Block is one of the three pineapple blocks added in Super Pineapple Party 2019, alongside the Dangerous Pineapple Block and Rotten Pineapple Block.
    * Unlike the other two, the Dangerous Pineapple Block acts as a pain block, hurting players upon contact.
