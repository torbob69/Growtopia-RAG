##  Function
Fuelled for lift off!

Under maintenance!

When equipped, the **Armored WinterBot - Back** grants the _Double-Jump: WinterBot!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and transform into the Armored WinterBot when the entire Armored WinterBot set is equipped.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Armored WinterBot set, which consists of the following items:

    * Armored WinterBot - Back (back)

    * Armored WinterBot - Mail (shirt)

    * Armored WinterBot - Greaves (pants)

    * Armored WinterBot - Feet (feet)

    * Armored WinterBot - Fists (hand)
