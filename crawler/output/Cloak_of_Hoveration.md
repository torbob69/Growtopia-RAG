The **Cloak of Hoveration** is an unsplicable back item which was added on August 1, 2018 as August 2018's Item Of The Month.
##  Function
This cloak is amazing!

Back in the closet you go!

When equipped, the **Cloak of Hoveration** grants the _Hoveration!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
When the player moves, the cloak will appear to be left behind and start chasing after them. When the player stops, the cloak will glow behind the player. The cloak leaves behind some magical particles while hovering.
##  Trivia
  * This item's name and appearance is based on the Cloak of Levitation, a fictional item appearing in comic books published by Marvel Comics and live-action movies made by Marvel Studio.
