The **Fancy Birdbath** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block which was added as part of Easter Week 2021 on April 1, 2021.
##  Function
Being a toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block, a Fancy Birdbath can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, the Fancy Birdbath will change its appearance, showing a 14-framed animation of two birds drinking from the birdbath.
##  Trivia
  * A bird bath (or birdbath) is a type of artificial water-filled basin in which birds may drink, bathe, and cool themselves. It is typically installed in order to attract many different species of birds to visit gardens during spring or summer.
