This is a 1 lb. Stonefish. This is one of the most poisonous fish in the world! It's also made of rock, which is weird.

### Perfect Stonefish
A perfect Stonefish weighs 160lb. Thus, it can only be caught by using Fishing Flies with a Cursed Fishing Rod or by using a Whizmo Gizmo. Catching a perfect Stonefish for the first time will complete the epic quest "_Catch a perfect Stonefish._ "
##  Trivia
  * The Stonefish is based on an animal of the same name.
  * Catching a perfect Stonefish for the first time will complete an Epic Quest.
  * When catching a perfect Stonefish, there is a chance for the perfect Stonefish to be replaced with a Stone Mask instead. Alternatively, when fishing in a world with a Weather Machine - Jungle activated or a Jungle Blasted world, a Mossy Mask may also replace the caught perfect Stonefish.
