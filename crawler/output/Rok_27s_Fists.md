# Rok's Fists

Equip yourself with the fists of a Brikarian giant made out of a rocky exterior! Wrench yourself and transform into the gentle and compassionate Rok. She believes in equality for all and dreams of becoming a Science Officer for the Federation!

The **Rok's Fists** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Star Trek Prodigy on June 27, 2022.
##  Function
Growing Up!

Growing Down!

When equipped, the **Rok's Fists** grants the _Grow Effect & Punch Damage: Rok's Fists_ mod, which allows the player to expand in size, break blocks faster, and switch between its wearable form and its morph form by wrenching oneself and clicking 'Transform'.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Rok set, which consists of the following items:

    * Rok's Hair (Hair)

    * Rok's Shirt (Shirt)

    * Rok's Overalls (Pants)

    * Rok's Shoes (Feet)

    * Rok's Fists (Hand)
