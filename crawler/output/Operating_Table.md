# Operating Table

Lay on this Operating Table and one of our doctors will see to you as soon as possible. The Reception Desk will send Surg-E Patients to these Operating Tables on a regular basis, failure to cure the Surg-E Patient in time will result in a failed surgery and the hospital's rating being reduced as a result.

The **Operating Table** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as a part of Player Appreciation Week 2024 Day 1 on August 26, 2024.
##  Function
Being a bed, executing the /sleep command in chat while standing on the same tile as a Hospital Bed will cause the player to sleep in it.
A Hospital Bed is different from other beds in the sense that a player can perform a surgery on another player who is standing on the same tile as this block.
If the Operating Table is in a hospital, a  Surg-bot will spawn on the Operating Table daily that functions similarly to a Surg-E. Players are required to cure the spawned Surg-bot in the patient duration time interval, which is shown in the "_Level up Hospital_ " menu when the player wrenchs the Reception Desk. Failure to cure the Surg-bot within the patient duration is counted as an unsuccessful surgery, increasing failed surgery count and decreasing the hospital's ratings. The patient's duration increases as the hospital level increases.
The number of Operating Tables to be placed in every hospital world is limited. This limitation decreases as the player levels the hospital up.
