The **Bed Bugs** is an unsplicable background block which was added as part of the Gross-Out update on May 1, 2019.
##  Function
When placed, the Bed Bugs serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is based on the real-life bed bugs, a type of insect from the genus _Cimex_ that feed on blood, usually at night. They typically dwell in beds, hence their name.
  * The Bed Bugs was made unobtainable as part of the Mutant Kitchen (update), being replaced by the Neon Green Mutation Layer.
