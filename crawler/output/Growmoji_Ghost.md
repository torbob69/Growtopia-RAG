# Growmoji Ghost

Celebrate halloween with a ghost in your chat as well as your worlds! Just consume this to add it to your available growmojis!
Growmoji Ghost (Rarity: 100)
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **5 Hits** **4 Hits** – Restores after **8s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **0 - 26**
**11d 14h 36m 40s**
Rewards of Sacrifice

Sacrifice **any item with a rarity of 100 or above** to Growganoth.
The **Growmoji Ghost** is an unsplicable growmoji consumable that was added as part of Halloween Week 2018 on October 26, 2018.
##  Function
When consumed, the player will be able to use the (ghost) Growmoji.
