The **Unicorn Horn** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of the Unicorn Madness update on May 30, 2014. It is a rare drop from harvesting an Awkward Friendly Unicorn.
##  Function
When equipped, the Unicorn Horn serves a fully cosmetic purpose.
