The **Composer's Cookie** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Role Up! update on September 17, 2018.
##  Function
View musical notes in the world.

Your stomach's rumbling to the beat.

When consumed, the Composer's Cookie grants the _Food: Flavorsome Music_ mod, which allows the player to see musical notes in the player's build range for 30 minutes.
