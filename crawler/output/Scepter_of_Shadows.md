The **Scepter of Shadows** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Black Friday 2022 on November 25, 2022.
##  Function
Out of the Shadows!

Back in the Shadows!

When equipped, the **Scepter of Shadows** grants the _Punch Damage: Scepter of Shadows_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster.
