The **Pants of Spring** is an unsplicable pants item that was added as part of Equinox Weekend on March 25, 2022.
##  Function
When equipped, the Pants of Spring serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Equinox Spring set, which consists of the following items:

    * Crown of Spring (hat)

    * Shirt of Spring (shirt)

    * Pants of Spring (pants)

    * Boots of Spring (shoes)
