The **Tarantula Legs** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2019.
##  Function
Creeping & crawling!

Back to Non-glowing human legs!

When equipped, the **Tarantula Legs** grants the _Speedy & Wall Climb: Tarantula!_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and cling onto Climbing Walls by pressing the jump button.
The item also allows the player to cling onto Climbing Walls by holding their jump. Other items which serve the same purpose include:

  * Claws of Barko

  * Climbing Boots

  * Climbing Picks

  * Ezio's Boots

  * Mountain Boots

  * S.P.I.D.E.R. Legs

  * Will of the Wild (earth version)

The  The Seven Seas : The Crust of the Crab! mission requires 10 of this item.
