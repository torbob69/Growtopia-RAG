Bunny Eggs may explode randomly when their sizes are above 100%.
The **Cuddly Bunny** is a splicable consumable which was added as part of Easter Week 2013 on March 30, 2013.
##  Function
When consumed on any player, the player will say "_Awwww!_ " and perform the /love command.
When harvested, a Cuddly Bunny Tree has a slight chance to drop a Psychotic Bunny or Psychotic Bunny Mask.
