The **Penguin Leash** is a splicable pet hand item which was added as part of the Block Party update on June 12, 2015.
##  Function
Your little friend blasts ice beams.

Your friend has flown the coop.

When equipped, a penguin pet appears and follows the player around. Moreover, the item also gives the player the _Icy Pet_ mod. The Penguin Leash replaces the player's punch effect with beams of ice fired by the penguin.
