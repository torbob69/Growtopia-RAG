Now you can play Wolfworld: The Home Game with your own handy wooden totem!

Complete WOLFWORLD

May yield **3** items each.
The **Wolf Totem** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2015 Day 2.
##  Function
When placed, the Wolf Totem serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * As part of of the Block Party 2 update, the quantity of Wolf Totems obtained from WOLFWORLD was increased from 1 to 3.
  * As part of the Quality of Life Changes update on May 20, 2022, the **Wolf Totem** was added for sale in the Sales-Man under Wolfworld items for 150 Wolf Tickets. This was later removed from the Sales-Man 30 minutes later because there were multiple complaints of the price being inconsistent.
