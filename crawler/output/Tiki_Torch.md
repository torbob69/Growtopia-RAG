The **Tiki Torch** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable animated foreground block which was added as part of SummerFest 2015 on July 3, 2015.
##  Function
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Tiki Torch can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Tiki Torch will change its appearance, showing a 2-framed animation of itself providing light to its surrounding. As a light source, a Tiki Torch emits light in dark areas.
