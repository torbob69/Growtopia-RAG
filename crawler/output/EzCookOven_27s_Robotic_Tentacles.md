Robotic allies to launch you to and fro! These high-tech tentacles were first developed to handle hazardous GrowTech waste, but their engineers discovered it was WAY more fun to use them to jump around instead (they can also play a mean game of chess, but most owners are too busy jumping to try)! **EzCookOven's Robotic Tentacles** is an unsplicable back item which was added on July 1, 2019. It was obtained by defeating Growlactus.
##  Function
The tenacity of tentacle!

Tentacles powered down!

When equipped, the **EzCookOven's Robotic Tentacles** grants the _Double Jump: Robotic_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
##  Trivia
  * EzCookOven's Robotic Tentacles is based on Doc Ock, a fictional character appearing in comic books published by Marvel Comics, whom is usually depicted with four strong and durable appendages resembling an octopus's tentacles, which extend from the back of his body.
  * This item is sponsored by EzCookOven for completing all the Epic Quests.
