The **Growmoji Halo** is an unsplicable Growmoji consumable which was released as part of Winter Clash. One can be obtained from the Winter Awards.
##  Function
The growmoji is a Growtopian's face with Golden Halo on its head. Once consumed, it allows the player to use the (halo) growmoji.
