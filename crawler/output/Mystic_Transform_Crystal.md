# Mystic Transform Crystal

Use this magical crystal on yourself to change your form and embody the Mystic Snow Fox!
Properties
This item cannot be dropped or traded.
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **2 Hits** **2 Hits** – Restores after **2s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
The **Mystic Transform Crystal** is an untradeable consumable that was added as part of December 3, 2020. It comes with the Mystic Snow Fox.
##  Function
When consumed, it will transform the Mystic Snow Fox into a mystic fox or back into a growtopian. However, despite being a consumable, consuming it will not disappear from the player's inventory.
