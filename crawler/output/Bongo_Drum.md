The **Bongo Drum** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2020 on May 1, 2020.
##  Function
<https://growtopiawiki.com/w/File:Conga_bongos.ogg>Bongo Drum's music
When the player enters the command /dance or while they are standing still, a conga music plays while the player performs a special animation of themself slamming their hand on the Bongo Drum.
##  Trivia
  * In the game files, the Bongo Drum's music is named conga_bongos.
