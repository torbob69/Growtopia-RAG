# Magical Rainbow Fishing Rod

Equip this and then use bait on water next to you to go fishing! All you have to do is wait for a bite, and then punch the fish before it escapes! If you miss, your bait is wasted, so punch fast. You will suffer mischievous effects when catching fish with this rod! Only found in Blarney 6 and 8.

The **Magical Rainbow Fishing Rod** is an unsplicable hand item that was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2018.
##  Function
When equipped, the player can go fishing. The effects of this rod include double items, death, and finding St. Patrick's Week items. It is similar to the Cursed Fishing Rod.
  * "Here, have a hug!"
  * "You get a tingly happy feeling..."
  * "A fish literally jumps through the rainbow in to your hands!"
  * "Your eyes go all shiny and bright..."
  * Overwhelming happiness floods your system! Mmm, endorphins!
    * The player gets an item.

  * "You almost cry with joy after you get your bait back!"
    * The player gets their bait back.

  * "Oh, what the-! *burrrp!* Aah! *burrrrp!* Help...! *BURP!*
    * The player burps but does not get any item.

  * "Aaah! A rabid Leprechaun! "Too late to hide, tee-hee-hee!"
    * The player gets kicked.

  * "You magically caught more fishy items! Yay for magic!"
    * The player gets **2** items.

  * "You almost cry with joy after you find an even better bait!"
    * The player gets **2** items, one being a bait 1-3 tiers superior to the one currently in use. It does not work for Shrimp and other superior baits, the same bait is returned.

  * "Blarney magic strikes again!"
