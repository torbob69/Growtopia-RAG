# Backpack Upgrade
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Backpack Upgrade** for **100 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"Sewing an extra pocket onto your backpack will allow you to store 10 additional item types. How else are you going to fit all these toilets and doors?"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Backpack Upgrade** is a store item that adds 10 backpack slots to the player. A player begins with 16 backpack slots, including the slots occupied by the Fist and the Wrench. The initial price is 100 Gems, and increases exponentially with every purchase. A player can have a maximum of 396 backpack slots.
The player earns three different achievements upon purchasing upgrades for their backpack capacity: the "Embiggened" achievement is earned on the first purchase, the "Space Commander" achievement on the seventh, and the "Master of Space and Time" achievement on the eighteenth.
Prior to 1 March 2020, players could have a maximum of 196 backpack slots, however, along with the introduction of the Road to Glory Pack, 200 extra backpack slots were added.
