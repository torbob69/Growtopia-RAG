The **Fountain** is a splicable non-solid toggleable foreground block which was added on 20 August 2015, the seventh day of Player Appreciation Week 2015.
##  Function
Being a toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block, a Fountain can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Fountain will change its appearance, showing a 4-framed animation of water flowing out of the fountain.
