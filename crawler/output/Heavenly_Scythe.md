The **Heavenly Scythe** is a hand item which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2015 on February 13, 2015.
##  Function
You wield the fury of the heavens.

Back to punching.

When equipped, the **Heavenly Scythe** grants the _Heavenly_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and knock back other players further. It also throws blue translucent versions of the scythe itself.
Step 7 of the  Quest For The Blade requires the player to deliver 3 of this item.
