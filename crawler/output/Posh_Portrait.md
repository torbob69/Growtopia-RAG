The **Posh Portrait** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2021 Day 6 on August 28, 2021.
##  Function
To use, a player must have 2 of each Paint Buckets in their inventory. Upon wrenching the Posh Portrait, the player gets to choose any player on the screen whose face will be painted on the Posh Portrait. Afterwards, the former can edit the latter's emotion and write a title for others to view. Unlike with the Painting Easel, the player isn't required to have a Paintbrush equipped in order to use a Posh Portrait.
Using 4 Paint Bucket - Varnish allows the player to remove the face painted on the Posh Portrait.
