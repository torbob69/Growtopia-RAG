The **Yin Yang Pendant** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2020 on September 24, 2020.
##  Function
Yin Yang and you don't stop a rockin'

That's plenty of yin'ing and yang'ing for now

When equipped, the **Yin Yang Pendant** grants the _Yin Yang Pendant : Speedy_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land. Fog also billows from the user's feet while small _taijitu_ particles (symbols representing the yin and yang) fade in and disappear.
The  The Seven Seas : The Heart of the Ocean! mission requires 1 of this item.
