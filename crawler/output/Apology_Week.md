# Apology Week

Growtopia Gazette
> July 14th: Apology Week!
> In light of the recent rollback incident, we are doing an **Apology Week** to help everyone catch up on the progress lost. Starting today you will be able to get unique benefits for 7 days - July 14th to 20th!
> Lucky, Skill Spice, and Beer-Battered Fish & Chips Mods are active on all accounts, 8% tree growth time reduction, 25% increased XP on all activities, 10% discount on all store items, 25% gem bonus on IAPs, Rollback Plaque VII, Game events will be activated on each day
> and free Growtoken when you logon within Apology Week!
The **Apology Week** is a special event that has only been held once, on July 14, 2020, days after Rollback VII due to two consecutive rollbacks that had been performed over a short period of time. It gave more buffs than an Apology Weekend.
The enhanced buffs given were:
  * Lucky, Skill Spice, and Beer-Battered Fish And Chips mods active on all accounts
  * 8% reduction in tree growth time
  * 25% increased XP on all activities
  * 10% discount on all store items
  * 25% gem bonus on IAPs
  * Rollback Plaque VII can be spliced over the whole week
  * Up to 15 Growtokens will be given if the player logs in on all seven days of the Apology Week or 5 Growtokens if they log in at least one day over the Apology Week, given the following conditions:
    * Account must be minimum level 10
    * Account must have AAP active
    * Account must be created before July 13, 2020
  * Game events were activated on each day:
    * July 14 - Day 1: Ghost Day
    * July 15 - Day 2: Surgery Day
    * July 16 - Day 3: Locke The Traveling Salesman
    * July 17 - Day 4: Mutant Kitchen
    * July 18 - Day 5: Mutant Kitchen
    * July 19 - Day 6: Mutant Kitchen
    * July 20 - Day 7: Geiger Day
