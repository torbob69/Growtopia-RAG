# Cherry Blossom Pinwheel

Buddy got them cherry blossom wheels, petals with the blur! With the blur! Got the whole world looking at nature!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.

Special Event

Found in the _Valentine's Card_ special event.
One may be rewarded to a player upon having a secret admirer.
The **Cherry Blossom Pinwheel** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2023 on February 10, 2023.
##  Function
When placed, the Cherry Blossom Pinwheel serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The Cherry Blossom Pinwheel's description is a reference to the lyrics of Low, the in 2007 released debut single by American rapper and singer Flo Rida featuring fellow American rapper and singer T-Pain.
