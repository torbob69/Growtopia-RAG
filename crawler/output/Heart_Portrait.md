The **Heart Portrait** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2026 on February 12, 2026.
##  Function
To use, a player must have 2 of each Paint Buckets in their inventory. Upon wrenching the Heart Portrait, the player gets to choose any player on the screen whose face will be painted on the Heart Portrait. Afterwards, the former can edit the latter's emotion and write a title for others to view. Unlike with the Painting Easel, the player isn't required to have a Paintbrush equipped in order to use a Portrait.
Using 4 Paint Bucket - Varnish allows the player to remove the face painted on the Heart Portrait.
