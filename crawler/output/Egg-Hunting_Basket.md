# Egg-Hunting Basket

With this basket, you can track down Easter Eggs hidden all around Growtopia! Once your basket is full, it somehow takes you half an hour to get the egg out of it. Clumsy. Easter Eggs can only be found during Easter Week.

The **Egg-Hunting Basket** is a hand item which was added as part of Easter Week 2015.
##  Function
When equipped, the **Egg-Hunting Basket** allows the player to locate hidden eggs during Easter Week only. This item is used in a rather similar manner to the Geiger Counter, emitting color-coded signals based on how close the player is to their reward.
Once the player has found an egg, however, it will be stuck in the basket for 30 minutes (the timer won't decrease while the player is in the EXIT menu or offline), becoming a Full Egg-Hunting Basket. After the 30-minute period has passed, the player will immediately obtain a random egg item. Should the player's inventory be full, the item will be dropped on the ground.
