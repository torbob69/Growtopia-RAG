# Mutated Seed

This strangely mutated seed won't grow much on its own... but maybe you can splice it with something else?
Mutated Seed (Rarity: 10)
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Seed
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **20 Hits** **15 Hits** – Restores after **2s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **0 - 2**
**21m 40s**
Consumption

Consume a(n)  Mutated Cell **(x200)**.
May yield **1** item each.

The Mutated Seed was introduced during the Night of the Sewing Dead.
##  Function
The item can be spliced with other seeds to make Night of the Sewing Dead related items such as the Skull Launcher, Skeletal Throne, Ruined Bricks, or Monster Truck.
