The **Harvest Shawl** is an unsplicable back item that was added as part of Harvest Festival 2020 on September 24, 2020.
##  Function
Leaf'ing you behind!

This tree has no leaves!

When equipped, the **Harvest Shawl** grants the _Harvest Shawl : Double Jump!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air. While jumping, the cape disappears and a trail of leaves follows the player. The cape blinks as the player jumps or lands. When the player is standing still, the cape moves from left to right.
