# Claws of Barko

Honed in lightless caves where only the most devious rabbits dare to hop, these claws are used for far deadlier things than digging cute bunny burrows!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.

May give **1** item each time.
The **Claws of Barko** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Easter Week 2019 on April 19, 2019.
##  Function
Claw your way up!

Nails... too smooth!

When equipped, the **Claws of Barko** grants the _Climbing: Barko_ mod, which allows the player to cling onto Climbing Walls by pressing the jump button.
