# Mentor Ticket - Level 20

One of your apprentices has progressed nicely, and here's the proof! Keep helping them out for bigger and better rewards!
Properties
This item cannot be dropped or traded.
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **3 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **4s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Mentorship

Obtained from having an apprentice reach level 20.
The **Mentor Ticket - Level 20** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Mentors & Apprentices update on March 11, 2019.
##  Function
When consumed, the player will receive a  Growtoken.
