# Easter Basket
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Easter Basket** for **6,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"Spring is in the air, celebrate with Magic Eggs (you can now get between 20 - 40 Magic Eggs), a Pastel Suit with pants, a Wicker Basket, a Bunny disguise (mask, ears, and tail), and Hippie Sandals - you may also get an Easter egg, including the RARE Easter Egg - Rainbow! Note: You get an Easter Crate, place it in your world and break it to get Easter treats!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Easter Basket** is a store pack which is only available during Easter Week. When purchased, it gives an Easter Crate which contains:
  * Magic Eggs ×20-40
  * Pastel Suit
  * Pastel Pants
  * Wicker Basket
  * Bunny Mask
  * Bunny Ears
  * Bunny Tail
  * Hippie Sandals
  * Random Easter Egg ( Easter Egg - Striped,  Easter Egg - Jagged,  Easter Egg - Wavey,  Easter Egg - Checkered,  Easter Egg - Polka Dots,  Easter Egg - Rainbow) ×1
