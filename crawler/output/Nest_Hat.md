# Nest Hat

Some little chicks snooze in this nest hat. How they managed to form a nest on the top of your head is pretty impressive. The more you move, the more agitated they seem to get, better slow down and not upset your new friends.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be transmuted.

This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Nest Hat** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Easter Week 2020 on April 9, 2020.
##  Function
When equipped, the Nest Hat serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * While wearing Nest Hat, moving around or doing the /furious command will cause the chicks in the nest to get angry, and eventually turn red.
