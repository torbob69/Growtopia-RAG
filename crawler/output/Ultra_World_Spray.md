The **Ultra World Spray** is an unsplicable consumable which was added on September 2, 2022.
##  Function
When consumed in a world, the Ultra World Spray will speed up the time required to grow for all trees in the world by 30 days.
##  Trivia
  * The Ultra World Spray does not speed up the time of the Lunar New Year event trees.
  * The Ultra World Spray is the first consumable sold as an In-App Purchase.
