The **Powerful Ponytail Hair** is a hair item which was introduced as April's Item Of The Month for 2020.
##  Function
Come out swinging!

Bad hair day.

When equipped, the **Powerful Ponytail Hair** grants the _Punch Damage: Hair Punch_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. When the player's standby, the hair will transform to either a thumbs up or a hair bow.
