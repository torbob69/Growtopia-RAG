The **Floating Throne** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2023 on November 17, 2023.
##  Function
O say, can you see!

By the dawn's early light!

When equipped, the **Floating Throne** grants the _Speedy + High Jump: Floating Throne_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and jump one tile higher.
