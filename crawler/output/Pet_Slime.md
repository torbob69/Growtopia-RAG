The **Pet Slime** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the Daily Challenges update on November 18, 2015.
##  Function
Your pet spits corrosive slime!

You feel empty inside.

When equipped, the **Pet Slime** grants the _Pet Slime_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. It also summons a yellow-colored slime pet that follows the player around.
The  Myths and Legends : Finding the Swamp Monster mission requires 1 of this item.
