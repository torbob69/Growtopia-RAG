The **Locke's Megaphone** is an unsplicable and untradable consumable which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2021 Day 6 on August 28, 2021.
##  Function
When consumed, Locke's Megaphone allows the player to send a super broadcast without having to spend gems, as long as they are over level 21, have role Supporter or Super Supporter, and have Advanced Account Protection (AAP) enabled.
##  Trivia
  * The design of  Locke's Megaphone is based on the classic  Megaphone.
  * In order to tackle the broadcast spamming issues Locke's Megaphone was added to the game to replace regular Megaphones, which were sold by Locke and the Lock-Bot.
  * Unlike its regular counterpart, Locke's Megaphone was untradeable, and required the player to be over level 21, have Advanced Account Protection enabled, and have the role Supporter or Super Supporter to be used.
    * Additionally, Locke's Megaphone costed 40 World Locks, which was 10 higher than the last price of the regular Megaphone.
      * Regardless of these differences, same information paragraph of regular Megaphone was displayed for Locke's Megaphone in the sale-screen of both Locke The Salesman and the Lock-Bot.
  * As of September 7, 2023, the Broadcast system undergone changes:
    * The Megaphone can only be purchased from In-App Purchases and has been removed from the Growtoken Store
    * The Locke's Megaphone is no longer sold by Locke The Salesman and Lock-Bot becoming unobtainable as a result.
    * The prices of Broadcast increased with the following prices:
      * Non-Supporters: 12,000 Gems
      * Supporters: 9,000 Gems
      * Super-Supporters: 6,000 Gems
