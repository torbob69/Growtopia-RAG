# Panda Mask

Aww, it's adorable! Don this mask (with associated suit) and you'll instantly look like the second-cutest type of panda (after red pandas) and second-most monochromatic bear (after polar bears) in the world! Just goes to show that you can be second-place in looks... and first in our hearts.

The **Panda Mask** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2018 on September 21, 2018.
##  Function
When equipped, the Panda Mask serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Panda set, which consists of the following items:

    * Panda Mask (Hat)

    * Panda Suit (Shirt)
  * The Panda Mask has the same combining recipe as the Panda Suit.
