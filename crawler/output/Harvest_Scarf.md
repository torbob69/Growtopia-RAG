# Harvest Scarf

A whirling wisp of fabric to accent your perfect harvest style. Will also keep you warm with extra snuzzly fibers. Seriously, this thing is, like, TEN times snuzzlier than the next leading scarf.

Harvest **trees of rarity no more than 100** with a  Hand Scythe during the Harvest Festival.
The **Harvest Scarf** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2017 on October 4, 2017.
##  Function
When equipped, the **Harvest Scarf** grants the _Slow Fall_ mod, which allows the player to glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
