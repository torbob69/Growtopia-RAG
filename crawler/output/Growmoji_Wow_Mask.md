The **Growmoji Wow Mask** is an unsplicable face item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2020 Day 7.
##  Function
When equipped, the Growmoji Wow Mask serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is one of the several masks based on growmojis available in the game. Other growmoji-themed masks include:

    * Chilled Smile Mask

    * Growmoji Cool Shades Mask

    * Growmoji Face Mask
