The **Toxic Waste** is an unsplicable public solid pain foreground block which was added as part of the Growtech Pharma update on May 16, 2013.
##  Function
Being a type of pain block, a Toxic Waste knocks the player back and stings the player until the player stops all motion. A player will die after making contact with a pain block five times in quick succession (recovering from damage takes roughly 5 seconds). The _Fireproof_ mod will not allow the player to take more hits, unlike with lava pain blocks.
When locking a Toxic Waste or any other public block with a Builder's Lock and setting it to only allow building, any other player will be limited from breaking the block.
##  Trivia
  * Several Toxic Waste are spawned randomly after breaking a Toxic Waste Barrel.
  * On August 20, 2015, developer @Seth accidentally handed out the Toxic Waste itself to the public during the seventh day of the Player Appreciation Week (Super Mod Dance Party), resulting in it becoming an acquirable rare item.
  * Being unmovable, Toxic Wastes are unable to be picked up using a Flashback Flan.
