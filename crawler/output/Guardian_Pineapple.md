The **Guardian Pineapple** is an unsplicable solid toggleable animated foreground block which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2016 on June 3, 2016.
##  Function
A Guardian Pineapple can be toggled on or off by punching. When toggled on, it blocks the use of consumables by players in the world.
The  Super Pineapple Party Exclusive Mission - Pucker up for more Pineapple fun! mission requires 1 of this item.
##  Trivia
  * Trying to place more than one Guardian Pineapple in a world will cause it to say "_This world already has a Guardian Pineapple somewhere on it, installing two would be dangerous!_ "
