# Spring Sale

Growtopia Gazette
> March 20th: Spring Sale & Voucher Dayz
> St. Patrick's Week might be over, but we are not done yet! TO celebrate the beginning of a new season, we're bringing you the Spring Sale! Discounts available when purchasing a wide array of items with Gems and Vouchers! So, hurry up as the sale only lasts for 24 hours! Happy Sunday!
> Make sure to read Morse's Dev Diary for more details!
> Wear it like a star and shine like a diamond with the Superstar Sneakers now available in the store! Purchase selected Gem Packs from the store to earn vouchers and redeem them against special store packs and exclusive cosmetic items. Get more than what you pay for during this event from the 18th to the 20th of March. Enjoy!
The **Spring Sale** is a 1-day event which happened on March 20, 2022. Gem prices in the Growtopia Store were discounted by roughly 25% (excluding World Lock and World Lock Pack). Coinciding with the last day of Voucher Dayz, various older cosmetic items from the event were also featured in the shop and were discounted by roughly 30%.
