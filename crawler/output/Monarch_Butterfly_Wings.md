The **Monarch Butterfly Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added on the 2nd of November, 2018. One could be purchased from the Store for 200,000 gems as November 2018's Item Of The Month.
##  Function
How royal!

Commoner!

When equipped, the **Monarch Butterfly Wings** grants the _Double Jump: Butterfly_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
