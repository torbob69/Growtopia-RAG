# Burrito

Mmmm, rolled up food. Who cares what's inside? There's a TORTILLA!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: Full Collision
Hardness: **5 Hits** **4 Hits** – Restores after **4s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Pinata Bash

Participate in successfully smashing an  Ultra Pinata with a  Lucky Pinata Hat equipped during the **Pinata Bash!** event.
May give **1** item each time.
Breaking

| May drop in sets of **1** from breaking:
•  Block De Mayo Block  |
The **Burrito** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2014 on May 2, 2014.
##  Function
When consumed, the player will say "YUM!" and do the :D ⦅⦆ emote.

### Silkworm Food     _Main article:Guide:Silkworm Care_
This consumable can be fed to a Silkworm to give it a aqua color, thus allowing it to produce 1–10  Silk Thread - Aqua per harvest.
