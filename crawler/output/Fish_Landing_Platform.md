The **Fish Landing Platform** is an unsplicable platform foreground block that was added as part of the Time-Tossed! update.
##  Function
A Fish Landing Platform behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath. Unlike the other types of platforms, it allows the player to fish right through it.
