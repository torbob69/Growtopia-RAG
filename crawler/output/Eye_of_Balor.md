##  Function
Eye Eye!

Eyes down!

When equipped, the **Eye of Balor** grants the _Punch Range: Eye of Balor_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further.
##  Trivia
  * This item is a reference to Balor from Irish Mythology, who was the leader of the Fomorians.
