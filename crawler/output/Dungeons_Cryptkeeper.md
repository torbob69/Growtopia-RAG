The **Dungeons Cryptkeeper** in unobtainable non-solid NPC foreground block which was added as part of the Dungeons update on December 9, 2024.
##  Function
The Dungeons Cryptkeeper is an NPC found when a player has used all their lives in a Dungeon.
Players can exchange 2 Scrolls to purchase an extra life and continue their journey in the current room of the dungeon they were in.
