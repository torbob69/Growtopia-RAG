The **Golden Jet Ski** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Brr-lloon Warz 2018.
##  Function
Golden motorbike of the Sea!

No more fancywater riding!

When equipped, the **Golden Jet Ski** grants the _Speedy in Water: Golden Jet Ski_ mod, which allows the player to move faster in water and float on water permanently with slow ascent.
##  Trivia
  * Prior to Brr-lloon Warz 2020, the Golden Jet Ski could be claimed after reaching 1,600,000 guild points.
  * Golden Jet Ski is a recolored version of the  Jet Ski.
  * Initially, the Golden Jet Ski's float-on-water effect was categorized as a _Surfboard_ , but at an unknown update, it was changed to _Forced Float_.
