# SummerFest Starter Pack
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **SummerFest Starter Pack** for **14.99 USD** in the Growtopia Store.
"All the gems you could ever want and everything you need to start your Summerfest off with a bang!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **SummerFest Starter Pack** is a Store-bought IAP which was added as part of the SummerFest 2022 update and only appears during the first day of SummerFest.
The first incarnation of the SummerFest Starter Pack contained the following:
  * Gems ×540,000
  * Summer Surprise ×10
  * Super Summer Surprise ×3
  * Super Fireworks ×2
  * Sunset Mask ×1
