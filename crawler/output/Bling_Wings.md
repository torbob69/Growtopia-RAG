The **Bling Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Wing Week 2020 Day 7 on October 7, 2020.
##  Function
Rising like the stock market!

Crashing like the stock market!

When equipped, the **Bling Wings** grants the _Double Jump: Bling Wings_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
##  Trivia
  * Bling Wings are the first wings that can be exclusively bought with money in the Growtopia Store.
