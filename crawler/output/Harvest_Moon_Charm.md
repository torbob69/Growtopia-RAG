# Harvest Moon Charm

Mystic moonlight seeps from this charm, tipping your fields toward balance. As a result, while you wear it, your crops will be blessed with a higher chance of dropping Balance Mooncakes during the harvest!

This item never drops any seeds.

The **Harvest Moon Charm** is an unsplicable chest item that was added as part of Harvest Festival 2019 on September 12, 2019.
##  Function
Blessings of the Harvest.

Out on your luck.

When equipped, the **Harvest Moon Charm** grants the _Fortune Favor: Moon Charm_ mod, which allows the player to increase the odds of obtaining Balance Mooncakes.
