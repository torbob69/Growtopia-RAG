# MyChips

**MyChips** is a platform that uses ads to generate virtual rewards for users, such as gems in _Growtopia_. MyChips can be found in game in the "Earn Free Gems" tab in the shop which is **only visible for mobile users**. Before October, Growtopia previously used Tapjoy as its **ad reward** platform.
##  Conditions
**MyChips Offerwall** is only available to players aged 13 and above and must be accessed from a mobile device. It will not appear in the Growtopia Store for younger accounts or on non-mobile platforms. To let MyChips work properly, Players also need to enable all options under “Edit Data Sharing Preferences” in the Growtopia settings.
To get rewards from **MyChips** , players need to complete several tasks that have been assigned. The rewards will be given to players when they successfully complete the tasks on time. After completing a task within the required time, players must log in to game to receive the Gems specified by the offers.
##  Rewards
  * Players can earn Gems for free by completing MyChips offers, different applications provide different amounts of Gems depending on the task.
  * Players can also obtain the Supporter or Super Supporter for free by earning Gems through **MyChips** offers.
