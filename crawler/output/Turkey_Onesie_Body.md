No turkey is complete without a gobbly body, and this is the best you can find! Gobble gobble gobble!

The **Turkey Onesie Body** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2017 Day 2 on November 24, 2017.
##  Function
When equipped, the Turkey Onesie Body serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Turkey set, which consists of the following items:

    * Turkey Onesie Hood (Hair)

    * Turkey Onesie Beak (Face)

    * Turkey Onesie Body (Shirt)

    * Turkey Onesie Legs (Pants)

    * Turkey Onesie Feet (Feet)

    * Turkey Onesie Tail (Back)

    * Turkey Carving Set (Hand)
  * In 2017, the Turkey Onesie Body could be obtained as a random drop by smashing blocks on the second day of Thanksgiving Week 2017.
