The **Wings of Ahool** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of the Garuda's Glory update on August 8, 2024.
##  Function
I am the night!

I will have my vengeance!

When equipped, the **Wings of Ahool** grants the _Double Jump + Night Vision : Wings of Ahool_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and eliminate the darkness effect created by dark background blocks. Unlike the Night Vision Goggles, Wings of Ahool does not require a D Battery in order to function in worlds the player doesn't own. Additionally, the player can enable and disable the wings and pet through their wrench menu.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Ahool set, which consists of the following items:

    * Ears of Ahool (Hat)

    * Mask of Ahool (Face)

    * Wings of Ahool (Back)

    * Body of Ahool (Shirt)

    * Legs of Ahool (Pants)

    * Feet of Ahool (Shoes)
  * The Ahool set is a reference to an Indonesian bat-like mythological creature of the same name. The beast is said to grow up to 10 feet in height when standing on its hind legs and has a wingspan of 28 feet. Additionally, the creature's name comes from the loud shriek it makes when it descends upon its prey.
