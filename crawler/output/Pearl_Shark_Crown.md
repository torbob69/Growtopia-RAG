The **Pearl Shark Crown** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Beach Party 2023 on July 6, 2023.
##  Function
We're gonna need a bigger boat!

We're gonna need a bigger shark!

When equipped, the **Pearl Shark Crown** grants the _Punch Damage + Punch Power : Pearl Shark Crown_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and knock back other players further.
