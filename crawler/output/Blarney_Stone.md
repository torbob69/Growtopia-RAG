The **Blarney Stone** is an unobtainable non-solid foreground block that was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2014 on March 14, 2014. It is found at the end of all ten BLARNEY worlds.
##  Function
During St. Patrick's Week, if a player walks past the stone, they will receive a Blarney Pebble, once per player per stone. They will also receive one St. Patrick's Week related item, the randomized prize is doubled for the first Blarney Stone passed each day.
