The **Black Balrog's Tail** is an unsplicable hand item that was introduced as part of Black Friday 2020 update on November 27, 2020.
##  Function
Black Demonic Blade!

Unarmed!

When equipped, the **Black Balrog's Tail** grants the _Punch Power: Black Balrog's Tail_ mod, which allows the player to knock back other players further. It will also replace the player's punching animation into black slashes.
