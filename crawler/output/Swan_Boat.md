The **Swan Boat** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2022 on February 11, 2022.
##  Function
Through the tunnel of love!

The swan needs to rest!

When equipped, the **Swan Boat** grants the _Speedy in Water & Surfboard : Swan Boat!_ mod, which allows the player to move faster in water and float on water for three seconds.
