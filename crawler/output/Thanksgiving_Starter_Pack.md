# Thanksgiving Starter Pack
On November 20, 2025

|
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Thanksgiving Starter Pack** for **14.99 USD** in the Growtopia Store.
"An extra sweet deal to go with the event!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Thanksgiving Starter Pack** is a Store-bought IAP which was added as part of the Thanksgiving Week 2023 on November 17, 2023, and is only available on the first day of the event each year.
The first incarnation of the Harvest Starter Pack contained the following:
  * Gems ×650,000
  * Organic Turkey ×15
  * Turkey Sucker ×50
  * Thanksgiving Bell ×50

The second incarnation of the Harvest Starter Pack contained the following:
  * Gems ×650,000
  * Organic Turkey ×15
  * Turkey Sucker ×50
  * Thanksgiving Bell ×50

The third incarnation of the Harvest Starter Pack contained the following:
  * Gems ×4,500,000
  * Turkey Sucker ×80
  * Thanksgiving Bell ×100
  * Organic Turkey ×25
  * Stuffins' Support Package ×60
  * Giant Turkey Leg ×1
