The **Minilith Stone** is an unsplicable solid foreground block which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2019 on March 15, 2019.
##  Function
When placed, the Minilith Stone serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * Minilith Stone's design is based on Stonehenge, a prehistoric monument regarded as a British cultural icon.
    * Its name is a portmanteau of "mini" and "monolith," referring to its appearance as the miniature version of a geological feature which consists of a massive rock.
