The **Sheet Music: Mexican Trumpet** is an unsplicable sheet music background block which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2021 on May 5, 2021.
##  Function
Sheet Music: Mexican Trumpet is a sheet music that plays a mexican trumpet note, depending on its position vertically.
