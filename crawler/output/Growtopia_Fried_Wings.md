The **Growtopia Fried Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Wing Week 2020 Day 3 on October 3, 2020.
##  Function
A mix of herbs and spices that's out of this world!

All winged out!

When equipped, the **Growtopia Fried Wings** grants the _Double Jump: Fried Wings_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
##  Trivia
  * Growtopia Fried Wings' recipe was accidentally made available on Day 2 before its suppose release; it was patched later during the day, which made the wings unobtainable until Day 3.
