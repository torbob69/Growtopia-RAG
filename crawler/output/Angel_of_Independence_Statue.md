The **Angel of Independence Statue** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2022 on May 5, 2022.
##  Function
When placed, the Angel of Independence Statue serves a fully aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The Angel of Independence Statue is based on the Angel of Independence, most commonly known by its shortened name _El Ángel_ , a monument erected in Mexico City in order to commemorate the centennial of the beginning of Mexico's War of Independence.
