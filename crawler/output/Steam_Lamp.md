The **Steam Lamp** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2016 Day 1.
##  Function
As a light source, a Steam Lamp emits light in dark areas for 10 seconds when activated by steam power from below.
##  Trivia
