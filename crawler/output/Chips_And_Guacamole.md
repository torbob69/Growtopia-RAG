The **Chips And Guacamole** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2017 on May 5, 2017.
##  Function
Get 50 Gems for beating a Pet Trainer.

Your stomach's rumbling.

When equipped, this item grants the _Food: Pet Gems_ mod, which will give the player 50 gems for every Pet Trainer defeated for 30 minutes.
