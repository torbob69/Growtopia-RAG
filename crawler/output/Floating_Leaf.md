# Floating Leaf

This magic leaf can float you around. Just cuz.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be transmuted.
Data

Type: Feet – Clothes
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: Full Collision
Hardness: **0 Hits** **0 Hits** – Restores after **0s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Scythe Harvesting

Harvest **trees of rarity no more than 100** with a  Hand Scythe during the Harvest Festival.
**Floating Leaf** is an unsplicable feet item which was added on Harvest Festival 2015.
##  Function
You can move faster!

You are slow again.

When equipped, the **Floating Leaf** grants the _Speedy_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
