The **Anomalizing Elegant Rolling Pin** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the Role Up! update.
##  Function
Anomalizing Elegant Rolling Pins are used to score points during during Cooking Conquerors. While equipping this item, each successfully cooked food yields 70,000 points. Elegant anomalizers, such as this item, may rarely break upon usage during their respective Grow Events.
