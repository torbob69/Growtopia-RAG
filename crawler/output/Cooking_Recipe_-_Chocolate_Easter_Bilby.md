**Cooking Recipe - Chocolate Easter Bilby** is an unsplicable hand item that was added as part of Easter Week 2020.
##  Function
While equipped, the player will be able to cook a Chocolate Easter Bilby. Attempting to cook one without equipping Cooking Recipe - Chocolate Easter Bilby will end up creating a Chocolate Bunny instead. This item is permanent and will not be consumed when the player cooks the item.
Additionally, this cooking recipe's description guides the player on how to cook the item.
##  Trivia
  * Unlike the cooking recipe items introduced in the Mutant Kitchen Rebalanced! update, Cooking Recipe - Chocolate Easter Bilby can be equipped in the hand clothing slot, and is necessary for players to cook the titular food.
