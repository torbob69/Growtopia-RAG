The **Grey Pet Pteranus Rex** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Growassic Park on October 7, 2016.
##  Function
When equipped, the Grey Pet Pteranus Rex serves a purely cosmetic purpose and summons a Grey Pet Pteranus Rex which follows the player around.
