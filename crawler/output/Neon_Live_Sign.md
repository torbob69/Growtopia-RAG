The **Neon Live Sign** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block which was added on March 15, 2023.
##  Function
When placed, the Neon Live Sign serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The **Neon Live Sign** is a block designed by JonVince, the winner of the Design a Block contest held on the Growtopia Forums and voted upon on the 10th Growtopia Livestream in Growtopia Official Twitch.

  * When **Neon Live Sign** was first introduced to the game, it could drop its seed when broken. This glitch went unpatched for a considerable amount of time because it was not recognized by anyone, due to the fact that **Neon Live Sign** is not a perma-block which caused players to avoid placing and breaking such an exclusive block. However, as soon as the glitch was recognized by the developer team it was patched quickly.
    * Additionally, all **Neon Live Sign Seeds** were removed from the game as the developer team did not want an exclusive block to be farmed to create more.
    * However, some **Neon Live Sign** seeds can be seen painted on Painting Easels nowadays, as a remark of their existence.

  * As of _March 27, 2025_ , the **Neon Live Sign** was made redeemable for 10, 000 Growpoints in Growtopia's Official Twitch Chat.
