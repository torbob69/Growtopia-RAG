The **Golden Cuff Beard** is an unsplicable face item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2020 Day 7.
##  Function
When equipped, the Golden Cuff Beard serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The Golden Cuff Beard is one of the three clothing items added on Player Appreciation Week 2020 Day 7 obtainable via breaking Golden Treasure Hoards, alongside the Long Nose and Handlebar Mustache.
