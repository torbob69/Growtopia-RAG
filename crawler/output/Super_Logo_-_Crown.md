# Super Logo - Crown

Emblazon this logo on your super costume since you won the Card Clash Daily Challenge! With this logo on, you get +2 health during card battles!

Place in the top three of the **Card Clash** Daily Challenge.
The **Super Logo - Crown** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of the Daily Challenges update on November 18, 2015.
##  Function
Super-powered!

Under-powered!

When equipped, the **Super Logo - Crown** grants the _Card Battle Health: Super Logo - Crown_ mod, which allows the player to add 2 additional health to the player during card battles against a villain or another player.
