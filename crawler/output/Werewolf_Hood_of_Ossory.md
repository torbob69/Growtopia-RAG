The **Werewolf Hood of Ossory** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2023 on March 17, 2023.
##  Function
Smell like you sound!

Hungry like the wolf!

When equipped, the **Werewolf Hood of Ossory** grants the _Speedy : Werewolf Hood of Ossory_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.
##  Trivia
  * Werewolf Hood of Ossory is a reference to a place called _Ossory_ in Ireland which was beleived to have werewolves as its descendants during medieval times: _"Werewolves of Ossory"_.
