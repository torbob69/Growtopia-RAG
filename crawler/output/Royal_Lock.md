The **Royal Lock** is a lock which was added on the 1st of July, 2017 as July's Item Of The Month, costing 200,000 gems in the Growtopia Store.
##  Function
It is a World Lock but with more features called the "Ye Royal Options":
  * _"SILENCE, Peasants"_ option, where all players in the world (with the exception of world owners, world administrators and moderators) who do not have access cannot talk. This also prevents players without access from usage of the /me command.
  * _"Rainbows For The King"_ option, where a rainbow trail is given to all players with access.

Also, all players who are in the world will have an arrow pointing to them (only seen by those with access) if they are out of view of the player with access.
