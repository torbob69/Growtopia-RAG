The **Cute Mutant Wonky Onesie Hood** is an unsplicable hair item which was added a part of the Mutant Kitchen monthly event update on August 6, 2021.
##  Function
When equipped, the Cute Mutant Wonky Onesie Hood serves as a purely cosmetic purpose.
