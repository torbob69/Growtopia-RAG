##  Trivia
  * Placing two Monochromatic Dirt next to each other will cause them to join, creating a continuous pattern.
  * The sprite of Monochromatic Dirt is a recolor of Dirt's along with Sand, Martian Soil, Deep Sand, and Digital Dirt.
