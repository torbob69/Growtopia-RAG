# Ancient Hinge

Creaking with legendary power, this must be the piece of something far greater. And doorlike. Maybe.

Wind Asteroid **(×2)** and
Fire Essence **(×5)**.

The **Ancient Hinge** is an unsplicable component which was added as part of the Hidden Riches update on September 1, 2017.
