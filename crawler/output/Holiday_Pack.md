# Holiday Pack
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Holiday Pack** for **14.99 USD** in the Growtopia Store.
"All the gems you could ever want for the holidays plus everything you need for the ultimate Winterfest!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Holiday Pack** is a Store-bought IAP which was added as part of WinterFest 2017 and has been re-released during WinterFest every year since. Since it's release, it has gone through many name changes.
The first incarnation of the Holiday Pack contained the following:
  * Gems ×160,000 (mistakenly given as 320,000 when first released)
  * Deluxe Winterfest Cracker ×1
  * Winterfest Cracker ×25
  * Snowball ×5
  * Gross Golden Holiday Sweater ×1
