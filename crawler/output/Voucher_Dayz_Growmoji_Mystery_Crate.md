Purchase the **Voucher Dayz Growmoji Mystery Crate** for **1 Grow Voucher** in the Growtopia Store.

Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Voucher Dayz Growmoji Mystery Crate** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Voucher Dayz 2026 on August 27, 2026.
##  Function
When broken, it will randomly drop one of the 8 growmojis that are exclusive to the crate:
  * Growmoji Fire
  * Growmoji Flirty
  * Growmoji Light Bulb
  * Growmoji Money-mouth
  * Growmoji Nerd
  * Growmoji Skull
  * Growmoji Trophy
  * Growmoji Wilted Flower

Note: Drop rates are not equal — some growmojis are significantly rarer than others.
