**Riding Raven** is an unsplicable feet item that was added during Halloween Week 2016. It is available for 150,000 Gems in the Growtopia Store during Halloween Week only.
##  Function
You can jump in mid-air! It's like flying, but less.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Riding Raven** grants the _Double Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
##  Trivia
  * Purchasing the Riding Raven from the Gem Store will complete one of the thirty-two Epic Quests.
  * Riding Raven is a redesigned version of  Riding Phoenix.
