# Ultraviolet Charm

Contained within this charm is pure ultraviolet energy. It is believed to enhance the capabilities of anyone who wears it. Warning: Ultraviolet Radiation is what causes sunburn. Prolonged exposure is not advised and don't forget to wear your hat and sunscreen.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be transmuted.

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Ultra Purple Fragment **(×1)** ,

Ultra Blue Neptune Fragment **(×30)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Ultraviolet Charm** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of SummerFest 2022 on July 28, 2022.
##  Function
Ultraviolet Power!

Power Down!!

When equipped, the **Ultraviolet Charm** grants the _Build Range: Ultraviolet Charm_ mod, which allows the player to build one tile further.
