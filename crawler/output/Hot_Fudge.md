The **Hot Fudge** is an unsplicable lava pain foreground block that was added as part of Voucher Dayz on November 14, 2024.
##  Function
Being a lava pain block, a Hot Fudge knocks the player back and puts the player on fire until the player stops all motion when touched. A player will die after making contact with a lava pain block five times in quick succession (recovering from damage takes roughly 5 seconds), but a player with the _Fireproof_ mod may take up to ten consecutive hits.
