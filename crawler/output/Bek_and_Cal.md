The **Bek and Cal** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of SummerFest 2025 on July 10, 2025.
##  Function
Long live the king!

Three's a crowd!

When equipped, the **Bek and Cal** grants the _Speedy + Build Range: Bek and Cal_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and build one tile further.
