The 14DEViL's Friendly Doggy Leash is an unsplicable pants item which was added as a part of Player Appreciation Week 2025 on September 25, 2025.
##  Function
Who's a good boy?

Bad dog!

When equipped, the **14DEViL's Friendly Doggy Leash** grants the _Punch Range + Card Battle Health : 14DEViL's Friendly Doggy Leash_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further and add 2 additional health to the player during card battles against a villain or another player. Additionally, if a player types the /sleep command the pet dog will also sleep alongside the player.
