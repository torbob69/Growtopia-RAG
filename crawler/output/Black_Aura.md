The **Black Aura** is a unsplicable back item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2015.
##  Function
You can jump in mid-air! It's like flying, but less.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Black Aura** grants the _Double Jump!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
##  Trivia
  * According to legends, the aura is a magical field of energy around a human being. The colour of the aura determines a person's personality. The Black Aura means that they are a dark person.
