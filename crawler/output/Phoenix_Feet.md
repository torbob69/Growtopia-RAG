The **Phoenix Feet** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of SummerFest 2022 on July 28, 2022.
##  Function
Feets of flame!

Slow down!

When equipped, the **Phoenix Feet** grants the _Speedy & Double Jump: Phoenix Feet_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and jump a second time in mid-air.
