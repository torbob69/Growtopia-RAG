The **Weather Machine - Digital Rain** is a non-solid weather machine foreground block which was added on Player Appreciation Week 2018.
##  Function
<https://growtopiawiki.com/w/File:Digital_rain.ogg>Weather Machine - Digital Rain's music.
When enabled (by punching), the background of the world will be changed into green lines of codes raining down from the sky. The machine also comes with background music, which can be toggled through its properties.
##  Trivia
  * The weather machine features a few easter eggs. A particular line says "_HELLO GROWTOPIAN_ " in a vertical manner of typing.
