A curious critter blending into the streets! Ever loyal and always ready to lend a hand when times are tough. Wrench yourself and choose Transform to transform!

The **Street Dog** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the Aliens update on January 27, 2023.
##  Function
Who let the dawgs out!

Who who who who who?

When equipped, the **Street Dog** grants the _Double Jump & Punch Range : Street Dog_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air, punch one tile further, and switch between its wearable form and its morph form by wrenching oneself and clicking 'Transform'.
##  Trivia
  * Street Dog's equip and unequip messages are reference to Baha Men's Who Let The Dogs Out song.
