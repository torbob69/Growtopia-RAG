The **Mah-Kah-Ron-Hat** is an unsplicable hat item which was added alongside the Block Party 2 update on March 1, 2019.
##  Function
When equipped, the Mah-Kah-Ron Hat serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The Mah-Kah-Ron-Hat is based on the macaron, a small round confection consisting of two halves sandwiching a creamy filling. The item itself seems to be specifically based on a strawberry macaron.
