The **Pineapple Parasol** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2022 on June 10, 2022.
##  Function
Pop to the top!

Popped to the floor!

When equipped, the **Pineapple Parasol** grants the _Double Jump & Slowfall : Pineapple Parasol_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
