The **Quicksand Block** is an unsplicable solid puddle foreground block which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2021 Day 7 on August 29, 2021.
##  Function
When placed, a Quicksand Block acts as a puddle block, slowing the player down and decreasing their jump height until they step on another block. The Mud Puddle shares this exact same function.
