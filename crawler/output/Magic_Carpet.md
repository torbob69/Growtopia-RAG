# Magic Carpet

More than just a rug! Step on and enter a whole new world! ...well, if you consider hovering slightly above the ground a "new world," that is.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Feet – Clothes
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: Full Collision
Hardness: **0 Hits** **0 Hits** – Restores after **0s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Unearthly Chemical **(×200)** ,
Onisim's Genie **(×1)** and
Green Bunting **(×50)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Magic Carpet** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of the Stuff! update.
##  Function
A magical carpet ride!

Carpet rolled up!

When equipped, the **Magic Carpet** grants the _Slow-Fall: Magic! A magical carpet ride!_ mod, which allows the player to glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
