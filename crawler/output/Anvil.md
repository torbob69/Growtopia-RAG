# Anvil

You can forge astounding weapons on this, if it's next to a Forge that's been heated to 1,000 degrees, and you have Smithing Tools, and wear a Ring of Smithing and a Blacksmith's Apron. Oh, and have some item you want to smith. If you've got all that, go ahead and use the item on the anvil!

##  Function
The Anvil is primarily used for forging items.
In order to forge an item, the Anvil must be placed next to a Forge. The Forge is then heated using Nuclear Fuel until it reaches a temperature of 1,000 degrees. Afterwards, the player will be able to consume the item's materials on the Anvil and forge the item, provided that they are equipping a Ring Of Smithing and Blacksmith's Apron. Each Anvil usage uses up 1 Crystal Smithing Tools.
