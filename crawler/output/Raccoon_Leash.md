"Hey friend, look what I have here, come close, don't worry he/she don't bite... found it nuzzling through my trash. This little critter will be your pal for life, and will help boost you up to out of reach places!"
The **Raccoon Leash** is an unsplicable pants item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2019 Day 6 on August 24, 2019.
##  Function
Sooo sneaky!

No more sneaking!

When equipped, the **Raccoon Leash** grants the _High Jump: Sneaky_ mod, which allows the player to jump one tile higher. It will also summon a raccoon pet which will follow the player around.
