The snake is the sixth of the zodiac animals. Limited only to the Year of the Snake! Myth has it, that the order of the Zodiac Animals, was determined by a race. The snake placed sixth after claiming it was the adoptive child of the dragon!!

##  Function
Oooh it sparkles!

I miss the sparkles...

When equipped, the Year of the Snake Mask grants the _Year of the Snake Mask: Sparkles_ mod, which emits sparkles from the player's body.
