# Machine Parts

**Machine parts** are various components that are used to create the Legen and Dary seeds, which in turn are required to make the Legendary Wizard.
##  Parts
Component: RecipeDoohickey: Found in the What's that? special event. The Winterfest Calendars also have a chance of dropping one.
Whatchamacallit: Splice a Compu Panel Seed and a Laboratory Seed.
Doodad: Available in the Growtopia Store for 75 Growtokens.
Kerjigger: A possible drop from breaking Military Radio, High Tech Block or High Tech Wall.
Thingamajig: Mix 4 Chemical Y, 3 Chemical P and 1 Mysterious Chemical in any Chemical Combiner.
Thingamabob: A possible reward from successfully performing surgeries. Available from the Sales-Man for 13,000 Caducei.
