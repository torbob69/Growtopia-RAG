The **Cloak of Falling Waters** is a back item, introduced on the 1st of November, 2017 as November 2017's Item Of The Month.
##  Function
Your back is a raging rapid!

You are a lot more dry, and a lot less cool.

When equipped, the **Cloak of Falling Waters** grants the _Cloak of Falling Waters_ mod, which allows the player to extinguish fires, punch one tile further, knock back other players further, and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
Punching other players will give them the _Soaked_ mod which will make them wet for 30 seconds.
##  Trivia
  * The item is the first Item Of The Month to have its name revealed as it came out, rather than a week before its release.
