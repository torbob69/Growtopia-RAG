The **Slip 'n' Slide Slime Floor** is an unsplicable slippery platform block which was added as part of the Role Up! update.
##  Function
A Slip 'n' Slide Slime Floor behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath. In addition, being a slippery block, walking on a Slip 'n' Slide Slime Floor causes the player to slide.
The  Myths and Legends : Finding the Swamp Monster mission requires 100 of this item.
