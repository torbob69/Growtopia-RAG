The **BP Water** is an unsplicable solid deadly foreground block which was added as part of Beach Party 2022 on June 24, 2022.
##  Function
During a BEACHPARTYGAME, BP Water blocks appear after a certain time. Being a deadly block, the BP Water kills players upon contact.
