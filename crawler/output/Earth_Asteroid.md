# Earth Asteroid

You might think most asteroids are made of earth already, but this one has EXTRA earth for the dirtiest, most magnificently rocky experience to be found in a floating piece of the heavens.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.

Gems Drop: **N/A**

Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Earth Essence **(×20)** ,
Water Essence **(×5)** and
Asteroid **(×1)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Earth Asteroid** was introduced during the Hidden Riches update.
##  Function
It is similar to the Asteroid, but has it's own animated sprite.
