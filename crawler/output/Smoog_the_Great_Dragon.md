The **Smoog the Great Dragon** is an unspliceable feet item that was added as part of Summer Clash 2018 on June 1st, 2018.
##  Function
Bareback Dragon riding!

Smoog is tired, night-night, sweet dreams!

When equipped, the **Smoog the Great Dragon** grants the _Smoog_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.
