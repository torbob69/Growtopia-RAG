# Firefighter Pack
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Firefighter Pack** for **10,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"You can rescue Growtopians from the fire! Includes a full Yellow Firefighter Outfit (Helmet, Jacket, Pants, and Boots), a Fire Hose, and a Rare Firehouse, which will protect your own world from fires."
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Firefighter Pack** is a store pack which was added on October 8, 2015.
##  Items
  * Firefighter Helmet - Yellow ×1
  * Firefighter Pants - Yellow ×1
  * Firefighter Jacket - Yellow ×1
  * Firefighter Boots ×1
  * Fire Hose ×1
  * Firehouse ×1

##  Trivia
  * The Firefighter Helmet - Red, Firefighter Pants - Red, Firefighter Jacket - Red, Fire Truck, Fire Ax, Pocket Lighter, Highly Combustible Box, Oxygen Mask, and the Water Balloon were also implemented into the game alongside this pack.
