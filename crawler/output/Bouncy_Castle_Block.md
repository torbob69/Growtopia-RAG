The **Bouncy Castle Block** is a unsplicable solid trampoline foreground block which was added as part of Grow4Good Week 2022 on April 22, 2022.
##  Function
Jumping on a trampoline block such as a Bouncy Castle Block will cause the player to bounce, and by holding the jump button, the player can jump even higher. The _High Jump_ mod also boosts one's jump height on a trampoline block.
##  Trivia
  * During Grow4Good Week 2025, the point requirement to unlock the Bouncy Castle Block was lowered from 40,000,000 to 35,000,000.
