The **Fishing Supply Crate** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Grow4Good Week 2021 on April 15, 2021.
##  Function
When broken, the Fishing Supply Crate will drop:
  * Wiggly Worm ×25
  * Shiny Flashy Thing ×15
  * Salmon Eggs ×10
  * Fishing Fly ×5
  * Shrimp Lure ×2
  * Whizmo Gizmo ×1

##  Trivia
  * As part of Grow4Good Week 2022, the number of items given in a Fishing Supply Crate was changed. The crate now drops 25 Wiggly Worm (previously 50), 15 Shiny Flashy Thing (previously 30), 10 Salmon Eggs (previously 20), 5 Fishing Fly (previously 15), 2 Shrimp Lure (previously 5), and 1 Whizmo Gizmo (previously none). Moreover, it no longer drops any Fishing Rod, Cutting Board, and Sushi Knife, whereas it previously dropped 1 of each.
