Dungeons Buff

| A player has a chance to purchase this Dungeons ability from the  Dungeons Shopkeeper after every room cleared for  Souls ×100.
Note: The ability disappears after the player leaves the Dungeon.   |
Berserker is a dungeon buff that is added during Dungeons update on October 23rd, 2025.
##  Function
When player received Berserker, the player's dungeon stats increases the damage by 100 but loses 250 max HP. If the player had negative number on their health, the player will die upon entering next room.
