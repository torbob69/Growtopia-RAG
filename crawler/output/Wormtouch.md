The **Wormtouch** is an unsplicable hand item that was added as part of the Blocktor's Orders! update on June 13, 2019.
##  Function
Wormtouched arms! Digging increased!

Your wormy arms have wiggled away!

When equipped, the **Wormtouch** grants the _Goin' down to wormtown!_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster.
