# Wolfy Block

This is the wolfiest block available with current technology.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.
Data: Paint PreviewType — Foreground Block – Foreground — Aqua Blue Charcoal Green Purple Red Varnish Yellow
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: Full Collision
Hardness: **5 Hits** **4 Hits** – Restores after **4s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Paint Preview Aqua Blue Charcoal Green Purple Red Varnish Yellow
Complete WOLFWORLD

May yield **5** items each.
The **Wolfy Block** is a unsplicable solid foreground block which was added as part of the Daily Challenges update on November 18, 2015.
##  Function
When placed, the Wolfy Block serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * As part of of the Block Party 2 update, the quantity of Wolfy Blocks obtained from WOLFWORLD was increased from 3 to 5.
  * As part of the Quality of Life Changes update on May 20, 2022, the **Wolfy Block** was added for sale in the Sales-Man under Wolfworld items for 250 Wolf Tickets. This was later removed from the Sales-Man 30 minutes later because there were multiple complaints of the price being inconsistent.
