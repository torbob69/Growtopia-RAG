The **Dark Doctor's Cloak** is an unsplicable back item that was added as part of the Challengers Approach! update.
##  Function
Hiding your contagions in your cloak.

You'd better go fill your pockets with posies.

When equipped, the **Dark Doctor's Cloak** grants the _Vicious Malady Increase + Double Jump: Dark Doctor Cloak_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and increase the odds of contracting another player with a vicious malady.
