The **Summer Glider** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of SummerFest 2021 on June 8, 2021.
##  Function
Glide through the air with the greatest of ease!

Back down to earth!

When equipped, the **Summer Glider** grants the _Double Jump + Slow Fall: Summer Glider_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
