Awarded each week to the player who finishes first in the Emerald League leaderboard in Dungeons.
The **Dungeons Badge - Emerald** is an unsplicable badge that was introduced as part of the Dungeons Quality of Life update on July 6, 2026.
##  Function
The sprite for this item is used for the Dungeons Badge - Emerald. Players with a Dungeons Badge - Emerald can toggle this on and off by wrenching themselves and clicking badge. Once toggled, it adds a  to the beginning of the player's name.
