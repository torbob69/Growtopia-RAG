The **Murf Hood** is an unsplicable hair item which was added as part of Star Trek Prodigy on June 27, 2022.
##  Function
When equipped, the Murf Hood serves a purely cosmetic purpose. Players can toggle the Murf Hood to switch between two sprites, closed and open hood.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the murf set, which consists of the following items:

    * Murf Hood (hair)

    * Murf Onesie (shirt)
