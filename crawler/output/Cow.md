The **Cow** is a splicable non-solid provider foreground block which was added alongside the Farm Pack on July 6, 2013.
##  Function
The Cow is a type of provider. When a Cow is harvested (by being punched), 1-2 Milk will drop. One can be harvested approximately every 24 hours.

##  Trivia
  * The Cow is one of the first two providers added in Growtopia, with the other one being the Chicken.
  * An update on July 1, 2020 introduced the  Cow Mask, the design of which was based on the Cow.
  * Collecting 100 items from providers such as **Cows** will earn the achievement **Milkin' It (Classic)**.
  * Collecting 500 items from providers such as **Cows** will earn the achievement **Moo Juice (Classic)**.
  * Collecting 10,000 items from providers such as **Cows** will earn the achievement **Collector Extraordinaire (Classic)**.
  * Collecting 1,000,000 items from providers such as **Cows** will earn the achievement **Providers Will Provide**.
