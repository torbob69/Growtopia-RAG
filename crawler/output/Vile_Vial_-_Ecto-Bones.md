The **Vile Vial - Ecto-Bones** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Blocktor's Orders! on June 13, 2019.
##  Function
You've been infected with Ecto-Bones!

Your bones are no longer ectoplasmic!

When consumed, the player will receive the _Ecto-Bones_ malady mod for 12 hours, which can be cured through a successful surgery. Should the malady get cured, both players—the surgeon and patient—will receive a prize.
##  Trivia
