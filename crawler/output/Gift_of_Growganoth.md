The **Gift of Growganoth** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Halloween Week 2020 on October 22, 2020.
##  Function
When consumed, it has a chance to drop the same item as sacrificing a rarity 75 item into the Maw of Growganoth. It also has a chance to drop the following items:
  * Spooky Gift
  * Spooky Rags
  * Spooky Box
  * Mimic Chest
  * Dark King's Offering (rare)

##  Trivia
  * As of 2014, this shop item costs 500 gems rather than 200.
