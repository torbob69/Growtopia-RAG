The **Riding Gorilla** is an unsplicable feet item that was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2020 Day 6.
##  Function
Go Go Gorilla Go Go!

Let's chill and grab a banana smoothie!

When equipped, the **Riding Gorilla** grants the _Speedy: Riding Gorilla!_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.
When the player punches, the gorilla's eyes turn red, and it will hurl Deep Sand projectiles at the target. Mud Glob particles splatter upon impact.
