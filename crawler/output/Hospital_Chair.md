The **Hospital Chair** is a non-solid seat block which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2024 Day 1 on August 26, 2024.
##  Function
When a player stands in front of a seat such as the Hospital Chair for a few seconds, the player will automatically be seen "sitting" on it. Additionally, players can set an ID, and have doors lead to them, however they cannot be entered into.
