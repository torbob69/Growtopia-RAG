# Autumn Bomb

I am become death, the destroyer of foliage. Toss this in the air to celebrate the fall!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **30 Hits** **23 Hits** – Restores after **4s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Balance Mooncake **(×2)** ,
Longevity Mooncake **(×7)** and
Harmony Mooncake **(×7)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
Mooncake Offering

| 1 may be received as a common reward from offering mooncakes using an Offering Table, when the offering contains:
•  Harmony Mooncake  |
The **Autumn Bomb** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2014 on September 5, 2014.
##  Function
When consumed, a bunch of autumn leaves occupies the foreground, falling from the sky.
