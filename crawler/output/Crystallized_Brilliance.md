Thought given form! The tangible intelligence surrounding it could certainly be used for something spectacular... or set on a shelf to look pretty!

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Chandelier **(×200)** ,
Typhoon Gate **(×4)** and
Wind Essence **(×5)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Crystallized Brilliance** is an unsplicable solid foreground block which was added as part of the Hidden Riches update.
##  Function
When placed, the Crystallized Brilliance serves a fully aesthetic purpose.

##  Trivia
  * There are currently five blocks in the crystallized series, all of which are primarily used to create and upgrade ancestral artifacts.
    * Crystallized Brilliance →  Ancestral Totem of Wisdom
    * Crystallized Nature →  Ancestral Seed of Life
    * Crystallized Reality →  Ancestral Tesseract of Dimensions
    * Crystallized Time →  Ancestral Orb of Time
    * Crystallized Wealth →  Ancestral Lens of Riches
  * The  Crystallized Brilliance's sprite features an  Ice Cube with Buddy's head in gold color inside.
