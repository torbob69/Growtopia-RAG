The **Startopia Update** was June 2020's monthly update. In this update, two new sectors were added which will vary depending if Growlactus was defeated or not: namely, The Desolated Wasteland if the player lost to Growlactus and the Heart of the Galaxy if the player was able to defeat Growlactus.
##  New Items
New items introduced include:

##  Achievements
New achievements introduced include:
Achievements: Description**Ascended**: Earned for landing a starship on an Ascended planet.
**Descended**: Earned for landing a starship on a Descended planet.
**Ascended Universe**: Earned for mapping the entire Ascended universe. _To earn this achievement, players must land 500 starships on an Ascended planet._
**Descended Universe**: Earned for mapping the entire Descended universe. _To earn this achievement, players must land 500 starships on a Descended planet._
**Interstellar Tycoon**: Earned for mapping the entirety of all universes. _To earn this achievement, players must complete both**Ascended Universe** and **Descended Universe**._
