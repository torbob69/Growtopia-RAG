The **Suit of Armor** is an unsplicable toggleable deadly foreground block which was added as part of Summer Clash 2018.
##  Function
Being a toggleable deadly foreground block, a Suit of Armor works like an Evil Eye, allowing player to open and close the Suit of Armor by punching it. When toggled on, the item acts as a deadly block. When toggled off, it acts as a non-solid block which players can walk through. It can be wrenched to be made public or not.
