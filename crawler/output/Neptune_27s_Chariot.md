The **Neptune's Chariot** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of SummerFest 2021 on July 8, 2021.
##  Function
Command the 7 seas!

Your feet are back on dry land!

When equipped, the **Neptune's Chariot** grants the _Speedy & Speedy in Water: Neptune's Chariot_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and move faster in water. The Neptune's Chariot is drawn by three pairs of  War Seahorses, three facing the player's screen and three hidden at the back.
Step 16 of the  Quest Of Steel requires the player to deliver 1 of this item. 1 Mint Julep may also be used as an alternative.
##  Trivia
  * In Hellenistic and Roman imagery, the god Poseidon or Neptune can often be seen driving a sea-chariot drawn by hippocampi. This serves as the basis of inspiration for Neptune's Chariot.
  * There are only 500 Neptune's Chariots that can be obtained in-game.
