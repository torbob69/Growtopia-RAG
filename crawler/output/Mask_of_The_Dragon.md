The **Mask of The Dragon** is an unsplicable face item which was added as December 2021's Item Of The Season on December 1, 2021.
##  Function
Flames rise!

Flames fall!

When equipped, the **Mask of The Dragon** grants the _Double Jump & Punch Range: Mask of The Dragon_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air, punch one tile further, and switch between its wearable form and its morph form by wrenching oneself and clicking 'Transform'.
