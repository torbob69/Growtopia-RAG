##  Trivia
  * Sword is the first item in _Growtopia_ to be based on the eponymous weapon.
  * _Growtopia_ 's creator Seth once indicated that he was planning to give Sword a _Punch Power_ -type mod akin to Just One Boxing Glove.[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Sword#cite_note-1) As of today, however, the Sword still works exactly the way it did upon release, functioning as a cosmetic item only.
