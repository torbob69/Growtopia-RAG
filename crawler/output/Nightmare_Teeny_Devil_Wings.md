The **Nightmare Teeny Devil Wings** is an unsplicable back item that was added as part of Halloween Week 2019.
##  Function
Float away!

Too scary!

When equipped, the **Nightmare Teeny Devil Wings** grants the _Double Jump: Nightmare_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
