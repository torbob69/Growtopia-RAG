The **Mermaid Tail** is an unsplicable pants item.
##  Function
Swim faster, walk slower!

You are a land mammal.

When equipped, the **Mermaid Tail** grants the _A Little Fishy_ mod, which allows the player to move faster in water and runs slower on land.
##  Trivia
  * When equipped, the player's legs disappear, as well as any feet items they are wearing.
