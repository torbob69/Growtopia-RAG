The **Forged Iron Skin** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as part of Spring Clash 2019 on 1 March 2019.
##  Function
Immunity!

Back to normal and boring human skin!

When equipped, the **Forged Iron Skin** grants the _Iron Skinned_ mod, which allows the player to resist a percentage of knockback when punched by other players, change their skin color to iron grey, and decrease the player's jump height. It will also decrease moving speed.
The  Myths and Legends : Here come the Shady Agents mission requires 1 of this item.
