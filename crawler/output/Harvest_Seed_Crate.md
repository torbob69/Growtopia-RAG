The **Harvest Seed Crate** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2023 on September 21, 2023.
##  Function
When broken, the Harvest Seed Crate may drop the following items:
  * Balance Shrub Seed
  * Courage Shrub Seed
  * Decorative Shrine Seed
  * Floating Lotus Flower Seed
  * Pebbles Background Seed
  * Pebbles Block Seed
  * Purity Shrub Seed
  * Stone Block Seed
