# Mixed Fruit Bender

A long time ago, the fruit academy worked together in harmony. But, everything changed when the vegetables attacked. Only the Mixed Fruit Benders, capable of bending all the fruits, could stop them, but when the world needed them most, they vanished. The Tropical Merchant has discovered that Growtopians have the skill to learn Mixed Fruit Bending and while they have much to learn, he believes they can save the world!

##  Function
Jack of all fruits!

Master of none!

When equipped, the **Mixed Fruit Bender** grants the _Punch Range + Punch Damage : Mixed Fruit Bender_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further and break blocks faster.
