If you find yourself lost in a world without any Toilet Seeds, you'll be glad you have this. Plus it's shiny!

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Diaper **(×1)** ,
Candy Heart **(×200)** and
Golden Heart Crystal **(×1)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Golden Diaper** is an unsplicable pants item that was added as part of Valentine's Week 2014 on February 10, 2014.
##  Function
When equipped, the Golden Diaper serves a fully cosmetic purpose.
Step 7 of the  Quest For Honor requires the player to deliver 3 of this item.
