**Sakura's Revenge** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Wonder Week 2021 Day 2 on October 12, 2021.
##  Function
Pink and black never look back!

No longer a ninja!

When equipped, the **Sakura's Revenge** grants the _Punch Power + Punch Damage: Sakura’s Revenge_ mod, which allows the player to knock back other players further and break blocks faster. Black and pink petals would also emerge from the ground as long as the player has the item equipped.
