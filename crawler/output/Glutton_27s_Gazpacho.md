# Glutton's Gazpacho

A soup for the discerning overeater, one sip and you'll find that everything just tastes better! Food Buff: Eat to increase the duration of food buffs received from other food items by 30%! Note: Will only affect food buffs consumed after consuming the Glutton's Gazpacho!

The **Glutton's Gazpacho** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Role Up! update.
##  Function
Increase the duration of food buffs by 30%

Your stomach can't take anymore buffs!

When consumed, it will grant the _Food: Buff Duration_ mod which increases the duration of food buffs by 30%.
