The **Rainbow Silk Scarf** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2025 on March 14, 2025.
##  Function
Rainbows are visions!

Only an illusion!

When equipped, the **Rainbow Silk Scarf** grants the _Double Jump + Slow Fall: Colorful Silk Scarf_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
##  Trivia
  * Rainbow Silk Scarf is a redesigned version of  Silk Scarf and its golden counterpart  Golden Silk Scarf.
