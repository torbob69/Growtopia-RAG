# Ectoplasm Sample

This is an important scientific discovery that could change our understanding of life itself! But you know what'd be fun? Throwing it at somebody.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **2 Hits** **2 Hits** – Restores after **2s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Spirit Storage Unit Reward

Store a  Ghost-In-A-Jar **(x21)** in a  Spirit Storage Unit, increasing its capacity by **105%**.
May yield **1** item each.
The **Ectoplasm Sample** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Battlers Of the Otherworldly update on July 15, 2016.
##  Function
You're covered in ectoplasm!

You got cleaned up.

When consumed on a player, this item will grant them the _Slimed!_ mod for 6 seconds, causing them to walk slower and jump lower than normal.
