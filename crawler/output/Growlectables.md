The **Growlectables** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Role Up! update.
##  Function
25% chance of double Growtokens from Daily Quests.

Your stomach's devasted!

When consumed, the item grants the player the _Food: Delectable_ mod which gives the player a 25% chance of getting double Growtokens when completing Daily Quests for 30 minutes.
