The **Spray Tan** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of SummerFest 2016 on June 30, 2016.
##  Function
You are orange.

The spray tan wore off.

When consumed, the Spray Tan grants the _Spray Tan_ mod for one minute, giving the player a bronzed skin color, resembling a suntan.
##  Trivia
  * The Spray Tan is based on bronzers, a cosmetic product intended to give the skin a temporary bronze-like color, imitating a suntan.
