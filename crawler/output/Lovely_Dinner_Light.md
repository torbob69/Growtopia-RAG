# Lovely Dinner Light

Table's set, dishes are ready, and smiles are sparkling - now you just need an atmospheric way to see it all! Set up this lovely light and enjoy a relaxingly, romantic mood at the flick of a switch!

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Lovewillow's Lace **(×5)** ,
Table Lamp **(×1)** and
Golden Block **(×1)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Lovely Dinner Light** is an unsplicable toggleable animated foreground block which was added as part of the Valentine's Week 2020.
##  Function
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Lovely Dinner Light can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Lovely Dinner Light will change its appearance, showing a 2-framed animation of itself providing light to its surrounding. As a light source, a Lovely Dinner Light emits light in dark areas.
