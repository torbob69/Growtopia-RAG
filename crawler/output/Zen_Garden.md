The **Zen Garden** is an unsplicable background block which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2017 on October 4, 2017.
##  Function
As an SFX background block, a Zen Garden will play a sound effect and undergo a slight visual change when a player passes through. The block will be reset back to normal when a player with access punches it, or if all players have left the world.
##  Trivia
  * The Zen Garden is one of the only two SFX background blocks in _Growtopia_ , alongside the Bubble Wrap.
