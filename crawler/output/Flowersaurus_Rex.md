The **Flowersaurus Rex** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the Growassic Park update on October 7, 2016.
##  Function
When equipped, it summons a Flowersaurus Rex that follows the player arround. Additionally, it replaces the player's punching animation with the Flowersaurus Rex attacking with slashes instead.
