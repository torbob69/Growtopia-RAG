The **Equinox Box** is an unsplicable consumable that was added as part of Equinox Weekend on March 25, 2022.
##  Function
When consumed, the player will earn one of the following rewards:
  * Winter Background ×10
  * Spring Background ×10
  * Summer Background ×10
  * Autumn Background ×10
  * Winter Block ×10
  * Spring Block ×10
  * Summer Block ×10
  * Autumn Block ×10
  * Shirt of Winter ×1
  * Shirt of Spring ×1
  * Shirt of Summer ×1
  * Shirt of Autumn ×1
  * Pants of Winter ×1
  * Pants of Spring ×1
  * Pants of Summer ×1
  * Pants of Autumn ×1
  * Crown of Winter ×1
  * Crown of Spring ×1
  * Crown of Summer ×1
  * Crown of Autumn ×1
  * Boots of Winter ×1
  * Boots of Spring ×1
  * Boots of Summer ×1
  * Boots of Autumn ×1
  * Equinox Scarf ×1 (rare)
