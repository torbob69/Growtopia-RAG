# Mystery Portal

Where will you be transported to? Who knows? It's a mystery. Wrench this to choose up to 5 destinations to randomly send players to.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item has special properties you can adjust with the Wrench.
Data: Paint PreviewType — Portal – Foreground — Aqua Blue Charcoal Green Purple Red Varnish Yellow
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **5 Hits** **4 Hits** – Restores after **8s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Paint Preview Aqua Blue Charcoal Green Purple Red Varnish Yellow
Spirit Storage Unit Reward

Store a  Ghost-In-A-Jar **(x30)** in a  Spirit Storage Unit, increasing its capacity by **150%**.
May yield **1** item each.
The **Mystery Portal** is a non-solid foreground portal block which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2021 Day 2 on August 24, 2021.
##  Function
In addition to behaving like a typical Portal, a Mystery Portal will move the entering player to 1 of up to 5 destinations possible randomly.
