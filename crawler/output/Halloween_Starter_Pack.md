# Halloween Starter Pack
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Halloween Starter Pack** for **14.99 USD** in the Growtopia Store.
"Treat yourself with all the gems you could ever want and everything you need to give a scare this Halloween!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Halloween Starter Pack** is a Store-bought IAP which was added as part of the Halloween Week 2022 update and was only available on the first day.
The first incarnation of the Halloween Starter Pack contained the following:
  * Gems ×540,000
  * Dark King's Offering ×25
  * Dark Ticket ×5
  * Gift of Growganoth ×75
  * Carved Pumpkin Hat ×1
  * Carved Pumpkin Onesie ×1
