**Riding Silkworm - Aqua** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of the Pet Battles update on June 11, 2016. One can be obtained from consuming a Silkworm Saddle on an adult aqua Silkworm.
##  Function
You can move faster!

You are slow again.

When equipped, the **Riding Silkworm - Aqua** grants the _Speedy_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.
