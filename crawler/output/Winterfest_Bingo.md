# Winterfest Bingo

Winterfest Bingo is a feature which was added as part of WinterFest 2023 on December 19, 2023. This feature can be accessed through the store.
Players get a random Winterfest Bingo card which contains a 5x5 grid of WinterFest-related items. Players have to collect these items across Winterfest to get prizes. A player has the option to reset the card for 5 world locks. This will discard any progress made on the card, and replace all the prizes with new prizes.
##  Rewards
Upon getting all 5 items in a row or column, the player will be able to claim the prize displayed at the end of that row or column. The following prizes can be obtained:
  * Winter Gift (×100)
  * Lumber Axe (×20)
  * Winterfest Cracker (×1-50)
  * Deluxe Winterfest Cracker (×5-10)
  * Icy Heart Of Winter (×1)
  * Snowtopian (×1)
  * Mountain Boots (×1)
  * Rift Cape (×1)
  * Rift Wings (×1)
  * Infinity Aura (×1)
  * Infinity Crown (×1)
  * Fist of Infinity (×1)
  * Ice King's Crown (×1)
  * Snowglobe Head (×1)
  * Chilled Chucks (×1)
  * Snow Monarch's Mantle (×1)
  * Northern Lights Aura (×1)
  * Weather Machine - Holiday Haven (×1)
  * Winter Wishing Star (×1)
  * Snowglobe Staff (×1)
  * Wings of Infinity (×1)
