The **Fossilized Spirit Block** is an unsplicable solid foreground block which was added as part of Halloween Week 2020 on October 22, 2020.
##  Function
Much like typical Ghosts, the Fossilized Spirit Block can only be seen by players equipping certain kinds of clothes, such as the Spectral Goggles.
