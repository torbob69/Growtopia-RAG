# Easter Egg - Golden

A solid gold egg. Worth its weight in eggs!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data: Paint PreviewType — Foreground Block – Foreground — Aqua Blue Charcoal Green Purple Red Varnish Yellow
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: Full Collision
Hardness: **10 Hits** **8 Hits** – Restores after **8s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Paint Preview Aqua Blue Charcoal Green Purple Red Varnish Yellow
Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Golden Egg Shard - Top **(×1)** ,
Golden Egg Shard - Middle **(×20)** and
Golden Egg Shard - Bottom **(×50)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
Randomly Spawning

Randomly spawns at certain intervals in EGGRESCUE worlds (the more players are inside, the more will spawn).
This item only appears during Easter Week.
**Easter Egg - Golden** is an unsplicable solid foreground block which was added as part of Easter Week 2021 on April 1, 2021.
##  Function
When placed, Easter Egg - Golden serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
