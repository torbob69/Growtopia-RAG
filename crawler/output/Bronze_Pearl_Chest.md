# Bronze Pearl Chest

What might be inside? Open it and see!
Properties
This item is PUBLIC: Even if it's locked, anyone can smash it.
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **5 Hits** **4 Hits** – Restores after **3s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
NPC Trading

Buy 1 of this item from ** The Bartender** for  Pearls Currency **(x60)**.
"Welcome to my bar! Take a seat. I'll poor you a drink and show you my wares! If it's pearls you've got, I'll take them in exchange for one of these lovely pearl chests! What might be inside? It's a pearly mystery!"
The **Bronze Pearl Chest** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Beach Party 2023 on June 16, 2023.
##  Function
When consumed, the player will earn one of the following rewards:
  * Pearl Castle Stone (×2)
  * Pearl Castle Stone Background (×2)
  * Pearl Castle Turret (×2)
  * Pearl Floral Lei (×1)
  * Pearl Wallpaper (×2)
  * Mother of Pearl (×2)
  * Pearl Arch (x1)
  * Pearl Lamp (x1)
