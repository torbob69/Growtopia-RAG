# Growassic Park

Growtopia Gazette
> October 7th: Growassic Park is here!
> Become a paleontologist, or foolishly try to tame mother nature, in our new Paleontology update. More items than we’ve ever released in one update before: over 100!
> Keep your eyes peeled for fossils in the rocks in your world, they are tricky to spot!
> - @Seth & @Hamumu
The **Growassic Park** is an update that was added on October 7, 2016. It introduced fossils and other items related to prehistory, geology and paleontology.
##  New Additions
###  Paleontologist's Kit
  * The **Paleontoligist’s Kit** is an item pack that costs 20,000 Gems in the Store. It contains the tools needed to obtain fossils
