# Mike Hommel
Mike Hommel

| Born   | December 10, 1974 (age 51)
United States   |
Occupation: Founder of Hamumu Software
Partner: Solange Hommel (@Solorien)
Years active: 1998–present
**@Hamumu (Mike Hommel)** is one of Growtopia's developers alongside @Seth and @Ubisoft. Hamumu owns Hamumu Software and was Growtopia's art designer.
##  Trivia
  * He gave Phonecats the Phonecats Hat as a thank-you gift for drawing more people towards Growtopia in its early stages.
  * Hamumu's level is displayed as "?".
  * Hamumu's main world is HAMUMUTOWN.
  * Hamumu's wife is Solange Hommel. In Growtopia, Solange is known as @Solorien.
  * Hamumu is often associated with the Rainbow Hair, Tuxedo, Black Pants, and the Heroman Cape.
  * Most event worlds, such as Growch and Growganoth, are owned by @Hamumu.
