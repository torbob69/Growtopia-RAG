The **BBQ Bacon Burger** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Cooking update.
##  Function
Get 100 gems from every Villain you defeat.

Your stomach's rumbling.

When consumed, this item grants the _Food: Superhero Gems_ mod, which gives the player 100 gems for every villain the player defeats for the next 30 minutes.
##  Trivia
  * During Player Appreciation Week 2019, the BBQ Bacon Burger's effect was changed from giving 50 gems per villain defeated to giving 100 each.
