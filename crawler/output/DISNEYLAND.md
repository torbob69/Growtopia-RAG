# DISNEYLAND

Players will be redirected to **DISNEYLAND** if one enters a world with a name that includes a vulgar word or a slang. **DISNEYLAND** is locked by @Anulot with a Royal Lock, the world can be seen within a Black Room which contains two Dungeon Doors, one leads to the world START the other one leads to STOPDONTDROP. It also has three Exclamation Signs informing the player to use better names. However, outside the room has gems and items from Events scattered around the world except the room.@Hamumu blocked DISNEYLAND up with two Mushrooms on August 15, 2014. @Solorien also formally owns DISNEYLAND. Players who try to enter HELL will be redirected here to DISNEYLAND.
##  Trivia
  * The player will sometimes not enter DISNEYLAND when typing a specfic inapproriate name for a world on the menu, instead an error message saying "To _reduce confusion, that is not a valid world name. Try another_?"
