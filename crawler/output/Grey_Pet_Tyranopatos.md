The **Grey Pet Tyranopatos** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Growassic Park on October 7, 2016.
##  Function
When equipped, the Grey Pet Tyranopatos serves a purely cosmetic purpose and summons a Grey Pet Tyranopatos which follows the player around.
