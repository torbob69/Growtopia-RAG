# Teeny Devil Wings

Even as tiny as they are, these wings allow you to jump in mid-air!

The **Teeny Devil Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2015 2015 on October 29, 2015.
##  Function
You can jump in mid-air! It's like flying, but less.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Teeny Devil Wings** grants the _Double Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
##  Trivia
  * Upon consumption of 16 teeny wing fragments, a message will appear saying "_SQUISH! You built a pair of Teeny Devil Wings!_ ", and the Teeny Devil Wings will instantly be equipped.
  * Teeny Devil Wings has a recolored version:  Nightmare Teeny Devil Wings.
