Glittering secrets! But be careful! Don't tell anyone about it! If you do, it won't be hidden anymore - it'll just be regular treasure, and who wants that?

Treasure Hunting

Has a chance to be obtained from completing a Treasure Map.
The **Hidden Treasure** is an unsplicable solid treasure foreground block which was added in the Treasure Hunt Update. One can be found in a Treasure-blasted world.
##  Function
Up to 10 Map Fragments may drop when a Hidden Treasure is broken. It has a chance to drop a complete Treasure map, and a 5% chance of dropping a Stone Egg.
