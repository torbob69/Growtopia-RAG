The **Desperado Mustache** is an unsplicable face item which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2013 on May 3, 2013.
##  Function
When equipped, the Desperado Mustache serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The description of this item is a reference to the style of stock characters found in silent movies and earlier stage melodrama, wearing black clothing and a top hat, and featuring a handlebar mustache.
  * This item is worn by the  The Ringmaster along with these items:
    * Ringmaster Hat
    * Flat-top
    * Desperado Mustache
    * Ringmaster Suit
    * Black Pants
    * Patent Leather Shoes
    * Fistful Of Rings
