The **SLaminator's Boomerang** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of WinterFest 2018 on December 14, 2018.
##  Function
Slam!

It did not return!

When equipped, it grants the _Punch Range: Slaminator_ mod which allows the player to punch one tile further. It also replaces the player's normal punching effect with boomerang thrown from their hands and returns.
