# Mods/Super Pineapple Magic

< Mods
?

The Super Pineapple Magic has come to an end!

**Super Pineapple Magic** is a mod that occurs to a player who feeds a Super Pineapple to another player with a Pineapple Body (from eating 600 Pineapples).
When a player gets this mod, they will also receive two random items from the same loot table as the regular Pineapple Magic. While having this mod, the player is temporarily prevented from obtaining any more Pineapple Magic reward. The mod will end after a 20-minute waiting period.
This mod is visually identical to the regular Pineapple Magic mod, causing a pineapple to revolve around the player. Additionally, the player will be given an aura effect akin to the Golden Aura, minus the double-jump buff.
