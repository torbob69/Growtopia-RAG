The **Rock** is an unsplicable solid basic foreground block. Small seams of this block can be found naturally-generated in normal worlds.
##  Function
Fossils may generate on Rocks in worlds randomly, depending on world's popularity and activity. One can obtain a Fossil from one using a Rock Hammer and a Rock Chisel, or a Polished Fossil, by using a Fossil Brush on an unpicked Fossil.
A Rock will morph into a Heartstone when a Love Potion #8 is used on it, and has a 1% chance of turning into it if the Ruby Lock is used in a world, which can then be broken to drop Valentine's Week item(s) such as Angel Wings, Perfume, etc.
