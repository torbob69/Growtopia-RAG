In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Transmog Crystal **(×1)** ,
Blue Star Wallpaper **(×20)** and
Growboard **(×1)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **StarBoard** is an unsplicable feet item which was added on Player Appreciation Week 2014 Day 3 on August 10, 2014.
##  Function
You can move faster!

You are slow again.

When equipped, the **StarBoard** grants the _Speedy_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.
