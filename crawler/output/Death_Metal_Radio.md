The **Death Metal Radio** is a unsplicable toggleable animated foreground block which was introduced during Halloween Week 2017.
##  Function
<https://growtopiawiki.com/w/File:Death_metal_radio.ogg>The Death Metal Radio's music.
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Death Metal Radio can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Death Metal Radio will change its appearance, showing a 2-frame animation, and play a piece of music.
##  Trivia
  * In the game files, the Death Metal Radio's music is named death_metal_radio.
