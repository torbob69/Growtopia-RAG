The **Pegasus** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2017 on February 10, 2017.
##  Function
You can jump in mid-air! It's like flying, but less.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Pegasus** grants the _Double Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
### Pegasus Pursuit
During the Pegasus Pursuit event, equipping a Pegasus increases the player's chance by an unknown percentage to obtain a Pegasus Chest whilst fishing, performing surgeries, or completing startopia missions.
