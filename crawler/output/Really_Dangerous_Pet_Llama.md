"This Llama is ready for anything Growtopia throws at it! Armed with a silo of Growtech Missiles, experimental Growtech hardened steel armor and a rather snazzy helmet (Llama's own) this Llama is Dangerous with a capital D! Note: This Really Dangerous Pet Llama is UNTRADEABLE."

The **Really Dangerous Pet Llama** is an unsplicable and untradeable pet hand item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2020 Day 3.
##  Function
Armed to the back Llama Teeth!

No Longer REALLY dangerous!

When equipped, the **Really Dangerous Pet Llama** grants the _Punch Damage : Really Dangerous Pet Llama_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. It also replaces the player's punch effect into firing missiles.
##  Trivia
  * The Really Dangerous Pet Llama was one of the three items obtainable for purchase with Growtokens added on Player Appreciation Week 2020 Day 3, alongside the Growboy and Tactical Stealth Espionage Device.
  * When Player Appreciation Week 2020 Day 4 arrived, the pet textures of the Really Dangerous Pet Llama went missing. The textures of the inventory icon and equipped clothing were still available, however.
    * This issue was patched several hours thereafter.
