##  Function
Let us be glad!

No one mourns the wicked!

When equipped, the **Bubble Wand** grants the _Punch Range: Bubble Wand_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further.
##  Trivia
  * The Bubble Wand's equip and unequip messages are references to a fictional character called Glinda Upland from the theatrical play and movie Wicked.
