# Fashion Pack
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Fashion Pack** for **6,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"The hottest new looks for the season are here now with 3 random Fashion Clothing (dress, shoes or purse), Jade Blocks to pose on, and a Rare Spotlight to shine on your fabulousness."
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Fashion Pack** is an item pack which was introduced on May 15, 2015.
##  Items
The pack includes:
  * Spotlight x1
  * Jade Block x3

Three random items of a random color, with exception to the Tied-up Buns, the Silver-framed Sunglasses and the Hot Cross Bun Beret from the following list can also be obtained from the pack:
  * Fashionable Dress
  * Stylin' Dress
  * Fashion Purse
  * High Heels
  * Tied-up Buns x1
  * Silver-framed Sunglasses x1
  * Hot Cross Bun Beret x1 (available during Easter Week)

##  Trivia
  * The cost of the pack was previously set at 4,000 Gems, but was later changed to 6,000 Gems.
  * As of Player Appreciation Week 2020, Tied-up Buns can be obtained from purchasing the pack.
  * As of Player Appreciation Week 2021, Silver-framed Sunglasses can be obtained from purchasing the pack.
  * As of  Easter Week 2022, Hot Cross Bun Beret can be obtained from purchasing the pack.
