The **Tropical Wrench Decoration** is a wrench decoration which was added as part of the June Season on June 2, 2025.
##  Function
When equipped through the 'Wrench Customization' tab when wrenching oneself, the Tropical Wrench Decoration adds dark blue spikes surrounding the player's wrench connected to their name.
