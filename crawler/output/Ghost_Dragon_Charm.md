# Ghost Dragon Charm

A dragon pulled from the Other Side! Top Dragonologists have been debating whether this creature is actually the ghost of a single dragon or a conjoined ectoplasmic hive composed of many smaller ghosts working together, but all of them can agree that it keeps eating anyone who asks about it, so it's best not to worry.

The **Ghost Dragon Charm** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of the Dragon Invasion update on February 4, 2019.
##  Function
Ghastly draconic wisdom

Your ghastly wisdom is no more.

When equipped, the **Ghost Dragon Charm** grants the _XP Buff: Ghost Busting_ mod, which allows the player to gain an extra 200 XP when catching a ghost.
