# Mentorship & Referral (update)

Growtopia Gazette
> April 9th: Easter Week!
> Dear Growtopians!
> Something new has arrived! You can now share a game-generated link on your social media accounts to invite your friends to play the game and receive rewards together!
> Check out the details in our joint announcement with ThePsyborg's Dev Diary here.
> - The Growtopia Team
The **Mentorship & Referral** update is a monthly update which was added alongside Easter Week 2020 on April 9, 2020. It introduced a new way of training apprentices by using invite links to send to friends on various social media platforms or inviting a player with Level 1-3.
##  Items
Item: RecipeWacky Rocket Sled: 1 can be claimed from having 500 level 20 apprentices.
Dumb Meet the DEVS: Unobtainable; cannot be obtained by regular means.
Dumb Mentor Title: Unobtainable; cannot be obtained by regular means.
Dumb Player Tribute: Unobtainable; cannot be obtained by regular means.
