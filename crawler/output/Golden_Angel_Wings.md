The **Golden Angel Wings** is an unsplicable back item that was added as part of Valentine's Week 2014 on February 10, 2014.
##  Function
You can jump in mid-air! It's like flying, but less.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Golden Angel Wings** grants the _Double Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
Step 16 of the  Quest Of The Heavens requires the player to deliver 3 of this item.
