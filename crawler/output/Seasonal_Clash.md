# Seasonal Clash

This is a **stub** page.
Please add more information to this page.
A Seasonal Clash is a cooperative and solo event that runs for four months. During a Seasonal Clash, various Grow Events occur which allow players to earn points by performing certain tasks while equipping appropriate tools. Players can contribute to their own leaderboard or their Guild's leaderboard in order to earn Seasonal Tokens and claim special prizes.
##  Seasonal Clash Cycle
There are a total of three Seasonal Clashes:
  * Summer Clash - July to October
  * Winter Clash - November to February
  * Spring Clash - March to June

Each year a new "clash series" occurs where rewards and events may be changed based on player feedback. The series that has occurred so far has been:
  * Series 1 - 1 July 2018 to 1 July 2019
    * Summer Clash 2018
    * Winter Clash 2018
    * Spring Clash 2019
  * Series 2 - 1 July 2019 to 1 July 2020
    * Summer Clash 2019
    * Winter Clash 2019
    * Spring Clash 2019

  * Series 3 - 1 July 2020 to 1 July 2021
  * Series 4 - 1 July 2021 to 1 July 2022
  * Series 5 - 1 July 2022 to 1 July 2023

##  Rewards

Each Grow Event has its own personal rewards. By earning a certain number of points, a maximum of 20 personal rewards can be claimed. Seasonal tokens are obtainable from each reward and special chests are obtainable from every 5th reward. Personal Event leaderboards stay up for the entire month that the Grow Event is running for and players have three days from the end of the month to claim their rewards.
