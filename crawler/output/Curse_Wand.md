Curse Wand

Given exclusively to moderators and cannot be obtained by regular players by normal means, except when a moderator gives away or sells theirs.
The **Curse Wand** is an unobtainable consumable which has been added since Growtopia's launch.
##  Function
You've been cursed to (the world) hell!

You are no longer bound to the netherworld, you're free to go.

When consumed on a player, the player will receive the _Curse_ mod and get sent to the world HELL for 10 minutes.
When a player gets cursed, their skin color becomes grey and they will lack any facial feature other than the nose, similarly to the No-Face item.
##  Trivia
  * The description of this item is a reference to the AC/DC song "_Highway to Hell._"
  * As of version 1.50, the avatars of cursed players only appeared as they usually do, except with a grey color overlay.
    * This change had since been reverted by the developers.
  * The Curse Wand is the rarest of the exclusive wands acquirable by players; there are very few players who have managed to own this item via trading.
  * Upon their introduction in December 2012, the Wands' sprite was offset by a pixel up and to the right. This was fixed upon the release of the game in 2013.
