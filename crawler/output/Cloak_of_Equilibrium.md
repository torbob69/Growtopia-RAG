# Cloak of Equilibrium

A perfectly balanced cloak! Half infused with power by the sun and half by the moon!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be transmuted.
Data

Type: Back – Clothes
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: Full Collision
Hardness: **0 Hits** **0 Hits** – Restores after **0s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Ultimate Corruption

Collect corrupted souls by sacrificing items with a total rarity of at least 48,000 or higher in the different Sacrificial Wells found all around the TOMBOFGROWGANOTH to get this tier.
May drop in sets of **1** each.
The **Cloak of Equilibrium** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Halloween Week/2020 on October 22, 2020.
##  Function
Onwards to the sun and... and moon!

All outta balance!!

When equipped, the **Cloak of Equilibrium** grants the _Double Jump: Cloak of Equilibrium_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
