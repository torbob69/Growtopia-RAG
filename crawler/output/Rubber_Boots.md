# Rubber Boots

These are tight-fitting boots made of a natural rubber that protects from slime while still allowing your feet to breathe.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be transmuted.

Type: Feet – Clothes

Store a  Ghost-In-A-Jar **(x21)** in a  Spirit Storage Unit, increasing its capacity by **105%**.
May yield **1** item each.
The **Rubber Boots** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of the Battlers Of the Otherworldly update on July 15, 2016.
##  Function
When equipped, the **Rubber Boots** grants the _Ghost Immunity_ mod, which allows the player to be immune from  regular Ghosts.
##  Trivia
  * There are several items in _Growtopia_ which grant immunity against certain types of ghosts.
    * Rubber Boots works against  regular Ghosts.
    * Foil Hat and  Alien Mind Protector work against  Mind Ghosts.
    * Ghostking's Glory works against all ghost variants.
