This crystal can be combined with 200 Candy Hearts and any Valentine's Clothing in a Laboratory or E-Z Cook Oven to infuse the clothing with golden magic!

The **Golden Heart Crystal** is an unsplicable component that was added as part of Valentine's Week 2014 on February 10, 2014.
##  Function
The Golden Heart Crystal can be combined in any Chemical Combiner with 200 Candy Hearts and a Valentine's Week clothing item to make the golden counterpart of the item, with the exception for Datemaster's Heart Locket and Stained Glass Heartwings.
Step 14 of the  Quest For Honor requires the player to deliver 3 of this item.
