The **Shamrock Shuriken** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2021 on March 11, 2021.
##  Function
Rock the Shamrocks!

All rocked out!

When equipped, the **Shamrock Shuriken** grants the _Punch Damage : Shamrock Shuriken_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster.
The  Myths and Legends : The Eye of the Heavens mission requires 5 of this item.
