# Star Trek Prodigy

Growtopia Gazette
> June 27th: Star Trek Prodigy!
> Star Trek Prodigy - An unknown object is travelling through space at warp speed towards the two-dimensional universe of Growtopia! But no need for Phasers Growtopians, they mean no harm! The U.S.S. Protostar and her crew plan to boldly go where no one has gone before!

**Star Trek Prodigy** is a limited-time event which started on June 27, 2022. As its name suggests, this event is a crossover with the _Star Trek: Prodigy_ animated television series.

  * Each Star Tool is now categorized as a consumable, instead of a component.

###  In-App Purchases
  * The Star Trek Starter Pack is available in the Growtopia Store for $14.99 throughout the event.
  * Several in-app purchases came with bonus  Starfleet Officer's Supply Boxes when purchased throughout the event.
    * It's Rainin' Gems ($9.99 for 350,000 gems, 1 Megaphone, and 2 Growtokens) came with 2 bonus supply box.
    * Gem Bounty ($29.99 for 1,100,000 gems, 3 Megaphones, and 6 Growtokens) came with 6 supply box.
    * Gem Abundance ($49.99 for 1,850,000 gems, 5 Megaphones, and 10 Growtokens) came with 12 supply box.

##  "Help The Crew" Feature
### Questlines

Help the crew of Protostar!

The trip through the wormhole to the two-Dimensional Universe of Growtopia has taken its toll on the Protostar. Help the crew get the Protostar back up and running so they can return to their own dimension.
There are five questlines available throughout the event, accessed through the Growtopia Store. Each questline comprises 10 tasks; finishing a single task will unlock the next task in the list. Players may opt to skip a task for 10 World Locks; however, there is a waiting time of one hour before they will be able to skip another quest. Finishing a questline allows the player to claim a Starfleet Officer's Supply Box.

### Protostar Missions

Help the crew of Protostar!

With the Protostar up and running we can now hunt for an anomaly that would allow us to create another wormhole like the one that brought them here. There is talk of one at the very edge of the galaxy that a small group of scientists have been researching. Maybe it can get the crew back home.

The Protostar repairs are ready...
Once all five questlines have been completed, a repair button will appear on the "Help the Crew" screen. When pressed, a menu will prompt the player to choose a world name — the world will then be generated as a Protostar Starship. A Protostar Starship Blast can also be purchased anytime during the event as part of the Star Trek Starter Pack, which costs $14.99.
Inside a Protostar Starship, players are able to complete interactive Startopia missions using Star Tools. Every mission completed awards the player a Starfleet Cadet's Supply Box, which can be consumed for a random prize revolving around _Star Trek: Prodigy_. Every time the player reaches 550,000 star miles (about 20 missions), they will reach The Anomaly, a special mission with a Starfleet Officer's Supply Box as its reward.
For a step-by-step guide on how to beat Protostar missions, please visit this page.
