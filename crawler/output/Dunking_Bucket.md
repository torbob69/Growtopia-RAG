The **Dunking Bucket** is a non-solid foreground block introduced in the Brr-lloon Warz 2018 update on December 7, 2018.
##  Function
When placed, if a player goes under the _Dunking Bucket_ , they will get the _Soaked_ mod for a short period of time.
