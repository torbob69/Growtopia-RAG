The **Skeletal Horsie** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2013 on October 28, 2013.
##  Function
You can move faster!

You are slow again.

When equipped, the **Skeletal Horsie** grants the _Speedy_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.
