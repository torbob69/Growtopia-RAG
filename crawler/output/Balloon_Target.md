The **Balloon Target** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Brr-lloon Warz 2019.
##  Function
During Balloon Warz, 20-30 Balloon Target spawn every three minutes for 30 seconds in the BALLOONWARZ worlds as part of the _Balloon Hunt_ Special Event. Hitting them with war balloons gives equal points for each one.
