# Funhouse Mirror

These mirrors make you look entirely wacky!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.

Carnival Prize

Won as a rare prize in **Mirror Maze** in the Carnival.
The **Funhouse Mirror** is an unsplicable background block which was added as part of The Carnival update on October 13, 2014.
##  Function
When a player stands in front of a Funhouse Mirror, it will visually stretch the player's character, making them either tall and skinny or short and stubby.
