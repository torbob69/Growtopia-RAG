# Vile Vial - Lupus

Filled with the howling madness at the heart of every Wolf World, this deadly vial will afflict ANYONE it touches with Lupus! (Except if they already have another malady, of course - these diseases are very polite in that regard)
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: Full Collision
Hardness: **20 Hits** **15 Hits** – Restores after **8s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Complete WOLFWORLD

Possible bonus reward from finishing a WOLFWORLD.
The **Vile Vial - Lupus** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Blocktor's Orders! on June 13, 2019.
##  Function
You've been infected with lupus!

You no longer have lupus!

When consumed, the player will receive the _Lupus_ malady mod for 12 hours, which can be cured through a successful surgery. Should the malady get cured, both players—the surgeon and patient—will receive a prize.
##  Trivia
  * On August 4, 2022, the amount of gems received from recycling a Vile Vial - Lupus was changed from 250 to 0.
