The **Pearls Currency** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Beach Party 2022 on June 24, 2022.
##  Function
The Pearls Currency can be used to purchase Bronze Pearl Chest for 60 Pearls Currency, Pearl Chest for 300 Pearls Currency and Golden Pearl Chest for 2,000 Pearls Currency from The Bartender.
