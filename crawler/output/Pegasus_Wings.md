The **Pegasus Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as the Item Of The Month of July 2020.
##  Function
Take flight and hold tight!

Woah there nelly, back to earth!

When equipped, the **Pegasus Wings** grants the _Double-Jump: Pegasus Wings!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
### Pegasus Pursuit
During the Pegasus Pursuit event, equipping a Pegasus Wings increases the player's chance by an unknown percentage to obtain a Pegasus Chest whilst fishing, performing surgeries, or completing startopia missions.
