# Pineapple Fish Leash

This sweet (and slightly sour) fish has flopped out of the tropical waters it calls home and onto a leash! Take him out for your next luau!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.

Fish Training

1 can be obtained from training a(n)  Pineapple Fish.
The **Pineapple Fish Leash** is an unsplicable pet hand item which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2018 on June 1, 2018.
##  Function
When equipped, a Pineapple Fish pet appears and follows the player around. The Pineapple Fish leash serves a purely cosmetic purpose. This pet can't be trained as a battle pet.
