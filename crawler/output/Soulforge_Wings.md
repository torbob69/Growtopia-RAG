The **Soulforge Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Wonder Week 2023 Day 5 on October 13, 2023.
##  Function
Light 'em up!!

Wings clipped!

When equipped, the **Soulforge Wings** grants the _Lightsource + Double-Jump: Soulforge Wings_ mod, which allows the player to emit light in dark areas and jump a second time in mid-air.
