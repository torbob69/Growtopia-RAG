The **Slopestyle Bike** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of UbiWeek 2023 Day 1 on February 24, 2023.
##  Function
I am slow!

I am speed!

When equipped, the **Slopestyle Bike** grants the _Speedy & Double Jump : Slopestyle Bike_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and jump a second time in mid-air.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Riders Republic set, which consists of the following items:

    * Iroquois Helmet (Hat)

    * Alveolar Crossed Lens (Face)

    * Slopestyle Bike (Feet)
