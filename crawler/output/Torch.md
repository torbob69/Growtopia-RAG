The **Torch** is a splicable non-solid toggleable animated foreground block which was added alongside the Dungeon Pack on April 19, 2013.
##  Function
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Torch can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Torch will change its appearance, showing it being lit up. As a light source, a Torch emits light in dark areas.
