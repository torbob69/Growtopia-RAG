# Ghost Block

A slimy ghost block. Careful now, what did your mother say about stepping on ghosts? You step on a ghost and you get SLIMED! So the choice is yours. Really!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.

The **Ghost Block** is an unsplicable solid foreground block that was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2020 Day 5.
##  Function
When a player walks on a Ghost Block, they will be granted the _Slimed!_ mod for 6 seconds. This does not count as a variant of ghost so players are still able to get the _Slimed!_ mod even while wearing the Ghostking's Glory.
