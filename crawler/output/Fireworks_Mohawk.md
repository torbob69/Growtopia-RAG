The **Fireworks Mohawk** is an unsplicable hair item which was added as part of Lunar New Year 2024 on February 1, 2024.
##  Function
Boom boom boom!

Boo boo boo!

When equipped, the **Fireworks Mohawk** grants the _Fireproof + Light Source : Fireworks Mohawk_ mod, which allows the player to take 50% less damage from lava pain blocks and emit light in dark areas.
