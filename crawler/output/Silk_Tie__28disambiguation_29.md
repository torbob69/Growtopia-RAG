# Silk Tie (disambiguation)

This is a **disambiguation** page.
There are more than one subjects in the **Growtopia Wiki** using this name.
**Silk Ties** are chest items introduced during the Silkworm Update. They are all made through sewing.
They come in red, yellow, black, blue and grey. There are no green, purple, white or aqua Silk Ties.
  * Silk Tie - Red
  * Silk Tie - Yellow
  * Silk Tie - Black
  * Silk Tie - Blue
  * Silk Tie - Grey
