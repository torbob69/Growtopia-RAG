The **Coffee** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Unicorn Madness update on May 30, 2014.
##  Function
You are full of caffeine!

Ugh. Caffeine crash.

When consumed, Coffee grants the _Caffeinated_ mod, which allows the player to run faster on land and emit stink line particles, making the player do the _:P_ emote. This mod lasts for 5 seconds.

### Silkworm Food     _Main article:Guide:Silkworm Care_
This consumable can be fed to a Silkworm to give it a white color, thus allowing it to produce 1–10  Silk Thread - White per harvest.
