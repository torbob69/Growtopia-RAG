The **Valentine's Card** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2018.
##  Function
Waiting on Delivery!

Card Delivered!

When consumed during Valentine's Week, one Valentine's Dust is given to the player, while another random user online is given one Golden Booty Chest or Cherry Blossom Pinwheel. Five Valentine's Dusts will additionally spawn randomly in the chosen user's current world. It also grants the _Card Sent: Playing Cupid_ mod, which restricts the player to send more cards for 10 seconds. The Valentine's Card cannot be consumed outside of the event.
