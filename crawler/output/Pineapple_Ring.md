The **Pineapple Ring** is an unsplicable solid slippery foreground block which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2016 on June 3, 2016.
##  Function
Being a slippery block, walking on a Pineapple Ring causes the player to slide. Upon walking over the block, it starts to rotate in the direction the player walks, similar to the Funtime Block.
