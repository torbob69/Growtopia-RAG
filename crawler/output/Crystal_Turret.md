The **Crystal Turret** is an unsplicable toggleable multi-framed animated block which was added as part of the Dungeons update on October 23, 2025.
##  Function
Found in a Crystal Boss dungeons boss room, the **Crystal Turret** shoots purple fireballs at players, dealing damage from a distance.
