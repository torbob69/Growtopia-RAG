The **Mythic Tiger Crown** is a hat item which was added as part of Lunar New Year 2022 on February 1, 2022.
##  Function
You've got the power!

You've lost the power!

When equipped, the **Mythic Tiger Crown** grants the _Punch Damage + Punch Power: Mythic Tiger Crown_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and knock back other players further. Additionally, when punching, a white tiger sprite appears from the player's body and pulses.
