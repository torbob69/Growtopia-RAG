The **Darkest Skull Parasol** is an unspliceable hand item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2020 on October 22, 2020.
##  Function
Skulls ignite and send me skyward!

What skullduggery is this!

When equipped, the **Darkest Skull Parasol** grants the _Double Jump & Slowfall : Darkest Skull Parasol_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
