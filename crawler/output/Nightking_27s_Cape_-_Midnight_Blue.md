**Nightking's Cape - Midnight Blue** is an unsplicable back item which was added on July 1, 2019. The item, along with the Nightking's Cape - Black, Dark Purple, and Dark Red were possible drops from purchasing July 2019's Item Of The Month for 200,000 gems in the Store.
##  Function
Some seriously helpful bats!

Bats are chillin'

When equipped, the **Nightking's Cape - Midnight Blue** grants the _Double Jump: Batastic!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
