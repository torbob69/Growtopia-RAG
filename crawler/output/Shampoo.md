The **Shampoo** is a splicable consumable which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2016 Day 2 on August 6, 2016.
##  Function
Shampoo is used to remove one layer of Black, Red, Blue, or Green Hair Dye from a player. A player can only consume a Shampoo while standing in front of a Bathtub.
