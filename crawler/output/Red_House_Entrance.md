The **Red House Entrance** is an unsplicable entrance foreground block which was added as part of the Stuff! update.
##  Function
Being an entrance, a Red House Entrance allows players with access to walk through it, or, when its property is changed, allowing any player to walk through it. When placed in a public area, it is impossible to be closed for anyone.
