The **Space** is an unobtainable and unbreakable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Startopia update on July 1, 2018.
##  Function
**Space** is used at the bottom of starship worlds to prevent players from falling off.
##  Trivia
  * The Space is one of the two invisible blocks in _Growtopia_ , the second is Inviso-Block.
  * The Dimension Block looks almost the same with this item, except that it has markings which appear every five seconds.
