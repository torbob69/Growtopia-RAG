The **Fists of Iron** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Lunar New Year 2026 on February 19, 2026.
##  Function
Powered up!

Powered down!

When equipped, the **Fists of Iron** grants the _Light Source + Punch Damage: Fists of Iron_ mod, which allows the player to emit light in dark areas and break blocks faster.
##  Trivia
  * This item is a reference to the superhero mantle Iron Fist from Marvel Comics.
