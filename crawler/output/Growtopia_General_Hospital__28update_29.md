# Growtopia General Hospital (update)

Growtopia Gazette
> Nov 22nd: Growtopia General Hospital! - Use the new Surgical Tools to save lives and win fabulous prizes!
> NEW IN STORE: Surgical Kit - Surgical Tools and more!
> NEW IN STORE: Ambulance - Ride to the rescue!
> NEW IN STORE: Punch Jammer - Stop people from punching in your races and quizzes!
**Growtopia General Hospital** is an update which was added on November 22, 2013. It introduced surgery, a feature that allows players to perform various types of surgeries on other players using tools available in a new store pack.

##  Other Changes
### Surgical Kit
  * Added Surgical Kit, which could be purchased for 8,000 Gems and included the following items:
    * Heart Monitor ×1
    * Hospital Bed ×1
    * Surgical Anesthetic ×10
    * Surgical Antibiotics ×10
    * Surgical Antiseptic ×10
    * Surgical Scalpel ×10
    * Surgical Splint ×10
    * Surgical Sponge ×10
    * Surgical Stitches ×10
