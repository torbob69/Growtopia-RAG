Dungeons

It can be found naturally generated in Dungeon worlds.

The **Conveyor Belt** is an unsplicable foreground block which was added as part of the Dungeons update on October 23, 2025.
##  Trivia
  * Originally, the Conveyor Belt was unobtainable upon release, only being seen in Dungeons. However, as of Player Appreciation Week 2026, it can now be obtained from the Player Appreciation Week Bingo Card.

##  Function
As a conveyor belt, the Conveyor Belt moves players standing on it in the direction it faces.
