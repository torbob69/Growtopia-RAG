Purchase the **Ambulance** for **25,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"Rush to the scene of an accident while lawyers chase you in this speedy rescue vehicle. Not available any other way!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Ambulance** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of the Growtopia General Hospital update on November 22, 2013.
##  Function
You can move faster!

You are slow again.

When equipped, the **Ambulance** grants the _Speedy_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land. It plays a short siren sound once while the lights at the back of the ambulance blink blue and red alternately, emitting smokes at the back.
##  Trivia
  * The Ambulance is one of the two clothes that will play a siren sound when the player equips the item, the second item is Fire Truck.
