**Sheet Music: Spooky** is an unsplicable Sheet Music background block which was added as part of Halloween Week 2016. It is obtainable from sacrificing any Sheet Music (except Sheet Music: Blank) into the maw of GROWGANOTH.
##  Function
Sheet Music: Spooky is a sheet music that plays a Halloween-related sound, depending on its position vertically.
