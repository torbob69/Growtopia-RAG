# Marshmallow

Bouncy and delicious. Hold Jump as you land for maximum bounce.

Break a  Bunny Egg at 25-29% completion. 7 of this item is given out each time.
Bunny Eggs may explode randomly when their sizes are above 100%.
The **Marshmallow** is an unsplicable solid trampoline foreground block which was added as part of Easter Week 2017.
##  Function
Jumping on a trampoline block such as a Marshmallow will cause the player to bounce, and by holding the jump button, the player can jump even higher. The _High Jump_ mod also boosts one's jump height on a trampoline block.

##  Trivia
  * The Marshmallow is based on a real-life confectionery with the same name, which is typically made from sugar, water and gelatin whipped to a solid-but-soft consistency.
  * The association between marshmallows and Easter seems to be caused by the popularity of Peeps, a brand of confectionery historically marketed during Eastertide.[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Marshmallow#cite_note-1)
    * The Marshmallow Basket item is a direct reference to Peeps itself, with the chick-esque pet being based on the marshmallow's shape.
  * As part of Player Appreciation Week 2019 Day 2, the quantity of Marshmallows earned from a Bunny Egg was increased from 1 to 7.
