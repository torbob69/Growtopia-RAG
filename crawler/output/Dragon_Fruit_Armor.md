The **Dragon Fruit Armor** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2026 on June 11, 2026.
##  Function
Come get me!

You got me!

When equipped, the **Dragon Fruit Armor** grants the _Double Jump + Light Source: Dragon Fruit Armor_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and emit light in dark areas.
