The **Mariachi Guitar** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2014 on May 2, 2014.
##  Function
When the player enters the command /dance or while they are standing still, a music plays while the player performs a special animation of themself playing the guitar.

##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the mariachi set, which consists of the following items:

    * Mariachi Hat (hat)

    * Mariachi Suit (shirt)

    * Mariachi Pants (pants)

    * Mariachi Guitar (hand)
  * The  Mariachi Bobble Head can be seen wearing the full mariachi set.
  * Wearing this item alongside the Fiesta Sombrero will award the player with the achievement La Vida De La Fiesta.
