##  Function

##  Trivia
  * Both the Red Coat and Red Riding Hood are a reference to the clothes worn by the titular main character of _Little Red Riding Hood_ , a popular European fairy tale.
  * As part of the Quality of Life Changes update on May 20, 2022, the **Red Coat** was added for sale in the Sales-Man under Wolfworld items for 150 Wolf Tickets. This was later removed from the Sales-Man 30 minutes later because there were multiple complaints of the price being inconsistent.
