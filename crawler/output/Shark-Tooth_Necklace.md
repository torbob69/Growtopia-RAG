The **Shark-Tooth Necklace** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of SummerFest 2016 on June 30, 2016.
##  Function
When equipped, the Shark-Tooth Necklace serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * In real life, shark teeth are often the only part of a shark to be fossilized,[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Shark-Tooth_Necklace#cite_note-1) thus making them an attractive material for accessories considering they won't deteriorate over time.
    * According to Hawaiian and Polynesian myths, wearing a shark tooth while in the ocean will prevent its wearer from shark bites. Nowadays, it is still common for surfers to wear shark-tooth necklaces, both as a fashion accessory and a talisman.[[2]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Shark-Tooth_Necklace#cite_note-2)
