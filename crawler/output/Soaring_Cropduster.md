The **Soaring Cropduster** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2024 on September 12, 2024.
##  Function
To infinity and beyond!

Mayday! Mayday!

When equipped, the **Soaring Cropduster** grants the _Speedy + High Jump : Soaring Cropduster_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and jump one tile higher.
