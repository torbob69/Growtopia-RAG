# Year of the Rabbit Mask

The rabbit is the fourth of the zodiac animals. Limited only to the Year of the Rabbit! Myth has it, that the order of the Zodiac Animals, was determined by a race. The rabbit placed fourth thanks to its ability to hop on stepping stone at the river!!

The **Year of the Rabbit Mask** is an unsplicable face item which was added as part of Lunar New Year 2023.
##  Function
When equipped, the Year of the Rabbit Mask grants the _Year of the Rabbit Mask: Sparkles_ mod, which emits golden sparkles from the player.
