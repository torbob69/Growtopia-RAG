The **Crown of Seasons** is an unsplicable hat item that was added on March 26, 2021.
##  Function
Seasons... I choose you!

Season finale!

When equipped, the **Crown of Seasons** grants the _Double-Jump: Crown of Seasons_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
The Crown of Seasons features special customizable functions. The player can set the aura season and trail season by wrenching oneself. The player can choose between Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter, or none for the aura or trail's effect separately.
