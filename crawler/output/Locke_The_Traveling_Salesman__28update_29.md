# Locke The Traveling Salesman (update)

Growtopia Gazette
> 22 March 2015: Today, Growtopia is being paid a visit by Locke The Traveling Salesman! He comes once a day a month to hawk his fabulous wares, though this time he will stick around a day and a half to introduce himself. - @Seth and @Hamumu

The **Locke The Traveling Salesman** update is a major update which was introduced on March 22, 2015. It introduced Locke The Salesman and the Clothing Compactor into the game.

##  Trivia
  * The Locke The Traveling Salesman update was mainly intended to combat the World Lock inflation.
  * This update introduced a way for smaller locks to be traded for bigger locks.
    * 5 Small Lock can be traded with Locke for a Big Lock.
    * 3 Big Lock can be traded with Locke for a Huge Lock.
    * 5 Huge Lock can be traded with Locke for a World Lock.
