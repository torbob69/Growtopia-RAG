# Festering Fish Guts Taco

Why. It's like you took all that's awful about fish guts - everything - and then jammed them into a taco to add a little crunch to the squish.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **4 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **5s** of inactivity

**1h**
Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Festering Urchin & Poop Pie **(×1)** ,
Disgusting Mess **(×3)** and
Water Bucket **(×10)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Festering Fish Guts Taco** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Gross-Out update on May 1, 2019.
