# Fish Mount (block category)

**Fish Mounts** are non-solid foreground blocks which can display perfect or massive Fishes as well as some other items, these items are:
  * Battle Trout
  * Cuddly Piranha
  * Great White Shark
  * Jellyfish
  * Puffer Fish Block

##  List of Fish Mounts
Items: Special FunctionFancy Fish Wall Mount: Displays all Fishes and special items aside Crabs.
Fish Wall Mount: Displays all Fishes and special items aside Crabs.
Crab Wall Mount: Displays Crabs only.
