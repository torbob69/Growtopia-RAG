# Trampoline Block (block category)

This is the page for **Trampoline Block (block category)**.
You may be looking for **Bouncy Block (block category)** instead.
A **trampoline block** is a type of foreground block which causes the player to bounce, and by holding the jump button, the player can jump even higher. The _High Jump_ mod also boosts one's jump height on a trampoline block.
