The **Bunny Egg** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Easter Week 2013 on March 30, 2013, while the **Magic Egg** is the Bunny Egg's seed.
##  Function
### Magic Egg
A Magic Egg works like a seed, which can be spliced with another eligible seed to grow particular trees. Unlike other seeds, however, the Magic Egg needs to be planted after the other seed to prevent it from becoming a Bunny Egg instead.

### Bunny Egg
A Bunny Egg is created when a Magic Egg is placed or dropped on an empty tile. More Magic Eggs can be placed on it to increase its size percentage by 0.1%. Bunny Eggs can drop exclusive items when broken at specific percentages. Breaking one at the wrong percentage will drop a random item splicable with Magic Eggs instead.

##  Trivia
  * The description of the Magic Egg was "_Think of it as a seed, only magic, and you can't plant it. You can splice it though! But with what?_ " until Easter Week 2014.
  * During the first few hours of Easter Week 2018, players were given Bunny Eggs instead of Magic Eggs from the EGGRESCUE mini-game. The Bunny Eggs were later changed into Magic Eggs.
  * Prior to Easter Week 2021, players only had a chance to obtain 1 Magic Egg from EGGRESCUE at a time.
