The **Augmented Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as December 2024's Item Of The Season on December 1st, 2024.
##  Function
Goodbye, gravity!

I guess I'll walk then.

When equipped, the **Augmented Wings** grants the _Double Jump + Light Source : Augmented Wings_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and emit light in dark areas.
