The **Stone Gargoyle Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of the Stuff! update on October 1, 2018.
##  Function
Those are some evil-lookin' wings!

Time has worn your wings to dust!

When equipped, the **Stone Gargoyle Wings** grants the _Double Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
