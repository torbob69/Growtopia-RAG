You bear the wings of a true savior, a surgeon without peer who has saved countless lives in their pursuit of the greater good. All who can hear the beat of these magnificently medicinal wings will know that their salvation has arrived.

Earned for joining the Angels of Mercy. _To earn this**achievement** , players must cure a vicious malady while equipping a Doctor's Blingin' Booties, Doctor's Blingin' Cap, Doctor's Blingin' Cross, Doctor's Blingin' Mask, Doctor's Blingin' Pants, and Doctor's Blingin' Top provided the player has already successfully performed at least 2,000 surgeries_.
The **Angel of Mercy's Wings** is an unsplicable and untradable back item which was added as part of the Blocktor's Orders! update.
##  Function
Mercy from above!

Merciful no more!

When equipped, the **Angel of Mercy's Wings** grants the _Double Jump and Slowfall: Angel of Mercy!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
##  Dr. Title
To obtain the Dr. Title, the player needs to complete 3,000 surgeries while wearing the Angel of Mercy's Wings (progress is displayed in the description of the wings, for every surgery done, it counts as an Angelic Healing). This will add "Dr." before the player's name and changing the player's name's color to red which can be toggled on/off by wrenching oneself and tapping on 'Title'.
Surgeries performed before the release of the Blocktor's Orders! update will **not** be counted.
##  Trivia
  * Performing surgery towards Angel of Mercy's Wings can only be counted by successfully doing a surgery on another player or a Surg-E (Train-Es don't count).
  * The player can obtain multiple Angel of Mercy's Wings by simply wearing the full Blingin' set and curing another vicious malady, after obtaining the wings.
  * Obtaining Angel of Mercy's Wings will consume the full Blingin' set.
