##  Function
A heavenly touch revives you.

No more grapefruit and health drinks, back to that unhealthy diet of yours.

When equipped, it grants the _Quick Recover: Healing Tome_ mod which reduces the cooldown for the Malpractice and Recovery mods by 25%.
