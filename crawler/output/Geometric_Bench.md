# Geometric Bench

Aliens have cooler versions of everything... including benches!

Consumption

Consume a(n)  Alien Landing Pod **(x1)**.
May yield **2** item each.

The **Geometric Bench** is an unsplicable non-solid seat foreground block which was added as part of the Aliens update on November 14, 2021.
##  Function
When a player stands in front of a seat such as the Geometric Bench for a few seconds, the player will automatically be seen "sitting" on it.
##  Trivia
  * As part of the Aliens update on January 27, 2023, the quantity of Geometric Bench obtained from consuming an Alien Landing Pod was increased from 1 to 2.
