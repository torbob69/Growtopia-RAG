# Apprentice Ticket - Level 20

A sign of your progress as an apprentice! Keep going for bigger and better rewards!
Properties
This item cannot be dropped or traded.
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **3 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **4s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Apprenticeship

Obtained by being another player's apprentice and having reached level 20.
The **Apprentice Ticket - Level 20** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Mentors & Apprentices update on March 11, 2019.
##  Function
When consumed, the player will receive a  World Lock.
