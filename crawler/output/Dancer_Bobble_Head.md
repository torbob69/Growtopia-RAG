The **Dancer Bobble Head** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2026 on May 1, 2026.
##  Function
<https://growtopiawiki.com/w/File:Harmonipan2022.ogg>The Dancer Bobble Head's music.
Being a toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block, an Dancer Bobble Head can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Dancer Bobble Head will change its appearance, showing a 6-framed animation of itself moving in place while also playing music.
