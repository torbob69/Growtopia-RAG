# Bunny Ear Magnifying Glass

A little bit of detective noir given the Growtopia treatment! Perfect for solving crimes and finding eggs! Careful where you point it as those sun rays are hot!

This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Bunny Ear Magnifying Glass** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Easter Week 2021 on April 1, 2021.
##  Function
Curiouser and Curiouser!

Solved the case!

When equipped, the **Bunny Ear Magnifying Glass** grants the _Punch Damage : Bunny Ear Magnifying Glass!_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster.
The  The Seven Seas : The Ray of the Manta! mission requires 1 of this item.
