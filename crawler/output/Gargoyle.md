##  Trivia
  * The Gargoyle is based on an architectural feature of the same name, a grotesque sculpted figure projecting from the gutter of a building, often made in the image of mythical creatures. Gargoyles are historically constructed to convey water from a roof and away from the side of a building. Nowadays, gargoyles have remained popular as a decorative building feature in certain cities.[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Gargoyle#cite_note-1)
