# Meat

A big honkin' hunk of deliciousness! Take a bite and you'll find yourself infused with gigantic meaty might! Time to feast!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **6 Hits** **5 Hits** – Restores after **5s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Treasure Hunting

Has a chance to be obtained from completing a Treasure Map.
**Meat** is an unsplicable consumable which was added in the Treasure Hunt Update. One can be obtained from a Treasure Map.
##  Function
Your appetite has enlarged your body.

Your tummy rumbles as you return to your normal size.

When consumed, it grants the player the Big: Huge Appetite mod which makes the player larger than usual for 10 seconds similar to equipping the Glove of the Giants.
