# Treasure Map

This map is covered in strange designs and shifting letters! They get a little easier to see when you're moving faster, though...

The **Treasure Map** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the Treasure Hunt update.
##  Function
First, the player needs to check their Treasure Map's description to find a hint regarding the required background weather for the map. Once the player is in a world with the correct weather, they need to look at the description of the map again to discover the second hint. The player will then obtain a random reward once a specific action has been done.
There are a total of 6 riddle possibilities.
### Solution
#### **Riddle 1**
1st Hint: This map is covered in strange designs and shifting letters! They're hard to make out in the light of day, though...Solution: Any world with Weather Machine - Night
2nd Hint: In pulse of life, in beat of blood, from health and hearth will treasure flood, when in your hand this map you hold.
Solution: Perform Surgery
#### **Riddle 2**
1st Hint: This map is covered in strange designs and shifting letters! The paper seems incredibly dry, though...Solution: Any world with Weather Machine - Rainy City
2nd Hint: In deeds of might and songs of strife, you'll find your treasures given life when in your hand this map you hold.
Solution: Defeat Villain with Crime Cards
#### **Riddle 3**
1st Hint: This map is covered in strange designs and shifting letters! The paper seems quite damp, though...Solution: Any world with Weather Machine - Arid
2nd Hint: In ticks and clicks, in woeful rays, your treasure waits 'til end of days.
Solution: Use Geiger Counter
#### **Riddle 4**
1st Hint: This map is covered in strange designs and shifting letters! The language isn't like anything you've heard of on Earth, though...Solution: Mars Blasted world
2nd Hint: In bubbling theory and thoughts abstract, lies treasure to the ceiling stacked, when in your hand this map you hold.
Solution: Complete Chemsynth
#### **Riddle 5**
1st Hint: This map is covered in strange designs and shifting letters! They get a little easier to see when you're moving faster, though...Solution: Any Starship blasted world
2nd Hint: In constellations bright and bold, you'll find your treasures gleaming gold.
Solution: Successfully carry out a Startopia mission.
#### **Riddle 6**
1st Hint: This map is covered in strange designs and shifting letters! The runes have a dangerous sheen of gold, though...Solution: Any world with Weather Machine - Jungle
2nd Hint: In fang and claw, in might and skill, this treasure's yet the greatest thrill.
Solution: Win a Pet Battle against a Pet Trainer
