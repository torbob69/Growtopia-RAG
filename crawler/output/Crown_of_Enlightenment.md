The **Crown of Enlightenment** is an unsplicable hat item that was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2020.
##  Function
When equipped, the Crown of Enlightenment serves a fully cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Enlightenment set, which consists of the following items:

    * Crown of Enlightenment (hat)

    * Dress of Enlightenment (shirt)

    * Torch of Enlightenment (hand)
  * When the full set is worn, the player will leave behind trails of blue, white, and red stars when walking.
  * The full Enlightenment set is based on the Statue of Liberty, which is considered as a symbol of freedom of the United States.
