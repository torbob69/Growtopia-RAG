The **Whip of Truth** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the B.O.O. Update on May 1, 2018.
##  Function
The truth is yours!

What is true?

When equipped, the **Whip of Truth** grants the _Whip of Truth_ mod, which allows the player to get a secret message similar to the Secret Of Growtopia. There are some messages which can appear in the Whip of Truth that do not appear in the Secret Of Growtopia such as:

  * I hacked the elections.
  * When I'm at the store, I take all the free samples for myself.
  * I was the Left Shark.
  * Growtopia is better for having you in it.
  * I wear a cape when nobody's around.
  * I eat my body weight in popcorn every night.
  * I am secretly obsessed with lemur documentaries.
  * I take about 80 selfies for each one I actually share.
  * I thought you were cool before anyone else did.
  * If zombies ever destroy the world, I hope I get to ride out the apocalypse with you.
  * I DJ at cat cafes on the weekends.
  * I got kicked out of band camp for eating the sheet music.
  * I once set my pants on fire while sleepwalking.
  * I'm terrified of babies.

##  Quest Steps
  1. Deliver 2,000 Secret Of Growtopias.
  2. Defeat 100 Growtopians in Games or Deathmatch Events.
  3. Break 10,000 blocks.
  4. Deliver 1,000 Mind-Ghost-In-A-Jars.
  5. Plant 100,000 rarity worth of trees.
  6. Earn 50 Growtokens.
  7. Deliver 10 Super Squirt Gun Jetpacks.
  8. Earn 20,000 XP.
  9. Deliver 5 Soul Stones.
  10. Deliver 140,000 Gems.
  11. Destroy 200,000 rarity worth of blocks.
  12. Defeat 100 Villains.
  13. Train 20 fishes.
  14. Deliver 3 Celestial Lance.
  15. Harvest 200,000 rarity of fruits from trees.
  16. Deliver 5 Ring Of Shrinkings.
  17. Deliver 3 Golden Talarias.
  18. Deliver 1 Summer Event Player Medal: Gold, Winter Event Player Medal: Gold, or Spring Event Player Medal: Gold.
  19. Deliver 3 Ancestral Totem of Wisdom.
  20. Deliver the Legendary Orb.

##  Trivia
  * Snapchot was the first player to complete the Quest For Candour and receive the Whip of Truth.
  * This item is a reference to the Lasso of Truth, a weapon used by Wonder Woman in DC Comics.
  * During Player Appreciation Week 2021, Step 18 gives the player the option to deliver a Summer Event Player Medal: Gold, Winter Event Player Medal: Gold, or Spring Event Player Medal: Gold, previously only allowing Summer Event Player Medal: Gold.
