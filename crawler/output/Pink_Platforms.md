The **Pink Platforms** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as May 2021's Monthly Subscriber's Item on May 2, 2021.
##  Function
When equipped, the Pink Platforms serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The Pink Platforms is based on the real-life platform shoes, a type of footwear with very thick soles and heels to make the wearer appear much taller than they actually are.
