# Cooking Recipe - Rotting Fish Guts Club Sandwich

To cook this recipe you will need the following:Ingredients: - Rotting Sea Goo - Fish Chunk - Tomato - Burnt SlimeSeasoning: - A pinch of Salt - Acid to tasteProcess:Add Rotting Sea Goo to an oven on Low heat. While the Rotting Sea Goo is still cold, add Fish Chunk and then Tomato. Before the Fish Chunk begins to cook, add Burnt Slime. Add the seasonings and wait until perfectly cooked all the way through before removing from the oven.Timings may vary so you will need to experiment.
To cook this recipe you will need the following:

Ingredients:
- Rotting Sea Goo
- Fish Chunk
- Tomato
- Burnt Slime

Seasoning:
- A pinch of Salt
- Acid to taste

Process:
Add Rotting Sea Goo to an oven on Low heat. While the Rotting Sea Goo is still cold, add Fish Chunk and then Tomato. Before the Fish Chunk begins to cook, add Burnt Slime. Add the seasonings and wait until perfectly cooked all the way through before removing from the oven.

Timings may vary so you will need to experiment.

The **Cooking Recipe - Rotting Fish Guts Club Sandwich** is an unsplicable component which was added as part of the Mutant Kitchen Rebalanced! update on November 12, 2019.
##  Function
This cooking recipe's description guides the player on how to cook a Rotting Fish Guts Club Sandwich. It is permanent and will not be consumed when the player cooks the item.
