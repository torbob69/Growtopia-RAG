# KYDERBY

The **Kentucky Derby** , also referred to as **Kyderby,** is an annual event traditionally hosted by former voluntary moderator Aimster. After her departure it was continued by former voluntary moderator Sabaei, former Support Specialist Rimanah, and currently voluntary Veteran Moderator @Misthios.
Based on a real-life horse racing event with the same name, the Derby revolves around the concept of racing across player-made tracks, which have been selected through a contest. The Kentucky Derby is typically hosted around the first Saturday in May, similarly to its real-life counterpart, and lasts for a duration of 24 hours to allow people in all time-zones a chance at participating in the races.
Each year a Kentucky Derby World Building Contest is hosted, and the top 20 player-created racetracks are used to conduct the races throughout the event. Winning this building contest and winning a race during the event are the only ways to obtain a  Mint Julep.
