The **Toy Lock-Bot** is a pet hand item that was added as part of the Pet Battles update.
##  Function
Clearly you have too many world locks.

I guess you ran out!

When equipped, the **Toy Lock-Bot** grants the _Too Many Locks!_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. It will also summon a toy Lock-Bot pet and follow the player around. This pet can also be trained as a fire battle pet. Additionally, it throws emerald lock projectiles while punching.
