The **Flamesaber** is a splicable hand item which was added alongside the Vegas Pack on May 24, 2013. It is the rarest of the six Growsabers.
##  Function
Float like a butterfly, sting like a punch in the face.

Boxing time over.

When equipped, the **Flamesaber** grants the _Fist O' Fury_ mod, which allows the player to knock back other players further.
Step 14 of the  Quest For The Blade requires the player to deliver 20 of this item.
