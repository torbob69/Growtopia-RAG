# Tea Basket

Toss this on and any items you collect from a fully-grown tree will fall directly into your inventory! A little flimsy, so it's likely to break after you've collected a lot of stuff, but no less useful for it!

This item never drops any seeds.

The Tea Basket is an unsplicable back item introduced during Harvest Festival 2019.
##  Function
Straight to your backpack with the harvest.

Back to picking up scraps from the ground.

When equipped, the Tea Basket gives the _Harvest Collector: Tea Basket_ mod which allows the player to automatically collect drops from a tree after punching, however, there is a chance that it could break at any time, turning into a Broken Tea Basket.
