# Pineapple Gift Box

What could be inside this most Pineapple'y of Prizes!!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **3 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **4s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Breaking

| May drop in sets of **1** from breaking:
•  Super Pineapple Party Crate  |
Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Pineapple Root Cutting **(×70)** ,
Dangerous Pineapple Root Cutting **(×1)** and
Super Pineapple Root Cutting **(×1)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Pineapple Gift Box** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2020 on June 4, 2020.

  * Pineapple Pizza Piece (×8)
  * Pineapple Plough

  * Pineapple Robes

  * Pineapple Sombrero Hat
  * Pineapple Superhero
