The **Chinese Crackers** is a non-solid toggleable animated foreground block that was added as part of Chinese New Year 2018.
##  Function
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Chinese Crackers can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Chinese Crackers will change its appearance, showing itself being lit up. As a light source, a Chinese Crackers emits light in dark areas.
