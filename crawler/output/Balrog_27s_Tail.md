The **Balrog's Tail** is a hand item introduced during the Spelunkorama Strikes Back! update. It is a rare drop from exploding a Pure Magic Ore using Mining Explosives.
##  Function
Demonic blade.

Unarmed.

When equipped, the **Balrog's Tail** grants the _Punch Power: Balrog's Tail_ mod, which allows the player to knock back other players further. It will also replace the player's punching animation into golden slashes.
