The **Enlightenment Bell** is an unsplicable foreground block that was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2024 on November 21, 2024.
##  Function
When placed, the Enlightenment Bell serves a purely aesthetic purpose. When punched, the Enlightenment Bell produces a short animation and a gong sound effect.
