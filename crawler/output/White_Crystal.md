##  Function
The White Crystal is one of the five crystals available from using a Geiger Counter, alongside Green, Blue, Red, and Black. It is the second rarest crystal obtainable.
Despite technically not being a block, a White Crystal can be placed down by the player, similar to how blocks are placed. Up to 5 crystals can be placed on the same tile. Breaking a harmonized crystal that consists of 5 eligible crystals will drop a unique reward.
