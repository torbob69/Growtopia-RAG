# Pure Love Essence

Consume to double your chances of getting a Golden Heart Crystal when opening Golden Booty Chests, Super Golden Booty Chests and Heartstones for 30 minutes.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **3 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **4s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Well of Love

One may be received after filling a Well of Love with 200 Golden Booty Chests.
After receiving the Pure Love Essence, the Well of Love will turn into a Dry Well of Love.
The **Pure Love Essence** is an unsplicable consumable that was added as part of Valentine's Week 2021 on February 13, 2021.
##  Function
Increased chance of Golden Heart Crystal!

The honeymoon phase is over!

When consumed, it grants the player the _Lucky in Love: Increased chance of Golden Heart Crystal_ mod, which gives the player double the chance to receive a Golden Heart Crystal from breaking a Golden Booty Chest, Super Golden Booty Chest, or Heartstone.
##  Trivia
  * Throughout 19 until 20 February, 2021, 2 Pure Love Essence were available as extra bonuses from purchasing the Gem Bounty IAP.
