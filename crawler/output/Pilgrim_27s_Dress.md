# Pilgrim's Dress

There are all kinds of things to be thankful for during the holidays, but maybe one of the most important is just how dang awesome those pilgrims looked! Elevate your ensemble with this traditional dress and join the fun!

The **Pilgrim's Dress** is an unsplicable shirt item that was added as part of the Thanksgiving Week 2018 update on November 22, 2018.
##  Function
When equipped, Pilgrim's Dress serves a fully cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the pilgrim (female) set, which consists of the following items:

    * Pilgrim's Bonnet (hat)

    * Pilgrim's Dress (shirt)

    * Pilgrim's Shoes (feet)
