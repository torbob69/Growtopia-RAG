# Black Ultraviolet Charm

Contained within the new black edition of this charm is pure ultraviolet energy. It is believed to enhance the capabilities of anyone who wears it. Warning: Ultraviolet Radiation is what causes sunburn. Prolonged exposure is not advised and don't forget to wear your hat and sunscreen.

The **Black Ultraviolet Charm** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of Black Friday 2022 on November 25, 2022.
##  Function
Ultraviolet Power!

Power Down!!

When equipped, the **Black Ultraviolet Charm** grants the _Build Range: Black Ultraviolet Charm_ mod, which allows the player to build one tile further.
