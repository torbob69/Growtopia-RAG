# Geiger V-Neck (disambiguation)

This is a **disambiguation** page.
There are more than one subjects in the **Growtopia Wiki** using this name.
**Geiger V-Necks** are shirt items that were released during Cinco De Mayo Week 2017 as a part of the Geiger Day event. They come in 4 different colours.
  * Orange Geiger V-Neck
  * Purple Geiger V-Neck
  * Green Geiger V-Neck
  * Blue Geiger V-Neck
