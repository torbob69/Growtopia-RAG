##  Function
Soarin and scarin!

On the floor!

When equipped, the Banshee's Hair grants the _Floating: Banshee's Hair_ mod, which allows the player to float in the air.
##  Trivia
  * The Banshee's Hair is a reference to a hair worn by a female spirit in Irish folklore of the same name.
