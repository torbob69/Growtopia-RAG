# Cooking Oven (block category)

**Cooking Ovens** are special blocks that are used to cook food (mainly consumables) by using a handful of ingredients. Unlike Chemical Combiners, Cooking Ovens are much more complex as it takes into consideration the oven heat, timing, and ingredients.
##  List of Cooking Ovens
Items: Special FunctionCommercial Oven: Basic oven, no benefits.
Home Oven: Basic oven, no benefits.
Master Chef's Oven: Decrease the threshold to fail from 60% to 50%.
Replicator: Has a 5% chance to double foods.
Taco Truck Oven: Basic oven, no benefits.
