The **Grow-Cola Block** is a splicable solid trampoline foreground block which was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2021 on November 18, 2021.
##  Function
Jumping on a trampoline block such as a Grow-Cola Block will cause the player to bounce, and by holding the jump button, the player can jump even higher. The _High Jump_ mod also boosts one's jump height on a trampoline block.
##  Trivia
  * The Grow-Cola Block's name and design is a reference to Coca-Cola, a soft drink manufactured by The Coca-Cola Company.
