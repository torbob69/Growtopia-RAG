**The Bouncer** is a non-solid NPC foreground block which was added as part of the Beach Party 2022 on June 24, 2022. The Bouncer can be found in the world BEACHPARTYGAME.
##  Function
Punching The Bouncer during Beach Party allows the player to join the mini-game in the BEACHPARTYGAME world.
