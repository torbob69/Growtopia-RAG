The **Blazing Electro Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of The Carnival update on October 13, 2014.
##  Function
You can jump in mid-air! It's like flying, but less.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Blazing Electro Wings** grants the _Double Jump: Blazing Electro Wings_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
Step 9 of the  Quest For The Sky requires the player to deliver 800 of this item.
