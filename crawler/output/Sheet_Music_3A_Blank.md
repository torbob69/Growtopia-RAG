The **Sheet Music: Blank** is a splicable and farmable background block which was added alongside the Composer's Pack on March 1, 2013.
##  Function
Sheet Music: Blank is a sheet music that plays no sound at all, thus allowing it to be used to delay the end of a song for a few seconds before the song restarts.
