# Dark Magic Katana

This is a katana that's got your back! I would advise not get hit by it, the blade traps the souls of its victims!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be transmuted.

The **Dark Magic Katana** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2023 Day 2 on August 29, 2023.
##  Function
Fight fire with fire!

Out of fuel!

When equipped, the **Dark Magic Katana** grants the _Punch Damage + Punch Range: Dark Magic Katana!_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and punch one tile further.
##  Trivia
  * The **Dark Magic Katana** is a reference to Demon-Slasher Katana, from Japanese manga and anime series of Black Clover.
