# Pineapple Panic Pack
During Super Pineapple Party

|
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Pineapple Panic Pack** for **18,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"A super pineapply crate of juicy Pineapples. You can also get the RARE Slippery Pineapple Squishers and have a chance of getting a Pineapple Roots Gatcha ! Note: You get a Super Pineapple Party Crate, place it in your world and break it to get pineapply goodies! This item is Untradeable."
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Pineapple Panic Pack** is a store pack which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2021. The starting price of the pack is 18,000 Gems and increases after every purchase as well as giving 1 additional Super Pineapple Party Crates.
After purchasing 20 packs, the player may purchase a Pineapple Warrior Charm for 1 Gem. After purchasing the Pineapple Warrior Charm, the pack is still available for purchase for 40,000 gems and gives 5 Super Pineapple Party Crates.
