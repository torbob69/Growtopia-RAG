The **Braided Golden Hair** is an unsplicable hair item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2022 Day 1 on August 15, 2022.
##  Function
Let down thy golden extensions!

Got a new haircut!

When equipped, the **Braided Golden Hair** grants the _Punch Range + Build Range: Braided Golden Hair_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further and build one tile further.
##  Trivia
  * The Braided Golden Hair's description and appearance is a reference to Rapunzel's long and magical hair, a protagonist from the American 3D computer-animated musical adventure fantasy comedy film, Tangled.
