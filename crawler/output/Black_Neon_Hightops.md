The **Black Neon Hightops** is an unsplicable feet item that was introduced as part of Black Friday 2020 update on November 27, 2020.
##  Function
When equipped, the Black Neon Hightops serves a fully cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Black Neon set, which consists of the following items:

    * Black Neon Hoodie (Shirt)

    * Black Neon Pants (Pants)

    * Black Neon Hightops (Feet)
