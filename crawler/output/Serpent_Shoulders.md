The **Serpent Shoulders** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2022 on May 5, 2022.
##  Function
A taste of poison paradise!

Ssssslipped away!

When equipped, the **Serpent Shoulders** grants the _Punch Range + Punch Damage: Serpent Shoulders_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further and break blocks faster. Additionally, while punching, the serpent heads spit toxic balls alternately.
##  Trivia
  * The Serpent Shoulders is based on Quetzalcoatl, a deity in Aztec mythology whose name comes from the Nahuatl language and means "precious serpent" or "quetzal-feathered serpent".
