The **Confetti Cannon** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2017 on May 5, 2017.
##  Function
You're bringing the party whether they like it or not!

Party's over.

When equipped, the **Confetti Cannon** grants the _Confetti_ mod, which allows the player to kill zombies during a Pandemic. It will replace the player's punching animation with a volley of confetti.
