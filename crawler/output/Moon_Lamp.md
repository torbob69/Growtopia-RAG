The **Moon Lamp** is a non-solid unsplicable and toggleable animation block which was added as a part of the Startopia 2.0 update on June 1, 2020.
##  Function
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Moon Lamp can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Moon Lamp will change its appearance, showing the Moon Lamp lit up and blinking every frame. It also functions as a light source, emitting light in dark areas.
