# White Chocolate Block

Also known as albino chocolate, white chocolate is just regular chocolate that's been left out in the sun too long.
White Chocolate Block (Rarity: 40)
Properties
None

Hardness: **5 Hits** **4 Hits** – Restores after **3s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **0 - 11**

Consumption

Consume a(n)  Winterfest Cracker **(x1)**.
May yield **5** item each.

The **White Chocolate Block** is an unsplicable solid foreground block that was added as part of WinterFest 2017 on December 18, 2017.
##  Function
When placed, the White Chocolate Block serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
