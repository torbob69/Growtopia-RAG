The **Solar Collector** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block.
##  Function
A Solar Collector can be toggled to affect either trees when splicing, planting or neither (disabled). It requires 24 hours to charge.
If there is more than one Solar Collector in the world, they will not all be used to boost one tree, however, each tree planted can only be affected by a maximum of one Solar Collector (i.e. 4 Solar Collectors will not boost a tree to 100%).
The  Harvest Festival Exclusive Mission - I can seed clearly now my gun I've got! mission requires 50 of this item.
