The **Grey Pet Pterosaurus** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Growassic Park on October 7, 2016.
##  Function
When equipped, the Grey Pet Pterosaurus serves a purely cosmetic purpose and summons a Grey Pet Pterosaurus which follows the player around.
