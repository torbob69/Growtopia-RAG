The **Easter Creature** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Easter Week 2022 on April 15, 2022.
##  Function
Hatched to perfection!

Had a big fall!

When equipped, the **Easter Creature** grants the _Build Range: Easter Creature_ mod, which allows the player to build one tile further. When punching, the eyes of the Easter Creature will turn red.
