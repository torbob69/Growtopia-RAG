The **Megalodon** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2017 Day 6.
##  Function
Swim faster!

No shark for you.

When equipped, the **Megalodon** grants the _I'm On A Shark!_ mod, which allows the player to move faster in water. Unlike items such as the Swim Fins and Mermaid Tail, the Megalodon does not slow the player down on land.
The Megalodon shares exactly the same mod as its Halloween counterpart, the Demon Megalodon.

##  Trivia
  * The Megalodon is based on an extinct, prehistoric shark of the same name that is believed to have lived approximately 2.5 million years ago.
  * As referenced in the item's description, the Megalodon appears as a playable character in Hungry Shark World, another game published by Ubisoft.
