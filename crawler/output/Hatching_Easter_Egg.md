The **Hatching Easter Egg** is an unsplicable non-solid provider foreground block which was added as part of Easter Week 2019.
##  Function
When harvested, a Hatching Easter Egg has a chance to drop a Drabbit's Foot, an Easter Creature, or a basic dragon, and then breaks. Additionally, it has a chance to drop a Hatched Dancer during Easter Week only.
