# Finale Claim

Players that touch this get a clash prize, and get kicked out of the finale world.

##  Function
### CLASHPARKOUR
Finale Claim 1-9 can be seen in the CLASHPARKOUR world upon using a Summer Clash Finale Ticket, a Winter Clash Finale Ticket, or a Spring Clash Finale Ticket. When stepped on, the player will receive a random prize and get kicked out of the world.
