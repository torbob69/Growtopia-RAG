# Songpyeon

This sweet steamed rice cake is said to lead to a bright future for whoever eats it. Eat up! You can also mix it with Mooncakes in a Laboratory or E-Z Cook Oven to create special Harvest Festival items.

The **Songpyeon** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2013 on September 18, 2013.
##  Function
You're luckier than before!

Your luck has worn off.

When consumed, a Songpyeon grants the _Lucky!_ mod to the player for thirty minutes, giving a 10% chance to multiply gem drops from breaking blocks by up to 5. It also provides a 10% chance for an extra block to be dropped.
This item's effect does not stack with that of Lucky Clover or Rabbit's Foot, but stacks with Apology Weekend's bonus.

### Silkworm Food     _Main article:Guide:Silkworm Care_
This consumable can be fed to a Silkworm to give it a purple color, thus allowing it to produce 1–10  Silk Thread - Purple per harvest.
##  Trivia
  * This item is based on a real-life food with the same name, which is a traditional Korean rice cake filled with bean paste made during the Korean harvest festival.
  * During the first Harvest Festival in 2013, the Songpyeon was distributed as a rare drop from harvesting trees. It was also distributed through in-app purchases, rewarding players with one per 1,000 Gems purchased.
    * Harvest Festival 2014 changed the way the Songpyeon is obtained. The rare drop from trees is now the newly added Balance Mooncake, which can be used in Songpyeon's new recipe.
  * During the first twenty minutes of Harvest Festival 2017, a Songpyeon would be given out for purchasing any in-app purchase. This, however, was later removed for being too overpowered.
