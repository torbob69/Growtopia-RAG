The **Rollback Plaque IV** is a plaque introduced during the fourth Rollback. The block could be made by splicing a Cave Background Seed with a Sign Seed during the Apology Weekend.
The plaque is green, possibly due to the fact that thousands of Emerald Locks were unfairly released into the game.
##  Rollback IV
On the 11th of December, 2017, a security exploit was used on the Growtopia Forums to gain unauthorized access to developers @Seth and @Hamumu's accounts.
Valuable items such as Phoenix Wings, Da Vinci Wings and Emerald Locks were unfairly distributed throughout the game, world data was corrupted and the beta server database along with the forum's were breached resulting in password hashes, date of birth, IP addresses and emails being illegally accessed, leading the Growtopia Team's decision to Rollback to the 10th of December, 2017, and force all forum users to reset their passwords.
