The **Eldritch Flame Wall Torch** is a unsplicable non-solid toggleable foreground block which was added as a part of Halloween Week 2019.
##  Function
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, an Eldritch Flame Wall Torch can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, an Eldritch Flame Wall Torch will change its appearance, showing a 2-framed animation of itself providing light to its surrounding. As a light source, an Eldritch Flame Wall Torch emits light in dark areas.
