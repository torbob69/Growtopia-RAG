The **Sheet Music: Sharp Piano** is a splicable and farmable background block which was added alongside the Composer's Pack on March 1, 2013.
##  Function
Sheet Music: Sharp Piano is a sheet music that plays a sharp piano note, depending on its position vertically. Its note is half-step higher than that of Sheet Music: Piano Note's.
