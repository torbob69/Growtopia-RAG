# Mysterious Cultist

This is an **NPC** , or a non-player character.
The **Mysterious Cultist** is a NPC which was added as part of the Halloween Week 2014 update. It appears on a special event that usually occurs several days before Halloween Week, visiting random player's worlds, usually being the World Of The Day.
When visiting random worlds, the Mysterious Cultist can leave after a short amount of time. NPC talks in a blue colour and walks around the world. Some of its lines are:
  * _Silence mortals! You are not worthy._
  * _You cannot stop the awakening._
  * _It knows only hunger, only hatred._
  * _Shadows grow longer, something waits in the silence._
  * _Growga Growga phtah PHTAH._
  * _Cling your sanity while you still can!_
  * _It is older than this world._
  * _K'Tesh an perok mos kalah!_
  * _The end is coming..._

###  Appearance
**Mysterious Cultist** has the appearance of a Growtopian wearing the following:
  * Special Cultist Hood (Hair)
  * Cultist Robe (Shirt)

## Trivia
  * Mysterious Cultist is controlled by a moderator or developer.
