The **Halfling House Roof** is a splicable platform foreground block which was added as part of Easter Week 2021 on April 1, 2021.
##  Function
A Halfling House Roof behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath.

##  Trivia
  * The Halfling House blocks (Halfling House, Halfling House Door, and Halfling House Roof) are based on the fictional Hobbit-holes, the preferred dwellings of hobbits, a fictional race found in the works of J. R. R. Tolkien.
    * The author would sometimes use term "halfing" to describe the fictional race. Traditionally, myths surrounding halflings often depict them as being very similar to humans, except about half as tall, yet not quite as stocky as the similarly-sized dwarves.
