The **Console Appreciation Plaque** is an unobtainable solid foreground block that was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2020 Day 5.
##  Function
The Console Appreciation Plaque was prematurely added in-game during Player Appreciation Week 2020. As its name suggests, it was meant to be given to ex-players of the console edition of Growtopia, which had been discontinued since July 30, 2020. Due to the fact the developers never found a way to connect mobile and console player accounts, the plaque ended up being unused.
When placed, the Console Appreciation Plaque would serve a purely aesthetic purpose.
