The **Lion Dancer Mask - Green** is an unsplicable hair item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2023 on September 21, 2023.
##  Function
When equipped, the Lion Dancer Mask - Green serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
When equipped with Lion Dancer Cape - Green, Lion Dancer Shirt - Green, Lion Dancer Pants - Green, and Lion Dancer Feet - Green, the player will gain an extra pair of legs resembling a Lion Dancer costume.
