Purchase the **Little Red Corvette** for **25,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"Cruise around the neighborhood in style with this sweet convertible. It moves at enhanced speed and leaves other Growtopians in your dust. Not available any other way!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Little Red Corvette** is an unsplicable feet item which was added alongside the City Pack on August 18, 2013.
##  Function
You can move faster!

You are slow again.

When equipped, the **Little Red Corvette** grants the _Speedy_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land. While moving, the player emits smoke at the back of the corvette.
The  Event Exclusive Mission - All Aboard For Easter Fun! mission requires 10 of this item.
##  Trivia
  * The Little Red Corvette was the first item based on a car to be added in Growtopia.
  * The Little Red Corvette's name and description reference "Little Red Corvette," a song by Prince.
  * The  Edison Zoomster is a recolor of  Little Red Corvette.
