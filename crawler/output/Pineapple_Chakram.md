The **Pineapple Chakram** is an unsplicable hand item which was added part of Super Pineapple Party Week 2021 on June 3, 2021.
##  Function
You are now a pineapple ninja!

Back to the shadows of the pineapple!

When equipped, the **Pineapple Chakram** grants the _Punch Range: Pineapple Chakram_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further. It will also replace the player's punching animation with a pineapple throwing effect along with three pineapple slices revolving around the player's body.
The  The Seven Seas : The Heart of the Ocean! mission requires 1 of this item.
