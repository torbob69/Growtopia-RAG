The **Fall Wings** is an unsplicable back item added as part of Harvest Festival 2021 on September 16, 2021.
##  Function
Rising not falling!

Falling not rising!

When equipped, the **Fall Wings** grants the _Double Jump : Fall Wings_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air. When punching, the player shoots leaf-like animations when punching.
