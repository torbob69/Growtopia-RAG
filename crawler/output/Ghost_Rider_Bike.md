The **Ghost Rider Bike** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Time-Tossed! update on September 5, 2019.
##  Function
Catch up to the wicked!

Penance stare: Complete!

When equipped, the **Ghost Rider Bike** grants the _Speedy: Ghost Bike_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.
##  Trivia
  * The Ghost Rider Bike is only visible to players who have a Spectral Goggles or Synthetic Oculum equipped.
