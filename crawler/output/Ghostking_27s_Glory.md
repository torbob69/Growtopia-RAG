The **Ghostking's Glory** is an unsplicable hat item that was added as part of the Blocktor's Orders! update.
##  Function
You WILL suffer me!

The dead do not suffer the living to pass!

When equipped, the **Ghostking's Glory** grants the _Ghost Immunity: Ghostking_ mod, which allows the player to be immune from all ghosts variants and change their skin color to translucent teal.
##  Trivia
  * There are several items in _Growtopia_ which grant immunity against certain types of ghosts.
    * Rubber Boots works against  regular Ghosts.
    * Foil Hat and  Alien Mind Protector work against  Mind Ghosts.
    * Ghostking's Glory works against all ghost variants.
