The **Draconic Soul Aura** is an unsplicable chest item that was added as part of Lunar New Year 2021.
##  Function
The flames and your soul combine!

Human, all too human!

When equipped, the **Draconic Soul Aura** grants the _Double Jump: Draconic Soul Aura_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air. When punching, a dragon will appear from the back of the player. The item will also continuously glow with every punch.
Step 19 of the  Quest Of The Owl requires the player to deliver 1 of this item.
