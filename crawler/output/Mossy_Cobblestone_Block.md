The **Mossy Cobblestone Block** is an unsplicable solid farmable foreground block item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2022 Day 6 on August 20, 2022.
##  Function
When placed, the Mossy Cobblestone Block serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The Mossy Cobblestone Block is the mossy counterpart of  Cobblestone Block.
  * The **Mossy Cobblestone Block's** design is a reference to a variant of cobblestone of the same name in Minecraft.
  * Despite its status as a farmable, it is not advisable to farm the **Mossy Cobblestone Block** due to hardcoded properties of rarity 999 (rarity-less) blocks, which includes lacking the chance for block bonuses to proc (such as the Harvester of Sorrows and Ancestral Tesseract Of Dimensions) on top of the very low seed rate when harvesting its tree.
