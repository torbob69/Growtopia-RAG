The **Gibbet** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable animated foreground block that was added as part of Summer Clash 2018.
##  Function
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Gibbet can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Gibbet will change its appearance, showing a 2-frame animation.
