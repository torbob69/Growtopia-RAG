The **Aquatic Volcanic Crown** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2024 on May 3, 2024.
##  Function
Flame on!

Flame off!

When equipped, the **Aquatic Volcanic Crown** grants the _Light Source and Fireproof: Aquatic Volcanic Crown_ mod, which allows the player to emit light in dark areas and take 50% less damage from lava pain blocks.
