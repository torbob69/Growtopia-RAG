The **Spirit Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as June 2023's Item Of The Season on June 2, 2023.
##  Function
Onto the Astral Plane!

Back down to earth!

When equipped, the **Spirit Wings** grants the _Double Jump + Ghastly: Spirit Wings_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and change their skin color to translucent dark green.
