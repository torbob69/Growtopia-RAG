A wonderful work of bamboo architecture!

Consume a(n)  Garuda Chest **(x1)**.
May yield **5** item each.

The **Bamboo Bridge** is an unsplicable foreground platform block which was added as part of the Garuda's Glory update on August 26, 2023.
##  Function
A Bamboo Bridge behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath.
##  Trivia
  * The Bamboo Bridge's appearance is reference to the bridge located on the Pepe River, Central Java.
