The **Porcupineapple Pair** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2023 on June 9, 2023.
##  Function
I have pineapples!

I don't have pineapples!

When equipped, the **Porcupineapple Pair** grants the _Punch Damage : Porcupineapple Pair_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster.
##  Trivia
