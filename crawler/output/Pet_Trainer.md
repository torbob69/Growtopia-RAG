The **Pet Trainer** is a non-solid NPC foreground block which was added as part of the Pet Battles update. One can be directly summoned by consuming a Pet Trainer Whistle, or can be randomly spawned in a world through a special event.
##  Function
A player may battle a Pet Trainer using battle pets.
Once a trainer has been defeated, they will reward the player with a Pet Trainer Medal, which can be traded with another Pet Trainer for certain items, and immediately disappear. A Pet Trainer will disappear if not defeated within 24 hours.
