The **Starry Night Aura** is an unsplicable back item which was added as a part of Harvest Festival 2025 on September 11, 2025.
##  Function
You're a sky full of stars!

That's a blank sky!

When equipped, the **Starry Night Aura** grants the _Double Jump : Starry Night Aura!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
##  Trivia
  * This item is a reference to the painting "The Starry Night" painted by Vincent van Gogh.
