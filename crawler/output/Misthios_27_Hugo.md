Exclusive Item

This item was given to **Misthios** for their contributions as a voluntary moderator.
The **Misthios' Hugo** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as a part of Harvest Festival 2025 on September 11, 2025.
##  Function
Bark, bark, bark!

Sleeping!

When equipped, the **Misthios' Hugo** grants the _Punch Damage : Misthios' Hugo_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and switch between its wearable form and its morph form by wrenching oneself and clicking 'Transform'.
##  Trivia
  * Misthios' Hugo is the wearable version of the  Dachshund Statue block.
  * There are two copies of this item. One was given to the Misthios account, and another was given to his main account, JackBowe.
