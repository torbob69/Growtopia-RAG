# Balloon Warz Silver Trophy - Build

Second place ain't bad! Stick this in the trophy cabinet and show it off to all your friends!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can't be destroyed - smashing it will return it to your backpack if you have room!
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.

The **Balloon Warz Silver Trophy - Build** is a trophy awarded after the war phase to the Build team if they place 2nd overall. This item can be collected from the Balloon Warz Leaderboard once the war is over.
##  Function
When placed, the Balloon Warz Silver Trophy - Build serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
