The **Logarithmic Wheel** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2016 on October 27, 2016.
##  Function
I guess.. you throw it?

Enough of that.

When equipped, the **Logarithmic Wheel** grants the _Wheelin' n' Dealin_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and knock back other players further. Additionally, it makes the player throw wheels as its punch effect and plays a special punching sound shared with the Chainsaw Hand.
