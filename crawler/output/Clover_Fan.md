# Clover Fan

A super powerful clover fan! Probably best not to use in small spaces as that was quite the kerfuffle!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.

The **Clover Fan** is an unspliceable non-solid pushing foreground block that was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2021 on March 11, 2021.
##  Function
As a  pushing block, the Clover Fan will push away colliding players according to the direction it is placed.
