**Val** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of UbiWeek 2022 Day 5 on January 28, 2022.
##  Function
Hammer time!

Dropped the hammer!

When equipped, the **Val** grants the _Double Jump & Punch Range: Val!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air, punch one tile further, and switch between its wearable form and its morph form by wrenching oneself and clicking 'Transform'.
##  Trivia
  * Val is based on the playable character Val in the platform game Brawlhalla, which was published by Ubisoft.
  * Val was accidentally released on Day 4, four hours after the release of Prince of Persia Lock and Prince of Persia Crate, but was later removed from the store.
  * During UbiWeek 2022, Val could be obtained from performing surgeries. As of UbiWeek 2023, Val is only obtainable from UbiCrates.
