The **Common Damage Up** is a Dungeon ability which was added as part of the Dungeons update on December 9, 2024.
##  Function
When the player receives this ability, their damage increases by 5.
##  Trivia
  * The Common Damage Up uses the same sprite as the following items:
    * Rare Damage Up
    * Epic Damage Up
  * After the Dungeons updated on October 23rd,2025, a changes has been made that **Common Damage UP** adjusted their damage increase from 10 to 5.
