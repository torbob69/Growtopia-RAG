The **Cauldron Cannon** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2021 on October 21, 2021.
##  Function
Fire burn and cauldron bubble!

The charm is firm and good!

When equipped, the **Cauldron Cannon** grants the _Punch Damage: Cauldron Cannon_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster.
