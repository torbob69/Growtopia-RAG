# Brown Riding Raptor

I know, they're supposed to have feathers and stuff nowadays... Well, this is an old-school raptor!

This item can be transmuted.

Raptor DNA Strand Alpha,

This recipe will yield **5** items each.
The **Brown Riding Raptor** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Growassic Park on October 7, 2016.
##  Function
You can move faster!

You are slow again.

When equipped, the **Brown Riding Raptor** grants the _Speedy_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.
##  Trivia
  * This item is one of five Riding Raptors in Growtopia, which include Blue Riding Raptor, Golden Riding Raptor, Red Riding Raptor, and Riding Raptor.
  * As part of the Player Appreciation Week 2022 Day 4, the quantity of all raptors obtained from a DNA Combination was increased from 1 to 5.
