"Your power level is over 9! Perhaps as high as 9.3! This device is actually a tiny backpack you attach to your belt, which projects a field of glowing flame behind you. Pick your color, Golden it is! Don't worry, it probably won't burn you. It also allows you to double jump."
The **Golden Aura** is an unsplicable back item that was added alongside the Basic Blue Block and Electric Bow on May 18, 2015.
##  Function
You can jump in mid-air! It's like flying, but less.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Golden Aura** grants the _Double Jump!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
The  Myths and Legends : The Menace of the Mini Minokawa mission requires 1 of this item.
