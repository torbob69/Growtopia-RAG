The **Rayman's Hair** is an unsplicable hair item which was added as part of the Rayman update on January 1, 2018.
##  Function
Fly like a bird!

The ground is your friend!

When equipped, the **Rayman's Hair** grants the _Helicopter Hair_ mod, which allows the player to glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button and transform into the Rayman when the entire Rayman set is equipped.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Rayman set, which consists of the following items:

    * Rayman's Hair (Hair)

    * Rayman's Nose (Face)

    * Rayman's Hoodie (Shirt)

    * Rayman's Shoes (Feet)

    * Rayman's Fist (Hand)
