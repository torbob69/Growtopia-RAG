# Assassin's Creed Celebration

Growtopia Gazette
> September 23rd: Assassin's Creed 15th Anniversary Celebration!
> Join us as we celebrate the 15th Anniversary of Assassin's Creed on this special Voucher Dayz weekend! Purchase select Gem packs from the store to earn vouchers and redeem them against special Assassin's Creed transforms. Get more than what you pay for during this event from the 23rd to 25th of September. Enjoy!
> In the shadow, transform your Growtopian into Altair, Eivor or Bayek, for you are what you choose to be! Let history take you back to Renaissance-era with the Assassin's Creed Lock and work from the dark with your fellow assassins to serve the light!
> Learn more about the Creed here.

**Assassin's Creed 15th Anniversary Celebration** started on September 23, 2022.
