The **Candy Wrench Decoration** is a wrench decoration which was added as part of the March Season on March 1, 2026.
##  Function
When equipped through the 'Wrench Customization' tab when wrenching oneself, the Candy Wrench Decoration adds a red bow to the player's wrench connected to their name.
