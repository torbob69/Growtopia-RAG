# Water Essence

The slippery, liquid soul of blocks. Not only looks nice - it's also refreshing!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item has no use... by itself.
Data

Type: Component
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: Full Collision
Hardness: **9 Hits** **7 Hits** – Restores after **4s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Breaking

| May drop in sets of **1** from breaking:
• Any block with the  Water Chi  |
The **Water Essence** is an unsplicable component which was introduced during the Hidden Riches update. It is obtained by breaking any blocks with Water Chi.
