The **Feminine Sunflower Shirt** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2021 on September 16, 2021.
##  Function
When equipped, the Feminine Sunflower Shirt serves a purely comsetic purpose.

##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the feminine sunflower set, which consists of the following items:

    * Feminine Sunflower Hair (hair)

    * Feminine Sunflower Shirt (shirt)

    * Sunflower Dress (pants)
  * Upon equipping the Masculine Sunflower Shirt or Feminine Sunflower Shirt, it used to restrict all caught Fishes to a maximum of 1lb while Fishing. This was later fixed on September 13, 2022. [[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Feminine_Sunflower_Shirt#cite_note-1)
