The **Zombie Hand Chair** is an unsplicable non-solid seat foreground block which was added as part of Halloween Week 2021 on October 21, 2021.
##  Function
When a player stands in front of a seat such as the Zombie Hand Chair for a few seconds, the player will automatically be seen "sitting" on it.
