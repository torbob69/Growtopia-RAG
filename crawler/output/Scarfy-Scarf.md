The **Scarfy-Scarf** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as May 2019's Monthly Subscriber's Item on 1 May 2019.
##  Function
When equipped, the Scarfy-Scarf serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * As of May 2, 2019, the Scarfy-Scarf was renamed from TraxyScarfy as the names of Monthly Subscriber's Items aren't meant to reference their designers' own.
    * The sentence "_TraxyScarfy is the May's Subscriber's Item, designed (of course) by TraxyGT!_ " in the news was also rewritten into "_Scarfy-Scarf is May's Subscriber's Item, chosen by TraxyGT!_ " as part of this change.
  * According to the subscriber, TraxyGT, the scarf is a reference to a scarf worn by a deuteragonist of the series Attack on Titan, Mikasa Ackerman.
