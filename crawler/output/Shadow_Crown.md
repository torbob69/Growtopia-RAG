The **Shadow Crown** is an unsplicable hat that was added as part of Black Friday 2020 update on November 27, 2020.
##  Function
May you have the blackest of Fridays!

Weekend is over!

When equipped, the **Shadow Crown** grants the _Punch Damage & Punch Range: Shadow Crown_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and punch one tile further. When walking, black flame sprites come out of the player and a black eagle comes out of the crown when jumping. Additionally, it replaces the player's punching animation to the black eagle shooting out black triangular sprites.
