# Rocket Block

Light up your world with the sparkles of your very own Rocket Block. It's so powerful you can only have 10 in your world at the same time.

The **Rocket Block** is an unsplicable foreground block which was added as part of Easter Week 2026 on April 3, 2026.
##  Function
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Rocket Block can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, the Rocket Block will change its appearance, shooting rockets to a three block distance.
