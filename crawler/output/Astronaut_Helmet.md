##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the astronaut set, which consists of the following items:

    * Astronaut Helmet (hair)

    * Space Suit (shirt)

    * Space Pants (pants)

    * Moon Boots (feet)

    * Astronaut Pack (back)
  * The Astronaut Helmet is the only item in the astronaut set which cannot be spliced.
  * Unlike the visor of the  Space Helmet, the  Astronaut Helmet's is completely opaque.
