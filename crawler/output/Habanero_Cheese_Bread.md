The **Habanero Cheese Bread** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Rise of the Desert Dragons update on August 3, 2013.
##  Function
When consumed, the player will receive the _On Fire!!_ mod for 5 seconds. Its effect is similar to the Habanero Pepper, except that it won't make the player's face color red.
### Silkworm Food     _Main article:Guide:Silkworm Care_
This consumable can be fed to a Silkworm to give it a grey color, thus allowing it to produce 1–10  Silk Thread - Grey per harvest.
