The **Diving Board** is a splicable trampoline platform foreground block which was added on Player Appreciation Week 2014 Day 5 on August 12, 2014.
##  Function
A Diving Board behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath. Moreover, it also counts as a trampoline block. Jumping on one will cause the player to bounce, and by holding the jump button, the player can jump even higher. The _High Jump_ mod also boosts one's jump height on a trampoline block.
