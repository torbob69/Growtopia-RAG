The **Data Bedrock Candy** is an unobtainable and unbreakable block which was added as part of the Voucher Dayz 2024 update on November 14, 2024.
##  Function
A Data Bedrock Candy is presumably located in the bottom left of the world. It is used to store data about ghosts and events that are present in the world.
