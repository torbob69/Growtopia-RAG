Unlocked during Grow4Good after at least **130,000,000** global points have been accumulated.
The **Dirt Aura** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of Grow4Good Week 2024 on April 26, 2024.
##  Function
Let it grow, Let it grow!

Run away and slam the door!

When equipped, the Dirt Aura grants the _Build Range: Dirt Aura_ mod, which allows the player to build one tile further.
##  Trivia
  * During Grow4Good Week 2025, the point requirement to unlock the Dirt Aura was lowered from 180,000,000 to 130,000,000.
