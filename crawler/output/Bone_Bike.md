The **Bone Bike** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Wonder Week 2025 - Day 5 on October 10, 2025.
##  Function
I've got a bone to pick!

Shivers down your spine!

When equipped, the **Bone Bike** grants the _Double Jump + Speedy : Bone Bike_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air, move faster on land, and switch between its wearable form and its morph form by wrenching oneself and clicking 'Transform'.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Bone Bike set, which consists of the following items:

    * Bone Bike Helmet (Hat)

    * Bone Bike (Feet)
