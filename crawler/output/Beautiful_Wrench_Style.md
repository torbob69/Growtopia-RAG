The **Beautiful Wrench Style** is a wrench style which was added as part of the May Season on May 3, 2024.
##  Function
When equipped through the 'Wrench Customization' tab when wrenching oneself, the Beautiful Wrench Style replaces the player's wrench connected to their name.
