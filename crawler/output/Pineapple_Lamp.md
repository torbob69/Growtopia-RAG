The **Pineapple Lamp** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable animated foreground block which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2020 on June 4, 2020.
##  Function
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Pineapple Lamp can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Pineapple Lamp will change its appearance, showing the lamp being lit up. As a light source, a Pineapple Lamp emits light in dark areas.
