##  Trivia
  * The Plumbing's design is based on warp pipes, a traditionally green-colored pipe commonly used as a mode of transportation in the _Mario_ franchise.
    * The item's name references the franchise's titular protagonist, Mario, who is well-known for his plumber attire.
