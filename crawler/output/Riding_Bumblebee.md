The **Riding Bumblebee** is an unsplicable feet item that was added as part of Easter Week 2016.
##  Function
You can jump in mid-air! It's like flying, but less.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Riding Bumblebee** grants the _Double Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
##  Trivia
  * Purchasing the Riding Bumblebee from the Growtopia Store will complete one of the thirty-two Epic Quests.
    * As of Easter Week 2022, player must receive the Riding Bumblebee as a prize from EGGRESCUE to complete the one of the thirty-two Epic Quests.
  * As of Easter Week 2022, the Riding Bumblebee was removed from the Store.
