# Growmoji Fireworks

Pop a burst of fireworks into your chat! Just consume this to add it to your available growmojis!!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **5 Hits** **4 Hits** – Restores after **8s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Super Fireworks Reward

May be obtained from firing off  Super Fireworks with **200** Fireworks in your inventory.
The **Growmoji Fireworks** is an unsplicable growmoji consumable which was added as part of SummerFest 2018 on July 6, 2018.
##  Function
When consumed, the player will be able to use the (fireworks) growmoji.
