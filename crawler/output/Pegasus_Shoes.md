# Pegasus Shoes

Why would a Pegasus need shoes? Don't they have hooves? Oh well, best not to think about it and just enjoy some feather embossed shoes.

The **Pegasus Shoes** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of the Pegasus Pursuit update on June 28, 2021.
##  Function
With the wind beneath your winged shoes

Slow down!

When equipped, the **Pegasus Shoes** grants the _Speedy: Pegasus Shoes_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Pegasus set, which consists of the following items:

    * Pegasus Crown (hat)

    * Pegasus Pauldrons (neck)

    * Pegasus Shirt (shirt)

    * Pegasus Pants (pants)

    * Pegasus Shoes (shoes)

    * Pegasus Lance (hand)
  * Pegasus Shoes' design is based on  USMom's Talaria and its counterpart  Golden Talaria.
