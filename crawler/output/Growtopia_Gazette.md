# Growtopia Gazette

The **Growtopia Gazette** is a news GUI which appears when a player logs in. Its purpose is for the Developers to keep the players up to date on the latest changes and additions to the game.
The following are common things the Gazette may include:
  * Events and Monthly Updates
  * Info about tweaks, nerfs and upcoming changes
  * Sponsored Items
  * The Community Hub
  * The Item Of The Month
  * The World Of The Day
  * The Video Of The Week

Also included in the Gazette are links to Growtopia's official social media pages, the Forums, the Growtopian Code and Privacy Policy & Terms of Use.
##  Trivia
  * The Gazette can be found by typing /news, equipping a Newspaper or from the Player Portal.
