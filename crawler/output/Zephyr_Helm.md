The **Zephyr Helm** is a hat item which was added on December 1, 2017 as December 2017's Item Of The Month, along with the Winterfest Calendar - 2017 as a bonus.
##  Function
Ha! You have a helm fit for a king!

You don't like to be the king?

When equipped, the **Zephyr Helm** grants the _Zephyr Helm_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
##  Trivia
  * Every WinterFest, the Zephyr Helm gives a snowflake visual effect.
