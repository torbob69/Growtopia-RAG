# Ghost Trap

Snags spirits in a snap! Place this trap down and it'll suck in a ghost that's above it. The Ghost Trap generates an enhanced field that is larger than a ghost jar, but you'll probably still need some friends with Neutron Guns to keep the ghost contained or it will escape! A trap takes 2 seconds to activate, and then only collects spirits for 5 seconds, so time it carefully.

The **Ghost Trap** is a splicable consumable which was added as part of the B.O.O. Update on May 1, 2018.
##  Function
When consumed, the player can capture ghosts with a larger range than standard Ghost Jars. It requires two seconds to activate and will only collect ghosts for five seconds.
