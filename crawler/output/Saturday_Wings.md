The **Saturday Wings** is an unsplicable back item exclusive to game Moderator @Anulot, rewarded on April 2, 2016.
##  Function
You can jump in mid-air! It's like flying, but less.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Saturday Wings** grants the _Double Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air. Additionally, when the player dances with the wings equipped, it will play a song similar to the Party Boombox.
