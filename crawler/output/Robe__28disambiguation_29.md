# Robe (disambiguation)

This is a **disambiguation** page.
There are more than one subjects in the **Growtopia Wiki** using this name.
**Robe** s are torso items that can be obtained in a variety of different ways. **Robe** may refer to:
Item: Method(s) to ObtainCultist Robe: Possible drop from sacrificing an item of rarity 90 or above into Growganoth's Maw. One can also be made by mixing 1 Growch Cultist Robe, 20 Purple Block and 1 Transmog Crystal in any Chemical Combiner.
Winter Robe: Harvesting a Giving Tree between 70-79% decorated with Tree Decorations.
Winter Freezer Robe: One can be purchased for 200 Winter Tokens from Branch 2 of the Winter Clash Rewards Store.
Ultra Winter Freezer Robe: One can be purchased for 1,000 Winter Tokens from Branch 2 of the Winter Clash Rewards Store.
Ghastly Robe: October 2017's Item Of The Month. No longer obtainable.
Wizard's Robe: Splicing Purple Wallpaper Seed + Happy Joy Plaque Seed. One also comes with the purchase of the Fantasy Pack for 5,000 Gems in store.
Architect's Robes: One of the Summer Clash Awards prizes.
Sprite's Robe: Possible drop from the Winter Clash Parkour.
Druid's Robes: Possible reward from PVP Card Battle.
Drednok's Robe: One may be obtained by consuming a Starfleet Cadet's Supply Box or a Starfleet Officer's Supply Box.
Temple Robes: Mixing 5 Bountiful Jungle Temple Root Cutting, 3 Bountiful Corpse Flower Root Cutting, and 2 Bountiful Jungle Temple Pillar Root Cutting in any Chemical Combiner.
Heartqueen's Robes: Possible drop from Valentine Loot.
Heartking's Robes: Possible drop from Valentine Loot.
Pineapple Robes: Possible reward from the Pineapple Magic mod. One may also be obtained by consuming a Pineapple Gift Box.
Robe of Tentacles: Made by completing "The Seven Seas : A Tale of Tentacles!" Marvelous Mission.
Emperor's New Robes: Sewing with a Silk Bolt - Black, then a Silk Bolt - Red using an Overcasting Stitch, then a Silk Bolt - Yellow using a Straight Stitch.
The Chosen One Robes: Possible reward by completing a Star Voyage in the _Epsilon Sector_ , _Growlactus_ , or the _Heart of the Galaxy Sector_.
Creepy Chocolatier's Robe: One may be obtained by consuming a Halloween Gift Box or a Halloween Special Gift Box.
Edgy Anime Robe: One can be purchased for 25 World Locks from Locke The Salesman during Black Friday.
Ghost of WinterFest Present Robe: Mixing 20 Winter Gift, 1 Wizard's Robe, and 1 Ghost-In-A-Jar in any Chemical Combiner during WinterFest.
Incognito Robe: Possible drop from harvesting a Wonder Provider.
Aurora Robe: One may be obtained by consuming a Deluxe Winterfest Cracker.
Growch Cultist Robe: Mixing 1 Cultist Robe, 20 Snowball and 1 Transmog Crystal in any Chemical Combiner.
Plague Doctor Robe: Rare prize from completing surgery on Surgery Day.
