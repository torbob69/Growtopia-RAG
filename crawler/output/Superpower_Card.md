# Superpower Card

This is a **disambiguation** page.
There are more than one subjects in the **Growtopia Wiki** using this name.

**Superpower Cards** are blocks that are used to fight Villains that have spawned in a world (through consuming a Crime Wave, a Henchman or from a Special Event).
There are 5 different elements: Lightning, Ice, Fire, Muscle and Cosmic Power. Some elements may block the abilities of other elements, as noted in the Crimefighting Rules.

##  Cosmic Superpower Cards
Cosmic Superpower Cards are special cards which can be obtained by splicing with Galactic Starseed.
**Superpower Card** — Type — Effect — RecipeCosmic Power - Comet Strike — Cosmic — 25% chance of doing 1 extra damage whenever your card counters the opponents. _This card has a passive effect for the entire duration of the fight._ — Splicing a Galactic Starseed with a Lightning Superpower Card Seed.
Cosmic Power - Star Burst — Cosmic — _When you are hit for more than 1 damage at once, the opponent is blasted for 1 damage._ _This card has a passive effect for the entire duration of the fight._ — Splicing a Galactic Starseed with a Fire Superpower Card Seed.
Cosmic Power - Lunar Barrier — Cosmic — _When you are at or below 25% life, there's a 25% chance to deflect damage done to you._ _This card has a passive effect for the entire duration of the fight._ — Splicing a Galactic Starseed with a Muscle Superpower Card Seed.
Cosmic Power - Nebula Gas — Cosmic — _When your cards are countered, there's a 25% chance that your card works anyway._ _This card has a passive effect for the entire duration of the fight._ — Splicing a Galactic Starseed with an Ice Superpower Card Seed.
