The **Hanging Bats** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Halloween Week 2017 on October 30, 2017.
##  Function
When placed, the Hanging Bats serves a purely aesthetic purpose. When a player passes through, a short animation featuring the bats fluttering will be triggered.
