The **Blood Oath** is a Dungeons ability which was added as part of  Dungeons Update, along with Halloween Week on October 23rd,2025.
##  Function
When player receives Blood Oath ability in Dungeons, Player's attack effect is blade slash in red color. Upon hitting enemies, Player's health also decrease by 2% of damage dealt.
