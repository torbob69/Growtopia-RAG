**Master Peng** is an NPC which appears in the world TOURNAMENT. He is also known as _Peng Nguyen._ Players can purchase a Tournament Ticket from him for 10,000 Gems anytime, except during the tournament. Players can also view the current prize pool for the tournament winners.
##  Trivia
  * Master Peng's name is a pun on the word _"peng-guin"_ as his appearance suggests that he is a penguin due to his beak.
  * If a player is caught cheating and punches Master Peng, he will say _"Get out of here you scummy cheater, the tournament is an honorable battle"._
  * Master Peng uses the same sprite as Alan, The Alien.
