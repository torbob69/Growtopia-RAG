The **Blueberry Pie** is a splicable consumable which was added on February 26, 2013.
##  Function
When consumed, the player will say "YUM!" and do the :D ⦅⦆ emote.
### Silkworm Food     _Main article:Guide:Silkworm Care_
This consumable can be fed to a Silkworm to give it a purple color, thus allowing it to produce 1–10  Silk Thread - Purple per harvest.
##  Trivia
  * The Blueberry Pie is one of the three food-based consumables which require Blueberry in their recipes, besides the Blueberry Muffin and Berry Crepes.
  * Unlike most food consumables, the Blueberry Pie is made through splicing, instead of through combining or cooking.
  * The Blueberry Pie is one of the five kitchen items added on February 26, alongside:
    * Stove
    * Apron
    * Chef Hat
    * Spatula
