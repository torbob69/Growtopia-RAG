Live an undead life of terror with the flaming face of a dreaded ghost pirate!

The **The Seven Seas : The Ray of the Manta!** mission needs to be unlocked first before accessing this mission.
The **Ghost Pirate Head** is an unsplicable face item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2022 Day 3 on August 17, 2022.
##  Function
A pirate's life for me!

Shiver me timbers!

When equipped, the **Ghost Pirate Head** grants the _Punch Range: Ghost Pirate Head_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Ghost Pirate set, which consists of the following items:

    * Ghost Pirate Scimitar (Hand)
