The **Royal CyberWings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as September 2024's Item Of The Season on September 1st, 2024.
##  Function
Roll out!

Rolling in the deep!

When equipped, the **Royal CyberWings** grants the _Double Jump + Speedy: Royal CyberWings_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and move faster on land.
##  Trivia
Royal CyberWings is the first Royal Item of the Season to occupy a different slot than its normal version: CyberCycle, which is a feet item.
