The **Heart Necklace** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2018 on February 9, 2018.
##  Function
When equipped, the Heart Necklace serves a purely cosmetic purpose. The item appears to feature a broken heart, but if a Valentine is used on a player wearing it, the necklace will become temporarily whole.
