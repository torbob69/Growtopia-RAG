The **Riding Skeletal Shark** is an unsplicable feet item that was added as part of Halloween Week 2018 on October 26, 2018.
##  Function
Swim faster!

No shark for you.

When equipped, the **Riding Skeletal Shark** grants the _I'm On A Shark!_ mod, which allows the player to move faster in water.
