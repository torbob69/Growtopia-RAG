The **Steam Launcher** is a splicable solid trampoline foreground block. Up to 4 of this block can drop from harvesting its tree, which is made by splicing a Steam Tubes Seed with a Pinball Sproinger Seed.
##  Function
This block will act as a trampoline block for a short while when steam passes through it.
