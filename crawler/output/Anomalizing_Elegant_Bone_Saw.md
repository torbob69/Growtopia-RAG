The **Anomalizing Elegant Bone Saw** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the Role Up! update.
##  Function
Anomalizing Elegant Bone Saws are used to score points during Surgery Stars. While equipping this item, each successful surgery yields 20,000 points. Elegant anomalizers, such as this item, may rarely break upon usage during their respective Grow Events.
