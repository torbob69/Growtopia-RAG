The **Occean's Eternal Wake** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of SummerFest 2025 on July 10, 2025.
##  Function
Servin' Waves!

Washed away!

When equipped, the **Occean's Eternal Wake** grants the _Double Jump : Occean's Eternal Wake_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
##  Trivia
  * This item is sponsored by Occean for completing all the Epic Quests.
  * The design of  Occean's Eternal Wake is based on  Legendary Wings.
