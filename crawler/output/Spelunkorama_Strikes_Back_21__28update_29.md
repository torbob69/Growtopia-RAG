# Spelunkorama Strikes Back! (update)

Growtopia Gazette
> February 6th: Spelunkorama Strikes Back!
> Hello Growtopians!
> Are you ready to explore some deeper caves beyond the Cave Blast and gather some ores? That's right, Spelunkorama Strikes Back! Get ready to find all NEW Magic Stones and explore a whole new cave system and mine its Magic Ore deposits!
> The ancient magic deposits are so strong that even the rarest pickaxes won't break them. You'll need to collect Mining Explosives scattered throughout the caves in order to break the Ores and reveal their riches. These unstable caves only last for a limited time, so get ready to nab as much treasure as you can before the cave collapses!
> -The Growtopia Team
**Spelunkorama Strikes Back!** is an update introduced during February 6, 2020. It features a new mine that can be found by entering a door that can only be seen using Magic Stones in a cave-blasted world. The player will have 30 minutes to roam around the mines, special prizes can be obtained by exploding Pure Magic Ore using Mining Explosives.
