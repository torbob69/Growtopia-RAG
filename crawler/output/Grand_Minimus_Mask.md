##  Function
When equipped, the **Grand Minimus Mask** serves a purely cosmetic purpose. However, equipping the entire Grand Minimus set will transform the player into the Grand Minimus.
##  Trivia

    * Grand Minimus Crown (Hat)

    * Grand Minimus Mask (Face)

    * Grand Minimus Robe (Shirt)

    * Grand Minimus Magic (Hand)
