Each recipe gives **5** items.
The **Green & Yellow Awning** is an unsplicable platform trampoline foreground block which was added in the Night of the Sewing Dead update on February 3, 2017.
##  Function
A Green & Yellow Awning behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath. Moreover, it also counts as a trampoline block. Jumping on one will cause the player to bounce, and by holding the jump button, the player can jump even higher. The _High Jump_ mod also boosts one's jump height on a trampoline block.
