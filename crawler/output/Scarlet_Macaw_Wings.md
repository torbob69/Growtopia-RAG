The **Scarlet Macaw Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Wildlife Weekend on March 10, 2023.
##  Function
Chase the Rainbow!

Out of colour!

When equipped, the **Scarlet Macaw Wings** grants the _Double-Jump: Scarlet Macaw Wings_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Scarlet Macaw set, which consists of the following items:

    * Scarlet Macaw Hood (Hair)

    * Scarlet Macaw Charm (Chest)

    * Scarlet Macaw Pants (Pants)

    * Scarlet Macaw Wings (Back)
