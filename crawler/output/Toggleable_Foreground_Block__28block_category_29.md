# Toggleable Foreground Block (block category)

A **toggleable foreground block** is a type of foreground block which can be toggled on or off via punching.
##  List of Toggleable Foreground Blocks
Items: Special Function8th Anniversary Block: When toggled on, an 8th Anniversary Block will change its appearance, showing a 6-framed animation of itself moving in place.
10th Anniversary Block: When toggled on, the block will change its appearance, showing an animation of the block flickering.
11th Anniversary Block: When toggled on, the block will change its appearance, showing an animation of the block flickering.
Adventure Barrier: Acts as a solid block when toggled on, and as a non-solid block when toggled off; however, when an Adventure Brazier is lit up in a world, all Adventure Barriers in the same world will either be toggled on/off based on their previous states for 10 seconds.
Adventure Idol Gate: Acts as a solid block when toggled on, and as a non-solid block when toggled off; however, when an Adventure Pedestal is activated, all Adventure Idol Gates in the same world will either be toggled on/off based on their previous states.

Blender Bop: When toggled on, a Blender Bop will change its appearance, showing a 4-framed animation, and play a piece of music.
Buddy Balloons: When toggled on, the block will change its appearance, showing an animation.
