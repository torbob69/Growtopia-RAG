The **Fists of the Undercity** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as November 2025's Item Of The Season on November 1, 2025.
##  Function
Bleeding for vengeance

Just raw knuckles

When equipped, the **Fists of the Undercity** grants the _Punch Damage + Punch Range: Fists of the Undercity_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and punch one tile further.
