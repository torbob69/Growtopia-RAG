# Grow Voucher

A voucher used to purchase items in the store. This item can be redeemed against all items which use the voucher currency, when they are available in store.

The **Grow Voucher** is a currency which was added as part of Voucher Dayz on May 21, 2021. It is a bonus reward from purchasing an In-app Purchase, quantity depends on the pack purchased (25 from Gem Abundance, 15 from Gem Bounty, 4 from It's Rainin' Gems, and 1 from Gem Fountain) during the first weekend of every month.

The Grow Voucher can be used to purchase the following items in the Growtopia Store during a random weekend every month (however, items listed are random).
