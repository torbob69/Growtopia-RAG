The **Alien Mind Protector** is an unsplicable hair item which was added as part of the Startopia update.
##  Function
When equipped, the **Alien Mind Protector** grants the _Ghost Immunity_ mod, which allows the player to be immune from  Mind Ghosts.
##  Trivia
  * There are several items in _Growtopia_ which grant immunity against certain types of ghosts.
    * Rubber Boots works against  regular Ghosts.
    * Foil Hat and  Alien Mind Protector work against  Mind Ghosts.
    * Ghostking's Glory works against all ghost variants.
