# Drabbit's Foot

Once upon a time, a dragon and a rabbit loved each other very much. Even the wisest sages aren't sure how this happened, and nobody likes to think about how it worked, but the wondrous drabbit was the result, and now with this talisman, a touch of its provider-enhancing wisdom can be yours!

The **Drabbit's Foot** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of Easter Week 2019.
##  Function
Bunny-dragon wisdom!

Your wisdom is no more.

When equipped, the **Drabbit's Foot** grants the _XP Buff: Providers_ mod, which allows the player to gain triple XP when harvesting providers. A drabbit pet will also appear and fly around, following the player.
