A demon-possessed pineapple, out for blood! Or maybe just hugs! Either way, its touch is deadly, so you'd better MOVE!

The **Looming Pineapple** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2019 on June 7, 2019.
##  Function
Being a looming deadly block, a Looming Pineapple acts as a deadly block for a while when a player passes by.
