# Cosmic Power - Life Harvest

This is a Rare Cosmic Superpower card for use in fighting crime. You can also place it as a block! Cosmic Cards aren't used, they just have an effect as long as they are in your hand. Card effect: Heal 1 life every time you inflict more than 1 damage.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.
