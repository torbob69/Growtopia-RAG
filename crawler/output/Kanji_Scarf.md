The **Kanji Scarf** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of Wonder Week 2022 Day 6 on October 20, 2022.
##  Function
It's Kanji Time!

It's Not Kanji Time!

When equipped, the **Kanji Scarf** grants the _Double Jump & Speedy: Kanji Scarf_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and move faster on land.
##  Trivia
  * _Kanji_ ( 漢字 ) are the logographic Chinese characters taken from the Chinese script and used in the writing of Japanese. They were made a major part of the Japanese writing system during the time of Old Japanese and are still used.
  * Performing certain actions while wearing the Kanji Scarf will show kanji translations of said action:
    * Punching will show the character 竅, which translates to 'punch'.
    * Walking, regardless of direction, will show the character 動, which translates to 'motion'.
    * Jumping will show the character 翔, which translates to 'fly'.
