##  Trivia
  * The Monster Truck is based on a real-life vehicle with the same name, which can be described as an extremely large pickup truck, typically with greatly oversized tires. Such trucks are often used to race across rough terrains or displayed in exhibitions in which they drive over and demolish smaller automobiles.
