The **Microwave** is a splicable chemical combiner foreground block which was added alongside the addition of Home Pack on February 26, 2014.
##  Function
A Microwave can be opened or closed by being punched. As a Chemical Combiner, it can be used to combine three different sets of items. A stack of new items will be created afterwards.
The **Microwave** is used to create the Chocolate Sprinkles Block. Trying to use a Chemical Combiner other than a **Microwave** will show the message "_This recipe would melt the chocolate beyond recognition!_ "
