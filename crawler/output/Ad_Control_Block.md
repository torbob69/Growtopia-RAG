# Ad Control Block

Use this block to check and collect your gem earnings and turn off background ad elements. You may only use 1 of these per world.

This item never drops any seeds.

The **Ad Control Block** is an unsplicable solid foreground block which was added as part of the Growtopia ADventures update on June 22, 2021.
##  Function
When wrenched by the owner of a world, it allows the player to toggle the billboard and airplane advertisements seen in a world with a Weather Machine - Sunny activated. Moreover, the owner may collect up to 30,000 Gems that are accummulated when players view any non-Growtopia advertisements in the world.
##  Trivia
  * Attempting to place more than one Ad Control Block in a world will produce the message, "_Air Traffic Safety Regulations prohibit more than one Ad Control Block in a world._ "
