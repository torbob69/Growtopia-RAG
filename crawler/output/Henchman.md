# Henchman

This is the page for **Henchman**.
You may be looking for **Crime In Progress** instead.
Looks like you captured one of that villain's henchmen... Of course, you COULD let him loose somewhere to cause a little havoc! Note: Henchmen take 24 hours to complete their crime, then they disappear!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **8 Hits** **6 Hits** – Restores after **8s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Crimefighting Reward

Defeat  Villains in crimefighting.
May give **1** item each time.
**Henchman** is an unsplicable consumable that can be obtained by defeating a criminal.
##  Function
When used in a world, it will spawn a random villain. The villain leaves the world if it stays undefeated for 24 hours.
