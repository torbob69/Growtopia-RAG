# Portrait

Demonstrating your speed-painting skills by painting pictures of anyone in your world in moments! Wrench the portrait to begin painting. It costs 2 of each color of paint for each painting.
Properties
This item has special properties you can adjust with the Wrench.
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.

The **Portrait** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2016.
##  Function
To use, a player must have 2 of each Paint Buckets in their inventory. Upon wrenching the Portrait, the player gets to choose any player on the screen whose face will be painted on the Portrait. Afterwards, the former can edit the latter's emotion and write a title for others to view. Unlike with the Painting Easel, the player isn't required to have a Paintbrush equipped in order to use a Portrait.
Using 4 Paint Bucket - Varnish allows the player to remove the face painted on the Portrait.
