In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Bountiful Bamboo Ladder Root Cutting **(×3)** ,

The **Bamboo Sword** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the Roots update on July 11, 2019.
##  Function
Bamboozled!

Sword concealed!

When equipped, the Bamboo Sword grants the _Bamboo Slash_ mod, which gives the player a slashing effect when punching.
