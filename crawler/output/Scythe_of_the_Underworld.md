# Scythe of the Underworld

Dripping with dark magic, the blade of this horrific scythe is capable of cutting through the veil of life and death itself! Wrenched into the mortal plane by dire sorcery, it's now free to wreak havoc among the living!

The **Scythe of the Underworld** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2019 on October 28, 2019.
##  Function
Consumed by the power of the Underworld!

The scythe returns to its domain!

When equipped, the **Scythe of the Underworld** grants the _Punch Damage: Underworld!_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. It also replaces the player's punching animation with red skulls.
