**Morty the Orange Elephant** is an unsplicable face item which was added in the Spring Clash update as March's Item Of The Month for 2019. The item, along with the Morty the Gray, Pink, and Diamond Elephant were possible drops from purchasing March 2019's Item Of The Month for 200,000 gems in the Store.
##  Function
Elephants can fly!

No more ear power!

When equipped, the **Morty the Orange Elephant** grants the _Slow-Fall: Ear Flap_ mod, which allows the player to glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button. It will also replace the player's punching animation with peanuts being fired from the trunk.
