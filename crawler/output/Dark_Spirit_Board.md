# Dark Spirit Board

This is the page for **Dark Spirit Board**.
You may be looking for **Spirit Board** instead.
Able to commune with dark and devastating powers from beyond the veil, this board can rip answers from the other side in the hands of a talented medium! Requires 5 brave Growtopians to stand in a circle around the board in order to properly contact the Realms Beyond. Only the owner of a world can place this board for safety reasons. WARNING: Using this means meddling with incredibly strong powers that can damage - and maybe destroy - worlds! Be careful where you use it. Your bedroom may not be the best idea!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can only be used in World-Locked worlds.

The **Dark Spirit Board** is an unsplicable solid foreground block which was added as part of the B.O.O. Update on May 1, 2018.
##  Function
_SeeSpirit Board for more information on how to complete the Dark Spirit Board's riddles._
_SeeBoss Ghost for more information on how to defeat the Boss Ghost summoned by the Dark Spirit Board._
Similar to the Spirit Board, when wrenched, the board displays riddles that the player must solve. After solving the riddles it will either make the board explode and nothing happens, spawns in 6-8 amount of Ghosts/Mind Ghosts or will summon the Boss Ghost.
##  Trivia
  * The Dark Spirit Board functions similarly to the Spirit Board. Unlike the latter, however, it has the capability to summon the Boss Ghost.
