# Super Tights (disambiguation)

This is a **disambiguation** page.
There are more than one subjects in the **Growtopia Wiki** using this name.
**Super Tights** are pants items which can be obtained randomly by splicing or defeating a villain. There are 6 kinds which consists of 6 different colours.
  * Super Tights - Red
  * Super Tights - Blue
  * Super Tights - Green
  * Super Tights - Purple
  * Super Tights - Black
  * Super Tights - Gold

There are also 4 **Super Tights** that are based on St. Patrick's Week, Easter Week, Harvest Festival and Super Pineapple Party events.
  * Super Tights - Emerald
  * Super Tights - Pink
  * Super Tights - Autumn
  * Super Tights - Pineapple
