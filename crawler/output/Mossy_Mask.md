The **Mossy Mask** is an unsplicable face item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2023 Day 7 on September 3, 2023.
##  Function
When equipped, the Mossy Mask serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The  Mossy Mask is the mossy counterpart of the  Stone Mask.
  * When fishing in a world with a Weather Machine - Jungle activated or a Jungle Blasted world, a Mossy Mask may replace a caught perfect  Stonefish.
