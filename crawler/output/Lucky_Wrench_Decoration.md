The **Lucky Wrench Decoration** is a wrench decoration which was added as part of the March Season on March 1, 2025.
##  Function
When equipped through the 'Wrench Customization' tab when wrenching oneself, the Lucky Wrench Decoration adds a pile of gold at the bottom left of the player's wrench connected to their name.
