**The One Ring** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of The Carnival update on October 13, 2014.
##  Function
No one will take your precious!

I guess someone took your precious.

When equipped, the **The One Ring** grants the _The One Ring_ mod, which allows the player to change their skin color to invisible.
##  Trivia
  * The One Ring is a reference to a ring with the same name and function that appears in J. R. R. Tolkien's novel The Lord of the Rings.
  * When The One Ring is recycled, the player will gain 2,000 Gems.
