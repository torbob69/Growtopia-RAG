Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Voucher Dayz Cooking Crate** for **6 Grow Voucher** in the Growtopia Store.
"Each crate contains 40 ingredients and 1 Home Oven to cook a multitude of meals! Top Chef here you come."
Every purchase will give **5** items.
The **Voucher Dayz Cooking Crate** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block that was added as part of Voucher Dayz on June 25, 2021.
##  Function
When broken, the Voucher Dayz Cooking Crate will drop:
  * 1 of each Cooking Recipe:
    * Cooking Recipe - Apple Strudel
    * Cooking Recipe - Arroz Con Pollo
    * Cooking Recipe - BBQ Bacon Burger
    * Cooking Recipe - Beer-Battered Fish And Chips
    * Cooking Recipe - Berry Crepes
    * Cooking Recipe - Homemade Fish Taco
  * 2 of each ingredient:
    * Apple
    * Bacon
    * Blueberry
    * Chicken Meat
    * Dough
    * Egg
    * Fish Chunk
    * Flour
    * Green Beer
    * Ground Beef
    * Honey
    * Lettuce
    * Milk
    * Onion
    * Potato
    * Rice
    * Tomato
    * Sugar
    * Pepper
    * Salt
  * 1 Home Oven:
    * Home Oven
