The **Cactus Pot Block** is a splicable non-solid toggleable foreground block which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2023 on May 5, 2023.
##  Function
Being a toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block, a Cactus Pot Block can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Cactus Pot Block will change its appearance, showing an animation of an angry cactus. When toggled on, a Cactus Pot Block will act as Deadly Block (block category).
