# Cooking Recipe - BBQ Bacon Burger

To cook this recipe you will need the following:Ingredients: - Ground Beef - Baking Chocolate - Dough - Bacon - Tomato - LettuceSeasoning: - Salt - Pepper - SugarProcess:Add Ground Beef to an oven on Low heat. While the Ground Beef is still cold, add Dough and shortly after add the Bacon. Add the seasonings and wait until cooked all the way through. Then add Tomato and wait until perfectly cooked. When fully cooked, add Lettuce and immediately remove from the oven.Timings may vary so you will need to experiment.
