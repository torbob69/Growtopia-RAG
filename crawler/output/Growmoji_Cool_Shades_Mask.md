A totally non creepy mask to give those facial muscles a rest when you want everyone to know you are feeling like totally Cool Shades all day!

The **Growmoji Cool Shades Mask** is an unsplicable face item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2020 Day 7.
##  Function
When equipped, the Growmoji Cool Shades Mask serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is one of the several masks based on growmojis available in the game. Other growmoji-themed masks include:
