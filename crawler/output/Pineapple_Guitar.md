The **Pineapple Guitar** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2025 on June 5, 2025.
##  Function
<https://growtopiawiki.com/w/File:Hula.ogg>The Pineapple Guitar's music
When the player types the command /dance, a music plays while the player performs a special animation of themself playing the guitar.
##  Trivia
  * Pineapple Guitar uses the same music as Hula Bobblehead.
