The **Mythic Vermillion Crown** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Lunar New Year 2023 on January 21, 2023.
##  Function
You've got the power!

You've lost the power!

When equipped, the **Mythic Vermillion Crown** grants the _Punch Damage + Punch Power: Mythic Vermillion Crown_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and knock back other players further.
