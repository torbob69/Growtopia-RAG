# Silkworms! (update)

Growtopia Gazette
> Feb 1st: Silkworms!
> Raise, feed, and play with your own cuddly pet silkworm today! They might even help you make some new stuff.
> - @Seth and @Hamumu
The **Silkworm** update was introduced on February 1, 2016. It focused the addition of Silkworms, and sewing as a whole.
