The **Hay Cart** is an unsplicable platform foreground block which was added as part of Ezio update on October 14, 2018.
##  Function
A Hay Cart behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath.
##  Trivia
  * If the player jumps into the cart from a height of 11 blocks or above while wearing the full Ezio set, they will say a random quote from Assassin's Creed II.
