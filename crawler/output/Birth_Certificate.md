The **Birth Certificate** is an unsplicable consumable which was added on December 6, 2013.
##  Function
Consuming a Birth Certificate changes the user's GrowID. The item gives the _Recently Name-Changed_ mod, which lasts for 60 days and prevents the player from using another Birth Certificate until the mod expires.
Step 19 of the  Quest For Honor requires the player to deliver 3 of this item.
##  Trivia
  * As part of Player Appreciation Week 2019, the duration of the _Recently Name-Changed_ mod was increased to 60 days instead of 30.
  * As part of Player Appreciation Week 2020, players under the age of 13 can no longer use a Birth Certificate to change their GrowIDs. This change was made in order to comply with statutory law.[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Birth_Certificate#cite_note-1)
  * As part of Player Appreciation Week 2021, the Birth Certificate was removed from the Growtopia Store and now can be purchased from Locke for 15 World Locks.
    * The Birth Certificate's store description used to be _"Tired of being who you are? By forging a new birth certificate, you can change your GrowID! The Birth Certificate will be consumed when used. This item only works if you have a GrowID, and you can only use one every 60 days, so you're not confusing everybody."_.
