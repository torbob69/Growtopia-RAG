The **Pineapple Koa Lantern** is an unsplicable toggleable animated foreground block which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2018 on June 1, 2018.
##  Function
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Pineapple Koa Lantern can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Pineapple Koa Lantern will change its appearance, showing the lantern light up on fire. As a light source, a Pineapple Kow Lantern emits light in dark areas.
