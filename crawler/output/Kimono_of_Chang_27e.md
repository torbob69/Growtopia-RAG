The **Kimono of Chang'e** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2022 on September 9, 2022.
##  Function
Over the Moon!

Back to Earth!

When equipped, the **Kimono of Chang'e** grants the _High Jump : Kimono of Chang'e_ mod, which allows the player to jump one tile higher.
Additionally, when equipped along the Mantle of Chang'e, clouds and sparkles will emit from the player during movement.
##  Trivia
  * The **Kimono of Chang'e** is a reference to the Chinese mythology goddess of the moon of the same name's kimono.
  * This item is considered a part of the Chang'e set, which consists of the following items:

    * Mantle of Chang'e (chest)

    * Kimono of Chang'e (shirt)
