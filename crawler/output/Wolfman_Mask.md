The **Wolfman Mask** is an unsplicable hair item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2013 on October 28, 2013.
##  Function
When equipped, the Wolfman Mask serves a fully cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The Wolfman Mask is based on the head of a werewolf, a folkloric creature typically depicted as a human with the ability to shapeshift into a wolf.
  * As part of the Quality of Life Changes update on May 20, 2022, the **Wolfman Mask** was added for sale in the Sales-Man under Wolfworld items for 150 Wolf Tickets. This was later removed from the Sales-Man 30 minutes later because there were multiple complaints of the price being inconsistent.
