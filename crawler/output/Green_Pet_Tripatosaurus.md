The **Green Pet Tripatosaurus** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Growassic Park on October 7, 2016.
##  Function
When equipped, the Green Pet Tripatosaurus serves a purely cosmetic purpose and summons a Green Pet Tripatosaurus which follows the player around.
