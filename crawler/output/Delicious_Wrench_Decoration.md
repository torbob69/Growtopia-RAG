The **Delicious Wrench Decoration** is a wrench decoration which was added as part of the November Season on November 1, 2025.
##  Function
When equipped through the 'Wrench Customization' tab when wrenching oneself, the Delicious Wrench Decoration adds three slashes to the lower right of the player's wrench connected to their name.
