The **Creepy Caged Doll** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable animated foreground block which was added as part of Halloween Week 2019.
##  Function
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Creepy Caged Doll can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Creepy Caged Doll will change its appearance, showing a 2-frame animation of the doll banging on its cage.
