**Sacrifical Well** is an unobtainable block that was added during Halloween Week 2019. It is found in the world TOMBOFGROWGANOTH, and a total of 6 wells are placed throughout the world.
##  Function
The player has 20 minutes to explore the world and find a total of 6 Sacrificial Wells and sacrifice items to it. The wells only accept items between rarity 10-200, and 1 rarity worth of items grants 1 corrupted soul. Each well will accept only up to 8,000 rarity.
