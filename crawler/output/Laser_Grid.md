The **Laser Grid** is an unsplicable non-solid looming deadly foreground block that was added as part of the Cyblocks and Growmojis update.
##  Function
Being a looming deadly block, a Laser Grid acts as a deadly block for a while when a player passes by.
When placed on the top most layer in a world, the Laser Grid does not activate, and will not kill the player.
