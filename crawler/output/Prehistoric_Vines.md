# Prehistoric Vines

You can hide from rampaging dinosaurs in these vines.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.

Render Type: Vertical

Hardness: **3 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **4s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**

DNA Processing

Process the following DNA strands:
Plant DNA Strand X-277,
Plant DNA Strand Y-985 and

This recipe will yield **15** items each.
The **Prehistoric Vines** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of the Growassic Park update.
##  Function
When placed, the Prehistoric Vines serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * During Player Appreciation Week 2020 Day 4, the Prehistoric Vines' recipe was changed to drop 5 blocks each (from 1).
  * During Player Appreciation Week 2022 Day 4, the Prehistoric Vines' recipe was changed to drop 15 blocks each (from 5).
