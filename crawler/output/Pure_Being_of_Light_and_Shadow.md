The **Pure Being of Light and Shadow** an unsplicable pants item which was added as June 2021’s Item Of The Month on June 1, 2021.
##  Function
Your essence has been purified!

Fallen from grace!

When equipped, the **Pure Being of Light and Shadow** grants the _Speedy: Pure Being_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and change their skin color to white or black. Equipping the item will allow the player to have a light or dark glow, re-equipping will interchange the appearance while replacing the punch effect into a beam of whichever shade the player has equipped.
