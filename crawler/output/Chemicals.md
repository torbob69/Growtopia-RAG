# Chemicals

A **Chemical** is an unsplicable component with the appearance of a filled test tube.
##  Function
Chemicals can be combined with other items in a Chemical Combiner in order to create a new item.
##  Varieties
### Science Station Chemicals
Items: RecipesChemical G: Harvesting a Science Station. Most common chemical.
Chemical R: Harvesting a Science Station. Less common than Chemical G.
Chemical Y: Harvesting a Science Station. Less common than Chemical R.
Chemical B: Harvesting a Science Station. Less common than Chemical Y.
Chemical P: Harvesting a Science Station. The least common of all chemicals produced by one.
