The **Campfire** is a splicable non-solid toggleable animated foreground block which was added alongside the Wild West Pack on September 11, 2013.
##  Function
<https://growtopiawiki.com/w/File:Campfire.ogg>The Campfire's music
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Campfire can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Campfire will change its appearance, showing a 2-frame animation, as a light source, it emits light in dark areas and play a piece of music.
