# Block Party (update)

Growtopia Gazette
> June 12th: BLOCK PARTY! Today we bring an assortment of odds and ends that will let you build cooler worlds. Air ducts for your horror world? Check. -@Seth and @Hamumu
**Block Party** is an update which mainly introduced multiple new blocks. The update was released on June 12, 2015.
A sequel was implemented on April 2, 2019, bringing an even wider variety of new blocks.
