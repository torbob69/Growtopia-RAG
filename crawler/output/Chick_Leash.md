The **Chick Leash** is an unsplicable hand item which was added alongside the Farm Pack on July 6, 2013.
##  Function
When equipped, a chick pet appears and follows the player around. The Chick Leash serves a purely cosmetic purpose.

##  Trivia
  * Due to being a rare drop from breaking Chickens or harvesting their trees, the Chick Leash was found several months after the Farm Pack's release. The Calf Leash was found on an even later date.[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Chick_Leash#cite_note-1)
