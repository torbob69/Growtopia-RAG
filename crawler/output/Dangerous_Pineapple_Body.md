# Dangerous Pineapple Body

In order to obtain the **Dangerous Pineapple Body** , a player with a normal Pineapple Body (from eating 600 Pineapples) must be fed a Dangerous Pineapple by another player, also with a Pineapple Body. This will turn the former's body into a rotten pineapple with spikes.
The feeder will get the mod _Pineapple Tragic_ , which makes a dangerous pineapple orbit them for 20 minutes. When this mod is removed, they will get a free item from the _Pineapple Tragic_ loot table. If a regular player feeds a Dangerous Pineapple Body, however, they will only get the normal _Pineapple Magic_ mod.
Attempting to feed another player who has a Dangerous Pineapple Body will make the feeder explode and say the line, "_**OUCH!** Tragedy befalls those who try to feed the Dangerous Pineapple!_"
Once fed 20 Pineapples while having the Pineapple Body, the player explodes. This explosion removes one's Pineapple Logo, Pineapple Skin, and Pineapple Body.
After the explosion, the player can repeat the process again and again.
