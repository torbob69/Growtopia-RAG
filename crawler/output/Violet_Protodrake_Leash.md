The **Violet Protodrake Leash** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2014 on September 5, 2014.
##  Obtaining
### Achievements Required
  * **Bright Future** - Earned for harvesting a Balance Mooncake.
  * **Harvester of Worlds** - Earned for using a Harvest Moon Blast.
  * **Sacrifice** - Earned for feeding Growganoth 50 times.
  * **Costume Contest** - Earned for dressing as a vampire (Vamp Vest, Vampire Fangs, Vampire Cape and Slick Black Hair)
  * **Spirit of Giving** - Earned for giving the Growch 100 Winter Gifts.
  * **Deer Hunter** - Earned for shooting a player in an Antler Hat.
  * **Celebrate Good Times** - Earned for showing up for Growtopia's Anniversary!
  * **Life of the Party** - Earned for firing a Skyrocket while wearing a full Party outfit (Party Hat, Party Glasses or Party Horn, Party Vest, Party Pants and Party Socks).
  * **Heart Breaker** - Earned for smashing a Heartstone or a Golden Booty Chest.
  * **Stupid Cupid** - Earned for wearing a complete Cupid outfit, including Golden Halo. (Diaper, Ruby Necklace, Angel Wings and a Heartbow)
  * **Four Leaves** - Earned for using a Lucky Clover.
  * **Little Green Man** - Earned for catching a Leprechaun.
  * **Sixteen Dozen** - Earned for collecting 192 Magic Egg.
  * **Bouncing Baby Bunny** - Earned for hatching a Pet Bunny.
  * **Bash Cinco** - Earned for smashing 5 Ultra Pinatas
  * **La Vida De La Fiesta** - Earned for playing a Mariachi Guitar while wearing a Fiesta Sombrero.
  * **The Last Celebration** - Earned for detonating Super Fireworks.
  * **Summer Grillin** - Earned for cooking a Cheeseburger on a Barbecue Grill.

Upon completion, the player will receive the  **Growtopian Of The Year** achievement.
##  Function
When equipped, a purple dragon pet appears and follows the player around. The Violet Protodrake Leash serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
### Pet Battle Ability
Pet Battles

Violet Protodrake Leash
Fire Battle Pet
Ability
**Purple Haze** : Weather Effect. Cover the world in burning haze for 10s, increasing all Fire damage by 25%, and reducing all other damage by 25%. Replaces any other active Weather Effect.
**Cooldown** : 30 seconds.
##  Trivia
  * The Violet Protodrake is a reference to the Mount “Reins of the Violet Proto-Drake” from World Of Warcraft which is also obtained by completing achievements.
  * Although the Violet Protodrake Leash is untradeable, it can still be traded via a Battle Pet Cage.
