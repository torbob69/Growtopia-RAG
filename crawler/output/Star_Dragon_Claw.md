The **Star Dragon Claw** is an unsplicable pet hand item which was added as part of the Dragon Invasion update.
##  Function
Star draconic wisdom!

Your astronomical wisdom is no more.

When equipped, the **Star Dragon Claw** grants the _XP Buff: Startopia_ mod, which allows the player to gain an extra 200 XP when successfully completing a star voyage. It will also stack with Chaos Dragon's effect, which gives a bonus of 300 XP.
