The **Deluxe Grow Spray** is an unsplicable consumable which was added on Player Appreciation Week 2014 Day 7 on August 14, 2014.
##  Function
When consumed on a tree, the Deluxe Grow Spray will speed up the time required for the tree to grow by 24 hours (1 day).
