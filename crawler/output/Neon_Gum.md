**Neon Gum** is a consumable introduced in the Strange Crystals update.
##  Function
Dazzle your friends as super funky neon lines course across your skin.

Party's over.

When consumed, it gives the player the Neon Gum! mod which allows the player to run faster with veins on the player's face for 5 seconds.
