The **Skull of Burning Horrors** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2021 on October 21, 2021.
##  Function
Fatal terror!

Free to breathe again!

When equipped, the **Skull of Burning Horrors** grants the _Punch Damage: Skull of Burning Horrors_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster.
The  The Seven Seas : The Curse of the Ghost Pirate! mission requires 1 of this item.
