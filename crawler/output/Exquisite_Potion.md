The **Exquisite Potion** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Role Up! update.
##  Function
Gain extra Points from exquisite anomalizers!

Your stomach's rumbling for more potion goodness!

When consumed, this item grants the _Exquisite!_ mod for 30 minutes, which increases the points gained from all anomalizing tools by 10% during a Seasonal Clash.
##  Trivia
  * Previously, consuming an Exquisite Potion would increase the points gained from exquisite anomalizers by 10%. As of Summer Clash 2019, exquisite anomalizers could no longer give any point. Thus, the Exquisite Potion's function was changed to affect all kinds of anomalizers.
