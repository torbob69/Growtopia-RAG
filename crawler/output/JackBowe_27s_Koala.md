# JackBowe's Koala

They are very cute until they get angry! This little furry guy stands as tribute to all of those who lost their lives in Australian Wild Fires. They will always be remembered. Sponsored by JackBowe for completing all epic quests!

The **JackBowe's Koala** is an unsplicable hand item which was added on November 1, 2020.
##  Function
Extinguish those flames!

Stopped, for now!

When equipped, the **JackBowe's Koala** grants the _Fire Hose : JackBowe's Koala_ mod, which allows the player to extinguish fires. It will also replace the player's punching effect with streams of water. No damage can be dealt to blocks while the mod is active.
##  Trivia
  * Upon completing the _Wild Fire Savior_ achievement, the player will be rewarded with JackBowe's Koala.
  * This item is sponsored by JackBowe for completing all the Epic Quests.
