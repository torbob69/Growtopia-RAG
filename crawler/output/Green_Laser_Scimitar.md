The **Green Laser Scimitar** is an unsplicable hand item which was added on November 1, 2019. The item, along with the Blue, Red, Purple, and Green Laser Scimitars were possible drops from purchasing November 2019's Item Of The Month for 200,000 Gems.
##  Function
Feel the power of the dark side!

Tucked away for safe keeping!

When equipped, the **Green Laser Scimitar** grants the _Punch Power + Build Range: Laser Scimitar_ mod, which allows the player to knock back other players further and build one tile further.
