The **Grow Punk Helmet** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2022 Day 3 on August 17, 2022.
##  Function
When equipped, the Grow Punk Helmet serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The Grow Punk Helmet, alongside the Grow Daft Helmet, is a reference to the French electronic duo Daft Punk.
  * Daft Punk were a musical duo from France and they were widely known for their music. The duo then split in 2021.
