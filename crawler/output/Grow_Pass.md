**Grow Pass** is a feature which was added as part of an update with the same name on October 5, 2021. In this battle–pass-inspired system, players are rewarded with in-game items as they complete tasks throughout each month. Two reward tiers are available: a free one which is available to all players, and a "royal" one which can be unlocked by consuming a Royal Grow Pass Token, purchased with real-world funds.
##  Item Of The Season     _Main article:Item Of The Season_
The Item Of The Season was added as a replacement for the Item Of The Month.
Every player can purchase the previous Item Of The Season for gems once in the Growtopia Store after they have reached 2,100 Grow Pass Points. Additionally, Royal Grow Pass users can purchase the current Item Of The Season for gems once after they have reached 2,100 Grow Pass Points.
A royal version of each Item Of The Season exists; such item can only be claimed as a Royal Grow Pass reward.

##  Tasks
  * Biweekly Quests
    * Grants 300 Grow Pass Points
  * Daily Quests
    * Grants 40 Grow Pass Points
  * Role Quests
    * Grants 20 Grow Pass Points
  * Life Goals
    * Grants 20 Grow Pass Points
  * Daily Bonuses
    * Grants 10 Grow Pass Points for all bonuses
    * Grants 40 Grow Pass Points when Mega Bonus has been completed

##  Royal Grow Pass Perks
In addition to unlocking rewards in the royal tier, Royal Grow Pass users get all the perks of the Monthly Subscriber Token:
  * Earning 5,500 gems daily
  * Having a reduced grow time on all seeds planted
  * Getting a small chance to earn bonus XP
  * Unlocking exclusive skin colors

Moreover, these players are given access to all Daily Bonuses, regardless of their player or awesomeness level.
