The **Rollback Plaque IX** is a solid foreground block which was added as part of the ninth Rollback. The block could be made by splicing a Cave Background Seed with a Sign Seed during the Apology Weekend that commenced on January 2, 2022.
##  Function
When placed, Rollback Plaque IX serves a fully aesthetic purpose.
##  Rollback VIII
On the 16th of December, a bug was found relating to accounts that have not yet earned Royal Winter Seals allowed them to generate millions of Royal Winter Seals. The servers were quickly placed into maintenance to protect the game's economy. However, due to system's limitations, a 23-hour rollback occurred since it was impossible to remove the generated Royal Winter Seals.
