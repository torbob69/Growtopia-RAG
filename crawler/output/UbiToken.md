The **UbiToken** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of UbiWeek 2023 Day 1 on February 24, 2023.
##  Function
Upon consuming 100 UbiTokens, the player will earn a random reward from the UbiCrate's loot table. Once enough UbiTokens have been found by Growtopians, the UbiCrate will become available in the store for 3 hours.
