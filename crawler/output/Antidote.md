The **Antidote** is a splicable consumable which was added alongside the Zombie Defense Pack on May 17, 2013.
##  Function
You are now immune to zombie bites! Temporarily...

Your immunity is gone.

When consumed on a player, it grants the player the _g-Virus Immunity_ mod, which grants the player a 15 minute-long immunity from the zombie infection (it can't protect from direct infection via the g-Virus itself). It is also used to cure players of the g-Virus, Doctor Repulsion or cures player's Silkworm when it gets sick. It doesn't cure players of the g-Virus during the Pandemic (it can still cure other consumables effect.)
##  Trivia
  * This item uses a similar texture to the Spike Juice, albeit with a darker liquid and a lack of spikes.
  * As of the Player Appreciation Week 2023, the Antidote's effect now lasts 15 minutes, a decrease from the previous 60 minutes.
