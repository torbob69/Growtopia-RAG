# WinterFest Starter Pack

The **WinterFest Starter Pack** is an annual Store-bought IAP which was added as part of the WinterFest 2023 update. It features the Winterfest Calendar and the Exclusive Pack Item of the year.
On 15 December, 2023

|
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **WinterFest Starter Pack** for **14.99 USD** in the Growtopia Store.
"An extra sweet deal to go with the event! Buying this pack will give you access to exlcusive Winterfest Club Items as they become available in the store."
Every purchase will give **1** item.
 |
## Items
The pack includes:
  * Gems ×650,000
  * Winterfest Calendar - 2023 ×5
  * Deluxe Winterfest Cracker ×15
  * Winterfest Cracker ×75
  * Snowball ×10
  * Santa's Spectacles
  * Royal Winter Seal ×1,800
