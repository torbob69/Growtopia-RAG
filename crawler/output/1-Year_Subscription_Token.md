Purchase the **1-Year Subscription Token** for **49.99 USD** in the Growtopia Store.
"One full year of special treatment AND 33 Growtokens upfront! You'll get 70 season tokens (as long as there's a seasonal clash running), and 9600 gems, and 1 World Lock every day and a chance of doubling any XP earned, growtime reduction on all seeds planted and Exclusive Skins!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **1-Year Subscription Token** is a consumable that was added on April 1, 2019. One can be purchased for $49.99 from the Growtopia Store, also giving 25 Growtokens.
##  Function
When consumed, the player will receive one year worth of benefits. They will receive 70 season tokens (if there is one running), 9600 gems and 1 World Lock every day. They will also obtain access to two exclusive skin colors, have all XP doubled and tree growth time reduced and will be given the Monthly Subscriber's Item every month.
##  Trivia
  * On 11th January 2019, the following was updated to Premium Subscriptions:
    * Players with a Premium Subscription now have a chance to get a new exclusive item every month. Every player with an active Premium Subscription (earned by consuming either a 30-Day or 365-Day token) who logs in between the 1st and the 5th of __ month will be entered into a draw. One player will be chosen to design and name a single wearable item or block for the game with the following rules:
      * No mods
      * No animations
      * No special effects
      * No player name reference (that's reserved for the sponsored items!)
      * If the chosen player does not respond within 48 hours of selection, a new candidate will be chosen from the same pool.
    * On the 1st to the 5th of the following month, all players with an active Premium Subscription (earned by consuming either a 30-Day or 365-Day token) will be automatically given the custom item. See more _here_
  * The 1-Year Subscription Token uses the same sprite as the 365-Day Premium Subscription Token.
  * As of 1 April 2019, the 1-Year Subscription Token replaced the 365-Day Premium Subscription Token in the Growtopia Store where the former is made untradable.
  * As of 24 February 2021, 365-Day Premium Subscription Token, 1-Year Subscription Token and 30-Day Premium Subscription Token now rewards the player 4000 Gems daily, previously 2500.
  * As of an unknown date, the 1-Year Subscription Token and 365-Day Premium Subscription Token now rewards the player 7000 Gems daily, previously 4000.
  * As part of Player Appreciation Week 2022 Day 5, the 1-Year Subscription Token and 365-Day Premium Subscription Token now rewards the player 8000 Gems + 1 World Lock daily, previously 7000 Gems and no World Locks. Additionally, Growtokens given upfront has been increased to 33 Growtokens, previously 25.
  * As part of Player Appreciation Week 2023 Day 4, the 1-Year Subscription Token and 365-Day Premium Subscription Token now rewards the player 9,600 Gems, previously 8,000 Gems.
