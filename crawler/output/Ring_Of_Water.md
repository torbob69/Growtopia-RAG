This mystical ring encases you in a bubble, allowing you to swim at high velocity. The cool bubble also protects you from dangerous burns like lava.

The **Ring of Water** is a hand item that was introduced during Player Appreciation Week 2015, Day 5.
##  Function
You are keeping your cool!

It's getting hot in here!

When equipped, the **Ring Of Water** grants the _Slightly Damp_ mod, which allows the player to move faster in water, take 50% less damage from lava pain blocks, and change their skin color to light blue. It will also surround the player with a bubble.
