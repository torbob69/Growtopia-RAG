The **Stone Mask** is an unsplicable face item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2023 Day 7 on September 3, 2023.
##  Function
When equipped, the Stone Mask serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The  Stone Mask has a mossy counterpart:  Mossy Mask.
  * When catching a perfect  Stonefish, there is a chance for the perfect Stonefish to be replaced with a Stone Mask instead.
