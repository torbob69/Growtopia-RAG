The **Frozen Wrench Decoration** is a wrench decoration which was added as part of the December Season on December 1, 2025.
##  Function
When equipped through the 'Wrench Customization' tab when wrenching oneself, the Frozen Wrench Decoration adds two snowflakes to the corners of the player's wrench connected to their name.
