The **Adventurer's Whip** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of The Adventure Pack Strikes Back! on June 1, 2017.
##  Function
Whip it good!

Cool it with that whip.

When equipped, the **Adventurer's Whip** grants the _Whipped Up_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further. It will also give the player a whipping punch effect.
##  Trivia
  * The Adventurer's Whip is based on a bullwhip used by the titular protagonist of the _Indiana Jones_ series.
