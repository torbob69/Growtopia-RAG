**Deep Rock** is an unsplicable solid foreground block which was added alongside the addition of Undersea Blast on April 11, 2014.
##  Function
When placed, the Deep Rock serves a purely aesthetic purpose.

##  Trivia
  * Deep Rock is a dark green retextured version of Boulder.
  * Deep Rock can turn into a Deep Fossil Rock if there are people in the world, similar to how a Rock can turn into a Fossil Rock.
