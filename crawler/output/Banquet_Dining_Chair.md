The **Banquet Dining Chair** is a non-solid seat foreground block that was added as part of Summer Clash 2018 on June 1, 2018.
##  Function
When a player stands in front of a seat such as the Banquet Dining Chair for a few seconds, the player will automatically be seen "sitting" on it.
