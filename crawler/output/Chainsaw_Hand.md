The **Chainsaw Hand** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2015 on October 29, 2015.
##  Function
You slice and dice!

Back to punching.

When equipped, the **Chainsaw Hand** grants the _Slasher_ mod, which allows the player to kill zombies during a Pandemic. It will also replace the player's punching animation with a slashing effect.
As of WinterFest 2019, when a player equipping a Chainsaw Hand punches an Ice Sculptures, its appearance will transform into one of the four available ones: candy cane, flower, teddy bear, or star. Each punch will guarantee a different outcome.
##  Trivia
  * The item is a reference to the Evil Dead series of films, in which the protagonist severs his possessed hand and attaches a chainsaw in its place.
