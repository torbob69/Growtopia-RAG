##  Function
When wrenched, one gives the current status of the pandemic.
### Pandemic Levels
  * Low Risk: 10k-140k player infected.
  * Threatening: 140k-250k player infected.
  * High Risk: 250k-400k player infected.
  * Nightmare: 400k-500k **(**G-Virus Pandemic starts at 500k**)** player infected.
