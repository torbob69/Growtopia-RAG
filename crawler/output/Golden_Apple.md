The **Golden Apple** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Halloween Week 2014 on October 30, 2014.
##  Function
Doctors won't come near you!

You no longer repel doctors.

When consumed, it gives the player the _Doctor Replusion_ mod, which prevents players from performing surgery on the player for 24 hours. However, unlike the Apple, the Golden Apple can be used on other players, thus, it is able to remove other player's Legendary Title status for 24 hours.
### Silkworm Food     _Main article:Guide:Silkworm Care_
This consumable can be fed to a Silkworm to give it a yellow color, thus allowing it to produce 1–10  Silk Thread - Yellow per harvest. However, due to the Golden Apple's rarity, it isn't recommended to feed the item to a Silkworm.
##  Trivia
  * Golden Apples were given out by the ex-moderator STYX for completing the parkour in the world **TALL**. However, due to STYX's resignation, the item is now only obtainable by breaking a Golden Treasure Hoard, which have an extremely low chance of dropping one.
  * The Golden Apple was previously used in Step 16 of the Quest For The Blade, until it was replaced by the Neptune's Gauntlet during SummerFest 2026.
