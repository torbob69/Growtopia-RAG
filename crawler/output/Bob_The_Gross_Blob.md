**Bob The Gross Blob** is an unsplicable pet chest item which was added as part of the Gross-Out update on May 1, 2019.
##  Function
Slippery Grossness!

Bob is tired!

When equipped, the **Bob The Gross Blob** grants the _Bob The Gross Blob_ mod, which allows the player to glide on any surface as if it is slippery.
