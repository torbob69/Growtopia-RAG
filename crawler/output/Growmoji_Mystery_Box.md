# Growmoji Mystery Box
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Growmoji Mystery Box** for **15 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"Express yourself! This mysterious box contains one of six fun growmojis you can use to spice up your chat! Which will you get?"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Growmoji Mystery Box** is a store pack which was added as part of March Update on March 1, 2018.
##  Items
When purchased, it has a chance to give out one of the following:
  * Growmoji World Lock
  * Growmoji Grow
  * Growmoji Gems
  * Growmoji Growtoken
  * Growmoji Plead
  * Growmoji Vend
