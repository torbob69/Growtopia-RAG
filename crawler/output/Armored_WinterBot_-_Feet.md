**Armored WinterBot - Feet** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of WinterFest 2019.
##  Function
Speedy Feet!

Your walking on thin ice!

When equipped, the **Armored WinterBot - Feet** grants the _Speedy: Winterbot Shoes!_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and transform into the Armored WinterBot when the entire Armored WinterBot set is equipped.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Armored WinterBot set, which consists of the following items:

    * Armored WinterBot - Back (back)

    * Armored WinterBot - Mail (shirt)

    * Armored WinterBot - Greaves (pants)

    * Armored WinterBot - Feet (feet)

    * Armored WinterBot - Fists (hand)
