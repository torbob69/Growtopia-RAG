The **Wings of Daidalos** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of UbiWeek 2022 Day 3 on January 26, 2022.
##  Function
Take to the skies!

Down to earth!

When equipped, the **Wings of Daidalos** grants the _Double-Jump & Slow-Fall: Wings of Daidalos_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
##  Trivia
  * Wings of Daidalos is based on the Daidalos Wings, a piece of equipment in the action-adventure game _Immortals: Fenyx Rising_ , which was developed by Ubisoft Quebec.
  * During UbiWeek 2022, the Wings of Daidalos could be obtained from exploding Pure Magic Ores using Mining Explosives. As of UbiWeek 2023, the Wings of Daidalos is only obtainable from UbiCrates.
