# Amber Necklace

The ancient magic of this necklace calls back to the creatures of a long-lost age, bringing them closer to your world! Gives you a 5% chance of getting double fossils when smashing Fossil Rocks!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.

The **Amber Necklace** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2018 Day 6 on August 11, 2018.
##  Function
Bontiful harvest!

Aw, no more bonus fossils.

When equipped, this item grants the _Extra Fossils from fossil rocks..._ mod, which gives a 5% chance of doubling the amount of Fossils earned from breaking any type of fossil rocks.
