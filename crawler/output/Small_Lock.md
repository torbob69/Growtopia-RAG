The **Small Lock** is an unsplicable solid lock foreground block which was added as part of Growtopia's launch.
##  Function
A Small Lock can lock a maximum of ten tiles. It can be wrenched to change its settings such as giving other players access, ignoring empty air, or setting the locked area to public.
##  Trivia
  * During the early times of Display Block's release, all kind of locks could be displayed in Display Blocks. However, this lead to arise of a new scamming method, where world owners made the world public and asked other players to place locks into the Display Blocks, so they could steal the locks afterwards. Hence, in order to prevent this type of scams, the DEV team decided to make locks undisplayable. However, some worlds with displayed Small Locks were left behind, which are quite rare nowadays.
