The **Riding Bell** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2019.
##  Function
Celebrate the coming of the harvest in style.

Your feet are back on solid ground.

When equipped, the **Riding Bell** grants the _Speedy:Riding Bell_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land. Jumping while equipping the item produces a bell sound effect.
