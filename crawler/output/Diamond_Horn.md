The **Diamond Horn** is a hat item which was added on the 1st of June, 2017 as June 2017's Item Of The Month.
##  Function
You shoot lightning from your horn(s)!

Tired of tooting your own horn?

When equipped, the **Diamond Horn** grants the _Energized Horn_ mod, which allows the player to build one tile further. The Diamond Horn cycles between 3 items — unequipping and re-equipping it transforms it into another horn.
  * **1st form** (Diamond Horn): Retextured version of Unicorn Horn with a similar punch effect with Focused Eyes.
  * **2nd form** (Diamond Horns): Retextured version of Demonic Horns (MAHHTIN's moderator item), but thinner with a punch effect similar to a Heavenly Scythe.
  * **3rd form** (Diamond Devil Horns): Retextured version of Helmet Of Darkness horns with an original new punch effect that highly resembles to lightning.

##  Trivia
  * On the 1st of June 2017, a duplication exploit was found for the Diamond Horn involving the Phone Booth which resulted in 1300 players being banned. 600 of those were perma-banned. Some exploiters had their Diamond Horns removed and were unbanned.
