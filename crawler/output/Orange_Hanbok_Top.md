The **Orange Hanbok Top** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2016 on September 15, 2016.
##  Function
When equipped, the Orange Hanbok Top serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia

    * Blue Hanbok Top
    * Blue Hanbok Pants
    * Orange Hanbok Pants
    * Green Hanbok Skirt
    * Yellow Hanbok Skirt
