The **Golden Leprechaun Hat** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2021 on March 11, 2021.
##  Function
When equipped with the Golden Leprechaun Suit, it will give a chance at receiving an extra bonus item when kissing the Blarney Stone.
