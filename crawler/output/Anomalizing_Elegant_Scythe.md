The **Anomalizing Elegant Scythe** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the Role Up! update.
##  Function
Anomalizing Elegant Scythes are used to score points during Harvest Heroes. While equipping this item, each tree harvested yields 220 points. Elegant anomalizers, such as this item, may rarely break upon usage during their respective Grow Events.
