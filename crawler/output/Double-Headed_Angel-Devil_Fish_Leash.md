The **Double-Headed Angel-Devil Fish Leash** is an unsplicable hand item, introduced during the Gone Fishin'! update on January 14, 2018.
##  Function
When equipped, a double-headed angel-devil fish pet appears and follows the player around. The Double-Headed Angel-Devil Fish Leash serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
