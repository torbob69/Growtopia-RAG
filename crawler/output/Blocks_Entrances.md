# Blocks/Entrances

< Blocks
**Entrances** are non-solid foreground blocks which allows players with access to walk through it, or, when its property is changed, allowing any player to walk through it. When placed in a public area, it is impossible to be closed for anyone.
**Entrances**

Item: Description
Air Vent: How else are you going to get into the air ducts?
Ancient Stone Gate: What could lurk behind such a barrier? Ancient secrets? Cursed mummies? Perhaps a stash of purple blocks.
Baroque Iron Gate: The only problem with this gate is the constant repair bills, but you know what they say: If it ain't baroque, don't fix it!
Coin Door: The gateway to prosperity! May it always be open to you.
Dark Magic Barrier: This demonic energy field only lets the right people through. Wrench it to adjust settings like any other doorway.
Forcefield: This directed energy field has the power to function as solid matter for people who aren't invited, while being completely permeable to anyone else. I guess that makes it a door.
Growtorial Entrance: It's like a wall, except you can choose to let your friends come in! Only usable in the Growtorial!
Guild Entrance - Normal: Members only! This plain - but effective - private door only lets people in your guild (or folks you like) pass through. Wrench it to add guild members and any 'others'. Note: Guild members will automatically be removed if they leave the guild!
Guild Entrance - Ornate: Members only! This ornate private door only lets people in your guild (or folks you like) pass through. Wrench it to add guild members and any 'others'. Note: Guild members will automatically be removed if they leave the guild!
Hidden Door: Looks like an Art Deco Block, but with extra friendship - you can use this to allow access to others! Just, y'know, keep it secret. Hidden Doors are only cool if they're secret.
House Entrance: It's like a wall, except your friends can come in!
Jade Portcullis: Lock out the ruffians with this decorative gateway.
Jail Door: You don't need a spoon to dig out, just open this.
Locked Adventure Door: This door can be walked through by anyone holding an Adventure Item - Key.
Portcullis: Lock out the ruffians with this medieval gateway.
Red House Entrance: It's like a wall, except your friends can come in! ...and it's red! That's an important difference, too!
Secret Passage: Looks like a Wood Block, but your friends can come in!
Team Entrance - Build: The only people getting through this door are the ones who are on team Build. Outside of Balloon Warz, this functions as a normal entrance.
Team Entrance - Grow: The only people getting through this door are the ones who are on team Grow. Outside of Balloon Warz, this functions as a normal entrance.
Team Entrance - Punch: The only people getting through this door are the ones who are on team Punch. Outside of Balloon Warz, this functions as a normal entrance.
