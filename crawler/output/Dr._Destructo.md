# Dr. Destructo

This is an **NPC** , or a non-player character.
**@Dr. Destructo** is an NPC who appears during the _Dr. Destructo_ Special Event where players in the world are required to defeat him by punching him. Dr. Destructo has a Destructo Ray. When players are hit with it, they are frozen for a few seconds. Dr. Destructo also has the ability to spawn Public Lava around the world which can be broken by all players. A health bar will appear, displaying his health, which reduces as he gets punched. Dr. Destructo has the ability to regenerate if not kept under attack.
###  Appearance
Dr. Destructo has the appearance of a Growtopian wearing the following:
  * Baneful Mask (Face)
  * Pinstripe Suit (Shirt)
  * Pinstripe Pants (Pants)
  * Boots (Feet)
  * Destructo Ray (Hand)

## Trivia
  * Dr. Destructo is usually controlled by a moderator or developer.
  * Upon defeating Dr. Destructo, everyone in the world will receive the _Superhero_ achievement.
  * Dr. Destructo’s real name is Dennis as seen in Super Logo - D’s description.
