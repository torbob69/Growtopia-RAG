The **Crimson Thief Mask** is an unsplicable face item which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2023 on February 10, 2023.
##  Function
No power, no love!

That's the power of love!

When equipped, the **Crimson Thief Mask** grants the _Punch Damage : Crimson Thief Mask_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Crimson Thief set, which consists of the following items:

    * Crimson Thief Hair (Hair)

    * Crimson Thief Mask (Face)

    * Crimson Thief Coat (Shirt)

    * Crimson Thief Pants (Pants)
