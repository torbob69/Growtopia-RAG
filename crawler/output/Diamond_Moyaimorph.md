The **Diamond Moyaimorph** is a unsplicable chest item which was added as part of the Pre-WinterFest 2023 on December 1, 2023.
##  Function
Diamond Head!

Just a myth...

When equipped, the Diamond Moyaimorph grants the _Diamond Moyaimorph: Sparkes_ mod, which causes the player to emit sparkles.
##  Trivia
  * The  Diamond Moyaimorph is a recolor of  Moyaimorph.
  * There are a total of 4 Moyaimorphs in Growtopia, including  Moyaimorph,  Golden Moyaimorph,  Diamond Moyaimorph, and  Pearl Moyaimorph.
