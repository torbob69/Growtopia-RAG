# Prehistoric Red Fern

It's sad to think about where this grows.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.

The **Prehistoric Red Fern** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Growassic Park on October 7, 2016.
##  Function
When placed, the Prehistoric Red Fern serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The description of the Prehistoric Red Fern is a reference to the children's novel _Where the Red Fern Grows_.
  * As part of Player Appreciation Week 2020 Day 4, the quantity of Prehistoric Red Fern's obtained from a DNA Combination was increased from 1 to 5.
  * As part of Player Appreciation Week 2022 Day 4, the quantity of Prehistoric Red Fern's obtained from a DNA Combination was increased from 5 to 15.
