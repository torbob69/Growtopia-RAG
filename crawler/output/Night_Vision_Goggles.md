The **Night Vision Goggles** is a face item that came with the Spelunkorama! update. It can be purchased from the Growtoken Store for 110 Growtokens.
##  Function
When equipped, the **Night Vision Goggles** grants the _Night Vision_ mod, which allows the player to eliminate the darkness effect created by dark background blocks. Additionally, it will also require a D Battery to work every minute, if the player is not in a world they own.
