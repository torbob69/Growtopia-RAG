The **Mad Hatter** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of the Winter Clash update on November 2, 2018.
##  Function
Mad as a hatter!

That's odd. You have no hat!

When equipped, the **Mad Hatter** grants the _Punch Damage : Madness!_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. Upon punching other players, they will receive the _We are all mad here!_ mod, which temporarily gives the players one of the three Mad Hats. This mod lasts 10 seconds.
