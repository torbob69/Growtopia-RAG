The **Battle Boar** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of the Treasure Hunt update.
##  Function
When equipped, the Battle Boar serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The Battle Boar is based on the war pigs, which historically have been reported to be used in ancient warfare as military animals.
  * The Battle Boar is one of the more uncommon rewards that can be obtained from completing a Treasure Map.
    * The  Golden Battle Boar, which is a recolor of the  Battle Boar, is even rarer than the latter.
