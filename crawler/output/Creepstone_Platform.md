The **Creepstone Platform** is an unsplicable platform foreground block which was added as part of Halloween Week 2013 on October 28, 2013.
##  Function
A Creepstone Platform behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath.
### Exclusive Function
Creepstone Platforms can be found in GROWGANOTH worlds, allowing the player to gain access to Growganoth's maw. They are also used in the Growganoth Gulch, a miniature CARNIVAL version of GROWGANOTH.
Any of the Creepstone Platforms found in such worlds may randomly transform into the non-solid Disabled Platforms, and vice versa, thus making it more challenging for players to climb to the top.
