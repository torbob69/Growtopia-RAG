The **Chinese Fireworks** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Chinese New Year 2018.
##  Function
When consumed, several fireworks will fly out vertically and explode into colorful explosions. This item's effect is purely decorative and identical to that of SummerFest's Fireworks or Anniversary Week's Skyrocket.
##  Trivia
  * The description of Chinese Fireworks alludes to the fact that fireworks were originally invented in China.
