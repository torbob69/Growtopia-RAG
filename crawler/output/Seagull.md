The **Seagull** is a splicable platform foreground block which was added on Player Appreciation Week 2014 Day 5 on August 12, 2014.
##  Function
A Seagull behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath. In addition, it hovers around the tile it is placed on visually.
