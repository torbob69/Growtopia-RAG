The **Anomalizing Pristine Trowel** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Summer Clash Series 1.
##  Function
Anomalizing Pristine Trowels are used to score points during Block Builders. While equipping this item, each block placed down yields 80 points. Pristine anomalizers, such as this item, may sometimes break upon usage during their respective Grow Events.
