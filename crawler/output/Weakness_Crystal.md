The **Weakness Crystal** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Wonder Week 2025 - Day 1 on October 6, 2025.
##  Function
When placed, the Weakness Crystal serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
The Weakness Crystal is a reference to the fictional material Kryptonite from DC Comics' Superman.
