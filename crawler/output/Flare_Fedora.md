# Flare Fedora

Flame on with this dapper fedora!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be transmuted.
Data

Type: Hat – Clothes
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: Full Collision
Hardness: **1 Hits** **1 Hits** – Restores after **2s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Combustion

Burn a(n)  Fedora **(x200)** in a  Highly Combustible Box.
The **Flare Fedora** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2023 Day 7 on September 3, 2023.
##  Function
Burn! Buddy! Burn!

Noir Infenro!

When equipped, the **Flare Fedora** grants the _Fireproof_ mod, which allows the player to take 50% less damage from lava pain blocks.
##  Trivia
  * The **Flare Fedora** is a reference to Portgas D. Ace, better known by his epithet "Fire Fist" Ace, from the Japanese manga and anime series One Piece.
