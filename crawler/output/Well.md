The **Well** is a non-solid provider foreground block which was added as part of the Block Party update on June 12, 2015.
##  Function
The Well is a type of provider. When a Well is harvested (by being punched), 1-2 Water Bucket will drop. One can be harvested approximately every 24 hours.
