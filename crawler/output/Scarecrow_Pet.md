# Scarecrow Pet

This little guy's tired of frightening birds (that time a flock of ravens stole his hat was the last straw), so he's decided to join up with you and see if your adventures are his kind of field!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Hand – Clothes
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: Full Collision
Hardness: **0 Hits** **0 Hits** – Restores after **0s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Purity Mooncake **(×50)** ,
Ancestor Mooncake **(×35)** and
Peace Mooncake **(×150)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Scarecrow Pet** is a unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2019.
##  Function
Throw hay from far away!

Hay, hay, hay!

When equipped, the **Scarecrow Pet** grants the _Punch Range + Reduced Damage: The Last Straw_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further and break blocks more slowly.
