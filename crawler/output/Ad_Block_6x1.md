Place these blocks in a 6x1 grid to show ads in your world. Check your ad earnings for your world with the Ad Control Block.

Purchase the **Advertisers Pack** for **10,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"With this pack you can build custom Ad billboards in your world. There are 3 sizes you can choose from, 2x3, 3x2 and 6x1. You need to arrange these in a grid to activate the Ad. Remember to check your Gem earnings each day using the Ad Control Block."
Every purchase will give **24** items.
The **Ad Block 6x1** is an unsplicable solid foreground block that was added as part of the Growtopia ADventures update on June 22, 2021.
##  Function
In order to fully utilize the Ad Block's function, it needs to be placed together with other Ad Blocks of the same type in an 6×1 grid. The area will then be covered with an 6×1 advertisement.
##  Trivia
  * As of July 16, 2021, the requirement of 8x1 ad blocks was changed to 7x1 to show an AD. This caused the name change, and the reduction of receiving 14 blocks instead of 16 being needed to create 2 ad placements.
  * As of September 21, 2023, the requirement of 7x1 ad blocks was changed to 6x1 to show an AD. This caused the name change, and the reduction of receiving 12 blocks instead of 16 being needed to create 2 ad placements.
