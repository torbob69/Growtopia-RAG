##  Trivia
  * The Fist of Vengeance's equip and unequip messages are a reference to the fictional catchphrase "_It's Morbin' Time_ ", attributed to the main character in the 2022 American superhero film Morbius as part of the ongoing joke surrounding the film of inventing aspects of the movie.
