The **Mechanical Dove Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Easter Week 2022 on April 15, 2022.
##  Function
Soaring, Flying!

Diving, Falling!

When equipped, the **Mechanical Dove Wings** grants the _Double Jump: Mechanical Dove Wings!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
