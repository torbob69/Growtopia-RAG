The **Nobleman's House Door** is an unsplicable door which was added during the Winter Clash update on November 2, 2018.
##  Function
As a door, the Nobleman's House Door can be entered (via punching) by a player, moving them instantly to another tile. A block with an ID must first be placed on the tile for this to work.
