The **Huge Lock** is an unsplicable solid lock foreground block which was added as part of Growtopia's launch.
##  Function
A Huge Lock can lock a maximum of 200 tiles. It can be wrenched to change its settings such as giving other players access, ignoring empty air, or setting the locked area to public.
### Possibilities
Item: RecipeWorld Lock: 1 can be purchased from Locke The Salesman or Lock-Bot for Huge Lock (x5).
##  Trivia
  * During the early times of Display Block's release, all kind of locks could be displayed in Display Blocks. However, this lead to arise of a new scamming method, where world owners made the world public and asked other players to place locks into the Display Blocks, so they could steal the locks afterwards. Hence, in order to prevent this type of scams, the DEV team decided to make locks undisplayable. However, some worlds with displayed Huge Locks were left behind, which are quite rare nowadays.
  * The  Huge Lock is a recolor of  World Lock and  Diamond Lock.
