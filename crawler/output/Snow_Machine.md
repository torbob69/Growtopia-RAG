The **Snow Machine** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2025 on September 18, 2025.
##  Function
When placed, being a toggleable animated foreground block, a _Snow Machine_ can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a _Snow Machine_ will change its appearance, showing snow being created by the machine. Players can also paint the Snow Machine to alter the color of the snow particles accordingly.
##  Trivia
  * A maximum of ten **Snow Machines** can be placed in one world at a time.
