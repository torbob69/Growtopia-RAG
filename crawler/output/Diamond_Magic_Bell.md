The **Diamond Magic Bell** is an unsplicable non-solid SFX foreground block which was added as part of Pre-WinterFest 2025 on December 1, 2025.

##  Function
Being a type of SFX foreground block, a Diamond Magic Bell plays a short animation and a ringing sound effect when punched.
##  Trivia
  * The  Diamond Magic Bell is a recolor of the  Magic Bell.
