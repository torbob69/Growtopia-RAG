**Dino DNA Strand A-273** is a DNA strand which was released along as part of the Growassic Park Update. It is obtainable from extracting fossil drops using a DNA Extractor.
##  Trivia
The alphabet "A" stands for adenine, one of the four nucleobases found in a DNA in real life.
