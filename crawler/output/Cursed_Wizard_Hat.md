The **Cursed Wizard Hat** is a splicable hat item which has been added since Growtopia's launch. It is one of the five Wizard Hats.
##  Function
Ye art accursed by evil magicks.

Thine curse be lifted!

When equipped, the **Cursed Wizard Hat** grants the _Black Magic_ mod, which allows the player to decrease the player's jump height, break blocks more slowly, and change their skin color to yellow. It will also decrease moving speed.
##  Trivia
  * While it may appear using the Cursed Wizard Hat with the Cyclopean Visor, Heartbow or Battle Trout will make the player unable to break any blocks it still deals an incredibly low amount of damage to the blocks but with enough hits you can still destroy blocks.
  * Using the Cursed Wizard Hat with the Dear John Tractor or Swim Fins will make the player move extremely slowly.
