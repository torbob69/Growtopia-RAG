The **Military Radio** is a splicable non-solid toggleable animated foreground block which was added alongside the Zombie Defense Pack on May 17, 2013.
##  Function
<https://growtopiawiki.com/w/File:Unspeakable.ogg>The Military Radio's music.
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Military Radio can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Military Radio will change its appearance, showing a 2-frame animation, and play a piece of music.
