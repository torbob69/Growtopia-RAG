The **Lucky Clover** is an unsplicable consumable that was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2013 on March 13, 2013.
##  Function
You're luckier than before!

Your luck has worn off.

When consumed, a Lucky Clover grants the _Lucky!_ mod to the player for thirty minutes, giving a 10% chance to multiply gem drops from breaking blocks by up to 5. It also provides a 10% chance for an extra block to be dropped.
This item's effect does not stack with that of Rabbit's Foot or Songpyeon, but stacks with Apology Weekend's bonus.
