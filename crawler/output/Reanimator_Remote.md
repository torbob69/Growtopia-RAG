The **Reanimator Remote** is an unsplicable hand item which was added alongside the Mad Science Kit on July 20, 2013.
##  Function
It's ALLLIVVVEE!!

It's dead.

When equipped, the **Reanimator Remote** grants the _Frankenpunch_ mod, which allows the player to knock back other players further. It will also summon a Frankenstein's monster pet which will follow the player around.

##  Trivia
  * The Reanimator Remote pet's design is based on the Frankenstein's monster, a fictional charactor who first appeared in Mary Shelley's 1818 novel Frankenstein; or, The Modern Prometheus.
    * The description of the item is a reference to a famous quote from the 1931 film adaptation.
