In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...

Golden Heart Crystal **(×1)** and
Heartbreak Wings **(×1)**.

The **Golden Heartbreak Wings** are an unsplicable back item which were added as part of Valentine's Week 2022 on February 11, 2022.
##  Function
Break some hearts!

You broke the wings!

When equipped, the **Golden Heartbreak Wings** grants the _Double Jump : Golden Heartbreak Wings!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
