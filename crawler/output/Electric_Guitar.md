# Electric Guitar

This is the page for **Electric Guitar**.
You may be looking for **Lightning Guitar** instead.
You can't punch when holding this, you're too busy ROCKING OUT! Punch anywhere to play music. If you punch to your left or right, you can bend the note higher or lower. Punch in your own column to play the same notes that Sheet Music: Piano Note plays.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be transmuted.
Data

Type: Hand – Clothes
Render Type: Single

Party Reward

It has a chance to drop as one of the five items from the _Anniversary Party!_ special event.
In addition to spawning randomly during Anniversary Week, the event can also be spawned directly from consuming a  Party-In-A-Box.
The **Electric Guitar** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Anniversary Week 2021 on January 7, 2021.
##  Function
You're too busy rocking to punch anybody!

The music has stopped.

When equipped, it grants the _Musical_ mod which loses the player's ability to break blocks and push other players by punching, but gains the ability to play Sheet Music: Electric Guitar notes by doing so. The sounds produced depend on the punched tile. Players can wrench themselves to see the note of their tile.
