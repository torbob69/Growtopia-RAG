The **Super Pineapple Party Crate** is an unsplicable and untradable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2019 on June 7, 2019.
##  Function
When broken, a Super Pineapple Party Crate may drop the following items:
  * Dangerous Pineapple ×3
  * Pineapple ×20-30
  * Pineapple Gift Box (rare)
  * Slippery Pineapple Squishers (rare)
  * Super Pineapple ×2
