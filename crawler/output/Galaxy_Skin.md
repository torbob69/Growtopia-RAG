##  Function
The power of the universe IS in your hands.

Back to reality!

When equipped, the **Galaxy Skin** grants the _Extra Blocks : Galaxy Skin_ mod, which allows the player to get a 10% chance for a block to drop an extra of itself when broken and change their skin color to purple-black.
