The **Riding Sand Shark** is a foot item, introduced during the Gone Fishin'! update. It is made by successfully completing level 10 training for a Sand Shark.
##  Function
Swim faster!

No shark for you.

When equipped, the **Riding Sand Shark** grants the _I'm On A Shark_ mod, which allows the player to move faster in water. It will also decrease moving speed on land.
