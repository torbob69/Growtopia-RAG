**Edison, The Lightbulb** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Grow4Good Week 2022 on April 22, 2022.
##  Function
Lighting up your world!

Light's out!

When equipped, the **Edison, The Lightbulb** grants the _Punch Damage: Edison, The Lightbulb_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster.
##  Trivia
  * During Grow4Good Week 2025, the point requirement to unlock the Edison, The Lightbulb was lowered from 120,000,000 to 110,000,000.
