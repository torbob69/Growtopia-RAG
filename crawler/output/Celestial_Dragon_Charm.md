The **Celestial Dragon Charm** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as November 2020’s Item Of The Month on November 1, 2020.
##  Function
The essence of the sun, moon and stars; I command you!

The new moon has risen!

When equipped, the **Celestial Dragon Charm** grants the _Lightsource: Celestial Dragon Charm_ mod, which allows the player to emit light in dark areas. It will also illuminate a slightly bigger area when compared to a Flashlight.
A special button will appear when the wearer wrenches themself, allowing the player to customize or lock the Celestial Dragon Charm varieties, transition time, and more.
