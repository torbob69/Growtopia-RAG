The **Vile Vial - Moldy Guts** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Blocktor's Orders! on June 13, 2019.
##  Function
You've been infected with moldy guts!

You no longer have moldy guts!

When consumed, the player will receive the _Moldy Guts_ malady mod for 12 hours, which can be cured through a successful surgery. Should the malady get cured, both players—the surgeon and patient—will receive a prize.
##  Trivia
