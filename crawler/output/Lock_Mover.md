# Lock Mover

Use this fabulous device anywhere in a world you have world-locked to move the World Lock to a new location. There must be an empty space where you tap for this to work.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **3 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **4s** of inactivity

**1h**
NPC Trading

Buy 1 of this item from ** Locke The Salesman or  Lock-Bot** for  World Lock **(x20)**.
"Tired of your World Lock sitting in the same old boring place in your world all day? Feeling nervous about smashing it and placing a new item? Fret no longer! With my handy-dandy Lock Mover, you can just choose a new spot in your world and slammo blammo, the lock is there! Works with all kinds of world locks."

The **Lock Mover** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo 2016 on May 2, 2016.
##  Function
A Lock Mover can be used to move any type of world locks to another part of the world without having to break and replace the lock. Doing so will consume the Lock Mover.
