# Guild Flag - Peak (Cross)

Show off your guild mascot and let everyone know you're at the top of your game with this peak-shaped and cross-patterned flag! Its colors will adapt to the seeds from your guild mascot!

Harvest a  Guild Flag - Peak Tree.__
The **Guild Flag - Peak (Cross)** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added alongside the Guilds update on April 1, 2018.
##  Function
When placed, the Guild Flag - Peak (Cross) will display the color of the seeds of the player's guild mascot.
