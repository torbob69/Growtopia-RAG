The **Achievement Block** is a splicable solid foreground block which was added alongside the City Pack on August 18, 2013.
##  Function
Upon wrenching, the owner of the world can display an achievement that they have earned in the game. By punching the block, players can view a message stating the achievement's title as well as the recipient's name.

##  Trivia
  * Punching a blank Achievement Block will read "_It's blank. Will no hero step up to etch it?!_ "
  * Wrenching an Achievement Block as the world's co-owner will read "_An Achievement Block can only be etched by the owner of this world. (Requires World Lock)_ "
  * Upon trading worlds with an etched Achievement Block, the block will not be reset and the block will state the new owner as the recipient. However, the new owner does not actually receive the achievement.
