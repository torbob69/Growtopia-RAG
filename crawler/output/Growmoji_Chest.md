A chest filled with some classic Growmoji's. These Growmoji's were previously only available with the monthly packs.

"You want Growmojis? You want rare ones? You want one's previously only available in Monthly Bundles? Yes? Then this is for you. What might you find inside? Slip me some locks and find out."
The **Growmoji Chest** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2021 Day 6 on August 28, 2021.
##  Function
When broken, it will randomly drop one of the 11 growmojis that were previously only available from purchasing Monthly Bundle IAPs:
  * Growmoji Alien
  * Growmoji Cake
  * Growmoji Clapping Hands
  * Growmoji Evil Devil
  * Growmoji Gift
  * Growmoji Grin
  * Growmoji Heart with Arrow
  * Growmoji Ill
  * Growmoji Pizza
  * Growmoji Shamrock
  * Growmoji Turkey

Note: Drop rates are not equal — some growmojis are significantly rarer than others.
