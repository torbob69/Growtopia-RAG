The **Zombie Sign** is a non-solid sign foreground block which was added as part of the Night of the Sewing Dead update.
##  Function
Being a sign, a Zombie Sign allows players with access to it to edit its message for other players to view.
A Zombie Sign is different from normal signs in the sense that it only allows players infected with the g-Virus to view the message on the sign.
