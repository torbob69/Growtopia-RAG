The **Monkey Warrior's Pants** is an unsplicable pants item which was added as part of Lunar New Year 2022 on February 1, 2022.
##  Function
When equipped, the Monkey Warrior's Pants serves a purely cosmetic purpose. Additionally, it features an animation of its tail moving.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Monkey Warrior set, which consists of the following items:

    * Monkey Warrior's Circlet (hat)

    * Monkey Face Mask (face)

    * Monkey Warrior's Shirt (shirt)

    * Monkey Warrior's Pants (pants)

    * Monkey Warrior's Staff (hand)
