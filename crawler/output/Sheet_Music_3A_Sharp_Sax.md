The **Sheet Music: Sharp Sax** is a splicable sheet music background block which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2017 on May 5, 2017.
##  Function
Sheet Music: Sharp Sax is a sheet music that plays a sharp sax note, depending on its position vertically. Its note is half-step higher than that of Sheet Music: Sax Note's.
