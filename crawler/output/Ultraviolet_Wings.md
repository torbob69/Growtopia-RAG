# Ultraviolet Wings

The Growtopian Eye has evolved to see more of the light spectrum than some creatures, which allows them to see these beautiful Ultraviolet Wings. Warning: Ultraviolet Radiation is what causes sunburn. Prolonged exposure is not advised and don't forget to wear your hat and sunscreen.

The **Ultraviolet Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of SummerFest 2024 on July 11, 2024.
##  Function
Light it up!

Lights out!

When equipped, the **Ultraviolet Wings** grants the _Double Jump & Light Source : Ultraviolet Wings_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and emit light in dark areas.
