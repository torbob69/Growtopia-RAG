The **Prince of Persia: Cave Torch** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable animated foreground block which was added as part of UbiWeek 2022 Day 4 on January 27, 2022.
##  Function
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Prince of Persia: Cave Torch can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, it will change its appearance, showing a 2-framed animation. As a light source, it emits light in dark areas.
