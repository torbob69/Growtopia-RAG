The **Alien Landing Pod** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Aliens update on November 14, 2021.
##  Function
When consumed, the player will receive one of the following items:
Alien Landing Pods can only be consumed during Aliens events.
  * Alien Recon Wagon
  * Channel G News Van
  * Geometric Bench (×2)
  * Greetings Earthlings Sign (×2)
  * Space Badger (rare)
  * Space Badger Ears
  * Space Cat (rare)
  * Space Dog (rare)
  * Space Dog Ears
  * Space Mouse (rare)
  * Space Mouse Ears
  * Space Pig (rare)
  * Space Pig Ears
  * Space Rabbit (rare)
  * Space Rabbit Ears
  * Space Rover
  * Space Station Sign (×2)
  * Space Stowaway Mask
  * Space Stowaway Pants
  * Space Stowaway Shirt
  * Subway Sign (×2)
  * Subway Stairs (×10)
  * Suspicious Weather Balloon
  * Techno Grass (×20)
  * Unknown Space Pet
