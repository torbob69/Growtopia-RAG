The **Etheral Rainbow Dragon** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of the Stuff! update on October 1, 2018.
##  Function
Flame On!

The fire has died out!

When equipped, this item grants the _Rainbow Flame!_ mod. A rainbow dragon pet will also appear and follow the player around. The dragon replaces the player's punch with rainbow flames; the dragon and its flames constantly cycle through colors in a similar style to the Shifty Block.
