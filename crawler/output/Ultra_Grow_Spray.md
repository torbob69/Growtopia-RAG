# Ultra Grow Spray

Spray a tree to instantly grow it to full size! Can only grow 30 day's growth at the most, but that's longer than almost any item takes.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **30 Hits** **23 Hits** – Restores after **4s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Songpyeon **(×5)** ,
Prosperity Mooncake **(×20)** and
Longevity Mooncake **(×20)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
Mooncake Offering

| 1 may be received as an uncommon reward from offering mooncakes using an Offering Table, when the offering contains:
•  Songpyeon, or
•  Prosperity Mooncake  |
The **Ultra Grow Spray** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2013 on September 18, 2013.
##  Function
When consumed on a tree, the Ultra Grow Spray will speed up the time required for the tree to grow by 30 days.
