# UV Vaccine

This wonder of modern medicine is the Universal Vicious Vaccine, and your only hope against the most dangerous of maladies! Hold onto this as you operate, and be careful, because it'll get used whether you're successful or not!

The **UV Vaccine** is an unsplicable hand item that was added as part of the Blocktor's Orders (update) on June 13, 2019.
##  Function
When equipped while curing a vicious malady, it will make the surgery easier to perform. The item will be used up even if the player fails the surgery.
