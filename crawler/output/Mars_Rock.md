The **Mars Rock** is an unsplicable solid foreground block which was added as part of the Astro Pack update on October 18, 2013.
##  Function
When placed, the Mars Rock serves a fully aesthetic purpose.

##  Trivia
  * Mars Rock is farmable.
  * Mars Rock can turn into an Alien Fossil Rock, if there are people in the world, similar to how a Rock can turn into a Fossil Rock.
