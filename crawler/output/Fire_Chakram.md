The **Fire Chakram** is an unsplicable artifact item which was added as part of SummerFest 2022 on July 28, 2022.
##  Function
Flames of Fury!

Flame off!

When equipped, the **Fire Chakram** grants the _Double Jump : Fire Chakram_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
