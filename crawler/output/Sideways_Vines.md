The **Sideways Vines** is an unsplicable slippery platform block which was added as part of Wildlife Weekend on March 10, 2023.
##  Function
A Sideways Vines behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath. In addition, being a slippery block, walking on a Sideways Vines causes the player to slide.
