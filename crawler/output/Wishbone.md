# Wishbone

Feeling lucky, well now you're shrunk!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **6 Hits** **5 Hits** – Restores after **5s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Complete BLARNEY

May yield **1** item each.
The **Wishbone** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2026 on March 12, 2026.
##  Function
Smol!

Back to normal!

When consumed, it grants the player the _Wishbone: Small Appetite_ mod, which allows the player to become smaller than usual for 10 seconds, similar to equipping the Ring Of Shrinking.
