##  Function
I'm attacking the darkness!

My torch went out.

When equipped, the Hand Torch grants the _Carrying a Torch_ mod, which brightens the area around the player, allowing them to see through the dark. The light will be brighter than the one emitted by the permanent items, such as the Spelunker's Headlamp.
