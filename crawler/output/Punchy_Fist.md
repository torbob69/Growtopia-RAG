The **Punchy Fist** is an unsplicable solid trampoline foreground block that was added as part of the Blocktor's Orders! update.
##  Function
Jumping on a trampoline block such as a Punchy Fist will cause the player to bounce, and by holding the jump button, the player can jump even higher. The _High Jump_ mod also boosts one's jump height on a trampoline block.
