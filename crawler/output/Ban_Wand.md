This item does not have any recipe because it is no longer, or was never obtainable.
The **Ban Wand** is an unobtainable consumable which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2013 on May 3, 2013.
##  Function
Reality flickers as you begin to wake up.

You are no longer banned. Now be good!

When consumed on a player, the Ban Wand, as its name suggests, grants the _Ban_ mod, banning a player from _Growtopia_ for ten minutes. It is made exclusive to the developers, and will presumably not be made available to the public in any form as its power could easily be abused.
The appearance of the wand is exactly the same as that of the  Fire Wand, although they have different seed designs.
The Ban Wand will also ban the player's IP address, making it impossible for them to login using another account.
##  Trivia
  * When a Ban Wand has been used, a message will be sent out in the chat if the player is currently in the same world as the banned player: **"_< Mod Name> has used ban on <player>!_"**
  * Contrary to popular belief, moderators and developers do not use ban wands under normal circumstances, and instead use specialized commands.
  * A warning message is given to a player when they get banned: **"_Warning From System: You've been BANNED from Growtopia for <Set amount of Time>._"**
  * In the past, there was a Global System Message announcing that  Ban Wand Trees will no longer look like  Hammer Pants Trees.
