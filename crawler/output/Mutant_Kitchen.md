# Mutant Kitchen

**Mutant Kitchen** (previously named **Totally Gross**) is a randomly-occurring three-day monthly event introduced as part of the Gross-Out update on May 1, 2019. In this event, players can stuff themselves with mutant food, which will earn them exclusive prizes when consecutively consumed.
##  Update History
When it was first released on May 1, 2019, the Mutant Kitchen event was known as Totally Gross. The first and only Totally Gross event lasted from May 3, 2019 until May 5, 2019.
Due to the lukewarm player reception, the developers soon proposed a revamp to Totally Gross. Days after the first event had ended, a survey was sent out to players through Twitter and the Growtopia Gazette. The survey read as follows:
Hello Growtopians! We’ve gathered a lot of feedback from you about last month’s update, Gross-Out, and after reviewing it, we’ve come up with some interesting ideas to make it more fun and we want YOUR vote to help us decide what to do with it next! One is to keep it as-is, but rebalance things a little to make it easier (and cheaper) to explode for gross prizes. Another is to tweak the balancing AND change the items to be a little less gross, but keep the weirdness with strange new Mutations! Finally, another option is to go in a completely different direction with a balancing change-up and a total item alteration, focusing on food-themed items and explosions in an all-out Food Fight!  Let us know what you’d prefer in the poll below!

―Survey
The survey contained only one question and three preset answers for players to choose from.
**Question:** How do you see the future of Gross-Out?
  * **Keep (and tweak) Gross-Out!** - We love all the slime and ickiness, like the Riding Poo, Bugs, Worms, Puggly Pups, Fly Heads, and Brain Blocks – just make them easier to get!
  * **Shift things around a little with Mutations!** - Tone down the gross but keep it weird with items like Extra Arms and Eyes on Stalks while adding strange new things like Mutant Eyes, Tentacle Feet, Claw Hands, and more!
  * **Give us a complete change-up with Food Fight!** - We don’t want weirdness at all – give us tons of food-themed fun with things like Riding Cupcakes, Frosted Feet, Croissant Blocks, Rainbow Buttercream Hair, and more!

Based off the popular vote, Totally Gross was rebranded two months later as Mutant Kitchen on July 26, 2019. Moreover, the Mutant Kitchen update made several items deemed "too gross" unobtainable, and replaced them in the loot tables with new items.
A balancing change was released on November 12, 2019 in order to address further flaws regarding the event, and cooking as a whole.
As part of multiple small monthly event revamps, an update to Mutant Kitchen was added on August 1, 2021. The items themselves are obtainable since August 6, 2021, the day Mutant Kitchen arrived.
##  Mutant Mixers
There are four types of mutant mixers:  Burnt Slime,  Putrid Gas,  Rotting Sea Goo, and  Space Poop. Each mutant food recipe requires a combination of two mutant mixers.
Up to 4 random mutant mixers may be dropped from harvesting a Mutant Mixers Tree, which is made by splicing a Ruined Sign Seed with an Acid Seed. Mutant mixers can also be obtained through purchasing a Bag of Mutant Mixers from the Store for 10,000 gems. Each pack contains 10 of each mixer type, plus 10 Strawberry Slime Blocks.

##  Daily Challenge
In the Mutant Kitchen Daily Challenge, players compete to cook Mutant Food for points (more points for higher quality) without assistance.
Prizes of **Mutant Kitchen** daily challenge include:
Regular Prize — Special Ability — Guild Prize — Special AbilityRiding 'roach — When equipped, it grants the _Firefly: 'roach_ mod to the player, which allows them to move faster. — Riding Firefly — When equipped, it grants the _Firefly: Speedy_ mod to the player, which allows them to move faster.
##  Miscellaneous Items
Cooking Recipes of mutant food were implemented as part of the Mutant Kitchen Rebalanced! update on November 12, 2019. A random Cooking Recipe may be included in the purchase of a Bag of Mutant Mixers.
A player can check the description of a mutant food's Cooking Recipe in order to view its ingredients and oven timings. One, however, doesn't need to have any of these items in their inventory to be able to cook the mutant food.
