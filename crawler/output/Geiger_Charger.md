# Geiger Charger

Tap a Geiger Counter in here to charge it up! It's half the speed of hand-charging, but it's convenient. Punch to retrieve it when it's ready.

The **Geiger Charger** is an unsplicable solid foreground block which was added as part of the Geiger Day update on May 5, 2017, the same day Cinco De Mayo Week 2017 was released.
##  Function
Tapping a Geiger Charger with a Dead Geiger Counter selected in one's inventory allows the player to put the latter inside. It takes one hour for a Geiger Charger to fully charge a Dead Geiger Counter (twice as much time as normal). Punching a Geiger Charger when the Geiger Counter is ready allows the player to retrieve the item.
##  Trivia
  * When a player attempts to retrieve their Geiger Counter before it has finished charging, they will say, "_That's not done charging!_ "
  * When a player attempts to retrieve their Geiger Counter with a full inventory, they will say, "_No room to pick it up!_ "
  * When a player attempts to put their Geiger Counter into a Geiger Charger when another Geiger Counter has already been placed inside, they will say, "_That charger is already in use!_ "
  * Being unmovable, Geiger Chargers are unable to be picked up using a Flashback Flan or moved with a Gauntlet of Elements.
  * As part of the Quality of Life Changes update on May 20, 2022, the Geiger Charger is now only accessible by the world owner.
