The **Avenie Golden Horse** is an unsplicable feet item which was added on November 14, 2014.
##  Function
You can move faster!

You are slow again.

When equipped, the **Avenie Golden Horse** grants the _Speedy_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land. It also leaves a trail of golden sparkles when the player moves.
