# Legendary Destroyer

Step up and aboard the Legendary Destroyer and command the ultimate machine. Your legendary status is assured when you pilot this magnificent mech!
Properties
This item cannot be dropped or traded.
This item never drops any seeds.

Legendary Quest Reward

Approach the  Legendary Wizard and complete the  Quest Of The Mech.
The **Legendary Destroyer** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2021 Day 5 on August 27, 2021.
##  Function
Fists of fury - Activate!!

No more punchy punchy!

When equipped, the **Legendary Destroyer** grants the _Punch Damage: Legendary Destroyer_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster.
##  Quest Steps
  1. Deliver 2,000 Dwarven Backgrounds.
  2. Kill 100 players in PvP via a Game Generator.
  3. Break 10,000 Blocks.
  4. Deliver 150 Lost Startopian Helmets
  5. Plant seeds that add up to 100,000 rarities.
  6. Earn 50 Growtokens.
  7. Deliver 15 Monster Trucks.
  8. Earn 20,000 XP.
  9. Deliver 10 Matrix Auras.
  10. Deliver 100,000 Gems.
  11. Break 200,000 rarity worth of blocks.
  12. Complete 100 Star Voyages.
  13. Collect from 1,000 providers.
  14. Deliver 3 Digger's Spades.
  15. Collect 200,000 rarity worth of fruit from trees.
  16. Deliver 10 Ambu-Lances.
  17. Deliver 5 Mining Mechs.
  18. Deliver 1 Phoenix Armor.
  19. Deliver 1 Volcanic Cape.
  20. Deliver the Legendary Orb.
