The **Chaos Dragon** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of the Dragon Invasion update.
##  Function
Chaos draconic wisdom!

Your chaotic wisdom is no more.

When equipped, the **Chaos Dragon** grants the _XP Buff: Startopia_ mod, which allows the player to gain an extra 300 XP when successfully completing a star voyage. It will also stack with Star Dragon Claw's effect, which gives a bonus of 200 XP.
When the player walks while equipping the Chaos Dragon, a dark purple trail of sparks will be created. Additionally, a rift will also appear upon respawning, sucking the player's corpse in.
Step 17 of the  Quest For The Sky requires the player to deliver 5 of this item.
