Participate in successfully smashing an  Ultra Pinata during the **Pinata Bash!** event.
May give **2-5** items each time.
The **Pueblock Wallpaper** is an unsplicable background block which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2019 on May 3, 2019.
##  Function
The item has four different textures and when placed, a random one will appear. The four textures are:
  * A blue polka-dot textured wallpaper.
  * A green polka-dot textured wallpaper.
  * An orange polka-dot textured wallpaper.
  * A pink polka-dot textured wallpaper.
