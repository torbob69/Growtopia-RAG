The **Blueberry** is a splicable consumable which was added as part of _Growtopia_ 's launch.
##  Function
A blueberry slides down your throat!

The effects of the blueberry have worn off.

When consumed, a Blueberry grants the _Feelin' Blue_ mod for one hour, giving the player a dark blue skin color.
