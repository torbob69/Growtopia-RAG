# Legendary Orb

This is the page for **Legendary Orb**.
You may be looking for **Great Legendary Orb** instead.
This orb is legendary and filled with a great power. However, you don't have an adapter for it, so the power isn't any use to you. Perhaps a wizard could use it?
Properties

The **Legendary Orb** is an unsplicable and untradable component which was added as part of the Legendary update on August 22, 2014.
##  Function
It is the last quest item required for all legendary items that can be obtained from the Legendary Wizard.
##  Trivia
  * The effect when the player obtains a Legendary Orb is same as when a player levels up.
  * Disposing or recycling the orb will allow the player to obtain another one when they go to the Great Legendary Orb.
  * Players can only carry 1 Legendary Orb in their inventory at a time.
  * Players can complete the parkour in LEGENDARYMOUNTAIN as many times as they want if they are doing the Legendary Quest.
  * Before 2017, players were able to enter LEGENDARYMOUNTAIN before starting a Legendary Quest. Most of the players who went there had no quest and caused the world to lag.
  * The Legendary Orb is consumed upon completing a Legendary Quest. The player must obtain another to complete their next quest.
