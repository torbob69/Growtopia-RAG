The **Marty Jacket** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2021 on November 18, 2021.
##  Function
When equipped, Marty Jacket serves a fully cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The Marty Jacket is based on the jacket worn by Marty McFly, the protagonist of the _Back to the Future_ franchise.
  * The  Marty Jacket used to have the same sprite as the  Wax Jacket at the release of Thanksgiving Week 2021; this was later changed on November 23, 2021.
