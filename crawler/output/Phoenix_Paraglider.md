The **Phoenix Paraglider** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of SummerFest 2025 on July 10, 2025.
##  Function
Phoenix Rising!

Phoenix Falling!

When equipped, the **Phoenix Paraglider** grants the _High Jump + Slow Fall: Phoenix Paraglider_ mod, which allows the player to jump one tile higher and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
