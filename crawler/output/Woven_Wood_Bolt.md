# Woven Wood Bolt

All those wood chips made for terrible snacks, but with a little sew-how, you found a new purpose for them in this beautiful bolt! Now what can you make with THIS, I wonder...?
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **4 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **8s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Consumption

Consume a(n)  Wood Chips **(x200)**.
May yield **1** item each.

The **Woven Wood Bolt** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2019.
##  Function
**Accurate as of:** 24 June 2020
The Woven Wood Bolt is needed in a total of one sewing recipe listed below:
Items: RecipeTea Basket: Woven Wood Bolt +  Silk Bolt - Blue (Overcasting Stitch) +  Woven Wood Bolt (Saddle Stitch)  Broken Tea Basket +  Silk Bolt - Blue (Overcasting Stitch) +  Woven Wood Bolt (Saddle Stitch)
##  Trivia
  * The Woven Wood Bolt is one of the silk bolts available for sewing, alongside: Silk Bolt - White, Silk Bolt - Blue, Silk Bolt - Floral, Silk Bolt - Green, Silk Bolt - Grey, Silk Bolt - Purple, Silk Bolt - Red, Silk Bolt - Black, Silk Bolt - Yellow, Wool Bolt, and Silk Bolt - Aqua.
