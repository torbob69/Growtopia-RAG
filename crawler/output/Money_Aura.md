The **Money Aura** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2020.
##  Function
I'm in the money!

I need a loan!

When equipped, the **Money Aura** grants the _Double-Jump: Money Aura_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
