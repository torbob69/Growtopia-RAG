# Beach Blast Bingo!
During SummerFest

|
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Beach Blast Bingo!** for **20,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"It's hot out there! Don't you want to go to the beach? Now you can make your own beach with a Beach Blast device and the 100 Fireworks needed to power it. The Beach Blast creates a brand new world set up as a beach, full of cool new Summer items. As a bonus, you'll get a Summer Surprise and a chance of a Ultra White Fragment or Ultra Purple Fragment too! (available during Summerfest week only!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Beach Blast Bingo!** is a store pack that is only available during SummerFest.
##  Items
The pack includes:
  * Beach Blast (×1)
  * Summer Surprise (×1)
  * Fireworks (×100)
  * Ultra White Fragment (×1) (ultra rare chance)
  * Ultra Purple Fragment (×1) (ultra rare chance)

##  Trivia
  * As of SummerFest 2016, the price was changed from 5,000 to 15,000 gems.
    * As of SummerFest 2021, the price was changed again from 15,000 to 20,000 gems.
