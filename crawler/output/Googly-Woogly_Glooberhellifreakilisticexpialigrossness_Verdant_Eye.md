##  Function
Being a toggleable deadly foreground block, a Googly-Woogly Glooberhellifreakilisticexpialigrossness Verdant Eye works like an Evil Eye, allowing player to open and close itself by punching it. When toggled on, the item acts as a deadly block. When toggled off, it acts as a non-solid block which players can walk through. It can be wrenched to be made public or not.
