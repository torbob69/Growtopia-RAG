The **Blue Mailbox** is a splicable solid mailbox foreground block which was added alongside the City Pack on August 18, 2013.
##  Function
The Blue Mailbox is one of the several mailbox blocks available in _Growtopia_. Anyone who has access to a Blue Mailbox can view messages left behind by other players as well as the players' names. Anyone can also punch the Blue Mailbox in order to see how many mails there are. A maximum of 20 mails can be placed inside a mailbox.
The  Letter item serves as an image placeholder for the mail icon.
