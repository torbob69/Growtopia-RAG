# Blood Filled Crepes

Why. These things are practically heaving with decomposing gas, ready to pop in a fountain of dirty blood the second you bite down!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **4 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **5s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Putrid Gas Filled Blood Maggot Crepes **(×1)** ,
Disgusting Mess **(×3)** and
Water Bucket **(×10)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Blood Filled Crepes** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Gross-Out update on May 1, 2019.
##  Function
When consumed, the Blood Filled Crepes raises your mutation level, which when maxed out will give a Mutant Kitchen prize.
