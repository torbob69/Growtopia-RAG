League Moderator

Given to the chosen **League Moderators** during the Pet Battle Royale 2025.
The **Pet Battle League Moderator** is an unsplicable and unobtainable component which was added on May 15, 2025.
##  Function
The sprite for this item is used for the League Moderator Title. Players with a League Moderator title can toggle this on and off by wrenching oneself and clicking title. Once toggled, it shows the  Pet Battle League Moderator on the left of the player's name and moves other flags to the left.
##  Trivia
  * The chosen League Moderators for Pet Battle Royale 2025 were Anarion, Misthios, Moniuet, HollowDragon, WindyPlay, Kailyx, Miu, and Xinyi.
