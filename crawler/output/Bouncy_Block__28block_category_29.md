# Bouncy Block (block category)

This is the page for **Bouncy Block (block category)**.
You may be looking for **Trampoline Block (block category)** instead.
**Bouncy blocks** are solid foreground blocks that push the player to the direction that the latter approach from. They are not to be confused with trampoline blocks.
