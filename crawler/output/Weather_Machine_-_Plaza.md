The **Weather Machine - Plaza** is an unsplicable non-solid weather machine foreground block which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2022 on May 5, 2022.
##  Function
As a weather machine, this item can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, it will change its appearance, transforming the world's background weather into a Mexican plaza.
##  Trivia
  * The word _plaza_ is a Spanish word which refers to a town-square or central place of gathering.[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Weather_Machine_-_Plaza#cite_note-1)
