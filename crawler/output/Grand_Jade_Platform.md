The **Grand Jade Platform** is an unsplicable platform foreground block which was introduced during The Grand Tournament update. It can be obtained from either the Prize Ladder or from eating Tournament snacks.
##  Function
A Grand Jade Platform behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath.
