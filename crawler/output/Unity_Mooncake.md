# Unity Mooncake

This pastry is filled with a sweet bean paste. "Unity" is imprinted on the top in ancient Growtopian script. Mix this with other Mooncakes in a Laboratory or E-Z Cook Oven to create special Harvest Festival items.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **3 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **4s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Consumption

Consume a(n)  Mooncake Bundle **(x1)**.
May yield **1** item each.

The **Unity Mooncake** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2021 on September 16, 2021.
