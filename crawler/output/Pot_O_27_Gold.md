The **Pot O' Gold** is an unsplicable solid foreground block which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2013 on March 13, 2013. A Pot O' Gold has a chance to be obtained from the _Rabid Leprechaun Attack!_ special event.
##  Function
When placed, a partial rainbow appears, extending in one direction a few tiles long. When broken, a Pot O' Gold yields up to 200 Gems.
### Possibilities
Item: RecipeRide'O'Gold: 1 has a chance to be dropped from breaking: •  Pot O' Gold
Rainbow Silk Scarf: 1 has a chance to be dropped from breaking: •  Pot O' Gold while having an active Lucky Clover effect
##  Trivia
  * A bug existed where on rare occasions, the pot could have a second rainbow flowing in the other direction, though cut off beyond the first tile. This was later fixed in a patch during the Easter Week event of 2013.
