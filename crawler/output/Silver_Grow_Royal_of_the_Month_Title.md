# Silver Grow Royal of the Month Title

Show off your pride with this epic title. Awarded for placing 2nd in the Grow Pass Leaderboard. Only lasts for the month and then will be handed over to the next victor.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item has no use... by itself.

Grow Pass Leaderboard

Awarded to the player who ranked second on the Grow Pass leaderboard for accumulating the second highest number of Grow Pass points during the month.
The **Silver Grow Royal of the Month Title** is an unsplicable component which was added as part of the Grow Pass second update on March 6, 2024.
##  Function
The sprite for this item is used for the Silver Grow Royal of the Month Title. Players with a Silver Grow Royal of the Month Title can toggle this on and off by wrenching oneself and clicking title. Once toggled, it adds a  on both ends of the player's name.
