The **Donut Donation Box** is an unsplicable solid donation box foreground block which was added as part of Grow4Good Week 2021 on April 15, 2021.
##  Function
As a type of donation box, this block accepts item donations of rarity 2 or more and can store up to twenty gifts from players.

##  Trivia
  * When someone donates an item, it will say in the chat, "_< name> has donated <amount of items donated> <items donated>._" It formerly said "_< name> has donated <amount of items donated> <items donated>. How nice!_"
  * Each type of donation box will only allow 20 gifts; once it's full, players will be unable to donate anymore until the box has been emptied.
  * When retrieving items from a box, the player will say, "_< name> receives <amount of items> <items> from <name>, how nice!_"
  * When putting in an item in a box, it will say, "_Choose an item to give_ ". Putting in items into a donation box one has access to will cause it to say, "_Choose an item to give yourself_ "
  * Should a player try to donate to a single box more than three times, they will end up saying, "_Woah nelly, you don't want to be TOO nice. Try again later._ "
    * Moreover, when the player uses their Wrench on the box, they will say, "_You see <amount of item stacks> gifts in the box! You've already crammed 3 of your gifts into the box, better wait._"
  * If a player tries breaking a donation box with gifts in it, they will say, "_Empty the donation box before breaking it!_ "
  * When someone donates a fish, it will say "_You can't put a live fish in the box! That would be mean._ "
  * When someone donates an item with rarity 1, it will say "_This box only accepts items rarity 2+ or greater_ "
  * However even though the donation box doesn't receive rarity 1, the player can still put Chemical G which is rarity 1.
