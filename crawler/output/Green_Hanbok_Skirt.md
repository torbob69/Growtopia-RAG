The **Green Hanbok Skirt** is an unsplicable pants item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2016 on September 15, 2016.
##  Function
When equipped, the Green Hanbok Skirt serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * It isn't guaranteed that the player will get a Green Hanbok Skirt by following the item's combining recipe, as all the items below share the same recipe:
    * Blue Hanbok Top
    * Blue Hanbok Pants
    * Orange Hanbok Top
    * Orange Hanbok Pants
    * Green Hanbok Skirt
    * Yellow Hanbok Skirt
