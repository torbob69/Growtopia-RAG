The **2023 - Zodiac Year of the Rabbit** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as a part of Lunar New Year 2023 on January 21, 2023.
##  Function
Zodiac Powers!

Another Year!

When equipped, the **2023 - Zodiac Year of the Rabbit** grants the _Speedy & Cookie Hunter : Zodiac Year of the Rabbit_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and provides a significant chance of doubling fruit drops from Fortune Cookie and Lucky Fortune Cookie Trees.
