This item was given to **Reg4shi** for their contributions as part of the Ubisoft Abu Dhabi team.
**Reg4shi's Archangel Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of the Gross-Out update on May 1, 2019.
##  Function
When equipped, the **Reg4shi's Archangel Wings** grants the _Double Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
