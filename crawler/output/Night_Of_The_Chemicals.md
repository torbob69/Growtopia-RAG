# Night Of The Chemicals

Growtopia Gazette
> March 2nd: Night of the Chemicals!
> It's the Night of the Chemicals! We've updated the Night of the Comet with new items, and added a whole new type of chemical... and an insane new way to create them!
> The amazing new GrowTech Chemsynth not only creates Synthetic Chemicals - it's also the key to curing the g-Virus Pandemic. Get one and figure it out before the pandemic begins!
> This month, we introduce the Item Of The Month! We'll have a new item every month in the gem store, and you'll never be able to get it again after the month ends. It will always be rare and special!
> -@Seth and @Hamumu
**Night of the Chemicals** is a monthly update which was added on the 2nd of March, 2017. It mainly focuses on the new Chemsynth Pack and the Night Of The Comet. It is also the first time an Item Of The Month has been featured.
