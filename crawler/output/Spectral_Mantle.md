# Spectral Mantle

Writhing chains from the Other Side! These spirit-forged links are meant to capture the most terrible of ghosts, but since you're not, y'know, DEAD, they just look very cool (and maybe a little creepy).
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be transmuted.

**1h**
Daily Challenge

Place in the top three of the **BOO Blast** Guild Daily Challenge, only if the player had contributed points to the challenge.
The **Spectral Mantle** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of the Challengers Approach! update on October 9, 2019.
##  Function
Chained to the other side.

Released from your soul bondage.

When equipped, the **Spectral Mantle** grants the _Slowfall: Spectral Mantle_ mod, which allows the player to glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
