The **Cybernetic Eye** is an unsplicable face item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2015 Day 7.
##  Function
Blast blocks from farther away! But weaker than punch.

Your vision is normal.

When equipped, the **Cybernetic Eye** grants the _Eye Beam_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further and break blocks more slowly. It will also replace the player's punching effect with a laser beam fired from their eyes.
##  Trivia
