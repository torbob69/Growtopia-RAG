The **Trade-Scan** is an unobtainable component which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week Day 3 on August 30, 2023.
##  Function
This item serves as an image placeholder for the trade-scan credits found in the Trade-Scan window. A player may exchange a World Lock for five **Trade-Scan Credits** which allows the player to view the average price of five items.
##  Trivia
  * Trade-Scan Credit uses the same sprite as the Trade-Scan.
