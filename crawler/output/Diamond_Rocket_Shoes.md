Won as an ultimate prize in **Growganoth Gulch (Right)** in the Carnival.
The **Diamond Rocket Shoes** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of The Carnival update on October 13, 2014.
##  Function
You can move faster!

You are slow again.

When equipped, the **Diamond Rocket Shoes** grants the _Speedy_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.
The  Winterfest Exclusive Mission - Shred the Ice! mission requires 20 of this item.
