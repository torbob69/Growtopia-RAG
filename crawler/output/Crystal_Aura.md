The **Crystal Aura** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of the Startopia 2.0 update on June 1, 2020.
##  Function
The power of the crystal propels you!

Taken the shine right off.

When equipped, the **Crystal Aura** grants the _Double Jump: Crystal Power!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
The  The Seven Seas : The Heart of the Ocean! mission requires 1 of this item.
