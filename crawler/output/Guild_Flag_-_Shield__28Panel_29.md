Stand proud and show off your guild mascot with this shield-shaped and panel-patterned flag! Its colors will adapt to the seeds from your guild mascot!

Harvest a  Guild Flag - Shield Tree.__
The **Guild Flag - Shield (Panel)** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added alongside the Guilds update on April 1, 2018.
##  Function
When placed, the Guild Flag - Shield (Panel) will display the color of the seeds of the player's guild mascot.
