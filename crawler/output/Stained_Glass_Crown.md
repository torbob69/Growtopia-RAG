**1h**
Well of Love

One may be received after filling a Well of Love with 200 Golden Booty Chests.
After receiving the Stained Glass Crown, the Well of Love will turn into a Dry Well of Love.
The **Stained Glass Crown** is an unsplicable hat item that was added as part of Valentine's Week 2022 on February 11, 2022.
##  Function
Let the light shine!

Sun's down!

When equipped, the **Stained Glass Crown** grants the _Punch Damage + Punch Range: Stained Glass Crown_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and punch one tile further.
