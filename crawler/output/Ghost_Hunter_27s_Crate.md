Purchase the **Ghost Hunter's Pack** for **10,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"A crate containing the essentials for Ghost Hunting! Guaranteed to have at least 5x Ghost Jars, plus one or more bonus items! Bonuses can include: Neutron Focus Cores, Containment Field Power Nodes, EXTRA Ghost Jars, Ghost Traps, Spirit Boards, and maybe even a Dark Spirit Board!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Ghost Hunter's Crate** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2023 Day 3 on August 30, 2023.
##  Function
When broken, the Ghost Hunter's Crate will drop the following item:
  * Ghost Jar ×5

The Ghost Hunter's Crate will also drop one of the following items randomly:
  * Neutron Focus Core ×2
  * Containment Field Power Node ×5
  * Ghost Trap ×2
  * Spirit Board ×3
  * Dark Spirit Board ×1
  * Ghost Jar ×5
