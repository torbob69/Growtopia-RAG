The **Cardboard Nessie** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Myths and Legends, the first set of Marvelous Missions, on March 18, 2021.
##  Function
Wait, does cardboard float!

Eugh, papier mache!

When equipped, the **Cardboard Nessie** grants the _Speedy & Surfboard: Cardboard Nessie_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and float on water for three seconds.
##  Trivia
  * This item is a reference to the Loch Ness Monster, a mythical creature also known as Nessie said to inhabit the Scottish Highlands.
