The **Anomalizing Reliable Cultivator** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Summer Clash Series 1.
##  Function
Anomalizing Reliable Cultivators are used to score points during Speedy Splicers. While equipping this item, each tree spliced yields 20 points. Reliable anomalizers, such as this item, will not break upon usage.
