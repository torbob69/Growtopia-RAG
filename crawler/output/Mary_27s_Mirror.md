The **Mary's Mirror** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block Halloween Week 2022 on October 22, 2022.
##  Function
Being a toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block, a Mary's Mirror can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Mary's Mirror will change its appearance, showing a 18-framed animation.
##  Trivia
  * The Mary's Mirror is a reference to the folklore Bloody Mary, a ghost, phantom, or spirit conjured to reveal the future. She is said to appear in a mirror when her name is chanted repeatedly.
