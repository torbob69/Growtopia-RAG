The **Calliope** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable animated foreground block which was added as part of The Carnival update on October 13, 2014.
##  Function
<https://growtopiawiki.com/w/File:Funtime.ogg>The Calliope's music.
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Calliope can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Calliope will change its appearance, showing a 2-frame animation, and play a piece of music.
##  Trivia
  * In the game files, the Calliope's music is named funtime.
