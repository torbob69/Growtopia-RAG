##  Trivia
  * The  Black Bowtie is a recolor of  Green Bowtie.
  * The Black Bowtie's description is a reference to the catchphrase _Shaken, not stirred_ , which is often used by Ian Fleming's fictional British Secret Service agent James Bond as this is how he prefers his martini cocktail.
