# Carnival Sign

Write stuff on it to tell your friends! Use your Wrench on it to change the message.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item has special properties you can adjust with the Wrench.

Carnival Prize

Won as a rare prize in **Concentration (Right)** in the Carnival.
The **Carnival Sign** is an unsplicable non-solid sign foreground block which was added as part of The Carnival update on October 13, 2014.
##  Function
Being a sign, a Carnival Sign allows players with access to it to edit its message for other players to view.
