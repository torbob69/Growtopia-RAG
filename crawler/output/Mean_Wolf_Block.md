The **Mean Wolf Block** is an unsplicable solid pain foreground block that was added as part of the Blocktor's Orders! update.
##  Function
Being a type of pain block, a Mean Wolf Block knocks the player back and stings the player until the player stops all motion. A player will die after making contact with a pain block five times in quick succession (recovering from damage takes roughly 5 seconds). The _Fireproof_ mod will not allow the player to take more hits, unlike with lava pain blocks.
