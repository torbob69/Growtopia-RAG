Participate in successfully smashing an  Ultra Pinata with a  Lucky Pinata Hat equipped during the **Pinata Bash!** event.
May give **1** item each time.
The **Siesta Sombrero Bed** is an unsplicable non-solid bed foreground block which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2021 on May 5, 2021.
##  Function
Being a bed, executing the /sleep command in chat while standing on the same tile as the Siesta Sombrero Bed will cause the player to sleep on it.
##  Trivia
  * The Siesta Sombrero Bed is seemingly based on the popular image of a Mexican resting on a rock while wearing a sombrero to protect himself from the heat.
  * The Siesta Sombrero Bed partly uses the original  Rock sprite.
