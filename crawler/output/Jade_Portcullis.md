The **Jade Portcullis** is an unsplicable gate which was introduced during The Grand Tournament update. It can be obtained from either the Prize Ladder or from eating Tournament snacks.
##  Function
Being an entrance, a Jade Portcullis allows players with access to walk through it, or, when its property is changed, allowing any player to walk through it. When placed in a public area, it is impossible to be closed for anyone.
