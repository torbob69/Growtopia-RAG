The **Pineapple Plough** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2020 on June 4, 2020.
##  Function
Tractors are not speedy.

You can walk faster than that thing!

When equipped, the **Pineapple Plough** grants the _Putt Putt Putt_ mod, which allows the player to move slower, but allows the player to harvest trees by walking through them. Additionally, it will also give a 10% chance to double the fruits earned from a harvested tree while a Fuel Pack is equipped. The Fuel Pack will only be consumed when the bonus is activated. This effect does not apply to trees of rarity 100 or above.
The Pineapple Plough serves the exact same function as the Harvester.
