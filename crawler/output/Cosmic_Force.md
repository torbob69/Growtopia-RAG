The **Cosmic Force** is an unsplicable face item which was added as part of Wonder Week 2023 Day 1 on October 9, 2023.
##  Function
Higher, further, faster!

Lower, closer, slower!

When equipped, the **Cosmic Force** grants the _High Jump + Speedy + Punch Range : Cosmic Force!_ mod, which allows the player to jump one tile higher, move faster on land, and punch one tile further.
