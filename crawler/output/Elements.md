# Elements

**Elements** , also referred to in-game as **chis** , are a feature that was added as part of the Crystals update. Every non-clothing item has one, indicated with an icon next to the item's name accessible by pressing the INFO button.
Any block with a chi has a chance to drop one of their corresponding Essence when broken:
  * Wind Chi: Wind Essence
  * Fire Chi: Fire Essence
  * Water Chi: Water Essence
  * Earth Chi: Earth Essence

Chi elements are also used in crystal balancing.
