The **Good Witch's Wand** is an unsplicable hand item which was added added as a part of Valentine's Week 2025 on February 13, 2025.
##  Function
<https://growtopiawiki.com/w/File:Twinkling_lights.wav>Good Witch's Wand music
La la, la la!

Magic Wands, need they have a point?

When equipped, the **Good Witch's Wand** grants the _Punch Range + Light Source: Good Witch's Wand_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further and emit light in dark areas. When punching, the Good Witch's Wand will play a chime.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Good Witch set, which consists of the following items:

    * Good Witch's Crown (hat)

    * Good Witch's Dress (shirt)

    * Good Witch's Wand (hand)
  * The full Good Witch set is a reference to a fictional character called Glinda Upland from the theatrical play and movie Wicked.
  * In the game files, the Good Witch's Wand chime is named twinkling_lights.
