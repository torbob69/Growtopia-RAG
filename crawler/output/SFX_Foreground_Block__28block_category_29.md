# SFX Foreground Block (block category)

An **SFX foreground block** is a type of foreground block which plays an animation and a sound effect when punched.
All types of providers are considered as SFX foreground blocks.
##  List of SFX Foreground Blocks
Items: Special FunctionATM Machine: A player can punch an ATM every 22 hours and be given between 0 and 100 gems.
Awkward Friendly Unicorn: Can be punched about every week and will give one of these possible drops.
Bathtub: When punched, a water faucet sound plays.
Buffalo: Can be punched every 20-24 hours and will give 1-2 Milk.
Cannon: When punched, a cannon sound plays.
Chicken: Can be punched every 24 hours and will give 1-2 Eggs.
Coffee Maker: Produces Coffee once every 24 hours.
Cow: Can be punched every 20-24 hours and will give 1-2 Milk.
Cash Register: When punched, a coin will fly out the top of the register.
Dinner Bell: When punched, a ringing sound plays.
Drumkit: When punched, a short drum solo plays.
Harp: When punched, an animation and a harp sound effect plays.
Magic Bell: When punched, a ringing sound plays.
Mariachi Bobble Head: When punched, a spring sound plays.
Royal Gilt Bathtub: When punched, a water faucet sound plays.
Rubber Ducky: When punched, a quacking sound plays.
Roulette Wheel: Will also display a random number from 0 to 36 and either the color red, black, or green (for 0 only).
Science Station: Produces a random Chemical every 8–12 hours.
Sheep: Can be punched about every 3 days and will give 1-3 Wool Punching a Sheep with Sheep Shears equipped will result in the Wool drop doubling.
Sink: When punched, a water faucet sound plays.
Silkworm: Produces 1-10 silk threads every day when it is more than 2 days old. A Silkworm's color can be changed depending on the color of the food it is fed.
Slot Machine: When punched, a slot machine sound plays. Will also make the player say, "_[ <playername> wins at slots!]_" (10% chance of happening) or "_[ <playername> loses at slots.]_" (90% chance of happening).
Target: When punched, a ringing sound plays and lights around the target light up.
Tea Set: Produces Tea once every 24 hours.
Thermometer: When punched, a ringing sound plays. Will also make the player say, "_WOW! It's soooo hot!_ "
Toilet: When punched, a flushing sound plays. Also functions as a seat.
