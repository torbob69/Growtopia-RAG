##  Trivia
  * The description of the Air Duct alludes to the Air-Vent Passageway trope, in which fictional characters break through or escape from a facility by crawling through the ventilation duct, which can somehow support the weight of an adult and are usually wide enough for one to pass through.
  * The Air Vent's description contains a reference to the Air Duct, which was added in the same update as the former.
