**Nightwings** is a back item that was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2018.
##  Function
Phew, the moonlight is back!

Where did the moonlight go?

When equipped, the **Nightwings** grants the _Night_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
The  Myths and Legends : Mothman Rising mission requires 1 of this item.
