The **Aura of Zen** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2024 Day 1 on August 26, 2024.
##  Function
Inner Peace!

Frustrated!

When equipped, the **Aura of Zen** grants the _Build Range + High Jump: Aura of Zen_ mod, which allows the player to build one tile further and jump one tile higher. Additionally, the Aura of Zen's color will change based on the seed color of the pants the player is wearing.
