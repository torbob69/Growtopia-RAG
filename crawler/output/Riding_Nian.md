The **Riding Nian** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Lunar New Year 2023 on January 21, 2023.
##  Function
Fight fire with fire!

Scared away!

When equipped, the **Riding Nian** grants the _Speedy + Punch Range: Riding Nian_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and punch one tile further.
