The **Heart of Diamond Crown** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Wonder Week 2023 Day 4 on October 12, 2023.
##  Function
Shine bright like a diamond!

No shine for you!

When equipped, the **Heart of Diamond Crown** grants the _Light Source + Punch Damage: Crystal Crown_ mod, which allows the player to emit light in dark areas and break blocks faster.
##  Trivia
  * This item's equip message is a reference to Rihanna's song: Diamonds.
  * Heart of Diamond Crown is the only item that its label features a different item's name, Crystal Crown, instead of its own name.
