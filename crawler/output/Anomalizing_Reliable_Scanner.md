The **Anomalizing Reliable Scanner** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Winter Clash Series 1.
##  Function
Anomalizing Reliable Scanners are used to score points during Super Startopians. While equipping this item, each successful star voyage yields 2,000 points. Reliable anomalizers, such as this item, will not break upon usage.
