The **Coin Door** is an unsplicable entrance foreground block which was added as part of Chinese New Year 2018.
##  Function
Being an entrance, a Coin Door allows players with access to walk through it, or, when its property is changed, allowing any player to walk through it. When placed in a public area, it is impossible to be closed for anyone.
##  Trivia
  * The Coin Door is one of the three blocks obtainable from breaking any block during Chinese New Year, alongside the Chinese Arch and Paper Dividing Wall.
