The **Dungeons Cave Background** is an unsplicable background block which was added as part of the Dungeons update on December 9, 2024.
##  Function
When placed, the Dungeons Cave Background serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item shares the same sprite as the  Dark Snowy Cave Background.
  * On the first day of the Dungeons 2024 update,  Dungeons Cave Background trees could appear in Harvest Moon Blasted worlds, allowing players to harvest them and drop a seed. Additionally, being rarity 1,  Dungeons Cave Background Seeds were obtainable through breaking as well. The glitch was patched the next day and all existing blocks and seeds were locked, followed by a removal from the game. However, Dungeons Cave Background blocks and seeds that were painted on Painting Easels or placed in Weather Machine - Stuff were left behind as a remark of their existence.
