# Balance Mooncake

This pastry is filled with a sweet bean paste, in 4 flavors combined with a perfect balance. A random jumble of four symbols is imprinted on the top in ancient Growtopian script. Mix this with other Mooncakes in a Laboratory or E-Z Cook Oven to create special Harvest Festival items.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.

The **Balance Mooncake** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2014 on September 5, 2014. One is also available in the Harvest Pack.
##  Function
Like other Mooncakes, Balance Mooncakes can be used to create Harvest Festival-related items. It, however, cannot be consumed. Attempting to eat a Balance Mooncake will make the player say "_Ew, it has raisins! I'm not eating that._ "

### Silkworm Food     _Main article:Guide:Silkworm Care_
This consumable can be fed to a Silkworm to give it a yellow color, thus allowing it to produce 1–10  Silk Thread - Yellow per harvest.
##  Trivia
  * The higher a tree's rarity is, the more likely it is for the tree to drop a Mooncake.
  * Obtaining a **Balance Mooncake** from a tree for the first time will grant the player the _Bright Future (Classic)_ Achievement.
