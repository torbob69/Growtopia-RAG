The **Space Stowaway Pants** is an unsplicable pants item which was added as part of the Aliens update on November 14, 2021.
##  Function
When equipped, Space Stowaway Pants serves a fully cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia

    * Space Stowaway Mask (Face)

    * Space Stowaway Shirt (Shirt)
