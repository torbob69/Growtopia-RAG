Egg Hunting

Replaces the Egg-Hunting Basket after an Easter Egg has been found for 30 minutes, which reverts back to the Egg-Hunting Basket after.
The **Full Egg-Hunting Basket** is a hand item which was added as part of Easter Week 2015.
##  Function
When equipped, the Full Egg-Hunting Basket serves a purely cosmetic purpose and does not actively hunt for Easter Eggs.. After 30 minutes, the Full Egg-Hunting Basket is replaced back to the Egg-Hunting Basket, where the player can hunt of Easter Eggs again.
