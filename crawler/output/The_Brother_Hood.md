**The Brother Hood** is an unsplicable hat item that was added as part of the Voucher Dayz update on June 25, 2021.
##  Function
The Brother Hood will disappear when a player who wears it stands still for some while. It will reappear when the player moves or does an action like punching.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Dark Assassin set, which consists of the following items:

    * The Brother Hood (hat)

    * Dark Assassin's Vest (shirt)

    * Dark Assassin's Cape (back)

    * Dark Assassin's Armguards (hand)

    * Dark Assassin's Trousers (pants)

    * Dark Assassin's Boots (shoes)
  * The Brother Hood is one out of five items that were previously only available to the discontinued console edition of Growtopia, along with the Energy Drink, Glowing Shirt, Plunger, and Unicorn.
    * All of them were obtained from Ubisoft Club Rewards and are now being released to the mobile/PC version of Growtopia through the Console Classix Pack.
