##  Trivia
One can become a **Supporter** if they meet at least one of the following criteria:
  * The player has earned 20,000 Gems through Tapjoy offers.
  * The player has made at least 1 In-App Purchase.

One can become a **Super Supporter** if they meet at least **one** of the following criteria:
  * The player has earned 600,000 Gems through TapJoy offers.
  * The player has spent a total of 30 USD worth of In-App Purchases.

One can become a **Subscriber** by consuming a Royal Grow Pass Token or a 1-Year Subscription Token.
