The **Curse of the Pink Pearl** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of Beach Party 2024 on July 26, 2024.
##  Function
Sparkling, shimmering, splendid!

The curse has been lifted!

When equipped, the **Curse of the Pink Pearl** grants the _Curse of the Pink Pearl : Pearl Skin_ mod, which allows the player to change their skin color to pink.
