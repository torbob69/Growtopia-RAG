# G-Virus Pandemic

The **g-Virus Pandemic** is an event introduced in the Night of the Sewing Dead update that occurs after the total amount of infection cases reaches more than 500,000. During the event, all players are unable to become immune to the g-Virus, especially through usage of an Antidote or any other means, and all Zombie Jammers are disabled.
##  Possibilities
item: RecipeZombie Brain: Drops once after killing a zombie.
Mutated Cell: Drops once after infecting a player. May also randomly drop in a world.
Vile Vial - Moldy Guts: Drops once after infecting a player.
