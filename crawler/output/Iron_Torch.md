The **Iron Torch** is a non-solid toggleable animated foreground block that was released as part of the Summer Season Events update.
##  Function
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, an Iron Torch can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, an Iron Torch will change its appearance, showing a 2-framed animation. As a light source, an Iron Torch emits light in dark areas.
