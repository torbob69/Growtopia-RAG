The **Clockwork Eyes** is an unsplicable face item that was added as part of Blocktor's Orders! update. It is a possible drop from completing Level 10 training for a Clockwork Fish, the other possible drop being a Clockwork Fish Leash.
##  Function
Give people the evil eye.

Your vision is normal.

When equipped, the **Clockwork Eyes** grants the _Punch Range: Focused Eyes_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further. It also replaces the player's punching effect with a claw slash effect.
