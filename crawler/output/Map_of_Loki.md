The **Map of Loki** is an unsplicable hand item that was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2019 Day 4.
##  Function
###  First Hint
This map's shifting letters seem to sizzle with doom and destruction, as if they're waiting for something - or somewhere - terrible...

#### Solution
Go to any world with Weather Machine - Apocalypse.
###  Second Hint
In the ruined light of Apocalypse, the shapes on the page begin to swirl and glow, taking shape... AOLPY WYLZLUJL JOPTLZ PU KFPUN OLHYAZ BWSPMAPUN AOL JOVZLU HZ IHAASL KLWHYAZ ZLLR AOLPY NYHJL PU MVYT HZ WBYL H DPUNLK ZOHWL VM IHSHUJL ZBYL

#### Solving
Use Caesar Shift +7 to decode this into:
THEIR PRESENCE CHIMES IN DYING HEARTS UPLIFTING THE CHOSEN AS BATTLE DEPARTS SEEK THEIR GRACE IN FORM AS PURE A WINGED SHAPE OF BALANCE SURE.

#### Solution
Shatter a Harmonic Crystal to obtain Crystal Wings.
###  Prize
Successfully completing the quest has a chance to reward the player with 1  Valkyrie's Wings.
