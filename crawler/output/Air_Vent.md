The **Air Vent** is a splicable entrance foreground block which was added as part of the Block Party update on June 12, 2015.
##  Function
Being an entrance, an Air Vent allows players with access to walk through it, or, when its property is changed, allowing any player to walk through it. When placed in a public area, it is impossible to be closed for anyone.
