The **Edison Zoomster** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of the Growtopia General Hospital update on November 22, 2013.
##  Function
You can move faster!

You are slow again.

When equipped, the **Edison Zoomster** grants the _Speedy_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.
Step 7 of the  Quest Of Steel requires the player to deliver 3 of this item.
