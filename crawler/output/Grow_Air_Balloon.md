**Grow Air Balloon** is a feet item that was added as March 2021's Item Of The Month on March 10, 2021.
##  Function
Inflate and I won't be late!

Going dowwwwwwwn!

When equipped, the **Grow Air Balloon** grants the _Double Jump & Slowfall : Grow Air Balloon_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button. Players can change the item's appearance by equipping and unequipping it, similar to the Diamond Horns.
##  Trivia
  * Grow Air Balloon's name is a portmanteau of "grow" and "hot-air balloon", the latter referring to the type of aircraft it is based on.
