The **Mexican Pointy Boots** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2020 on May 1, 2020.
##  Function
Hola Pointy Shoes go go go!

My feet ache!

When equipped, the **Mexican Pointy Boots** grants the _Speedy: Arriba Arriba!_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land. Additionally, beans bounce next to the player.
