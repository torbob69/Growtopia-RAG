The **Basic Blue Block** is an unsplicable solid foreground block that was added alongside the Electric Bow and Golden Aura on May 18, 2015.
##  Function
When placed, the Basic Blue Block serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The item's seed was previously obtainable from the Small Seed Pack; however, it was later removed from the pack as part of the Systems update.
