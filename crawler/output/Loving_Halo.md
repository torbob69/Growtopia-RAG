The **Loving Halo** is an unsplicable hat item that was added as part of Blocktor's Orders! update.
##  Function
It's the power of love!

Where is the love!

When equipped, the **Loving Halo** grants the _Healing: Loving Halo!_ mod, which allows the player to heal faster than usual after receiving damage from hitting a pain block.
