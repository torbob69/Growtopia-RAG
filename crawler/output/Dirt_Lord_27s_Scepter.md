Purchase the **Dirt Lord's Scepter** for **200 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"Plant seeds with soil and strife, rid this block of its life! Note: The Scepter is UNTRADABLE."
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Dirt Lord's Scepter** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2023 Day 5 on September 1, 2023.
##  Function
You can't stop the seeds!

You stopped the seeds!

When equipped, the **Dirt Lord's Scepter** grants the _Punch Range : Dirt Lord's Scepter_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further.
