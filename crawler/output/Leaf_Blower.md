# Leaf Blower

Tired of dealing with people? Wish they'd all just leaf you alone? Well, fire up this blower's GrowTech turbine and a storm of wind and fallen foliage will blast them into next year!

Scythe Harvesting

Harvest **trees of rarity no more than 100** with a  Hand Scythe during the Harvest Festival.
The Leaf Blower is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2019 on September 12, 2019.
##  Function
It's awfully windy around here!

Power off!

When equipped, the **Leaf Blower** grants the _Punch Power: Leaf Blower_ mod, which allows the player to knock back other players further.
The  Super Pineapple Party Exclusive Mission - Pucker up for more Pineapple fun! mission requires 5 of this item.
