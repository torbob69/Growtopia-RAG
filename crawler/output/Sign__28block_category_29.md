# Sign (block category)

A **sign** is a type of non-solid foreground block which allows players with access to it to edit its message for other players to view.

##  Trivia
  * If a sign is broken while being edited, the player will say: "_Huh? The sign is gone!_ " after submitting the message.
  * Attempting to write invalid symbols on a sign will make the player say "_You try to write the magic symbols down but they dissapear!_ "
  * A sign cannot be edited while the player is being duct-taped.
