The **Phoenix Armor** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as part of SummerFest 2021 on July 8, 2021.
##  Function
Run with the phoenix!

No more flame in your set!

When equipped, the **Phoenix Armor** grants the _Speedy : Phoenix Armor_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land. Additionally, this item can stack with up to two other Speedy clothing items and respawning will produce a unique flaming effect.
Step 18 of the  Quest Of The Mech requires the player to deliver 1 of this item.
