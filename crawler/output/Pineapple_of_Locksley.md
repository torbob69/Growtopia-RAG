The **Pineapple of Locksley** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2022 on June 10, 2022.
##  Function
Robbing the full!

Dropping the bow!

When equipped, the **Pineapple of Locksley** grants the _Punch Range & Punch Damage : Pineapple of Locksley_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further and break blocks faster.
##  Trivia
  * Locksley, Nottinghamshire, is known to be the fictional home of English folk hero Robin Hood — sometimes also referred to as Robin of Loxley (Locksley) — who is said to have robbed from the rich and given to the poor.
