**OVEROLL's Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Brr-lloon Warz 2018.
##  Function
OVEROLL says "rollover!"

Your wings rolled away!

When equipped, the **OVEROLL's Wings** grants the _Double Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
##  Trivia
  * These wings were sponsored by OVEROLL, the sixth completer of the Epic Quests.
  * Prior to Brr-lloon Warz 2020, OVEROLL's Wings could be claimed after reaching 24,000 personal points.
