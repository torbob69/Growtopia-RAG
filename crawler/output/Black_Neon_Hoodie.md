The **Black Neon Hoodie** is an unsplicable shirt which was added as part of Black Friday 2021 on November 26, 2021.
##  Function
When equipped, the Black Neon Hoodie serves a fully cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Black Neon set, which consists of the following items:

    * Black Neon Hoodie (Shirt)

    * Black Neon Pants (Pants)

    * Black Neon Hightops (Feet)
