The **Dueling Star Fighter - Imperial Enforcer** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as December 2019's Item Of The Month on December 1, 2019, along with the Dueling Star Fighter - Rebel Raider.
##  Function
I have you in my sights...

Back to base!

When equipped, the **Dueling Star Fighter - Imperial Enforcer** grants the _Speedy + High Jump: Imperial_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land, jump one tile higher, and switch between its wearable form and its morph form by wrenching oneself and clicking 'Transform'.
##  Trivia
  * As of the Player Appreciation Week update on August 16, 2022, player can now switch between the wearable item and its morph by wrenching oneself and clicking "Transform".
