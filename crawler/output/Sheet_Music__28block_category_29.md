# Sheet Music (block category)

**Sheet Musics** are background blocks that plays music depending on the instrument of the sheet music and the note that it is on. They can only be placed in worlds that are World Locked. When the owner of a world wrenches the World Lock, they can choose to disable the Sheet Music, change the beats per minute (BPM), or make the Sheet Music invisible.

##  Trivia
  * When sheet music blocks were first added, there was no option in the world lock's dialog box that allows the world's owner to control whether these blocks are visibly shown to visiting players. Having listened to player feedback,[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Sheet_Music_\(block_category\)#cite_note-1) the developers decided to implement the option.
