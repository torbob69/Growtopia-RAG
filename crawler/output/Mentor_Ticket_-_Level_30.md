# Mentor Ticket - Level 30

One of your apprentices has progressed nicely, and here's the proof! Wow, they've reached the maximum possible ticket level (so far)! This calls for a celebration for both of you!
Properties
This item cannot be dropped or traded.
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **3 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **4s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Mentorship

Obtained from having an apprentice reach level 30.
The **Mentor Ticket - Level 30** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Mentors & Apprentices update on March 11, 2019.
##  Function
When consumed, the player will receive 2  Growtoken.
