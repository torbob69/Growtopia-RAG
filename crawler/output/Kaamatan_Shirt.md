The **Kaamatan Shirt** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as a part of Harvest Festival 2024 on September 12, 2024.
##  Function
When equipped, the Kaamatan Shirt serves a fully cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia

    * Kaamatan Shirt (shirt)

    * Kaamatan Dress (pants)
