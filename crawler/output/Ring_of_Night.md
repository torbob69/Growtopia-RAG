Binds its wielder to the darkness and makes them one with the night! You won't need the hated touch of light to illuminate the dark with this imposing artifact on your finger...

**Ring of Night** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the Time-Tossed! (update) on September 5, 2019.
##  Function
Piercing the darkness!

Dark, darker, darker still! Your eyes fade to black.

When equipped, the **Ring of Night** grants the _Night Vision: Ring of Night_ mod, which allows the player to emit light in dark areas. It will illuminate up to 18 blocks range.
