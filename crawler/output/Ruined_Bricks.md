##  Trivia
  * The Ruined Bricks is the only block obtained from a tree spliced with a Mutated Seed that has a chance to drop a seed when broken.
    * Due to this fact, players may farm Ruined Bricks using special equipment, thus making them much more abundant compared to the other Mutated Seed blocks.
