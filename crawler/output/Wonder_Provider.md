The **Wonder Provider** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground provider block which was added as part of Wonder Week 2022 Day 1 on October 15, 2022.
##  Function

##  Trivia
  * On October 14, 2022, the Growtopia Store accidentally released the Wonder Provider before the Wonder Week 2022 update. However, on October 15, 2022, the Wonder Provider dropped Dirt upon harvesting instead of the usual Wonder Week drops. The Growtopia Team took note of the issue and went into maintenance temporarily to attempt to fix the bug. After the server went up a few hours later, servers repeatedly crashed, resulting in multiple worlds corrupted due to the Wonder Providers being near the World Lock. The corruption was later fixed hours after and disabled the Wonder Provider temporarily. On October 18, 2022, the Wonder Provider was made available again.
