The **Golden Midas Masterpants** is an unsplicable pants item which was added as part of Winter Clash 2018.
##  Function
You are glittering in gold!

Back to normal, boring skin!

When equipped, the **Golden Midas Masterpants** grants the _Golden Touch_ mod, which allows the player to change their skin color to golden yellow.
