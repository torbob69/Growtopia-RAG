The **Acid** is a splicable solid foreground pain block which was added in version 1.18.1 on April 26, 2013.
##  Function
Being a type of pain block, Acid knocks the player back and stings the player until the player stops all motion. A player will die after making contact with a pain block five times in quick succession (recovering from damage takes roughly 5 seconds). The _Fireproof_ mod will not allow the player to take more hits from Acid, unlike with lava pain blocks. Instead, the _Acid Proof_ mod, which is currently exclusively granted by the Krampus Crampons, serves this purpose instead.

##  Trivia
  * Acid is a recolor of  Lava.
    * The description of this item also refers to the latter's. Unlike Lava, however, Acid isn't considered a lava pain block.
  * After the Pre-WinterFest 2020 update on December 3, 2020, a glitch caused players to be banned upon touching Acid.
  * The only item that makes the player more resistant to acid is the Krampus Crampons, this does not modify other acid pain blocks damage (e.g Jellyfish).
