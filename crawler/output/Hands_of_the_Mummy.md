# Hands of the Mummy

Mummy, mummy, mummy. Must be scary, in a dead man's world!

The **Hands of the Mummy** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2022 on October 22, 2022.
##  Function
Dead on the outside!

Dead on the inside!

When equipped, the **Hands of the Mummy** grants the _Punch Range : Hands of the Mummy_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Monster Mummy set, which consists of the following items:

    * Monster Mummy Nemes (hat)

    * Monster Mummy Mask (face)

    * Monster Mummy Chestplate (shirt)

    * Monster Mummy Tunic (pants)

    * Monster Mummy Feet (feet)

    * Hands of the Mummy (hand)
  * The Hands of the Mummy's description is a reference to the lyrics of a song recorded by a Swedish pop group ABBA, Money, Money, Money.
