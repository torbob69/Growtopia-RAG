# Night Of The Comet (original update)

Growtopia Gazette
> Nov 28th: It's the NIGHT OF THE COMET! The comet will arrive at
> midnight Growtopia /time and will only be here for 24 hours. Under the cosmic glow of the comet, strange new materials can be discovered, and possible even spliced into amazing new items... If you miss out though, don't worry - the comet will return every month!
> WORLD RECLAMATION PROJECT: We will soon be resetting World Locked worlds that have been inactive for more than ONE YEAR, *or* the WL owner hasn't logged on in ONE YEAR, freeing up some very good world names up for new owners. Instead of happening on a certain date, it will slowly happen over time.
> - @Seth and @Hamumu
The first ever **Night Of The Comet** update was introduced on November 27, 2014. The first Night Of The Comet soon commenced the day after. It introduced the Comet Dust and its rarer counterpart, the Antimatter Dust.
