# Portrait (block category)

A **portrait** is a type of non-solid foreground block. To use, a player must have 2 of each Paint Bucket in their inventory. Upon wrenching the portrait, the player gets to choose any player on the screen whose face will be painted on the portrait. Afterwards, the former can edit the latter's emotion and write a title for others to view. Using 4 Paint Bucket - Varnish allows the player to remove the face painted.
