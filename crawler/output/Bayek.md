##  Function
Jump to adventure!

Laying low.

When equipped, the **Bayek** grants the _Double Jump & Punch Range : Bayek!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air, punch one tile further, and switch between its wearable form and its morph form by wrenching oneself and clicking 'Transform'.
