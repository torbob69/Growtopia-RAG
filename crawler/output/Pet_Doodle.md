The **Pet Doodle** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2023 Day 6 on September 2, 2023.
##  Function
When equipped, the Pet Doodle serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The **Pet Doodle** is a reference to DoodleBob, the main antagonist of the SpongeBob SquarePants episodes "Frankendoodle" & "Doodle Dimension".
