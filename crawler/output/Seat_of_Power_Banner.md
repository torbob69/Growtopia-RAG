# Seat of Power Banner

A banner depicting a powerful mage's throne.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.

Render Type: Horizontal

Hardness: **4 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **2s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Paint Preview Aqua Blue Charcoal Green Purple Red Varnish Yellow
Dungeons

It can be found naturally generated in Dungeon worlds.

Dungeons Reward

5 may be awarded to the player for completing a Dungeon at **Silver Tier or higher**.
The **Seat of Power Banner** is an unsplicable non-solid platform block which was added as part of the Dungeons update on December 9, 2024.
##  Function
When placed, the Seat of Power Banner serves a fully aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * Despite being a platform, the Seat of Power Banner cannot be stood on due to it being non-solid.
  * Any seed cannot be planted on top of Seat of Power Banner due to it being non-solid, thus displaying the message to players: "(Can't plant a tree in the air!)".
