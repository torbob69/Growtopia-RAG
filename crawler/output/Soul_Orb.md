# Soul Orb

This orb devours the souls of anything it touches - Growtopians, blocks, trees, whatever!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be transmuted.
Data

Type: Hand – Clothes
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: Full Collision
Hardness: **0 Hits** **0 Hits** – Restores after **0s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Rewards of Sacrifice

Sacrifice a(n)  Orbs Of Elixir or  Dark King's Offering (chance) to Growganoth.
The **Soul Orb** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2015.
##  Function
Suck the souls of innocent blocks!

You are disarmed.

When equipped, the **Soul Orb** grants the _Devouring Souls_ mod, which allows the player to pull other players towards them upon punching them, punch one tile further, and break blocks more slowly.
##  Trivia
  * The Soul Orb was originally obtained through sacrificing a Heartbow, not an Orbs Of Elixir. This was changed as part of Halloween Week 2016.
