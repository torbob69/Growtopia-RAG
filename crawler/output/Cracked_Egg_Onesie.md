The **Cracked Egg Onesie** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as part of Easter Week 2022 on April 15, 2022.
##  Function
When equipped, the Cracked Egg Onesie serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
When equipped together with the Cracked Egg Hat, the player will leave a trail of yolk behind when walking.
