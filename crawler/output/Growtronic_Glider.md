The **Growtronic Glider** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as April 2024's Item Of The Season on April 1st, 2024.
##  Function
Love is in the air!

Nobody loves you!

When equipped, the **Growtronic Glider** grants the _Speedy + High Jump: Growtronic Glider_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and jump one tile higher.
