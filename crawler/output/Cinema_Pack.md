# Cinema Pack
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Cinema Pack** for **6,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"It's movie time! This pack includes a Clapboard, a Black Beret, 3D Glasses, 6 Theater Curtains, 6 Marquee Blocks, a Director's Chair, 4 Theater Seats, 6 Movie Screens, a Movie Camera, and a rare GHX Speaker that plays the score from Growtopia: The Movie."
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Cinema Pack** is an item pack which was released on May 16, 2014.
##  Items
The pack contains the following items:
  * Clapboard x1
  * Black Beret x1
  * 3D Glasses x1
  * Theater Curtain x6
  * Marquee Block x6
  * Director's Chair x1
  * Theater Seat x4
  * Movie Screen x6
  * Movie Camera x1
  * GHX Speaker x1 (Rare)

##  Trivia
  * On the same day of the pack's release, the Red Glass Block and the Blue Glass Block were added into the game, the Video Of The Week (VOTW) was introduced, the Growie Award was added into the game and the first VOTW winner was announced.
