The **Oxen Battle Helm** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Lunar New Year 2021 on February 4, 2021.
##  Function
Power of the Ox!

Xo no!

When equipped, the **Oxen Battle Helm** grants the _Punch Range : Oxen Battle Helm_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Oxen Battle set, which consists of the following items:

    * Oxen Battle Helm (Hat)

    * Oxen Battle Shirt (Shirt)

    * Oxen Battle Pants (Pants)

    * Oxen Battle Boots (Feet)
