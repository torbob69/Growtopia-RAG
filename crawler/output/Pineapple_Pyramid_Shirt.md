# Pineapple Pyramid Shirt

A shirt modelled after the Great Pyramids of Pineapple Pizza. Wearing the full set drops crumbs of pinapples slices behind you!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be transmuted.
Data

Type: Shirt – Clothes
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: Full Collision
Hardness: **0 Hits** **0 Hits** – Restores after **0s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Chichen-Itza Shirt **(×1)** ,
Dangerous Pineapple Root Cutting **(×200)** and
Super Pineapple Root Cutting **(×200)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Pineapple Pyramid Shirt** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2022 on June 10, 2022.
##  Function
When equipped, the Pineapple Pyramid Shirt serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
When equipped together with the Pineapple Pyramid Hat, the player will leave a trail of crumbs of pineapple slices behind when walking.
##  Trivia
  * The Great Pyramids of Pineapple Pizza mentioned in the item's description is a pun on the Great Pyramid of Giza.
