# Gruel

This bland flavorless mush is not delicious. The only nice thing about it is that you can feed it to a Silkworm without changing its color!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **3 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **4s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Wheat **(×2)** ,
Rice **(×2)** and
Water Bucket **(×1)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
**Gruel** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Night of the Sewing Dead update.
##  Function
A Gruel can be fed to a Silkworm without changing its color. When consumed, the player will say, "Bleh." and stick their tongue out.
