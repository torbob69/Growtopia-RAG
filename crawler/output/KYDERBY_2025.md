# KYDERBY/2025

< KYDERBY
##  World Building Contest
A world-building contest is held throughout April as a preliminary to the actual racing event. The deadline for world submissions is set at 12:00 PM GT time on Friday, April 25. submitting their own race worlds through a forum thread,[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/KYDERBY/2025#cite_note-build2-1) players in the community will get a chance of having their worlds selected as the official race worlds during the Derby. 20 race worlds will be selected from the pool of entries. The players who have built each of these worlds will be given a  Mint Julep.
A full list of the contest's rules can be viewed here.
