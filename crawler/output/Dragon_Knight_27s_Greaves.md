# Dragon Knight's Greaves

Forged in a master blacksmith's furnace and stamped with runes of protection, a Dragon Knight's suit of armor is their last and best line of defense against the horrid flames and claws of their scaly foes! These greaves are but one link of many in that chain of defense, a perfect way to keep your legs safe! Trust me, when you're dealing with dragons, preserving your ability to run away is critical.

The **Dragon Knight's Greaves** is an unsplicable pants item which was added as part of the Dragon Invasion update on February 4, 2019.
##  Function
When equipped, the Dragon Knight's Greaves serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
