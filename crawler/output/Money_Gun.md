The **Money Gun** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2019 on March 15, 2019.
##  Function
When equipped, a Money Gun gives the player a special punch effect of coins being fired, which burst into smaller versions of themselves upon hitting the targeted tile.
