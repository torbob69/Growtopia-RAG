The **Nutri-goop** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Star Trek Prodigy on June 27, 2022.
##  Function
Big and strong!

Back to being small!

When consumed, the Nutri-goop grants the _Nutri-goop: Grow Effect_ mod, which increases the players size in a similar manner to Meat or Glove of the Giants. This mod lasts for 10 minutes.
