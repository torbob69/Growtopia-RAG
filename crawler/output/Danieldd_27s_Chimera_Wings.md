**Danieldd's Chimera Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of SummerFest 2018 on July 6, 2018.
##  Function
You can double jump! It's like flying but not!

Your feet are back on the ground!

When equipped, the **Danieldd's Chimera Wings** grants the _Double Jump: Chimera Wings!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
##  Trivia
  * This item is sponsored by Danieldd for completing all the Epic Quests
  * The  Danieldd's Chimera Wings is a recolored and slightly changed sprite of the  Phoenix Wings.
