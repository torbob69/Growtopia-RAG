The **Villain Portrait - H.R. Geiger** is an unsplicable background block which was added as part of the Geiger Day update on May 5, 2017, the same day Cinco De Mayo Week 2017 was released.
##  Function
When placed, the Villain Portrait - H.R. Geiger serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
