The **Vacation Van** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Beach Party 2022 on August 6, 2022.
##  Function
To the beach!

Back home!

When equipped, the **Vacation Van** grants the _Speedy + Light Source: Vacation Van_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and emit light in dark areas.
