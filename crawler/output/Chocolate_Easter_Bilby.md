# Chocolate Easter Bilby

This is the page for **Chocolate Easter Bilby**.
You may be looking for **Chocolate Bunny** instead.
Show your support to the Australian wildlife with a chocolate Easter Bilby! Rather than selling the traditional Easter bunnies, stores in Australia sell a Bilby variant in order to raise money for the endangered species. Consuming this item removes the egg hunting basket debuff and allows you to instantly collect the egg you found! Egg-cellent!

This recipe **does not use up** the item in the player's inventory.
The **Chocolate Easter Bilby** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Easter Week 2020.
##  Function
When consumed, it will immediately transform the player's Full Egg-Hunting Basket into an Egg-Hunting Basket, thus bypassing the 30-minute cooldown and allowing the player to instantly collect the egg they've found.
##  Trivia
  * Attempting to cook a Chocolate Easter Bilby without equipping Cooking Recipe - Chocolate Easter Bilby will end up creating a Chocolate Bunny instead.
