The **Go Anywhere Surfboard** is an unsplicable feet item which was the added as June 2020's Item Of The Month on June 1, 2020.
##  Function
Surfs Up, this wave is huge!

Total Wipeout dude!

When equipped, the **Go Anywhere Surfboard** grants the _Speedy: Rip Tide!_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and float on water for three seconds. Additionally, when standing still, a huge wave will appear behind the player.
