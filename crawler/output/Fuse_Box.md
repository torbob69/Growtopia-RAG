# Fuse Box

Your standard wall, now with electrical wires and junctions and stuff! Everything's cooler when you stick a fuse box on it.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.

May yield **10** item(s) each.

The **Fuse Box** is an unsplicable background block which was added as part of the B.O.O. Update on May 1, 2018.
##  Function
When placed, the Fuse Box serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * As part of the Block Party 2 update, the quantity of Fuse Box obtained from a Spirit Storage Unit explosion was increased from 1 to 3.
