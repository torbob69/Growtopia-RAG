# Owlbeard

Blue Dwarf needs owl, badly.
Properties
This item cannot be dropped or traded.
This item never drops any seeds.

Type: Face – Clothes

Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Exclusive Item

This item was given to **BlueDwarf** for their contributions as a voluntary moderator.
The **Owlbeard** is an unsplicable face item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2014 on October 30, 2014.
##  Function
When equipped, it grants the Fiery Pet mod, which shoots fireballs when punching. When the player is standing still, an owl pops out of the beard and flies around him.
