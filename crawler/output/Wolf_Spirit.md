# Wolf Spirit

Congratulations on winning the War of Wolves Daily Challenge! With this spirit equipped (it is a back item, by the way), you can double jump, and the guidance of the Wolf Spirit will improve your Wolfworld rewards (usually).
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be transmuted.

Daily Challenge

Place in the top three of the **War of Wolves** Daily Challenge.
The **Wolf Spirit** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of the Daily Challenges update on November 18, 2015.
##  Function
You can jump in mid-air! It's like flying, but less.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Wolf Spirit** grants the _Double Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and increase the odds of obtaining rare prizes in a WOLFWORLD.
When used in a WOLFWORLD, the following items are removed from the prize pool.
•  Red Coat
•  Wolfman Mask
