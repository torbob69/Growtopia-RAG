The **Lucid Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Wonder Week 2021 Day 1 on October 11, 2021.
##  Function
Shine on you crazy diamond!

No more dazzle!

When equipped, the **Lucid Wings** grants the _Double-Jump: Lucid Wings!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
##  Trivia
  * This item's equip message is a reference to the song: "Shine On You Crazy Diamond" by Pink Floyd.
