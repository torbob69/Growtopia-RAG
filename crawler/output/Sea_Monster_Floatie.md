# Sea Monster Floatie

Blow it up and take a swim!

**1h**
Super Fireworks Reward

May be obtained from firing off  Super Fireworks with **200** Fireworks in your inventory.
The **Sea Monster Floatie** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of the SummerFest 2014 on June 27, 2014.
##  Function
When equipped, the **Sea Monster Floatie** allows the player to float on water permanently with slow ascent.
