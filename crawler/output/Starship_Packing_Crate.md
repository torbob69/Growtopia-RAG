# Starship Packing Crate

This is the page for **Starship Packing Crate**.
You may be looking for **Box of Supplies** instead.
Need to transport something valuable across the gulf of space? Well, you can't trust someone to just carry it by hand, and you can't pack it in a lame, ground-based crate! Enter: The Starship Packing Crate! Specially made to endure the rigors of the void, this will - hopefully - keep the goods inside it safe, as well.
