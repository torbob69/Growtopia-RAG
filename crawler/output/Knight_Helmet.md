The **Knight Helmet** is a splicable hat item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2014 Day 1 on August 8, 2014.
##  Function
When equipped, the Knight Helmet serves a fully cosmetic purpose.
Step 4 of the  Quest For The Sky requires the player to deliver 1,000 of this item.
