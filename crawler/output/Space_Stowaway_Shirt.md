The **Space Stowaway Shirt** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as part of the Aliens update on November 14, 2021.
##  Function
When equipped, Space Stowaway Shirt serves a fully cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Space Stowaway set, which consists of the following items:

    * Space Stowaway Mask (Face)

    * Space Stowaway Shirt (Shirt)

    * Space Stowaway Pants (Pants)
