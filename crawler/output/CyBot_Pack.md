# CyBot Pack
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **CyBot Pack** for **15,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"Grants one random (Rare) CyBot! Use CyBlock Commands to send these mechanical monsters into action! Note: Each CyBot is a perma-item, and will never be lost when destroyed."
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **CyBot Pack** was released alongside the March Update. It is available for 15,000 gems in the gem store and will provide one CyBot, besides the ShockBot - Level 1.
##  Contents of the Pack
This pack includes either one of the stated below:
  * ShooterBot - Level 1

OR
  * FlameBot - Level 1

OR
  * SpikeBot - Level 1
