The **Rainbow Charm** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2023 on March 17, 2023.
##  Function
Charmed, I'm sure!

Not very charming!

When equipped, the **Rainbow Charm** grants the _Double-Jump & Speedy: Rainbow Charm_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and move faster on land.
