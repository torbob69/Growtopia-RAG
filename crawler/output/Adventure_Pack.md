# Adventure Pack

Growtopia Gazette
> June 14th: ADVENTURE PACK! Throw me the idol! Create your own exciting adventures using new blocks that allow dialog choices and story item collecting. - @Seth and @Hamumu
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Adventure Pack** for **20,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"Join Dr. Exploro and her father (also technically Dr. Exploro) as they seek out adventure! You can make your own adventure maps with the tools in this pack: 4 Gateways To Adventure, 4 Path Markers, a Lazy Cobra, an Adventure Brazier, 4 Adventure Barriers, 4 Adventure Items (Rope, Torch, Key, and Golden Idol), and a Rare Adventuring Mustache, Explorer's Ponytail and Sling Bag."
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Adventure Pack** is an item pack which was released on the 14 June 2014.
##  Items
Currently, the **Adventure Pack** contains the following items:
  * Gateway To Adventure x4
  * Path Marker x4
  * Lazy Cobra x1
  * Adventure Brazier x1
  * Adventure Barrier x4
  * Adventure Item - Rope x1
  * Adventure Item - Torch x1
  * Adventure Item - Key x1
  * Adventure Item - Golden Idol x1
  * Adventuring Mustache x1 (Rare)
  * Explorer's Ponytail x1 (Rare)
  * Adventurer's Sling Bag x1 (Rare)

##  Trivia
  * The Adventure Item - Banana, Pith Helmet, Jodhpurs, Explorer's Shovel, and the Safari Jacket were also implemented into the game alongside this pack.
  * The Adventure Pack was updated in the The Adventure Pack Strikes Back! update on the June 1, 2017 with additional items, and the price was changed from 8,000 Gems to 20,000 Gems.
