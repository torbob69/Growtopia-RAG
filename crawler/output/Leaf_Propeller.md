The **Leaf Propeller** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of SummerFest 2023 on July 13, 2023.
##  Function
Have a graceful decent!

Battered by the wind!

When equipped, the **Leaf Propeller** grants the _Slow Fall: Leaf Propeller_ mod, which allows the player to glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
