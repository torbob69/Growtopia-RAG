# Winter Clash Finale Ticket

Beware! Using this ticket will send you off to a world of great dangers and riches! When you are there, you will have only 25 MINUTES before the power of this ticket diminishes and you are returned to the real world. Can you conquer the chaos of Winter Clash in time?
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **6 Hits** **5 Hits** – Restores after **4s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
The **Winter Clash Finale Ticket** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as a part of Winter Clash 2019. It can be obtained after completing a branch which can be found by tapping on the Leaderboard Button and tapping on "Winter Clash Rewards". The player needs to buy everything in the branch before receiving the Legendary Clash Key & Finale Ticket.
##  Function
When consumed, it will direct the player to the FINALEWORLD in which the player has 25 minutes to finish the parkour - the farther the player goes, the better and rarer prizes the player has a chance to receive.
