The **Golden Ribbon Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2026 on February 12, 2026.
##  Function
Tied the knot!

We all fall down!

When equipped, the **Golden Ribbon Wings** grants the _Double Jump : Golden Ribbon Wings_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
