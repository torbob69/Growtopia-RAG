# Ghost

This is an **NPC** , or a non-player character.
A **ghost** is an NPC which inhabits haunted worlds (worlds which haven't been visited for a long time). They were added as part of the Battlers Of the Otherworldly update.
##  Spawning
Ghosts spawn in a world which has not been visited for a long time. They can spawn in any world, with the exception of worlds with Ghost Charms placed in them. A limit of 25 ghosts is imposed upon each world.
##  Behavior
A ghost flies around mindlessly. It, however, moves towards a player with a Neutron Gun and a Neutron Pack whose punch is in its way. Depending on its type, a ghost either does not visibly appear or appears faintly to players without Spectral Goggles or Synthetic Oculum.
Ghosts can grant certain mods to players upon contact unless special items are worn, such as the Rubber Boots. When a ghost comes in contact with the rings of a placed Ghost Jar or Ghost Trap, the ghost will be sucked in and disappear alongside the item. Depending on its type, a Ghost-In-A-Jar, Mind-Ghost-In-A-Jar, or an Ancestor Mooncake will then appear in one's backpack.
Equipping a Neutron Pack along with a Neutron Power Glove allows the player to instantly remove surrounding ghosts with each punch. Unlike the Neutron Gun, a Neutron Power Glove does not nullify the player's punch damage, nor does it kill the player should they end up crossing fire with other players.
##  Versions
###  Ghost
“ — AIYEE! A ghost! — ”— _1st message type_
“ — I've been slimed! — ”— _2nd message type_
  * First added as part of the Battlers Of the Otherworldly update on July 15, 2016.
  * Slows the player down for 6 seconds upon contact.
    * Players equipping Rubber Boots or Ghostking's Glory are immune to this effect.
  * Does not visibly appear to players without Spectral Goggles or Synthetic Oculum equipped.
  * Automatically spawns in worlds which haven't been visited by any player for some time.
    * Can be directly spawned by consuming a Ghost-In-A-Jar or completing the riddles of a Spirit Board.
  * Provides a Ghost-In-A-Jar when captured.

###  Mind Ghost
  * First added as part of the B.O.O. update on May 1, 2018.
  * Causes the player's control to become inverted for 6 seconds upon contact.
    * Players equipping Foil Hat, Alien Mind Protector, or Ghostking's Glory are immune to this effect.
  * Moves faster than the other types of ghosts, making it considerably more difficult to catch.
  * Does not visibly appear to players without Spectral Goggles or Synthetic Oculum equipped.
  * Replaces a percentage of a haunted world's ghosts each time the world is loaded.
    * Can be directly spawned by consuming a Mind-Ghost-In-A-Jar.
  * Provides a Mind Ghost-In-A-Jar when captured.

###  Ghost Shark
“ — GHOOOOST SHAAAARRRKK!!! — ”— _1st message type_
“ — GHOST SHARK ATTACK! — ”— _2nd message type_
“ — CHOMP CHOMP — ”— _3rd message type_
  * First added as part of Halloween Week 2016 on October 27, 2016.
  * Kills the player upon contact.
    * Players equipping Ghostking's Glory are immune to this effect.
  * Appears faintly to players without Spectral Goggles or Synthetic Oculum equipped.
  * Replaces Ghosts and Mind Ghosts during the entirety of Halloween Week. Reverts back once the event has ended.
    * Can be directly spawned by consuming a Ghost-In-A-Jar while the event is active.
  * Provides a Ghost-In-A-Jar when captured.

###  Ghost of Winterfest Past
“ — It's the ghost of Winterfest past! — ”— _1st message type_
“ — I feel a chill in the air! — ”— _2nd message type_
  * First added as part of WinterFest 2016 on December 16, 2016.
  * Freezes the player for 2 seconds upon contact.
    * Players equipping Ghostking's Glory are immune to this effect.
  * Appears faintly to players without Spectral Goggles or Synthetic Oculum equipped.
  * Replaces Ghosts and Mind Ghosts during the entirety of WinterFest. Reverts back once the event has ended.
    * Can be directly spawned by consuming a Ghost-In-A-Jar while the event is active.
  * Provides a Ghost-In-A-Jar or Last Christmas Cut when captured.

###  Ancestor Ghost
“ — The ghost became an Ancestor Mooncake! — ”— _Message after capturing_
  * First added as part of Harvest Festival 2016 on September 15, 2016.
  * Acts similarly to the regular Ghost, slowing the player down for 6 seconds upon contact.
    * Players equipping Rubber Boots or Ghostking's Glory are immune to this effect.
  * Randomly spawns from harvested trees during Harvest Festival; the higher the rarity, the more likely it is for the ghost to spawn. Reverts into a regular Ghost when a world becomes inactive (all players have left the world).
    * Cannot be directly spawned.
  * Provides an Ancestor Mooncake when captured.

### Boss Ghost     _Main article:Boss Ghost_
  * First added as part of the B.O.O. update on May 1, 2018.
  * Randomly spawns in abandoned worlds (worlds that are not locked) during Ghost Day.
    * Can be directly spawned by completing the riddles of a Dark Spirit Board.
  * Partaking in defeating one gives the player a Boss Goo.

##  Trivia
  * Ghost will not spawn in worlds with a Ghost Charm enabled.
    * Ghosts that were spawned before the Ghost Charm is placed will still stay there until removed.
  * Ghosts can be removed instantly by consuming a Ghost-Be-Gone.
  * All ghosts in a world can be removed by calling 52368 on a Telephone and paying 15 World Locks.
