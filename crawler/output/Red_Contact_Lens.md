# Red Contact Lens

Give your eyes an overhaul and get the red in with these pupil-painting lenses! You may need multiple lenses - and other colors - to achieve a desired effect.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.

Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Contact Lens Pack** for **15,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"Need a colorful new look? This pack includes 10 random Contact Lens colors (and may include Contact Lens Cleaning Solution, to return to your natural eye color)!"
Every purchase has a chance of giving **1-4** items.
The **Red Contact Lens** is a consumable which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2018 Day 6 alongside the Contact Lens Pack on August 11, 2018.
##  Function
To use, one must stand in front of a Bathroom Mirror, then consume the contact lens. This will give a light shade of red to the player's pupils. The more eye drops one use (maximum of 8 drops), the darker the color gets.
