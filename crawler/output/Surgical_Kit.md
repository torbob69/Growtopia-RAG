# Surgical Kit
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Surgical Kit** for **12,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"Get all the tools you need to start up your own private surgical practice and 1 Surg-E bot to practice on. and 5 each of thirteen different Surgical Tools you'll needto do that surgery!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Surgical Kit** was released on November 21, 2013 as part of the Growtopia General Hospital update.
This pack includes:
  * Surg-E ×1
  * Surgical Anesthetic × 5
  * Surgical Antibiotics ×5
  * Surgical Antiseptic ×5
  * Surgical Clamp ×5
  * Surgical Defibrillator ×5
  * Surgical Lab Kit ×5
  * Surgical Pins ×5
  * Surgical Scalpel ×5
  * Surgical Splint ×5
  * Surgical Sponge ×5
  * Surgical Stitches ×5
  * Surgical Transfusion ×5
  * Surgical Ultrasound ×5
  * Surgical Love Mallet ×2 (Only during Valentine's Week)

##  Trivia
  * The January 2017 monthly update changed the Surgical Kit price from 8,000 to 12,000 Gems. The update also reduced the amount of tools received from 10 to 5 and added 6 new tools : Surgical Lab Kit, Surgical Defibrillator, Surgical Pins, Surgical Clamp, Surgical Transfusion and Surgical Ultrasound.
  * On June 13, 2019, during the Blocktor's Orders! update, x10 Med-A-Check and a Train-E were added to the pack.
  * On February 13, 2020, on Valentine’s Week 2020, a new tool called the Surgical Love Mallet was added to the Surgical Kit but is only obtainable by purchasing the pack during Valentine's Week.
  * On August 19, 2022, on Player Appreciation Week 2022 Day 5, Train-E, Heart Monitor, Hospital Bed, and Med-A-Check are no longer available in this pack.
