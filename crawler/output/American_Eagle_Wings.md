The **American Eagle Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2021 on November 18, 2021.
##  Function
Red, white and blue!

Now I feel blue

When equipped, the **American Eagle Wings** grants the _Double-Jump: Eagle Wings_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
The  The Seven Seas : The Wings of Atlantis! mission requires 1 of this item.
