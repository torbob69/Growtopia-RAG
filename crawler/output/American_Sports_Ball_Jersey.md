The **American Sports Ball Jersey** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2020.
##  Function
MVP PAYDAY...

Aw, no more bonus gems.

When equipped, the **American Sports Ball Jersey** grants the _Extra Gems..._ mod, which allows the player to get a 10% chance to receive extra gems when breaking blocks. The higher rarity of blocks broken, the higher amount of gems would be received. This item's mod is similar to that of Ancestral Lens of Riches : Level 6; however, the former's effect does not stack with the latter's.
