##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Greatest Swordsman's set, which consists of the following items:

    * Greatest Swordsman's Hair (hair)

    * Greatest Swordsman's Third Blade (face)

    * Greatest Swordsman's Kimono (shirt)

    * Greatest Swordsman's Sandals (feet)

    * Greatest Swordsman's Cloak (back)

    * Greatest Swordsman's Dual Wield (hand)
  * The full Greatest Swordsman's Set is a reference to a character with name Roronoa Zoro, from a Japanese manga series called One Piece.
