This Omega-class firework is actually illegal in all countries. All of them. LAUNCH IT ANYWAY!!! Like cereal, it comes with a special prize inside. Note: it will use up 200 Fireworks from your inventory to ignite the Saturn V thruster it uses.

The **Super Fireworks** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of SummerFest 2014 on June 27, 2014.
