The **The Oillipheist** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2023 on March 17, 2023.
##  Function
Transformation sanctum!

Are you ready?!

When equipped, the **The Oillipheist** grants the _Speedy + Punch Range: The Oillipheist_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land, punch one tile further, and float on water for three seconds.
##  Trivia
  * The Oillipheist is a reference to Oilliphéist, which is a monstrous sea serpent in Irish mythology.
    * Its equip and unequip messages are reference to a song called _Friends on the Other Side_ which is from the movie: "The Princess and the Frog".
