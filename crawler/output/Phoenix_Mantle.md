The **Phoenix Mantle** is an unsplicable neck item which was added as part of SummerFest 2024 on July 11, 2024.
##  Function
Rise from the ashes!

Turned to dust!

When equipped, the **Phoenix Mantle** grants the _Fireproof + Punch Range: Phoenix Mantle_ mod, which allows the player to take 50% less damage from lava pain blocks and punch one tile further.
