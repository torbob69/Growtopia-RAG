The **Builder's Lock** is an unsplicable solid lock foreground block which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2017 Day 4 on August 20, 2017.
##  Function
A Builder's Lock can lock a maximum of 200 tiles. It can be wrenched to change its settings such as giving other players access, ignoring empty air, or setting the locked area to public. Additionally, it shows an option to allow building, breaking, or both.
##  Trivia
  * As part of the Quality of Life Changes, the Builder's Lock now shows the number of days the owner of the lock last played and disintegrates after 180 days of inactivity, similar to Small Lock, Big Lock, and Huge Lock.
