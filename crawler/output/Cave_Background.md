The **Cave Background** is an unsplicable background block which has been added since Growtopia's launch. Layers of Cave Background are naturally generated in most worlds, excluding certain blasted ones.
##  Function
When placed, the Cave Background serves a purely aesthetic purpose.

##  Trivia
  * Dark Cave Background and Monochromatic Cave Background looks very similar to Cave Background, and will only appear in cave-blasted and monochrome-blasted worlds.
  * A Digger's Spade will allow the player to break both Dirt and Cave Background in one hit.
  * Cave Background Seeds can be spliced with Sign Seeds to make Rollback Plaques during Apology Weekends.
