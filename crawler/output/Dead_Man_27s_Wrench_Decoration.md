The **Dead Man's Wrench Decoration** is a wrench decoration which was added as part of the October Season on October 1, 2025.
##  Function
When equipped through the 'Wrench Customization' tab when wrenching oneself, the Dead Man's Wrench Decoration adds a grave to the lower right of the player's wrench connected to their name.
