The **Rice Crop Wallpaper** is an unsplicable background block which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2021 on September 16, 2021.
##  Function
When placed, Rice Crop Wallpaper serves a fully aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * As part of Harvest Festival 2022, the quantity of Rice Crop Wallpaper obtained from harvesting Building Blocks Machines was decreased from 5 to 1.
