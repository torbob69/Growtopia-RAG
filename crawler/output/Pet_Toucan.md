# Pet Toucan

Caw! This adorable avian ally likes tropical fruits, crunchy nuts, and keeping its beautiful beak sharp!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Chest – Clothes
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: Full Collision

**1h**
Super Fireworks Reward

May be obtained from firing off  Super Fireworks with **200** Fireworks in your inventory.
The **Pet Toucan** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of SummerFest 2019 on June 27, 2019.
##  Function
When equipped, a toucan appears and follows the player around. The Pet Toucan serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
