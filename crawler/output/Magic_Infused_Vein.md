Magic Infused Veins are an unbreakable block introduced during the Spelunkorama Strikes Back! update. They naturally spawn in Mines.
##  Function
Magic Infused Veins can be found near Pure Magic Ores. They block the path for players to reach Pure Magic Ores. They can only be destroyed using Mining Explosives and will drop either Magic Infused Stone or its seed.
