The **Draconic Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Chinese New Year 2018. Winning the Lucky Token raffle or the building competition could also reward the player with 1 Draconic Wings.
##  Function
You can jump in mid-air! It's like flying, but less.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Draconic Wings** grants the _Double Jump: Draconic Wings_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
Step 18 of the  Quest For The Sky requires the player to deliver 1 of this item.
##  Trivia
  * Draconic Wings were launched in the Chinese New Year 2018 update with 500 in existence. Since then, the total has grown by 100 every year, reaching 1,300 as of 2026.
  * In 2018, the Lucky Token Raffle was introduced, giving 100 winners a chance to receive Draconic Wings.
  * In 2018, 10 Draconic Wings were given out through the Chinese New Year building competition, rewarded on February 26th, 2018.
  * 2019 onward: +100 added each year via Lucky Fortune Cookie, with the item's description updated alongside each increase.
