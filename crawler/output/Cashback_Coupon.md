# Cashback Coupon

A Black Friday Cashback Coupon! Exclusive just for Black Friday weekend! Filled with gems or the chance at a Black Friday Black Box!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **5 Hits** **4 Hits** – Restores after **8s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
The **Cashback Coupon** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Black Friday 2020 on November 27, 2020. During the Black Friday event, every hour two Store Packs are featured that would reward a new Cashback Coupon with every 1,000 Gems of the Store Pack purchase.
##  Function
After a player consumes a Cashback Coupon, they would receive up to 900 Gems or a chance to obtain a Black Friday Black Box.
##  Trivia
  * As of Black Friday 2022, the Cashback Coupon gives up to 900 gems upon consumption, previously up to 800.
