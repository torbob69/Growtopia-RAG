The **Pineapple Trooper Helmet** is an unsplicable hair item which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2020 on June 4, 2020.
##  Function
When equipped, the Pineapple Trooper Helmet serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The Pineapple Trooper Helmet drops in place of the Startopian Empire - Combat Troop Mk.II Helmet during Super Pineapple Party.
