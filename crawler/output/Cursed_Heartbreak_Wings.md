The **Cursed Heartbreak Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2022 on October 22. 2022.
##  Function
Break some hearts!

You broke the wings!

When equipped, the **Cursed Heartbreak Wings** grants the _Double Jump: Cursed Heartbreak Wings!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
##  Trivia
  * Cursed Heartbreak Wings is a recolor of  Heartbreak Wings and its golden counterpart  Golden Heartbreak Wings.
