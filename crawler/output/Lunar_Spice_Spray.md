The **Lunar Spice Spray** is an unsplicable consumable that was added during Chinese New Year 2018.
##  Function
When consumed on a tree, it speeds up the growth of a Chinese New Year event tree by 24 hours.

##  Trivia
  * Players can't use **Lunar Spice Spray** on trees that aren't from Chinese New Year event.
  * As of Lunar New Year 2021, the Oriental Spice Spray's price increased to 5,000 Gems from 2,000 Gems.
  * As of Lunar New Year 2022, the Oriental Spice Spray's price increased to 10,000 Gems from 5,000 Gems.
  * As of Lunar New Year 2024, the Oriental Spice Spray's named was changed to Lunar Spice Spray.
