# Fire Asteroid

Tearing through the atmosphere, this chunk of rock has ignited with whirls of flame! Now, thanks to magnets or something, it will float and burn forever!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.

Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Fire Essence **(×20)** ,
Earth Essence **(×5)** and
Asteroid **(×1)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The Fire Asteroid is an animated block which was added along with the Hidden Riches update.
