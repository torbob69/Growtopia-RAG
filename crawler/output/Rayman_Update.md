# Rayman Update

Growtopia Gazette
> January 1st: Winterfest Has Ended... and Rayman Strikes!
> Pack up the wrapping paper and brew a celebratory mug of cocoa, because Winterfest has come to an end! You all did an amazing job with an amazingly grumpy Growch, and we couldn't be happier (seriously, our hearts are all at level 10)! Well done Growtopians - now let's all rest up and look forward to a fantastic new friend to welcome in the New Year... Rayman!
> That's right: our favorite Rayperson ever, Rayman himself, has joined Growtopia! Except... it looks like he's lost a few things in his journey. Yikes!
> Rayman's precious Hoodie, Shoes, Nose, and Hair are scattered throughout our worlds! See if you can locate these pieces of his classic outfit - we've already found his Fist and made it our Item of the Month to celebrate his arrival! It even comes with a friend of his: Globox! He might know more about where to find his buddy's missing items too...
> I wonder what happens when you wear all those pieces at once...
> Happy hunting!
> -The Growtopia Team
The **Rayman Update** was released on January 1st, 2018. It came following the conclusion of Winterfest 2017. The Item of the Month is the Rayman's Fist and Globox. The Rayman's Fist would spawn the Globox which would give out clues to finding other parts of Rayman.
New items introduced include:
Item: RecipeRayman's Fist: January 2018's Item Of The Month.
Rayman's Hair: Possible drop from successfully performing surgery while wearing a Pirate Hat.
Rayman's Hoodie: Possible drop from successfully defeating Dr. Destructo or Almighty Seth in crimefighting.
Rayman's Shoes: Possible drop from cutting a perfect Gar on a Cutting Board.
Rayman's Nose: Possible drop from using an Amber Block in a DNA Extractor.
