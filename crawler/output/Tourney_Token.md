# Tourney Token

Your reward for victory in The Grand Tournament. Put it in the Prize Ladder to win fabulous prizes!
Properties
This item cannot be dropped or traded.
This item never drops any seeds.
This item has no use... by itself.
Data

Type: Component
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **6 Hits** **5 Hits** – Restores after **5s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
The **Tourney Token** is a prize that a player obtains when they win a battle in the world TOURNAMENT during The Grand Tournament.
##  Function
It can be placed in a Prize Ladder for a chance to obtain rewards.
There is a 1/3 chance that every time a player places a Tourney Token, that they will lose all their tokens and have to restart. There are a maximum of 16 levels, where the chance of reaching the last level is 65536 in 43046721, or, approximately 0.15%.
