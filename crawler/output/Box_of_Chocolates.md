The **Box of Chocolates** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Voucher Dayz on April 16, 2026.
##  Function
When consumed, it gives the player six of the following Chocolates:
  * Square Heart Chocolate
  * Milky Heart Chocolate
  * Heart Chocolate
  * Hazelnut Chocolate
  * Crystal Chocolate
  * Crunchy Chocolate
  * Buttercream Chocolate
  * Bar Chocolate
