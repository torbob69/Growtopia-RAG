The **Mechanical Wrench Style** is a wrench style which was added as part of the September Season on September 1, 2024.
##  Function
When equipped through the 'Wrench Customization' tab when wrenching oneself, the Mechanical Wrench Style replaces the player's wrench connected to their name.
