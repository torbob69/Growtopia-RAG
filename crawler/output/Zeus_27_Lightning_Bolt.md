The **Zeus' Lightning Bolt** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2014 on September 5, 2014.
##  Function
You electrocute things.

The tingling sensation is gone.

When equipped, it grants the _Fistful of Thunder_ mod, which replaces the player's fist with thunder.
##  Trivia
  * Despite the item being untradeable, players can still trade it by making it, not collecting the item, and selling the World Key.
  * The phrase "1.21 gigawatts" on the item's description is a reference to the time machine in Back to the Future, which required 1.21 gigawatts to run.
  * On 14th December, 2020, a glitch occured whereby players were able to obtain **Zeus' Lightning Bolt seeds**. Upon realisation, the developer team quickly took action and locked the **Zeus' Lightning Bolt seeds** with many other items that were affected by the same glitch in order to prevent the issue from growing bigger. After the glitch was patched, all the **Zeus' Lightning Bolt seeds** were removed from the game. However, there are some Painting Easels exist with **Zeus' Lightning Bolt seed** painted on them as a remark of their existence.
