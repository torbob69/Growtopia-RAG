The **Thermometer** is an unsplicable non-solid SFX foreground block which was added as part of SummerFest 2021 on July 8, 2021.
##  Function
Being a type of SFX foreground block, a Thermometer plays a short animation and a ringing sound effect when punched. It also makes the player say, "_It's getting warmer!_ " during Easter Week, "_WOW! It's soooo hot!_ " or, "_It's a sunny 77F (25C) in Growtopia!_ " during the summer season and "_It's a bit chilly!_ " during the winter season.
