##  Function
Ready to glide and be radical!

Less radical by half!

When equipped, the **Golden Radical Rider** grants the _Speedy: Golden Radical Rider_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land. Additionally, when the player dances (using the `/dance` command), it forces nearby players to dance as well.
