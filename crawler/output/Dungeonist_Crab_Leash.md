# Dungeonist Crab Leash

Once a many-limbed delver of dungeons, now a happy follower for you! Just... don't tell him how tasty he looks, okay? It makes him crabby.

1 has a chance to be obtained from training a(n)  Dungeonist Crab.
The **Dungeonist Crab Leash** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of SummerFest 2019 on June 27, 2019.
##  Function
When equipped, a crab leash appears and follows the player around. The Dungeonist Crab Leash serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
