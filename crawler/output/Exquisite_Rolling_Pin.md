The**Exquisite Rolling Pin** is a hand item which was added as part of Winter Clash 2018.
##  Function
Exquisite cooking skills.

You're not even a sous chef.

When equipped, this item grants the _Cooking Skills: Exquisite Rolling Pin_ mod, which increases cooked food's quality by 3%. This effect stacks with other items that provide a similar function, such as the Sweet Potato Tots and Dumplings Hat.
