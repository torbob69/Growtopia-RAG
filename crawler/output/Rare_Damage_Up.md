The **Rare Damage Up** is a Dungeon ability which was added as part of the Dungeons update on December 9, 2024.
##  Function
When the player receives this ability, their damage increases by 10.
##  Trivia
  * The Rare Damage Up uses the same sprite as the following items:
    * Common Damage Up
    * Epic Damage Up
  * After the Dungeons updated on October 23rd,2025, a changes has been made that **Rare Damage Up** adjusted their damage increases from 40 to 10.
