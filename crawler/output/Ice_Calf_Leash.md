The **Ice Calf Leash** is a splicable pet hand item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2014 on October 30, 2014.
##  Function
Your little friend blasts ice beams.

Your friend has flown the coop.

When equipped, a blue calf pet appears and follows the player around. Moreover, this item grants the _Icy Pet_ mod, which replaces the player's punch with ice beams fired from the calf's mouth.
