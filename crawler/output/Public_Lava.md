The **Public Lava** is an unobtainable solid lava pain foreground block which was added alongside the City Pack on August 18, 2013.
##  Function
Public Lava are generally used during the Dr. Destructo special event, where Dr. Destructo has the ability to spawn them in the world. Since it is considered a public block, anyone in the world can destroy Public Lava regardless of if they have access to the lock.
Being a lava pain block, a Public Lava knocks the player back and puts the player on fire until the player stops all motion when touched. A player will die after making contact with a lava pain block five times in quick succession (recovering from damage takes roughly 5 seconds), but a player with the _Fireproof_ mod may take up to ten consecutive hits.
When locking a Public Lava or any other public block with a Builder's Lock and setting it to only allow building, any other player will be limited from breaking the block.
##  Trivia
  * On August 14, 2014, developer Seth gave some Public Lava out to the public by mistake at the Super Mod Dance Party.
  * Being unmovable, Public Lavas are unable to be picked up using a Flashback Flan.
  * The  Public Lava's design is based on the regular  Lava.
