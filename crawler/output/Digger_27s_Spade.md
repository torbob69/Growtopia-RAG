# Digger's Spade

This is the page for **Digger's Spade**.
You may be looking for **Explorer's Shovel** instead.
This shovel is very specifically designed for one purpose: to shovel dirt. It is pretty useless at digging anywhere else, but dang if it doesn't rip dirt apart (and Cave Background).
Properties
This item cannot be dropped or traded.
This item never drops any seeds.

Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Digger's Spade** for **200 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"This may appear to be a humble shovel, but in fact it is enchanted with the greatest magic in Growtopia. It can smash Dirt or Cave Background in a single hit! Unfortunately, it's worthless at digging through anything else. Note: The spade is UNTRADEABLE."
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Digger's Spade** is an unsplicable hand item which was added added during the Player Appreciation Week 2015 Day Six.
##  Function
Dirt, beware!

-

When equipped, the **Digger's Spade** grants the _Dig, Dug._ mod, which allows the player to break Dirt and Cave Background blocks in one hit and break blocks more slowly.
Step 14 of the  Quest Of The Mech requires the player to deliver 3 of this item.
