##  Function
Blast blocks from farther away! But weaker than punch.

Your vision is normal.

When equipped, the **Cyclopean Visor** grants the _Eye Beam_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further and break blocks more slowly. It will also replace the player's punching effect with a laser beam fired from their eyes.

##  Trivia
  * This item is based on the visor worn by Cyclops, a fictional Marvel superhero.
  * When it was first released, the Cyclopean Visor had neither its current punch effect or its mod. Two separate updates early in _Growtopia_ 's release in 2013 made the item works the way it currently does.[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Cyclopean_Visor#cite_note-1)
