The **Diamond Lock** is an unsplicable solid lock foreground block which was added as part of the Legendary update on August 22, 2014.
##  Function
As a type of world lock, a Diamond Lock has all the basic properties of a regular World Lock.
Diamond Locks are often used to store and trade World Locks in a compact fashion, as it can be shattered back into 100 World Locks when its inventory icon is double-tapped.
→ When compressing, it will say: "_You compressed 100 World Locks into a Diamond Lock!_ "
→ When shattering, it will say: "_You shattered a Diamond Lock into 100 World Locks!_ "
A Blue Gem Lock can be purchased from the Sales-Man for 100 Diamond Locks, which can be turned back into 100 Diamond Locks when the player double taps on its icon in their inventory.

##  Trivia
  * During the early times of Display Block's release, all kind of locks could be displayed in Display Blocks. However, this lead to arise of a new scamming method, where world owners made the world public and asked other players to place locks into the Display Blocks, so they could steal the locks afterwards. Hence, in order to prevent this type of scams, the DEV team decided to make locks undisplayable. However, some worlds with displayed Diamond Locks were left behind, which are very rare nowadays.
  * As of Anniversary Week 2026, Diamond Locks now recycle for 0 Gems, previously recycling for 20,000.
