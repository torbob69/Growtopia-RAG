The **Easter Spring** is an unsplicable solid trampoline foreground block which was added as part of Easter Week 2021 on April 1, 2021.
##  Function
Jumping on a trampoline block such as an Easter Spring will cause the player to bounce, and by holding the jump button, the player can jump even higher. The _High Jump_ mod also boosts one's jump height on a trampoline block.
The  Event Exclusive Mission - All Aboard For Easter Fun! mission requires 50 of this item.

##  Trivia
  * The word "spring" in the item's name contains a double meaning; the first refers to the season of the year during which dormant animals and plants spring back to life, while the second one refers to a flexible or coiled mechanical device that exerts force and attempts to spring back when bent, compressed, or stretched.[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Easter_Spring#cite_note-1)
    * The block's design itself is based on a coil spring.
