The **Super Pineapple** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2017 on June 8, 2017.
##  Function
During the Super Pineapple Party, consuming a Super Pineapple has the equivalent of consuming 200 Pineapples. When used to feed a player with a Pineapple Body, the player will receive the Super Pineapple Magic mod. This will give them two prizes instead of one. The fed player will instantaneously explode, forcing them start over to gain a Pineapple Body again.
The  Super Pineapple Party Exclusive Mission - Pucker up for more Pineapple fun! mission requires 200 of this item.
