# Star Trek Starter Pack
During Star Trek Prodigy

|
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Star Trek Starter Pack** for **14.99 USD** in the Growtopia Store.
"Everything you need to get your Star Trek journey started!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.

The **Star Trek Starter Pack** is a Store-bought IAP which was added as part of the Star Trek Prodigy update.
The pack includes:
  * Starfleet Officer's Supply Box ×40
  * Starfleet Cadet's Supply Box ×50
  * Startopia Supply Crate ×10
  * Protostar Starship Blast ×1
  * Murf Hood ×1
  * Murf Onesie ×1

##  Trivia
  * On July 5, 2022, the amount of Starfleet Officer's Supply Boxes obtainable from one pack got increased from 20 to 40.
