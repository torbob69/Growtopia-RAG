The **Disabled Platform** is an unobtainable non-solid foreground block which was added on Halloween Week 2013 on October 28, 2013.
##  Function
The item serves as a disabled state for the Creepstone Platform and can only be seen in GROWGANOTH (GROWGANOTH1–GROWGANOTH150) during Halloween Week or in CARNIVAL (CARNIVAL1–CARNIVAL50) at the Growganoth Gulch game.
