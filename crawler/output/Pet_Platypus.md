The **Pet Platypus** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2023 Day 3 on August 30, 2023.
##  Function
When equipped, the Pet Platypus serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The Pet Platypus' description and design is a reference to Perry the Platypus, a fictional bipedal platypus from the American animated series Phineas and Ferb.
