The **Dragon Hand** is an unsplicable pet hand item which was added as part of the Rise of the Desert Dragons update on August 3, 2013.
##  Function
You command a mighty (small) dragon!

Your dragon has abandoned you.

When equipped, the **Dragon Hand** grants the _Dragonborn_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and knock back other players further. Additionally, it summons a red dragon that follows the player around, which also replaces the player's punch animation with bursts of flames.
Step 7 of the  Quest For Fire requires the player to deliver 10 of this item.
