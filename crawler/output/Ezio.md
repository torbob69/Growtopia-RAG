**Ezio** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of UbiWeek 2022 Day 2 on January 25, 2022.
##  Function
Jump to adventure!

Laying Low.

When equipped, the **Ezio** grants the _Double Jump & Punch Range: Ezio!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air, punch one tile further, and switch between its wearable form and its morph form by wrenching oneself and clicking 'Transform'.
##  Trivia
  * Ezio is based on the main character in the action adventure game _Assassin's Creed II_ , which was developed by Ubisoft Montreal.
  * During UbiWeek 2022, Ezio could be obtained from completing WOLFWORLDs. As of UbiWeek 2023, Ezio is only obtainable from UbiCrates.
