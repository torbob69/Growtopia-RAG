This potion isn't FDA approved for use on humans yet, maybe a later version will be. But spray a rock with it to upgrade it into a WAY better rock.

The **Love Potion #8** is a consumable that was added as part of Valentine's Week 2016 on February 12, 2016.
##  Function
When consumed on a Rock, it will convert them into Heartstones.
##  Trivia
  * The name **Love Potion #8** is a reference to the Love Potion #9 mentioned in the 2013 animated film Madly Madagascar.[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Love_Potion_8#cite_note-1)
