The **Frankenstein's Monster Mask** is an unsplicable face item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2020 on October 22, 2020.
##  Function
Me going to scare you!

Back to being less scary!

When equipped, the **Frankenstein's Monster Mask** grants the _Farming Role Requirement Reduction : Frankenstein's Monster Mask_ mod, which allows the player to reduce the amount of items required to complete all Farmer Role quests by 10%..
