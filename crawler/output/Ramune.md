**Ramune** is an unsplicable consumable that was added as part of The Grand Tournament update on December 1, 2016.
##  Function
When consumed, a special eating animation will be triggered, featuring the player slowly raising their arms and burping.
Consuming a Tournament snack such as this item may give the player a random block typically earned from the lower tiers of the Prize Ladder.

##  Trivia
  * In real life, _ramune_ is a carbonated soft drink that was originally sold in Japan, the country where _Growtopia_ 's ex-developer Seth lives. Its name is believed to be derived from the English word "lemonade".[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Ramune#cite_note-1)
  * Consuming Pow Stones with Ramune at the same time would cause the player to respawn. This is a reference to the Pow Stone's description, which tells the player that combining carbonated candy with soda would be dangerous.
