The **Obsidian** is an unsplicable solid slippery foreground block which was added as part of the Hidden Riches update on September 1, 2017.
##  Function
Being a slippery block, walking on an Obsidian causes the player to slide.
Step 1 of the  Quest For The Sky requires the player to deliver 2,000 of this item.
