The **Rainbow Shoes** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2021 Day 2 on August 24, 2021.
##  Function
Taste my Rainbows!

Wait, where's the pot of gold?

When equipped, the **Rainbow Shoes** grants the _Speedy: Rainbow Shoes_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land. Moreover, the player leaves a trail of rainbows when walking.
