# Ring of Wisdom

This ring makes you feel a whole lot smarter. So smart, in fact, that you can tell that it gives you a 10% chance of getting double XP!
Properties
This item cannot be dropped or traded.
This item never drops any seeds.

This item is obtained as a random prize!
The **Ring Of Wisdom** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2014 update on October 31, 2014.
##  Function
When equipped, the **Ring of Wisdom** grants the _XP Buff_ mod, which allows the player to gain a 10% chance of getting double XP.
