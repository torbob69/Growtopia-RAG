The **Monkey Mask** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Lunar New Year 2022 on February 1, 2022.
##  Function
When equipped, the Monkey Mask serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Monkey Warrior set, which consists of the following items:

    * Monkey Mask (hat)

    * Monkey Warrior's Circlet (hat)

    * Monkey Warrior's Shirt (shirt)

    * Monkey Warrior's Pants (pants)

    * Monkey Warrior's Staff (hand)
  * As of Lunar New Year 2025, this item's sprite was changed to distinguish between the Monkey Face Mask (face item) and the Monkey Mask (hat item).
