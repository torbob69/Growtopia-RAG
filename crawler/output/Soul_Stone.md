The **Soul Stone** is an unsplicable component which was first introduced during Ancestral Power update. It can be obtained by completing the Biweekly Soul Quest.
##  Function
The Soul Stone is used to upgrade Ancestral Artifacts as well as upgrade the Gauntlet of Elements.
Step 9 of the  Quest For Candour requires the player to deliver 5 of this item.
##  Trivia
  * Only one **Soul Stone** can be purchased every month from Locke The Salesman.
