The **Mid-Pacific Owl** is an unsplicable pet hand item which was added on April 11, 2014.
##  Function
When equipped, the Mid-Pacific Owl will summon an owl which follows the player around.

##  Trivia
  * Any actions such as walking, getting punched, dancing, etc will cause the owl to come off the player's head and fly around.
  * The Mid-Pacific Owl will fall asleep on the player's head when the player's stands still.
  * The Mid-Pacific Institute is a school in Hawaii that uses the Hawaiian short-eared owl as their mascot. The item was dedicated to the school for their use of Growtopia as a learning tool.
