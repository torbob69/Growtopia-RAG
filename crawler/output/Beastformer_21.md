The **Beastformer!** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as December 2023's Item Of The Season on December 1, 2023.
##  Function
Welcome to the jungle!

This aint fun and games!

When equipped, the **Beastformer!** grants the _Speedy + Double Jump: Beastformer!_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land, jump a second time in mid-air, and switch between its wearable form and its morph form by wrenching oneself and clicking 'Transform'.
