# Ultra Performance Enhancing Hot Cocoa

Enhances the performance of the 'Balloon-Frosting War Gloves' by increasing the points gained for every hit. Lasts for 15 minutes but doesn't stack with the bonus points granted by a Power Play.

##  Function
Increased Points with Gloves!

Get back to the competition!

When equipped, it grants the _Performance Enhanced!_ mod which doubles balloon points every splash during Balloon Warz. If it is the team's powerplay, the Ultra Performance Enhancing Hot Cocoa will not take effect.
