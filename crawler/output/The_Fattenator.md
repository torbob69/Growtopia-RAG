**The Fattenator** is an unsplicable hand item that was added as part of Blocktor's Orders! update.
##  Function
Because I'm fat!

You are the biggest loser!

When equipped, the **The Fattenator** grants the _Knock Back Reduction: Fattenator_ mod, which allows the player to resist a percentage of knockback when punched by other players and become fat.
