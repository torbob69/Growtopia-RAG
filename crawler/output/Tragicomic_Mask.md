The **Tragicomic Mask** is an unsplicable hat item that was added as part of Halloween Week 2018 on October 26, 2018.
##  Function
When equipped, the Tragicomic Mask serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
### Appearance
The appearance of the Tragicomic Mask changes in accordance to the direction the player is facing. It will have a smiling look when the player faces left, and a frowning look when the player faces right.
