##  Function
Just keep swimming, just keep swimming!

Just keep drowning, just keep drowning!

When equipped, the **Cloak of the Manta Ray** grants the _Speedy in Water + Double Jump : Cloak of the Manta Ray_ mod, which allows the player to move faster in water and jump a second time in mid-air.
