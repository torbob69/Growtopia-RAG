The **Sheet Music: Flat Sax** is a splicable sheet music background block which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2017 on May 5, 2017.
##  Function
Sheet Music: Flat Sax is a sheet music that plays a flat sax note, depending on its position vertically. Its note is half-step lower than that of Sheet Music: Sax Note's.
