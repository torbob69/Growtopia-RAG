# Cactus Hat

Hollow out a cactus and wear it on your head. This is the go to choice of headgear for when you want to wear a hollowed out cactus on your head.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be transmuted.

The **Cactus Hat** is an unsplicable hair item which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2021 on May 5, 2021.
##  Function
When equipped, the Cactus Hat serves a fully cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Cactus set, which consists of the following items:

    * Cactus Hat (Hair)

    * Cactus Onesie (Shirt)

    * Cactus Cannon (Hand)
