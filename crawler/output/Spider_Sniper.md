The **Spider Sniper** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2021 on 21 October, 2021.
##  Function
Got you in my sights!

I'm all tied up!

When equipped, the **Spider Sniper** grants the _Punch Range: Spider Sniper_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further.
