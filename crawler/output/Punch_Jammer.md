Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Punch Jammer** is an unsplicable solid foreground jammer block which was added as part of the Growtopia General Hospital update on November 22, 2013.
##  Function
When toggled on (via punching), a Punch Jammer will change its appearance and prevent players from punching each other in the world it is placed in.
