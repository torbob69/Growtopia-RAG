Dungeons

It can be found naturally generated in Dungeon worlds.

Dungeons Reward

1 may be awarded to the player for completing a Dungeon at **Gold Tier or higher**.
The **Grace of Light** is an unsplicable non-solid checkpoint block which was added as part of the Dungeons update on December 9, 2024.
##  Function
Being a checkpoint block, the Grace of Light acts as where the player respawns after dying. It also serves as the starting point for a dungeon room.
