# Gangland Style
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Gangland Style** for **5,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"Step into the 1920's with a Fedora, a Dame's Fedora, a Pinstripe Suit, a Pinstripe Pants, a Flapper Headband and Flapper Dress, a Cigar, a Tommygun, a Victrola that plays Jazz Music, and 10 Art Deco Blocks. It's the whole package!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The Gangland Style pack is the first pack to come out in the game, with the Fedora sponsored by i20HH. Added into game at 13 March, 2013 on Valentine's Week/2013 Update. It is purchasable for 5,000 Gems.
The pack includes:
  * Fedora x1
  * Dame's Fedora x1
  * Pinstripe Suit x1
  * Pinstripe Pants x1
  * Flapper Headband x1
  * Flapper Dress x1
  * Cigar x1
  * Tommygun x1
  * Victrola x1
  * Art Deco Block x10
