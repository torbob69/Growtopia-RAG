The **Pastel Green Block** is a splicable solid foreground block which was added as part of Easter Week 2013 on March 30, 2013.
##  Function
When placed, the Pastel Green Block serves a purely aesthetic purpose.

##  Trivia
  * The Pastel Green Block is one of the 7 pastel color block variations in _Growtopia_. They are:
    * Pastel Aqua Block
    * Pastel Blue Block
    * Pastel Green Block
    * Pastel Orange Block
    * Pastel Pink Block
    * Pastel Purple Block
    * Pastel Yellow Block
    * Note that the basic  Black Block,  Brown Block,  Grey Block, and  White Block do not have a Pastel variant.
  * Prior to Easter Week 2021, players only had a chance to obtain 1 Pastel Green Block from EGGRESCUE at a time.
