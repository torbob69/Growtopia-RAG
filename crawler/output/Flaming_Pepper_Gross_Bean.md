Each explosion may give **10** items.
The **Flaming Pepper Gross Bean** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Gross-Out update on May 1, 2019.
##  Function
When consumed, a Flaming Pepper Gross Bean grants an unknown mod for 10 seconds, giving the player a dark red skin color. The player's head will also emit huge flame particles while the mod is still active.
