The **Barricaded Door** is a non-solid door foreground block which was added as part of the Night of the Sewing Dead update.
##  Function
As a door, the Barricaded Door can be entered (via punching) by a player, moving them instantly to another tile. A block with an ID must first be placed on the tile for this to work.
A Barricaded Door is different from normal doors in the sense that it can't be entered by zombies. When attempted, the player will receive a message saying "_Zombies can't get through this door!_ "
