Upgrading Your Hospital Level

Obtained as a prize from upgrading your Hospital world to levels 2, 3, 5, 7, 10, 13, 16, 21, 25, 31, 39, 48.
The **Auto Surgeon Station** is an unsplicable foreground block which was added as a part of Player Appreciation Week 2024 Day 1 on August 26, 2024.
##  Function
When Wrenched by the world owner, they can add up to 1,000 surgery tools, select a malady for the Auto Surgeon Station to cure, and a price for one cure (including a Growtopia world tax of 1 World Lock). Players can stand on top of the Auto Surgeon Station and wrench it, allowing them to spend World Locks to cure their malady. The player will not receive a prize and the recovery mod. As the hospital level increases, the number of options an Auto Surgeon Station increases as well.
Number of Auto Surgeon Stations to be placed in every hospital world is limited. This limitation decreases as the player levels the hospital up.
