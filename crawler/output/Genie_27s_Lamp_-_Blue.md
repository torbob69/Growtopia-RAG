The **Genie's Lamp - Blue** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as May 2019’s Item Of The Month alongside the Genie's Lamp - Green, Genie's Lamp - Red and Genie's Lamp - Yellow on May 1, 2019.
##  Function
Wish granted!

Chillin' in the lamp!

When equipped, the **Genie's Lamp - Blue** grants the _Speedy: Genie's Lamp_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and change their skin color to blue.
