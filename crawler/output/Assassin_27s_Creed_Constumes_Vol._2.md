# Assassin's Creed Constumes Vol. 2
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Assassin's Creed Costumes** for **50,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"All you need to dress as Basim, Connor and Kassandra!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Assassin's Creed Costumes Vol. 2** is an item pack which was added as part of Assassin's Creed Weekend on September 28, 2023.
##  Items
The pack contains the following items:
  * Basim's Hood
  * Basim's Beard
  * Basim's Vest
  * Basim's Trousers
  * Basim's Boots
  * Connor's Hood
  * Connor's Vest
  * Connor's Trousers
  * Connor's Boots
  * Kassandra's Hair
  * Kassandra's Armor
  * Kassandra's Skirt
  * Kassandra's Sandals
