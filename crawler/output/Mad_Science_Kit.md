# Mad Science Kit
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Mad Science Kit** for **5,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"It's SCIENCE! Defy the natural order with a Science Station that produces chemicals, a Laboratory in which to mix them, and a Lab Coat, Combover, and Ze Goggles to do so safely! You'll also get a starter pack of assorted chemicals. Mix them up! Special bonus: a Rare Death Ray to make your science truly mad!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Mad Science Kit** is a store pack which was added on July 20, 2013 and costs 5,000 gems from the Store.
##  Items
The pack contains the following items:
  * Science Station ×1
  * Laboratory ×1
  * Lab Coat ×1
  * Combover or  Mad Scientist Hair ×1
  * Ze Goggles ×1
  * Death Ray ×1 (Rare)
  * Chemical R ×5
  * Chemical G ×10
  * Chemical Y ×5
  * Chemical B ×5
  * Chemical P ×5

##  Trivia
  * The Mysterious Chemical, Slime, Steel Table, Shockinator, Shocking Hair, Red Bones, Enhanced Intellect, Dragon Tail, and the Reanimator Remote were also implemented into the game alongside this pack.
