The **Unicorn** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Voucher Dayz on December 31, 2021.
##  Function
When equipped, the Unicorn serves as a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The Unicorn is one out of five items that were previously only available to the discontinued console edition of Growtopia, along with the Energy Drink, Glowing Shirt, Plunger, and The Brother Hood.
    * All of them were obtained from Ubisoft Club Rewards and are now being released to the mobile/PC version of Growtopia through the Console Classix Pack or its sequel.
  * The description of this item seemingly alludes to the Microsoft ninjacat.
