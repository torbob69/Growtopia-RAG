# Blocks/Pain Blocks (Spike)

< Blocks
**Pain Blocks (Spike)** are blocks which knocks the player back and stings the player until the player stops all motion. A player will die after making contact with a pain block five times in quick succession (recovering from damage takes roughly 5 seconds). The _Fireproof_ mod will not allow the player to take more hits, unlike with lava pain blocks.
