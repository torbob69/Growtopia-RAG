##  Function
The Dungeons Shopkeeper is an NPC found after a player completes a room in a Dungeon.
Players can interact with the shopkeeper by punching them, which opens a window displaying purchasable abilities in exchange for Souls. Players can buy up to three abilities after each room is cleared, enhancing their performance for the challenges ahead. The different abilities, along with their prices, are as follows:
### Single-Variant Abilities
Ability — Price (Souls) — FunctionSlash — Given to all players at the start of the dungeon. — Grants the ability to preform a melee slash that deals damage to nearby enemies.
Fireball — ×200 — Grants the ability to shoot a fireball that deals a small amount of damage to enemies from a distance.
Vampirism — ×200 — Converts 2% of damage dealt to enemies into health.
Explosive Attack — ×500 — Grants a 20% chance that attacks will explode, dealing damage over a large area.
Healing Aura — ×500 — Summons a healing aura every 10 seconds that heals a small amount of health to the player and nearby players.
Protecting Shield — ×500 — Summons a shield around the player every 20 seconds that blocks a single attack.
Extra Life — ×500 — Grants the player an extra life.
