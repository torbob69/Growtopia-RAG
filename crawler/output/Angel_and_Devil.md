The **Angel and Devil** is an unsplicable ancestral item which was added as part of Grow4Good Week 2024 on April 26, 2024.
##  Function
Blessed!

Corrupted!

When equipped, the **Angel and Devil** grants the _Extra Gems or Blocks : Angel and Devil_ mod, which allows the player to get a chance to receive extra gems when breaking blocks and get a chance for a block to drop an extra of itself when broken. Additionally, the player also stops smiling, raises their eyebrows, and constantly glances at the angel and devil, one after the other.
