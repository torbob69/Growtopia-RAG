The **Ancient Stone Gate** is a splicable entrance foreground block which was added as part of the Rise of the Desert Dragons update on August 3, 2013.
##  Function
Being an entrance, an Ancient Stone Gate allows players with access to walk through it, or, when its property is changed, allowing any player to walk through it. When placed in a public area, it is impossible to be closed for anyone.
