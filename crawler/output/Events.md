# Events

Events are temporary occurrences that can happen server-wide or in singular worlds. Some events can occur randomly during gameplay, while others are triggered directly by the developers. While active, an event may offer exclusive items unobtainable elsewhere. Most, if not all events last until a certain time limit is reached.
##  Annual Events
Annual events occur once every year and are generally held to celebrate holidays, such as the Harvest Festival, which is based on the Mid-Autumn Festival. Annual events run for a week, except for WinterFest, which lasts for two.

##  Daily Challenges
_See main article:Daily Challenges_
A randomly-picked Daily Challenge comes every 23 hours and lasts until the 2-hour limit is reached. During this time, players or can participate in a competition where the top five winners receive prizes.
As of the Challengers Approach! update, guilds can also participate in the Guild Daily Challenge, which has the same task as the Daily Challenges.
Event — Duration — FrequencyDaily Challenge (random) — 2 hours — Every 23 hours

##  Other Events
###  Apology Weekend
The Apology Weekend is set up as compensation for a rollback. Eight Apology Weekends have happened so far; the last one happening during the day of January 3, 2021.
In addition, an Apology Week has been held once, on July 14, 2020, days after Rollback VII. Rollback Plaque VII was made available during the week.
###  Euphoria
During Anniversary Week, Euphoria begins when 5,500 points worth of consumables related to the former (excluding Skyrocket) have been accumulated in a world. All players with at least 500 points of contribution will receive a Golden Party-In-A-Box.
###  Discount Day
The Discount Day was an event that lasted for 24 hours. The event happened on Player Appreciation Week 2022 Day 6. During the event most items in the Growtopia Store would be 20% off the regular price and items in the Growtoken Store would be 5% off. The prices of the World Lock and the World Lock Pack were not changed for the day.
In addition, during the event there would be a chance of double gems dropping from breaking blocks.
##  Moderator-hosted Events
####  Kentucky Derby
@Aimster holds a racing event at KYDERBY each year, a reference to the Kentucky Derby. It is usually hosted by moderators and some non-moderators who run the event for 24 hours. A rare item, the Mint Julep is given out as a prize to the winner of the racing event. The owner of the best race track design is also rewarded a prize. @Aimster chooses the top nine worlds from the forums.
###  Fire Festival
STYX (now a former Moderator) used to hold a Fire Festival, which is a reference to the Philippines fire festival. It was held in the world FIRE, which he later sold as he was no longer a moderator, therefore discontinuing the event. Golden Apples were rewarded to players who won the event. Since the event has been discontinued, Golden Apples can now only be obtained from breaking Golden Treasure Hoards.

##  Removed Events
###  Super-Block Event
The Super-Block Event was the first guild event added to the game, and was meant to be the first in a series of Grow Events. The only instance of this event was held during April 13–16, 2018.
Since the event's mechanisms were considered very unsatisfactory, the developers decided to scrap the Super-Block Event and any plans for subsequent Grow Events. Seasonal Clashes were later introduced as a replacement for the original Grow Events.
###  Brr-lloon Warz
Balloon Warz was an annual event occuring once per year, starting from 2017 till 2022. Due to unknown reasons, it was discontinued and there has been no follow up since then.
