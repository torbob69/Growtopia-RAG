# November 14, 2014 Update

Growtopia Gazette
> Nov 14th: NEW STUFF! Today we've added some new items but we aren't saying what. Find 'em yourself! CUSTOM ITEM SPONSORS: Avenie, Fredi & Fynx & Ludo
