The **Parade Leader's Baton** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2026 on August 24, 2026.
##  Function
Take a look at me, 'cause I could not care at all!

Disappointed faces of your peers.

When equipped, the **Parade Leader's Baton** grants the _Punch Damage + Punch Range: Parade Leader's Baton_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and punch one tile further.
##  Trivia
