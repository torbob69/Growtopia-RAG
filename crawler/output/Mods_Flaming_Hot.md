The **Flaming Hot** mod allows the user to shoot flames of colors depending on that of the sprite.
Items that grant the Flaming Hot mod include:
  * Playful Wood Sprite
  * Playful Fire Sprite
  * Playful Water Sprite
  * Playful Wind Sprite
