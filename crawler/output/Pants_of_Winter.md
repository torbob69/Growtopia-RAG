The **Pants of Winter** is an unsplicable pants item that was added as part of Equinox Weekend on March 25, 2022.
##  Function
When equipped, the Pants of Winter serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Equinox Winter set, which consists of the following items:

    * Crown of Winter (hat)

    * Shirt of Winter (shirt)

    * Pants of Winter (pants)

    * Boots of Winter (shoes)
