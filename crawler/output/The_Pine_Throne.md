**The Pine Throne** is an unsplicable non-solid seat foreground block which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party Week 2021 on June 3, 2021.
##  Function
When a player stands in front of a seat such as The Pine Throne for a few seconds, the player will automatically be seen "sitting" on it.
##  Trivia
  * The description of this item references a quote by the fictional character Cersei Lannister from the novel _A Game of Thrones_, which says, "When you play the game of thrones, you win or you die. There is no middle ground."
