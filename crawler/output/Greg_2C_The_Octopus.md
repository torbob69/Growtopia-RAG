# Greg, The Octopus

A friendly octopus buddy who clings to you wherever you go! With 8 legs and a bulbous head he's got an inky surprise for anyone who gets in your way!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.

Type: Back – Clothes

**1h**
Super Fireworks Reward

May be obtained from firing off  Super Fireworks with **200** Fireworks in your inventory.
The **Greg, The Octopus** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of SummerFest 2020 on July 3, 2020.
##  Function
8 Arms up, up and away!

All suckered out!

When equipped, the **Greg, The Octopus** grants the _Double Jump: Greg The Octopus!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
The  The Seven Seas : A Tale of Tentacles! mission requires 1 of this item.
