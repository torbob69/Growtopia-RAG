The **Bubble Wrap** is a splicable non-solid SFX background block which has been added since Growtopia's launch.
##  Function
As an SFX background block, a Bubble Wrap will play a "popped" sound effect and undergo a slight visual change when a player passes through. The block will be reset back to normal when 10 seconds have passed, or all players have left the world.
