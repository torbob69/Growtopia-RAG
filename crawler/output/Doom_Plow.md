# Doom Plow

Are your crops not sufficiently terrified? Well, buckle up, because the power of Growganoth has overcharged the measely chicken plow into this fire-belching, flame-spewing monstrosity. Crazed chicken included. Do not operate while on prescription medication. Or for any other reason.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.

Bridle Design **(×1)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Doom Plow** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2017.
##  Function
When equipped with a stack of seeds selected in one's inventory, it will automatically plant all the seeds, provided that the player has access to the world.
##  Trivia
  * The Doom Plow is the second plow to be added in Growtopia, with the first one being the Chicken Plow, which is used in the former's recipe.
  * Doom Plow is a spookier redesign of  Chicken Plow. Both of them serve the same function.
