The **Rose Cape** is an unsplicable back item which was added added as a part of Valentine's Week 2025 on February 13, 2025.
##  Function
Ring-a-ring o' roses!

We all fall down!

When equipped, the **Rose Cape** grants the _Double Jump + Speedy: Rose Cape_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and move faster on land.
