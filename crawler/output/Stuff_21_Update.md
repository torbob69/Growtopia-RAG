# Stuff! Update

Growtopia Gazette
> October 1st: October Update!
> Hello Growtopians,
> This month we are happy to release a bunch of cool new stuff!
> - IOTM: Shadow Spirit of the Underworld! This demonic creature will follow you wherever you go!
> - New Achievements for Startopia, Boo, Roles, and more!
> - A new Carnival game and prizes!
> - New rewards for Roles and other activities!
> - Guild Name Changer!
> And many more!
> - The Growtopia Team
The **Stuff! Update** is an update that was released on October 1, 2018. The update introduced 46 new Achievements, a new Carnival game, new Roles rewards and much more
##  New Items
Item: RecipeShadow Spirit of the Underworld: October 2018's Item Of The Month, 200,000 Gems from the Growtopia Store.
Guild Name Changer: Can be bought for 100,000 Gems in the Growtopia Store.
Cat Eyes: Can be bought for 100 Growtokens from the Growtopia Store.
Fallen Pillar: Can be received as a bonus reward from harvesting a tree upon reaching Level 4 Farmer.
Stone Gargoyle Wings: Can be crafted upon reaching Level 4 Builder with 20 Fallen Pillar, 5 Aquamarine Stone, & 5 Tiled Hospital Wall.
Tiled Hospital Wall: Can be received as a bonus reward from performing a Surgery after reaching Surgeon Level 4.
Plasma Hair: Can be crafted after reaching Level 7 Chef.
HyperTech Multi-Jet Energy Pulser: Can be received as a bonus reward from performing a Star Voyage after reaching Star Captain Level 6.
Oil Slick: Can be received as a bonus reward from Fishing after reaching Fishing Level 6.
Red House Entrance: Can be randomly received from smashing blocks.
Blue Gem Lock: Can be purchased from Sales-Man for 100 Diamond Locks.
Mountain Dire Wolf: Can be obtained from completing a Wolfworld.
Carnival Ladders: Can be obtained from the Spiky Survivor Carnival Game.
Dragon Block: Can be obtained from the Spiky Survivor Carnival Game.
Ethereal Rainbow Dragon: Can be obtained from the Spiky Survivor Carnival Game.
Carnival Creep Platform: Unobtainable. As same as Ringmaster. Used in Carnival.
Disabled Carnival Creep Platform: Unobtainable. As same as Ringmaster. Used in Carnival.
Hour Glass: Can be randomly received from smashing blocks.
Atomic Gate: Mix 20 Earth Essence, 5 Wind Essence, & 1 Crystal Gate in any Chemical Combiner.
Magic Carpet: Mix 200 Unearthly Chemical, 1 Onisim's Genie, & 50 Green Bunting in any Chemical Combiner.
Ancestral Orb of Time: Mix 1 Crystallized Time, 2 Ancient Lens, & 5 Ancient Pins in any Chemical Combiner.
Ancestral Orb of Time : Level 2: Upgrade the Ancestral Orb of Time.
Ancestral Orb of Time : Level 3: Upgrade the Ancestral Orb of Time : Level 2.
Ancestral Orb of Time : Level 4: Upgrade the Ancestral Orb of Time : Level 3.
Ancestral Orb of Time : Level 5: Upgrade the Ancestral Orb of Time : Level 4.
Wriggling Tentacle: Can be received as a bonus reward from performing a Star Voyage.
Tamed Looming Triffid: Forge 50 Wriggling Tentacle.
Super Logo - Fist: Defeat a Villain.
Heroic Tights of Prevention: Defeat a Villain.
Crystallized Time: Combine 200 Hour Glass, 4 Atomic Gate, & 5 Earth Essence in any Chemical Combiner.
Hacker's Dialog: Unobtainable item, exact use is unknown.
