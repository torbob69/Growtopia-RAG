##  Function
Phenomenal Cosmic Power!

Teeny Tiny Living Space!

When equipped, the **Anomaly Aura** grants the _Extra Gems: Anomaly Aura_ mod, which allows the player to get a chance to receive extra gems when breaking blocks.
This aura features several special customization functions. Players can switch between Light Anomaly and Dark Anomaly by wrenching oneself. Additionally, setting the Time Dilation Cycle Time defines how often the aura will change between the two styles. The cycle time is in seconds, with the maximum number of seconds allowed being 86400 seconds (24 hours).
##  Trivia
  * The **Anomaly Aura** is based on the Hexcore at Anomaly phase, from League of Legends : Arcane TV Series.
  * When first added to the game, the Anomaly Aura was available in exchange for Series 8 Clash Keys instead of Series 7 Clash Keys, the glitch was fixed a few hours later.
