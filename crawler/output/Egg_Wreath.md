# Egg Wreath

Decorate with Easter flair! This collection of hand painted Easter eggs is perfect to hang around doorways, ceilings or wherever you like.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.
Data: Paint PreviewType — Foreground Block – Foreground — Aqua Blue Charcoal Green Purple Red Varnish Yellow
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **5 Hits** **4 Hits** – Restores after **2s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Paint Preview Aqua Blue Charcoal Green Purple Red Varnish Yellow
Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Easter Egg - Dark **(×1)** ,
Easter Egg - Growtopia **(×1)** and
Easter Egg - Ghost **(×1)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Egg Wreath** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Easter Week 2020 on April 9, 2020.
##  Function
When placed, the Egg Wreath serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
