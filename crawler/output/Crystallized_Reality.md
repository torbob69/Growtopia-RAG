# Crystallized Reality

A raw chunk of reality itself! The pure magic surrounding it could certainly be used for something spectacular... or set on a shelf to look pretty!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.

Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Ancient Plant Seed **(×100)** ,
Verdant Gate **(×4)** and
Earth Essence **(×5)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Crystallized Reality** is an unsplicable solid foreground block which was added as part of the Hidden Riches update.
##  Function
When placed, the Crystallized Reality serves a fully aesthetic purpose.
