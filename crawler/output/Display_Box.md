The **Display Box** is a splicable non-solid display box foreground block which was added alongside the Shop Pack on January 24, 2014.
##  Function
The Display Box is one of the two types of display boxes in _Growtopia_ (alongside the Transmatter Field). Items dropped inside one can only be picked up by players with access to the block. It may also be used to hold gems.
Step 4 of the  Quest For Honor requires the player to deliver 600 of this item.
