The **Easter Viking Cape** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Easter Week 2023 on April 7, 2023.
##  Function
Bringing the thunder!!

Out of charge!!

When equipped, the **Easter Viking Cape** grants the _Double Jump: Easter Viking Cape!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
