# KYDERBY/2022

< KYDERBY
**Kentucky Derby 2022** was an in-game racing event, based on an annual real-life horse race with the same name. Unlike the past Kentucky Derby events, which had been held former voluntary moderator Aimster, this incarnation of the event is hosted other members of the staff.
Aimster first announced that she was retiring from her position as a moderator on April 7, 2022.[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/KYDERBY/2022#cite_note-1) Speculation soon arose as to whether the event, which had been held on her own initiative since 2013, would be cancelled for good.
On April 11, 2022, Ubisoft Abu Dhabi's staff member @Hufflewitz broke down the silence, assuring players that the Derby would still be hosted members of the staff.[[2]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/KYDERBY/2022#cite_note-2) Voluntary moderator @Sabaei soon posted a race world submission thread on the Growtopia Forums,[[3]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/KYDERBY/2022#cite_note-build-3) officially announcing the beginning of Kentucky Derby 2022.
##  World Building Contest
A world-building contest is held throughout April as a preliminary to the actual racing event. The deadline for world submissions is set at 00:00 on Saturday, April 30. submitting their own race worlds through a forum thread,[[3]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/KYDERBY/2022#cite_note-build-3) players in the community will get a chance of having their worlds selected as the official race worlds during the Derby. 20 race worlds will be selected from the pool of entries. The players who have built each of these worlds will also be given a  Mint Julep.
A full list of the contest's rules can be viewed here.

##  Racing Contest
The world DERBYHUB was built in order to provide players with quick access to each available race world. Each year the race links are archived and the world is re-used next year. The worlds for KYDERBY 2022 can now be found in DERBYHUB2022.
