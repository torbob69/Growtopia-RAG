# Rainbow Crystal Block

Congratulations on winning the Crystal Crusher Daily Challenge! Enjoy this rainbowlicious block. Don't you just wanna lick it? Also, if you smash a Harmonic Crystal that is sitting on this block, there's a 20% chance of getting back one of the crystals you put into it.
Properties
This item can't be destroyed - smashing it will return it to your backpack if you have room!
This item never drops any seeds.

Daily Challenge

Place in the top three of the **Crystal Crusher** Daily Challenge.
The **Rainbow Crystal Block** is a solid foreground block which was added as part of the Daily Challenges update on November 18, 2015.
##  Function
Smashing a Harmonic Crystal placed on top of a Rainbow Crystal Block has a 20% chance of giving the player one of the crystals used.
