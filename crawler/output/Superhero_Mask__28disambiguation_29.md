# Superhero Mask (disambiguation)

This is a **disambiguation** page.
There are more than one subjects in the **Growtopia Wiki** using this name.

There are 6 **Superhero Masks** which consists of 6 different colours.
  * Superhero Mask - Blue
  * Superhero Mask - Green
  * Superhero Mask - Purple
  * Superhero Mask - Red
  * Superhero Mask - Gold
  * Superhero Mask - Black

There are also 4 **Superhero Masks** that are based on St. Patrick's Week, Easter Week, Harvest Festival and Super Pineapple Party.
  * Superhero Mask - Pink
  * Superhero Mask - Emerald
  * Superhero Mask - Autumn
  * Superhero Mask - Pineapple
