The **Death Ray** is an unsplicable hand item which was added alongside the Mad Science Kit on July 20, 2013.
##  Function
You can vaporize bricks more quickly.

Smash time is over.

When equipped, the **Death Ray** grants the _Death To Blocks!_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster.
##  Trivia
  * Contrary to popular belief, the Death Ray does not increase seed drop rate.
