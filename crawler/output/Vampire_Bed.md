The **Vampire Bed** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Halloween Week 2024 on October 24, 2024.
##  Function
Being a bed, executing the /sleep command in chat while standing on the same tile as a Vampire Bed will cause the player to sleep in it.
