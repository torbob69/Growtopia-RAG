The **Mystery Dress** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added alongside the Fashion Pack on May 15, 2015.
##  Function
When equipped, the Mystery Dress serves a purely cosmetic purpose. The color of the dress alternates between black and blue, and gold and white every few seconds.
##  Trivia
  * The Mystery Dress is based on a dress which became a viral phenomenon on the Internet in 2015. Due to differences in human color perception, viewers of the image disagreed on whether the dress was colored black and blue, or white and gold.
  * Obtaining a Mystery Dress from a Clothing Compactor for the first time will complete an Epic Quest.
