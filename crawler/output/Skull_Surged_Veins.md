The **Skull Surged Veins** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2022 on October 22. 2022.
##  Function
Powered up with skull!

Just dead on the inside now!

When equipped, the **Skull Surged Veins** grants the _Punch Damage: Skull Surged Veins_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster.
