The **Solar Surfer Hair** is an unsplicable hair item which was added as part of Wonder Week 2024 Day 1 on October 14, 2024.
##  Function
When equipped, the Solar Surfer Hair serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * Solar Surfer Hair's design and description are a reference to fictional character called Jim Hawkins from a Disney-made film Treasure Planet.
