The **Street Sidewalk** is an unsplicable solid foreground block which was added as part of the Aliens update on November 14, 2021.
##  Function
When placed, the Street Sidewalk serves a fully aesthetic purpose.
The  The Seven Seas : The Crust of the Crab! mission requires 10 of this item.
##  Trivia
  * The Street Sidewalk's description is a reference to Vanessa Carlton's 2001 song "A Thousand Miles".
  * The  Street Sidewalk's design is based on that of  Surgical Block.
