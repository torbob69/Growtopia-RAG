The **Awkward Friendly Unicorn** is an unsplicable non-solid provider foreground block which was added as part of the Unicorn Madness update on May 30, 2014.
##  Function
The Awkward Friendly Unicorn is a type of provider. When an Awkward Friendly Unicorn is harvested (by being punched), the following items will drop. One can be harvested approximately every week.
This list of possible drops is sorted from the most common to the rarest (if the rarity/chances of getting it is unknown it is put in the bottom of the list)
  * Clouds (1/~6)
  * Dreamstone Block (1/~6)
  * Rainbow Block (1/~6)
  * Rainbow Wig (1/~6)
  * Enchanted Spatula (1/~6)
  * Happy Unicorn Block (1/~15)
  * Angry Unicorn Block (1/~15)
  * Unicorn Jumper (1/~30)
  * Horse Mask (1/~30)
  * Scroll Bulletin (1/~60)
  * Very Bad Unicorn (1/~150)

##  Trivia
  * When punched, the Awkward Friendly Unicorn says "_Ow, stop!"_ , which was the voice line for A Burglar. Hamumu provided both items' sound.
  * Collecting 100 items from providers such as Cows will earn the achievement **Milkin' It (Classic)**.
  * Collecting 500 items from providers such as Cows will earn the achievement **Moo Juice (Classic)**.
  * Collecting 10,000 items from providers such as Cows will earn the achievement **Collector Extraordinaire (Classic)**.
  * Collecting 1,000,000 items from providers such as Cows will earn the achievement **Providers Will Provide**.
