# Pinata Lasso

Make the Piñata Bash more fun than ever by snaring the Ultra Piñata with this lasso! It'll seriously reduce the speed it travels for 15 seconds!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **3 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **4s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Pinata Bash

Participate in successfully smashing an  Ultra Pinata during the **Pinata Bash!** event.
May give **1** item each time.
Breaking

| May drop in sets of **1** from breaking:
•  Block De Mayo Block  |
The **Pinata Lasso** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2018 on May 4, 2018.
##  Function
When consumed on an Ultra Pinata, its speed is greatly reduced for 15 seconds.
##  Trivia
  * As of Cinco De Mayo Week 2020, the Pinata Lasso was removed from the Store and instead became a prize from smashing an Ultra Pinata or a Block De Mayo Block.
