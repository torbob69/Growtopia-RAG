##  Function
A merrily played tune!

Stop harping on!

When equipped, the **Harp of Aibell** grants the _Punch Range: Harp of Aibell_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further. Additionally, when punching, it will play a harp SFX and emit spontaneous bursts of golden note particles. The player will also show the mad emote excluding the red eyes.
##  Trivia
  * Harp of Aibell is a reference to Aibell, who is a fairy queen in Irish folklore.
