#  Title
The Game title is shown when starting up the game Growtopia.
  * This page will only document **title changes** throughout the game Growtopia.

## 2012 Title
Growtopia's title in 2012 was made by @Hamumu, ex-developer of Growtopia, in early development. The title is written in Century Gothic and has a tree behind the letter "t".

_The title is shown here representing the game's iconic startup screen._
**Year: 2012**
**Date of creation: November 12th, 2012 (Confirmed)**
**Presence: No longer existing**
##  2012/2013 Title
Growtopia's official title was released in 2013 made by @Hamumu, ex-developer of Growtopia. This title was a revamped version of the 2011 title. It is unknown how the title was written in and what was the title made with. The tree appears to replace the letter "t".

_The title is shown here representing the game._
**Year: 2012-2017**
**Date of creation: November 30th, 2012**
**Presence: No longer in use, this title still pops up in the startup screen, shown in old posts.**

## 2017 Title
Growtopia's official title oversimplified by Ubisoft after acquisition. Reason for being oversimplified is unknown, Ubisoft states the title needed to be sleek. The title is a redrawn, shaded version of the 2012 title. The title appears to be in 4K.

_The title is shown here representing the game. The title appears outside of Growtopia._
**Year: 2017-present**
**Date of creation: February 28th, 2017 (Confirmed)**
**Presence: Not shown in the startup screen, shown outside of Growtopia.**

##  Growtopia's Title Facts
The game's title is the representation for the game's name, Growtopia. @Seth confirms that Growtopia is a portmanteau of "Grow" and "Utopia".
The game's title is bouncy looking due to ex-developer @Seth and ex-developer @Hamumu wanting to originally make the game for ages 6+.
The tree in the center of game's title symbolizes "Grow" while "Utopia" symbolizes "Punch" and "Build".
The game's title is spelt "Grow opia" but the tree in the middle represents the letter "t", this is a reference to their first title, where the letter "t" was on top of a silhouette tree.
