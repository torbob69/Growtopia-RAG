The **Ultra Pinata** is an unsplicable event mystery block which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2013 on May 3, 2013.

##  Trivia
  * Previously, players would surround a world with Dirt in order to prevent the prizes being scattered all over the world. As of Cinco De Mayo Week 2017, the mechanics of the Ultra Pinata changed, giving players 90 seconds to chase a moving Ultra Pinata that can pass through blocks and smash it, making it more difficult.
