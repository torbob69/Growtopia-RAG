**Clouds** is a splicable solid foreground block which was added in version 1.18.1 on April 26, 2013.
##  Function
When placed, Clouds serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
Step 1 of the  Quest Of The Heavens requires the player to deliver 1,000 of this item.
