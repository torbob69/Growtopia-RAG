# Daily Bonuses

Players earn the right to reap the rewards of Daily Bonuses at certain milestones of Awesomeness, as they complete Life Goals for Crazy Jim. Each one grants bonus rewards upon completing various activities in Growtopia, and you can receive each bonus once per day. The first daily bonus becomes available at 5% awesomeness (ie after completing 5 life goals), the next is at 10%, and so on.
Daily Bonus — Minimum awesomeness & level — BonusGem Bonus — 5 — Earn _**[level*10]**_ Gems for _**[level]**_ bonus Gems (e.g. 550 gems for 55 bonus gems when level 55).
Fish Bonus — 10 — Double Fish for your first catch when fishing.
Surgery Bonus — 15 — Double prize for your first surgery.
Geiger Bonus — 20 — Double prize for your first Geiger Counter use.
Super Bonus — 25 — Double prize for your first villain defeated.
Token Bonus — 30 — Get 1,000xp with your first Growtoken.
Lobster Bonus — 35 — Double items in your first Lobster Trap.
Wolf Bonus — 40 — Double prize for your first Wolfworld.
Pet Bonus — 45 — Bonus Pet Trainer Medal for your first Pet Battle.
Synth Bonus — 50 — Double prize for your first Chemsynth.
Cooking Bonus — 55 — Get a Mac n' Cheese Star for your first edible meal cooked.
Life Bonus — 60 — Get a Growtoken for finishing all 3 Life Goals.
Mega Bonus — 65 — Get 2 Growtokens for earning all other Daily Bonuses!
##  Trivia
  * During Anniversary Week, completing a Daily Bonus will grant the player an Anniversary Block.
