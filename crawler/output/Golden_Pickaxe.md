The **Golden Pickaxe** is an untradable hand item that was awarded to former moderator diichaan.
##  Function
You can smash bricks more quickly.

Smash time is over.

When equipped, the **Golden Pickaxe** grants the _Enhanced Digging_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster.
##  Trivia
  * The item was originally designed for former moderator _diichaan_. Approximately two months after his demotion, the item was made available to the public through the Growtoken Store on 30 March, 2014.
  * Purchasing the Golden Pickaxe from the Growtoken store will complete the _Buy a Golden Pickaxe with Growtokens_ Epic Quest.
