The **Deathtrap Wall** is an unsplicable toggleable deadly foreground block which was added as part of Summer Clash 2018 on June 1, 2018.
##  Function
Being a toggleable deadly foreground block, a Deathtrap Wall manually toggles itself every 3 seconds, periodically transforming itself from a non-deadly block into a deadly one. When it enters its deadly phase, the Deathtrap Wall acts as a solid block and kills players upon contact, similar to the Prickly Pineapple.
