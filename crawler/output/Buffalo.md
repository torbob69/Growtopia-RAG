The **Buffalo** is an unsplicable non-solid provider foreground block which was added alongside the Wild West Pack on September 11, 2013.
##  Function
The Buffalo is a type of provider. When a Buffalo is harvested (by being punched), 1-2 Milk will drop. One can be harvested approximately every 24 hours.
