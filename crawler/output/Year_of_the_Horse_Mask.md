# Year of the Horse Mask

The horse is the seventh of the zodiac animals. Limited only to the Year of the Horse! Myth has it, that the order of the Zodiac Animals, was determined by a race. The horse, who placed seventh was, rushing through the race so quickly that it failed to notice the snake curled around its hoof!

##  Function
Oooh it sparkles!

When equipped, the Year of the Horse Mask grants the _Year of the Horse Mask: Sparkles_ mod, which emits sparkles from the player's body.
