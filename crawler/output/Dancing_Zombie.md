The **Dancing Zombie** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block which was added as part of Halloween Week 2022 on October 22, 2022.
##  Function
<https://growtopiawiki.com/w/File:The_Dancing_Zombie%27s_music..ogg>The Dancing Zombie's music
Being a toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block, a Dancing Zombie can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Dancing Zombie will change its appearance, showing a 11-framed animation, and play a piece of music.
##  Trivia
  * Dancing Zombie's appearance is a reference to Michael Jackson's outfit in the music video for his song Thriller.
  * In the game files, the Dancing Zombie's music is named thriller.
