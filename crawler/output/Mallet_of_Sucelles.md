The **Mallet of Sucelles** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2022 on March 11, 2022.
##  Function
Mallet Time!

Stop Mallet Time!

When equipped, the **Mallet of Sucelles** grants the _Punch Damage: Mallet of Sucelles_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. Additionally, the player throws the mallet instead of using the player's Fist when punching.
##  Trivia
  * This item is a reference to Sucellus who is a god in ancient Celtic mythology that is associated with agriculture, forests, and alcoholic beverages. Often depicted carrying a large hammer or mallet, he symbolizes prosperity and protection.
  * The item's description is a play on Mjolnir's weapon enchantment from Marvel Comics.
