**Seil's Magic Orbs** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of SummerFest 2013 on June 21, 2013.
##  Function
You can jump in mid-air! It's like flying, but less.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Seil's Magic Orbs** grants the _Double Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
##  Trivia
  * This item is very similar to the back item  Orbs Of Elixir, which is obtained by completing the Shooting Gallery game in the world CARNIVAL.
