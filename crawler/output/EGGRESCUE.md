# EGGRESCUE

**EGGRESCUE** is a world open during Easter Week only. It was first introduced during Easter Week 2018. Players can join the game by consuming an Egg Rescue Ticket.
In the world, 10-60 people will join via a Game Generator and will have 180 seconds to find as many Game Eggs as possible and bring them to a Game Goal Egg Basket, located around the world. The game will end after 180 seconds, or if a total of 100 eggs have been collected.
The world is owned by @Ubidev and like The Carnival, there are many worlds (EGGRESCUE_2, EGGRESCUE_3, etc). At the end of the game, all players will receive a prize.
