# Heroic Tights (disambiguation)

This is a **disambiguation** page.
There are more than one subjects in the **Growtopia Wiki** using this name.
**Heroic Tights** can be obtained randomly by defeating a villain
There are 6 **Heroic Tights** which consists of 6 different colours.
  * Heroic Tights - Red
  * Heroic Tights - Blue
  * Heroic Tights - Green
  * Heroic Tights - Purple
  * Heroic Tights - Black
  * Heroic Tights - Gold

There are also 4 **Heroic Tights** that are based on St. Patrick's Week, Easter Week, Harvest Festival and Super Pineapple Party.
  * Heroic Tights - Emerald
  * Heroic Tights - Pink
  * Heroic Tights - Pineapple
  * Heroic Tights - Autumn

There are 1 additional **Heroic Tights** which aren't based on anything.
  * Heroic Tights of Prevention
