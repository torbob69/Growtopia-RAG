The **Towering Hair** is an unsplicable platform foreground block which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2022 on February 11, 2022.
##  Function
When placed, the Towering Hair behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath.
##  Trivia
  * The Towering Hair is based on a tower which played a central role in the German fairy tale _Rapunzel_, specifically the version found in the 2010 animated feature film _Tangled_, which was produced by Walt Disney Animation Studios.
