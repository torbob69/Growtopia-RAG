The **Shadow Spirit of the Underworld** is an unsplicable chest item which was released as October 2018's Item Of The Month.
##  Function
Spirit summoned!

The spirit is banished!

When equipped, the **Shadow Spirit of the Underworld** grants the _Build Range: Shadow Force_ mod, which allows the player to build one tile further. When walking, the Shadow Spirit detaches from the player's head and follows the player around. When punching, it replaces the player's fist to black claws with a red outline being. When respawning, multiple black flames shoot out from the player.
