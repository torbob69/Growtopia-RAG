The **Immortal Da Vinci Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of The Immortal Collection Day 3 on June 27, 2026.
##  Function
You can jump in mid-air! It's like flying, but less.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Immortal Da Vinci Wings** grants the _Double Jump: Immortal Da Vinci Wings_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
