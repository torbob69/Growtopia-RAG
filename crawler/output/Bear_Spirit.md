The **Bear Spirit** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of the Guilds update on April 1, 2018.
##  Function
You are one with your spirit animal!

Back to the realm of the living!

When equipped, the **Bear Spirit** grants the _Spirit Form_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
