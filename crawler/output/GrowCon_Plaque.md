# GrowCon Plaque

If you close your eyes near this block, you can almost feel the warmth of camaraderie and good cheer from many fellow con-goers! A beautiful plaque for a beautiful person, this was specially made for those who attended a live GrowCon event!

The **GrowCon Plaque** is an untradeable permanent unsplicable foreground block.
##  Obtaining
  * The GrowCon Plaque can only be obtained from attending a live GrowCon event.
  * 10 GrowCon Plaques were given away through an official giveaway on the official Growtopia Youtube Channel.

##  Trivia
  * Growcon PH is the first official Growtopia Community Gathering event. It was held in the Philippines at Max’s QC Circle on April 21, 2019. 32 active GT players from different communities attended the event.
  * Another GrowCon was held between the 24th and 26th October, 2019 at Abu Dhabi National Exhibition Centre in Abu Dhabi.
