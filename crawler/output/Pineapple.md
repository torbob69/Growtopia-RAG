The **Pineapple** is a splicable consumable which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2015 on May 30, 2015. This item can only be effectively used during the week-long event.
To grow one, seeds that don't normally splice into anything need to be spliced during the event. However, the sum of the seeds rarity must add up to a rarity between 4 to 60, in order to work. There is also a chance that the tree will turn into a Super Pineapple Tree (41-50 rarity) or Dangerous Pineapple Tree (51-60 rarity), with the higher the rarity, the higher the chance.
If a Pineapple Tree is grown on top of a Fertile Soil Block, it will grow roots; when the roots gets harvested, it has a 10% chance of dropping a single Pineapple Root Cutting. This also applies to Super Pineapple and Dangerous Pineapple Trees, which may drop their respective root cuttings.
##  Function
You sure like pineapples!

### Pineapple Magic
During the Super Pineapple Party, players can consume and feed each other Pineapples to achieve certain stages. If a player feeds a Pineapple to another person with a Pineapple Body while not having one themself, they will receive the basic type of _Pineapple Magic_ mod.
Having _Pineapple Magic_ will cause a pineapple to revolve around the player for twenty minutes. Once the twenty minutes are over, the player will receive a random Super Pineapple Party prize; the higher the level of _Pineapple Magic_ , the better the prize.
#### Full of Pineapples
That's a lot of pineapples! [Pineapple Luck Increased]

At 200 Pineapples consumed, the player's country flag will turn into a Pineapple, and they will receive the _Full of Pineapples_ mod. If a player feeds a Pineapple to another person with a Pineapple Body while having this mod themself, they will receive the _Double Pineapple Magic_ mod, which is more likely to grant rarer rewards.
#### Turning into a Pineapple
The pineapples are beginning to affect your body in strange ways! [Pineapple Luck Increased]

At 400 Pineapples consumed, the player's skin color and clothes turn yellow, and they will receive the _Turning into a Pineapple_ mod. If a player feeds a Pineapple to another person with a Pineapple Body while having this mod themself, they will receive the _Triple Pineapple Magic_ mod, which is more likely to grant even rarer rewards.
#### Pineapple Form
You are what you eat! [Pineapple Luck at maximum!]

At 600 Pineapples consumed, the player will gain a Pineapple Body and receive the _Pineapple Form_ mod. If a player feeds a Pineapple to another person with a Pineapple Body while having this mod themself, they will receive the _Ultimate Pineapple Magic_ mod, which is more likely to grant even rarer rewards. They will also earn the Too Many Pineapples achievement if they have not obtained it before.
At this point, the player cannot feed themself any more Pineapples; only others can feed the player. The player will explode when they reach an amount of 620 Pineapples consumed and therefore losing their Pineapple Body, forcing the player to start over to reach the status again.
### Pineapple Guzzler
The Pineapple Guzzler can be fed with a Pineapple by a player in order for them to receive the _Pineapple Magic_ mod. Feeding it once will increase the Pineapple Guzzler's chance of explosion by 25%. However, feeding it with a Super Pineapple will cause it to instantly explode.
When consumed, the player will say "YUM!" and do the :D ⦅⦆ emote.
