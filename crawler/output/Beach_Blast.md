The **Beach Blast** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of SummerFest 2013 on June 21, 2013.
##  Function
Upon consumption, the blast will create a new world featuring a sunset on the beach background weather and hidden buried treasures beneath the sand. The Treasure Chests may contain SummerFest items. It requires 100 Fireworks for it to detonate.
