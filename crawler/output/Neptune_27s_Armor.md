The **Neptune's Armor** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as part of SummerFest 2023 on July 13, 2023.
##  Function
Waves of Glory!

Washed Away!

When equipped, the **Neptune's Armor** grants the _Speedy in Water + Punch Damage: Neptune's Armor_ mod, which allows the player to move faster in water and break blocks faster.
Step 16 of the  Quest For The Sky requires the player to deliver 1 of this item. 1 Golden Dragon Statue may also be used as an alternative.
