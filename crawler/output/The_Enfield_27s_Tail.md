##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the The Enfield set, which consists of the following items:

    * The Enfield (Hat)

    * The Enfield's Tail (Chest)
  * The Enfield is a reference to a mythical creature from Irish and Scottish heraldry. An Enfield features the head of a fox, the body of a dog or wolf, the talons of an eagle, and the hindquarters of a lion. Symbolizing cunning, strength, and protection, it was often used in family crests to represent noble guardianship.
