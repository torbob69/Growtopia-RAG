# Tesseract Manipulator

Tired of smashing blocks on your own? Perhaps it's time we meddled in the darker side of science and made our lives easier! Fuel this advanced machinery with enough Gems, tell it to target any block of your choosing, and it will break them AUTOMATICALLY! Note: Blocks broken by this device will not drop gems! It's convenient, but inefficient!

The **Tesseract Manipulator** is an unsplicable foreground autobreak block which was added as part of the Role Up! update on September 17, 2018.
##  Function
When wrenched, it lets the player add Gems to be able to break blocks automatically starting from the top-left up to the bottom-right of the world with a rate of 1 gem per 1.5 seconds.
##  Trivia
  * The Tesseract Manipulator will not be able to break targetted Background Blocks if it is behind a Solid Block.
