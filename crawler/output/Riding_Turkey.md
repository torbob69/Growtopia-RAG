The **Riding Turkey** is an unsplicable feet item that was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2018 on November 22, 2018.
##  Function
Just as fast as a chicken!

Back to normal feet!

When equipped, the **Riding Turkey** grants the _Speedy: Gobble-Gobble_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.
##  Trivia
  * When it was first added, the Riding Turkey had a different inventory icon, presumably based on an early design of the item.[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Riding_Turkey#cite_note-1) By December 2018, the sprite has been changed to the one currently used.
