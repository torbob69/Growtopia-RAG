# Doppelganger Potion

Toss this vile-smelling liquid on an impure soul, and the darkest deeds of their past will return to haunt them for a while!
Doppelganger Potion (Rarity: 75)
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.

Hardness: **4 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **5s** of inactivity

**4d 21h 48m 45s**
Rewards of Sacrifice

Sacrifice **any item with a rarity of 75 or above** to Growganoth.
The **Doppelganger Potion** is a consumable that was added as part of Halloween Week 2014 on October 30, 2014.
##  Function
Something is lurking in the shadows...

Everything's back to normal!

When consumed, the Doppelganger Potion grants the _Haunted_ mod which, similar to the effect of the Cape of Shadows creates shadows which mimics the player's position when they move and dissipates after a few seconds. The mod lasts for 5 minutes.
