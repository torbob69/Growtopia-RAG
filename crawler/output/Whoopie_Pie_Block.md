The **Whoopie Pie Block** is an unsplicable trampoline block which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2021 on September 16, 2021.
##  Function
Jumping on a trampoline block such as a Whoopie Pie Block will cause the player to bounce, and by holding the jump button, the player can jump even higher. The _High Jump_ mod also boosts one's jump height on a trampoline block.
