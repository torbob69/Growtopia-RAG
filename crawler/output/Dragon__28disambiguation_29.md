# Dragon (disambiguation)

This is a **disambiguation** page.
There are more than one subjects in the **Growtopia Wiki** using this name.
**Dragon** may refer to:
Items: Method(s) to ObtainDragon Hand: Purchasable from the Growtopia Store for 50,000 gems. Breathes fire.
Ice Dragon Hand: WinterFest holiday item, made by mixing 20 Snowballs, 1 Transmog Crystal and Dragon Hand in any Chemical Combiner during WinterFest. Breathes icy mist.
Green Dragon Mask: Exclusive moderator item for former moderator Greenswe. Spawns a large green dragon which breaths acid.
Fiesta Dragon: Cinco De Mayo Week holiday item, made by smashing a Mystery Pinata with a Dragon Hand inside. Breathes confetti.
Violet Protodrake Leash: Untradable dragon for players who have completed all event achievements. Breathes purple fire.
Skeletal Dragon Claw: Made by consuming 20 Dragon Bones. Breathes a green fire.
Dragon of Legend: Made by completing the Quest For Fire. Summons a large purple dragon which breathes out a large amount of fire.
Black Crystal Dragon: Made by harmonizing five Black Crystals. Sponsored by v0ren, it breathes out black crystals.
Diamond Dragon: Purchasable from Locke The Traveling Salesman for 100 Diamond Locks. Breathes out crystal blocks, similar to the Black Crystal Dragon.
Ethereal Rainbow Dragon: Can be obtained as an ultimate prize from the spiky survivor Carnival game. Breathes chromatic fire, which changes color overtime.
Gourmet Dragon Claw: One has a chance to be dropped from harvesting any Hatching Dragon Egg.
Super-Dragon Mask: One has a chance to be dropped from harvesting any Hatching Dragon Egg. Shoots lasers.
Emergency Dragon Claw: One has a chance to be dropped from harvesting any Hatching Dragon Egg. Breathes light green fire.
Prehistoric Dragon Claw: One has a chance to be dropped from harvesting any Hatching Dragon Egg. Breathes fire.
Star Dragon Claw: One has a chance to be dropped from harvesting any Hatching Dragon Egg. Breathes blue fire.
Dragon Turtle Charm: One has a chance to be obtained from training a Dragon Turtle.
Chaos Dragon: One has a chance to be dropped from harvesting a ready Hatching Void Egg.
Ghost Dragon Charm: One has a chance to be dropped from harvesting a ready Hatching Ghost Egg.
Jormungandr: One has a chance to be dropped from harvesting a ready Hatching Water Egg.
Euphoric Dragon: One has a chance to be dropped from harvesting a ready Hatching Euphoric Dragon Egg. Breathes out purple, green, yellow, or a mixed variety fireworks.
Celestial Dragon Charm: November 2020's Item Of The Month. No longer obtainable.
Friendship Dragon Claw: Can be obtained as a prize from being in the top three of the **Treasure Turmoil** Guild Daily Challenge, only if the player had contributed points to the challenge. Breathes blue and pink fire with addition of hearts in the flames.
Pet Blood Dragon: Purchase from the Growtopia Store for 9.99 USD on January 24, 2022, one also has a chance to be obtained by using a Geiger Counter during Ubiweek. Shoots lasers.
Dragon Fruit Dragon: One has a chance to be obtained by blending 3 different kind of fruits in a Super Fruit Mixing Machine when the recipe contains Dragon Fruit.
