##  Trivia
  * The Pineapple Fish is based on the real-life pineapplefish (_Cleidopus gloriamaris_). This type of fish has yellow to whitish scales with black rear margins, forming a pattern that resembles a pineapple.
  * The description of the Pineapple Fish alludes to the fact that Hawaii was once known as the world's largest producer of canned pineapples, thus crowning the fruit as Hawaii's de facto state fruit.[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Pineapple_Fish#cite_note-1)
    * Currently, Hawaii's actual state fish is the reef triggerfish (_Rhinecanthus rectangulus_).
