# SGTSTUFFINSREVENGE

**SGTSTUFFINSREVENGE** is a world open during Thanksgiving Week only. It was first introduced during Thanksgiving Week 2022 on November 19, 2022.
The Growtopia community has to catch turkeys in order to anger Sgt. Stuffins. Once Sgt. Stuffins' anger has reached 100%, The _Sgt. Stuffins' Revenge_ special event will start in SGTSTUFFINSREVENGE. In the world, the community will have 60 minutes to defeat Sgt. Stuffins by landing as many punches as possible or by blasting them with a Turkey Sucker to damage them. A health meter is visible on screen. If the player dies while attempting to defeat Sgt. Stuffins, they can either wait 1 minute and 30 seconds or pay a World Lock to immediately play again. Upon Sgt. Stuffins' defeat, the top 5 players who dealth the most damage globally will receive a Sgt. Stuffins' Wings and the top 1 player will receive a Thanksgiving Title! If the time expires and Sgt. Stuffins escapes, then no rewards will be given.
The world is owned by @Ubidev and like The Carnival, there are many worlds (SGTSTUFFINSREVENGE_121, SGTSTUFFINSREVENGE_136, etc). The world has dimensions **50x40** , which differs from the normal **100x60**.
##  Rewards
### Random Rewards
For every 150 damage dealt to Sgt. Stuffins, players earn a random reward. These can be claimed from the store UI. The following rewards can be obtained:
  * Celebration Charm ×1
  * Diner Background ×5
  * Welcome To Growtopia Sign ×1
  * Diner Couch ×1
  * Campaign Poster ×1
  * Riveting Bow Hair ×1
  * Stars and Stripes Cap ×1
  * Stars and Stripes Dress ×1
  * Stars and Stripes Hat ×1
  * Stars and Stripes Pants ×1
  * Stars and Stripes Shawl ×1
  * Stars and Stripes Suit ×1
  * Stars and Stripes Cape ×1
  * Star Spangled Shield ×1
  * Feathered Scarf ×1
  * Floating Throne ×1
  * Cornucopia Cannon ×1

### Special Rewards
Upon Sgt. Stuffins' defeat, the top 1 player who dealt the most damage globally will be able to claim the following reward:
  * Thanksgiving Title

Upon Sgt. Stuffins' defeat, the top 5 players who dealt the most damage globally will be able to claim one of the following rewards:
  * Sgt. Stuffins' Wings ×1
  * Ridin' Sgt. Stuffins' ×1

##  Trivia
  * During the first day of Thanksgiving Week 2022, players were able to do an extreme amount of damage by punching Sgt. Stuffins while wearing a Rayman's Fist. This was later patched by disabling the Rayman's Fist from doing any damage to Sgt. Stuffins.
  * AirSupply is the first player to receive the Thanksgiving Title.
