# Irish Sport Horsie

The Irish Sport Horsie is traditionally used for all purposes from transportation to riding to working the land, and it is popular as a competition riding horsie. It also wears a silly hat.

The **Irish Sport Horsie** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2015 on March 14, 2015.
##  Function
You can move faster!

You are slow again.

When equipped, the **Irish Sport Horsie** grants the _Speedy_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.

##  Trivia
  * Upon consumption of 4 lucky horseshoes, a message will appear saying "_You've tamed an Irish Sport Horse!_ ", and a Irish Sport Horsie will be instantly equipped.
  * If the player says "LOL" or ":D" while riding the horse, the horse will smile too.
  * The horse appears to wear an Actual Leprechaun Hat.
