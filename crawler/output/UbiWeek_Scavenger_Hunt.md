# UbiWeek Scavenger Hunt

The UbiWeek Scavenger Hunt is a feature which was added as part of UbiWeek 2024 on March 28, 2024. This feature can be accessed by clicking its icon in the top left of the screen UI.
Players get a random UbiWeek Scavenger Hunt card which contains a 5x5 grid of UbiWeek-related items. Players have to collect these items across UbiWeek to get prizes. A player has the option to reset the card for 5 world locks, each purchase will rise the reset price by 5 World Locks and cap at a maximum of 50 World Locks. This will discard any progress made on the card, and replace all the prizes with new prizes.
