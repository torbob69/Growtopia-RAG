The **Dragon Fruit Bussin' Sword** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2023 on June 9, 2023.
##  Function
Juiced up!

Out of Juice!

When equipped, the **Dragon Fruit Bussin' Sword** grants the _Punch Damage + Punch Range : Dragon Fruit Bussin' Sword!_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and punch one tile further.
