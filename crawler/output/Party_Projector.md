The **Party Projector** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Grow4Good Week 2025 on April 10, 2025.
##  Function
When placed, the Party Projector serves a purely aesthetic purpose. When placed, a selected circular pattern spins and moves in a 12x7 area depending on the direction the Party Projector is facing in.
This block features several special customization functions. Players can choose four different projection patterns, as well as modify the movement speed and spinning speed of the pattern.
