The **Jack's Vest** is a unsplicable shirt item that was released as part of the Winter Clash.
##  Function
Giving you extra Role points for Supplier and Crafter roles!

No more a Jack of All Trades!

When equipped together with the Jack's Shoes, Jack's Jeans, and Jack's Hat, it grants the _Jack of All Trades!_ mod, which allows role quests to give 3% bonus points. It can be stacked with other items that grants a similar mod, such as Supplier's Cape or Crafter's Cape.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Jack set, which consists of the following items:

    * Jack's Hat (Hat)

    * Jack's Vest (Shirt)

    * Jack's Jeans (Pants)

    * Jack's Shoes (Feet)
