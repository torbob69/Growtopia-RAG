The **Black Friday Black Box Xtreme** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Black Friday 2021 on November 26, 2021.
##  Function
When consumed, the player will randomly obtain one of the following items:
  * Black Balrog's Tail
  * Black Big Fluffin' Giga-Ears
  * Black Bow of the Rainbow
  * Black Burning Eyes
  * Black Egg Champion Cape
  * Black Harvest Shawl
  * Black Neon Party Yo-Yo
  * Black Radical Rollers
  * Black Ultraviolet Charm
  * Blackfire Crown
  * Matrix Aura
  * Matrix Trenchcoat
  * Matrix Visor
  * Neon LED Mask
  * Noir American Eagle Wings
  * Pet Stealth Mech Robot
  * Scepter of Shadows
  * Shadow Crown
  * Shadow Cubes Aura
  * Shadow Soul
  * Shirt Rack
  * Weather Machine - Black Digital Rain

##  Trivia
