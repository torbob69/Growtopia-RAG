The **Mecha Med Shirt** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as part of the Surgery Day monthly event update on July 19, 2021. The item was formally introduced on July 30, 2021, the date Surgery Day arrived.
##  Function
When equipped, the Mecha Med Shirt serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Mecha Med set, which consists of the following items:

    * Mecha Med Helmet (hair)

    * Field Medics Mecha Med Scarf (neck)

    * Mecha Med Shirt (shirt)

    * Mecha Med Pants (pants)

    * Mecha Med Shoes (feet)

    * Ambu-Lance (hand)
  * Unlike the other items in the set, the Field Medics Mecha Med Scarf was added earlier, during Grow4Good Week 2021.
