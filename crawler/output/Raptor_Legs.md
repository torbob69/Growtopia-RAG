**Raptor Legs** is a pants item that was released as June 2018's Item Of The Month.
##  Function
Run like a dinosaur!

Millions of years have aged to your legs!

When equipped, the **Raptor Legs** grants the _Raptor Legs_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land. When the player has the Raptor Legs equipped and is killed, a meteor will come down from the sky and strike them.
##  Trivia
  * The item stacks with feet items with the Speedy Mod, meaning that it will make the player move faster than usual if they equip both.
