The **Ghost Dumplings Hat** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2022 on October 22. 2022.
##  Function
Your ancestors would be proud!

Grandma would be ashamed!

When equipped, the Ghost Dumplings Hat grants the _Cooking Skills: Ghost Dumplings Hat_ , which increases cooked food's quality by 3%. This effect stacks with other items that provide a similar function, such as the Sweet Potato Tots and Exquisite Rolling Pin.
