The **Petals of Purity** is an unsplicable back item which was added as October 2023's Item Of The Season on October 1, 2023.
##  Function
Health rains from above!

Dive dive dive!

When equipped, the **Petals of Purity** grants the _Double Jump + Card Battle Health: Petals of Purity_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and add 2 additional health to the player during card battles against a villain or another player.
