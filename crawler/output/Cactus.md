The **Cactus** is a splicable solid pain foreground block which has been added since Growtopia's launch.
##  Function
Being a type of pain block, a Cactus knocks the player back and stings the player until the player stops all motion. A player will die after making contact with a pain block five times in quick succession (recovering from damage takes roughly 5 seconds). The _Fireproof_ mod will not allow the player to take more hits, unlike with lava pain blocks.
