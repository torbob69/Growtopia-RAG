# Water Asteroid

Laced with unobtanium, this floating rock comes complete with lovely cinematic waterfalls! The perfect touch to elevate a zen garden, or at least make your neighbors really jealous.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.
Data: Paint PreviewType — Animated Block – Foreground — Aqua Blue Charcoal Green Purple Red Varnish Yellow
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: Full Collision
Hardness: **4 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **5s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Paint Preview Aqua Blue Charcoal Green Purple Red Varnish Yellow
Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Water Essence **(×20)** ,
Wind Essence **(×5)** and
Asteroid **(×1)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Water Asteroid** was added with the Hidden Riches update.
##  Function
It is similar to the Asteroid, but its own sprite is also animated.
