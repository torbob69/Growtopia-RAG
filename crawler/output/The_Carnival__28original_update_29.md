**The Carnival** is an update which was introduced on October 13, 2014. It introduced the Carnival, a 3-day event which takes place on a random Friday every month and can be accessed in the world CARNIVAL. In this event, players can play a wide variety of minigames, as well as complete a quest in order to obtain a ring from the Ringmaster.

##  Changes
### CARNIVAL
  * Added the worlds CARNIVAL1–CARNIVAL150.
  * The number of worlds were reduced in a later update, leaving only CARNIVAL1–CARNIVAL50.

### Carnival Games
  * 4 Carnival games were added in total.
  * Added Concentration (Left) and Concentration (Right).
    * Card Blocks are laid face down; the player must punch a card to flip it over. When two matching cards are punched consecutively, the pair becomes flipped for the rest of the game. The goal is to turn over all the card blocks.
    * Based on a card game of a similar name.
  * Added Growganoth Gulch (Left) and Growganoth Gulch (Right).
    * The player must climb a tower and hit the Bullseye. Creepstone Platforms and Evil Eyes may be toggled on or off at any time, potentially killing the player, forcing them to respawn at the bottom.
    * Based on the world GROWGANOTH.
  * Added Mirror Maze.
    * The player needs to navigate through a maze of Mirror Maze Blocks in order to reach the end.
    * Based on a carnival game called the house of mirrors.
  * Added Shooting Gallery (Left) and Shooting Gallery (Right).
    * The player has to punch Bullseyes as quickly as possible; 3 points are rewarded for each punch. A minimum score is 30 is required to win.
    * Based on a carnival game of a similar name.
