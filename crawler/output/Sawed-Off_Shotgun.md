""The zombie invasion has come. Protect yourself with a Rare Sawed-Off Shotgun, Combat Vest, Zombie-Stompin' Boots, 3 Traffic Barricades, a Military Radio, and Best of all, you get an Antidote! Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Sawed-Off Shotgun** is an unsplicable hand item which was added alongside the Zombie Defense Pack on May 17, 2013.
##  Function
Blast those zombies!

You are disarmed.

When equipped, the **Sawed-Off Shotgun** grants the _Armed And Dangerous_ mod, which allows the player to kill zombies during a Pandemic and knock back other players further.
##  Trivia
  * This item's description is a reference to the horror film Army of Darkness. "_Shop smart, shop S-Mart!_ " is the tagline of the store that the protagonist worked in.
