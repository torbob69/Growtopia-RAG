The **Prince of Persia Crate** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of UbiWeek 2022 Day 4 on January 27, 2022.
##  Function
When broken, a Prince of Persia Crate will drop:
  * Prince of Persia: Cave Background ×100
  * Prince of Persia: Cave Column ×50
  * Prince of Persia: Cave Spikes ×20
  * Prince of Persia: Cave Torch ×20
  * Prince of Persia: Hourglass of Time ×20
  * Prince of Persia: Palace Arch ×50
  * Prince of Persia: Palace Background ×100
  * Prince of Persia: Palace Dome ×50
  * Prince of Persia: Palace Spikes ×20
  * Prince of Persia: Palace Turret ×50
  * Prince of Persia: Palace Wall ×100

##  Trivia
  * A Flashback Flan may be used on a Prince of Persia Crate in order to retrieve the block once it has been placed.
