# Buddy's Sign

A sign belonging to Buddy himself!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item has special properties you can adjust with the Wrench.
Data: Paint PreviewType — Sign – Foreground — Aqua Blue Charcoal Green Purple Red Varnish Yellow
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **4 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **4s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Paint Preview Aqua Blue Charcoal Green Purple Red Varnish Yellow
Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Sign **(×1)** ,
Hero Portrait - Buddy **(×1)** and
Sun Block **(×5)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Buddy's Sign** is an unsplicable non-solid sign foreground block which was added as part of SummerFest 2022 on July 28, 2022.
##  Function
Being a sign, a Buddy's Sign allows players with access to it to edit its message for other players to view.
