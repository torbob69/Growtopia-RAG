The **Garuda Statue** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of the Garuda's Glory update on August 26, 2022.
##  Function
When placed, the Garuda Statue serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The Garuda Statue is a statue of a Hindu demigod named Garuda described as the king of the birds known as the state insignia of India, Indonesia, and Thailand.
