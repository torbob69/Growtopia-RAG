This item was given to **Redbone** for their contributions as a voluntary moderator.
**Red Bones** is an unobtainable face item which was added alongside the Mad Science Kit on July 20, 2013.
##  Function
Your bones, and all the stuff stuck to them, are red!

Normal bones restored.

When equipped, this item grants the _Red Bones_ mod, which turns the color of the player's skin slightly dark red.
##  Trivia
  * Red Bones' appearance may be based on the Red Skull, a fictional supervillain appearing in comic books published by Marvel Comics.
  * The  Skull Mask and  Crystal Skull are seemingly designed after  Red Bones.
