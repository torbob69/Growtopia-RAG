The **Starship Mess Hall Stool** is a splicable non-solid seat foreground block which was added as part of the Block Party 2 update.
##  Function
When a player stands in front of a seat such as the Starship Mess Hall Stool for a few seconds, the player will automatically be seen "sitting" on it.
