The **Avocado Advocate** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2023 on May 5, 2023.
##  Function
Legally Strong!

Legally Gone!

When equipped, the **Avocado Advocate** grants the _Build Range + Card Battle Health: Avocado Advocate_ mod, which allows the player to build one tile further and add 2 additional health to the player during card battles against a villain or another player.
