The **Presidential Chair** is an unsplicable non-solid seat foreground block that was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2022 on November 19, 2022.
##  Function
When a player stands in front of a seat such as the Presidential Chair for a few seconds, the player will automatically be seen "sitting" on it.
##  Trivia
  * The  Presidential Chair's design is based on the  Presidential Throne.
