##  Function
Unlimited power!

No... no.... no!

When equipped, the **Grand Minimus Magic** grants the _Punch Damage + Punch Range: Grand Minimus Magic_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster, punch one tile further, and transform into the Grand Minimus when the entire Grand Minimus set is equipped.

    * Grand Minimus Mask (Face)

    * Grand Minimus Magic (Hand)
