The **Starship Sickbay Bed** is an unsplicable non-solid bed foreground block which was added as part of the Startopia update.
##  Function
Being a bed, executing the /sleep command in chat while standing on the same tile as a Starship Sickbay Bed will cause the player to sleep in it.
A Starship Sickbay Bed is different from other beds in the sense that a player can perform a surgery on another player who is standing on the same tile as this block. They will have a chance to receive unique Startopia-related prizes in addition to the normal rewards.
