The **Sungate** is an unsplicable non-solid portal foreground block which was added as part of the Rise of the Desert Dragons update on August 3, 2013.
##  Function
Unlike normal portals, a Sungate can only be entered while seven Obelisks are activated in the world.
##  Trivia
  * The Sungate is likely a reference to the _Stargate_ franchise. The item's sprite resembles the Earth's Milky Way Stargate from the series, and the name is similar to the titular device's.
  * A destroyed Sungate was able to drop a seed during the early period of its release. The Sungate Seed is one of the rarest glitch seeds.
