# FASHIONSHOWGAME

**FASHIONSHOWGAME** is a world that opens once a month and lasts for four days. Players can attend fashion shows to with their outfits and earn prizes, or vote for contestants in a fashion show.
##  Update History
It was first introduced during Player Appreciation Week 2025.
##  Gameplay
Each FASHIONSHOWGAME world contains six stage rooms. Players can join one by purchasing a Fashion Show Ticket from the Growtopia Store and punching the Fashion Show Bouncer near the Fashion Show room while wearing at least four different clothing items.
Upon joining, the participant is moved to a solo waiting room and can no longer change their clothes. Once there are five participants, a 10 seconds countdown begins. When it reaches zero, each participant is featured on the fashion stage in one of five rounds for 20 seconds. After each round, there is an 'After Stage' phase, which lasts for 5 seconds. Players who are not participating in an active Fashion Show can use the Fashion Show Voting Box to vote for the participant who best matches the theme. The participant(s) with the most votes win.

##  Changes
On August 24, 2026, during Player Appreciation Week 2026, feedback regarding the Fashion Show was taken into account and the reward payout was adjusted. Rewards are now given to only a single best-dressed winner. If there is more than one winner, they will get the cost of their Fashion Show Ticket back.
##  Trivia
Similar to CARNIVAL, there are many FASHIONSHOWGAME world instances (FASHIONSHOWGAME, FASHIONSHOWGAME_2, FASHIONSHOWGAME_3, etc). These worlds are owned by @Ubidev.
