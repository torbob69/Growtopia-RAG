The **WOTD Trophy 2025** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block that is given to players who win World Of The Day during 2025 by Ubisoft Staff.
##  Function
When placed, the WOTD Trophy 2025 serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
Step 16 of the  Quest For Fire requires the player to deliver 1 of this item. (or the subsequent trophies)
