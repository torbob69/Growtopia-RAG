##  Function
You can jump in mid-air! It's like flying, but less.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Ripper Wings** grants the _Double Jump!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
Step 17 of the  Quest Of The Heavens requires the player to deliver 100 of this item.
