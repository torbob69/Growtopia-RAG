# Hip Hops

Hop hop hop, hop to the top!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be transmuted.
Data

Type: Feet – Clothes
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: Full Collision
Hardness: **0 Hits** **0 Hits** – Restores after **0s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Bunny Egg Reward

Break a  Bunny Egg at 160-169% completion. 1 of this item is given out each time.
Bunny Eggs may explode randomly when their sizes are above 100%.
The **Hip Hops** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Easter Week 2022 on April 15, 2022.
##  Function
Up, up and away!

Dow, down and here!

When equipped, the **Hip Hops** grants the _Speedy & High Jump: Hip Hops_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and jump one tile higher.
