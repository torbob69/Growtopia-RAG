The **Crystal Infused Sword** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the Startopia 2.0 update on June 1, 2020.
##  Function
The power of the crystal flows through you!

The crystal power flows out of you!

When equipped, the **Crystal Infused Sword** grants the _Punch Damage: Crystal Infused Sword!_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster.
