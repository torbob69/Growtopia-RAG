The **Television** is a splicable non-solid toggleable foreground block which was added alongside the addition of Home Pack on February 26, 2014.
##  Function
<https://growtopiawiki.com/w/File:Western.ogg>The Television's music.
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Television can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Television will change its appearance, showing a 2-frame animation, and play a piece of music.
