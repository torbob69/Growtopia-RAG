The **Peace Mooncake Hat** is an unsplicable hat item that was added as part of Harvest Festival 2020 on September 24, 2020.
##  Function
When equipped, the **Peace Mooncake Hat** serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
The  Harvest Festival Exclusive Mission - I can seed clearly now my gun I've got! mission requires 10 of this item.
