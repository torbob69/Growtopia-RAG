# Bouncy Wall

Build your own bouncy obstacle course with these inflatable walls.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data: Paint PreviewType — Trampoline Block – Foreground — Aqua Blue Charcoal Green Purple Red Varnish Yellow
Render Type: Horizontal
Collision Type: Full Collision
Hardness: **5 Hits** **4 Hits** – Restores after **3s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Paint Preview Aqua Blue Charcoal Green Purple Red Varnish Yellow
Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Beach Umbrella **(×5)** ,
Water Bucket **(×5)** and
Beach Blast **(×1)**.
This recipe will yield **3** items.
The **Bouncy Wall** is an unsplicable solid trampoline foreground block which was added as part of SummerFest 2023 on July 13, 2023.
##  Function
Jumping on a trampoline block such as a Bouncy Wall will cause the player to bounce, and by holding the jump button, the player can jump even higher. The _High Jump_ mod also boosts one's jump height on a trampoline block.
