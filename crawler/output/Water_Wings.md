# Water Wings

Not a strong swimmer? Strap these on so at least your arms will stay above water as you drown.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Hand – Clothes
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: Full Collision
Hardness: **0 Hits** **0 Hits** – Restores after **0s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Super Fireworks Reward

May be obtained from firing off  Super Fireworks with **200** Fireworks in your inventory.
The **Water Wings** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the SummerFest 2014 on June 27, 2014.
##  Function
When equipped, the **Water Wings** allows the player to float on water permanently with slow ascent.
