The **Dear John Tractor** is an unsplicable feet item which was added alongside the Farm Pack on July 6, 2013.
##  Function
Tractors are not speedy.

You can walk faster than that thing!

When equipped, the **Dear John Tractor** grants the _Putt Putt Putt_ mod, which allows the player to move slower, but allows the player to harvest trees by walking through them. Unlike certain items such as the Harvester, the Dear John Tractor does not work in conjunction with Fuel Packs or any similar item.

##  Trivia
  * The in-game name is a reference to a farm tractor company called "_JOHN DEERE TRACTORS._ " The tractor itself is almost identical to the ones the company actually makes.
  * Simultaneously wearing other items that cause the player to move slower, such as a Cursed Wizard Hat or a Mermaid Tail will make the player move at a extremely slow speed.
  * Harvesting a tree with the Dear John Tractor is considered punching the tree. Thus, players cannot punch anything else while harvesting trees.
