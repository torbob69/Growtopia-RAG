The **Picasso Triggerfish Leash** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the Gone Fishin'! update on January 14, 2018.
##  Function
When equipped, a Picasso Triggerfish pet will appear and follow the player around. The Picasso Triggerfish Leash serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
