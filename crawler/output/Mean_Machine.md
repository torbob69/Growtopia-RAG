The **Mean Machine** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as March 2023's Item Of The Season on March 2, 2023.
##  Function
They see me rollin'!

They hatin'!

When equipped, the **Mean Machine** grants the _Speedy + High Jump: Mean Machine!_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and jump one tile higher.
##  Trivia
  * The Mean Machine's description is a reference to the movie The Incredibles.
    * Its equip and unequip messages is a reference to Chamillionaire's 2005 song Ridin'.
