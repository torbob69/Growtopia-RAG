The **Pineapple Guzzler** is an unsplicable non-solid animated foreground block which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2021 on June 3, 2021.
##  Function
The Pineapple Guzzler can be fed with Pineapples, Dangerous Pineapples, or Super Pineapples by a player in order to receive the Pineapple Magic or Pineapple Tragic mod. Feeding it with a Pineapple or Dangerous Pineapple will increase its chance of explosion by 25%. However, feeding it with a Super Pineapple will cause it to instantly explode.
##  Trivia
  * When a player with a spiky pineapple body feeds a Pineapple Guzzler with a Dangerous Pineapple, it will revert the player's body back to a normal pineapple body, although destroying the Pineapple Guzzler in the process.
  *   * The  Baby Guzzler is designed after the  Pineapple Guzzler.
