The **Ghastly Robe** is an unsplicable shirt item that was added as October 2017's Item Of The Month.
##  Function
Whoooaaaa! You're a scary ghooooost! Boo!

You are back among the living.

When equipped, the **Ghastly Robe** grants the _Ghastly Robe_ mod, which allows the player to change their skin color to translucent dark grey. It will also allow the player to float with smoke particles surrounding them. Additionally, it will replace the player's punching animation with souls.

##  Trivia
  * Every Halloween Week, the Ghastly Robe is given a pumpkin visual effect.
