The **GrowBeats Headphones** is an unsplicable face item which was added alongside the Pinball Bumper and Phonecats Hat in version 1.13.4 on March 7, 2013.
##  Function
When equipped, the GrowBeats Headphones serves a fully cosmetic purpose. By typing the _/dance_ command, the player will automatically dance while emitting yellow music note particles. The headphones will also beat in intervals.
