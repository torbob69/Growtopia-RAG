# Ring Of Savings

Congratulations on winning the Carnie Contest Daily Challenge! With this ring equipped, you save 10% on Golden Ticket prices (pay 90 rarity per ticket, or get 33 per World Lock).

Daily Challenge

Place in the top three of the **Carnie Contest** Daily Challenge.
The **Ring Of Savings** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the Daily Challenges update on November 18, 2015.
##  Function
When equipped, the **Ring Of Savings** allows the player to reduce the price for Golden Tickets sold at Golden Ticket Booths by 10%.
