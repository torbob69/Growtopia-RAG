The **Pueblock Door** is an unsplicable non-solid door foreground block which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2019 on May 3, 2019.
##  Function
As a door, the Pueblock Door can be entered (via punching) by a player, moving them instantly to another tile. A block with an ID must first be placed on the tile for this to work.
The item has four different textures and when placed, a random one will appear. The four textures are:
  * Brown rectangular door with an orange handle and a blue Pueblock background.
  * Glass rectangular door with curved edges and a green Pueblock background.
  * Brown rectangular door with an orange handle and an orange Pueblock background.
  * Pastel orange oval door with two vertical stripes, an orange handle and a pink Pueblock background.
