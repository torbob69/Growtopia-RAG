The **Gold Guild Chest** is a consumable that was added as part of Guilds Update on April 1, 2018.

  * Guild Banner - Normal (Vertical)

  * Guild Entrance - Ornate

##  Trivia
  * Gold Guild Chest,  Silver Guild Chest and  Bronze Guild Chest were added as Guild Leaderboard prizes for Guild Events in 2018.
  * In the first ever Guild Event, players had to perform several activities with anomalizers equipped to earn points for their guild. (Click here for more info)
  * After the first Guild Event ended, the players voiced their discontent about the complexity of this event. Hence, the DEV team reworked the event into Seasonal Clashes, while removing all guild chests from the prize pool.
