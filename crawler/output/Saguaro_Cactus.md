The **Saguaro Cactus** is an unsplicable solid pain foreground block which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2019 on May 3, 2019.
##  Function
Being a type of pain block, a Saguaro Cactus knocks the player back and stings the player until the player stops all motion. A player will die after making contact with a pain block five times in quick succession (recovering from damage takes roughly 5 seconds). The _Fireproof_ mod will not allow the player to take more hits, unlike with lava pain blocks.
##  Trivia
  * The Saguaro Cactus is almost visually indistinguishable to the regular Cactus. Unlike the latter, however, the former connects with other blocks of its kind.
