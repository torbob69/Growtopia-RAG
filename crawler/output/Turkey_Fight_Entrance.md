The **Turkey Fight Entrance** is an unsplicable foreground block which has been added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2022 on November 19, 2022.
##  Function
During a _Sgt. Stuffins' Revenge_ special event in SGTSTUFFINSREVENGE, this entrance will close for 90 seconds once a player has been killed by Sgt. Stuffins' and it's minions, preventing the player from joining the fight. Players, however, has the option to pay 1,000 Gems to rejoin the fight immediately.
