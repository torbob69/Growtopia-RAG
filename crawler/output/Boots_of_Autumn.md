# Boots of Autumn

Move at the speed of sound this fall!

Consume a(n)  Equinox Box **(x1)**.
May yield **1** item each.

The **Boots of Autumn** is an unsplicable feet item that was added as part of Equinox Weekend on March 25, 2022.
##  Function
Run over!

Fell over!

When equipped, the **Boots of Autumn** grants the _Speedy: Boots of Autumn_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Equinox Autumn set, which consists of the following items:

    * Crown of Autumn (hat)

    * Shirt of Autumn (shirt)

    * Pants of Autumn (pants)

    * Boots of Autumn (shoes)
