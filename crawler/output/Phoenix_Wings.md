The **Phoenix Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of SummerFest 2014 on June 27, 2014.
##  Function
You can jump in mid-air! It's like flying, but less.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Phoenix Wings** grants the _Double Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
Step 18 of the  Quest Of The Heavens requires the player to deliver 1 of this item. 1 Neptune's Trident may also be used as an alternative.
##  Trivia
  * Up until SummerFest 2021, Only 500 Phoenix Wings were available in the game. Every so often, the existence of Phoenix Wings were decreasing due to players delivering for the Quest Of The Heavens or players being permanently banned with Phoenix Wings in their possession.
    * To counter the dwindling supply of Phoenix Wings and other Phoenix Items, the Growtopia Team released Phoenix Returns on SummerFest 2021 (Renamed to Phoenix Rising on SummerFest 2022) which brought back the old and unobtainable Phoenix Items including the Phoenix Wings.
