# Crab Claw & Shield

Somewhere, there's a very sad, very unarmed crab out there. Don't feel bad, though - these were better off in your hands.

The **Crab Claw & Shield** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of SummerFest 2019 on June 27, 2019.
##  Function
Prepare to be pinched!

Aaaah, soothing!

When equipped, the **Crab Claw & Shield** grants the _Punch Damage: Dungeonist_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster.
The  The Seven Seas : The Crust of the Crab! mission requires 1 of this item.
