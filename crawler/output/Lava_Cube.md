The **Lava Cube** is an unsplicable lava pain foreground block which was added in the Unicorn Madness update on May 30, 2014.
##  Function
Being a lava pain block, a Lava Cube knocks the player back and puts the player on fire until the player stops all motion when touched. A player will die after making contact with a lava pain block five times in quick succession (recovering from damage takes roughly 5 seconds), but a player with the _Fireproof_ mod may take up to ten consecutive hits.
##  Trivia
  * Despite only one Lava Cube is given upon combining the needed items, there is an abundance of Lava Cubes due to the fact that they are farmable.
  * The sprite of Lava Cube is same with the sprite of Lava during Growtopia's beta development phase.
