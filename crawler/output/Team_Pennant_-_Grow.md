The **Team Pennant - Grow** is a non-solid block which was added in the Balloon Warz update. This item is a rare drop from a Balloon-O-Matic for every 8000 rarity given to it, when Balloon Fragments are given to it, or when it explodes.
##  Trivia
Team Pennant - Grow is one of 3 Team Pennants added in Balloon Warz, alongside Team Pennant - Punch and Team Pennant - Build. Each pennants represent their own teams. As an animated foreground block, a Team Pennant - Grow features an automatic 2-frame animation of its flag waving.
