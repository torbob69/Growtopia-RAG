The **Toilet** is a splicable non-solid SFX foreground block which was added as part of Growtopia's launch.
##  Function
When a player stands in front of a seat such as the Toilet for a few seconds, the player will automatically be seen "sitting" on it. Additionally, the player will be seen reading a newspaper, protecting their privacy. Punching a Toilet makes a flushing sound effect.
