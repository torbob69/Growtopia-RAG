The **Anubis Mail** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as part of Time-Tossed! on September 5, 2019.
##  Function
When equipped, the Anubis Mail serves a fully cosmetic purpose.
When equipped together with the Anubis Mask, Anubis Seal, Anubis Greaves, Anubis Boots and Sun Blade in a world with an arid weather, one can enter an active Sungate which target leads to (DUAT:three letters the player obtained on the first riddle) with a Sandstone and Solar Collector on top of the Sungate to receive the Spirit of Anubis.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Anubis set, which consists of the following items:

    * Anubis Mask (Hat)

    * Anubis Seal (Chest)

    * Anubis Mail (Shirt)

    * Anubis Greaves (Pants)

    * Anubis Boots (Feet)

    * Sun Blade (Hand)
