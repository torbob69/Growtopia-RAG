The **Stove** is a splicable non-solid toggleable animated foreground block which was added in February 2013.
##  Function
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Stove can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Stove will change its appearance, showing a 2-framed animation of a turkey being roasted inside.
