# Conquest's Helm

BEHOLD! The conqueror approaches. All who view this helm shall flee in terror before its bearer, and those who remain will find naught but ruin.
Properties
This item cannot be dropped or traded.
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Hat – Clothes

Rewards of Sacrifice

Sacrifice a(n)  Dancemaster's Crown or  Dark King's Offering (chance) to Growganoth.
The Conquest's Helm is an unsplicable hat item that was added as part of Halloween Week 2018 on October 26, 2018.
##  Function
When equipped, nearby players will automatically perform the /omg command.
##  Trivia
  * Players who are sitting in a chair, or performing another command will not force them to perform the /omg command.
  * Conquest's Helm is the only untradeable item that you can get from Growganoth.
