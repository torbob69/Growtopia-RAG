**Skeletal Dragon Claw** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the Halloween Week 2014 update on October 31, 2014.
##  Function
You command a mighty (expired) dragon!

Your dragon has abandoned you.

When equipped, the **Skeletal Dragon Claw** grants the _Dragondead_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and knock back other players further. Additionally, it summons a dragon that follows the player around, which also replaces player's punching animation with green flames.
