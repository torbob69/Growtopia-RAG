The **Growchimilco Boat** is an unsplicable platform foreground block which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2023 on May 5, 2023.
##  Function
A Growchimilco Boat behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath.
##  Trivia
  * The Growchimilco Boat is a reference to a Mexican colorful gondola-like boats known as trajineras commonly found in Xochimilco, a borough of Mexico City.
