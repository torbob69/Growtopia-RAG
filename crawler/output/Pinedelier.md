The **Pinedelier** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable animated foreground block which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2019 on June 7, 2019.
##  Function
<https://growtopiawiki.com/w/File:Spooky_tiki.ogg>The Pinedelier's music.
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Pinedelier can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Pinedelier will change its appearance, showing a 2-frame animation of itself being lit up, and play a piece of music. As a light source, a Pinedelier emits light in dark areas.
##  Trivia
  * In the game files, the Pinedelier's music is named spooky_tiki.
