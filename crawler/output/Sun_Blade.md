The **Sun Blade** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Time Tossed! on September 5, 2019.
##  Function
Unsheathed the sun! My eyes!

The sun has set!

When equipped, the **Sun Blade** grants the _Punch Damage: Sun Smash!_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster.
When equipped together with the Anubis Mask, Anubis Mail, Anubis Greaves, Anubis Boots and Anubis Seal in a world with an arid weather, one can enter an active Sungate which target leads to (DUAT:three letters the player obtained on the first riddle) with a Sandstone and Solar Collector on top of the Sungate to receive the Spirit of Anubis.
##  Trivia

    * Anubis Mask (Hat)

    * Anubis Seal (Chest)

    * Anubis Mail (Shirt)

    * Anubis Greaves (Pants)

    * Anubis Boots (Feet)

    * Sun Blade (Hand)
