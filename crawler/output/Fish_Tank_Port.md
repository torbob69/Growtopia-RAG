# Fish Tank Port

This is a handy hole in a fish tank, through which you can insert or remove fish! Use your wrench to manage the fish, just like real fish. You can only place this on a fish tank.
Properties
This item can't be destroyed - smashing it will return it to your backpack if you have room!

##  Function
A Fish Tank Port can only be placed on top of a Fish Tank. The owner of a world containing a Fish Tank Port can wrench it to insert fish and crabs into the Fish Tanks, allowing them to move freely inside. They can also remove any selected fish and move them to their inventory from the menu.
The only player allowed to access a Fish Tank Port is the owner themself. As a co-owner, wrenching one would only display the contents of the tank, while punching the block would cause the message "_Only the world owner can mess with fishies!_ " to appear. No damage would be done to the Fish Tank Port.
##  Trivia
  * When a Fish Tank Port's perfect fish glow bar is ticked, all perfect fish (indicated by the color of their weight) will emit glowing outlines.
  * A Fish Tank Port can hold at most 10 fishes.
