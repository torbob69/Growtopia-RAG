# Mods/Pineapple Tragic

< Mods

The tragedy has come to an end!

**Pineapple Tragic** is a mod that occurs to a player with a Pineapple Body (from eating 600 Pineapples) who feeds a Dangerous Pineapple to another player, also with a Pineapple Body.
