The **Wings of Virtue** is an unsplicable back item which was added as January 2025's Item Of The Season on January 2, 2025.
##  Function
Let there be light!

Hello darkness, my old friend!

When equipped, the **Wings of Virtue** grants the _Double Jump + Light Source : Wings of Virtue_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and emit light in dark areas.
