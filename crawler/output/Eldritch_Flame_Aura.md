"Burn with blue aura of the eldritch flame!"
The **Eldritch Flame Aura** is an unsplicable artifact item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2023 Day 3 on August 30, 2023.
##  Function
Flame on!

Flame off!

When equipped, the **Eldritch Flame Aura** grants the _Fireproof : Eldritch Flame Aura_ mod, which allows the player to take 50% less damage from lava pain blocks.
##  Trivia
  * Eldritch Flame Aura is the most expensive item to be sold by Locke The Salesman.
