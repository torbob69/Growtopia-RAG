# Growtopia Store

The **Growtopia Store** is the in-game store where players can purchase various items including locks, jammers, packs, clothes and more. Everything in the Store can be bought with a certain amount of Gems, Growtokens, Grow Vouchers or Money.
To access the store, tap the red gem on the screen, open the menu, or press the 'Store' button in the inventory.

##  Trivia
  * When an old item which needs to be purchased directly from the Store is recycled, it will give a one-tenth of the item's price (e.g. A Tiny Horsie will give 2500 gems, compared to the original price of 25000 gems). This no longer applies to World Locks or newly added items.
  * On March 1, 2020, an update focused on enhancing and optimizing the UI of the **Growtopia Store** , along with Backpacks.
  * On August 20th 2022 during Player Appreciation Week 2022 Day 6, everything in the **Growtopia Store** , except the World Lock and World Lock Pack was 20% cheaper for the day as a part of Discount Day.
