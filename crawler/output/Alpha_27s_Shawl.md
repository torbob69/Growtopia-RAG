Place in the top three of the **War of Wolves** Guild Daily Challenge, only if the player had contributed points to the challenge.
The **Alpha's Shawl** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of the Challengers Approach! update on October 9, 2019.
##  Function
Lead the pack!

You run alone.

When equipped, the **Alpha's Shawl** grants the _Woof Time: Alpha's Shawl_ mod, which allows the player to get an extra minute to complete a WOLFWORLD.
