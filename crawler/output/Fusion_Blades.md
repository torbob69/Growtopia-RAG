The **Fusion Blades** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as May 2026's Item Of The Season on May 1, 2026.
##  Function
Fusion! Hah!!

Fusion? Nah!!

When equipped, the **Fusion Blades** grants the _Punch Range + Punch Damage: Fusion Blades_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further and break blocks faster. When worn, players can switch between single and dual blade by equipping and unequipping the item.
