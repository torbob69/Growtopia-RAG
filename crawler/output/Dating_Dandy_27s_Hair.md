The **Dating Dandy's Hair** is a hair item which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2020.
##  Function
When equipped, the Dating Dandy's Hair serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Dating Dandy set, which consists of the following items:

    * Dating Dandy's Hair (Hair)

    * Dating Dandy's Suit (Shirt)
  * Equipping a Dating Dandy's Hair along with any level 6 ancestral artifact makes the player leave a heart trail as they move.
