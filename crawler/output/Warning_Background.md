# Warning Background

When something is so dangerous there's no time for words, just throw down some yellow and black stripes!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.
Data: Paint PreviewType — Background Block – Background — Aqua Blue Charcoal Green Purple Red Varnish Yellow
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: Full Collision
Hardness: **6 Hits** **5 Hits** – Restores after **3s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**

Spirit Storage Unit Reward

Store a  Ghost-In-A-Jar **(x21)** in a  Spirit Storage Unit, increasing its capacity by **105%**.
May yield **5** items each.
The **Warning Background** is an unsplicable background block which was added as part of the Battlers Of the Otherworldly update on July 15, 2016.
##  Function
When placed, the Warning Background serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * As part of the Block Party 2 update, the quantity of Warning Backgrounds obtained from a Spirit Storage Unit explosion was increased from 1 to 5.
