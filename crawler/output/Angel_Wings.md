The **Angel Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2013 on February 10, 2013.
##  Function
You can jump in mid-air! It's like flying, but less.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Angel Wings** grants the _Double Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.

  * The description of Angel Wings references the Golden Halo.

  * Angel Wings has a golden counterpart:  Golden Angel Wings.
