**Mr. Grownopoly's Suitcase** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2022 on March 11, 2022.
##  Function
Uptown top ranking!

Downtown!

When equipped, the **Mr. Grownopoly's Suitcase** grants the _Punch Range : Mr. Grownopoly's Suitcase_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further. Additionally, the player will also leave cash particles as they move around. The item's punch effect features cash being flung away.
##  Trivia
  * Mr. Grownopoly's Suitcase is a reference to Mr. Monopoly, the mascot of the popular board game _Monopoly_.
