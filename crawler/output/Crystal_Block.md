##  Trivia
  * Very early on, when the Crystal Block was introduced, its seed was accidentally made obtainable through the Rare Seed Pack. It was later removed, making the item extremely rare. It was then made available through the _Beautiful Crystal_ special event on March 13, 2013.
  * On March 18, 2013, a server update implemented a tweak on the texture of the Crystal Block to make it more distinguishable from the Glass Block.
    * The design was credited to the ex-moderator meow, who posted a suggestion of the current graphic on the forums.
