# Apple Pie

The most delicious incarnation of the humble apple.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **3 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **4s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Apple **(×2)** ,
Dough **(×3)** and
Sugar Cane **(×2)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Apple Pie** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Rise of the Desert Dragons update on August 3, 2013.
##  Function
When consumed, the player will say "YUM!" and do the :D ⦅⦆ emote.
### Silkworm Food     _Main article:Guide:Silkworm Care_
This consumable can be fed to a Silkworm to give it a yellow color, thus allowing it to produce 1–10  Silk Thread - Yellow per harvest.
