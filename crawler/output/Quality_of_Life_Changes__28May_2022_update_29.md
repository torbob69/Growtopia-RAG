# Quality of Life Changes (May 2022 update)

Growtopia Gazette
> May 20: Quality of Life Changes!
> Good News Growtopians! We've heard you and made some fixes and optimizations that you have suggested during the past few months!
> Please make sure to read Morse's Dev Diary to find out more details!
**Quality of Life Changes** is an update that was introduced on May 20, 2022.
##  Changes
###  Block Glue
  * The quantity of Block Glue obtained from combining is increased from 2 to 10.

###  Sales-Man
  * The Sales-Man has been rebalanced with regards to Wolf Tickets.
    * Dark Castle Stone's price has been decreased from 20 to 1.
    * Dark Castle Stone Background's price has been decreased from 20 to 1.
    * Dark Castle Door's price has been decreased from 20 to 1.
    * Dark Castle Turret's price has been decreased from 20 to 1.

###  The Ringmaster
  * When trading rings with The Ringmaster, the number of rings needed to trade have been reduced and The Ringmaster from 10 to 5 and will not give the player the same ring they have traded.

###  World Owners
  * World owners now have the ability to enter their worlds even if they are at capacity.

###  World Owner Accessible Items
  * Legendary Wizard and Phone Booths, and Geiger Chargers are now only accessible by the world owner.

###  High-Value Items
  * Several high value items are now no longer placeable in public worlds and worlds the player is an admin in.
    * Growie Award
    * WOTD Trophies
    * Legen Seed
    * Dary Seed
    * Golden Dragon Statue
    * Geiger Charger
    * Black Crystal
    * White Crystal
    * Blue Crystal
    * Red Crystal
    * Green Crystal
    * DNA Processor
    * Crime Wave
    * Henchman

###  House Entrance SFX Bug
  * House Entrance and other similar blocks' SFX no longer randomly plays when entering a world.

###  Rune Carved Door
  * Worlds that teleports the player to the Rune Carved Door instead of the Main Door when entering has now been fixed.

###  Builder's Lock
  * Now shows the number of days the owner of the lock last played and now disintegrates after 180 days of inactivity, similar to Small Lock, Big Lock, and Huge Lock.

###  Murder Hole - Hot Liquid Tar
  * Players can no longer be killed by a Murder Hole - Hot Liquid Tar if it is above the Main Door of a world.

###  PVP Card Battle
  * Added enhancements done to PVP Card Battle.
