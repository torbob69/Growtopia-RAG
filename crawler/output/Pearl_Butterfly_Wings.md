The **Pearl Butterfly Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Beach Party 2022 on June 24, 2022.
##  Function
Fly away!

Knocked down!

When equipped, the **Pearl Butterfly Wings** grants the _Double Jump : Pearl Butterfly Wings_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
