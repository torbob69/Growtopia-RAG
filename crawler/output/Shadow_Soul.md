The **Shadow Soul** is an unsplicable artifact item which was added as part of Black Friday 2023 on November 24, 2023.
##  Function
Into the shadows!

Out of the shadows!

When equipped, the **Shadow Soul** grants the _Double Jump: Shadow Soul_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
##  Trivia
  * The Shadow Soul bears some resemblance to the Shade, an enemy in the Metroidvania game Hollow Knight, which appears when The Knight's shell breaks.
