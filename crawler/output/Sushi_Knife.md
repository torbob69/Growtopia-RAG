##  Function
You slice and dice!

Back to punching.

When equipped, the **Sushi Knife** grants the _-_ mod. It will also allow the player chop certain dropped items into cooking ingredients on a Cutting Board and perform a similar function to the Sushi Knife. Additionally, It will replace the player's punching animation with throwing knives.
### Cutting Recipes
Cutting: MakesEvery 10lb of fish* — **→** — Fish Chunk
Cow — **→** — Beef
Chicken — **→** — Chicken Meat
Coconut — **→** — Coconut Water
Glass Piglet Squid (perfect size) — **→** — Oink-Oink Nose (chance to drop)
Gar (perfect size) — **→** — Rayman's Shoes (chance to drop)
  * _Note: For every 10lb of fish, oneFish Chunk is given. Fish weighing 9lb or lower will only give one Fish Chunk when cut. However, for fish weighing 9lb or lower, there is a chance that blood will splatter on the board instead, wasting the fish._
