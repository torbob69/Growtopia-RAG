The **Papel Picado Banner** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2022 on May 5, 2022.
##  Function
When placed, the Papel Picado Banner serves a fully aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The Papel Picado Banner is based on a traditional Mexican craft of the same name, which is made by cutting elaborate designs into sheets of tissue paper.
