The **Alien Block** is a splicable solid foreground block which was added as part of the original Night Of The Comet update on November 27, 2014.
##  Function
As a light source, an Alien Block emits light in dark areas. The color of the Alien Block flickers from light to dark.
##  Trivia
  * As of Player Appreciation Week 2020, several blocks whose trees are made by splicing Starseed have been made farmable.
    * This change includes the Alien Block, Ion Conduit, and Xenoid Block.
