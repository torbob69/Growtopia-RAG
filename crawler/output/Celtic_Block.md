The **Celtic Block** is an unsplicable solid foreground block which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2014 on March 14, 2014.
##  Function
When placed, the Celtic Block serves a purely aesthetic purpose.

##  Trivia
  * The design of the Celtic Block is based on the real-life Celtic knot, a variety of knots used extensively in Celtic ornamentation.
