The **Six Shooter** is an unsplicable hand item which was added alongside the Wild West Pack on September 11, 2013.
##  Function
You shoot bullets instead of punching!

You are disarmed.

When equipped, the **Six Shooter** grants the _Armed and Dangerous_ mod, which allows the player to kill zombies during a Pandemic. It will also replace the player's punching animation with shooting bullets out of the gun.
