# Pilgrim's Bonnet

There are all kinds of things to be thankful for during the holidays, but maybe one of the most important is just how dang awesome those pilgrims looked! Top your ensemble with this traditional bonnet and join the fun!

The **Pilgrim's Bonnet** is an unsplicable hat item that was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2018 on November 22, 2018.
##  Function
When equipped, Pilgrim's Bonnet serves a fully cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the pilgrim (female) set, which consists of the following items:

    * Pilgrim's Bonnet (hat)

    * Pilgrim's Dress (shirt)

    * Pilgrim's Shoes (feet)
