# Tamago Sushi

Wait, this sushi doesn't even have fish in it!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **4 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **5s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Seaweed **(×1)** ,
Egg **(×3)** and
Rice **(×10)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Tamago Sushi** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2016 Day 7.
##  Function
When consumed, the player will say "YUM!" and do the :D ⦅⦆ emote.
### Silkworm Food     _Main article:Guide:Silkworm Care_
This consumable can be fed to a Silkworm to give it a grey color, thus allowing it to produce 1–10  Silk Thread - Grey per harvest.
##  Trivia
  * The Tamago Sushi is based on a real-life sushi variant of the same name.
  * In real life, fish such as salmon, tuna or other seafood are often used as the sushi's topping.[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Tamago_Sushi#cite_note-1)
    * The description of this item alludes to the fact that the item's in-game recipe does not actually include any fish-related item.
