**Fashion Show Voting Box** is a non-solid foreground block which was added as part of the Player Appreciation Week 2025 on September 25, 2025. The voting box can be found in the world FASHIONSHOWGAME.
##  Function
Punching the Fashion Show Voting Box in FASHIONSHOWGAME allows the player to vote for the participant who best matches the theme.
##  Trivia
  * On first day of the Fashion Show update,  Fashion Show Voting Box trees could appear in Harvest Moon Blasted worlds, allowing players to harvest them and drop a seed. Additionally, being rarity 1,  Fashion Show Voting Box Seeds were obtainable through breaking as well. The glitch was patched the shortly after the update was pushed, and all existing seeds were removed from the game. However, Fashion Show Voting Box blocks and seeds that were painted on Painting Easels or placed in Weather Machine - Stuff were left behind as a remark of their existence.
