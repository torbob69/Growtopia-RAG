The **Chopping Wood Block** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block that was added as part of Harvest Festival 2019.
##  Function
Chopping Wood Block is similar to the Cutting Board where one can make Wood Chips by dropping 10 Weeping Willows or Sequoia Trees and punch it with a Headsman's Axe or a Lumber Axe equipped.
