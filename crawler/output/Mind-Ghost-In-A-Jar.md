# Mind-Ghost-In-A-Jar

Spooky! This jar contains a ghost - a mental one! You can throw it to break the jar and unleash the ghost, or instead drop it gently in front of a Spirit Storage Unit, where you can keep the ghost for good!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.

The **Mind-Ghost-In-A-Jar** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the B.O.O. Update on May 1, 2018.
##  Function
Consuming a Mind-Ghost-In-A-Jar anywhere in a world will release a Mind Ghost. These items can also be dropped inside a Spirit Storage Unit to increase its percentage by 5%.
Step 4 of the  Quest For Candour requires the player to deliver 1,000 of this item.
