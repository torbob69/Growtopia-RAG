Wearing these impedes lip movement just enough to induce a Transylvanian accent.

The **Vampire Fangs** is an unsplicable face item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2013 on October 28, 2013.
##  Function
You don't vant to pronounce w's.

Your teeth has shrunk.

When equipped, the **Vampire Fangs** grants the _Transylvanian Accent_ mod, which allows the player to change their skin color to mint green. It will also turn the chat messages color into red, with all the W's changed to V's which represents the stereotypical vampire voice.
Equipping this item, alongside a Vampire Cape, Slick Black Hair and Vamp Vest, will earn one the _Costume Contest_ achievement.
##  Trivia
  * Typing /sleep command while wearing the fangs will keep the eyes of the wearer open.
  * Typing any word that begins with _W_ will change the _W_ into a _V_. Ex: _World Lock_ will be converted into _Vorld Lock_.
  * The color of the text will also be permanently red, while preventing the player to edit the color of the text that they typed in.
  * Vampire Fangs is also necessary to complete the _Costume Contest (Classic)_ achievement, which is done by equipping the full set below:
  * This item is considered a part of the Vampire set, which consists of the following items:

    * Slick Black Hair (hair)

    * Vampire Fangs (face)

    * Vamp Vest (shirt)

    * Vampire Cape (back)
