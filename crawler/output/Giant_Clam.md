The **Giant Clam** is a splicable chest foreground block which was added alongside the addition of Undersea Blast on April 11, 2014.
##  Function
Being a chest, a Giant Clam can be punched to be opened or closed. When opened, the chest will act as a non-solid block, and items can be dropped inside in order for them to be stored; when closed, nobody can see or access the items, and the chest will become a solid block. In addition, when found in an Undersea blasted world, it will contain one of the following:
  * Diving Bell
  * Wetsuit Pants
  * Wetsuit Top
