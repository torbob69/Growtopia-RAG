The **Love Bug** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2015 on February 13, 2015.
##  Function
You can move faster!

You are slow again.

When equipped, the **Love Bug** grants the _Speedy_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.
