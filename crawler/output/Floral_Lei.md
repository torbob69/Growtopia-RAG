The **Floral Lei** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of SummerFest 2017 on June 30, 2017.
##  Function
When equipped, the Floral Lei serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The Floral Lei is based on the lei, a type of floral garland or wreath traditionally given as gifts to honour people throughout the Pacific. They have since gained popularity in pop culture due to the common Hawaiian practice of presenting one to arriving or leaving tourists.
