The **Tropical Bar** is an unsplicable background block which was added as part of SummerFest 2021 on July 8, 2021.
##  Function
When placed, the Tropical Bar serves a fully aesthetic purpose. Placing two Tropical Bars next to each other will cause them to join, creating a continuous pattern.
##  Trivia
  * The Tropical Bar is one of the two blocks added during SummerFest 2021 that are obtained from the Building Blocks Machine, the other being Tropical Bar Stool.
