The **Snowtopian** is an unsplicable non-solid mannequin foreground block which was added on December 1, 2019.
##  Function
Being a mannequin, it allows clothes to be put on it by a player with access. One can also edit its message for other players to view, as if it were a sign.
Unlike the other mannequins, the Snowtopian cannot equip pants or feet items.
##  Trivia
  * When Snowtopian was first introduced to the game, there was a glitch that a **Snowtopian Seed** could be dropped when a player broke Snowtopian blocks. Due to Snowtopian being a recognizably rare item to obtain, very few seeds were obtained by the players which were planted and harvested by the players unconsciously later on. Hence, no instances of **Snowtopian Seeds** exist nowadays.

  * Attempting to use a consumable on a mannequin will generate the message: "_Must be used on a person._ "
  * Attempting to place a clothing item on a mannequin that it is already wearing will generate the message: "_You giggle as you swap two identical items._ "
  * Attempting to place an artifact item on a mannequin will generate this message: "_That would just be weird. Try putting clothes on your Mannequin instead!_ "
  * Attempting to place a clothing item with a mannequin placed in the dark will generate the message: "_It's too dark to use this mannequin!_ "
  * Attempting to place any untradeable clothing on the mannequin will generate the message: "_That item is simply too valuable to part with._ "
  * When placing any hair on the mannequin, the hair will show up in the color of the player's hair dye if any is applied.
