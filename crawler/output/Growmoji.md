# Growmoji

**Growmojis** were released as part of the Cyblocks and Growmojis update on March 1, 2018. Growmojis are icons that players can use when typing.
In order to view unlocked Growmojis, the player must wrench themselves. They can be unlocked in many different ways. For example, adding friends, being a supporter/super supporter, purchasing them from the store or purchasing a Growmoji mystery box

##  Trivia
  * During the early days of growmojis, players could type "_(;)growmoji)_ ", "_(:)growmoji)_ " or "_/me (growmoji)(growmoji)_ " to use any growmoji they wanted, including the ones they haven't unlocked yet. Since then, this glitch has been fixed.
  * As of Player Appreciation Week Day 6 on August 28, 2021, growmojis previously exclusive to purchasing monthly bundles now has a chance to be dropped from breaking a Growmoji Chest purchasable from Locke The Salesman for 150 World Locks.
