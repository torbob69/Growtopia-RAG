The **Vile Vial - Chaos Infection** is an unsplicable consumable that was added as part of Blocktor's Orders! on June 13, 2019.
##  Function
You've been infected with CHAOS!

You no longer have CHAOS!

When consumed, the player will receive the _Chaos Infection_ malady mod for 12 hours, which can be cured through a successful surgery. Should the malady get cured, both players—the surgeon and patient—will receive a prize.
##  Trivia
  * On August 4, 2022, the amount of gems received from recycling a Vile Vial - Chaos Infection was changed from 250 to 0.
