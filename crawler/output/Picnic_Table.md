The **Picnic Table** is an unsplicable non-solid seat foreground block which was added as part of SummerFest 2022 on July 28, 2022.
##  Function
When a player stands in front of a seat such as the Picnic Table for a few seconds, the player will automatically be seen "sitting" on it.
