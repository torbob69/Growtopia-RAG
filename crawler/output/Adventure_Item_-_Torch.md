The **Adventure Item - Torch** is a splicable non-solid adventure item foreground block which was added alongside the Adventure Pack on June 14, 2014.
##  Function
As a type of adventure item, the Adventure Item - Torch may work in conjunction with the Gateway To Adventure, the Adventure Brazier and Adventure Barrier. Once a player who has picked up an Adventure Item - Torch steps on an Adventure Brazier, all Braziers in the world will be lit up for 10 seconds. During this short period, all Adventure Barriers will be toggled on or off for that player only, depending on its previous state.
