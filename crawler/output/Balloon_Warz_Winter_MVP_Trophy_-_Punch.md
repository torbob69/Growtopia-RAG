The **Balloon Warz Winter MVP Trophy - Punch** is an unsplicable non-solid block introduced during Brr-lloon Warz 2018. It is a trophy awarded to players who were part of the Punch team and ranked in the top 20 for most hits during Brr-lloon Warz.
