##  Function
Nothing will stand in my way!

The scythe returns to its velvet case!

When equipped, the **Apocalypse Scythe** grants the _Punch Damage: AXE!_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. Green smoke particle will also appear on the bottom of the player. Additionally, the item replaces the player's punch with a skull projectile surrounded by green flames.
##  Trivia
  * Starting Halloween Week 2020, the Apocalypse Scythe can no longer be purchased from the Growtopia Store and is now obtainable from Ultimate Corruption by collecting corrupted souls by sacrificing items with a total rarity of at least 48,000 or higher in the different Sacrificial Wells found all around the TOMBOFGROWGANOTH.
