Honed over centuries by very bored monks and layered with precious stones and runed filigrees, this axe is a masterwork of ancient magic and EVEN MORE ANCIENT weaponsmithing. Wield it and you'll tear through foes, blocks, and butter like... um... even softer butter!

The **Jade Crescent Axe** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2018 September 21, 2018.
##  Function
Nothing will stand in my way!

The axe returns to its velvet case!

When equipped, the **Jade Crescent Axe** grants the _Punch Damage: AXE!Punch Damage_ mod.
The  Myths and Legends : The Eye of the Heavens mission requires 2 of this item.
