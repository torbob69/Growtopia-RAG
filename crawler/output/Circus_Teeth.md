The **Circus Teeth** is a splicable hair item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2025 on October 23, 2025.
##  Function
When equipped, the **Circus Teeth** serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Circus set, which consists of the following items:

    * Circus Teeth (hair)

    * Circus Eyes (face)
  * The **Circus Teeth** appear to be heavily based on Caine from The Amazing Digital Circus.
