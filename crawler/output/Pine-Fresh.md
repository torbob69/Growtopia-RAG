# Pine-Fresh

Pine-Fresh is a villain added during Super Pineapple Party Week 2015. It is an event-limited villain and can be summoned by consuming Crime Wave (chance) during Super Pineapple Party Week or by consuming a Pineapple Air Freshener.
Cards he uses

Superpower - Pineapple Spear
Superpower - Flame On!
Superpower - Frost Breath
Superpower - Super Speed
Superpower - Overcharge
Heath: 15
Method: Bring Incinerate, Overheat, Crush, Regeneration and Thunderstorm. Use **Overheat** and **Incinerate** after using **Thunderstorm** as Pine-Fresh might try to remove the effect of **Thunderstorm** with Super Speed. Use **Crush** when Pine-Fresh is exhausted as the next card he will use is Overcharge. Use **Regeneration** when low on health.
Alternative Method: Bring Incinerate, Flame On!, Crush, Regeneration and a random card. Use **Flame On!** and then **Incinerate** in the beginning. Wait until Pine-Fresh gets exhausted and use **Crush** as the next card he will use is Overcharge. If health is low, use **Regeneration**.
Upon defeating, the possible rewards to receive are:
  * Super Eye Mask - Pineapple
  * Crimefighter Mask - Pineapple
  * Superhero Mask - Pineapple
  * Super Logo - Pineapple
  * Super Cape - Pineapple
  * Super Shirt - Pineapple
  * Superheroine Top - Pineapple
  * Heroic Tights - Pineapple
  * Heroic Boots - Pineapple
  * Muscle Shirt - Pineapple
  * Superpower - Pineapple Spear
  * Villain Portrait - Pine-Fresh
  * Pineapple Head
  * Pineapple Spear
  * Pineapple Wheel
  * Pineapple Mohawk
  * Super Exploding Pineapple

The player will also receive the "Fresh Air" achievement after defeating him for the first time.
