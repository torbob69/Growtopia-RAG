The **Surgical Gloves** is an unobtainable component which was added as part of the Growtopia General Hospital update on November 22, 2013.
During surgeries they can be seen as the "Fix It" button on the bottom right of the tool kit.
##  Function
You will only see the Surgical Gloves when they are needed, such as after making Incisions to correct an internal issue (nose jobs, tumors, internal bleeding, etc), or to simply cure issues such as Hypochondria.
