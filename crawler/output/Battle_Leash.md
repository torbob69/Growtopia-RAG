# Battle Leash

This amazing octohedron can actually contain two Battle Pets, primed and ready to strike. Unequip and re-equip to swap which pet is active. Wrench a Battle Pet Cage to load a pet into the Leash. Wrench another player with a Battle Leash to battle them! You must have 2 pets in the Battle Leash to battle.
Battle Leash (Rarity: 100)

The **Battle Leash** is a splicable pet hand item which was added as part of the Pet Battles update on June 11, 2016.
##  Function
### Battle Pets
A Battle Leash can hold two sets of battle pets, each with three pet abilities. The player can swap between the two sets by unequipping and reequipping their Battle Leash.
##  Trivia
  * Selling a Battle Leash to another player doesn't exchange the player's battle pets to the other player. In order to sell one, it must be traded in a world with the pets still inside placed Battle Pet Cage.
  * This item is also worn by the  Pet Trainer, along with these items:
    * Pet Trainer Cap
    * Long Black Hair
