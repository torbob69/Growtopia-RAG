# Easter Island Head

This isn't a REAL Moai - those are much bigger and weigh thousands of pounds - but rather a cunningly-painted and carved chunk of styrofoam. Looks pretty convincing, though!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.

When placed, the Easter Island Head serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The Easter Island Head is based on the real-life moai, a group of monolithic human figures carved by the Rapa Nui people who lived on the eponymous Easter Island.
