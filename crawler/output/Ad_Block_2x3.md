Place these blocks in a 2x3 grid to show ads in your world. Check your ad earnings for your world with the Ad Control Block.

Purchase the **Advertisers Pack** for **10,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"With this pack you can build custom Ad billboards in your world. There are 3 sizes you can choose from, 2x3, 3x2 and 6x1. You need to arrange these in a grid to activate the Ad. Remember to check your Gem earnings each day using the Ad Control Block."
Every purchase will give **24** items.
The **Ad Block 2x3** is an unsplicable solid foreground block that was added as part of the Growtopia ADventures update on June 22, 2021.
##  Function
In order to fully utilize the Ad Block's function, it needs to be placed together with other Ad Blocks of the same type in a 2×3 grid. The area will then be covered with a 4×3 advertisement.
##  Trivia
  * As of September 21, 2023, the requirement of 4x3 ad blocks was changed to 2x3 to show an AD. This caused the name change.
