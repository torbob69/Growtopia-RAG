# Super Supporter

In September 2016, as part of the Systems update, a new player rank was implemented, called the **Super Supporter**. The Super Supporter rank is an upgrade from the Supporter status that can be achieved in the game by purchasing In-App Purchases or doing MyChips offers.
Compared to normal Supporters, players who have this rank have a slightly larger name display, extra skin colors, discount to broadcasts, ability to use the /warp command, and various more.
##  Requirements
One can become a Super Supporter if they meet at least **one** of the following criteria:
  * The player has earned 800,000 Gems through MyChips offers.
  * The player has spent a total of 30 USD worth of In-App Purchases.

##  Benefits
Super Supporters have extra special benefits and privileges compared to normal Supporters. They include:
###  Larger Name & National/Regional Flag
Super Supporters, by default, have a slightly larger name and national/regional flags display, which can be toggled by using the command _/hidestatus_.
Additionally, while a player has their Wrench selected, the wrench icon beside a Super Supporter's name is slightly enlarged. And instead of it being a stationary and grey wrench that of a normal player, the Super Supporter's wrench would be in the color green, and it would be gently swinging sideways. This green colour is overriden if the user applies a Wrench Style.
### Warp Command
Super Supporters can also use the /warp command, which allows them to enter worlds without having to go to the World Selection Menu. Therefore, by using the command, Super Supporters can easily hop in between worlds just by typing it into their chat. However, there is a 15 second cool-down after the command is used.
### More Skin Colors
Super Supporters gain access to 4 additional skin colors: Blue, Light Blue, Light Orange, and Translucent Red.
### Cheaper Guild Creation
Super Supporters pay half the gem price (100,000 Gems) when creating a guild compared to the 200,000 Gems of a normal Supporter.
### Cheaper Broadcasts
Super Supporters get even more discounts on Broadcasts compared to Supporters.
##  Trivia
  * The requirements for Super Supporter were changed as part of Player Appreciation Week 2021.
    * The player must earn a total of 600,000 Gems through MyChips offers, previously 80,000.
    * Or, the player may choose to spend a minimum total of 30 USD worth of In-App Purchases, previously 25 USD.
