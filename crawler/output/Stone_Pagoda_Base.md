# Stone Pagoda Base

This is a special piece - it looks just like the bottom row of a Stone Pagoda, and it even tiles properly with Stone Pagoda blocks. The trick is, it won't tile with ones below it. Use it to put a roof in the middle of your pagoda! Kind of a key feature of pagodas, right? It will also tile with Decorative Roof Dragons.
