**Popcorn** is an unsplicable consumable that was added as part of The Grand Tournament update on December 1, 2016.
##  Function
When consumed, a special eating animation will be triggered, featuring the player rapidly munching on popcorn.
Consuming a Tournament snack such as this item may give the player a random block typically earned from the lower tiers of the Prize Ladder.
