The **Lunar Drum** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Lunar New Year 2023 on January 21, 2023.
##  Function
Sound the alarm!

Put on mute.

When equipped, the **Lunar Drum** grants the _Punch Range + Punch Damage: Lunar Drum_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further and break blocks faster.
