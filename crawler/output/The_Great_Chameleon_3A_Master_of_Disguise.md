**The Great Chameleon: Master of Disguise** is an item that was released part of the Winter Clash Event.
##  Function
No one sees you now...

You are visible again!

When equipped, the **The Great Chameleon: Master of Disguise** grants the _Master of Disguise_ mod, which allows the player to blend in with the block they are standing on while idle.
##  Trivia
Attempting to pick up an item right after the disguise will display a message: _“You can’t pickup items right after the disguise! Wait for couple of seconds”_ This is made to prevent fraud involving **The Great Chameleon: Master of Disguise**
