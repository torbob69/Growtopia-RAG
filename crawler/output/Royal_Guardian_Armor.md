The **Royal Guardian Armor** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as January 2022's Item Of The Season on January 2, 2022.
##  Function
Guardians assemble.

Going it alone.

When equipped, the **Royal Guardian Armor** grants the _Punch Range + Double Jump: Royal Guardian Armor_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further and jump a second time in mid-air.
