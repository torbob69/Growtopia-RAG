# Colored Corn

Little-known fact about colored corn: It tastes nearly exactly like regular corn.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.

Participate in successfully smashing an  Ultra Pinata with a  Lucky Pinata Hat equipped during the **Pinata Bash!** event.
May give **1-3** items each time.
The **Colored Corn** is an unsplicable background block which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2019 on May 3, 2019.
##  Function
When placed, the Colored Corn serves a fully aesthetic purpose. When placed, the Colored Corn picks one between four different sprites, each featuring a different corn color.
##  Trivia
  * Corn, also known as maize, was first domesticated by indigenous peoples in southern Mexico about 10,000 years ago. The traditional maize varieties cultivated in Mexico can typically be found in a variety of colors, such as white, red, and blue.[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Colored_Corn#cite_note-1)[[2]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Colored_Corn#cite_note-2)
