Given to the four chosen **League Captains** during the Pet Battle Royale 2025.
The **Pet Battle League Organizer** is an unsplicable and unobtainable component which was added on May 15, 2025.
##  Function
The sprite for this item is used for the League Captain Title. Players with a League Captain title can toggle this on and off by wrenching oneself and clicking title. Once toggled, it shows the  Pet Battle League Organizer on the left of the player's name and moves other flags to the left.
##  Trivia
  * The four chosen League Captains for Pet Battle Royale 2025 were bluewood, erwinher, uzxi, and Wontaz.
