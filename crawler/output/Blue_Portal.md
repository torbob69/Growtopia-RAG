The **Blue Portal** is a non-solid portal foreground block which was added as part of Growtopia's launch.
##  Function
Being a type of portal, the Blue Portal immediately moves the player to another tile when touched. A block with an ID must first be placed on the tile for this to work.

##  Trivia
  * The Blue Portal and its recolor, the Orange Portal, are based on the eponymous portals found in the puzzle-platform video games series Portal.
    * The description of the Blue Portal is a reference to a cake found as a recurring element in the series.
  * The Orange and Blue Portal were seemingly added based on the suggestion of a player,[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Blue_Portal#cite_note-1) who wanted them to function similarly to the Time-Space Rupture.
