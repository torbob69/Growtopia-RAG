The **Master Magic** is an unsplicable artifact item which was added as Septembers 2022's Item Of The Season on September 1, 2022.
##  Function
Got the magic in me!

Houdini!

When equipped, the **Master Magic** grants the _Double Jump: Master Magic_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
