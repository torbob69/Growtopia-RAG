# List of Super Logos

**Super logos** are chest items which feature a small logo emblazoned on the player's torso. Most super logos take the color of the shirt the player is wearing.
This is a list of all super logos which are currently in the game.
##  Basic
These super logos are obtained from purchasing a Superhero Pack and/or defeating any kind of villain.
