The **Cherry Blossom Tree** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block that was added as part of Harvest Moon Festival 2018 on September 21, 2018.
##  Function
When placed, the Cherry Blossom Tree serves a purely aesthetic purpose. Particles of pink leaves fall out whenever a player passes by.
