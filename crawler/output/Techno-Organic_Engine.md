# Techno-Organic Engine

What has science DONE?! By fusing the verdant powers of nature and the chaotic energies of the void, you've managed to create a device capable of breaking blocks OR harvesting trees, and then collecting a target drop, all in one go! Just fuel it with enough Gems, set a target, and let 'er rip! Note: Blocks broken by this device will not drop gems! It's convenient, but inefficient!

**Techno-Organic Engine** is an unsplicable foreground block which was added in the Role Up! update. One can be crafted after reaching Builder Level 10.
##  Function
When wrenched, it lets the player add Gems which gives the option to harvest trees or break blocks automatically starting from the top-left up to the bottom-right of the world with a rate of 1 gem per 1.5 seconds. It also collects items with a capacity of 3,000, but does not have a remote unlike the MAGPLANT 5000 Remote.
