##  Function
You can fly!! Well, you can jump higher than normal.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Astronaut Pack** grants the _High Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump one tile higher.

    * Space Suit (shirt)

    * Space Pants (pants)

    * Moon Boots (feet)

    * Astronaut Pack (back)
  * The Astronaut Pack is the only item in the astronaut set which grants a playmod.
