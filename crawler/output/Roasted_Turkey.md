# Roasted Turkey

The centerpiece of every feast! What better way to celebrate a season of thanks than with a gorgeous roast turkey? Smells divine, tastes even finer!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **4 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **5s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Combustion

Burn a(n)  Dead Thanksgiving Turkey **(x1)** in a  Highly Combustible Box.
Turkey Sucker

| A Turkey Sucker has a 10% chance to explode after capturing a Thanksgiving Turkey, which will give:
•  Organic Turkey ×1 (slightly rare)
•  Roasted Turkey ×6–11 (common)   |
There is also a small chance that a  Turkey Feather will be left behind when a Turkey is caught, regardless if the sucker explodes or not.
The **Roasted Turkey** is an unsplicable consumable item that was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2018 on November 22, 2018.
