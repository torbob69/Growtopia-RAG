Combines a fairly boring color with a moderately boring building material to create... a somewhat boring wall. Huzzah.

The **White Brick Wall** is an unsplicable background block which was added as part of the B.O.O. Update on May 1, 2018.
##  Function
When placed, the White Brick Wall serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * As part of the Block Party 2 update, the quantity of White Brick Walls obtained from a Spirit Storage Unit explosion was increased from 1 to 10.
