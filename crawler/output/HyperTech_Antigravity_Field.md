# HyperTech Antigravity Field

Gravity got you down? Well, thanks to this salvaged HyperTech artifact, you can kick it to the curb and float in freedom! As long as you stay inside the antigrav field, you and the ground will be nothing more than distant acquaintances!

The **HyperTech Antigravity Field** is a non-solid foreground block which was added on 8 August 2018, the third day of Player Appreciation Week 2018.
##  Function
A player will be able to jump indefinitely while they are standing on the same tile as this block.
