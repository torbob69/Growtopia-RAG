The **Mighty Tiger** is an unsplicable feet item which was added alongside the Startopia update.
##  Function
Hear me roar!

Meee-oww!

When equipped, the **Mighty Tiger** grants the _Eye of the Tiger_ mod, which allows the player to jump one tile higher. Additionally, a translucent tiger mask will appear on the player's face, and their punching animation will be replaced by tiger slashes.
