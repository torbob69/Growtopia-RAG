The **Chaos Corruption** is an unsplicable shirt item that was added as part of the Blocktor's Orders! update.
##  Function
Fueled by dark energy!

The darkness has left you! ...for now...

When equipped, the **Chaos Corruption** grants the _Chaos Corruption!_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land. Additionally, this item can stack with up to two other Speedy clothing items.
