# Crystals (update)

Growtopia Gazette
> Jan 20th: Have a Geiger Counter? You might find something beautiful and strange out there... but what does it do?
> NEW: Crystals. But how do they work?
> NEW ITEM SPONSORED BY: v0ren, Dupie and Contesso

**Crystals** is an update which was introduced on January 20, 2015. It introduced five crystals that can be found using a Geiger Counter: the Green Crystal, Red Crystal, Blue Crystal, White Crystal and Black Crystal. These crystals could be harmonized together to make exclusive items.
