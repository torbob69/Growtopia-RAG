The **Lotus Lamp** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable animated foreground block which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2020 on September 24, 2020.
##  Function
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Lotus Lamp can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Lotus Lamp will change its appearance, showing a 2-framed animation of itself providing light to its surrounding. As a light source, a Lotus Lamp emits light in dark areas.
The  Cinco De Mayo Exclusive Mission - So you really are an artist! mission requires 1 of this item.
