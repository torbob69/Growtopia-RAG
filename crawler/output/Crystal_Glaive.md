##  Function
You're in danger of losing a finger.

You got through with all digits intact!

When equipped, the **Crystal Glaive** grants the _Cool_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further. It will also replace the player's punching animation into throwing glaives.
Step 19 of the  Quest For The Blade requires the player to deliver 20 of this item.
