The **Haunted Pants** is an unsplicable pants item which was added as part of the Battlers Of the Otherworldly update on July 15, 2016.
##  Function
When equipped, a pet ghost will appear and follow the player around. Just like normal ghosts, this pet can only be seen with Spectral Goggles or a Synthetic Oculum.
