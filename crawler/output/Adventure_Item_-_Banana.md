The **Adventure Item - Banana** is a splicable non-solid adventure item foreground block which was added alongside the Adventure Pack on June 14, 2014.
##  Function
As a type of adventure item, the Adventure Item - Banana may work in conjunction with the Gateway To Adventure and the Angry Adventure Gorilla. The Angry Adventure Gorilla acts as a deadly block. When a player who has picked up an Adventure Item - Banana punches an Angry Adventure Gorilla, the gorilla will "eat" the banana and let the player pass for ten seconds.
