The **Safe Vault** is an unsplicable foreground block which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2019 Day 5 on August 23, 2019.
##  Function
To use, the player can set a password, along with a recovery answer that can be used to unlock the vault. Upon opening the vault with the password, players can store up to 20 items by clicking on the "Deposit Item" button and remove items by clicking on the item to retrieve. If the player forgets the password to the vault, they can enter a recovery answer that was previously set.
##  Trivia
  * As of Player Appreciation Week 2021 Day 6, the Safe Vault was removed from the Growtopia Store and now can be purchased from Locke for 6 World Locks.
