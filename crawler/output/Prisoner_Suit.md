The **Prisoner Suit** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2023 Day 5 on September 1, 2023.
##  Function
When equipped, the Prisoner Suit serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Prisoner set, which consists of the following items:

    * Prisoner Cap (Hat)

    * Prisoner Suit (Shirt)
