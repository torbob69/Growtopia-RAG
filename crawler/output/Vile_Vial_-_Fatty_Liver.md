# Vile Vial - Fatty Liver

Filled with the ultra-caloric output of only the most perfect cooking, this deadly vial will afflict ANYONE it touches with a Fatty Liver! (Except if they already have another malady, of course - these diseases are very polite in that regard)
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: Full Collision
Hardness: **20 Hits** **15 Hits** – Restores after **8s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Cooking

Possible bonus reward from cooking a food item with a quality of 95% or greater.
The **Vile Vial - Fatty Liver** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Blocktor's Orders! on June 13, 2019.
##  Function
You've been infected with fatty liver!

You no longer have fatty liver!

When consumed, the player will receive the _Fatty Liver_ malady mod for 12 hours, which can be cured through a successful surgery. Should the malady get cured, both players—the surgeon and patient—will receive a prize.
##  Trivia
  * On August 4, 2022, the amount of gems received from recycling a Vile Vial - Fatty Liver was changed from 250 to 0.
