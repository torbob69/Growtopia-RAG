The **Mystery Lens** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2025 on September 18, 2025.
##  Function
When consumed, it will spawn the _Hidden in Plain Sight!_ special event. Players have 90 seconds to locate a randomly spawned block, and the first to punch it wins one of the following prizes:
  * Flowstone
  * Honeycomb
  * Lightning Horns
  * Pine Needles
  * Spiezels' Galactic Scarf
  * Stone Chimney
  * Swordfish Sword
  * Wings of Dune
  * Zodiac Ring
  * Zodiac Wings
