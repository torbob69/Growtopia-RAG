The **Subway Stairs** is an unsplicable platform foreground block which was added as part of the Aliens update on November 14, 2021.
##  Function
A Subway Stairs behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath.
##  Trivia
  * As part of the Aliens update on January 27, 2023, the quantity of Subway Stairs obtained from consuming an Alien Landing Pod was increased from 2 to 10.
