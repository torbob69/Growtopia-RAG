# Blue Awning

These are surprisingly bouncy. Hold Jump as you land for maximum bounce.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.

Sewing

With a  Sewing Machine...
Start with a  Silk Bolt - Blue.
Sew a  Silk Bolt - Black to the previous bolt using a(n) **Triple Stretch Stitch**.
Lastly, sew a  Silk Bolt - Aqua to the previous bolt using a(n) **Triple Stretch Stitch**.
Each recipe gives **5** items.
The **Blue Awning** is an unsplicable platform trampoline foreground block which was added as part of the Night of the Sewing Dead update.
##  Function
A Blue Awning behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump past through it from underneath. Moreover, it also counts as a trampoline block. Jumping on one will cause the player to bounce, and by holding the jump button, the player can jump even higher. The _High Jump_ mod also boosts one's jump height on a trampoline block.
##  Trivia
  * This item is one of the four awnings added in the update, alongside the Black Awning, Green & Yellow Awning and Red & White Awning.
