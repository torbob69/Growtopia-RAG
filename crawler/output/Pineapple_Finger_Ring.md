The **Pineapple Finger Ring** is an unsplicable pet hand item which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2017 on June 8, 2017.
##  Function
When equipped, it will summon a Pineapple Finger Ring next to the player and follows the player around. It can be trained as an Earth Battle Pet.
