# Lucky Pendant

This pendant symbolizes two snakes eating themselves. Whatever that means, it also causes a luck dragon to follow you around! Note: it's the dragon that's lucky, not you. I guess you're just lucky to get to hang around with it!

The **Lucky Pendant** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2016 on March 11, 2016.
##  Function
You command a mighty (lucky) dragon!

Your dragon has abandoned you.

When equipped, this item grants the _Lucky Dragon_ mod, which summons a white dragon that flies around the player and shoots green fire, much like the Skeletal Dragon Claw.
