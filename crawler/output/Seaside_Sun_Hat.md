The **Seaside Sun Hat** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of SummerFest 2024 on July 11, 2024.
##  Function
Walking on sunshine!

I don't feel good!

When equipped, the **Seaside Sun Hat** grants the _Fireproof : Seaside Sun Hat_ mod, which allows the player to take 50% less damage from lava pain blocks.
##  Trivia
  * The description is a reference to the chorus from the 1969 song Here Comes The Sun by the Beatles.
  * The equip message of the Seaside Sun Hat, "Walking on sunshine!" is a reference to the 1985 song of the same name released by the British rock band Katrina and the Waves.
