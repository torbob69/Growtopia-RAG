The **Swimming Goggles** is an unsplicable face item which was added as part of SummerFest 2024 on July 11, 2024.
##  Function
Dive, Dive, Dive!

Surface Dweller!

When equipped, the **Swimming Goggles** grants the _Speedy in Water : Swimming Goggled_ mod, which allows the player to move faster in water.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Swimming set, which consists of the following items:

    * Swimming Cap (Hair)

    * Swimming Goggles (Face)
