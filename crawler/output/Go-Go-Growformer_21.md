The **Go-Go-Growformer!** is an unsplicable feet item. One can be obtained from purchasing it from the store for 200,000 gems as December 2018's Item Of The Month.
##  Function
Let's roll out!

Energy cube drained!

When equipped, the **Go-Go-Growformer!** grants the _Speedy: Go-Go!_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and switch between its wearable form and its morph form by wrenching oneself and clicking 'Transform'. Additionally, Players can also transform into the robot form upon consuming the Growformer PowerKey. While in the robot mod, it will replace the player's punching animation with exploding rockets, similar to the Legendbot-009.
##  Trivia
  * The Go-Go-Growformer!'s design and transformation effect is a reference to the Transformers.
  * As of the Player Appreciation Week update on August 16, 2022, player can now switch between the wearable item and its morph by wrenching oneself and clicking "Transform".
