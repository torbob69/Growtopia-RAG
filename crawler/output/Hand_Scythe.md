This creepy blade can chop down trees with great precision - you'll get twice as much fruit! The only downside is that it's highly likely to break when used. It has no effect (but won't break either!) on trees of rarity 100 or more.

The **Hand Scythe** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2013 on September 18, 2013.
##  Function
When equipped, the **Hand Scythe** allows the player to double the drops earned from harvesting trees below rarity 100, as well as drop a rare Harvest Festival-related item as a bonus.
This effect is similar to the Harvester and the Harvester Of Sorrows, wearing both items do not stack with each other. It will also have a high chance of breaking when used.

##  Trivia
  * Prior to Harvest Festival 2014, players had to combine 3 Harmony Mooncakes, 3 Longevity Mooncakes and 3 Songpyeons in a Chemical Combiner to obtain a Hand Scythe. Players could also obtain three Hand Scythes from the Harvest Pack.
    * Hand Scythes could also be used on trees with a rarity of 100 or more during this period.
