# Adventure Item (disambiguation)

This is a **disambiguation** page.
There are more than one subjects in the **Growtopia Wiki** using this name.
Adventure Items are items which players are able to punch and use during their time in the world. They can be used on other blocks such as Gateway To Adventures, Locked Adventure Doors, Angry Adventure Gorillas, Tomb Robbers, Adventure Idol Gates, Adventure Rope Pitons or Adventure Braziers.
