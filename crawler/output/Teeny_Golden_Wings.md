# Teeny Golden Wings

Even as tiny as they are, these wings allow you to jump in mid-air!

Golden Heart Crystal **(×1)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Teeny Golden Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2015.
##  Function
You can jump in mid-air! It's like flying, but less.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Teeny Golden Wings** grants the _Double Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
