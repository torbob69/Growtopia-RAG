##  Trivia
  * The appearance of the Sequoia Tree imitates the trunk of a Sequoiadendron giganteum, commonly known as the giant sequoia.
  * A Sequoia Tree's sprite connects with the sprites of other Sequoia Trees located beside it, above it, or below it.
