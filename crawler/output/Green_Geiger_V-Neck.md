The **Green Geiger V-Neck** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as part of the Geiger Day update on May 5, 2017, the same day Cinco De Mayo Week 2017 was released.
##  Function
When equipped, the Green Geiger V-Neck serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
Geiger V-Necks come in 3 other colors:  Orange Geiger V-Neck,  Blue Geiger V-Neck, and  Purple Geiger V-Neck.
