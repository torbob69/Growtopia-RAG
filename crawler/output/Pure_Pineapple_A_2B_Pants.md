The **Pure Pineapple A+ Pants** is an unsplicable pants item which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2021 on June 3, 2021.
##  Function
When equipped, the Pure Pineapple A+ Pants serves a fully cosmetic purpose.

    * Pure Pineapple A+ Scarf (Chest)
