The **Organ Piano** is an unsplicable toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block which was added as part of Halloween Week 2021 on October 21, 2021.
##  Function
<https://growtopiawiki.com/w/File:Spooky_organ.ogg>The Organ Piano's music.
Being a toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block, an Organ Piano can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, an Organ Piano will change its appearance, showing a 4-framed animation, and play a piece of music.
