The **Sheet Music: Flat Mexican Trumpet** is a splicable sheet music background block which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2021 on May 5, 2021.
##  Function
Sheet Music: Flat Mexican Trumpet is a sheet music that plays a flat mexican trumpet note, depending on its position vertically. Its note is half-step lower than that of Sheet Music: Mexican Trumpet's.
