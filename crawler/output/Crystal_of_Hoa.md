The **Crystal of Hoa** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2021 on September 16, 2021. It was only made obtainable from September 18, 2021 onwards.
##  Function
Blooming Awesome!

Wilted!

When equipped, the **Crystal of Hoa** grants the _Punch Damage & Punch Power: Crystal of Hoa!_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and knock back other players further. It will also leave a trail of flowers behind as the player walks.
