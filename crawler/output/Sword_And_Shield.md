# Sword And Shield

This is a classic warrior's combo - a nice cutlass for your main hand, and a shield in the other. The shield makes you harder to knock back!
Properties
This item can kill zombies during a Pandemic!
This item never drops any seeds.

The **Sword and Shield** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the Daily Challenges update on November 18, 2015.
##  Function
You slice and dice!

Back to punching.

When equipped, the **Sword And Shield** grants the _Slasher_ mod, which allows the player to resist a percentage of knockback when punched by other players. It will also replace the player's punching animation with a slashing effect.
