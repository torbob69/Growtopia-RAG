The dragon is the fifth of the zodiac animals. Limited only to the Year of the Dragon! Myth has it, that the order of the Zodiac Animals, was determined by a race. The dragon placed fifth and was immediately noticed by the Jade Emperor!!

##  Function
Oooh it sparkles!

I miss the sparkles...

When equipped, the Year of the Dragon Mask grants the _Year of the Dragon Mask: Sparkles_ mod, which emits sparkles from the player's body.
