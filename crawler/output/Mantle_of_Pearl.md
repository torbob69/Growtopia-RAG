The **Mantle of Pearl** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of Beach Party 2026 on June 18, 2026.
##  Function
Shining, shimmering, splendid!

Too shell-fish!

When equipped, the **Mantle of Pearl** grants the _Double Jump + Light Source: Mantle of Pearl_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and emit light in dark areas.
