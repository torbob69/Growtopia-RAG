The **Octopus Head** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2016 Day 7 on August 11, 2016.
##  Function
When equipped, the Octopus Head serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
The  The Seven Seas : A Tale of Tentacles! mission requires 200 of this item.
##  Trivia
  * Obtaining an Octopus Head from a Lobster Trap is one of Crazy Jim's Epic Quests.
