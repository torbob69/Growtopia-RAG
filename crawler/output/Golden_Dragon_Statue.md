# Golden Dragon Statue

Congratulations, you won the Grand Tournament!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can only be used in World-Locked worlds.
This item can't be destroyed - smashing it will return it to your backpack if you have room!
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.

Grand Tournament

One can be obtained by placing 1st in the monthly Grand Tournament.
The **Golden Dragon Statue** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of the Grand Tournament update on December 1, 2016.
##  Function
When placed, the Golden Dragon Statue serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
Step 16 of the  Quest For The Sky requires the player to deliver 1 of this item. 1 Neptune's Armor may also be used as an alternative.
##  Trivia
  * As part of the Quality of Life Changes update on May 20, 2022, the Golden Dragon Statue is no longer placeable in public worlds and worlds the player is an admin in.
