The **Celestial Wrench Decoration** is a wrench decoration which was added as part of the August Season on August 1, 2025.
##  Function
When equipped through the 'Wrench Customization' tab when wrenching oneself, the Celestial Wrench Decoration adds star particles to the lower left of the player's wrench connected to their name.
