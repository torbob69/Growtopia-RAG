Contained in this crown is pure Ultraviolet Energy. It's erratic nature leads to a crystalized fragment look that channels the pieces used for its creation.

##  Function
Time to spike things up!

Out of energy!

When equipped, the **Ultraviolet Crown** grants the _Build Range + Light Source: Ultraviolet Crown_ mod, which allows the player to build one tile further and emit light in dark areas.
