The **Techno Grass** is an unsplicable solid foreground block which was added as part of the Aliens update on November 14, 2021.
##  Function
When placed, Techno Grass serves a fully aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The  Techno Grass' design is based on  Dirt.
  * As part of the Aliens update on January 27, 2023, the quantity of Techno Grass obtained from consuming an Alien Landing Pod was increased from 5 to 20.
