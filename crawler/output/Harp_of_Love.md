The **Harp of Love** is an unsplicable hand item which were added as part of Valentine's Week 2024 on February 14, 2024.
##  Function
The sound of music!

The sound of silence!

When equipped, the **Harp of Love** grants the _Punch Range: Harp of Love_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further.
