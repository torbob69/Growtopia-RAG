Crafted to be the sharpest vegetable on the market, the Carrot Lance is said to be the strongest blade there is!

The **Carrot Lance** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Easter Week 2023 on April 7, 2023.
##  Function
Superior eyesight!

I can't see clearly now!

When equipped, the **Carrot Lance** grants the _Punch Damage + Punch Range: Carrot Lance_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and punch one tile further.
