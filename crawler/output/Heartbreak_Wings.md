The **Heartbreak Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2022 on February 11, 2022.
##  Function
Break some hearts!

You broke the wings!

When equipped, the **Heartbreak Wings** grants the _Double Jump : Heartbreak Wings!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
