The **Halloween Lantern** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block which was added as part of Halloween Week 2021 on October 21, 2021.
##  Function
Being a toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block, a Halloween Lantern can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Halloween Lantern will change its appearance, showing a 3-framed animation of itself providing light to its surrounding. As a light source, a Halloween Lantern emits light in dark areas.
