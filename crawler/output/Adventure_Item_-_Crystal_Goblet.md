Place this in your world to be used in your Gateways To Adventure! Players can pick it up by punching it. They lose it if they leave your world. Unlike other Adventure Items, this one breaks if you die while holding it!

The **Adventure Item - Crystal Goblet** is a splicable non-solid adventure item foreground block which was added as part of The Adventure Pack Strikes Back! update on June 1, 2017.
##  Function
As a type of adventure item, the Adventure Item - Crystal Goblet may work in conjunction with the Gateway To Adventure. In addition, when a player who has picked up an Adventure Item - Crystal Goblet gets damaged, such as from touching lava pain blocks, pain blocks or deadly blocks, the player will lose their Adventure Item - Crystal Goblet.
