# BLARNEY

The **BLARNEY** maps are worlds which are only accessible during St. Patrick's Week, which players can earn Blarney Pebbles and a limited St. Patrick's Week item upon completion.
All the BLARNEY worlds are parkours, and if a player completes the world, they can "kiss" (walk past) the Blarney Stone to receive a Blarney Pebble, a maximum of once every day. Multiple versions of each world have been created to allow more players to join BLARNEY, and to reduce lag.
The world **BLARNEY** is not a BLARNEY world, but is a world which links players to all BLARNEY worlds, BLARNEY1-8. The world is owned by former moderator Greenswe.
Upon entering a BLARNEY world when it isn't St. Patrick's Week, the player will enter a world with two signs saying "_Aye, 'tis a fine day for not doing parkour!"_ and "_Sure 'n begorrah, the Blarney will return!"_

Due the maximum people allowed in a world, every time one of the maps are inputted, the server will automatically add (insert number here) followed by a random number once you enter it. This is similar to Growganoth, Growch, The Carnival and The Grand Tournament.
**BLARNEY9** was released for 2018's St. Patrick's Week, featuring the use of CyBots in an official BLARNEY world for the first time. It was revealed through one of Growtopia's tutorials. **BLARNEY10** was released for 2023's St. Patrick's Week, featuring the use of Conveyor Blocks.
As of St. Patrick's Week 2022, a Password Door can be seen nearing the end of every BLARNEY world with the question _"Which BLARNEY world are you in?"' to slow down hackers from automatically completing the parkour.[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/BLARNEY#cite_note-1)_
