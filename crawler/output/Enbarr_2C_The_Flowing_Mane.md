The **Enbarr, The Flowing Mane** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2024 on March 14, 2024.
##  Function
Makin' waves!

Wiped Out!

When equipped, the **Enbarr, The Flowing Mane** grants the _Speedy + Surfing: Enbarr, The Flowing Mane_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and float on water for three seconds.
##  Trivia
  * Enbarr, The Flowing Mane is a reference to a creature called _Énbarr_, a mythical creature from Irish mythology which could traverse both land and sea, and was swifter than wind-speed.
