The **Hair of Fionn** is an unsplicable hair item which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2024 on March 14, 2024.
##  Function
So handsome!

So ugly!

When equipped, the **Hair of Fionn** grants the _Build Range + Light Source: Hair of Fionn_ mod, which allows the player to build one tile further and emit light in dark areas.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Fionn set, which consists of the following items:

    * Hair of Fionn (Hair)

    * Tunic of Fionn (Shirt)
  * The full Fionn set is a reference to Fionn mac Cumhaill, who is a heroic figure from Irish mythology.
  * Hair of Fionn is a recolor of the  Winter Swept Hair.
