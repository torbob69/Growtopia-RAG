The **Super Squirt Gun Jetpack** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Balloon Warz.
##  Function
You can fly!! Well, you can jump higher than normal.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Super Squirt Gun Jetpack** grants the _High Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump one tile higher and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
Step 7 of the  Quest For Candour requires the player to deliver 10 of this item.
##  Trivia
  * Super Squirt Gun Jetpack provides slow fall effect as well, although it is not stated in its label. Similar to the  Soda Jetpack and  Super Squirt Gun Jetpack Pants.
  * Prior to Brr-lloon Warz 2020, the Super Squirt Gun Jetpack could be obtained by reaching the 2nd until 5th position in the Balloon Warz hourly challenge.
