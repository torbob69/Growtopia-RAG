The **Legendbot-009** is an unspicable shirt item that was introduced during the Legendary update on August 22, 2014.
##  Function
You are piloting a legendary robot suit.

You feel naked.

When equipped, the **Legendbot-009** grants the _Legendary!_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster, move faster on land, punch one tile further, knock back other players further, and jump a second time in mid-air. Additionally, all other clothing items gets unequipped (except face, hair, and hat slot) and replaces the player's fist into shooting explosive rockets when punching.
##  Quest Steps
  1. Deliver 2000 Chemical G.
  2. Kill 100 people in PvP.
  3. Smash 5000 blocks.
  4. Deliver 600 Robot Wants Dubstep.
  5. Plant trees adding up to 50,000 rarity.
  6. Earn 50 Growtoken.
  7. Deliver 3 Edison Zoomsters.
  8. Earn 10,000 XP.
  9. Deliver 1,000 High Tech Blocks.
  10. Deliver 100,000 Gems.
  11. Smash 100,000 rarity worth of blocks.
  12. Successfully perform surgery 100 times.
  13. Collect 1,000 items from providers (e.g. Cow).
  14. Deliver 3 Bride Of Reanimator Remote.
  15. Harvest 100,000 rarity worth of fruit from trees.
  16. Deliver a Mint Julep or Neptune's Chariot
  17. Deliver 5 Kerjigger.
  18. Deliver 5 Doohickey.
  19. Deliver 2 Thingamabobs.
  20. Deliver the Legendary Orb.

##  Trivia
  * noctisx was the first player to complete the Quest Of Steel and receive the Legendbot-009.
  * On July 8, 2021 step 16 was changed to allow players to use a Neptune's Chariot in place of a Mint Julep.
