The **Wooden Pagoda Door** is an unsplicable non-solid door foreground block which was introduced during The Grand Tournament update. It can be obtained from either the Prize Ladder or from eating Tournament snacks.
##  Function
As a door, the Wooden Pagoda Door can be entered (via punching) by a player, moving them instantly to another tile. A block with an ID must first be placed on the tile for this to work.
##  Trivia
  * 4 Wooden Pagoda Doors can be found in the official Grand Tournament world TOURNAMENT. Two of which resides below and above the pagoda towers on the left and right side of the Main Door to go to the top or bottom.
