The **Tropical Fruit Crate** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2023 on June 9, 2023.
##  Function
When broken, a Tropical Fruit Crate drops the following items:
  * Pineapple ×15-40
  * Papaya ×1-11
  * Kiwi ×1-11
  * Watermelon ×1-11
  * Dragon Fruit ×1
