**King of Hearts** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2021 on February 13, 2021.
##  Function
When equipped, the King of Hearts serves a fully cosmetic purpose and summons a King of Hearts looking pet that follows the player around.
##  Trivia
  * This item's name and description are a reference to Queen of Hearts from Alice's Adventures in Wonderland.
