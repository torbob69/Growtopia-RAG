##  Function
When placed, the Super Pineapple Window serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The complete super pineapple house set consists of the  Super Pineapple House,  Super Pineapple Door, and  Super Pineapple Window. These blocks are inspired by the pineapple house inhabited by the titular protagonist of the animated television series _SpongeBob SquarePants_.
