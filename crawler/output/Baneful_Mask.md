##  Function
This item has the appearance of a metallic mask which covers the player's mouth and some other parts of their face. As of version 1.12, the mask gives out puffs of smoke, which emulates heavy breathing.
##  Trivia
  * This mask's name and appearance is based on Bane, a fictional supervillain appearing in comic books by DC Comics.
  * This item is worn by Dr. Destructo along with these items:
    * Baneful Mask
    * Pinstripe Suit
    * Pinstripe Pants
    * Boots
    * Destructo Ray
