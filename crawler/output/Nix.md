##  Function
Psychopomping ain't easy!

Just helping out, really!

When equipped, the **Nix** grants the _Double Jump + Punch Range: Nix_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air, punch one tile further, and switch between its wearable form and its morph form by wrenching oneself and clicking 'Transform'.
