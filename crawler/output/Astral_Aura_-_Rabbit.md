The **Astral Aura - Rabbit** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2022 on September 9, 2022.
##  Function
Double Power!

Back to One!

When equipped, the **Astral Aura - Rabbit** grants the _Double Jump: Astral Aura - Rabbit!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
##  Trivia
  * The **Astral Aura - Rabbit** is one of the three Astral Auras released in Harvest Festival 2022, others include Astral Aura - Lion and Astral Aura - Dragon.
