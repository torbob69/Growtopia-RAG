The **Ecto Pack** is a back item that was added as part of Halloween Week 2014 on October 30, 2014.
##  Function
An Ecto Pack may be used to power up a Flamethrower. A single Ecto Pack will be consumed with each use of the Flamethrower. Using a Flamethrower while equipping an Ecto Pack will replace the player's punch with blue flames.
An Ecto Pack can also be used with a Harvester Of Sorrows (but not the other types of harvesters); there will be a slight chance that a harvested tree (with a rarity of less than 100) will drop a Devilfruit or Teeny Tiny Skull. The Ecto Pack will be consumed every time this occurs. Unlike the Fuel Pack, the Ecto Pack does not double fruit drops when harvesting trees.
Firing a T-Shirt Cannon while equipping an Ecto Pack will give the targeted player a Ghost Fan Shirt, while firing an Easter Egg Cannon while equipping one will give an Easter Egg - Ghost.
