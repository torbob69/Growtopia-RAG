The **Unhelpful Hand** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2025 on October 23, 2025.
##  Function
Deranged!

De-arranged!

When equipped, the **Unhelpful Hand** grants the _Double Jump + Slow Fall: Unhelpful Hand_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
##  Trivia
  * Unhelpful Hand is a redesigned version of the  Helping Hand.
