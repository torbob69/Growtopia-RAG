The **Sun Shooter Bow** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2020 on September 24, 2020.
##  Function
May the light of the sun empower you

Burned-out!

When equipped, the **Sun Shooter Bow** grants the _Sun Shooter Bow : Punch Damage & Punch Range_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and punch one tile further.
Step 9 of the  Quest Of The Owl requires the player to deliver 10 of this item.
