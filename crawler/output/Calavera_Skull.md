The **Calavera Skull** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2022 on May 5, 2022.
##  Function
When placed, the Calavera Skull serves a fully aesthetic purpose.
The  Cinco De Mayo Exclusive Mission - So you really are an artist! mission requires 50 of this item.
##  Trivia
  * The Mexican Spanish term _calavera_ ("skull") is most often applied to edible or decorative skulls made from sugar or clay that are used in the Mexican celebration of the Day of the Dead.[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Calavera_Skull#cite_note-1)
