# WOLFWORLD

**WOLFWORLD** is a world introduced during the second day of Player Appreciation Week 2015. It can only be entered by consuming a Wolf Whistle and players have ten minutes to complete the parkour in the world. There are nine different WOLFWORLDs and upon completing any seven, the player will receive a Challenge of Fenrir which can be used to enter the Halls of Valhowla during All Howl's Eve.
##  WOLFWORLD PRIZES
_Further information:WOLFWORLD Rewards_
Upon completing a WOLFWORLD, the player will receive one prize (or two if the Daily Bonus is unlocked and is the first completed world of the day) and a Wolf Ticket.
##  Trivia
  * Players can check how close they are to obtaining a Challenge of Fenrir by wrenching themselves, clicking the the _Goals & Quests_ button, and looking at 'The Trial of the Wolf.’
    * A "-" represents the number of WOLFWORLDs left until you obtain a Challenge of Fenrir, while an "X" represents how many WOLFWORLDs you've completed towards a Challenge of Fenrir.
