# Summer Event Player Medal: Gold

Awarded for achieving FIRST PLACE in the Player Leaderboards during the Summer season. Show off your victory by placing this medal in your world, and congratulations!
Properties
This item can't be destroyed - smashing it will return it to your backpack if you have room!
This item cannot be dropped or traded.
This item never drops any seeds.

##  Function
A Summer Event Player Medal: Gold behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath.
Step 18 of the  Quest For Candour requires the player to deliver 1 of this item.
