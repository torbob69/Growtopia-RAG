The **Unlucky Kitty** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block that was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2017 on March 17, 2017.
##  Function
When placed, the Unlucky Kitty serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The Unlucky Kitty's appearance is based on Maneki-neko, a common Japanese figurine which is often believed to bring good luck to the owner.
