The **Guardian Lion Statue** is an unsplicable foreground block which was added as part of Lunar New Year 2022 on February 1, 2022.
##  Function
When placed, the Guardian Lion Statue serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The Guardian Lion Statue is based on Chinese guardian lions, a traditional Chinese architectural ornament typically made of stone.
