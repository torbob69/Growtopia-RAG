The **Pineapple Aura** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2021 on June 3, 2021.
##  Function
Majestically floating like a pineapple!

Outta Juice!

When equipped, the **Pineapple Aura** grants the _Double Jump : Pineapple Aura_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
