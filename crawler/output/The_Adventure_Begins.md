**The Adventure Begins** is a splicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of The Adventure Pack Strikes Back! update on June 1, 2017.
##  Function
When a player crosses it, a custom message will be sent to the player, and all of the adventure items owned by the player will be reset. A number of lives or a timer can be assigned for players. Reaching an Adventure's End will finish the adventure and reset all owned adventure items again.
