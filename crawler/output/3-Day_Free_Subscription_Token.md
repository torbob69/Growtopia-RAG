Consume this for three full days of special treatment! You'll get a chance of doubling any XP earned, 10 season tokens (as long as there's a seasonal clash running), and 100 gems every day!

The **3-Day Premium Subscription Token** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2018 Day 5 on August 8, 2018.
##  Function
When consumed, the player will receive a free subscription for 3 days. They will receive 10 season tokens (if there is one running) and 100 gems every day.
##  Trivia
  * This item was removed during the WinterFest 2019 update along with the 14-Day Free Subscription Token.
