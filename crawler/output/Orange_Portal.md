The **Orange Portal** is a non-solid portal foreground block which was added as part of Growtopia's launch.
##  Function
Being a type of portal, the Orange Portal immediately moves the player to another tile when touched. A block with an ID must first be placed on the tile for this to work.

##  Trivia
  * The Orange Portal and its recolor, the Blue Portal, are based on the eponymous portals found in the puzzle-platform video games series _Portal_.
    * The description of the Orange Portal is a reference to a quote from Portal 2, the second game in the series, which says, "_Okay. Look. We both said a lot of things that you're going to regret. But I think we can put our differences behind us. For science. You monster._ "
  * The Orange and Blue Portal were seemingly added based on the suggestion of a player,[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Orange_Portal#cite_note-1) who wanted them to function similarly to the Time-Space Rupture.
