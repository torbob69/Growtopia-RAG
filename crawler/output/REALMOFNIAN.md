# REALMOFNIAN

**REALMOFNIAN** is a world open during Lunar New Year. The world was first introduced during Lunar New Year 2023 on January 21, 2023.
In the world, Growtopians can climb to the top of the mountain and use Chinese Fireworks on The Monster Nian in order to scare it. Doing so will grant player a reward. Wearing Draconic items while doing so will grant a chance of getting a bonus reward.
Once the Growtopia community has collectively used enough Chinese Fireworks on The Monster Nian, the Nian gets scared away from the realm for 3 hours. When the Nian is away, the Lunar Chest will be available in the store.
REALMOFNIAN is owned by @Ubidev and like The Carnival, there are many worlds (REALMOFNIAN_121, REALMOFNIAN_136, etc).
