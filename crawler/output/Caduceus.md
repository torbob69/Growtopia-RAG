# Caduceus

This symbol is your reward for saving someone with surgery. You can trade them for rare surgical prizes by calling Sales-Man on your Telephone at 53785. Double-tap this in your inventory to combine 100 of them into a Golden Caduceus.
Properties
This item cannot be dropped or traded.
This item never drops any seeds.
This item has no use... by itself.

The **Caduceus** is an untradeable component which was added as part of Anniversary Week 2017 on January 6, 2017.
##  Function
Caducei can be used to purchase items from the Sales-Man. A Golden Caduceus, which has the value of 100 Caducei, also serves a similar purpose.
