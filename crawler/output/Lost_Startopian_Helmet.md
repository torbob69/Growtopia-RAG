The **Lost Startopian Helmet** is an unsplicable hair item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2020 on October 22, 2020.
##  Function
When equipped, the Lost Startopian Helmet serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
Step 4 of the  Quest Of The Mech requires the player to deliver 150 of this item.
##  Trivia
  * The  Lost Startopian Helmet's design is based on the  Exo Suit Helmet.
