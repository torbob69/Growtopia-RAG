**Death's Scythe** is a hand item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2016.
##  Function
You wield the blade of death.

Back to punching.

When equipped, the **Death's Scythe** grants the _Deadly_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and knock back other players further.
