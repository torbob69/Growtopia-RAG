##  Trivia
  * This Venus Guytrap's name is a portmanteau of "Venus flytrap", a carnivorous plant which preys on insects and arachnids, and "guy", an informal word for a man.
    * The item's design is likely based on the Piranha Plants, a type of enemy found throughout the _Mario_ franchise.
  * The description of this item is a reference to its recipe, which includes a Daisy Seed and a Magic Bacon Wallpaper Seed.
