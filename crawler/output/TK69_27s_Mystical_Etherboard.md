##  Function
Growboard? Keep it, I've got an Etherboard now!

Back to the... shelf!

When equipped, the **TK69's Mystical Etherboard** grants the _Speedy: Mystical_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land. Upon player death, black particles will rush the opposite direction of the player, similar to the Orbs Of Elixir.
