The **Mystic Battle Lance** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Lunar New Year 2021 on February 4, 2021.
##  Function
Fire burns within you!

Extinguished!

When equipped, the **Mystic Battle Lance** grants the _Build Range & Punch Damage : Mystic Battle Lance_ mod, which allows the player to build one tile further and break blocks faster. Additionally, when punching, the Mystic Battle Lance will shoot consecutive flame spikes and when punching a block or player, the block or player will show continuous flow of flames for a number of seconds.
The  Myths and Legends : The Menace of the Mini Minokawa mission requires 1 of this item.
