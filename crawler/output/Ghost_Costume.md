The **Ghost Costume** is an unsplicable chest item that was added as part of Halloween Week 2015.
##  Function
When equipped, the Ghost Costume serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
Respawning while wearing this costume will play the zombie infection sound, and a special ghost particle will fly out instead of the regular Growtopian soul.
##  Trivia
  * The ghost particle effect used by the Ghost Costume is shared with the Haunted Mausoleum and Harvester Of Sorrows.
  * The Ghost Costume constantly tilts from side to side, simulating wind effects.
