The **Electric Eel** is a pain block that was added as part of the Fishin' Pack update on 11 September 2015.
##  Function
Being a pain block, an Electric Eel knocks the player back and stings the player until the player stops all motion when touched. A player will die after making contact with a pain block five times in quick succession (recovering from damage takes roughly 5 seconds). The _Fireproof_ mod will not allow the player to take more hits, unlike with lava pain blocks.
