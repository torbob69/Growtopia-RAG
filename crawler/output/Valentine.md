Use this on somebody to make them your Valentine for an hour! They'll appreciate it greatly.

The **Valentine** is an unsplicable consumable that was added as part of Valentine's Week 2013 on February 10, 2013.
##  Function
You are somebody's valentine!

Yuck!

When consumed on another player, a Valentine will grant them the _Valentine_ mod. If both the sender and receiver are in the same world, red hearts will begin coming out of the former and fly to the latter, whose skin turns reddish.
