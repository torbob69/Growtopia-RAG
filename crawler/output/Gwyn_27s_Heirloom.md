Wield an otherworldly artifact made of living matter and take out anything that comes in your way! Wrench yourself and transform into the master linguist Gwyn, the 'reluctant' Communications Officer of the Protostar crew who is smart, strategic, fearless and always calculating her next move!

The **Gwyn's Heirloom** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Star Trek Prodigy on June 27, 2022.
##  Function
Slicing!

Dicing!

When equipped, the **Gwyn's Heirloom** grants the _Build Range & Punch Damage: Gwyn's Heirloom_ mod, which allows the player to build one tile further, break blocks faster, and switch between its wearable form and its morph form by wrenching oneself and clicking 'Transform'.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Gwyn set, which consists of the following items:

    * Gwyn's Hair (Hair)

    * Gwyn's Shirt (Shirt)

    * Gwyn's Pants (Pants)

    * Gwyn's Shoes (Feet)

    * Gwyn's Heirloom (Hand)
