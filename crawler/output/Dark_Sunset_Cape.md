The **Dark Sunset Cape** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2020 on October 22, 2020.
##  Function
The sun has well and truly set!

Dark Sun guide me skyward!

When equipped, the **Dark Sunset Cape** grants the _Double Jump: Dark Sunset Cape_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
##  Trivia
  * Dark Sunset Cape is a recolor of  Cloud Nine Cape and its golden counterpart  Golden Sunset Cape.
