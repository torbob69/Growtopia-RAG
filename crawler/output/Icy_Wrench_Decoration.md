The **Icy Wrench Decoration** is a wrench decoration which was added as part of the December Season on December 1st, 2024.
##  Function
When equipped through the 'Wrench Customization' tab when wrenching oneself, the Icy Wrench Decoration adds an ice block design in the player's wrench connected to their name.
