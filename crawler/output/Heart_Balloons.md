The **Heart Balloons** is an unsplicable hand item that was added as part of Valentine's Week 2022 on February 11, 2022.
##  Function
Love is in the air!

Love's not in the air!

When equipped, the **Heart Balloons** grants the _High Jump + Slow Fall: Heart Balloons_ mod, which allows the player to jump one tile higher and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
