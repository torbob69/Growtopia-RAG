The **Beach Aura** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of SummerFest 2024 on July 11, 2024.
##  Function
Eatin' cake by the ocean floor!

Burning up for you Buddy!

When equipped, the **Beach Aura** grants the _Double Jump : Beach Aura_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
