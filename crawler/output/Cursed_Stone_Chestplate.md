The **Cursed Stone Chestplate** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of Wonder Week 2024 Day 3 on October 16, 2024.
##  Function
From the stone age!

To the modern age!

When equipped, the **Cursed Stone Chestplate** grants the _Double Jump + Build Range: Cursed Stone Chestplate_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and build one tile further.
