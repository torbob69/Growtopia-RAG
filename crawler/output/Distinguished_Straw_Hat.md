##  Function
When equipped, the Distinguished Straw Hat serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The Distinguished Straw Hat's design is a reference to the hat worn by Monkey D. Luffy or "Straw Hat" Luffy, a fictional character and the protagonist of the One Piece manga series, created by Eiichiro Oda.
