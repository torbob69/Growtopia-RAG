The **Growtronic Food Replicator** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2022 on November 19, 2022.
##  Function
Bon Appetite!

Bon Voyage!

When equipped, the **Growtronic Food Replicator** grants the _Punch Damage : Growtronic Food Replicator_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster.
##  Trivia
  * The Growtronic Food Replicator is a reference to an advanced invention known as the Flint Lockwood Diatonic Super Mutating Dynamic Food Replicator (FLDSMDFR) by protragonist Flint Lockwood in the animation film, Cloudy with a Chance of Meatballs.
