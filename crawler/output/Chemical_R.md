**Chemical R** is an unsplicable component which was added alongside the Mad Science Kit on July 20, 2013.
##  Function
Being a type of chemical, Chemical R can be used for combining alongside two other types of chemicals in a Chemical Combiner to create certain items.
Along with the other types of chemicals, Chemical R is needed in order to synthesize a chemical.

##  Trivia
  * Chemical R is the second most common drop one can receive from a Science Station.
  * When the Mad Science Kit was introduced in July 2013, the Chemical R seed could be found in the Rare Seed Pack. Shortly after its release, the seed was removed from the pack, making it extremely rare. A Chemical R Tree can be found in HALLOFWORLDS.
