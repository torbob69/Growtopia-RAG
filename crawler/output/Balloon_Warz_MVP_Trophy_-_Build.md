You were MVP in the great Balloon Warz. Stick this in the trophy cabinet and show it off to all your friends!

The **Balloon Warz MVP Trophy - Build** is a Balloon Warz trophy awarded after the war phase to the Build team if they are the top 20 players with the most hits throughout the war. This item will automatically appear in the player's backpack once the war is over.
##  Function
When placed, the Balloon Warz MVP Trophy - Build serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
Unlike the other Balloon Warz trophies, this one does not have a year on it and was only obtainable during Summer 2017.
