**Failed Butter Lamb** is an unsplicable solid foreground block that was added as part of Easter Week 2021 on April 1, 2021.
##  Function
When placed, the Failed Butter Lamb serves a fully aesthetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The Failed Butter Lamb is one of the three items added in Easter Week 2021 which was based on the real-life Easter tradition with the same name, alongside the Home Made Butter Lamb and Detective Butter Lamb.
    * In real life, a butter lamb is made by sculpting butter into a lamb, either by hand or in a lamb-shaped mold. This Easter food traditionally accompanies the Easter meal for many Russian, Slovenian and Polish Catholics.
