This item was given to **Jenuine** for their contributions as a voluntary moderator.
The **Genuine Rings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2014 on October 30, 2014.
##  Function
You can jump in mid-air! It's like flying, but less.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Genuine Rings** grants the _Double Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
##  Trivia
  * The Genuine Rings illustrate the rings of Saturn, as @Jenuine stated that she had interest in space.
  * The name of the item is based on @Jenuine’s genuine personality, according to her in an interview with WickEr10.
