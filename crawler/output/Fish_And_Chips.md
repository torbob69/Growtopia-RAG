# Fish And Chips

This is the page for **Fish And Chips**.
You may be looking for **Beer-Battered Fish And Chips** instead.
Deep fried goodness. Enjoy it with some malt vinegar!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **4 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **5s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Potato **(×1)** ,
Fish Chunk **(×2)** and
Wheat **(×3)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Fish And Chips** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2016 on March 11, 2016.
##  Function
When consumed, the player will say "YUM!" and do the :D ⦅⦆ emote.
### Silkworm Food     _Main article:Guide:Silkworm Care_
This consumable can be fed to a Silkworm to give it a yellow color, thus allowing it to produce 1–10  Silk Thread - Yellow per harvest.
