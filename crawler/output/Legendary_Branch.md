# Legendary Branch

The **Legendary Branch** is a series of limited items which appears during the Spring Clash of every season. As of Spring Clash 2020, players can exchange 10 Legendary Clash Keys for the Legendary Item of the season. Legendary Branch items are limited and will no longer be obtainable once the season ends.
Turbine Wings
Anomaly Aura
Galaxy Skin
Oldsock's Old Mate Ricky
Banner Bandolier
Steampunk Arm
Radiant Doom Staff
Legendary Comet Hair
Sonic Buster Sword
Gauntlet of Elements
