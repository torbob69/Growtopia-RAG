The **Super Cape - Black** is an unsplicable back item that was added as part of the Super Villains update.
##  Function
You can jump in mid-air! It's like flying, but less.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Super Cape - Black** grants the _Double Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
