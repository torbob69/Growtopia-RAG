**Chemsynth Centrifuge** is a splicable consumable which added in the Night Of The Chemicals update, alongside the addition of Chemsynth Pack. One can also be obtained from purchasing said pack.
##  Function
It is used for rotating chemicals around a selected Chemsynth Tank. The number of tanks that can be rotated to either left or right depends on how many tanks are to left or right of selected tank till end of tank row.
