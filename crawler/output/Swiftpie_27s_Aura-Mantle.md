# Swiftpie's Aura-Mantle

A lightweight set of mantle-jewelry that weighs as little as a feather but protects like a solid plate. It is favored by battle-mages who need to keep their vision unobstructed while maintaining a divine defense! Sponsored by swiftpie for completing all epic quests!
