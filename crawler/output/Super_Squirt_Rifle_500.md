##  Function
500 gallons per second!

Well. that sucks!

When equipped, the **Super Squirt Rifle 500** grants the _Punch Range: Super Squirt_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further and extinguish fires. Additionally, it will grant punched players the _Soaked_ mod, causing them to drip water particles for 30 seconds.
