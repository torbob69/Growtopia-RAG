The **Neutron Power Glove** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the B.O.O. Update on May 1, 2018.
##  Function
You can hurt ghosts!

You can't hurt ghosts.

When equipped, this item grants the _Ghost Puncher_ mod, which allows a player to punch a Ghost and make the ghost disappear (requires Neutron Pack to work).
