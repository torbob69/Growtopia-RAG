The **Cotton Candy Clouds** is an unsplicable solid foreground block which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2022 on February 11, 2022.
##  Function
When placed, the Cotton Candy Clouds serves a purely aesthetic purpose.

##  Trivia
  * The Cotton Candy Clouds' design is based on the real-life cotton candy, a sugar-based confection which resembles cotton. It usually contains small amounts of food coloring, typically pink in color.
