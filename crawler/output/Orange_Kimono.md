The **Orange Kimono** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as a part of Harvest Festival 2018 on September 21, 2018.
##  Function
When equipped, the Orange Kimono serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The recipe of the Orange Kimono may instead yield a Blue Kimono or a Pink Kimono due to the three having the same recipe.
