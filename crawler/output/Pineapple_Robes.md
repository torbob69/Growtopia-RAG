The **Pineapple Robes** is an unspliable shirt item which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2020 on June 4, 2020.
##  Function
When equipped, Pineapple Robes serves a fully cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * As stated by the item's description, the Pineapple Robes is designed after the leaves of the pineapple palm (_Phoenix canariensis_), a species of flowering plant with an oval, yellow to orange fruit.
    * The pineapple palms are named such due to their distinctively elongated trunks and long leaves, which cause them to resemble gigantic pineapple fruits.
