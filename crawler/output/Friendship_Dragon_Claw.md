# Friendship Dragon Claw

There are many dragons out there - dragons of fire and disaster, dragons of home and hearth, and even dragons from beyond the stars - but NONE are so friendly as this one! Known as Hugalovensnugglor (Hugsy to his closest friends), this dragon is only too happy to shower the world (and you) with hearts!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.

Daily Challenge

Place in the top three of the **Treasure Turmoil** Guild Daily Challenge, only if the player had contributed points to the challenge.
The **Friendship Dragon Claw** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the Challengers Approach! update on October 9, 2019.
##  Function
It's me, Hugsy! Hugsy'll be your pal!

Awww poor Hugsy!

When equipped, this item grants the _Hugsy_ mod, which changes the player's punch animation to blue flames with heart particles.
