The **Stars and Stripes Pants** is an unsplicable pants item which was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2022 on November 19, 2022.
##  Function
When equipped, the Stars and Stripes Pants serves a fully cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Stars and Stripes set, which consists of the following items:

    * Stars and Stripes Cap (Hat)

    * Stars and Stripes Hat (Hat)

    * Stars and Stripes Shawl (Chest)

    * Stars and Stripes Dress (Shirt)

    * Stars and Stripes Suit (Shirt)

    * Stars and Stripes Pants (Pants)

    * Star Spangled Shield (Hand)

    * Stars and Stripes Cape (Cape)
  * The item's description is a reference to J. M. Flagg's illustration of Uncle Sam pointing his finger in a recruitment poster for the American forces during World War I.
