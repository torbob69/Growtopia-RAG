The **Autumn Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2014 on September 5, 2014.
##  Function
You can jump in mid-air! It's like flying, but less.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Autumn Wings** grants the _Double Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
Step 14 of the  Quest For The Sky requires the player to deliver 10 of this item.
##  Trivia
  * The Autumn Wings is the first wings item to be based on autumn leaves, with the second one being the  Fall Wings.
