The **Firework Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Lunar New Year 2023 on January 21, 2023.
##  Function
Sound the alarm!

Put on mute.

When equipped, the **Firework Wings** grants the _Double-Jump: Firework Wings_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
