# Kelpie

A shape-shifting water spirit, this creature normally hangs out in the lochs of Scotland, but it's decided to make an exception to adventure with you! Only found in Blarney 2 and 3.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.

The **Kelpie** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2019 on March 15, 2019.
##  Function
Your feet smell of kelp!

Ahh, refreshing!

When equipped, the **Kelpie** grants the _Speedy in Water: Kelpie_ mod, which allows the player to move faster in water.
##  Trivia
  * During St. Patrick's Week 2020, the descriptions of certain items obtained from BLARNEY were altered; each description now signifies which BLARNEY world may specifically grant the item. This change affects:
    * Corncob Pipe (BLARNEY 2 and 3)
    * Slimy Green Hair (BLARNEY 5, 7 and 9)
    * Shamrock Hair Pin (BLARNEY 6 and 8)
    * Magical Rainbow Fishing Rod (BLARNEY 6, 8 and 10)
    * Kelpie (BLARNEY 2 and 3)
    * Fairy (BLARNEY 1 and 4)
