The **Mystery Door** is a splicable non-solid door foreground block which was added as part of The Adventure Pack Strikes Back! update on June 1, 2017.
##  Function
As a door, the Mystery Door can be entered (via punching) by a player, moving them instantly to another tile. A block with an ID must first be placed on the tile for this to work.
In addition to behaving like a typical door, a Mystery Door will move the entering player to 1 of up to 5 destinations possible randomly.
##  Trivia
  * The Mystery Door is the first door in Growtopia to be able to send players to random destinations.
