The **Jade Warrior Armor** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2022 on September 9, 2022.
##  Function
When equipped, the Jade Warrior Armor serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia

    * Jade Warrior Pauldrons (Chest)

    * Jade Warrior Armor (Shirt)
