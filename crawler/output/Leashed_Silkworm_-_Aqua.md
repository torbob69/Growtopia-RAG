Rewarded to a player upon using a Silkworm Leash on an adult Silkworm of its corresponding color.
The **Leashed Silkworm - Aqua** is an unsplicable pet hand item which was added of the Pet Battles update on June 11, 2016.
##  Function
When equipped, a leashed aqua silkworm pet appears and follows the player around. The Leashed Silkworm - Aqua serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
