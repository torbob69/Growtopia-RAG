# Horizontacles

Writhing, horrible mutant tentacles that love lying flat! Like, all the time (they're very lazy).
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.
Data: Paint PreviewType — Platform – Foreground — Aqua Blue Charcoal Green Purple Red Varnish Yellow
Render Type: Horizontal
Collision Type: Collision on top
Hardness: **8 Hits** **6 Hits** – Restores after **3s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**

Gross-Dosed Explosion

After eating **3** of any Mutant consumable, overload before reaching a specific explosion type by eating more consumables.
Each explosion may give **30** items.

The **Horizontacles** is an unsplicable platform foreground block which was added as part of the Mutant Kitchen update on July 26, 2019.
##  Function
A Horizontacles behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath.
##  Trivia
  * Horizontacles are the horizontal counterparts of Vertentacles. Unlike the latter, Horizontacles function as platforms.
  * Horizontacles replaced the Hairy Arm from Gross-Out update for being "too gross".
