# Sales-Man

This is an **NPC** , or a non-player character.
The **Sales-Man** is an NPC accessible by dialing 53785 on a Telephone. Upon calling him, a message will say:
It is I, Sales-Man, savior of the wealthy! Let me rescue you from your riches. What would you like to buy?

##  Overview
The Sales-Man will offer six categories of items: Surgery Items, Wolfworld Items, Zombie Defense Items, Cooking Items, Startopia Items, and Blue Gem Lock. Players can exchange Caducei for Surgery Items, Wolf Tickets for Wolfworld Items, Zombie Brains for Zombie Defense Items, Mac n' Cheese Stars for Cooking Items, Buckazoids for Startopia Items, or 100 Diamond Locks for a Blue Gem Lock.
Caducei are given when players receive common surgery rewards. Wolf Tickets are obtained when players receive common WOLFWORLD rewards. Zombie Brains are dropped from killing zombies during a zombie apocalypse. Mac n' Cheese Stars are obtained by players when they successfully cook items with a percentage of 95% or more. Buckazoids are obtained when players receive common star voyage rewards.
Upon buying an item from the Sales-Man, one has a chance to receive one of the following:
  * Buddy Balloon
  * Sales Shades
  * Sales-Man Standee
  * Super Stylish Black Hair
  * Showman's Mustache

The Sales-Man isn't reachable during a zombie apocalypse.

##  Trivia
  * When inverted, the Sales-Man's number, 53785, resembles the word "SALES".
  * As part of the Quality of Life Changes update on May 20, 2022, the Sales-Man has been rebalanced with regards to Wolf Tickets.
    * Dark Castle Stone's price has been decreased from 20 to 1.
    * Dark Castle Stone Background's price has been decreased from 20 to 1.
    * Dark Castle Door's price has been decreased from 20 to 1.
    * Dark Castle Turret's price has been decreased from 20 to 1.
  * The Sales-Man uses the same sprite as Walter, The Manager.
  * On September 28, 2023, a Growtoken was removed in the list of cooking items sold by the Sales-Man.
