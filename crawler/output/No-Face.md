The **No-Face** is an unsplicable face item added on Day 4 of Player Apppreciation Week 2016 on August 8, 2016.
##  Function
You've got quite a pokerface.

Your face is back!

When equipped, the **No-Face** grants the _Mark of Growganoth_ mod, which allows the player to have its eyes and mouth invisible.
##  Trivia
  * Up until October 1, 2020, it wasn't possible for the player character to be fully invisible while wearing the No-Face and The One Ring despite their mod effects.
  * During the Quality of Life Changes update on May 20, 2022, the price of No-Face and other rare items were abnormally priced low in the Sales-Man. This was reverted an hour after the update was release and the No-Face was made untradeable so the developers can remove it from players' inventories. It was made tradeable again on June 1, 2022.
