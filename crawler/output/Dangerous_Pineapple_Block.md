The **Dangerous Pineapple Block** is an unsplicable solid pain foreground block which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2019 on June 7, 2019.
##  Function
Being a type of pain block, a Dangerous Pineapple Block knocks the player back and stings the player until the player stops all motion. A player will die after making contact with a pain block five times in quick succession (recovering from damage takes roughly 5 seconds). The _Fireproof_ mod will not allow the player to take more hits, unlike with lava pain blocks.
##  Trivia
  * The Dangerous Pineapple Block is one of the three pineapple blocks added in Super Pineapple Party 2019, alongside the Sourloaf Pineapple Block and Rotten Pineapple Block.
    * Unlike the other two, the Dangerous Pineapple Block acts as a pain block, hurting players upon contact.
