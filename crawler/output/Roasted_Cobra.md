# Roasted Cobra

Deep fried on a stick! This tasty treat is found at your local county fair, obviously.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **2 Hits** **2 Hits** – Restores after **2s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Combustion

Burn a(n)  Lazy Cobra **(x1)** in a  Highly Combustible Box.
The **Roasted Cobra** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2016 on March 11, 2016.
##  Function
When consumed, the player will say "YUM!" and do the :D ⦅⦆ emote.

### Silkworm Food     _Main article:Guide:Silkworm Care_
This consumable can be fed to a Silkworm to give it a grey color, thus allowing it to produce 1–10  Silk Thread - Grey per harvest.
