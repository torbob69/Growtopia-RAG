The **Majestic Pineapple Cape** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2024 on June 13, 2024.
##  Function
Your majesty!

You peasant!

When equipped, the **Majestic Pineapple Cape** grants the _Double Jump : Majestic Pineapple Cape_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
