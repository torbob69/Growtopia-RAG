##  Function
You're a demon slayer!

Seriously? Are you joking? You're totally joking!

When equipped, the **Celestial Lance** grants the _Celestial Lance_ mod, which allows the player to kill zombies during a Pandemic. It will also replace the player's punching animation with golden beam. Breaking an active Demon Crystal using a Celestial Lance will drop a Celestial Halo.
Step 14 of the  Quest For Candour requires the player to deliver 3 of this item.
