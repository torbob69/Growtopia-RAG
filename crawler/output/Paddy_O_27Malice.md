# Paddy O'Malice

Paddy O'Malice is a villain added during St. Patrick's Week 2015. It is an event-limited villain and can only be found during St. Patrick's Week in Blarney worlds or by using a Crime Wave.
Cards he uses

Superpower - Lucky Shot
Superpower - Liquify
Superpower - Frost Breath
Superpower - Enrage
Superpower - Thunderstorm
Health: 15
Method: Bring Incinerate, Overheat, Frozen Mirror, Regeneration and any Lightning Superpower Card (either Shocking Fist, Megawatt Pulse or Thunderstorm). Always try to play Regeneration to keep the health at full, and if he uses Frost Breath, use Overheat and then Incinerate on the next turn.
Upon defeating, the possible rewards to receive are:
  * Henchman
  * Actual Leprechaun Hat
  * Villain Portrait - Paddy O'Malice
  * Superpower - Lucky Shot
  * Super Logo - Shamrock
  * Super Cape - Emerald
  * Superhero Mask - Emerald
  * Crimefighter Mask - Emerald
  * Super Eye Mask - Emerald
  * Super Shirt - Emerald
  * Superheroine Top - Emerald
  * Super Tights - Emerald
  * Heroic Boots - Emerald
  * Muscle Shirt - Emerald
  * Heroic Tights - Emerald
  * Lavish Shades
  * Gold Monitor Checkpoint ×5
  * Brawling Brute Beard

The player will also receive the "Got Lucky Charms" achievement after defeating him.
