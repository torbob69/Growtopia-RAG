The **Evil Eye** is an unsplicable toggleable deadly block which was added as part of Halloween Week 2013 on October 28, 2013.
##  Function
Being a toggleable deadly foreground block, the **Evil Eye** allows players to open and close itself by punching it. When toggled on, the item acts as a deadly block. When toggled off, it acts as a non-solid block which players can walk through.
Evil Eyes populate Growganoth's realm, occasionally switching between their open and closed state without warning. It is also used in the "Growganoth Gulch" minigame in the Carnival. It functions exactly the same in the Carnival as in the Growganoth event.
##  Trivia
  * The Evil Eye was changed from a non-solid tile to a solid block to prevent hackers from passing through it.
  * Before the non-solid tile to a solid block, this block could be used as a Display Box, allowing people to drop things into it.
