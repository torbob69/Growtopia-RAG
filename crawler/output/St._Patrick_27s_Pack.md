# St. Patrick's Pack
During St. Patrick's Week

|
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **St. Patrick's Pack** for **6,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"Celebrate the greenest of holidays with a complete Leprechaun outfit, your own personal Pot O' Gold, and a six-pack of Green Beer!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **St. Patrick's Pack** was released on March 13, 2013, alongside the first ever St. Patrick's Week. This pack is available for 6,000 gems in the gem store.
##  Contents
This pack includes:
  * Leprechaun Hat x1
  * Orange Beard x1
  * Leprechaun Suit x1
  * Green Pants x1
  * Leprechaun Shoes x1
  * Green Beer x6
  * Pot O' Gold x1 (rare)

##  Trivia
  * As of St. Patrick's Week 2022, the St. Patrick's Pack's price increased from 5,000 Gems to 6,000 Gems.
