The **Justice Lens** is an unsplicable face item which was added as part of Wonder Week 2025 - Day 4 on October 9, 2025.
##  Function
Great Power!

No Power!

When equipped, the **Justice Lens** grants the _High Jump + Punch Range: Justice Lens_ mod, which allows the player to jump one tile higher and punch one tile further. The mask's color will change based on the primary color of the player's shirt's seed.
