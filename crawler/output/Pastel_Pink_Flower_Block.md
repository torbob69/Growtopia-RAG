The **Pastel Pink Flower Block** is a splicable solid foreground block which was added as part of Easter Week 2014 on April 19, 2014.
##  Function
When placed, the Pastel Pink Flower Block serves a purely aesthetic purpose.

##  Trivia
  * The Pastel Pink Flower Block is one of the 7 pastel flower color block variations in _Growtopia_. They are:
    * Pastel Aqua Flower Block
    * Pastel Blue Flower Block
    * Pastel Green Flower Block
    * Pastel Orange Flower Block
    * Pastel Pink Flower Block
    * Pastel Purple Flower Block
    * Pastel Yellow Flower Block
    * Note that the basic  Black Block,  Brown Block,  Grey Block, and  White Block do not have a pastel nor a pastel flower variant.
  * Prior to Easter Week 2021, players only had a chance to obtain 1 Pastel Pink Flower Block from EGGRESCUE at a time.
