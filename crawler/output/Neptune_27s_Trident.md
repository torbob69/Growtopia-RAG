The **Neptune's Trident** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of SummerFest 2020 on July 3, 2020.
##  Function
Neptune Rising with the waves!

The storm has passed!

When equipped, the **Neptune's Trident** grants the _Punch Damage + Build Range: Neptune's Trident_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and build one tile further.
Step 18 of the  Quest Of The Heavens requires the player to deliver 1 of this item. 1 Phoenix Wings may also be used as an alternative.
