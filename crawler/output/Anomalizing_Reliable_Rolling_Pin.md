The **Anomalizing Reliable Rolling Pin** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Winter Clash Series 1.
##  Function
Anomalizing Reliable Rolling Pins are used to score points during Cooking Conquerors. While equipping this item, each successfully cooked food yields 5,500 points. Reliable anomalizers, such as this item, will not break upon usage.
