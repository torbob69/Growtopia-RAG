The **Playful Fire Sprite** is an unsplicable face item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2017 Day 3 on August 19, 2017.
##  Function
Your pet has a fiery temper!

Your pet has fled.

When equipped, this item grants the _Flaming Hot_ mod, which summons a purple-colored sprite pet. It also comes with a punching effect featuring flames being fired by the sprite.
