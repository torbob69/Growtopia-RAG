The **Unagi Hair** is an unsplicable hair item which was added as part of Anniversary Week 2022 on January 6, 2022.
##  Function
When equipped, the Unagi Hair serves a fully cosmetic purpose.
The  The Seven Seas : The Ray of the Manta! mission requires 20 of this item.
##  Trivia
  * _Unagi_ (ウナギ) is the Japanese word for freshwater eel, especially the Japanese eel, _Anguilla japonica_ , which is a common ingredient in Japanese cooking.
  * Unagi Hair is also a reference to Ross Geller, who is a character from an American television sitcom called _Friends_.
