The **Skateboard** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2017.
##  Function
You can move faster!

You are slow again.

When equipped, the **Skateboard** grants the _Speedy_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.
The  Winterfest Exclusive Mission - Shred the Ice! mission requires 20 of this item.
