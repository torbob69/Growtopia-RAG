The **Swim Fins** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of SummerFest 2013 on June 21, 2013.
##  Function
Swim faster, walk slower!

You are a land mammal.

When equipped, the **Swim Fins** grants the _A Little Fishy_ mod, which allows the player to move faster in water. It will also decrease moving speed on land
