# Black Friday Cart - Gold

You survived the Black Friday stampede. Show off this gilded shopping cart in recognition of your feat!

The **Black Friday Cart - Gold** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of Black Friday 2017 on November 23, 2017.
##  Function
When placed, the Black Friday Cart - Gold serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
