The **Baroque Fountain** is an unsplicable solid trampoline foreground block which was added as part of Spring Clash 2019 on March 1, 2019.
##  Function
Jumping on a trampoline block such as a Baroque Fountain will cause the player to bounce, and by holding the jump button, the player can jump even higher. The _High Jump_ mod also boosts one's jump height on a trampoline block.
