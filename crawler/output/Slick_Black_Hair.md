Sacrifice **any item with a rarity of 35 or above** to Growganoth.
The **Slick Black Hair** is an unsplicable hair item which was added on Halloween Week 2013 on October 28, 2013.
##  Function
When equipped, Slick Black Hair serves a fully cosmetic purpose.
Equipping this item, alongside some others such as Vampire Fangs, Vamp Vest and the Vampire Cape, will reward one the _Costume Contest_ achievement.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Vampire set, which consists of the following items:

    * Slick Black Hair (Hair)

    * Vampire Fangs (Face)

    * Vamp Vest (Shirt)

    * Vampire Cape (Back)
