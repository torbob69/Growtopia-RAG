The **Iroquois Helmet** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of UbiWeek 2023 Day 1 on February 24, 2023.
##  Function
I am speed!

I am slow!

When equipped, the **Iroquois Helmet** grants the _Speedy & High Jump : Iroquois Helmet_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and jump one tile higher.
##  Trivia

    * Alveolar Crossed Lens (Face)
