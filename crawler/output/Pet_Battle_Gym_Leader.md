The **Pet Battle Gym Leader** is a non-solid NPC foreground block which was added as part of the Pet Battle Update on March 19, 2025.
##  Function
A Pet Battle Gym Leader is seen in six Gym worlds: Terraforge, Zephyrwing, Abyssal Tide, Emberflame, Stormcrag, and Pyrodelve. To each a badge of honor of every gym world, players must first defeat six Pet Battle Gym Trainers in a Pet Battle by punching them before defeating the Pet Battle Gym Trainer. Earning a badge of honor in all six gym worlds allows the player to purchase a Tournament Ticket from Master Peng at The Grand Tournament.
