**Apple Strudel** is a consumable which was added as part of the Cooking update on April 1, 2016.
##  Function
Trees you plant grow 5% faster!

Your stomach's rumbling.

When consumed, this item grants the _Food: Tree Growth_ food buff mod, which reduces the grow time of trees planted by the player by 5% for 30 minutes.
