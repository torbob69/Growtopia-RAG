# Flying Disc

Whirls through the air with a simple toss. I bet you could play a really fun version of golf with this!

The **Flying Disc** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of SummerFest 2018 on July 6, 2018.
##  Function
Similar to the Paper Airplane, when consumed in an empty space the Flying Disc will spawn in the air for a few seconds before crashing into a block.
