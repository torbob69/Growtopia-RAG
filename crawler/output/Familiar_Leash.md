The **Familiar Leash** is an unspliceable hand item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2013 on October 28, 2013.
##  Function
When equipped, the Familiar Leash serves a fully cosmetic purpose.
### Pet Battle Ability
Pet Battles

Familiar Leash

**Black Magic** : Makes your target's abilities fail 10% of the time for the next 20s. Inflicts 10 Water Damage that can't be dodged.
**Cooldown** : 10 seconds.
