The **Lion Dancer Cape - Red** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2023 on September 21, 2023.
##  Function
Roar!!!

Boo!!!

When equipped, the **Lion Dancer Cape - Red** grants the _Double Jump: Lion Dancer Cape - Red!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
When equipped with Lion Dancer Mask - Red, Lion Dancer Shirt - Red, Lion Dancer Pants - Red, and Lion Dancer Feet - Red, the player will gain an extra pair of legs resembling a Lion Dancer costume.
