The **Dragon Tail** is an unsplicable back item which was added alongside the Mad Science Kit on July 20, 2013.
##  Function
When equipped, the Dragon Tail serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
Step 9 of the  Quest For Fire requires the player to deliver 1,000 of this item.
