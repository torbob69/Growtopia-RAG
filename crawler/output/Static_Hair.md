The **Static Hair** is an unsplicable hair item which was added as part of the Strange Crystals update on November 13, 2017.
##  Function
When a player equipping a Static Hair gets near an "electricity source", such as a Checkpoint or Plasma Globe, the hair will react to it, changing its appearance into a spikier one until the player leaves the area.
##  Trivia
  * On November 16, 2017, a glitch was found involving Static Hair and the Portrait. If a player equipping one is painted on a Portrait, the Static Hair would be shown outside of the Portrait, causing everybody in the world to have their devices crash whenever they log onto their accounts.
