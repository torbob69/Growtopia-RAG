The **Zinc** is an unsplicable face item which was added as part of SummerFest 2017 on June 30, 2017.
##  Function
Fire walk with me! Fire does 50% damage.

Your feet are no longer safe.

When equipped, the **Zinc** grants the _Fireproof_ mod, which allows the player to take 50% less damage from lava pain blocks.
