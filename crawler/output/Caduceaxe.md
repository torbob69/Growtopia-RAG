The **Caduceaxe** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the Blockstor's Orders update on June 13, 2019.
##  Function
When equipped, the **Caduceaxe** allows the player to increase the odds of dropping 1-10 Vaccine Drops when breaking blocks. The higher the rarity, the more Vaccine Drops you can get. However, it has a chance of breaking when doing so.
