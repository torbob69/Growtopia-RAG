The **Missile Toad Pet** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of WinterFest 2024 on December 19, 2024.
##  Function
Target Acquired!

Canopy! Canopy! Canopy!

When equipped, the **Missile Toad Pet** grants the _Punch Damage & Punch Range : Missile Toad Pet!_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and punch one tile further. Additionally, it summons a frog with missiles on it back, which replaces player's punching animation with missiles.
