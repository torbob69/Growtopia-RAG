# Summer Breeze

This is the page for **Summer Breeze**.
You may be looking for **Winter Wind** instead.
These gentle breezes smell like freshly mown grass. They also blow with great ferocity!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.
Data: Paint PreviewType — Pushing Block – Foreground — Aqua Blue Charcoal Green Purple Red Varnish Yellow
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **7 Hits** **6 Hits** – Restores after **3s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Paint Preview Aqua Blue Charcoal Green Purple Red Varnish Yellow
Super Fireworks Reward

May be obtained from firing off  Super Fireworks with **200** Fireworks in your inventory.
The **Summer Breeze** is an unsplicable non-solid pushing foreground block which was added as part of Summerfest 2015 on July 3, 2015.
##  Function
As a pushing block, a Summer Breeze will push away colliding players according to the direction it is placed.
