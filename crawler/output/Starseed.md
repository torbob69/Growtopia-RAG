# Starseed

This is the sparkly dust from a passing comet. It seems to whirl around like a tiny galaxy. Perhaps if you collected enough of it, it would merge into a solid mass!
Comet Dust (Rarity: 10)
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: Full Collision
Hardness: **6 Hits** **5 Hits** – Restores after **8s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **0 - 2**
**21m 40s**
Consumption

Consume a(n)  Comet Dust **(x200)**.
May yield **1** item each.

The **Starseed** is a special seed which was added as part of the original Night Of The Comet update on November 27, 2014.
##  Function
A Starseed is used to splice certain exclusive trees during the Night Of The Comet, such as the Cosmic Hair, Rocket Pack, Unearthly Chemical and a range of other items. If the seed is planted by itself, it will drop a Starseed back upon harvest.
The  Myths and Legends : Here come the Shady Agents mission requires 10 Starseeds.

##  Trivia
  * When the player tries to plant the Starseed when it's not in the Night Of The Comet, it will say: "_The Starseed won't seem to grow without cosmic radiation striking it!_ "
  * Making a Starseed for the first time will give the **We Are All Stardust** achievement.
