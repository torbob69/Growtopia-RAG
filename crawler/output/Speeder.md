The **Speeder** is an unsplicable feet item that was added as part of the Time-Tossed! update On September 5, 2019.
##  Function
Speed is on your side!

Back to your chores!

When equipped, the **Speeder** grants the _Speedy: Speeder_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.
##  Trivia
  * The Speeder is based on the X-34 landspeeder from the _Star Wars_ franchise.
