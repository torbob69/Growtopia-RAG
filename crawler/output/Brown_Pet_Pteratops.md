The **Brown Pet Pteratops** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Growassic Park on October 7, 2016.
##  Function
When equipped, the Brown Pet Pteratops serves a purely cosmetic purpose and summons a Brown Pet Pteratops which follows the player around.
