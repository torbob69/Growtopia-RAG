# Grow4Good Special Gift Hamper

Thank you for your generosity! Here's a special gift as a token of our appreciation! Opening this will give you an awesome prize, which could include an EPIC LIMITED QUANTITY ITEM. A MAXIMUM of only 5 of each of these EPIC items will drop over the course of the event.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **3 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **4s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Grow4Good Rewards

**1** of this item can be claimed upon reaching **240** Personal points.
Grow4Good Rewards

**1** of this item can be claimed by Grow4Good Pass Members only upon reaching **240** Personal points.
Consumption

Consume a(n)  Grow4Good Gift Hamper **(x1)**.
May yield **1** item each.

The **Grow4Good Special Gift Hamper** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Grow4Good Week 2022 on April 22, 2022.
