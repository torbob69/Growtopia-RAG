The **Pumpkin Candy** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Halloween Week 2017.
##  Function
Consuming 100 Pumpkin Candy will give a Demented Cowl, provided that the player has at least one of all the following items in their inventory:
  * Killer Doctor's Cap
  * Killer Doctor's Face Mask
  * Killer Doctor's Pants
  * Killer Doctor's Smock
  * Fish Monster Mask
  * Demon Clown Mask
  * Looming Shadows
  * Hanging Bats
  * Plastic Ghost
  * Totally Harmless Doll
  * Iron Maiden
  * Neon Voodoo Mask - Pure White
