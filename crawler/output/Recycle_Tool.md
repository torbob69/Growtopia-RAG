# Recycle Tool

As seen from the backpack.
The **Recycle Tool** is the replacement for the Trash Tool. Instead of simply trashing an item it gives the player a random amount of gems based on rarity, just like breaking block with Fist, or if the item can be bought from the Growtopia Store, it gives 10% the price that was used to buy it. For example, World Lock costs 2000 gems in store but if recycled it gives only 200 gems.
It can be obtained by earning the Supporter status, either by earning 26,000 Gems through Tapjoy (a mobile advertising and monetization platform), or by making at least 1 In-App Purchase.
If you attempt to recycle an Untradeable item then you'll be prompted with a Warning Screen to confirm you're sure you want to go ahead.
