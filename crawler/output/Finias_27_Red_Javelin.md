**Finias' Red Javelin** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2021 on March 11, 2021.
##  Function
Spear me a pun for once please!

Jav you had enough?

When equipped, the **Finias' Red Javelin** grants the _Punch Range : Finias' Red Javelin_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further. Additionally, when punching, the player throws the javelin. It will also mark the block with a symbol and scatter into tiny pieces of red particles.
The  Myths and Legends : Mothman Rising mission requires 1 of this item.
##  Trivia
  * Finias' Red Javelin is based on a mythological treasure called _Spear of Lugh_ which was found in Finias, one of the four mythical cities of the Tuatha Dé Danann.
