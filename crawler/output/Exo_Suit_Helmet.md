The **Exo Suit Helmet** is an unsplicable hair item which was added as part of the Startopia 2.0 update on June 1, 2020.
##  Function
When equipped, the Exo Suit Helmet serves a fully cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the exo suit set, which consists of the following items:

    * Exo Suit Helmet (hair)

    * Exo Suit Body (chest)

    * Exo Suit Pants (pants)
  * The  Lost Startopian Helmet's design is based on the  Exo Suit Helmet.
