# BEACHPARTYGAME

**BEACHPARTYGAME** is a world open during Beach Party. It was first introduced during Beach Party 2022 on June 24, 2022. Players can join the game by purchasing a Beach Party Ticket or Beach Party Training Ticket in the Growtopia Store and punching The Bouncer found in the right side of the world.
The player then warps to the waiting room, once enough players are in the waiting room, a timer counts down. When it hits zero, the floor is removed and the players have 2 minutes to smash as much sand as they can. Some sands hide Clam Chests. Upon breaking a Clam Chest, the player will receive Pearls Currency. Players can exchange Pearls Currency to The Bartender for a Bronze Pearl Chest, Pearl Chest or Golden Pearl Chest and consume them to receive pearl-related prizes.
Similar to CARNIVAL, there are many BEACHPARTYGAME worlds (BEACHPARTYGAME_2, BEACHPARTYGAME_3, etc). These worlds are owned by @Ubidev.
