# Sportsball Pack (update)

**Sportsball Pack** is an update which was introduced on July 17, 2015.[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Sportsball_Pack_\(update\)#cite_note-FB-1) It introduced the titular Sportsball Pack to the game.

##  Feature
### Sportsball Pack
  * Cost 15,000 Gems each.
  * Had a store description that stated, "We like sports and we don't care who knows! This pack includes 2 Basketball Hoops, 2 Sporty Goals, 5 Stadiums, 5 Crowded Stadiums, a Football Helmet, a Growies Cap, and randomly one of the six Rare sports items that you can use to launch sportsballs at people!"
  * Contained the following items:
    * Basketball Hoop ×2
    * Sporty Goal ×2
    * Stadium ×5
    * Crowded Stadium ×5
    * Football Helmet ×1
    * Growies Cap ×1
    * and one of the following:
      * Baseball Glove ×1 (Rare)
      * Basketball ×1 (Rare)
      * FC Cleats ×1 (Rare)
      * Man U Cleats ×1 (Rare)
      * Pigskin ×1 (Rare)
      * Tennis Racquet ×1 (Rare)
