The **Rocket Pack** is a splicable back item which was added as part of the original Night Of The Comet update on November 27, 2014.
##  Function
You can fly!! Well, you can jump higher than normal.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Rocket Pack** grants the _High Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump one tile higher.
