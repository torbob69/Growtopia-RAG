# Alien Fossil Rock

This rock seems to have a fossil embedded in it. Maybe you could crack it loose if you tap very carefully with a Rock Hammer. There is a rumor, as old as time itself, that having a busy world will generate more Fossil Rock. The Growtopia Archaeological Society have officially announced that this is totally untrue. Time to bury that rumor, once and for all!

The **Alien Fossil Rock** is an unsplicable solid foreground block which was added as part of the Growassic Park update.
##  Obtaining
The item spawns in popular worlds in the place of a Mars Rock; the more popular a world is, the more likely an Alien Fossil Rock may spawn.
##  Function
An Alien Fossil Rock should be punched using a Rock Hammer until the fossil has been unearthed, followed by a Rock Chisel until it breaks, dropping a Fossil. Using a Fossil Brush on the dropped Fossil will give a Polished Fossil.
##  Trivia
  * The Alien Fossil Rock has the appearance of a Mars Rock with a Fossil embedded in it.
