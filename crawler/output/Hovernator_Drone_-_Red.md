The **Hovernator Drone - Red** is an unsplicable feet item which was introduced as January's Item Of The Month for 2019. The item, along with the White, Black, and Golden Drone were possible drops from purchasing January's 2019's Item Of The Month for 200,000 Gems.
##  Function
Weapons free, fire at will.

Peace initiative in effect.

When equipped, the **Hovernator Drone - Red** grants the _Drone_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. It will also summon a red Hovernator Drone that will follow the player around and replace their punching animation with bullets coming from the drone.
