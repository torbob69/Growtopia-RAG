The **Dwarven Background** is a splicable background block which was added as part of the Spelunkorama Strikes Back! update.
##  Function
As a dark background block, a Dwarven Background enshrouds the tile it is placed on in darkness.
Light source blocks may provide light to neighboring dark tiles when placed. In a similar manner, a player can illuminate their dark surroundings by equipping certain light source clothes. The Night Vision Goggles disregards darkness completely.
Step 1 of the  Quest Of The Mech requires the player to deliver 2,000 of this item.
