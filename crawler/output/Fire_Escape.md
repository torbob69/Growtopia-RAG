The **Fire Escape** is a splicable platform foreground block which was added alongside the City Pack on August 18, 2013.
##  Function
A Fire Escape behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath.

##  Trivia
  * The Fire Escape is based on the real-life structure with the same name, a type of emergency exit mounted to the outside of a building, which provides a method of escape in the event of a fire or another sort of emergency.
