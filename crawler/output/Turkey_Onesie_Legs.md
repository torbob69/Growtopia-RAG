The **Turkey Onesie Legs** is an unsplicable pants item which was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2017 on November 25, 2017.
##  Function
When equipped, the Turkey Onesie Legs serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  *     * This item is considered a part of the Turkey set, which consists of the following items:

    * Turkey Onesie Hood (Hair)

    * Turkey Onesie Beak (Face)

    * Turkey Onesie Body (Shirt)

    * Turkey Onesie Legs (Pants)

    * Turkey Onesie Feet (Feet)

    * Turkey Onesie Tail (Back)

    * Turkey Carving Set (Hand)
  * In 2017, the Turkey Onesie Legs could be obtained as a random drop by smashing blocks on the third day of Thanksgiving Week 2017.
