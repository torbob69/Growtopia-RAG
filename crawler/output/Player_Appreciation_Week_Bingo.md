# Player Appreciation Week Bingo

This is a **stub** page.
Please add more information to this page.
Player Appreciation Week Bingo is a feature which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week on August 25, 2026. This feature can be accessed through the store or through the bingo button on the left-sidebar.
Players get a random Player Appreciation Week Bingo card which contains a 5x5 grid of items from various Growtopia systems. Players have to collect these items across Player Appreciation Week to get prizes. A player has the option to reset the card for 10 World Locks (increasing by 10 World Locks each reset and capping at 100 World Locks). This will discard any progress made on the card, and replace all the prizes with new prizes.
