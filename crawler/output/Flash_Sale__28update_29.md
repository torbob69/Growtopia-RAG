## Store Packs Sales

Following Store Packs were buffed, where players can get over 10× more value by purchasing them:
  * **Gem Abundance** : Players get 24,600,000 Gems, 46 Diamond Locks, 10 Growtokens and 5 Megaphones for $49.99.
_(Previously had 2,460,000 Gems and 230World Locks with same amount of Growtokens and Megaphones)_
  * **Gem Bounty** : Players get 14,400,000 Gems, 28 Diamond Locks, 6 Growtokens and 3 Megaphones for $29.99.
_(Previously had 1,440,000 Gems and 140World Locks with same amount of Growtokens and Megaphones)_
  * **It's Rainin' Gems** : Players get 4,450,000 Gems, 9 Diamond Locks, 2 Growtokens and 1 Megaphone for $9.99.
_(Previously had 445,000 Gems and 45World Locks with same amount of Growtokens and Megaphones)_

## Changes
Timer showing how much time left until the player can purchase locks again in Growtopia Store.
  * As of May 16th, World Lock purchases with Gems from the Growtopia Store are now capped and restocked daily, scaling with account age up to 100 per day. Every player can see how many World Locks they can purchase each day in the description screen of the World Lock packs. A timer is also added which shows how much time left until the World Lock packs restock.
  * On May 19th, same changes were implemented to other purchasable locks: Small Locks, Big Locks, and Huge Locks.

New description of World Lock Packs showing the available stock and the timer.
