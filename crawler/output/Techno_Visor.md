The **Techno Visor** is an unsplicable face item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2016 Day 4 on August 8, 2016.
##  Function
When equipped, the Techno Visor serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * Wearing the Techno Visor with an upgraded Ancestral Tesseract of Dimensions will give it special effects, depending on the upgrade level of the Ancestral Tesseract of Dimensions.
