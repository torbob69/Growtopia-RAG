# Monthly Event Update 2021/Mutant Kitchen

< Monthly Event Update 2021
Store Announcement

It's time to get messy in the Mutant Kitchen! During this 3 day event you can stuff yourself with weird and wonderful mutant food!  Consuming the crazy mutant food items consecutively will earn you some exclusive items!  The cutest mutants in the whole of Growtopia have been released into Mutant Kitchen, are you lucky enough to find one? Get cooking and eating to find out!
As part of the multiple small **Monthly Event** revamps, an update to Mutant Kitchen was added on August 1, 2021. The items themselves are obtainable since August 6, 2021, the day Mutant Kitchen arrived.
