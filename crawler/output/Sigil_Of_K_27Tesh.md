The **Sigil Of K'Tesh** is an unsplicable consumable that was added as part of Halloween Week 2013 on October 28, 2013.
##  Function
When consumed, a Sigil Of K'Tesh allows the player to teleport directly to Growganoth's maw, entirely bypassing the parkour leading up to it. Using this item does not count for the Growganoth's Game daily challenge.
When used in a non-GROWGANOTH world, the player says, "_The sigil fizzles... try using it near Growganoth!_ "
##  Trivia
  * Sigil Of K'Tesh has a redesigned version:  Elder Sigil that also serves a similar function. However, unlike the former, Elder Sigil has a 2 minute duration allowing the player to teleport to Growganoth's maw instead of being used up directly upon consumption.
