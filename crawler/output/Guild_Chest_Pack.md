# Guild Chest Pack

Purchase the **Guild Chest Pack** for **20,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"A 10-pack of Guild Chests! Loaded with guildy goodness - pop a chest open for a surprise item!"
Every purchase will give **10** items.

The **Guild Chest Pack** was released alongside the Guilds update on April 1, 2018.
