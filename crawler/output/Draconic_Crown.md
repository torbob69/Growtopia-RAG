The **Draconic Crown** is an unsplicable hat item that was added as part of Lunar New Year 2023 on January 21, 2023.
##  Function
Flames of fury!

Hosed away!

When equipped, the **Draconic Crown** grants the _Double Jump & Speedy: Draconic Crown_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and move faster on land.
