The **Axolotl Scarf** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2020 Day 1 on August 17, 2020.
##  Function
When equipped, the Axolotl Scarf serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Axolotl set, which consists of the following items:

    * Axolotl Hood (Hair)

    * Axolotl Hoodie (Shirt)

    * Axolotl Onesie (Shirt)

    * Axolotl Scarf (Chest)

    * Axolotl Slippers (Feet)
  * The Axolotl Scarf was one of the five clothing items added on Player Appreciation Week 2020 Day 1 that were based on the Axolotl, alongside Axolotl Hood, Axolotl Slippers, Axolotl Onesie, and Axolotl Hoodie.
