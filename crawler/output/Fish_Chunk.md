**Fish Chunk** is an unsplicable component which was added as part of the Gone Crabby! update on March 3, 2016.
##  Function
Upon being placed inside the Training Port, the fish/crab will start on level 1 and will require food to gain XP. Fish/Crab can be fed specific fish/crab respectively, Fish Chunks or Fish Flakes. Fish Chunks will give 1XP each.
