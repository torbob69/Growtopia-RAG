The **Growboard** is an unsplicable feet item which was added on Player Appreciation Week 2014 Day 6 on August 13, 2014.
##  Function
You can move faster!

You are slow again.

When equipped, the **Growboard** grants the _Speedy_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land. It also causes the player to slowly drift up and down, simulating levitation, while emitting flames at the back when moving.
