The **Dragon Knight's Spear** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the Dragon Invasion update on February 4, 2019.
##  Function
Dragon Skewer.

No more toothpicks left.

When equipped, the **Dragon Knight's Spear** grants the _Punch Range: Dragon Knight's Spear_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further.

    * Dragon Knight's Chestplate (shirt)

    * Dragon Knight's Greaves (pants)
