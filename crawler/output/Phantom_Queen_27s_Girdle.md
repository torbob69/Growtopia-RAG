The **Phantom Queen's Girdle** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2025 on March 14, 2025.
##  Function
When equipped, the **Phantom Queen's Girdle** grants the _Double Jump + Slow Fall : Phantom Queen's Girdle_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
##  Trivia
  * Phantom Queen's Girdle is a reference to The Morrígan, which is a figure from Irish mythology.
