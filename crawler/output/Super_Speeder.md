The **Super Speeder** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Wonder Week 2022 Day 4 on October 18, 2022.
##  Function
I am speed!

I am slow!

When equipped, the **Super Speeder** grants the _Speedy & High Jump : Super Speeder!_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and jump one tile higher. The Super Speeder is interchangeable between ground and hover modes by equipping and unequiping the item.
