The **Growtorial Entrance** is a block that was released as part of Growtopia's Tutorial. It is automatically given to new players as part of a Tutorial Quest.
##  Function
Being an entrance, a Growtorial Entrance allows players with access to walk through it, or, when its property is changed, allowing any player to walk through it. When placed in a public area, it is impossible to be closed for anyone.
##  Trivia
  * Unlike other items, only two of these can be held at a time.
