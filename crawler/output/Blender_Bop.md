The **Blender Bop** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block which was added as part of SummerFest 2022 on July 28, 2022.
##  Function
<https://growtopiawiki.com/w/File:Blenderbop.ogg>The Blender Bop's music.
Being a toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block, a Blender Bop can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Blender Bop will change its appearance, showing a 4-framed animation, and play a piece of music.
##  Trivia
  * In the game files, the Blender Bop's music is named blenderbop.
  * The Blender Bop's music comes from a traditional nursing song entitled The Muffin Man.
