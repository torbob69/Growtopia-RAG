# Candy Heart

Chalky goodness emblazoned with an incomprehensible slogan you can't help but shout.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **3 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **4s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Valentine Loot

|  **1** has a chance to be dropped from breaking:
  * Heartstone
  * Golden Booty Chest
  * Well of Love (every **5 Golden Booty Chests** filled)

 |
The **Candy Heart** is an unsplicable consumable that was added as part of Valentine's Week 2013 on February 10, 2013.
##  Function
When consumed, the player shouts out a random phrase and performs the /love command.

### Silkworm Food     _Main article:Guide:Silkworm Care_
This consumable can be fed to a Silkworm to give it a red color, thus allowing it to produce 1–10  Silk Thread - Red per harvest.
