The **HyperTech Multi-Jet Energy Pulser** is an unsplicable solid bouncy foreground block which was added as part of the Stuff! update.
##  Function
Being a type of bouncy block, a HyperTech Multi-Jet Energy Pulser will actively push away any player it collides with.
