The **Super Pineapple Door** is a unsplicable non-solid door foreground block which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2022 on June 10, 2022.
##  Function
As a door, the Super Pineapple Door can be entered (via punching) by a player, moving them instantly to another tile. A block with an ID must first be placed on the tile for this to work.
##  Trivia
  * The complete super pineapple house set consists of the  Super Pineapple House,  Super Pineapple Door, and  Super Pineapple Window. These blocks are inspired by the pineapple house inhabited by the titular protagonist of the animated television series _SpongeBob SquarePants_.
