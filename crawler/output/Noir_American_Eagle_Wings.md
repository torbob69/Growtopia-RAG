The **Noir American Eagle Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Black Friday 2022 on November 25, 2022.
##  Function
Back in time!

Applesauce!

When equipped, the **Noir American Eagle Wings** grants the _Double-Jump: Noir American Eagle Wings_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
##  Trivia
