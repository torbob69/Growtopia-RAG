The **Unicorn Floatie** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of Brr-lloon Warz 2018.
##  Function
When equipped, the **Unicorn Floatie** allows the player to float on water permanently with slow ascent.
##  Trivia
  * Prior to Brr-lloon Warz 2020, the Unicorn Floatie could be claimed after reaching 25,000 Guild points in Balloon Warz.
