The **Anomalizing Bone Saw** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Summer Clash Series 1.
##  Function
Anomalizing Pristine Bone Saws are used to score points during Surgery Stars. While equipping this item, each successful surgery yields 6,200 points. Pristine anomalizers, such as this item, may sometimes break upon usage during their respective Grow Events.
