The **Ridin' Truck** is an unsplicable feet item that was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2024 on November 21, 2024.
##  Function
Roll out!

Burnout!

When equipped, the **Ridin' Truck** grants the _Speedy + Light Source: Ridin' Truck_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and emit light in dark areas.
