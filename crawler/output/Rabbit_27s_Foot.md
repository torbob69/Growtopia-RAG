The **Rabbit's Foot** is an unsplicable consumable which was added alongside the Vegas Pack on May 24, 2013.
##  Function
You're luckier than before!

Your luck has worn off.

When consumed, a Rabbit's Foot grants the _Lucky_ mod to the player for thirty minutes, giving a 10% chance to multiply gem drops from breaking blocks by up to 5 times. It also provides a 10% chance for an extra block to be dropped.
This item's effect does not stack with that of Lucky Clover or Songpyeon, but stacks with Apology Weekend's bonus.
##  Trivia
  * In real life, a rabbit's foot is considered to bring good luck in various cultures.
  * Since the developers considered the Rabbit's Foot to be too overpowered, the Vegas Pack was removed from the Store on March 2, 2014, thus preventing players from obtaining the item. The pack returned on April 26, 2014, but with the Pink Cadillac as its exclusive item instead.
