##  Function
What pretty lights you have!

Lights... fading!

When equipped, the **MagTron LightCycle** grants the _MAGnificent!_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and emit light in dark areas. While moving, the player also leaves a white trail.
##  Trivia
  * The MagTron LightCycle is based on the 5th-generation light cycle from Walt Disney's _TRON: Legacy_. The item's name alludes to this fact.
  * When it was first released, the MagTron LightCycle left behind a rectangle trail that appeared blue to the wearer and red to others. An update released on an unknown date changed the color of the trail, causing everyone to see it as white.
