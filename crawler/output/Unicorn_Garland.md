##  Function
Your friend shoots rainbows!

You are lonely.

When equipped, this item grants the _Rainbows!_ mod, which changes the player's punch effect into rainbows. It also summons a unicorn pet which follows the player around and can be trained as an earth battle pet. The unicorn's horn changes to a rainbow color while the player is punching.
