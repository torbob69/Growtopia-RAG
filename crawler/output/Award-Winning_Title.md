# Award-Winning Title

Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item has no use... by itself.
Data

Type: Component
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **1 Hits** **1 Hits** – Restores after **1s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
The **Award-Winning Title** is an unsplicable component which was added as part of Anniversary Week/2025 on January 16, 2025.
##  Obtaining
### Riddle Solution
If you've found something ancient, perhaps order is the key! The shopkeeper, the scroll and the traveller three.

This message appears after submitting the current Daily Quest items to Crazy Jim. The shopkeeper references the Sales-Man, the Scroll references Secret Of Growtopia, and the traveller references Locke The Salesman.
#### Sales-Man
If being legendary isn't legendary enough, to help you upon your path to further fame, I've heard Wizards are fond of the color purple!

If the player has achieved the _"DARY!"_achievement, this message will appear. The word "purple" alludes to a part of the The Wizard's Staff Mystery riddle.
If the player has achieved the _"DARY!"_ achievement (i.e., players who completed a Legendary Quest), they can splice a Wizard's Staff tree, which will give one the following message, _"The Wizard's Staff you spliced contains an ancient inscription: XXX-"_. Here, "X" represents three random letters unique to the player.
#### Secret Of Growtopia
A mystery awaits a champion of the galaxy can defeat the Almighty Seth!

A Secret Of Growtopia may contain the sentences above.
If the player has achieved the the _"Interstellar Champion"_ achievement (i.e., players who have defeated Growlactus), they can defeat Almighty Seth, which will give one the following message, _"Almighty Seth whispers an ancient secret to you: XXXXX"_. Here, "X" represents five random letters unique to the player.
#### Locke The Salesman
That's a real hard one! If you consider yourself the G.O.A.T. facing off against a little version of the big man himself will help you prove your worth!

This message appears when the player searches for "Award-Winning Title" using Locke The Salesman's "Ask Locke Something" button.
If the player has achieved the _"Growtopian of the Year"_ achievement (i.e., players who have completed all holiday achievements), they can defeat a Pet Trainer using a deck that includes the Mini Growtopian. Upon success, the Pet Trainer may grant the following message: _"The Pet Trainer congratulates you and grants you a tome that contains an ancient inscription: -XXX"._ Here, "X" represents three random letters unique to the player.
#### Completing the Mystery
Once all three inscriptions are collected, the player must find Locke The Salesman, tap on "Ask Locke Something," and input the code in the format: XXX-XXXXX-XXX
  * The first three letters come from the Wizard's Staff riddle.
  * The next five letters come from the Secret of Growtopia riddle.
  * The final three letters come from the Locke the Salesman riddle.

If successful, Locke will display the following message: "Congrats! Such an Award-Winning performance deserves an Award-Winning Title! Wrench yourself to display it! Definitely worthy of someone who has ventured through Growtopia!" and will receive the Award-Winning Title.
##  Function
The player can toggle this title on and off upon wrenching oneself and tapping on Title. When equipped, the Award-Winning Title adds a laurel wreath on both sides of the player's name.
