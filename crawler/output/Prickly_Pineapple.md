The **Prickly Pineapple** is an unsplicable toggleable deadly foreground block which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2019 on June 7, 2019.
##  Function
Being a toggleable deadly foreground block, a Prickly Pineapple manually toggles itself every 3 seconds, periodically transforming itself from a non-deadly block into a deadly one. When it enters its deadly phase, the Prickly Pineapple acts as a solid block and kills players upon contact, similar to the Deathtrap Wall.
