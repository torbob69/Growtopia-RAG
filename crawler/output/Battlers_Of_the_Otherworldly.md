# Battlers Of the Otherworldly

Growtopia Gazette
> July 15th: OH NO! GROWTOPIA IS HAUNTED! Uninvited spectral inhabitants have invaded the most decrepit and unused worlds! Calling all B.O.O. (Battlers Of the Otherworldly) members for help. Team-work is key in capturing these spooky spirits. Good luck. -@Seth and @Hamumu
The **Battlers Of the Otherworldly** was an update introduced on July 15, 2016, along with the B.O.O. Training Pack.
