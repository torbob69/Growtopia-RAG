The **Golden Heart Aura** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2021 on February 13, 2021.
##  Function
The power of heart propels you.

Take the shine right off.

When equipped, the **Golden Heart Aura** grants the _Double Jump and Healing: Golden Heart Aura_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and heal faster than usual after receiving damage from hitting a pain block.
