The **Cultist Hood** is an unsplicable hair item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2013 on October 28, 2013.
##  Function
You've got quite a pokerface.

Your face is back!

When equipped, the **Cultist Hood** grants the _Mark of Growganoth_ mod, which allows the player to change their skin color to grey. It will also reduce the visibility of the player's facial features and clothing will become darker.
