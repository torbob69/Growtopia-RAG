The **Monster Mummy Feet** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2022 on October 22, 2022.
##  Function
When equipped, the Monster Mummy Feet serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia

    * Monster Mummy Nemes (hat)

    * Monster Mummy Mask (face)

    * Monster Mummy Chestplate (shirt)
