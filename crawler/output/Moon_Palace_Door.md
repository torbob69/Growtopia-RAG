The **Moon Palace Door** is an unsplicable door foreground block which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2022 on September 9, 2022.
##  Function
As a door, the Moon Palace Door can be entered (via punching) by a player, moving them instantly to another tile. A block with an ID must first be placed on the tile for this to work.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Moon Palace set, which consists of the following items:

    * Moon Palace Roof (roof)

    * Moon Palace (block)

    * Moon Palace Door (door)
