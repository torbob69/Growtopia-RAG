The **Cute Mutant Boogle** is an unsplicable hand item which was added a part of the Mutant Kitchen monthly event update on August 6, 2021.
##  Function
Cute Out!!

Cute In!

When equipped, the **Cute Mutant Boogle** grants the _Cute Mutant Boogle : Punch Range_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further. Additionally, it will summon an orange mutant pet that follows the player around, and shoots orange comet-like particles when punching.
