# Fish Taco

This is the page for **Fish Taco**.
You may be looking for **Homemade Fish Taco** instead.
Fresh Baja taste, with a guacamole cream sauce.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **4 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **5s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Wheat **(×2)** ,
Fish Chunk **(×3)** and
Swiss Cheese Block **(×1)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Fish Taco** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2016 on May 2, 2016.
##  Function
When consumed, the player will say "YUM!" and do the :D ⦅⦆ emote.
### Silkworm Food     _Main article:Guide:Silkworm Care_
This consumable can be fed to a Silkworm to give it a grey color, thus allowing it to produce 1–10  Silk Thread - Grey per harvest.
