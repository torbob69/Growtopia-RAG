The **Lucky Chair** is an unsplicable non-solid seat foreground block which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2019 on March 15, 2019.
##  Function
When a player stands in front of a seat such as the Lucky Chair for a few seconds, the player will automatically be seen "sitting" on it.
