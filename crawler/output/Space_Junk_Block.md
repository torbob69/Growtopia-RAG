The **Space Junk Block** is an unsplicable solid toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block which was added as part of the Startopia 2.0 update on June 1, 2020.
##  Function
Being a toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block, a Space Junk Block can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Space Junk Block will change its appearance, showing a 4-framed animation of itself spinning in place.
