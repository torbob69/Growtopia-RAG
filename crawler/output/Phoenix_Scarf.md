The **Phoenix Scarf** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of SummerFest 2020 on July 3, 2020.
##  Function
Phoenix Descend!

Rise Oh Phoenix and watch over me!

When equipped, the **Phoenix Scarf** grants the _Double-Jump: Phoenix Scarf!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
