# Team Entrance - Build

The only people getting through this door are the ones who are on team Build. Outside of Balloon Warz, this functions as a normal entrance.
Properties
This item has special properties you can adjust with the Wrench.
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.

The **Team Entrance - Build** was introduced during the Balloon Warz update.
##  Function
Being an entrance, a Team Entrance - Build allows players with access to walk through it, or, when its property is changed, allowing any player to walk through it. When placed in a public area, it is impossible to be closed for anyone. During the Balloon Warz, it only letting players from Team Build to walk through it.
