The **Crafter's Cape** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Winter Clash 2018.
##  Function
Some crafty extra Role points!

Argh, no more extra Role points!

When equipped, the **Crafter's Cape** grants the _Crafter's Cape_ mod, which allows the player to get 5% bonus points from completing Builder or Chef role quests.
