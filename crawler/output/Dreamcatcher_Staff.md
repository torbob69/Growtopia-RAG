# Dreamcatcher Staff

Empowered by bountiful spirits from the Other Side, this delicate dreamcatcher will grant its wielder clear skies... and 2% chance of getting extra blocks from harvesting trees!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be transmuted.

Store a  Ghost-In-A-Jar **(x55)** in a  Spirit Storage Unit, increasing its capacity by **275%**.
May yield **1** item each.
The **Dreamcatcher Staff** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2018 Day 6 on August 11, 2018.
##  Function
Bountiful harvest!

Aw, no more bonus blocks.

When equipped, the **Dreamcatcher Staff** grants the _Extra blocks from trees..._ mod, which allows the player to increase the odds by 2% of getting extra blocks from harvesting trees with rarity below 100.
