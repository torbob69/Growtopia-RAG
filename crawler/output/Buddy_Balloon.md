# Buddy Balloon

Need a friend? Need a balloon? Why not both? Buddy Balloon is always there to (literally) lift your spirits!

Bonus reward from purchasing any item from the  **Sales-Man** ( 53785).

The **Buddy Balloon** is a hand item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2021 Day 6 on August 28, 2021.
##  Function
When equipped, it summons a balloon inspired by the Growtopia mascot and follows the player around.
