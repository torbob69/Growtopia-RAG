The **Neptune Crown** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of SummerFest 2022 on July 28, 2022.
##  Function
Ocean Rising!

Ocean Falling!

When equipped, the **Neptune's Crown** grants the _Punch Range + Build Range: Neptune's Crown_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further and build one tile further.
Step 16 of the  Quest For Honor requires the player to deliver 1 of this item. 1 Growie Award may also be used as an alternative.
