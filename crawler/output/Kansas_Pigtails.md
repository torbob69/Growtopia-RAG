##  Function

##  Trivia
  * Prior to its addition in-game, a "Kansas Pigtails" could be made by wearing Pigtails and Kansas Curls at the same time. However, an update caused Pigtails to be considered as a hair item instead of hat, which prevented players from combining both items together.
    * Due to the high amount of players asking for this combination to be brought back, the developers implemented Kansas Pigtails as its own item.
  * A Kansas Pigtails Tree needs to be made by compacting Pigtails and Kansas Curls in a Clothing Compactor, obtaining their seeds and then splicing them together. It is the first tree to use clothing seeds in its recipe.
