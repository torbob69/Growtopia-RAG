The **Watermelon Fidget Spinner Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Super Pineapple Party 2025 on June 5, 2025.
##  Function
Juiced Up!

Out of Juice!

When equipped, the **Watermelon Fidget Spinner Wings** grants the _Double Jump : Watermelon Fidget Spinner Wings_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
