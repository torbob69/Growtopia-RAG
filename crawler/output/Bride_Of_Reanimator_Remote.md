The **Bride of Reanimator Remote** is an unsplicable hand item that was added as part of Halloween Week 2013 on October 28, 2013.
##  Function
It's ALLLIVVVEE!!

It's dead.

When equipped, the **Bride Of Reanimator Remote** grants the _Frankenpunch_ mod, which allows the player to knock back other players further. It will also summon a female Frankenstein's monster pet appears which will follow the player around.
Step 14 of the  Quest Of Steel requires the player to deliver 3 of this item.
