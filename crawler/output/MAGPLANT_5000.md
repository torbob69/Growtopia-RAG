The **MAGPLANT 5000** is an unsplicable block which was the Item Of The Month for February 2018, costing 200,000 gems in the Growtopia Store. It was announced on the 28th of January of the same year.
##  Function
When the player places one in their world, they can wrench it and select an item from their inventory to store inside. Upon placing an item inside, the MAGPLANT will automatically collect identical items that drop from a tree or from a player breaking a block in that world. It does not pick up items dropped by players or events.
Players can wrench the MAGPLANT 5000 to acquire its corresponding remote, which can be used to place items directly from its storage. A single MAGPLANT can hold a maximum of 5,000 items, making it a more efficient per-block storage than simply dropping stacks of items (as each stack contains up to 200 items and a single spot can hold 20 dropped stacks, a player can only drop up to 4000 items in the same spot).
##  Trivia
  * The reason why it has the number 5000 is that it can only store 5000 of the chosen item.
  * Players often create a so-called "BFG" (which stands for "break for gems") using this item, where players give other players access to the MAGPLANT 5000 so that they can take and break blocks in the world while the seeds are collected in another MAGPLANT 5000, leaving the player with gems.
  * If a specific item is dropped in a world and a MAGPLANT 5000 is placed afterward then it won't pick up those items. The MAGPLANT 5000 will only collect items dropped after it is placed.
