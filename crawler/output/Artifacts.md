# Artifacts

**Artifacts** are wearable items that were added as part of the Hidden Riches update. They have their own accessory slot, along with a purple frame and a red equip checkmark.
#  Ancestral Artifacts
* * *
Ancestral artifacts can be upgraded to add special visual effects and gain more powerful abilities (up to level 6).
Ancestral Tesseract of Dimensions
Ancestral Seed of Life
Ancestral Lens of Riches
Ancestral Totem of Wisdom
Ancestral Orb of Time

#  Non-ancestral Artifacts
