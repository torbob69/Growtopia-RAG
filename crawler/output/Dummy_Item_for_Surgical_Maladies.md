The **Dummy Item for Surgical Maladies** is an unobtainable consumable which was added as part of the Blocktor's Orders! update on June 13, 2019.
##  Function
The Dummy Item for Surgical Maladies is only used as an image placeholder for _Torn Punching Muscle_ , _Gem Cuts_ , _Chicken Feet_ , _Grumbleteeth_ , and _Broken Heart_ mods.
