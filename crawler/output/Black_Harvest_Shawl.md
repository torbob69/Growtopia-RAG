The **Black Harvest Shawl** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Black Friday 2021 on November 26, 2021.
##  Function
Black Leaf'ing you behind!

This tree has no black leaves!

When equipped, the **Black Harvest Shawl** grants the _Black Harvest Shawl : Double Jump!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air. While jumping, the cape disappears and a trail of black leaves follows the player. The cape blinks as the player jumps or lands. When the player is standing still, the cape moves from left to right.
