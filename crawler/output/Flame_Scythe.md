# Flame Scythe

The flame represents all of the fallen heroes that served in the Armed Forces. Sponsored by iFlySolo and Ghostego.

Won as prize in **The Brutal Bounce** in the Carnival.
The **Flame Scythe** is an unsplicable hand item.
##  Function
You shoot burning lightning!

Cool it, dude.

When equipped, the **Flame Scythe** grants the _Flame Scythe_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. It also replaces the player's punching animation with black lightning bolts.
Step 18 of the  Quest For The Blade requires the player to deliver 20 of this item.
