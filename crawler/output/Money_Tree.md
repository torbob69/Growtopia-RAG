The **Money Tree** is an unsplicable non-solid foreground block which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2019 on March 15, 2019.
##  Function
When placed, the Money Tree serves a purely aesthetic purpose. Particles of green banknotes fall out whenever a player passes by.
