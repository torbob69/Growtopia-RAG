# Daily Challenges (update)

**Daily Challenges** is an update which was introduced on November 18, 2015. It introduced the titular daily challenges to the game, as well as the forging mechanism.
Growtopia Gazette
> Nov 18th: Daily Challenges are here! Every day, a special challenge will begin that lasts two hours.
> The top three winners will receive unique prizes to celebrate their awesomeness! Oh, this update adds well over 30 new items. Go find 'em.
> - @Seth and @Hamumu
##  Daily Challenges     _Main article:Daily Challenges_
Daily Challenges are daily competitions which last for two hours. During this time, players can participate in these competitions and perform certain tasks, the players with the highest number of points are eligible to win prizes. They come every 23 hours, allowing all timezones to have a chance to participate.
