# Wrench

The amazing tool that can modify Doors, Signs, Time-Space Ruptures, and more. You can't lose it!
Properties
This item cannot be dropped or traded.

Type: Wrench – Tool

Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
The **Wrench** is an unsplicable item which all players have by default, along with the Fist.
##  Function
The Wrench allows players to view information about other players or to change the properties of certain items.
When the Wrench is selected, players can click on any wrench icon in the world to edit or view it. When the wrench icon in the backpack is clicked, the sound blip_lock.wav is played. When wrenching the icon next to a player's name, they will be able to view information about them such as their username and level as well as being able to select options to trade, kick, pull, ban, add as a friend, block/mute, or report player depending on their power in the world.
Wrenching a block or placed item will allow the player to edit the properties of the item. Wrenching a lock will allow the player to change properties such as public build or give people access. There are many other types of blocks which can be wrenched, based on the info of the item (if it says "_This item has properties which can be adjusted with the Wrench._ ")

##  Trivia
  * In the game's early development, the Wrench was given red borders instead of blue.
  * Attempting to trade, trash or recycle one's Wrench will generate the message "_You'd be sorry if you lost that._ ", while attempting to drop the item will generate the message "_You can't drop that._ "
  * Moderators have access to "_Freeze_ " and "_View/Punish_ " buttons when they wrench a player.
  * Super Supporters have bigger, green-coloured Wrenches at the end of their names.
  * As of Player Appreciation Week 2020 Day 4, players can now block other players by wrenching them.
  * As of the second update of the Grow Pass on March 6, 2024, players can now obtain wrench styles and decorations from the Grow Pass.
