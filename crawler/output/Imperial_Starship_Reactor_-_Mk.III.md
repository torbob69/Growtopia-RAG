# Imperial Starship Reactor - Mk.III

Empower your ship in the name of the Emperor with the Imperial reactor! Provides a Ship Rating of 280. One of three components - Helm, Viewscreen, and Reactor - needed to fly a starship. Wrench this to add Star Fuel to your ship!
Properties
This item has special properties you can adjust with the Wrench.
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.
