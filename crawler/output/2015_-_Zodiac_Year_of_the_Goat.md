The **2015 - Zodiac Year of the Goat** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Lunar New Year 2021 on February 4, 2021.
##  Function
Zodiac Powers!

Another Year!

When equipped, the **2015 - Zodiac Year of the Goat** grants the _Speedy & Cookie Hunter : Zodiac Year of the Goat_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and provides a significant chance of doubling fruit drops from Fortune Cookie and Lucky Fortune Cookie Trees.
