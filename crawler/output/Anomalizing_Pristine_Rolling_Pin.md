The **Anomalizing Pristine Rolling Pin** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Winter Clash Series 1.
##  Function
Anomalizing Pristine Rolling Pins are used to score points during Cooking Conquerors. While equipping this item, each successfully cooked food yields 22,000 points. Pristine anomalizers, such as this item, may sometimes break upon usage during their respective Grow Events.
