The **Barbershop Quartet Shirt** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2021 on November 18, 2021.
##  Function
When equipped, the Barbershop Quartet Shirt serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
When equipped together with the Barbershop Quartet Hat and Barbershop Quartet Pants, the player will leave a trail of blue music notes when walking.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Barbershop Quartet set, which consists of the following items:

    * Barbershop Quartet Hat (Hat)

    * Barbershop Quartet Shirt (Shirt)

    * Barbershop Quartet Pants (Pants)
