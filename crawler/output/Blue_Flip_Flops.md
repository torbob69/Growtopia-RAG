The **Blue Flip Flops** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of SummerFest 2016 on June 30, 2016.
##  Function
When equipped, the Blue Flip Flops serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * Blue Flip Flops is one of three flip flop items in _Growtopia_ , alongside Green Flip Flops and Red Flip Flops.
    * Each flip flops may or may not represent the three words in _Growtopia'_ s slogan, with red representing punch, blue representing build, and green representing grow.
