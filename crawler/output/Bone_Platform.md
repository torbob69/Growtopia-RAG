# Bone Platform

Are your guests tired of standing on shaky ground? Well, they'll be shaking in their shoes when they step foot on these terrifying platforms!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.

Lowly Corruption

Collect corrupted souls by sacrificing items with a total rarity of at least 16,000-31,999 in the different Sacrificial Wells found all around the TOMBOFGROWGANOTH to get this tier.
May drop in sets of **9** each.
Great Corruption

Collect corrupted souls by sacrificing items with a total rarity of at least 32,000-47,999 in the different Sacrificial Wells found all around the TOMBOFGROWGANOTH to get this tier.
May drop in sets of **12** each.
Ultimate Corruption

Collect corrupted souls by sacrificing items with a total rarity of at least 48,000 or higher in the different Sacrificial Wells found all around the TOMBOFGROWGANOTH to get this tier.
May drop in sets of **15** each.
The **Bone Platform** is a unsplicable platform which was added as part of Halloween Week 2019. It has a chance to be obtained from completing the TOMBOFGROWGANOTH.
##  Function
A Bone Platform behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath.
