The **Pinata Pal** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2016 on May 2, 2016.
##  Function
My pal is stuffed with candy!

All outta candy.

When equipped, the Pinata Pal grants the _Candy Blast_ mod, which replaces the player's punch. Additionally, a pet pinata appears and follows the player around.
