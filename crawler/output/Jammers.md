# Jammers

This is a **disambiguation** page.
There are more than one subjects in the **Growtopia Wiki** using this name.
Jammers are machine blocks which help world owners to block several things they don't want.
There are 4 types:
Items: What it doesSignal Jammer: Prevents the world from showing up in the world menu, and changes the world name in broadcasts or messages into JAMMED!.
Punch Jammer: Prevents visitors from pushing and pulling each other by punching around.
Zombie Jammer: Prevents infected visitors from infecting others in the world while the pandemic isn't running.
Balloon Jammer: Prevents players from throwing War Balloons in the world.
There are also several other items which act like a jammer, but don't have the looks of one:
Items: What it doesAntigravity Generator: Disables gravity in the world, and lets people jump as much as they want.
Ghost Charm: Prevents ghosts from spawning in the world.
Guardian Pineapple: Prevents consumables to be used in the world; was added to prevent people from exploding Pineapple Bodies in the world.
Firehouse: Prevents fires from spreading, gradually puts out fire active before the Firehouse was placed in the world.
Mini-Mod: Prevents people from dropping items in the world.
Xenonite Crystal: Blocking/granting certain Mods chosen.
