The **Ladybug Trampoline** is a splicable solid foreground trampoline block which was added as part of Valentine's Week 2025 on February 13, 2025.
##  Function
Jumping on a trampoline block such as a Ladybug Trampoline will cause the player to bounce, and by holding the jump button, the player can jump even higher. The _High Jump_ mod also boosts one's jump height on a trampoline block.
##  Trivia
  * The item's description is a reference to the main character from the show Miraculous: Tales of Ladybug & Cat Noir.
