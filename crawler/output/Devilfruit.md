The **Devilfruit** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Halloween Week 2014 on October 30, 2014.
##  Function
You little devil...

Off the naughty list!

When consumed on a player, it applies the _Devil Horns_ mod for 30 minutes to the player, just like how Devil Horns works.
A Devilfruit can be fed to a Silkworm to give it a red color, as well as giving the Silkworm Devil Horns.
