The **Pristine Potion** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Role Up! update.
##  Function
Gain extra Points from pristine anomalizers!

Your stomach's rumbling for more potion goodness!

When consumed, it will grant the player the Food: Anomalizers mod which will increase the points by 10% the player's gain from ALL pristine anomalizers (when used during an active Season Event).
