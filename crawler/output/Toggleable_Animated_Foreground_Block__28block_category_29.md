# Toggleable Animated Foreground Block (block category)

A **toggleable animated foreground block** is a type of foreground block which can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a toggleable animated foreground block will change its appearance, showing a 2-frame animation, and may perform another ability, if available.

##  Trivia
  * Back in the early days of _Growtopia_ , toggleable animated foreground blocks didn't have an option that only permits players with access to toggle the blocks, thus allowing any visiting player to turn them on or off. Due to player feedback,[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Toggleable_Animated_Foreground_Block_\(block_category\)#cite_note-1) ex-developer Seth decided to implement the option.
