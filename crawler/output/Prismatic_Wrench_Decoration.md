The **Prismatic Wrench Decoration** is a wrench decoration which was added as part of the Grow Pass second update on March 6, 2024.
##  Function
When equipped through the 'Wrench Customization' tab when wrenching oneself, the Prismatic Wrench Decoration adds pink, yellow, and green sparkles around the player's wrench connected to their name.
##  Trivia
  * The Prismatic Wrench Decoration is the first wrench decoration added to the game as part of the Grow Pass second update.
