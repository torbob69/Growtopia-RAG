The **Spring Clash Finale Ticket** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as a part of Spring Clash 2020. It can be obtained after completing a branch which can be found by tapping on the Leaderboard Button and tapping on "Spring Clash Rewards". The player needs to buy everything in the branch before receiving the Legendary Clash Key & Finale Ticket.
##  Function
When consumed, it will direct the player to the FINALEWORLD in which the player has 25 minutes to finish the parkour - the farther the player goes, the better and rarer prizes the player has a chance to receive.
