The **Weather Machine - Black Hole** is an unsplicable non-solid weather machine foreground block which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2023 Day 2 on August 29, 2023.
##  Function
As a weather machine, the Weather Machine - Black Hole can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, it will change its appearance, transforming the world's background weather into a scenery of an animated black hole.
