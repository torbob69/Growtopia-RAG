The **Celebration Charm** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2024 on November 21, 2024.
##  Function
Boom, boom, boom!

Let your colors burst!

When equipped, the **Celebration Charm** grants the _Build Range + Light Source: Celebration Charm_ mod, which allows the player to build one tile further and emit light in dark areas.
