**Buckets of War Balloons** are unsplicable consumables which were added as part of Balloon Warz 2017. There are 3 types of buckets:
•  Bucket of Small War Balloons
•  Bucket of Medium War Balloons
•  Bucket of Large War Balloons
##  Function
A Bucket of War Balloons is only used to store War Balloons in a compact fashion. Despite being categorized as a consumable, a bucket does not do anything when consumed anywhere on the screen.
