##  Function
Perfectly balanced, as all things should be!

When equipped, the **Muni's Caspiro** grants the _Speedy + Climbing : Muni's Caspiro_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and cling onto Climbing Walls by pressing the jump button. When worn, players can switch between black and white shoes by equipping and unequipping the item.
