The **Wall Like An Egyptian** is a splicable and farmable background block which was added as part of February 2013 update.
##  Function
When placed, the Wall Like An Egyptian serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
A Bountiful Jungle Temple Tree has a small chance to be made when splicing a Wall Like An Egyptian Tree.

##  Trivia
  * The name of the item is a play on the hit song Walk Like an Egyptian by the American band The Bangles.
  * The  Wall Like An Egyptian's design is based on the  Sandstone Wall, but with hieroglyphics carved into it. The hieroglyphs are randomized when the block is placed.
