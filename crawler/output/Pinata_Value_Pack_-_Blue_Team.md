# Pinata Value Pack - Blue Team
During Cinco De Mayo Week

|
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Pinata Value Pack - Blue Team** for **30,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"Make a fiesta all of your own and invite all your friends with 5 Pinata Whistles and 2 Block de Mayo Blocks to celebrate with. There is a chance of getting a Rare Lava Pinata item from this pack."
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Pinata Value Pack - Blue Team** was added during Cinco De Mayo Week 2023 on May 5, 2023.
The first incarnation of the Pinata Value Pack - Blue Team contained the following:
  * Pinata Whistle ×5
  * Block De Mayo Block ×2
  * Blue Volcanic Hat ×1
  * Lava Pinata ×1 (chance)
