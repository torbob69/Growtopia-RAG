The **Holy Jeans** is an unsplicable pants item which was added alongside the Block Party 2 update.
##  Function
The holiest of jeans!

Holy moly!

When equipped, this item grants the Halo! mod to the player, which adds a golden halo over the player's head for an indefinite amount of time, unlike the Golden Halo consumable.
##  Trivia
  * The name of this item is a portmanteau of "holey" and "holy". The former refers to the appearance of the item itself, while the latter to its mod.
