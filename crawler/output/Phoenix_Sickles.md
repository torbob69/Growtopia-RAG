The **Phoenix Sickles** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of SummerFest 2023 on July 13, 2023.
##  Function
Winged Warrior!

Weak Warrior!

When equipped, the **Phoenix Sickles** grants the _Fireproof + Punch Range: Phoenix Sickles_ mod, which allows the player to take 50% less damage from lava pain blocks and punch one tile further.
