The **Pearl Treasured Octopus** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Beach Party 2024 on June 21st, 2024.
##  Function
The power of the pearl in the palm of my feet!

Need a doctor!

When equipped, the **Pearl Treasured Octopus** grants the _Speedy + High Jump: Pearl Treasured Octopus_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and jump one tile higher.
