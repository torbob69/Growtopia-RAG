The **Mooncake Magnificence** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2020 on September 24, 2020.
##  Function
Let there be balance.

I feel dizzy.

When equipped, the **Mooncake Magnificence** grants the _Magnificent Favor: Mooncake Magnificence_ mod. If the player manages to find a Balance Mooncake, there is a chance that the amount of Balance Mooncakes dropped will be doubled or tripled. Once this occurs, however, the Mooncake Magnificence breaks. However, the item also decreases the chances of Peace, Harmony, Longevity and Prosperity Mooncakes dropping from trees.
When a Mooncake Magnificence ends up doubling a Balance Mooncake drop, a Mooncake Bundle will also be dropped as a bonus.
The  Harvest Festival Exclusive Mission - I can seed clearly now my gun I've got! mission requires 150 of this item.
##  Trivia
  * When enough Mooncake Magnificences are bought in total, a community goal is achieved, thus unlocking a temporary sale in the Store that allows players to purchase 3 Mooncake Magnificences for 12,500 gems. The sale lasts for two hours, after which the counter resets.
  * Harvest Festival 2021 saw an increase to the price of Mooncake Magnificence.
    * Each single purchase now costs 5,000 Gems instead of 4,000.
    * Each Magnificent Sale Pack now costs 12,500 Gems instead of 10,000.
