The **Double Growsaber** is a splicable hand item which was added alongside the Vegas Pack on May 24, 2013. It is the second rarest Growsaber in the series.
##  Function
Float like a butterfly, sting like a punch in the face.

Boxing time over.

When equipped, the **Double Growsaber** grants the _Fist O' Fury_ mod, which allows the player to knock back other players further.
