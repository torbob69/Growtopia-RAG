This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Flaming Horsie** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2017 Day 5.
##  Function
You can move faster!

You are slow again.

When equipped, the **Flaming Horsie** grants the _Speedy_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.
