# Dragon Knight's Helm

Forged in a master blacksmith's furnace and stamped with runes of protection, a Dragon Knight's suit of armor is their last and best line of defense against the horrid flames and claws of their scaly foes! This helm is but one link of many in that chain of defense, a perfect way to keep your noggin safe! Also doubles as a really cool motorcycle helmet.

The **Dragon Knight's Helm** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of the Dragon Invasion update on February 4, 2019.
##  Function
When equipped, the Dragon Knight's Helm serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
