The **Ring Of Shrinking** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the B.O.O. Update on May 1, 2018.
##  Function
You feel your body shrinking.

You grow back to your normal size.

When equipped, this item grants the _Ring of Shrinking_ mod, which allows the player to shrink to the size similar to a Mini-You. However, some auras and effects may not work while using the ring.
Step 16 of the  Quest For Candour requires the player to deliver 5 of this item.
