The **Tousled Hair** is an unsplicable hair item that was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2020 Day 7.
##  Function
When equipped, the Tousled Hair serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * As part of the Quality of Life Changes update on May 20, 2022, the **Tousled Hair** was added for sale in the Sales-Man under Wolfworld items for 120 Wolf Tickets. This was later removed from the Sales-Man 30 minutes later because there were multiple complaints of the price being inconsistent.
