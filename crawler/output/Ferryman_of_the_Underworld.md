The **Ferryman of the Underworld** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of the Voucher Dayz update on October 29, 2021. The item was formally introduced on October 30, 2021, the date when the event arrived.
##  Function
Come sail away to the after party!

Wet Feet!

When equipped, the **Ferryman of the Underworld** grants the _Punch Range : Ferryman of the Underworld_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further.
##  Trivia
  * The Ferryman of the Underworld is based on the vessel used by the Hades' ferryman, Charon, in order to transport the souls of the newly deceased across the river Acheron, which was said to divide the world of the living from the world of the dead.
