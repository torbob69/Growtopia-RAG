The **NewWool's Whip of Knit** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of Wonder Week 2025 - Day 1 on October 6, 2025.
##  Function
Watch me whip!

Watch me nae nae!

When equipped, the **NewWool's Whip of Knit** grants the _Punch Range + Punch Damage : NewWool's Whip of Knit_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further and break blocks faster.
