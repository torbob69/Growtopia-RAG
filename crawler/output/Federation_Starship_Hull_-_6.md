The **Federation Starship Hull - 6** is an unobtainable and unbreakable platform foreground block which was added as part of Star Trek Prodigy on June 27, 2022. It is used as the outer part for the Protostar Starship.
##  Function
A Federation Starship Hull - 6 behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath.
