The **Parrot Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2014 on May 2, 2014.
##  Function
You can jump in mid-air! It's like flying, but less.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Parrot Wings** grants the _Double Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
Step 19 of the  Quest Of The Heavens requires the player to deliver 50 of this item.
##  Trivia
  * The Parrot Wings is based on the red-and-green macaw (_Ara chloropterus_), a type of parrot well known for having red, green, and blue feathers on its wings.
  * The description of Parrot Wings contains a reference to "Polly want a cracker," a well-known phrase which seems to have originated in the 1800s.[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Parrot_Wings#cite_note-1)
