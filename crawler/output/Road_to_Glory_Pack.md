# Road to Glory Pack
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Road to Glory Pack** for **$24.99** in the Growtopia Store.
"Earn Gem rewards when you level up. Every 10 levels you will get additional Gem rewards up to Level 50! Claim all rewards instantly if you are over level 50!! 1.92M Gems in total!!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Road to Glory Pack** is a one-time purchase Store Pack that was added during March 1, 2020. It costs 24.99 USD, and gives the player 120,000 gems immediately upon purchase, starting from level 10 will award the player 120,000 gems and increase 120,000 gems after each succeeding 10 levels until level 50. Players who are above level 50 upon purchasing the pack will automatically give the player 1,920,000 gems.
##  Trivia
  * As part of Player Appreciation Week 2023 Day 4, the Road to Glory Pack now rewards the player up to 1,920,000 Gems, previously 1,600,000 Gems.
