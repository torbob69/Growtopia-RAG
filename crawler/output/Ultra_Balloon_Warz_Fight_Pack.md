# Ultra Balloon Warz Fight Pack
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Ultra Balloon Warz Fight Pack** for **30,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"Everything you need to take the fight to the next level in the Balloon Warz! Introducing the all new Ultra Performance Enhancing Hot Cocoa that increases the points gained when using a Balloon-Frosting War Glove for 15 min! However, the War Glove is prone to failure and may break... Use with care!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
**Ultra Balloon Warz Fight Pack** is a pack that was added as part of Brr-lloon Warz 2020.
It includes the following:
  * Balloon-Frosting War Glove x5
  * Ultra Performance Enhancing Hot Cocoa x1

##  Trivia
  * Prior to Brr-lloon Warz 2022, Ultra Balloon Warz Fight Pack can only be purchased during the preparation phase of Balloon Warz.
