The **Pet Bandage** is an unsplicable component which was added as part of the Pet Battle Update on March 19, 2025.
##  Function
A Pet Bandage is generated every hour and players can hold a maximum of five pet bandages. One bandage can be used to heal the whole battle pet team after performing a pet battle. Players can do this by wrenching themselves, clicking on _Battle Pets_ and then _Leashed Pets_ to acquire the health status of their team.
