The **Cruisin' Crab** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2022 Day 3 on August 17, 2022.
##  Function
Cruisin' for a bruisin'!

Left in the sand!

When equipped, the **Cruisin' Crab** grants the _Punch Damage: Cruisin' Crab_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster.
