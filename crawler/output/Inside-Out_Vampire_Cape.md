The **Inside-Out Vampire Cape** in an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2015 on October 29, 2015. It has an extremely low chance of obtaining when consuming 25 Cape Tatters on oneself.
##  Function
You can jump in mid-air! It's like flying, but less.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Inside-Out Vampire Cape** grants the _Double Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
##  Trivia
  * Upon consumption of 25 cape tatters and getting a Inside-Out Vampire Cape, a message will appear saying "_You screwed up and made your cape inside-out!_ ".
