The **Dumplings Hat** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Lunar New Year 2022 on February 1, 2022.
##  Function
Your ancestors would be proud!

Grandma would be ashamed!

When equipped, the Dumplings Hat grants the _Cooking Skills: Dumplings Hat_ mod, which increases cooked food's quality by 3%. This effect stacks with other items that provide a similar function, such as the Sweet Potato Tots and Exquisite Rolling Pin.
