# Blarney Pebble

The legend goes that if you gather up ten of these pebbles, you can charm a leprechaun to be your friend!
Properties
This item cannot be dropped or traded.
This item never drops any seeds.

Type: Consumable

| Can be obtained as an additional reward from touching the Blarney Stone located at each of the 10 different BLARNEY worlds (BLARNEY1-BLARNEY10) during St. Patrick's Week.
Players are limited to one Blarney Stone from each BLARNEY world everyday.   |
The **Blarney Pebble** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2014 on March 14, 2014.

##  Trivia
  * Obtaining a Pet Leprechaun for the first time by consuming 10 Blarney Pebbles will give the player the _Little Green Man_ achievement.
  * If the player attempts to consume fewer than 10 Blarney Pebbles at once, a message will appear saying "_Legend says that you need 10 Blarney Pebbles!_ "
  * Upon usage of ten Blarney Pebbles, a message will appear saying "_ZING! A leprechaun appears!_ " or "_ZING! Freckles appears!_ ", and the Pet Leprechaun or Freckles will be instantly equipped.
