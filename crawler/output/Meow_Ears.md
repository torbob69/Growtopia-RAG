The **Meow Ears** is an untradeable hair item which was added in version 1.18.1 on April 26, 2013.
##  Function
When equipped, the Meow Ears serves a fully cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The Meow Ears was the exclusive item of the ex-moderator Meow. It was released to the public as part of Player Appreciation Week 2018.
  * The Meow Ears was the first ever Moderator Item given to a moderator.
