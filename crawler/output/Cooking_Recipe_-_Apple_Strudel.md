The **Cooking Recipe - Apple Strudel** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Grow4Good Week 2021 on April 15, 2021.
##  Function
This cooking recipe's description guides the player on how to cook an Apple Strudel. It is permanent and will not be consumed when the player cooks the item.
