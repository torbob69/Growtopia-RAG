The **Royal Shiftcruiser** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as March 2025's Item Of The Season on March 1, 2025.
##  Function
More than meets the eyes!

In disguise!

When equipped, the **Royal Shiftcruiser** grants the _High Jump + Surfing + Speedy: Royal Shiftcruiser_ mod, which allows the player to jump one tile higher, float on water for three seconds, and move faster on land.
