A bizarre creature out of myth! The chimera may be a mash-up of many animals, but every last part is filled with an equal amount of friendship for you!

Daily Challenge

Place in the top three of the **Battler's Beatdown** Guild Daily Challenge, only if the player had contributed points to the challenge.
The **Chimera's Call** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of the Challengers Approach! update.
##  Function
Hello, is anybody there?

Hello? Hey, where did you go!?

When equipped, a chimera pet appears and follows the player around. The Chimera's Call grants the _Calling Chimera_ mod, which replaces the player's punching effect with fires being spewed out from the chimera.
