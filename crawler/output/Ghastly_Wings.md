The **Ghastly Wings** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2022 on October 22. 2022.
##  Function
Raise the dead!

Put them to sleep!

When equipped, the **Ghastly Wings** grants the _Double Jump : Ghastly Wings!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
##  Trivia
  * The item is based on a Dr. Strange variant in the superhero movie, Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness.
