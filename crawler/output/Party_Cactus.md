The **Party Cactus** is a solid pain foreground block which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2014 on May 2, 2014.
##  Function
Being a type of pain block, a Party Cactus knocks the player back and stings the player until the player stops all motion. A player will die after making contact with a pain block five times in quick succession (recovering from damage takes roughly 5 seconds). The _Fireproof_ mod will not allow the player to take more hits, unlike with lava pain blocks.
The Party Cactus features an automatic 2-frame animation of itself dancing.
