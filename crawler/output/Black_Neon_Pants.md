The **Black Neon Pants** is an unsplicable pants item which was added as part of Black Friday 2022 on November 25, 2022.
##  Function
When equipped, the Black Neon Pants serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Black Neon set, which consists of the following items:

    * Black Neon Hoodie (Shirt)

    * Black Neon Pants (Pants)

    * Black Neon Hightops (Feet)
