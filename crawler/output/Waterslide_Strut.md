The **Waterslide Strut** is an unsplicable slippery non-solid platform block that was introduced in the Balloon Warz 2018 update on December 7, 2018.
##  Function
A Waterslide Strut behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath. In addition, being a slippery block, walking on a Slip 'n' Slide Slime Floor causes the player to slide.
