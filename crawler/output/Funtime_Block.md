Wheeeee! This block spins wildly when somebody steps on it.

Obtained for every 50 Carnival games won.
The **Funtime Block** is an unsplicable solid spinning foreground block which was added as part of the Artsy April update on April 2, 2016.
##  Function
When a player walks over a Funtime Block, the block rotates in the direction that the player is walking, just like the Hour Glass.
