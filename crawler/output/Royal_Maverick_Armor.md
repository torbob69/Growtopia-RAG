The **Royal Maverick Armor** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as December 2025's Item Of The Season on December 1, 2025.
##  Function
Bleeding for vengeance

Just raw knuckles

When equipped, the **Royal Maverick Armor** grants the _Punch Range + Double Jump: Royal Maverick Armor_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further, jump a second time in mid-air, and switch between its wearable form and its morph form by wrenching oneself and clicking 'Transform'.
