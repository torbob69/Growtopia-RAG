The **Wrecked Wrench Decoration** is a wrench decoration which was added as part of the June Season on June 1st, 2024.
##  Function
When equipped through the 'Wrench Customization' tab when wrenching oneself, the Wrecked Wrench Decoration adds metal burrs around the player's wrench connected to their name.
