The **Spooky Window** is a unsplicable non-solid toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block which was added as part of Halloween Week 2020.
##  Function
Being a toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block, a Spooky Window can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Spooky Window will change its appearance, showing a 3-framed animation of itself flashing eerily.
This item, alongside several other Halloween Week blocks, are considered derivatives of the Haunted House block, which was added in 2013.
