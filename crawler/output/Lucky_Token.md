The **Lucky Token** is an unsplicable non-solid Lucky Token foreground block which was added on Chinese New Year 2018.
##  Function
Placing a Lucky Token in a world would enter the player in a raffle. Placing more would improve their chance of winning the raffle. The items given out from the raffle are:
  * 100 Draconic Wings
  * 200 Builder's Locks
  * 500 Vending Machines
  * 700 gems (×5,000)

##  Trivia
  * As of Chinese New Year 2019, this item can no longer be obtained from splicing a Paper Dividing Wall Seed with a Coin Door Seed anymore. Attempting to do so will result in the tree of the respective year's unique Lucky Token instead.
