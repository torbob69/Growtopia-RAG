# Blast (disambiguation)

This is a **disambiguation** page.
There are more than one subjects in the **Growtopia Wiki** using this name.
**Blasts** are consumable items that, upon consumption, generate a world that fits the blast's theme. The different types of blasts include:
Item — World — RecipeThermonuclear Blast — Generates a cleared world with the Main Door moved to either the far right or the far left of the world. — 15,000 Gems found in the Growtopia Store
Beach Blast — Generates a beach-themed world that includes 1-3 Treasure Chests that contains an item from SummerFest. — Purchasing the Beach Blast Bingo! for 20,000 gems found in the Growtopia Store (only available during SummerFest.
Harvest Moon Blast — Generates a Harvest-themed that includes random trees planted. — 8,000 Gems found in the Growtopia Store (only available during Harvest Festival.
Mars Blast — Generates a Mars-themed world, which has low gravity, allowing players to jump higher in the world. — 15,000 Gems found in the Growtopia Store
Desert Blast — Generates a desert-themed world that includes either a Sungate or Silver Idol. — From a special event.
Undersea Blast — Generates an undersea-themed world filled with Water. — 15,000 Gems found in the Growtopia Store
Jungle Blast — Generates a jungle-themed world that includes either an Adventurer's Whip or Minecart. — From a special event.
Imperial Starship Blast — Generates one of three Imperial Starship worlds that includes an Imperial Starship Helm - Mk.I, Imperial Starship Reactor - Mk.I, and an Imperial Starship Viewscreen - Mk.I. — 10,000 Gems found in the Growtopia Store.
Stellarix Starship Blast — Generates one of three Stellarix Starship worlds that includes an Imperial Starship Helm - Mk.I, Imperial Starship Reactor - Mk.I, and an Imperial Starship Viewscreen - Mk.I. — Successfully performing a Star Voyage.
Greezak Starship Blast — Generates one of three Greezak Starship worlds that includes an Imperial Starship Helm - Mk.I, Imperial Starship Reactor - Mk.I, and an Imperial Starship Viewscreen - Mk.I. — Successfully performing a Star Voyage.
HyperTech Starship Blast — Generates one of three Hypertech Starship worlds that includes an Imperial Starship Helm - Mk.I, Imperial Starship Reactor - Mk.I, and an Imperial Starship Viewscreen - Mk.I. — Successfully performing a Star Voyage.
Monochrome Blast — Generates black and white version of a regular world, that includes Monochromatic Dirt, Monochromatic Cave Background, Monochromatic Lava, and an Monochromatic Bedrock. — 25,000 Gems found in the Growtopia Store (available only during Black Friday.
Cave Blast — Generates a dark cave-themed world filled with Cave Dirt, Dark Cave Background, Aqua Cave Crystal and Golden Treasure Hoards. — 30,000 Gems found in the Growtopia Store.
SurgWorld Blast — Generates a surgery-themed world filled with Surgical Blocks, Surgical Backgrounds, Aggregate Concrete, House Entrances, Staircases and Hospital Windows. — 10,000 Gems found in the Growtopia Store.
Treasure Blast — Generates a frozen cliff-themed world filled with Frozen Stone Cliffss, Glacier Backgrounds, Rune Stones, Rune Carved Stone Pillars, Altars, Hidden Treasures, Icicles, Ices and Waters. — 10,000 Gems found in the Growtopia Store.
Bountiful Blast — Generates a world similar to the regular world which contains Fertile Soil Blocks, Signs, Bountiful Bamboo Background Trees, Bountiful Flowering Garland Trees, Bountiful White Doll's Eyes Trees and Bountiful Jungle Temple Trees. — 5,000 Gems found in the Growtopia Store.
Protostar Starship Blast — Generates a protostar starship blasted world where players can do interactive Startopia missions using the same Star Tools. — Purchase the **Star Trek Starter Pack** for **14.99 USD** in the Growtopia Store. (Only during Star Trek Prodigy)
Candyland Blast — Generates a world filled with Colorful Cakes, Colorful Cake Slices, Colorful Cake Toppings, Cherry on Top, Hot Fudge, Bedrock Candys, and Data Bedrock Candy. — 15 Grow Vouchers in the Growtopia Store during Voucher Dayz.
##  Trivia
  * A blast cannot be used to change an already existing world into the blast’s theme, on the contrary, creating one requires a world that has not been entered before.
