The **Crystal Crown** is an unsplicable hat item that was added as August 2021's Item Of The Month on August 1, 2021.
##  Function
Spikes and Sparkles!

No longer shiny!

When equipped, the **Crystal Crown** grants the _Punch Damage + Punch Power: Crystal Crown_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and knock back other players further. When punching, the player throws crystals instead of using their Fist. When walking, the player leaves a trail of crystals as well as a giant crystal particle whenever they touch the ground.
