# Chicken Plow

Ancient magic has unlocked the secrets of advanced chicken farming! Now, for the first time, your chicken will PLANT seeds instead of eating them! (When equipped, will plant whatever seed you have selected as you move!)
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be transmuted.
Data

Type: Feet – Clothes
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: Full Collision
Hardness: **0 Hits** **0 Hits** – Restores after **0s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Riding Chicken **(×1)** ,
Dear John Tractor **(×2)** and
Bridle Design **(×1)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Chicken Plow** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of the Hidden Riches update on September 1, 2017.
##  Function
When equipped with a stack of seeds selected in one's inventory, it will automatically plant all the seeds, provided that the player has access to the world.
##  Trivia
  * Doom Plow is a spookier redesign of  Chicken Plow. Both of them serve the same function.
