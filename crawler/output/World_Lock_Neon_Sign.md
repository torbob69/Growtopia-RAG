# World Lock Neon Sign

A glowing neon sign that looks like a World Lock.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item has special properties you can adjust with the Wrench.
Data: Paint PreviewType — Sign – Foreground — Aqua Blue Charcoal Green Purple Red Varnish Yellow
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **4 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **4s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**
**1h**
Paint Preview Aqua Blue Charcoal Green Purple Red Varnish Yellow
Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
World Lock **(×1)** ,
Neon Lights **(×5)** and
Carnival Sign **(×1)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **World Lock Neon Sign** is an unsplicable non-solid sign foreground block which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2023 Day 4 on August 31, 2023.
##  Function
As a sign, a World Lock Neon Sign allows players with access to it to edit its message for other players to view.
