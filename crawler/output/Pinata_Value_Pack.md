# Pinata Value Pack

The Pinata Value Pack is an store pack that is exclusive to Cinco De Mayo Week.
The **Pinata Value Pack** was added during Cinco De Mayo Week 2020 on May 1, 2020.
During Cinco De Mayo Week

|
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Pinata Value Pack** for **30,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"Make a fiesta all of your own and invite all your friends with 5 Pinata Whistles and 2 Block de Mayo Blocks to celebrate with. There is a chance of getting a Rare Lava Pinata item from this pack."
Every purchase will give **1** item.
 |
## Items
  * Pinata Whistle ×5
  * Block De Mayo Block ×2
  * Lava Pinata ×1 (chance)

##  Trivia
  * As of Cinco De Mayo Week 2021, the Pinata Value Pack's price increased to 30,000 Gems, previously 25,000, and included a chance to obtain a Lava Pinata.
  * As of Cinco De Mayo Week 2023, the Pinata Value Pack was splitted into two separate packs: **Red** and **Blue**. Each pack now also contains a color variant of that year's exclusive Value Pack item.
