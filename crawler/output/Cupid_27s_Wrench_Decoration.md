The **Cupid's Wrench Decoration** is a wrench decoration which was added as part of the February Season on February 1, 2025.
##  Function
When equipped through the 'Wrench Customization' tab when wrenching oneself, the Cupid's Wrench Decoration adds a bow of a cupid in the player's wrench connected to their name.
