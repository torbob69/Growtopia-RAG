##  New Changes
New changes implemented include:
  * Extractor Dynamo, Flaming Aura, Guild Night Stallion, Growmoji Peace Sign, Guild Steed, Growmoji Terrified Face, Growmoji Trollface, and World Architect are no longer obtained from the Summer Awards, as they are made CLASHPARKOUR prizes instead.
  * Amulet of Force, Aurora Wings, Dancemaster's Crown, Glove of the Giants, Scepter of the Honor Guard, Smoog the Great Dragon, and Squirrel King are no longer obtained from the Summer Clash Rewards Store, as they are made CLASHPARKOUR prizes instead.
  * Removed the fifth branch from the Summer Clash Rewards Store; all its items are now unobtainable.

##  Trivia
  * Summer Clash 2019 is the fourth seasonal clash held in Growtopia.
