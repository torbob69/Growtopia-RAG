The **Growmlin** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Halloween Week 2022 on October 22. 2022.
##  Function
Annoying annoying annoying!

Batteries dead!

When equipped, the **Growmlin** grants the _Punch Range: Growmlin_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further.
##  Trivia
  * The Growmlin's appearance is a reference to the folkloric mischievous creatures from the American black comedy horror film named Gremlins.
