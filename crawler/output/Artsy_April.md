# Artsy April

Growtopia Gazette
> April 2nd: The Ultimate Update - Oops, I guess we forgot these other items we also made for April. It's Artsy April!
> Get ready to paint the town red, blue, or whatever color you like with the amazing new art-related items!
> HEY: As with all new cool features, people will use it to scam. So don't trust the color of seeds or items to tell what something is, only use the trade system!
> - @Seth and @Hamumu
**Artsy April** is was monthly update which was introduced on April 2, 2016.
##  New Additions
New additions introduced include:
###  Painter's Pack
The Painter's Pack's store button.
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Painter's Pack** for **30,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"Want to paint your world? This pack includes a Rare Paintbrush, and 20 buckets of random paint colors (may include Varnish, to clean up your messes)! You can paint any block in your world different colors to personalize it."
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Painter's Pack** can be purchased from the Store for 30,000 Gems. It includes a Paintbrush and 20 Paint Buckets of random colors (including Varnish).
