The **Galaxy Aura** is a back item which was introduced as March's Item of The Month for 2020.
##  Function
The weight of the galaxy is on your shoulder!

A weight is removed!

When equipped, the **Galaxy Aura** grants the _Double Jump + Punch Pull: Gravity Pull_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and pull other players towards them upon punching them.
