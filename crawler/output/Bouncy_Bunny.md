Easter Egg - Polka Dots **(×1)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Bouncy Bunny** is an unsplicable solid trampoline foreground block which was added as part of Easter Week 2015.
##  Function
Jumping on a trampoline block such as a Bouncy Bunny will cause the player to bounce, and by holding the jump button, the player can jump even higher. The _High Jump_ mod also boosts one's jump height on a trampoline block.
