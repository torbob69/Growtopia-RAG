##  Function
When placed along with a Fish Tank Port, the owner of a world containing a Fish Tank Port can wrench it to insert fish and crabs into the Fish Tanks, allowing them to move freely inside. They can also remove any selected fish and move them to their inventory from the menu.
The only player allowed to access a Fish Tank Port is the owner themself. As a co-owner, wrenching one would only display the contents of the tank, while punching the block would cause the message "_Only the world owner can mess with fishies!_ " to appear. No damage would be done to the Fish Tank Port.

##  Trivia
  * If the graphics settings are lowered, the fish may become invisible.
  * The **Fish Tank** is farmable.
  * When the **Fish Tank** is broken, the fish will seem to shudder and glow slightly before teleporting to another part of the tank which is not broken.
  * There is an option for perfect or megafishes to glow.
