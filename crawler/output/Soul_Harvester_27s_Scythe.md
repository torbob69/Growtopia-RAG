The **Soul Harvester's Scythe** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2023 on September 21, 2023.
##  Function
Become the Reaper!

Mere mortal!

When equipped, the **Soul Harvester's Scythe** grants the _Punch Damage : Soul Harvester's Scythe_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. Upon breaking blocks, the sprite of the broken block will fly away with a pair of  Teeny Angel Wings.
