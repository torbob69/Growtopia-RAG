# Assassin's Creed Costumes
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Assassin's Creed Costumes** for **500,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"All you need to dress as Altair, Bayek and Eivor!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Assassin's Creed Costumes** is an item pack which was added as part of Assassin's Creed 15th Anniversary Celebration on September 23, 2022.
##  Items
The pack contains the following items:
  * Altair's Hood
  * Altair's Vest
  * Altair's Trousers
  * Altair's Boots
  * Bayek's Hood
  * Bayek's Vest
  * Bayek's Trousers
  * Bayek's Boots
  * Eivor's Hair
  * Eivor's Tattoos
  * Eivor's Vest
  * Eivor's Trousers
  * Eivor's Boots
