# Prehistoric Pack
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Prehistoric Pack** for **5,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"Travel way back in time with this pack, including full Caveman and Cavewoman outfits, 10 Cliffside, 5 Rock Platform, a Cave Entrance, 3 Prehistoric Palm, and a Rare Sabertooth Growtopian (that's a mask of sorts). Unleash your inner monster!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The Prehistoric Pack is a store pack introduced on 13 December 2013, along with the Prehistoric Update. It is available in the Growtopia Store for 5,000 Gems.
##  Items
The pack contains the following items:
  * Sabertooth Growtopian x1 (Rare)
  * Caveman Club x1
  * Cavewoman Hair x1
  * Caveman Hair x1
  * Sabertooth-Skin Toga x1
  * Fuzzy Bikini Top x1
  * Fuzzy Bikini Bottom x1
  * Cave Entrance x1
  * Cliffside x10
  * Rock Platform x5
  * Prehistoric Palm x3

##  Trivia
  * The Mini Mammoth Leash, Riding Raptor, Kerjigger, Frozen DNA Fragment A, Frozen DNA Fragment B, and the Ice Cube were also implemented into the game alongside this pack.
