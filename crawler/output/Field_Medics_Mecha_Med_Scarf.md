The **Field Medics Mecha Med Scarf** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of Grow4Good Week 2021 on April 15, 2021.
##  Function
Heres to a speedy recovery!

No medical enhancements here!

When equipped, the **Field Medics Mecha Med Scarf** grants the _Speedy + Quick Recover: Field Medics Mecha Med Scarf!_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and heal faster than usual after receiving damage from hitting a pain block.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Mecha Med set, which consists of the following items:

    * Mecha Med Helmet (hair)

    * Field Medics Mecha Med Scarf (neck)

    * Mecha Med Shirt (shirt)

    * Mecha Med Pants (pants)

    * Mecha Med Shoes (feet)

    * Ambu-Lance (hand)
  * Unlike the other items in the set, the Field Medics Mecha Med Scarf was added earlier, during Grow4Good Week 2021.
