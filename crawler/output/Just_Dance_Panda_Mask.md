The **Just Dance Panda Mask** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of UbiWeek 2023 Day 5 on February 27, 2023.
##  Function
When equipped, the Just Dance Panda Mask serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
When equipped together with the Just Dance Panda Onesie, a dancing panda will appear next to the player when dancing.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Just Dance Panda set, which consists of the following items:

    * Just Dance Panda Mask (Hat)

    * Just Dance Panda Onesie (Shirt)
