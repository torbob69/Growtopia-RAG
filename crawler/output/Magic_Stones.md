The **Magic Stones** is a splicable hand item which was added as a part of the Spelunkorama Strikes Back! update on February 6, 2020.
##  Function
I'm attacking the darkness!

My magic fades went out.

When equipped, the **Magic Stones** grants the _Lightsource: Magic Stones_ mod, which allows the player to emit light in dark areas. They are also used to enter Rune Carved Doors which gives players access to the mines.
