# Mods/Pineapple Magic

< Mods
**Pineapple Magic** is a mod that occurs to a player who feeds a Pineapple to another player with a Pineapple Body (from eating 600 Pineapples).
There are four different types: Pineapple Magic, Double Pineapple Magic, Triple Pineapple Magic, and Ultimate Pineapple Magic.
  * The **Pineapple Magic Mod** is obtained by feeding a Pineapple Body with no Pineapple mod.
  * The **Double Pineapple Magic Mod** is obtained by feeding a Pineapple Body with the **Full of Pineapples Mod**.
  * The **Triple Pineapple Magic Mod** is obtained by feeding a Pineapple Body with the **Turning Into A Pineapple Mod**.
  * The **Ultimate Pineapple Magic Mod** is obtained by feeding a Pineapple Body with the **Pineapple Form Mod**.
  * The **Ultimate Super Pineapple Magic Mod** is obtained by feeding a Pineapple Body using Super Pineapple with the **Pineapple Form Mod**.

A pineapple would then revolve around the player upon feeding a Pineapple Body. After 20 minutes, the Pineapple Magic ends and the player will be rewarded with a pineapple-themed item.
