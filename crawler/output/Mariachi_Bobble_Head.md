The **Mariachi Bobble Head** is an unsplicable non-solid SFX foreground block which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2020 on May 1, 2020.
##  Function
As it is an SFX foreground block, punching a Mariachi Bobble Head plays a bobbing animation and a guitar sound effect.
##  Trivia
  * The Mariachi Bobble Head is seemingly designed after a typical mariachi band member who plays the vihuela, a string instrument shaped like a guitar but tuned like a lute.
