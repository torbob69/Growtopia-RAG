# Superpower - Pineapple Spear

This is a Rare Lightning Superpower card for use in fighting crime. You can also place it as a block! Card effect: Impale your opponent for 3 damage. This exhausts you for a turn, but you can use a Lightning card to get a second wind. Even if countered, the spear still does 1 damage!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.
