# Turkey Hunting Pack
During Thanksgiving Week

|
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Turkey Hunting Pack** for **20,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"This pack includes a Turkey Sucker! This item will help you catch and cook Turkeys! This item has a chance of breaking, when it does there is a chance the regular turkeys you have caught will turn into a Rare Organic Turkey. Ring these bells to call Thanksgiving Turkeys to your world. Grab a Thanksgiving Bell to start a Turkey Surprise! Thanksgiving Bells only work once, so make sure you have some friends nearby, because it's difficult to bash all Thanksgiving Turkeys by yourself! There is a chance of getting a Rare Torch of Enlightenment Item from this pack."
Every purchase will give **1** item.
The **Turkey Hunting Pack** is a pack that was released on the 19th of November, 2020, as a part of the Thanksgiving Week 2020 update.
The Turkey Hunting Pack includes:
  * Thanksgiving Bell x1
  * Turkey Sucker x1

##  Trivia
  * As of Thanksgiving Week 2021, the pack's price increased from 15,000 to 20,000 Gems.
  * As of Thanksgiving Week 2021, the pack now only gives 1 Thanksgiving Bell, instead of 3.
