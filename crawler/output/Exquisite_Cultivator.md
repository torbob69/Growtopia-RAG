The **Exquisite Cultivator** is an unsplicable hand item that was released as part of Summer Clash update on June 1, 2018.
##  Function
Exquisite Growth

Aw, no more growth

When equipped, it provides the player the _Reduced Growtime: Exquisite Cultivator_ mod which provides the player a 1% growth time reduction on trees.

##  Trivia
  * Exquisite items can be converted back to 10 of it's Pristine version by combining them in a Laboratory or any chemical combiner with 5 Golden Block and 5 Steel Block.
  * It was used to earn points during the Speedy Splicers Grow Event by planting seeds with the Cultivator equipped. This item no longer awards points to players, but instead provides a 1% growth time reduction.
