The **Staff of the Deep** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of SummerFest 2020 on July 3, 2020.
##  Function
Let the fish fly!

Over fished and outta here!

When equipped, the **Staff of the Deep** grants the _Punch Damage: Staff of the Deep!_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster.
The  The Seven Seas : The Heart of the Ocean! mission requires 20 of this item.
