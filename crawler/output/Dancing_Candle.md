The **Dancing Candle** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block that was added as part of Valentine's Week 2022 on February 11, 2022.
##  Function
Being a toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block, a Dancing Candle can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Dancing Candle will change its appearance, showing a 7-framed animation.
##  Trivia
  * The Dancing Candle's appearance is based on Lumiere, a supporting character in _Beauty and the Beast_, a 1991 animated feature film produced by Walt Disney Feature Animation.
