The **Team Pennant - Build** is a a non-solid block which was added in the Balloon Warz update. It is a rare drop from a Balloon-O-Matic for every 8000 rarity given to it, when Balloon Fragments are given to it or when it explodes.
##  Trivia
  * Team Pennant - Build is one of 3 Team Pennants added in Balloon Warz, alongside Team Pennant - Punch and Team Pennant - Grow. Each pennant represents their own teams.As an animated foreground block, a Team Pennant - Build features an automatic 2-frame animation of its flag waving.
