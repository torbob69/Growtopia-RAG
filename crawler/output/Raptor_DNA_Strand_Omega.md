# Raptor DNA Strand Omega

This is a strand of prehistoric DNA. It appears to be the tail end of a velociraptor DNA sequence. Perhaps there's a way to recombine it and bring them back! That doesn't seem like a terrible idea at all.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item has no use... by itself.

The Raptor DNA Strand Omega is a DNA released along as part of the Growassic Park Update. It is obtainable from extracting fossil drops using a DNA Extractor and is used to make dinosaurs (riding raptors).
