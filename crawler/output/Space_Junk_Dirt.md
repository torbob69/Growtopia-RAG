The **Space Junk Dirt** is an unsplicable solid foreground block which was added as part of the Startopia 2.0 update on June 1, 2020. Multiple layers of Space Junk Dirt can be found in Descended Planet worlds after completing the Desolated Waste Sector.
##  Function
When placed, the Space Junk Dirt serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
