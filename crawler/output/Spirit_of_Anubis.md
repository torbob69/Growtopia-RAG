# Spirit of Anubis

Drawn back from the endless deserts of the afterlife, the power of Anubis flows through you! By combining his relics, you have summoned his spirit from across the gulf of time, and in thanks for freeing him from the embrace of the void, he has agreed to lend a portion of his might to you in your adventures!

The **Spirit of Anubis** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Time-Tossed! on September 5, 2019.
##  Obtaining
### Riddle Solution
Know your heart! Three parts it contains. The first close to home. The second is encased in lore. The third is broken to the bone.

A Secret Of Growtopia may contain the sentences above. They reveal the first half of the riddle's solution.
Using the command _/love_ in one's home world (set as such using _/sethome_) will give one the following message, "_Your heart begins anew with 'X'_ "), with X being a random letter.
Then, upon checking the info of Card Block - Heart, one will find an additional line stating "_The center of your heart is 'X'_ ".
After being cured from a Broken Heart malady (obtainable randomly from performing surgery), one will discover the final messsage, "_Your heart has been restored, the final piece is 'X'!_ ". With all three letters kept in mind, one may proceed to the last part of the riddle.
Acolyte, enter the gateway to the nether while the sun rests full atop the great wonder and the spirit will be appeased.

A Secret Of Growtopia may contain the sentences above. They reveal the second half of the riddle's solution.
While equipping the full Anubis clothing set (Anubis Boots, Anubis Greaves, Anubis Mail, Anubis Mask, Anubis Seal, and Sun Blade) in a world with an arid weather, which can either be natural or use a weather machine, one must activate a Sungate linking to DUAT:XXX (the Xs being one's three letters). The Sungate should have Sandstone placed on top and beside it, and on top of the Sandstone above the Sungate, there needs to be a charged Solar Collector.
Entering the Sungate will discharge the Solar Collector, blow up the Sungate, and gives one the following message, "_The great spirit of Anubis weighs your heart and finds you worthy!_ ". One will then receive the Spirit of Anubis at the cost of losing the Anubis set.
##  Function
You are one with the spirit of Anubis!

Return from the afterlife!

When equipped, the **Spirit of Anubis** grants the _Double-Jump + Punch Damage: Anubis Form_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and break blocks faster.

  * As of April 1, 2020, the Spirit of Anubis allows players to jump a second time in mid-air.
