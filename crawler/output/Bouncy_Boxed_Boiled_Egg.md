# Bouncy Boxed Boiled Egg

Jump on this bouncy boxed boiled egg!

**1h**

Bunny Egg Reward

Break a  Bunny Egg at 30-34% completion. 5 of this item is given out each time.
Bunny Eggs may explode randomly when their sizes are above 100%.
The **Bouncy Boxed Boiled Egg** is an unsplicable solid foreground block which was added as part of Easter Week 2023 on April 7, 2023.
##  Function
Jumping on a trampoline block such as a Bouncy Boxed Boiled Egg will cause the player to bounce, and by holding the jump button, the player can jump even higher. The _High Jump_ mod also boosts one's jump height on a trampoline block.
