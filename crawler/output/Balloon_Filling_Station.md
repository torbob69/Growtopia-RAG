# Balloon Filling Station

The ideal station for creating war balloons! Shame every Growtopian is out filling balloons, though - the water's running low and it'll take a while to fill yours!
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be placed in two directions, depending on the direction you're facing.

Balloon-O-Matic Reward

Use or blow up a  Balloon-O-Matic.
The Balloon Filling Station was introduced during the Balloon Warz update. It drops a random war balloon but can only drop during the war. It is a rare drop from a Balloon-O-Matic.
