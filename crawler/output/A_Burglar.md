# A Burglar

This is an **NPC** , or a non-player character.
Unobtainable

This item does not have any recipe because it is no longer, or was never obtainable.
**A Burglar** is a removed solid SFX foreground block which was added on May 16, 2013, a day before the addition of Zombie Defense Pack.
##  Function
As it was an SFX background block, punching A Burglar played a hurt animation and caused it to say, "_Ow, Stop!_ ". This item was used in the GrowTech Pharma storyline, representing an intruder who broke into the pharmacy. The dummy could be seen at the end of the course in the world GROWTECHPHARMA.
##  Trivia
  * A Burglar could be seen wearing these items:
    * Ski Cap
    * Burglar Mask
    * Referee Shirt
    * Black Pants
    * Patent Leather Shoes
    * Flashlight
  * As of the release of the g-Virus, A Burglar's data had been completely wiped and replaced by Tombstone's.
  * The item's "_Ow, Stop!_ " sound is now only played when an Awkward Friendly Unicorn is punched.
