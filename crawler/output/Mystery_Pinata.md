The **Mystery Pinata** is an unsplicable event mystery foreground block which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2014 on May 2, 2014.
##  Function
As an event mystery block, the Mystery Pinata can be placed on top of any dropped items. When smashed, it will behave similarly to the Pinata and sprays its contents over a small area. It swells up in size as the player punches it, rather than developing cracks like most items do while breaking. Certain items become different items when dropped inside.
