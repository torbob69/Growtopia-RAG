##  Function
When a player who is holding an Adventure Item - Torch walks past this block, it will use up the torch and all braziers in the world will be lit up for 10 seconds. While the braziers are lit up, the Adventure Barriers in the world will also either open or close. Open barriers will close, while closed barriers will open.
