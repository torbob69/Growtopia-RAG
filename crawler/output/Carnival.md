# Carnival

**Carnival** is a 3-day event which takes place on a random Friday every month where players can play a wide variety of minigames, as well as complete a quest in order to obtain a ring from the Ringmaster. The event can be accessed in the worlds **CARNIVAL1–CARNIVAL50** (previously CARNIVAL1–CARNIVAL150, but was later reduced).
##  Update History
The original Carnival update was introduced on October 13, 2014. It added four games—Concentration, Growganoth Gulch, Mirror Maze, and Shooting Gallery—and four rings—Fistful Of Rings, Ring Of Force, Ring Of Winds, and The One Ring. A side attraction called the Bouncy House was also introduced, which didn't come with any prize.
The Brutal Bounce game was implemented on April 18, 2015, replacing the Bouncy House. Prizes include Carnival Spikeball, Carny Vest, and Flame Scythe.
Death Race 5000 was introduced in WinterFest 2014, which began on December 19, 2014. Prizes include Carnival Pipe, Tiny Tank, and Edvoid's Fire-Nado.
Jester's Cap, the very first Mystery Prize, was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2015. The second one, Licorice Rod, was added alongside the Fishin' Pack on September 11, 2015.
Carnival Pinwheel, the third Mystery Prize, was added as part of the Daily Challenges update, alongside the Ring Of Savings. The latter can be used to save 10% on Golden Tickets. Globe, the fourth Mystery Prize, was added in Player Appreciation Week 2016.
The Stuff! update, which was released on October 1, 2018, introduced the Spiky Survivor game. Prizes include Carnival Ladders, Dragon Block, and Ethereal Rainbow Dragon.
The fifth Mystery Prize, Toucan's Bill, was added on Player Appreciation Week 2019 Day 3, while the sixth, Capuchin Leash, was added on Player Appreciation Week 2020 Day 6.
##  Gameplay
In CARNIVAL, players are able to purchase Golden Tickets from the Golden Ticket Booth for 100 rarity worth of any item (e.g. 1 Dirt would equal 1 rarity; 100 Dirt would be 100 rarity, which would be traded for 1 ticket) or 1 World Lock for 30 Golden Tickets. With a Golden Ticket, players are able to play any Carnival game.
A prize will be awarded if the player is able to complete the minigame within the time limit. Prizes are ranked from common to rare to ultimate, Ultimate Prize being the rarest and most valuable of the three prizes. Mystery Prizes may also be given randomly from playing any of the games. Prizes are awarded by luck and do not reflect the time spent playing the minigame. Winning 50 CARNIVAL games will give a Funtime Block.
The Ring Of Savings is a prize that players obtain when they rank in the top three in the Carnie Contest Daily Challenge. With this ring equipped, one can save 10% on Golden Tickets (pay 90 rarity per ticket, or get 33 per World Lock).

##  Carnival Games
### Shooting Gallery
Requires the player to punch targets as quickly as possible, points are rewarded for each punch. There are two locations for Shooting Gallery at the Carnival, they are placed directly to the left and right of the entrance, with different prizes at each. A score is 30 is required to win.
#### Prizes
Rarity — Left Shooting Gallery — Right Shooting GalleryUltimate Prize — Lion Taming Whip — Orbs Of Elixir
Rare Prize — Lion Block — Elephant Block
Common Prize — Yellow Pennant — Blue Pennant
### Growganoth Gulch
Requires the player to climb a tower and hit the target. Creepstone Platforms may turn off at any time and Evil Eyes may open at any time throughout, attempting to kill the player. There are two locations for Growganoth Gulch at the Carnival, one on either edge of the world, each with different prizes.
#### Prizes
Rarity — Left Growganoth — Right GrowganothUltimate Prize — Blazing Electro Wings — Diamond Rocket Shoes
Rare Prize — Carnival Awning — Ringmaster Suit
Common Prize — Red Pennant — Carnival Wallpaper
### Mirror Maze
Requires the player to run through an invisible maze to reach the end. One is located on the left of the concentration game.
#### Prizes
Rarity: PrizeUltimate Prize: Calliope
Rare Prize: Funhouse Mirror
Common Prize: Carnival Block
### Concentration
A game testing memory. Players must punch the same card consecutively. There are two locations for Concentration at the Carnival, located roughly in the center of the world, each with different prizes.
#### Prizes
Rarity — Left Concentration — Right ConcentrationUltimate Prize — Diamond Diaper — Diamond Wings
Rare Prize — Ringmaster Hat — Carnival Sign
Common Prize — Green Pennant — Carnival Platform
### Brutal Bounce
Replaced the Bouncy House. Requires at least 2 players to start. If a player punches anywhere in the house, a Carnival Spikeball will spawn. A new spikeball can be spawned by punching elsewhere. The Spikeball acts like a Shockinator. The game ends when there is one player left, otherwise, no one will win.
#### Prizes
Rarity: PrizeUltimate Prize: Flame Scythe
Rare Prize: Carny Vest
Common Prize: Carnival Spikeball
### Hall of Mirrors
A fun area with no prize. Mirrors are placed which causes the player's shape to be distorted.
### Death Race 5000
A mini-game where at least three other players race against each other in a Lava and Death Spike maze. It is located on the left of Ringmaster's Room and on the top left side of the world. The first player to reach the end wins.
#### Prizes
Rarity: PrizesUltimate Prize: Edvoid's Fire-Nado
Rare Prize: Tiny Tank
Common Prize: Carnival Pipe
### Spiky Survivor
A mini-game where players must survive falling Carnival Platforms. To kill other players, players must punch empty spaces, summoning Winter Wind which pushes players off into the Carnival Spikeballs below.
#### Prizes
Rarity: PrizesUltimate Prize: Ethereal Rainbow Dragon
Rare Prize: Dragon Block
Common Prize: Carnival Ladders
### Waste a Ticket
Use Coffee and a Parasol or a Das Red Balloon or Bubble Gum and float to the top right corner of the world to earn an achievement. (There is a secret attraction at the top right corner in the map. Using only a Double Jump mod item won't allow the player to reach the entrance.

##  Trivia
  * The amount of fireworks seen after a player wins correlates to the prize they won. There is one for the common prize, two for the rare prize and three for the ultimate prize.
  * The CARNIVAL world is filled to the brim with Calliopes, allowing players to hear the music from anywhere in the world.
  * A server restart notice just before The Carnival update said, "_You hear music, laughing, and the sound of hammers. Come back in a few minutes and they will be ready! (server being updated now)_ "
  * Winning the Brutal Bounce game during the Carnie Contest Daily Challenge does not give any point.
  * On March 6, 2015, a former moderator mrthadawee was tied to the leak of one of the door IDs of the CARNIVAL world, thus allowing many players to enter the Carnival while it wasn't available. The developers changed the IDs after few hours, while the Ringmaster and the Carnival games were made to be disabled during the off-time of the Carnival, in the event that anyone should ever again breach the world.
  * On May 29, 2017, another door ID leak occurred (ID was FJA987) and players managed to enter CARNIVAL. Due to the patch, however, nobody could use the Ringmaster or play any of the games.
  * Blocks obtained from CARNIVAL have a high chance to drop items from Hidden Riches
  * When Carnival ends, all players remaining in the CARNIVAL world will be instantly respawned and returned to the Main Door.
