# Soul Reaper

Get more souls for every enemy defeated.
Soul Reaper (Rarity: 1)
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **6 Hits** **5 Hits** – Restores after **4s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **0 - 1**
**31s**
Dungeons Buff

| A player has a chance to purchase this Dungeons ability from the  Dungeons Shopkeeper after every room cleared for  Souls ×60.
Note: The ability disappears after the player leaves the Dungeon.   |
The Soul Reaper is a dungeon ability that was added during dungeons update on October 23rd, 2025.
##  Function
When the player received Soul Reaper, the player will gain more  Souls than usual by defeating enemies.
