The **Dark Castle Door** is an unsplicable non-solid door foreground block which was added as part of Block Party 2 update on April 1, 2019.
##  Function
As a door, the Dark Castle Door can be entered (via punching) by a player, moving them instantly to another tile. A block with an ID must first be placed on the tile for this to work.
