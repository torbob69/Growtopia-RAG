The **Hidden Door** is an unsplicable entrance foreground block which was added as part of the Hidden Riches update on September 1, 2017.
##  Function
Being an entrance, a Hidden Door allows players with access to walk through it, or, when its property is changed, allowing any player to walk through it. When placed in a public area, it is impossible to be closed for anyone.
##  Trivia
  * Down to the graphics, it is an exact copy of the Art Deco Block.
  * Players managed to obtain the item through the Small Seed Pack and the Harvest Moon Blast, allowing players to obtain the item before the first Saturday since the update.
