The **Sonic Buster Katana** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2021 Day 4 on August 26, 2021.
##  Function
Slashing at the speed of sound!

I shealth my blade!

When equipped, the **Sonic Buster Katana** grants the _Punch Damage: Sonic Buster Katana_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster. It also replaces the player's fist into dark blue slashes when punching.
