Go ahead and jump around the world with your very own Pogo Stick!

The **Pogo Stick** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Easter Week 2025 on April 17, 2025.
##  Function
Up, up and away!

Down, down and near!

When equipped, the **Pogo Stick** grants the _Speedy + High Jump: Pogo Stick_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and jump one tile higher.
