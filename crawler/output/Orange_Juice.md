# Orange Juice

Oranges are great. Grind the heck out of them and they're still delicious! Only, like, wetter.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **3 Hits** **3 Hits** – Restores after **4s** of inactivity
Gems Drop: **N/A**

Food Grinding

In a Food Grinder, grind...
Orange **(x100)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Orange Juice** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Player Appreciation Week 2017 Day 1.
##  Function
When consumed, the player will say "_YUM!_ " and do the _:D_ emote.
