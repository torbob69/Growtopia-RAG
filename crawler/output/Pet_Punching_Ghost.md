The **Pet Punching Ghost** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2022 Day 5 on August 19, 2022.
##  Function
Spirt Unleashed!

Spirit Away!

When equipped, the **Pet Punching Ghost** grants the _Punch Range: Pet Punching Ghost_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further.
##  Trivia
  * The Pet Punching Ghost is a reference to Star Platinum, a powerful humanoid entity from JoJo's Bizarre Adventure.
    * The hat is also a reference to Jotaro Kujo's hat from the same series.
    * The item and mod description calls the entity _Spirit_ , a reference to the term _Stand_ that the series calls its humanoid entities.
  * The item description and equip message contain misspelled words, specifically "_your_ " and "_Spirt_ " respectively.
