# Glitched Worlds

**GlitchedWorlds** are worlds in the game where there is something unusual and abnormal in the worlds caused by a glitch. These can be extra/missing bedrock, abnormal size, etc.

##  Additional Notes
  * Worlds with Gems in Bedrock are common and are a result of Moderators who walk through Bedrock wearing a Sack O' Joy. Some Gems in Bedrock are a result of them being spawned in to celebrate a World Of The Day.
  * Worlds with painted Bedrock and Main Doors were due to people being able to paint them through a glitch involving painting items and moving the Main Door to the painted area. As a result, the Main Door and the Bedrock below would be painted.
  * Most worlds with broken Bedrock were due to a bug which allowed players to break Bedrock very early on in the game. Servers were shut down and all the worlds were manually repaired. Some worlds with broken Bedrock still exist.
  * Some additional Bedrock glitches are found in pre-glitched worlds (dating back to 2013) which allowed players to add Bedrock using a Door Mover if they had one glitched Bedrock. The Bedrock is still possible to add using a Door Mover to this day.
  * Worlds containing Public Lava were intentionally added by the developers during the Player Appreciation Week's SUPERMODDANCEPARTY.
  * Worlds with unstacked items (i.e., lots of Dirt stacked on one tile without being grouped) are a result of the feature not being introduced very early in the game. The same goes for unstacked Gems.
  * Worlds with more than 20 Giving Trees are a result of players placing them in worlds which were not locked with a World Lock. This was later patched.
  * Worlds with World Locks or Diamond Locks in Vending Machines or Display Blocks are a result of players being able to do so when the items were first introduced into the game. This was later patched.
  * Worlds which have zombie immunity without a Zombie Jammer were Worlds Of The Day. This effect is now no longer being given out.
  * On 31st October 2015, worlds which had edited text on their Main Door were reset to EXIT.
  * Worlds with a Dark Cave Background behind the Bedrock and without a Main Door (with spawn point as a Rune Carved Door) can still be generated by using a Cave Blast. The Dark Cave Background automatically spawns on the Bedrock. If you move the Main Door using a Door Mover anywhere, you will spawn in the Rune Carved Door. If you enter it, it will disappear, and you will spawn normally on the Main Door.
