The **Plasma Heart** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as May 2021’s Item Of The Month on May 1, 2021.
##  Function
I'm alive with static!

Electric shock!

When equipped, the **Plasma Heart** grants the _Light Source + Build Range_ mod, which allows the player to emit light in dark areas and build one tile further. The color of the Plasma Heart varies depending on the player's local time.
##  Trivia
  * Just like the Legendary Comet Hair, equipping the Plasma Heart whilst using a Spirit Guide will duplicate the former's effects. This bug has not been patched as of July 5, 2021.
