The **Riding Meteor** is a splicable feet item which was added as part of the original Night Of The Comet update on November 27, 2014.
##  Function
You can move faster!

You are slow again.

When equipped, the **Riding Meteor** grants the _Speedy_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land. Equipping a Riding Meteor also leaves a trail of blue comet dusts behind the player.
