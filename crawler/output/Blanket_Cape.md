The **Blanket Cape** is an unsplicable back item introduced on the Player Appreciation Week 2015 Day 6.
##  Function
When equipped, the Blanket Cape serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
Step 7 of the  Quest For The Sky requires the player to deliver 10 of this item.
##  Trivia
  * Obtaining a **Blanket Cape** for the first time will complete one of the Epic Quests.
  * Unlike other types of capes, it does not grant the player the _Double Jump_ mod.
