# Uranium Necklace

Wearing this will dramatically decrease your lifespan, but you'll be looking GOOD until it's over!

Daily Challenge

Place in the top three of the **Radiation Race** Daily Challenge.
The **Uranium Necklace** is an unsplicable chest item which was added as part of the Geiger Day update on May 5, 2017, the same day Cinco De Mayo Week 2017 was released.
##  Function
You are aglow with radiation!

You have recovered.

When equipped, the **Uranium Necklace** grants the _Irridated_ mod, which allows the player to be irridated for as long as it is worn.
