The **Turkey Hat** is an unspliceable hat item which was added as a part of Thanksgiving Week 2019.
##  Function
This silly turkey has found a new home on your head.

This takes hat hair to another level!

When equipped, the **Turkey Hat** grants the _Double Jump + Slow Fall: Turkey Hat_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
