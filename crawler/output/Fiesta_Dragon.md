The **Fiesta Dragon** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2014 on May 2, 2014.
##  Function
You command a mighty (happenin') dragon!

Your dragon has abandoned you.

When equipped, the **Fiesta Dragon** grants the _Dragonborn_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster and knock back other players further. Additionally, it summons a dragon that follows the player around, which also replaces the player's punch animation with gouts of confetti and streamer particles.
Step 14 of the  Quest For Fire requires the player to deliver 3 of this item.
