The **Boombox** is a splicable non-solid toggleable animated foreground block which has been added since Growtopia's launch.
##  Function
<https://growtopiawiki.com/w/File:Boombox.ogg>The Boombox's music
Being a toggleable animated foreground block, a Boombox can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Boombox will change its appearance, showing a 2-frame animation, and play a piece of music.
