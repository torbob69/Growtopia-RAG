The **Fairy Wings** is a splicable back item which was added as part of _Growtopia_ 's release.
##  Function
You can jump in mid-air! It's like flying, but less.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Fairy Wings** grants the _Double Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
Step 4 of the  Quest Of The Heavens requires the player to deliver 600 of this item.
##  Trivia
  * The Fairy Wings was the first wings added in the game.
  * This item is considered a part of the fairy set, which consists of the following items:

    * Fairy Wings (back)

    * Fairy Crown (hat)

    * Fairy Wand (hand)

    * Fairy Dress (shirt)

    * Fairy Skirt (pants)

    * Fairy Slippers (feet)
