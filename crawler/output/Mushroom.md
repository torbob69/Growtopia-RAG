The **Mushroom** is a splicable solid trampoline foreground block that has been added since Growtopia's launch.
##  Function
Jumping on a trampoline block such as a Mushroom will cause the player to bounce, and by holding the jump button, the player can jump even higher. The _High Jump_ mod also boosts one's jump height on a trampoline block.
