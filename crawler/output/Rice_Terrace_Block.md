# Rice Terrace Block

Take a walk by the beautiful rice terraces carved into the mountains!

Consumption

Consume a(n)  Garuda Chest **(x1)**.
May yield **5** item each.

The **Rice Terrace Block** is an unsplicable platform foreground block which was added as part of the Garuda's Glory update on August 26, 2022.
##  Function
A Rice Terrace Block behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath.
