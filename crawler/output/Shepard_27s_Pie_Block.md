The **Shepard's Pie Block** is an unsplicable solid trampoline foreground block which was added as part of St. Patrick's Week 2022 on March 11, 2022.
##  Function
Jumping on a trampoline block such as a Shepard's Pie Block will cause the player to bounce, and by holding the jump button, the player can jump even higher. The _High Jump_ mod also boosts one's jump height on a trampoline block.

##  Trivia
  * The Shepard's Pie Block is based on the Shepard's pie, a real-life English dish featuring minced meat topped with mashed potato.
  * The description of the Shepard's Pie Block alludes to the lyrics of the 1971 song "American Pie" by songwriter Don McLean.[[1]](https://growtopiawiki.com/w/Shepard's_Pie_Block#cite_note-1)
