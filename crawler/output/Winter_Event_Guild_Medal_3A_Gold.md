# Winter Event Guild Medal: Gold

Awarded for achieving FIRST PLACE in the Guild Leaderboards during the Winter season. Show off your victory by placing this medal in your world, and congratulations!
Properties
This item can't be destroyed - smashing it will return it to your backpack if you have room!
This item cannot be dropped or traded.
This item never drops any seeds.
