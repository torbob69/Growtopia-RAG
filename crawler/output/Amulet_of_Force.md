##  Function
The force is strong!

The Amulet is sad!

When equipped, the **Amulet of Force** grants the _FEEL THE FORCE_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster.
##  Trivia
  * The **Amulet of Force** used to stack with items such as the Pickaxe, allowing the player to break some blocks in 2 hits. However, this was changed in version 2.98.
