The **Crimson Eagle Wings** is a splicable back item added with Education Pack in April 13, 2013.
##  Function
You can jump in mid-air! It's like flying, but less.

Gravity - it's the law.

When equipped, the **Crimson Eagle Wings** grants the _Double Jump_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
Step 9 of the  Quest Of The Heavens requires the player to deliver 800 of this item.
