# Winterfest Paper Crown (disambiguation)

This is a **disambiguation** page.
There are more than one subjects in the **Growtopia Wiki** using this name.
The **Winterfest Paper Crowns** are unsplicable consumables which were added as part of WinterFest 2017. When a Winterfest Paper Crown is consumed on a player, it grants that player the _Winterfest Crown!_ mod and a paper crown of the respective color will appear on the targeted player's forehead for 5 minutes.
