The **Green Beer** is an unsplicable consumable that was added during St. Patrick's Week 2013 on March 13, 2013.
##  Function
It ain't easy being you.

Healthy color restored.

When consumed, the Green Beer grants the _Envious_ mod to the player, which turns their skin into light green for fifteen minutes. It can also be fed to a Silkworm to give it a green color. Doing so would make the silkworm sick, however.
