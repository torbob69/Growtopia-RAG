The **Comida Crusher** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2022 on May 5, 2022.
##  Function
It takes two to taco!

I dont want to taco bout it!

When equipped, the **Comida Crusher** grants the _Punch Damage: Comida Crusher_ mod, which allows the player to break blocks faster.
##  Trivia
  * _Comida_ is the Spanish word for food, a fact alluded to by the item's description.
