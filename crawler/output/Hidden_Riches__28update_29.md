# Hidden Riches (update)

Growtopia Gazette
> September 1st: Hidden Riches!
> Something has been unearthed...
> The rainbow light of the Prismatic Aura has visited our worlds! While only the most stylish of Growtopians will own it, its fantastic glow has illuminated hidden riches for EVERYONE! Breaking blocks - ANY blocks - might drop special blocks and essences... What could they combine to form? Pick the right day, and you could find something totally different!
> Maybe you're quick. Maybe you think you've seen it all. But there are deeper treasures to find, artifacts from an ancient world that await the most dedicated... and legend tells of each having a strange attraction to certain things...
> Also, we lost a chicken. Actually, a LOT of chickens. If anybody finds one, see if you can put it to work!
> We hope all of this will help you win big when the next event hits in early October - who's ready for an awesome Harvest Fest?!
> - The Growtopia Team
The **Hidden Riches** update was introduced on the 1st of September 2017. Special items drop from items based on the chi (which can be checked by clicking Info for any item and looking at the Fire/Water/Earth/Wind Chi next to the item name) and for every day of the week there is a chance for blocks to drop a specific item.
##  Daily Item Drops
Breaking any block has a chance to drop one of the following blocks, depending on the day of the week:
Day: ItemMonday: Aurora
Tuesday: Obsidian
Wednesday: Lava Lamp
Thursday: Fissure
Friday: Waterfall
Saturday: Hidden Door
Sunday: Anemone
The item is changed every day at 00:00 GMT/UTC, which is:
19:00 Growtopia Time during EST, or
20:00 Growtopia Time during EDT (Summer Daylight Saving Time)
