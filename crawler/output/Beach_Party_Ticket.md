The **Beach Party Ticket** is an unsplicable component which was added as part of Beach Party 2022 on June 24, 2022.
##  Function
During a Beach Party weekend, a player who wishes to join the BEACHPARTYGAME mini-game may punch  The Bouncer. Should the player has at least one Beach Party Ticket in their inventory, they will instantaneously join the queue and be teleported into a waiting room. A single Beach Party Ticket will be removed from the player once the next round starts, which will happen 30 seconds after at least ten players have joined the queue.
During the game, participants are given a time limit of two minutes to break  Clam Chests hidden inside the  sand, while dodging any appearing  spikes and bees. Breaking a Clam Chest will award  10-100 pearls to the player.
