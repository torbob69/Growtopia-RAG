The **Steppin' Ghost** is an unsplicable foreground block that was added as part of the Blocktor's Orders! update.
##  Function
A Steppin' Ghost behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath. In addition, it hovers around the tile it is placed on visually.
