The **Starship Airlock** is an unsplicable toggleable deadly foreground block which was added as part of the Startopia update.
##  Function
Being a toggleable deadly foreground block, a Starship Airlock works like an Evil Eye, allowing player to open and close the Starship Airlock by punching it. When toggled on, the item acts as a deadly block. When toggled off, it acts as a non-solid block which players can walk through. It can be wrenched to be made public or not. The Starship Airlock also features a unique client-based death animation.
##  Trivia
Dying 100 times to the Starship Airlock will grant you the Achievement "Deadly Vacuum"
