The **The Beast With A Thousand Eyes Mask** is an unsplicable hat item which was added as part of the Gross-Out update on May 1, 2019.
##  Function
I see you!

I don't see you!

When equipped, the **The Beast with a Thousand Eyes Mask** grants the _Eyeballs!_ mod. It will also replace the player's punching animation with eyeballs.
##  Trivia
  * As part of the Mutant Kitchen update on July 26, 2019, the The Beast With A Thousand Eyes Mask's recipe was removed for being "too gross". It was replaced with the Cute Bulb Mask. As part of the Pre-WinterFest 2022 update on December 1, 2022, the The Beast With A Thousand Eyes Mask became obtainable again by harvesting a Winterfest Calendar - 2022.
  * The The Beast with a Thousand Eyes Mask is a reference to the space alien from the American black-and-white science fiction film named The Beast with a Million Eyes.
