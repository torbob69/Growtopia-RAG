# Silk Skirt (disambiguation)

This is a **disambiguation** page.
There are more than one subjects in the **Growtopia Wiki** using this name.
The **Silk Skirt** is an item introduced in the Silkworm Update. The recipes are different depending of the color of the Silk Skirt.
They come in red, blue, aqua, black, white and purple. There are no yellow, green or grey Silk Skirts.
  * Silk Skirt - Red
  * Silk Skirt - Blue
  * Silk Skirt - Aqua
  * Silk Skirt - Black
  * Silk Skirt - White
  * Silk Skirt - Purple
