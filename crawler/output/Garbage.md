Garbage was first introduced in the world **GARBAGEMOUNTAIN**. When all of the Garbage was broken, it was no longer obtainable. Garbage is farmable and when it first came out, players farmed it to create more. Now, the only method of obtaining Garbage is from Clothing Compactors.
Garbage is a public block, meaning that anybody can break it, regardless of whether they have access to the area or not.
##  Function
When locking a Garbage or any other public block with a Builder's Lock and setting it to only allow building, any other player will be limited from breaking the block.
