One can be obtained for 10 Legendary Clash Key - Series 8, and is limited during Spring Clash 2026 only.

The **Turbine Wings** is an unsplicable and untradable shirt item which was added as part of Spring Clash 2026 on March 20, 2026.
##  Function
Up, up and away!

Out of fuel!

When equipped, the **Turbine Wings** grants the _Double Jump + Slow Fall : Turbine Wings_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
##  Trivia
  * The apperance of the Turbine Wings is a reference to the Boogie Pack, a machine that Rex Salazar uses in the TV series, Generator Rex.
