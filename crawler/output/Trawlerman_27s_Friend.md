The **Trawlerman’s Friend** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of Halloween Week 2020.
##  Function
Reduce fishing time

Normal fishing time

When consumed, the Trawlerman's Friend grants the _Trawlerman's Friend_ food buff mod, which halves the amount of time needed for the player's bait to be bitten for 30 minutes. This effect does not stack with the Licorice Rod's.
##  Trivia
  * The Trawlerman's Friend is based on Fisherman's Friend, a real-life brand of cough drops.
