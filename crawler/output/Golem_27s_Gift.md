The **Golem's Gift** is an unspliceable feet item which was added as August 2020's Item Of The Month on August 1, 2020.
##  Function
Behold the power of the Golem!

Golems Gone!

When equipped, the **Golem's Gift** grants the _Speedy & Punch Power: Golem's Gift!_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and knock back other players further.
Upon equipping the item, five rock fragments will float around the player. When punching, the fragments are launched towards the target one-by-one. These fragments come in three variations — fire, ice and earth — which can be altered by equipping and unequipping the Golem's Gift.
