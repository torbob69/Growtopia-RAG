The **Heartqueen's Crown** is an unsplicable hat item which was added on Valentine's Week 2019. One can be obtained from breaking a Heartstone or a Golden Booty Chest, or from purchasing a Valentine's Goodies.
##  Function
When equipped, the Heartqueen's Crown serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Heartqueen set, which consists of the following items:

    * Heartqueen's Crown (Hat)

    * Heartqueen's Robes (Shirt)
