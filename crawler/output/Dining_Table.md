# Dining Table

Grab your friends, grab some tasty food and drinks and sit down with your friends together at this infinitely extendable table! Perfect for when your extended Growtopian family comes to visit!

The **Dining Table** is an unsplicable foreground block that was added as part of Thanksgiving Week 2020 on November 19, 2020.
##  Function
When a player stands in front of a seat such as the Dining Table for a few seconds, the player will automatically be seen "sitting" on it.
##  Trivia
  * If the Dining Table is placed with another Dining Table horizontally, it will form a long Dining Table.
