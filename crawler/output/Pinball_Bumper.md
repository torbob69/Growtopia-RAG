The **Pinball Bumper** is a splicable and farmable solid bouncy foreground block which was added alongside the Phonecats Hat and GrowBeats Headphones in version 1.13.4 on March 7, 2013.
##  Function
Being a type of bouncy block, a Pinball Bumper will actively push away any player it collides with.
