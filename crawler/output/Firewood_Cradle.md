The **Firewood Cradle** is an unsplicable non-solid toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block which was added as part of Harvest Festival 2021 on September 16, 2021.
##  Function
Being a toggleable multi-framed animated foreground block, a Firewood Cradle can be toggled on or off via punching. When toggled on, a Firewood Cradle will change its appearance, showing a 4-framed animation of the cradle being lit. As a light source, a Firewood Cradle emits light in dark areas.
