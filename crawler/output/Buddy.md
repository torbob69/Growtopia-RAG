# Buddy

This is an **NPC** , or a non-player character.
**Buddy** is the official mascot of _Growtopia_. Buddy can be seen on _Growtopia_ 's app logo.
##  Clothes
  * Brown Shoes (feet)
  * Green Shirt (shirt)
  * Jeans (pants)
  * Red Hair (hair)

##  Trivia
  * In Seth's AMA thread, he said that the reason Buddy wears Green Shirt, Jeans, Brown Shoes, and Red Hair is because they are part of the first clothes to be added in _Growtopia_ on the day of its release date, January 11.
  * Before the release of Hero Portrait - Buddy, he didn't have an official name and was recognized as the "_Growtopia_ 's mascot" only.
  * The Mini Growtopian is a pet item that looks and has the same clothes as Buddy.
  * During the latest WinterFest, the _Growtopia_ 's app logo was changed to a more Christmas look. This was the first time that Buddy had a different appearance on the app logo.
