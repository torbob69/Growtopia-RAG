# Wild West Pack
Growtopia Store

Purchase the **Wild West Pack** for **8,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"Yippee-kai-yay! This pack includes a Cowboy Hat and Cowboy Boots (of course), War Paint, Face Bandana, Sheriff's Vest, Layer Cake Dress, Corset, Kansas Curls, 10 Western Building, Saloon Doors, 5 Western Banners, a Buffalo, 10 Rustic Fences, a Campfire that plays cowboy music, and even a Parasol that lets you drift down slowly. Special bonus: A Rare Six Shooter to blast criminals with!"
Every purchase will give **1** item.
**Wild West Pack** is a pack that was released on September 11, 2013.

  * Six Shooter x1 (Rare)
