Flexible footwear for an honor-bound warrior.

Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Bountiful Bamboo Background Root Cutting **(×30)** ,
Bountiful Climbing Hydrangea Lattice Root Cutting **(×30)** and

This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Samurai's Waraji** is an unsplicable feet item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2019 Day 4 on August 22, 2019.
##  Function
War cry!

Time for a soothing foot spa?

When equipped, the **Samurai's Waraji** grants the _Speedy: Samurai_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land.
##  Trivia
  * This item is considered a part of the Samurai set, which consists of the following items:

    * Samurai's Kabuto (Hat)

    * Samurai's Do Armor (Shirt)

    * Samurai's Suneate & Leggings (Pants)

    * Samurai's Waraji (Feet)
