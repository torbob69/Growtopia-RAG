# Night of the Sewing Dead

Growtopia Gazette
> Feb 3rd: Night Of The Sewing Dead
> It's the Return of the Growing Dead! We've added a new event but instead of happening on a random day, you make it happen! As people get infected with the g-Virus, we build up to the g-Virus Pandemic, a terrible day with amazing new rewards.
> Will you join the ranks of the undead, or save the world by slaying them? It's the first event that can end in two different ways!
> We've also updated sewing with tons of new items you can craft, and they're not just clothes! Plus, there's a handy new item for managing your silkworms a little better.
> - @Seth and @Hamumu
**Night of the Sewing Dead** is an update which introduced on the 3rd of February, 2017. Its theme revolves around sewing and the g-Virus Pandemic.
##  g-Virus Pandemic
As people get infected by zombies, it will build up to the g-Virus Pandemic. The g-Virus Pandemic begins when there are is a cumulative total of 500,000 total zombie infections since the last g-Virus Pandemic. The statistics of the pandemic are shown on G-Virus Info Signs.
During a pandemic, zombies can be killed by using Zombie Weapon items, such as the Chainsaw Hand, Elvish Longbow, Death Ray, etc. Players who are infected cannot use an Antidote to remove their zombie infection during this time either. If players kill a zombie during the apocalypse, there is a chance for a Zombie Brain to drop.
The statistics during the apocalypse are displayed in the chat throughout the event. Players can perform Chemsynth in order to prevent the zombies from winning, while players have to infect others for the zombies to win.

##  Zombie Apocalypse
After a g-Virus Pandemic ends, _if_ the zombies win, an Apocalypse will start. This results in Mutated Cells randomly spawning in worlds, similar to Comet Dust during Night Of The Comet. Combining 200 of them will create a Mutated Seed, which can be used to make a range of new items.
