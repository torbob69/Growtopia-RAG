The **2021 - Zodiac Year of the Ox** is an unspliceable hand item which was added as a part of Lunar New Year 2021.
##  Function
Zodiac Powers!

Another Year!

When equipped, the **2021 - Zodiac Year of the Ox** grants the _Speedy & Cookie Hunter : Zodiac Year of the Ox_ mod, which allows the player to move faster on land and provides a significant chance of doubling fruit drops from Fortune Cookie and Lucky Fortune Cookie Trees.
