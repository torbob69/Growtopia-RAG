The **Soaring Beast** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Wonder Week 2023 Day 6 on October 14, 2023.
##  Function
Take to the skies!

Dropped to the ground!

When equipped, the **Soaring Beast** grants the _High Jump + Double Jump + Slowfall: Soaring Beast!_ mod, which allows the player to jump one tile higher, jump a second time in mid-air, and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.
