The **Staff of Gale** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as July 2022's Item Of The Season on July 1, 2022.
##  Function
Tornado I command thee!

Calm again!

When equipped, the **Staff of Gale** grants the _Punch Range & Double Jump: Staff of Gale_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further and jump a second time in mid-air.
