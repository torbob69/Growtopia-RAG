The **Mexican Hot Sauce Rocket Pack** is an unsplicable back item which was added as part of Cinco De Mayo Week 2020 on May 1, 2020.
##  Function
Fire up the burners!

Cool the jets, eat some Guac.

When equipped, the **Mexican Hot Sauce Rocket Pack** grants the _Double Jump: Spice Boost!_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air.
The  Myths and Legends : Mothman Rising mission requires 1 of this item.
##  Trivia
  * The description of the Mexican Hot Sauce Rocket Pack contains a reference to the Scotch bonnet, a variety of chili pepper with a heat rating of 100,000–350,000 Scoville units. For comparison, most jalapeño peppers have a heat rating of 2,500 to 8,000 on the Scoville scale.
