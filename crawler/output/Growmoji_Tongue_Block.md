Tired of Growmojis only showing up in chat? Well, now you can use this to add a little 'cheekiness' to your world!

The **Growmoji Tongue Block** is an unsplicable solid foreground block which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2019 Day 1 on August 19, 2019.
##  Function
When placed, the Growmoji Tongue Block serves a purely aesthetic purpose.
