##  Function
Chi acitivated! Punch power increased!

All out of Chi

When equipped, the **Brutal Hand Fans** grants the _Punch Power: Brutal Hand Fans_ mod, which allows the player to knock back other players further. It will also replace the player's punching animation with gold slashes.
