**Silk Scarf** is a neck item that was added as part of Valentine's Week 2021 on February 13, 2021.
##  Function
Float through the air with the greatest of ease!

Back down to earth!

When equipped, the **Silk Scarf** grants the _Double Jump + Slow Fall: Silk Scarf_ mod, which allows the player to jump a second time in mid-air and glide slowly to the ground when holding the jump button.

##  Trivia
  * This item's appearance is a reference to Aqua's Scarf, the main deuteragonists in the KonoSuba series.
  * The Silk Scarf's description used to be identical to Sacred Scarf's. However, on an unknown date, Sacred Scarf's description was changed.
  * Silk Scarf has a golden counterpart, which is the  Golden Silk Scarf. Additionally, there exists other redesigned versions:  Holiday Lights Scarf and  Rainbow Silk Scarf.
