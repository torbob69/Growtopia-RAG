**The Ringmaster** is an unobtainable non-solid NPC foreground block which was added as part of The Carnival update on October 13, 2014.
##  Function
The Ringmaster can be seen in the world CARNIVAL. He will offer players of level 20 or above to embark on the Quest For The Ring for 10 Golden Tickets. Should a player manage to complete all ten tasks, they will receive one of his rings.
### Quest
"_You are about to embark on a magical journey! It won't be easy, but the reward is one of the fine rings from my vast collection. You don't get to pick which one, mind you._
_I've got a lot of rings sitting in my bag, with a lot of magical properties. If you want one, picked randomly at my discretion, you'll need to complete 10 little tasks for me._
_Doesn't sound so bad does it?_
_Remember that I travel with the carnival, so if you don't get your quest done this time, be sure to catch me again next month!_
_There's one last thing you should know before you begin. You can quit your quest at any time, but be aware that if you do, you'll lose all your progress, and you'll have to pay another 10Golden Tickets to start again!_
_So... now that you've seen it all, are you truly prepared to hand over 10Golden Tickets and embark on the Quest For The Ring?_"
To start a quest, players must find The Ringmaster and pay 10 Golden Tickets to him. Players can take as long as they require to complete the quest and may require multiple CARNIVALs to complete. Players are required to reach The Ringmaster if they are to move on to the next quest. Players can also give up a quest at any time but must pay another 10 Golden Tickets to start a new quest.

### Rings
**Note:** There is a possibility that players can obtain the same ring more than once. Five of the same ring can be exchanged for another random ring.
Ring: DescriptionFistful Of Rings: Three rings on each of the player's hands. These rings do not have any special effects.
The One Ring: A bronze ring that turns the player's body invisible except for their facial features and clothing.
Gemini Ring: A green ring with a gold Gemini astrological sign on the top. This ring grants a twin (same size and clothes) that follows the player and their actions.
Ring Of Force: A silver ring with a ruby-like jewel on top. This ring turns the player's punching fist into a translucent red fist. It provides players with Punch Power and Punch Damage mods that allows players to punch other players further and break blocks faster respectively. Upon breaking the blocks, the blocks will explode, and fragments of the block's seed will fly out.
Ring Of Winds: A bronze/gold ring with a light-blue jewel on the top. This ring summons clouds for the player to stand on, providing Slow Fall mod, similar to a Parasol.
Ring of Wisdom: A purple ring with a pink jewel on the top. It gives a 10% chance of getting double XP from breaking blocks, harvesting trees or using consumables, similar to the Ancestral Totem of Wisdom.
Ring Of Water: A blue ring with a deep-blue jewel on the top. This ring encases the player in a blue bubble that contracts and detracts and turns the player's skin blue. This ring provides Speedy in Water and Fireproof mods that allows the player to swim faster in water and reduces the damage the player receives from lava pain blocks.
Ring Of Smithing: A black ring with a miniature anvil on the top. This ring is required to forge items using an Anvil.
Ring Of Shrinking: A gold ring with a yellow jewel on top. This ring shrinks the player down like a Mini-You.
Ring of Nature: A gold ring with a dark green jewel on top and gives the player's fist an aqua pulse. Reduces growth time of planted seeds by 2% when equipped, similar to the Ancestral Seed of Life.
Ring of Night: A black ring with a diamond-like jewel on top. It provides Light Source mod, which illuminates an 18 block radius of darkened areas covered by Dark Cave Background around the player.
Ring of Range: A blue ring with a large Crystal Block like jewel on top of it. It increases the player's both punch and build range into 11x11 in worlds they own, and 7x7 in worlds they do not own. In other words, this ring provides both Punch Range and Build Range mods.
##  Trivia
  * Attempting to access The Ringmaster when The Carnival is closed will display the message, "_Don't bother the Ringmaster when he's resting._ " This was a feature added after the door IDs were leaked during March 2015.
  * The Ringmaster can be seen wearing these items:
    * Ringmaster Hat
    * Flat-top
    * Desperado Mustache
    * Ringmaster Suit
    * Black Pants
    * Patent Leather Shoes
    * Fistful Of Rings

He also has a fair skin with a little tan.
  * As part of Player Appreciation Week 2020, the difficulty of the Quest For The Ring was toned down to a degree.
  * As part of the Quality of Life Changes update on May 20, 2022, when trading rings with The Ringmaster, the number of rings needed to trade have been reduced from 10 to 5 and The Ringmaster will not give the player the same ring they have traded.
  * The Ringmaster uses the same sprite as the Founder of the Fancy Gentleman Club.
  * Having 10 different Ringmaster rings in your inventory completes an Epic quest.
  * With the addition of Ring of Range during Player Appreciation Week/2021, the epic quest was changed from _"Have all Ringmaster's rings in your inventory"_ to _"Have 10 different Ringmaster rings in your inventory"_.
