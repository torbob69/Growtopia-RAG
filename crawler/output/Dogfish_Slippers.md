# Dogfish Slippers

Arf! For times when your dogs are barkin', slide on a pair of these slippers and relax in fishy comfort.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
This item can be transmuted.

Fish Training

1 can be obtained from training a(n)  Dogfish.
The **Dogfish Slippers** is an unsplicable feet item that was added as part of the Gone Fishin'! update.
##  Function
When equipped, the Dogfish Slippers serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The Dogfish Slippers is one of the two possible drops from completing the level 10 training of a Dogfish, the other being the Dogfish Leash.
