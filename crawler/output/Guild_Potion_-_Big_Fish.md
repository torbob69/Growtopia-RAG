# Guild Potion - Big Fish

A mighty guild potion! Only a Guild Leader can consume it, which will give its buff to ALL guild members. Guild Potion Buff: 10% chance of catching a bigger fish! - Buff lasts for a limited time.
Properties
This item never drops any seeds.
Data

Type: Consumable
Render Type: Single
Collision Type: No Collision
Hardness: **2 Hits** **2 Hits** – Restores after **2s** of inactivity

**1h**
Combination

In any Chemical Combiner, mix together...
Guild Potion: Black Mixer **(×10)** ,
Guild Potion: White Mixer **(×10)** and
Radioactive Chemical **(×150)**.
This recipe will yield **1** item.
The **Guild Potion - Big Fish** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Guilds update on April 1, 2018.
##  Function
The Guild Leader released the magic!

The effects of the Guild Potion have ended!

When consumed by a guild leader, all guild members are granted a 10% chance of catching a fish with double the size. This effect lasts for 12 hours.
