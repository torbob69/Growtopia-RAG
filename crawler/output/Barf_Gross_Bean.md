The **Barf Gross Bean** is an unsplicable consumable which was added as part of the Gross-Out update on May 1, 2019.
##  Function
AAARGH! It shouldn't go in, it's meant to go out!

I survived!

When consumed, this item grants the _Barf!_ mod for 10 seconds, giving the player a yellow-green skin color. The player will also puke. This effect is purely visual.
