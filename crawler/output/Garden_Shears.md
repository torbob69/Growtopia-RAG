The **Garden Shears** is an unsplicable hand item which was added as part of Spring Clash 2019 on March 1, 2019.
##  Function
When a player equipping a Garden Shears punches a Topiary Hedge, its appearance will transform into one of the four available ones: box, bird, sphere, or spiral. Each punch will guarantee a different outcome.
As of Halloween Week 2019, players could use the Garden Shears to modify a Spooky Bunting to the similar effect. The four available appearances include witch hats, jack o' lanterns, black cats, and ghosts.
