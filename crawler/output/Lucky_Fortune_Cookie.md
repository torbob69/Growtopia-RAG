The **Lucky Fortune Cookie** is an unsplicable consumable added during Lunar New Year 2020 on January 23, 2020. It has a chance to be obtained from splicing or consuming a Fortune Cookie and can be obtained by spraying a Fortune Cookie tree with an Oriental Spice Spray. Harvesting **Lucky Fortune Cookie** trees with any Zodiac Animal equipped gives a chance of twice the yield.
