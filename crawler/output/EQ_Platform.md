The **EQ Platform** is an unsplicable foreground platform block which was added as part of Wonder Week 2022 Day 1 on October 15, 2022.
##  Function
An EQ Platform behaves as a platform, thus a player can jump pass through it from underneath.
##  Trivia
  * This is a block counterpart of the EQ Aura.
