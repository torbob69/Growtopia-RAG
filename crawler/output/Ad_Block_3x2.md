# Ad Block 3x2

Place these blocks in a 3x2 grid to show ads in your world. Check your ad earnings for your world with the Ad Control Block.

Purchase the **Advertisers Pack** for **10,000 ** in the Growtopia Store.
"With this pack you can build custom Ad billboards in your world. There are 3 sizes you can choose from, 4x3, 3x2 and 7x1. You need to arrange these in a grid to activate the Ad. Remember to check your Gem earnings each day using the Ad Control Block."
Every purchase will give **12** items.
The **Ad Block 3x2** is an unsplicable solid foreground block that was added as part of the Growtopia ADventures update on June 22, 2021.
##  Function
In order to fully utilize the Ad Block's function, it needs to be placed together with other Ad Blocks of the same type in a 3×2 grid. The area will then be covered with a 3×2 advertisement.
