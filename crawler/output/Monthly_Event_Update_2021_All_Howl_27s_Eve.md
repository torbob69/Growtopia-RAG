# Monthly Event Update 2021/All Howl's Eve

< Monthly Event Update 2021
Store Announcement

During All Howl's Eve, you can journey to the Halls of Valhowla using a Challenge of Fenrir! You will need to complete all 7 Wolf Worlds to earn it!  You have 3 minutes to find some very special items including the mighty new Beowulf's Blade!  You might be lucky and find some other Beowulf items in Wolf Worlds any time you complete one on All Howl's Eve!
As part of the multiple small **Monthly Event** revamps, an update to All Howl's Eve was added on July 19, 2021. The items themselves are obtainable since July 21, 2021, the day All Howl's Eve arrived.
