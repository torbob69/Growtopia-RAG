The **Mochi Twintails Hair** is an unsplicable hair item which was added as part of Player Appreciation Week 2022 Day 7 on August 21, 2022.
##  Function
When equipped, the Mochi Twintails Hair serves a purely cosmetic purpose.
##  Trivia
  * The item is based on the hairstyle of Tamako Kitashirakawa, the main protagonist in the Tamako Market anime series and the Tamako Love Story movie.
