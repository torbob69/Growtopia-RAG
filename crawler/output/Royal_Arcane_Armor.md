The **Royal Arcane Armor** is an unsplicable shirt item which was added as August 2024's Item Of The Season on August 1st, 2024.
##  Function
Abracadabra!

Misspelt!

When equipped, the **Royal Arcane Armor** grants the _Punch Range + Punch Damage: Royal Arcane Armor_ mod, which allows the player to punch one tile further and break blocks faster.
